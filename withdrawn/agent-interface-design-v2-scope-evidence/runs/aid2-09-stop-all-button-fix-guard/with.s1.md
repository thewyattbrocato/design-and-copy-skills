**Make "Stop all agents" a different kind of control, and put a confirmation step behind it.**

- **Look and position:** Give it a red or danger style, and move it away from Refresh. Put some space between them or move it to an overflow menu. Refresh is harmless and clicked constantly, so it should stay the plain, easy target.
- **Confirmation:** Clicking opens a dialog that says what will happen: "Stop all 12 agents? In-flight documents will halt." The confirm button should repeat the action ("Stop 12 agents"), not just say "OK". Make Cancel the default focus.

The styling change makes a wrong click less likely. The confirmation catches the ones that still happen. Stopping everything is rare and has a big blast radius, so the extra click is worth it. Refresh gets no confirmation, so you won't train people to click through prompts.
