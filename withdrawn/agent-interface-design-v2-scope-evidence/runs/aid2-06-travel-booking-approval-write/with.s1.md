## Push notification

**Title:** Flight cancelled. Rebook needs your OK
**Body:** 9:15pm United to Boston, $640. Seat held for 30 min.

The title says what happened and that she has to act. The body gives the new departure time, the price, and the deadline. The arrival time and the reason for approval are on the screen it opens.

## Approval screen

**Your 6:40pm to Boston was cancelled**

**Held for you: 29:41** *(live countdown; seat released at [time])*

> **Tonight, United [flight #]**
> Denver (DEN) **9:15pm** → Boston (BOS) **2:55am** (Thu)
> Lands 8h 15m after your original flight was due to leave.

**$640**, nonrefundable
Needs your approval because it's over your company's $500 limit.

**[ Approve and book, $640 ]**
**[ Don't book this ]**

*If you do nothing, the seat is released at [time] and nothing is charged.*

### Notes on the choices

- **The arrival time is "2:55am (Thu)".** She might read "tonight" as ending before midnight, so the date is spelled out.
- **"Nonrefundable" sits next to the price.** It's the fact that most affects her decision, so it shouldn't be in fine print.
- **The "do nothing" line is there so she knows a missed deadline costs nothing.** It also means ignoring the notification is a safe choice.
- **The decline button needs a defined next step.** I haven't promised one, because I don't know what the agent does after a decline (look for other options, wait for her, or something else). Whatever it is, the button label or a line under it should say so, for example "Don't book this. Show me other options".
- **Placeholders to fill in:** the flight number, the expiry clock time, and the time zone. Use local times with zone labels if DEN and BOS times could be confused. If the card on file is known, add "Charged to Visa ending 4242" under the price.
