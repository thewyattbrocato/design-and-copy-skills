## A caution on "overwhelmingly"

I'd drop that word, for two reasons.

- **The number doesn't support it.** 9 of 14 is 64%. That's a solid majority but not overwhelming, and the 2 who said no and the 3 maybes will be visible on the slide.
- **The sample is easy to attack.** All 14 came from your own newsletter list, so they're engaged customers. Someone in leadership will probably notice, and if you haven't said it first, it will cost you credibility at the moment you ask for approval.

A stronger framing is the honest one: "9 of 14 (64%) said yes, and 12 of 14 (86%) didn't rule it out." It still has a percentage. Your VP can say it without being caught out. If she insists on "overwhelmingly," that's her call, but I'd push back.
