The deck works. The titles read as an argument, there's one message per slide, the numbers add up (31 + 9 = 40, and 3 + 4 + 2 = 9), and six slides is right for 10 minutes. I'd make three changes before you send it.

1. **Check the March 4 date.** Slide 5 says the first shift is March 4, but the shifts are on Saturdays. In 2027, which is the March your "by March" points to, March 4 is a Thursday. The first Saturday is March 6. If you mean a different date, fix it on slide 5, and make sure "we will train on the first Saturday" in slide 4's notes matches. A volunteer lead is likely to catch this, and it would cost you credibility on a slide that asks for commitment.

2. **Drop slide 6, or merge it into slide 5.** Your ask is on slide 5, but the last thing on screen would be a "Thank you" slide with no QR code. During questions and as people leave, the sign-up should still be visible. Put the logo and phone number on slide 5, leave it up, and say the thank-you to the 31 out loud. That also gives you a minute back. The deck is then five slides.

3. **Soften slide 2's title.** "The wait is the reason families leave" claims more than your data shows. You know that 7% left before being served, mostly at 10 and 11. You don't know why they left. "Most families who leave without food leave at 10 and 11, when the wait is longest" is true as written and still makes your point.

Because it's a projector in a church hall, make the QR code large (at least a quarter of the slide height) with a plain white margin. The back rows can't scan a small one. Keep the chart labels big enough to read from the back too.
