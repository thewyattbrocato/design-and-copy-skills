The deck works. Read as titles alone, the six slides give the problem, the cause, the fix, the ask and the date. The numbers also add up: 31 + 9 = 40, and 3 + 4 + 2 = 9. I'd make three changes before you send it.

1. **Check the March 4 date.** If this is March 2027, March 4 is a Thursday, and you distribute on Saturdays. If it's a different year, check it there too. Slide 4's note also says you'll train on the first Saturday. Decide whether that training is the March 4 shift or a separate session, and make slides 4 and 5 agree. Volunteers will ask.

2. **Drop the "Thank you" slide.** It's the one slot where the ask should still be on screen during questions. Move the phone number and logo onto slide 5 and keep the QR code there. Then end on "Pick a role today" and say the thanks to the 31 out loud, as your notes already plan. That leaves five slides, which is a comfortable fit for 10 minutes.

3. **Put the 7% on slide 2.** The title claims the wait is why families leave, but the chart only shows wait times. The evidence for leaving is in your notes. Add one short line such as "7% left before being served, mostly at 10 and 11", so the slide proves its own title.

Since you're projecting in a hall, make sure the highlighted 11am bar and the QR code have strong contrast. Pale colors wash out on projectors. For the QR code, test that it scans from the back of the room.
