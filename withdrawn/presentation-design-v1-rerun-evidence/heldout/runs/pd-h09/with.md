Ask of each item: **does the room need to see this to follow the argument or make the decision?**

- **Slide:** what they need to see in order to decide. That means the claim, the one number or picture that proves it, and the ask.
- **Say out loud:** the explanation, the "why", and the context. If it's a sentence you'd naturally speak, it doesn't also go on the slide.
- **Handout or appendix:** anything someone would check or look up later, and anything you wouldn't mention unprompted.

**On slides**
