Here are a few directions for your homepage copy:

**Option 1 (emphasizes the co-op/local angle):**
- **Headline:** "Meet Your Neighborhood Dog Walkers"
- **Tagline:** "Same faces, same neighborhood, trusted care"

**Option 2 (trust-forward, personal):**
- **Headline:** "Trusted Dog Walkers in Your Neighborhood"
- **Tagline:** "Familiar faces. Reliable care. People you can know."

**Option 3 (warmest tone):**
- **Headline:** "Marrow Street Walkers"
- **Tagline:** "Your neighbors. Your dog's trusted walkers."

I'd lean toward **Option 1**—it hits all three notes (neighborhood, familiarity, trustworthiness) and the repetition of "same" + "neighborhood" builds confidence that people will see the same person each time. The co-op angle is actually your differentiator against big dog-walking apps.

Which direction resonates? Happy to refine any of these.
