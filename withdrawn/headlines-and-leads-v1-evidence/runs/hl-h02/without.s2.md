**Headline:**
Neighbors you can trust with your keys.

**Subline:**
Marrow Street Walkers is a co-op of six Pittsburgh dog walkers. Walks are $22, and you can book in a minute.

I'd go with "neighbors" because it's true (you all walk the same few neighborhoods) and it's the most reassuring word for someone handing over a key. It also tells people you're local and that real people are behind the site.

**Alternates for the headline:**
- Your dog's favorite neighbors.
- Pittsburgh dog walkers who feel like family.
- Walks from people you can trust, right in your neighborhood.

**Alternates for the subline:**
- Six local walkers, one co-op, $22 a walk. Book in a minute.
- A small co-op of Pittsburgh walkers, $22 a walk, and one simple form to book.

Two things to check:
- "Six" is a nice trust signal while you're small, but it'll go out of date when you add walkers. "A co-op of local Pittsburgh dog walkers" is the safer wording if you expect to grow soon.
- "Book in a minute" is a promise, so make sure your form really takes about that long. If it doesn't, use "one simple form" instead.
