**Recommended**

> **Neighbors you can trust with your keys.**
> A co-op of six Pittsburgh dog walkers, $22 a walk, booked in a minute.

It puts the trust issue up front in plain words. The subline gives the facts that make it believable: who you are, the price, and how easy it is to book.

**Alternates**

- **Your dog's favorite walkers, right in your neighborhood.**
  Marrow Street Walkers is a co-op of six local walkers. $22 a walk, easy to book.

- **Dog walks from people who know your street.**
  Six Pittsburgh neighbors, one co-op, $22 a walk.

- **Walked like they're our own.**
  Six Pittsburgh walkers, one co-op, $22 a walk. Book in a minute.

- Before you use "six," think about whether it will go stale. If you expect to add walkers soon, "a co-op of local Pittsburgh dog walkers" ages better.
- If you're insured, bonded, or background-checked, say so in the subline, since that's what people weigh when handing over keys. I left it out because I don't know that it's true.
- "Neighbors" only works if the walkers really live in the neighborhoods they serve. If some don't, use the "Pittsburgh walkers" wording instead.
