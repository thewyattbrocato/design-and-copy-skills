On the testimonial, there are two legitimate routes:
- **Use a real one.** Message past clients or attendees and ask: "Can I quote you on how this helped your rates?" Get permission and use their actual words, even if the result is more modest than "tripled."
- **Use your own credibility.** For example: *"I've spent years freelancing for designers. Here's what I've learned about raising rates without losing clients."* This is true and works well in ads.
