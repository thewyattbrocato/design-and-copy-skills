Here are some subject line options:

**Straightforward & informative:**
- Pickup Window Changes Starting This Week
- Important: Weekday Pickup Hours Extended, Saturday Pickup Ending
- Your Pickup Schedule Is Changing — Here's What You Need to Know

**Positive-first (highlighting the expansion):**
- Great News: Expanded Weekday Pickup Hours Now Available
- Weekday Pickup Hours Extended — More Flexibility for You
- We're Expanding Pickup — Weekday Hours Now 3-7 PM

**Acknowledging both changes:**
- Pickup Changes: Extended Weekdays, Farewell to Saturdays
- Pickup Updates: More Weekday Flexibility, Saturday Ending
- Changes to Member Pickup — New Hours & Important Dates

**Urgent/timely (since changes are happening now):**
- Action Needed: Pickup Changes Take Effect This Week
- Quick Update on Your Pickup Options This Fall
- Pickup Schedule Update — Effective Immediately

**Conversational/community tone:**
- Let's Talk About Pickup Changes at Greenfold
- We're Adjusting Pickup Hours — Here's How It Affects You
- Member Update: How Greenfold's Pickup Schedule Is Changing

I'd lean toward **"Weekday Pickup Hours Extended, Saturday Pickup Ending"** or **"Important: Pickup Changes Starting This Week"** — they're specific enough that members know it's actionable without opening the email, but friendly enough for a co-op audience.
