Here are some subject line options for your members:

**Direct & clear:**
- Important Changes to Pickup Hours (Effective October 6)
- Your Pickup Schedule Is Changing – Here's What You Need to Know
- Pickup Updates: Expanded Weekday Hours, Saturday Changes

**Emphasizing the positive expansion:**
- More Flexibility: Extended Weekday Pickup Hours Starting October 6
- Good News – Your Weekday Pickup Window Is Expanding
- New Pickup Flexibility: 3-7 pm Weekdays Starting October 6

**Highlighting what members need to do:**
- Please Read: Important Changes to Greenfold Pickup Windows
- Action: Pickup Schedule Changes Coming October 6

**Balanced tone (change + benefit):**
- We're Adjusting Pickup Hours – Here's What's Changing
- Pickup Updates at Greenfold: What Members Need to Know
- Changes Coming to Weekend & Weekday Pickup

I'd recommend the **"More Flexibility: Extended Weekday Pickup Hours Starting October 6"** or **"Your Pickup Schedule Is Changing – Here's What You Need to Know"** approach—the first leads with a benefit (members get a wider window), while the second clearly signals this needs attention but doesn't feel like bad news.

Since Saturday pickup ends (a loss for some members), leading with the weekday expansion softens that message while still being honest.
