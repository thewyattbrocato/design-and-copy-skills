1. "Saturday pickup ending; weekday hours now 3-7 pm"

2. "Weekday pickup expanded to 3-7 pm (Saturday ending)"

3. "Pickup schedule change starting October 6"

Option 1 leads with what's ending and why (the trade-off). Option 2 leads with the expansion first but clearly notes the loss. Option 3 is simpler and lets your preview text do the work. For a transactional member email, 1 or 2 will get opened and understood faster—members need to know Saturday is gone.
