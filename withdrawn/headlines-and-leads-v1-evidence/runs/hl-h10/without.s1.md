Here are some subject line options for the Greenfold Market email.

**Straightforward**
- New pickup hours: weekdays 3-7 pm starting Oct 6
- Pickup changes at Greenfold Market: what to know
- Your Greenfold pickup schedule is changing

**Lead with the benefit**
- Longer weekday pickup: now 3-7 pm
- More time to pick up your order, Monday to Friday

**Lead with the Saturday news**
- Saturday pickup ends Oct 4, and weekday hours get longer
- Heads up: Saturday pickup is ending

**Short and plain**
- Pickup update: new weekday hours, no more Saturdays
- Greenfold pickup: what's changing (and what isn't)

My pick is "Pickup update: new weekday hours, no more Saturdays". It names both changes up front, so members who rely on Saturday won't miss it. If you want a friendlier tone, use "Longer weekday pickup: now 3-7 pm" and put the Saturday news at the top of the email body.

The "(and what isn't)" option works well if members tend to worry about price changes. The email can then reassure them that prices, ordering, and the 9 pm cutoff stay the same.

Today is Oct 7, so both dates (Oct 4 and Oct 6) have already passed. If this email is going out now, I'd switch to present-tense wording like "New weekday pickup hours are now in effect" and drop the "starting Oct 6" phrasing. If the dates are meant to be in the future, the options above work as written. I can adjust either way.
