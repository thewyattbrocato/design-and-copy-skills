Here are some punchier alternatives that lean into the money-saving payoff:

**Emotion-focused (relief/empowerment):**
- "Stop bleeding money to forgotten subscriptions"
- "Uncover every dollar hiding in your subscriptions"
- "Take back money you didn't know you were wasting"

**Action-focused (discovery):**
- "See where your money's really going—then cancel it"
- "Find the subscriptions draining your account"
- "One tap to find every forgotten charge"

**Benefit-focused (the win):**
- "Get back hundreds in forgotten subscription fees"
- "Your money's trapped in subscriptions. We'll free it."

My pick would be **"Stop bleeding money to forgotten subscriptions"** — it's vivid, the action word "stop" is strong, and "bleeding money" emotionally lands better than the rational "forgot you were paying." But the best one depends on your brand voice. What's the tone you're going for—playful, urgent, straightforward?
