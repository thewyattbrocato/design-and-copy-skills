Here are some punchier alternatives:

**Action-focused:**
- "Stop bleeding money to forgotten subscriptions"
- "Find every subscription draining your account—and cancel them"
- "See your hidden charges. Take control back."

**Benefit-focused:**
- "Reclaim hundreds (or thousands) from forgotten subscriptions"
- "One login. Every recurring charge exposed."
- "Your bank data just got honest about where your money goes"

**Curiosity/discovery angle:**
- "See what you're actually paying for"
- "Your subscriptions, ranked by how much you forgot about them"
- "Surprise yourself with what your bank knows you're paying for"

**Short & punchy:**
- "Cancel subscriptions you didn't know you had"
- "Find the money you're leaving on the table"

I'd probably lean toward something like **"Stop bleeding money to forgotten subscriptions"** or **"Reclaim hundreds from forgotten subscriptions"** — they hit the emotional payoff (wasted money) while making the discovery feel like winning something back. The original is accurate but very passive; these flip it toward action and value.
