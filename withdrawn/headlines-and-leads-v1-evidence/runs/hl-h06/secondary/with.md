The current line is solid—it's clear and specific. The first option tightens it by switching to an action verb and removing a clause; the second flips to present-reality framing ("still charging"); the third leads with the outcome (money). If you're testing, **"Stop paying"** is probably the tightest and most direct for a landing-page hero, but **"See which subscriptions"** performs well when the discovery/revelation part of the value matters most in your traffic.
