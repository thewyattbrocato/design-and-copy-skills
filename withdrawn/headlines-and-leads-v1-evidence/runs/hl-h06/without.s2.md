Your cofounder's line is clear, which counts for a lot. It could use more edge and a stronger payoff. Here are some options, grouped by angle.

**Money-leak angle**
- "Your bank statement is hiding things. Let's find them."
- "Stop paying for stuff you forgot you had."
- "Every dollar you're losing to subscriptions you forgot about, found."

**Control and action angle** (leans on the flag-to-cancel feature)
- "See every subscription. Cancel the ones you don't want."
- "Find every recurring charge. Kill the ones you don't need."
- "Your subscriptions, exposed. Your cancel list, ready."

**Wit and personality**
- "That $9.99 a month adds up. So does the other 14."
- "You're probably paying for something you've never used."
- "Somewhere in your bank account, a forgotten subscription is still charging you."

**Short and punchy**
- "Find the charges you forgot."
- "Catch every recurring charge."
- "Forgot it. Still paying for it. Found it."

**My pick:** "Find every subscription you forgot about. Cancel the ones you don't want." It keeps your cofounder's original hook, which is relatable and specific, and adds the second half of the product. Finding and flagging is what makes the app useful, and the original headline doesn't mention it. It's a bit long for a headline, so you could split it into a headline and a subhead:

> **Find the subscriptions you forgot you were paying for.**
> Connect your bank, see every recurring charge, and flag the ones to cancel.

Splitting it also means you don't have to pick between the two versions. The plain headline does the hooking, and the subhead explains the product.
