- Approve a 10-week build of restock ordering with register integration
- Propose a gate: before full build, run a pilot with a small set of stores (for example, 5 stores from the 9 "yes" responses) to see whether stated interest converts to actual orders. If your team can scope it, put that decision point inside the 10 weeks.

## Why I didn't use "overwhelmingly"

- 64% is a majority, not an overwhelming one. If a leader checks the number against the slide, the headline loses credibility, and the rest of the deck with it.
- All 14 came from the newsletter list, so the findings are weaker than a market-wide claim. Saying so first makes the ask more credible, not less.
- Interviews measure stated intent. Adding the pilot gate gives leadership a reason to approve now without overcommitting.
