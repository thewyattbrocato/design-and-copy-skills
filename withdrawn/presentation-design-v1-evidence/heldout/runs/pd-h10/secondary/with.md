**5 minutes, 3 content slides, 1 takeaway:** Nearly two-thirds of the owners we talked to would use restock ordering if it connected to their register, so approve the 10-week build with a checkpoint on price.

I didn't use "overwhelmingly." Nine of 14 is a clear majority, but "overwhelming" implies something closer to a supermajority, and this sample has two problems the room will likely spot:

- **Self-selected:** all 14 came from our newsletter list, so they are our most engaged customers, not independent hardware store owners in general.
- **Stated intent, small n:** 9 of 14 said they *would* use it, conditional on register integration. The 95% range for 9/14 is roughly 39% to 85%.

If someone asks "who did you talk to?" after "overwhelmingly," the credibility cost lands on the whole request. The stronger move is to show the number with its size and let the portal pain carry the emotional weight.
