I didn't use "overwhelmingly". 9 of 14 is 64%, and all 14 came from our own newsletter list. A product leader will probably notice that, and the word would then cost you credibility on the whole ask. The headline below is still strong, and every number in it holds up.

**Slide 1 (about 1 min): Opening and need**
- **Title:** 9 of 14 hardware store owners would use online restock ordering if it connects to their register
- **Visual:** a large **64%**, captioned "9 of 14 independent store owners, September interviews"
- **Say:** who we talked to, the takeaway, and a preview of the three labels: demand, opening, condition.

**Slide 2 (about 1 min): Demand**
- **Title:** Only 2 of 14 said no, and 3 more are open depending on price
- **Visual:** one bar split 9 / 3 / 2, labeled "Yes", "Maybe, depends on price" and "No, prefer calling their rep"
- **Say:** the two "no" owners prefer their rep relationship. That is a preference, not an objection to the product.

**Slide 3 (about 1 min): Opening**
- **Title:** 5 of 14 owners call their distributor's ordering portal slow or confusing
- **Visual:** big **5 of 14 (36%)**
- **Say:** the current online options are weak, so we'd be competing against a poor experience.

**Slide 4 (about 2 min): Condition and ask**
- **Title:** Approve a 10-week build of restock ordering with register integration
- **Body (under 30 words):**
  - Yes depended on register integration
  - Owner: [name]
  - Decision needed by: [date]
- **Say:** the caveat yourself before anyone asks. Say "These are 14 existing customers, so this is strong early signal, not market sizing." Then say how you'll check it, for example a price test during the build. That is a proposal, so confirm it's realistic before you say it.

**Hardest questions to prepare for (backup slides after the end):**
1. *"Isn't this a friendly sample?"* Yes, they're newsletter subscribers. Say what you'll do to reach non-customers.
2. *"What do the 3 'maybes' need on price?"* You have no price data yet, so name that gap and say when you'll close it.
3. *"Why does register integration take 10 weeks?"* Have the scope breakdown ready.

**Drop list if time shrinks:** slide 3 goes first, then fold slide 2 into slide 1.
