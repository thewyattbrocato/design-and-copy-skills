**5 minutes, 4 slides, one takeaway:** 9 of 14 owners would use online restock ordering if it connects to their register, so approve the 10-week build.

**I'd drop "overwhelmingly" and the headline percentage.** 9 of 14 is 64%. That's a clear majority, but "overwhelmingly" invites someone to do the math. All 14 also came from your own newsletter list, so they're your most engaged customers. A leader who spots that will doubt the whole deck. "9 of 14 would use it" is still strong, and it holds up when someone pushes on it. I'd show "9 of 14" as the big number, with 64% beside it if your VP wants a percentage on the slide.
