**I'd drop "overwhelmingly."** The data shows 9 of 14, which is 64%. Two things make that word risky in front of product leadership:
- All 14 came from our own newsletter, so they're already engaged customers. That inflates the yes rate.
- 64% from 14 people is a strong signal, not an overwhelming one. If someone in the room does the math, the word costs you credibility on the whole deck.

The headline below is still strong and holds up: **"Nearly two-thirds"** is accurate. **"Only 2 of 14 said no"** is your best line, because it's true and it's the part that is actually lopsided.
