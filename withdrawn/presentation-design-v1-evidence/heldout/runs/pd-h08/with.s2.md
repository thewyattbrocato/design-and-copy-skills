**Plan: 10 minutes, 7 slides (an opening plus 6 content slides). The takeaway is "The portal is working and on budget, so approve phase 2 funding."**

**Keep, in this order**

2. **Slide 10, monthly active patients trend.** Retitle it as a claim, for example "Active patients grew from X to Y." This is the proof that patients use it.
3. **Slide 12, front-desk calls before and after.** This is the operational payoff. Say the staff-time number aloud from slide 13, and keep the method off screen.
4. **Slide 19, cost to date vs. budget.** It shows the committee that you handled phase 1 money well, which supports trusting you with phase 2.
5. **Slide 20, phase 2 scope.** Limit it to what phase 2 adds and why. If the bottom-clinic gap (slide 9) is a main driver, say so here.
6. **Slide 21, phase 2 cost and timeline.**
7. **Slide 25, the ask.** It should hold the amount, the owner, the decision date, and one line on the cost of not funding (from slide 22). Leave it up during questions.
