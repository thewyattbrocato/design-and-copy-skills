**The test:** ask what each item is for.

- **Slide:** what the room must see to follow and remember the argument. That means the claim, the key number, and the picture that proves it.
- **Out loud:** the explanation, the reasoning, and the story. If you'd say it anyway, don't also print it. Duplicated text makes people read instead of listen.
- **Handout:** anything people would look up, check, or act on later. It's reference material that would clutter the room if you showed it live.
