Overall the slides are clear and the numbers agree across them. Fix the date before you send, and soften a few claims that the data doesn't yet support.

**Fix before sending**

1. **Slide 5: March 4 isn't a Saturday.** March 4, 2027 is a Thursday. The first Saturday in March 2027 is **March 6**. Confirm the year and the date, then update slide 5 and any note that mentions it.

2. **Slide 4 vs. slide 1: "9 shifts" and "9 volunteers" are different units.** Slide 1 says 9 more volunteers, and the roles add up to 9 (3 + 4 + 2). Slide 4 calls them "9 shifts." If these are 9 people each working one 10am–1pm Saturday, say "9 volunteer spots each Saturday, 10am–1pm." Also check whether distribution starts at 8am, since the wait data covers 8–11am and the shifts start at 10.

3. **Slide 5 QR code: test it and add a backup.** Scan it with a phone that's not yours, and check that it opens the right form. Projected QR codes can be too small to scan from the back of the hall, so also put a short web address under it.

**Soften these claims**

4. **Slide 2 title: "The wait is the reason families leave."** The data shows 7% left, and that most left at 10 and 11. It doesn't prove the wait was the only reason. Try: "Long waits are why 7% of families leave without food."

5. **Slide 3 title: "fixes it."** The 25-minute figure is a projection, not a result. Try: "A second line at the east door should cut the wait." In the notes, say "we expect" rather than giving 25 minutes as fact.

6. **Slide 3: "costs no money."** The church approved the door and tables, which is good. Make sure bags, signs, printing, and the clipboard are covered too before you say that.

**Minor**

7. **Slide 5 "Pick a role today."** This works if you present it in person. If the slide is also shared in the email, change it to "Pick a role at the meeting."

8. **Slides 3 and 4 are images you can't check from text.** Look at the floor plan and the role table on the projector, or on a screen the size of the hall's, to make sure the text is readable from the back row.
