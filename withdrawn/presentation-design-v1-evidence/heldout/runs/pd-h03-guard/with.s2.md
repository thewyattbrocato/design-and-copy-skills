The deck is close. The order is right (need, cause, fix, ask), the titles read as an argument, and the numbers add up (31 + 9 = 40, and 3 + 4 + 2 = 9). Here is what I'd change before tonight, most important first.

**Fix before sending**
1. **Check the March 4 date.** I don't think March 4 falls on a Saturday this coming March. It looks like a Thursday in 2027. If your first shift is a Saturday, the date or the weekday is off. Also, the title of slide 1 says "by March" while slide 5 says the first shift is March 4. Pick one deadline and use it on both slides, such as "by March 4".
2. **Slide 2 doesn't show its own claim.** The chart shows waits growing, but the title says the wait is why families leave. The evidence for that, the 7% who left before being served (mostly at 10 and 11), is only in your notes. Put "7% of arrivals left unserved, mostly at 10 and 11" on the slide. The leads will receive this file tonight, and they won't have your notes.
3. **Slide 2 highlight.** Your notes say departures peak at 10 and 11, but only the 11am bar is highlighted. Highlight both bars, or say why 11 alone.
4. **"9 volunteers" vs "9 shifts."** Slide 1 says 9 volunteers and slide 4 says 9 shifts. Someone will ask whether that's 9 people every Saturday or 9 slots spread across a rotation. Say which, in one line on slide 4.

**Smaller changes**
- **Slide 1:** The "about 25 minutes" figure is a projection, not a measurement. Say "we expect" so nobody quotes it back to you as a fact. The 50-to-25 comparison is the payoff, so consider putting it on slide 3 next to the floor plan.
- **Slide 3:** "Costs no money" is a strong claim. Confirm that the tables, the door and any signage really cost nothing, because a lead might ask.
- **Slide 4:** Move "no heavy lifting unless you choose" onto the slide as a short line. It's the objection most likely to stop someone from signing up, so it shouldn't only be in your notes.
- **Slide 5:** Make the QR code large, since people in a church hall will be scanning it from their seats. Put the sign-up link under it in text for the emailed copy.

**Merge slides 5 and 6**
End on the ask, not "Thank you." A thank-you slide stays up during questions and says nothing. Combine them into one closing slide titled "Pick a role today, and your first shift is March 4" (or the corrected date). Put the QR code, the three roles with their counts (3, 4, 2), and the phone number on it. Leave the pantry logo off the closing slide, or make it small. Say the thanks out loud: "Thank you to the 31 who already show up." That keeps the sign-up on screen while you pass the clipboard and take questions.

That makes it five slides for 10 minutes, about 1.5 minutes each with time left for questions. If you run short, cut slide 3's detail first and just say it.

I've assumed the leads read the file on their own tonight and then see it projected tomorrow. If you'd rather not send your notes along, export the slides without them. Once the five items above are on the slides themselves, the deck makes sense even without your talk.
