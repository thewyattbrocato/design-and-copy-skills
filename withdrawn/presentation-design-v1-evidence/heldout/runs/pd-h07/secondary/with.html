What we changed in April

  1. 1

    Sort tickets twice a day

    Triage moved from a weekly sort to a morning and afternoon pass, so new tickets get a first look within hours.

  2. 2

    Route billing questions to a dedicated pair

    Two named people own every billing question, so those tickets stop waiting on general queue order.

  3. 3

    Name an owner for anything older than 5 days

    Aging tickets get a specific person accountable for resolution, not a shared queue.
