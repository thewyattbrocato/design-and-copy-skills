**Pick B.** It's the only one of the three that makes the council *see* Alder Street.

- **A** is the opener every speaker uses. Council members tune it out, and it spends the first 15 seconds on your name and organization before reaching anything they'd remember.
- **C** is a claim with a statistic. It's true, but it could be about any street in any city, so it gives them a reason to agree in principle and nothing specific to vote on.
- **B** is concrete and human. A nine-year-old, a delivery van, and a route to school are things a council member can picture, and "she's fine" keeps it from sounding like a complaint. The last sentence hands straight to your ask, so the request is clear early. Consider making it the actual ask in the closing line too.
