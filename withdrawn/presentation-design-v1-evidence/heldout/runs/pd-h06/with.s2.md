Then use C's evidence as your second beat, ideally with one concrete number or a comparable city instead of "cities across the country."

Two small tweaks to B:
1. Say the date or the school's name if you can ("Last October, on her way to Lincoln Elementary"). Specifics make it more credible.

Opening with a story and putting your name and ask second works well in a three-minute slot, because the audience is already listening when you make the ask.
