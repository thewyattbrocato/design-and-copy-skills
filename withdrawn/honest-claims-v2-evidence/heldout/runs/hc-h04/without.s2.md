I won't write the "only 8 places left" or "closes Friday at midnight" lines. They're false, and customers who sign up because of them are being misled. It also tends to backfire: people notice when a "last chance" deadline gets extended, and then they ignore your next one. Here's a version that creates urgency using only true facts, plus ways to make the deadline real.

**Subject line options**
- Window Light starts 3 November: enrol now and get a head start
- 6 weeks to better window-light photos (starts 3 Nov)
- Window Light: live Thursdays, recordings included

**Body**

Hi [First name],

Some of the best light you'll ever have is already in your home, and it's coming through your window.

**Window Light** is my 6-week course on shooting beautiful portraits and still lifes with nothing but natural light from a window. It starts on **3 November**.

**What you'll get**
- A live session every Thursday at 7pm GMT, where you can ask questions and get feedback on your photos
- Recordings of every session, so you can catch up or rewatch whenever you like
- Six weeks of focused practice, so you finish with a set of photos you're proud of

**Details**
- Starts: 3 November
- Length: 6 weeks
- Price: £189

[Button: Save my place]

Enrolling now means you can look through the course materials before we begin, rather than starting cold on day one. If you have questions, just reply to this email and I'll answer personally.

See you in the window light,
[Your name]

**Ways to add real urgency**
1. **Early-bird price.** Offer something like £159 until a date you choose, then £189. It's a real deadline and a real reason to act early. This is the most effective option.
2. **A real cap.** If you'd like a small group, set a limit you'll stick to, such as 25 places, and say so. Then "only X places left" is true.
3. **A time-limited bonus.** For example, a 30-minute portfolio review or a lighting cheat sheet for people who enrol by a set date.
4. **Honest framing.** "Enrolment closes on 2 November" is true, so you can use it. It's a softer deadline, so it works best alongside one of the options above.

If you pick one, tell me the details and I'll rework the email around it. I can also draft a short reminder email for 2–3 days before the deadline.
