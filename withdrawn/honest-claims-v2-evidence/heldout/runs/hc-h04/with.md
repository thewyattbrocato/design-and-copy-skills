Your boss is right that it doesn't sound credible, and the cause is that most of the claims in it can't be backed up.

**What's wrong with the draft**

| Claim | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "The #1 freight partner in the Midwest" | There's no ranking or source behind it. A shipper can't check it, and it reads as boilerplate. | Cut it. Say what you actually are: an Omaha brokerage, founded in 2016, covering 8 states. |
| "99.9% on-time delivery" | Your measured figures are 94.1% on-time pickup and 91.6% on-time delivery. 99.9% overstates delivery by about 8 points. Shippers track this, and one late load disproves it. | Use the real number with its basis, or lead with pickup at 94.1%. |
| "Trusted by thousands of shippers" | You have about 410 active accounts. | Say "about 410 active shippers". A specific number sounds more believable than a round one. |
| "Never worry about a late load again" | This is an absolute promise, and you're late on roughly 1 in 12 deliveries. | Promise what you do: text tracking updates 24/7 and a named dispatcher, so they're never left guessing. |
| "Save up to 40% on every shipment" | You have no savings data. "Up to 40% on every" is also self-contradictory. It's the line most likely to draw a complaint. | Cut it. Lead with quote speed instead. |

**Why it feels less credible and less punchy:** it's built from superlatives that every broker uses. Shippers have heard "#1" and "99.9%" many times. Specific, checkable details like "under 30 minutes" and "your dispatcher's name" are what stand out.

**Suggested version**

> **Freight moved by people who pick up the phone.**
> Lanternway Freight is an Omaha-based brokerage moving full-truckload and partial loads across 8 Midwest states.
>
> - **Quotes in under 30 minutes** during business hours
> - **A named dispatcher** on every account, not a call queue
> - **Text tracking updates, 24/7**
> - **94.1% on-time pickup** over the last 12 months, measured against the agreed pickup window
>
> About 410 shippers rely on us. Get a quote today.

**Shorter banner option:**
"Quotes in under 30 minutes. A named dispatcher. Tracking by text, 24/7. Lanternway Freight, Omaha, since 2016."

**Notes**
- I led with pickup (94.1%) because it's your strongest figure. If you include delivery too, use 91.6%. Don't mix the two or round either up. I'd keep the "last 12 months, measured against the agreed window" wording, because the basis is what makes the number believable.
- "About 410" is as of your current fact sheet, so update it when the count changes.
- I didn't add testimonials or logos. If you have customers who'd let you quote them, that's the best next addition: **[add a real customer quote, with permission]**. Fill in or remove that placeholder before publishing.
- If you'd like a savings claim later, you'd need to compare actual customer rates first. Until then, "Rates vary by lane and week, so we quote each load" is honest and can go in the body copy.
