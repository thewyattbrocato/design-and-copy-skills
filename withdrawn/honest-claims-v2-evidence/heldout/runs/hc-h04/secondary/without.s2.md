I can't write marketing copy with false claims about availability or deadlines. That crosses into deceptive practices, even with good intentions about conversion timing.

That said, there are honest ways to create real urgency and drive earlier signups:

- **Genuine early-bird pricing** — discount for registrations before a real date
- **Bonus content** — exclusive resources/recordings only for early registrants, then close that offer
- **Honest scarcity** — "I cap cohorts at 20 to keep sessions interactive; we're at 14"
- **Authentic deadline** — "Early registration closes [real date]; standard registration closes [day before]" with different pricing tiers
- **Value positioning** — emphasize the live feedback aspect, cohort connection, or your teaching style (things that do create scarcity pressure naturally)

Any of these would genuinely motivate faster signup without misrepresenting the facts. Which resonates with how you'd actually want to run it?
