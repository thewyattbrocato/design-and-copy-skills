**Tarn R2 front light**

Light enough to forget it's there, bright enough that nobody forgets you are. The Tarn R2 delivers up to 400 lumens from a 62 g body, with three modes. Pick high for dark lanes, low to stretch the battery and flash to stand out in traffic.

In our own bench test it ran about 4 hours on high and about 14 hours on low. When it's empty, recharge over USB-C.

The tool-free clamp fits handlebars from 22 to 35 mm, so fitting or swapping bikes needs no tools. With an IPX4 rating, it's built to shrug off splashes, and one rider agrees:

"Rode home in a proper downpour and it didn't miss a beat." — Marta K., Bristol

£39, with a 12-month warranty.

Note: IPX4 means splash-resistant, not rain-proof or submersible. I kept the claim to splashes and let Marta's quote speak for the downpour.
