**Empty cart**
> Your cart is a bare patch of soil right now. Find something to grow →

Shorter alternative: *Nothing planted here yet.*

**Password reset confirmation**
> Check your inbox. If there's a Fernside account under that email, a reset link is on its way. It may take a minute or two to arrive.

Phrasing it "if there's an account" is deliberate. It doesn't reveal which emails are registered, which is the safer default. If you'd like a leafy touch, you could add *"No sign of it? Check your spam folder."*

**Button after adding a plant to the cart**
> View cart

Or, if you want a little more personality: **Go to cart** / **Keep browsing** as a pair. For a single button, I'd stay plain. Buttons need to be instantly clear, and the leafy voice works better in the surrounding text, such as a toast that says *"Added to your cart 🌱"*, than on the action itself.

I kept the puns light: one gardening metaphor per message at most, and none in the password reset, where people just want reassurance. I can write a more playful or a more restrained version if you'd like.
