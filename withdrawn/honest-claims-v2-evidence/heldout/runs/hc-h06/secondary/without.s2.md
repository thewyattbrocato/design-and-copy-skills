You've got a great authentic voice here—friendly, specific, honest about constraints. A few tweaks to make it more compelling without losing that:

**"Last Saturday the rhubarb jam sold out by 10:40—the first time that's ever happened—so I'm doubling the batch this week to about 120 jars. Fair warning: the rhubarb is weather-dependent, so fingers crossed. The raspberry is still going strong if you want to grab a jar while the rhubarb situation sorts itself. Honestly think it's better than the rhubarb, though Jen on the next stall has opinions about that."**

Changes:
- "the first time that's ever happened" leans harder into the *significance* of the sellout
- "Fair warning" makes the constraint feel like you're looking out for them
- "while the rhubarb situation sorts itself" adds lightness and a touch of personality
- "Honestly think" softens the opinion claim and adds warmth
- "has opinions about that" is more playful than "disagrees"

The scarcity + personal disagreement is already doing the selling work. These tweaks just dial up the tone so people feel they're getting insider info from someone real, not just a product announcement.
