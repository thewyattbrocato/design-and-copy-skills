## How it works

1. **Set up your clients.** Save a rate card for each one: their per-word, hourly or project rates, all in one place.
2. **Add the job.** Import the word count from your translation tool's CSV export, or enter hours or a flat fee.
3. **Send the invoice.** Choose English, German, Spanish or French, and send. If it goes overdue, Wordtab sends the reminder so you don't have to.

---

## Features

**Bill the way the job works.** Charge per word, per hour or per project. You can mix all three across clients, or within one client.

**A rate card for every client.** Agencies and direct clients rarely pay the same. Save each client's rates once and stop looking them up.

**Import word counts from CSV.** Take the CSV export from the usual translation tools and bring the counts straight in. No retyping, no transposed digits.

**Invoices in four languages.** Templates come in English, German, Spanish and French, so your invoice matches your client's language.

**Overdue reminders.** Wordtab sends reminders for overdue invoices, so you aren't the one writing "just a gentle nudge" again.

**Your data stays yours.** Export everything as CSV at any time.
