## How it works

**1. Set up your clients.** Save a rate card for each one, with per-word, hourly, and project rates ready to go.

**2. Import the job.** Drop in the CSV export from your translation tool and Wordtab pulls in the word counts. No retyping.

**3. Send and get paid.** Choose a template in English, German, Spanish, or French and send. If an invoice goes overdue, Wordtab sends the reminder for you.

## Features

- **Three ways to bill.** Per word, per hour, or per project, and you can mix them across clients.
