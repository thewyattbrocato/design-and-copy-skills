## HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to your first invoice:

1. **Add your clients** — Set up a rate card for each one. Bill per word, per hour, or per project. Your rates stick.

2. **Import your work** — Paste word counts straight from CAT tool exports (Trados, memoQ, Wordfast, DejaVu, you name it). Or enter hours manually.

3. **Send & remind** — Generate an invoice in seconds. Wordtab sends automatic reminders if payment's overdue. You focus on translation.
