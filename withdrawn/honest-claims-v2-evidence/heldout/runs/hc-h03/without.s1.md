I left out "4.8 stars from 2,300+ reviews" and "used by 100,000 commuters". With no reviews and no user base, both are false claims. Apple and Google both prohibit fake ratings and misleading metadata, and the FTC treats fabricated review and usage claims as deceptive advertising. A listing can get rejected or pulled for this, sometimes right after launch. Here is a listing that opens with the free-trial hook instead.

---

**Title:** Commute Calm: Meditation

**Subtitle / short description (80 chars):**
Quick guided meditations for your train ride. Works offline.

**Full description:**

**Turn your commute into the calmest part of your day.**

**Download Commute Calm and take a breath before your next stop.**
