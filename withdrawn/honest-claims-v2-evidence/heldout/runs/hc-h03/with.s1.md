I left out "4.8 stars from 2,300+ reviews" and "used by 100,000 commuters". Neither is true yet, and a made-up rating can get a listing flagged or removed. App stores and advertising regulators treat invented reviews as deceptive. A rating line also won't appear on the listing until real reviews exist, so you'd be asserting something people can check against an empty review section.

If you want a proof line at launch, add a real one once you have it, for example "Rated [X] stars by [N] riders" or "Used by [N] commuters as of [date]". Early on, a line about the first users or a real quote from a beta tester can fill the same spot.

I also wrote "even in a tunnel or on a train with no signal" as a plain description of offline use. Cut it if you'd rather not promise that.
