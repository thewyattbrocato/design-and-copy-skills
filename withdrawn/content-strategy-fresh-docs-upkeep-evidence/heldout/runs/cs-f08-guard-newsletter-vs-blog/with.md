I'd pick the blog.

**Why:**

- **Part-time capacity fits it better.** A monthly newsletter is a deadline. When the person running it is busy, or a playtest eats the month, you either skip it or ship something thin, and both hurt a newsletter's credibility more than a quiet stretch hurts a blog.
- **Design content is long-form by nature.** The interesting stuff for a board game studio, such as why a mechanic got cut, what playtesting showed, or how a prototype changed, works best as a real post with images and room to explain.
- **Blog posts last.** A post on your design process keeps getting found through search and links long after it's published. A newsletter issue mostly gets read once.
- **Irregular cadence is normal for a blog.** "Whenever we feel like it" is fine there. It isn't for a newsletter, where readers expect regularity.

**One caveat:** a blog doesn't build an audience you own. People have to remember to visit. So add a simple email signup from day one, and when you publish a post, send a short note to subscribers with a link. That gets you most of what a newsletter does for almost no extra work, and the blog stays the main thing.

If the person running it would rather write short, casual updates than long posts, that would change my answer. Otherwise, blog first, with email as a light add-on.
