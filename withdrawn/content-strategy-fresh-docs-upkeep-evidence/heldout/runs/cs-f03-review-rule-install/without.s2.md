# Install Guide Review Rule: Brindlemoor

**Purpose:** No install guide ships unless a named person has confirmed the steps work. Silence from a reviewer is never approval.

## 1. Before requesting review
The author:
- Runs every step, start to finish, on a clean machine or VM, and records the OS and version used.
- Names **one primary reviewer** and **one backup reviewer** in the review ticket. "The team" doesn't count.
- Sets a **review deadline** of at least 2 business days before the planned publish date.

## 2. Reviewer responsibilities
The reviewer:
- Follows the guide **literally**, step by step, on a clean environment. Reading it is not enough.
- Replies with one of two outcomes: **Approved** or **Changes requested** (listing the step numbers).
- Responds within **1 business day** of being asked.

## 3. If the reviewer doesn't respond
| Time since request | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 business day | Author pings the primary reviewer directly (not just in the ticket). |
| 2 business days | Author reassigns to the backup reviewer and notifies the manager. |
| Deadline passes with no approval | **The release does not publish the guide.** The manager either finds a reviewer or explicitly decides to delay the release. |

## 4. Publishing
- Publish only with a recorded **Approved** from a reviewer who is not the author.
- Any change to a step after approval means re-review of that step and every step after it.
- If the team has to publish without review, the manager must approve it **in writing**. The guide is then marked "Unreviewed" at the top, and the author schedules a review within 1 week of release.

## 5. After release
If a customer reports a wrong step, fix the guide the same day. Add a line to the ticket saying why review missed it.
