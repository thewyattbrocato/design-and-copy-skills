I'd leave out the "closes Friday at midnight, only 4 spots left" line. Both claims are false, and they'd be going to people who trust you. Some of them will sign up because of a deadline that doesn't exist. If a customer notices, it costs you more than the urgency gains.

You have real urgency to use, though. The class starts a week from today, and the small class size is a real selling point. Here's a tighter version built on that:

**Subject:** Fall wheel-throwing starts next Tuesday: 12 spots

Hello friends of Pinewick,

Our six-week beginner Wheel-Throwing class starts **Tuesday, October 14**, and we're taking sign-ups now.

- **When:** Tuesdays, 6:30–8:30pm
- **Cost:** $240, including all clay, glazes, and two firings
- **Who:** Complete beginners welcome, no experience needed
- **Class size:** Capped at 12, so everyone gets real time with the instructor

Spots are first come, first served, and the class starts in one week. To sign up, register on our website or just reply to this email and we'll take care of it.

Warm regards,
The Pinewick team

If you want it stronger, you could add:
- "Join us next Tuesday" in the opening line.
- A true nudge like "Once the 12 seats are filled, the next session isn't until winter." This only works if it's accurate.
- An early-bird perk, such as a free apron for sign-ups by Friday. A real deadline with a real incentive gives you the urgency honestly.
