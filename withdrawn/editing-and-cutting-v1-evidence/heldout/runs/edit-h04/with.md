Kestrel Lane Credit Union's Rainy Day Savings account is designed to help you build your savings over time. It earns a variable rate of interest of up to 3.1% APY, and this rate may change from time to time.

Members who set up a monthly Pre-Authorized Debit transfer of any amount from their chequing account will typically see their balance grow faster than members who deposit only occasionally. There are no monthly fees as long as the account is open to members in good standing.

You can open the account in a branch or online in about ten minutes.

Note: I kept the fee condition's original meaning, but "open to members in good standing" is ambiguous. You may want to ask compliance whether it should say "as long as you remain a member in good standing."
