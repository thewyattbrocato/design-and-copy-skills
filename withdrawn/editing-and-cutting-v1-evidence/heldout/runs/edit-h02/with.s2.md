Hollis Park Physio was founded in 2014 by physiotherapists Rhea Okafor and Tom Lindqvist, who believed every patient deserves care personalized to their needs and goals. We've grown from one treatment room to four, but the attentive, one-on-one care we started with hasn't changed. We specialize in running injuries and rehabilitation after surgery, and we take the time to listen, to understand not just what hurts but what you want to get back to doing.

You'll find us on the second floor above the Saturday farmers market on Alder Street. We offer evening appointments until 8pm on Tuesdays and Thursdays for people who work during the day. Your first visit is a full 60 minutes, giving us time to talk, assess, and start building a plan together. You don't need a referral from your doctor, so if you're ready to get moving again, we'd love to hear from you.
