I left out "award winning" because you haven't won any awards. It's a false claim, and the FTC treats false advertising claims as deceptive even when they sit in alt text. Alt text is also meant to describe the image for screen-reader users, and stuffing it with keywords hurts that. Search engines increasingly discount keyword stuffing too.

If your teas are certified organic, add "organic" to the relevant lines. Don't add it if they aren't certified, because "organic" is a regulated term. "Best" is generally treated as harmless puffery, but it adds little for ranking.
