## Notes on the wording

- **The cause is stated plainly and once.** It's just "a storm closed the hub," with no apology spiral and no blame on the courier.
- **The courier request is worded as a request.** "We've asked them to move our packages first" is accurate. I'd avoid "we'll be first in line" unless the courier has confirmed it.
- **The email offers a way out without promising a specific remedy.** If you're willing to offer something concrete, like a cancellation, a discount code, or free shipping on the next order, add it. It would make the email feel more generous, but that's your call.
- **I didn't add plant-care advice.** If these are live plants, you might add a line such as "Open the box as soon as it arrives" or "Plants travel well for a few extra days." Tell me your actual guidance and I'll work it in.
