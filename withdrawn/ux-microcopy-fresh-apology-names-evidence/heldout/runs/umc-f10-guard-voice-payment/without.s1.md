**Screen: Payment Declined (Annual Plan Renewal)**

**Headline:** Your card said "not today."

**Message:** We tried to renew your annual plan, but your card was declined. Your budget's still safe and sound, but it'll sulk if we leave it waiting too long. Update your payment details or try another card to keep things rolling.

**Button:** Update payment method

---

A few notes on the choices:

- **Headline** keeps the wordplay light and personifies the card, like your wallet-on-holiday line, without blaming the user.
- **Message** states what happened (renewal, card declined), reassures them, nods to the houseplant "sulk" voice, and ends with the action.
- **Button** is a clear verb phrase so the next step is obvious. The cheek lives in the copy around it, not the button.

If you'd like a shorter version for a toast or banner: *"Your card declined our renewal. Update your payment method to keep your budget happy."*
