---

A few notes on the choices:
- **Headline is plain.** It states the problem before the jokes start, so nobody has to decode it.
- **Wordplay stays in the body.** "Said no thanks" and "keep your budgets in bloom" echo your houseplant sample without copying it.
- **The button says the action.** "Update payment method" tells people what happens next. A quirky label like "Fix it!" would be cuter but vaguer.
- **One claim to check:** "Your plan is still safe for now" assumes you have a grace period. If renewal failure cuts access right away, swap that line for something like "We'll pause your plan until this is sorted."
