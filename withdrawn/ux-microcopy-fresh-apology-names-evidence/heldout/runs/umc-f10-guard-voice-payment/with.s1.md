**Renewal declined screen**

**Title:** We couldn't renew your annual plan

**Message:** Your card was declined, so nothing was charged. Add a new card or try another one to keep your plan going. Your plan stays active until [end date].

**Primary button:** Update payment method

**Secondary button:** Not now

Notes:
- I left out the jokes on purpose. Declined payments are about money, and a quip can read as brushing off a worry. The voice is still there in the warm, plain, human wording, and the playful lines suit low-stakes screens.
- I don't know what happens to the plan after the renewal fails, so [end date] is a placeholder. Fill in the real date, or cut that sentence if access ends right away. Don't leave it vague.
