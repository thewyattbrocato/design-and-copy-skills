**Renewal declined screen**

**Title:** Your renewal didn't go through

**Message:** Your card was declined, so we couldn't renew your annual plan. [Your plan stays active until DATE.] Try a different card, or check with your bank if this seems wrong.

**Primary button:** Update payment method
**Secondary button:** Not now

- **Voice:** I left out the wordplay on purpose. Jokes in billing errors tend to read as brushing off a money problem. The warmth comes from the plain, no-blame wording ("your card was declined", not "you entered…"). Your playful voice can come back on the success screen after they renew.
- **Bracketed line:** I don't know what happens to their access or when. Fill in the real date, or replace the line with what actually happens (for example, that they lose access). I also didn't claim "nothing was charged" or give a retry time, since I couldn't verify either. Add them if they're true.
