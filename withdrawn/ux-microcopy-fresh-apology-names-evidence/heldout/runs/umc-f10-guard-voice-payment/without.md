**Title:** Your card said "not today"

**Message:** We tried to renew your annual plan, but your card was declined. Your budget's still safe and sound, but it'll go quiet on the sidelines until the payment goes through. Update your card details or try a different one, and we'll get you back in the game.

**Button:** Update payment method

---

**Why it works:**
- **What happened:** The title and first sentence say plainly that the renewal failed because the card was declined.
- **What to do:** The message and button both point to updating the card.
- **Voice:** The title and the "sidelines" line keep the cheeky tone. The reassurance that nothing is lost keeps it kind, since a declined payment is already a little stressful.

**Shorter variant** (for a toast or banner):
"Renewal hit a snag: your card was declined. Update your payment method to keep your budget humming."

Button: **Fix payment**

If you'd like it a bit cheekier, here's another title option: *"Your card ghosted us."* I'd keep the body text plain, though, so the instructions stay easy to follow.
