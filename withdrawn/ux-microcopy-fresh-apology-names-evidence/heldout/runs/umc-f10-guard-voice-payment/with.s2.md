**Renewal declined screen**

**Title:** Your card was declined

**Message:** We couldn't renew your annual plan because your card was declined. Your plan hasn't renewed yet. Add a different card or update this one, and we'll try again. If it keeps failing, your bank can tell you why. [Add what happens to access and by when.]

**Primary button:** Update card

**Secondary button:** Try again

**Help link:** Contact support

- I left out the wordplay on purpose. Billing failures are a moment where a joke can read as brushing off a real problem. The voice stays in the warm, plain wording, and you can use the playful lines on lower-stakes screens.
- The bracket is there because I don't know whether access continues, or for how long, after a failed renewal. Fill it with the real policy. Don't ship it vague.
