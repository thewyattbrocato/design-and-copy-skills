Notes for you:

- **Offers:** The only commitments in the message are the reprint and the rush shipping. I left a marked line for a refund or other gesture. I'd decide that before sending, because customers who read "we're still figuring out what else we can do" may feel it's one more thing they have to follow up on. A full refund, whatever they choose to do, is the simplest and kindest option.
- **Personalizing:** If the phone call already covered some details, add a first line like "Thank you for speaking with us on [day]." Use the name of the person who died only if your order data has it and you're sure it's right.
- **Contact:** Give them a named person and a direct line, not a generic support address.
- **Deadlines:** For any order where the event date has already passed, the "deadline" paragraph may sting. You may want a variant that says "If the date has already passed, we'd still like to make this book for you."
