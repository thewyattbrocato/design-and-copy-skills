**Notes for you:**

- **Order page version:** Use the same text with a shorter subject line. Put the upload link and the phone number at the top, because people will land there looking for the next step.
- **Personal touches:** Fill in the occasion where you know it, and make sure the signer is a real person who will pick up the phone. For anyone you reached by phone, add a line referring to that call.
- **The open question:** I left out any further offer, such as a refund or a free copy. If you decide on something, add it as its own short paragraph after the reprint details. Don't hint at it before it's decided. If you're considering a refund, I'd lean toward offering it unprompted rather than waiting for people to ask. It's also easier to add something later than to take it back.
- **Missing originals:** Some customers may not have their originals anymore. Check whether your team can offer anything for those cases before the message goes out, since that's the question you're most likely to get.
