**Notes on choices and open items**

- **Say what you'll do, not what you might do.** I left out the extra offer rather than hint at one. If you don't decide before sending, it's better to send without it than to write "we hope to make this up to you." You can follow up later with a refund or credit, and that's received better as a second message than as a vague promise now.
- **Deadlines matter most.** Some of the 7 may have a service or anniversary date that has passed or is close. Consider a slightly different version for those customers, with the date acknowledged and your best realistic delivery estimate. I can draft that.
- **Include a real person's name and direct line.** That does more than any wording.
- **Check that the re-upload really works for grieving customers.** If the originals were on a phone or a relative's account, offering to take them by email or help in person will matter.
- Since you've already phoned some of them, you may want to add a line such as "As we discussed by phone," for those customers.
