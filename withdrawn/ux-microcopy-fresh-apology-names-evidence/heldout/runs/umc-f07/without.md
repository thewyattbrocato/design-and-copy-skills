## Page titles

The format is: page-specific content first, then the service name, then the council name. This puts the part that differs between pages at the start, where screen reader users and people with many tabs open see it first.

| Page | Page title |
|---|---|
| Step 1 | Your address (step 1 of 3) – Resident parking permit – Eastbrook Borough Council |
| Step 2 | Your vehicle (step 2 of 3) – Resident parking permit – Eastbrook Borough Council |
| Step 3 | Upload proof of residence (step 3 of 3) – Resident parking permit – Eastbrook Borough Council |
| Check your answers | Check your answers – Resident parking permit – Eastbrook Borough Council |
| Confirmation | Application sent – Resident parking permit – Eastbrook Borough Council |

### When a step reloads with errors

Add "Error: " at the start of the title. The page reloads with an error summary, and the new title tells screen reader users that something went wrong.

- Error: Your address (step 1 of 3) – Resident parking permit – Eastbrook Borough Council
- Error: Your vehicle (step 2 of 3) – Resident parking permit – Eastbrook Borough Council
- Error: Upload proof of residence (step 3 of 3) – Resident parking permit – Eastbrook Borough Council

Remove the prefix once the error is fixed and the page loads normally. The fact sheet doesn't describe errors on the check-your-answers page, so I haven't given it an error title.

## Download link text

Each link names the document and then gives the file type, size and page count in brackets. Put all of it inside the link, so people know what they're downloading before they select it.

- [Permit zones map (PDF, 3.2 MB, 2 pages)]
- [Permit terms and conditions (Word document, 140 KB, 9 pages)]
