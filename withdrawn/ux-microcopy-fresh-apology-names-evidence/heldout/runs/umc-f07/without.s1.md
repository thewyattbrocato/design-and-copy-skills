## Page titles

Each title starts with the page-specific content, then the service name, then the council name. That puts the part that differs first, which helps people using screen readers or many open tabs.

| Page | Title |
|---|---|
| Step 1 | Step 1 of 3: Your address – Resident parking permit – Eastbrook Borough Council |
| Step 2 | Step 2 of 3: Your vehicle – Resident parking permit – Eastbrook Borough Council |
| Step 3 | Step 3 of 3: Upload proof of residence – Resident parking permit – Eastbrook Borough Council |
| Check your answers | Check your answers – Resident parking permit – Eastbrook Borough Council |
| Confirmation | Application sent – Resident parking permit – Eastbrook Borough Council |

**Error versions.** Steps 1–3 reload the same page with an error summary. Each of those titles gets an `Error: ` prefix, so screen reader users hear that something went wrong:

- Error: Step 1 of 3: Your address – Resident parking permit – Eastbrook Borough Council
- Error: Step 2 of 3: Your vehicle – Resident parking permit – Eastbrook Borough Council
- Error: Step 3 of 3: Upload proof of residence – Resident parking permit – Eastbrook Borough Council

Remove the prefix once the error is fixed and the page loads normally. Your fact sheet describes errors only on the three steps, so I haven't added error titles for "Check your answers" or "Application sent".

## Download link text

The link text names the document and gives its format, file size and page count. Users can then decide whether to download it before they click.

- [Permit zones map (PDF, 3.2 MB, 2 pages)]
- [Permit terms and conditions (Word document, 140 KB, 9 pages)]

The whole string, including the bracketed details, should be inside the link.
