**Welcome to Tidewater Food Pantry**

Thank you for volunteering. We've served the Tidewater area for over twenty years, and we depend on people like you. Everyone who comes through our door is a neighbor, not a case, and deserves good food and dignity. Keep that in mind whether you're stocking, packing, or greeting.

**Shifts and arriving**

Shifts are Tuesday and Thursday, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., and Saturday, 8 to 11 a.m. Arrive 15 minutes early to sign in, put your belongings away, and hear the shift lead's briefing. Clients are often already waiting, and doors open exactly at start time.

Use the side door. The front door is for clients. Sign in on the tablet just inside every time. We use it to track volunteer hours and to know who is in the building in an emergency.

If you can't make a shift, tell us at least 24 hours ahead: volunteers@tidewaterpantry.example or 555-0142. If you've had a fever, vomiting, or diarrhea in the past 48 hours, stay home.

**What to wear and hygiene**

Wear comfortable clothes that can get dusty. Closed-toe shoes are required, with no exceptions. Tie back long hair. We provide aprons and gloves. Wear disposable gloves when handling fresh produce or opening packages. Wash your hands with soap and warm water for at least 20 seconds when you arrive, after any break or restroom use, and when you change tasks.

**Food safety**

This is our most important responsibility. Ask your shift lead if anything is unclear.

- The walk-in cooler must stay at 41°F or below. The shift lead logs the temperature at the start of every shift. If it's above 41°F, take nothing out and put nothing in. Tell the shift lead right away, and don't try to fix it yourself.
- Don't shelve food that is open, home-canned, homemade, or past its "use by" date.
- Shelf-stable items may be distributed up to six months past their "best by" date if they're in perfect condition.
- Discard any can that is bulging, leaking, or dented on the seam. A small dent in the body, away from the seam, is fine.
- If you're unsure about an item, put it on the gray "Ask" cart for the shift lead to review.
- Peanut-free and gluten-free items have their own labeled shelf. Always return them there, and never mix them with other stock.

**Working with clients**

Never ask why a client needs food. Never photograph clients or the line outside. Don't share client information with anyone, including other volunteers outside the pantry. If a client seems upset, stay calm and kind, and get the shift lead. Otherwise, say hello, smile, and offer to carry bags to the car.

**Safety and emergencies**

The emergency exits are the side door and the loading dock door at the back. The fire extinguisher is on the wall by the kitchen. The first aid kit is on the shelf above the sign-in tablet. In an emergency, call 911 first, then tell the shift lead. Report any injury, however small, so it can go in the incident log.

**Checklist**

Arrive 15 minutes early. Sign in at the side door. Wear closed-toe shoes. Wash your hands. Keep the cooler at 41°F or below. Ask when in doubt. Give 24 hours' notice if you can't come.
