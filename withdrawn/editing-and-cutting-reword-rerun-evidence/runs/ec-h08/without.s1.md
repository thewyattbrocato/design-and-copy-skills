# Welcome to Tidewater Food Pantry

Thank you for volunteering! We've served the Tidewater area for over twenty years, and we couldn't do it without you. Everyone who walks through our door is a neighbor, not a case. Treat everyone with dignity, respect, and kindness.

## Shifts and arriving
- **Shifts:** Tuesday and Thursday, 9:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m.; Saturday, 8:00–11:00 a.m.
- **Arrive 15 minutes early** to sign in, put your things away, and hear the shift lead's briefing. Clients are often already waiting, and doors open exactly at start time.
- **Use the side door.** The front door is for clients. Sign in on the tablet just inside every time. It tracks volunteer hours and shows who is in the building in an emergency.
- **Can't make a shift?** Give at least 24 hours' notice: volunteers@tidewaterpantry.example or 555-0142.
- **Stay home if sick.** Don't volunteer if you've had a fever, vomiting, or diarrhea in the past 48 hours.

## What to wear and hygiene
- Wear comfortable clothes you don't mind getting dusty. **Closed-toe shoes are required, no exceptions.** Tie back long hair.
- Aprons and gloves are provided. Wear disposable gloves when handling fresh produce or opening packages.
- Wash hands with soap and warm water for at least 20 seconds on arrival, after breaks, after using the restroom, and when changing tasks.

## Food safety
This is our most important responsibility. If anything is unclear, ask your shift lead.

- **Cooler:** It must stay at **41°F or below**. The shift lead logs the temperature at the start of each shift. If it's ever above 41°F, don't take food out or put any in. Tell the shift lead right away, and don't try to fix it yourself.
- **Never shelve** food that is open, home-canned or homemade, or past its "use by" date.
- **"Best by" items:** Shelf-stable items can be distributed up to six months past the date if in perfect condition.
- **Cans:** Discard any that are bulging, leaking, or dented on the seam. A small dent in the body, away from the seam, is fine.
- **Not sure?** Put the item on the gray cart marked "Ask" for the shift lead to review.
- **Allergies:** Peanut-free and gluten-free items have their own labeled shelf. Always return them there and never mix them with other stock.

## Working with clients
- Never ask why someone needs food.
- Never photograph clients or the line outside.
- Don't share client information with anyone, including other volunteers outside the pantry.
- If a client is upset, stay calm and kind, and get the shift lead.
- Otherwise, say hello, smile, and offer to carry bags to the car.

## Safety and emergencies
- **Emergency exits:** the side door and the loading dock door at the back.
- **Fire extinguisher:** on the wall by the kitchen. **First aid kit:** on the shelf above the sign-in tablet.
- **In an emergency,** call 911 first, then tell the shift lead.
- **Report every injury,** however small, to the shift lead for the incident log.

Thank you for joining the Tidewater family. We can't wait to meet you!
