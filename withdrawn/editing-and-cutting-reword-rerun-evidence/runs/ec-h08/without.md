# Welcome to Tidewater Food Pantry

Thank you for volunteering! We've served neighbors in the Tidewater area for over 20 years, and we couldn't do it without you. We treat everyone who comes through our door with dignity, as a neighbor, not a case. Keep that in mind in everything you do.

## Shifts and arriving
- **Shifts:** Tuesday and Thursday, 9:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m.; Saturday, 8:00–11:00 a.m.
- Arrive **15 minutes early** to sign in, store your belongings, and hear the shift lead's briefing. Clients are often already waiting, and doors open exactly at shift start.
- Use the **side door**. The front door is for clients.
- **Sign in on the tablet** just inside the side door every time. We use it to track hours and to know who's in the building in an emergency.
- **Can't make a shift?** Give at least 24 hours' notice if you can: volunteers@tidewaterpantry.example or 555-0142.
- **Stay home if you're ill.** Don't volunteer if you've had a fever, vomiting, or diarrhea in the past 48 hours.

## What to wear and hygiene
- Wear comfortable clothes you don't mind getting dusty. We lift and move a lot of boxes.
- **Closed-toe shoes are required, no exceptions.** Tie back long hair.
- We provide aprons and gloves. Wear disposable gloves when handling fresh produce or opening packages.
- Wash your hands with soap and warm water for at least 20 seconds. Do this when you arrive, after breaks, after using the restroom, and whenever you change tasks.

## Food safety
This is our most important responsibility. If anything is unclear, ask your shift lead.

- **Cooler:** It must stay at **41°F or below**. The shift lead logs the temperature at the start of each shift. If it's above 41°F, don't remove or add any food. Tell the shift lead right away, and don't try to fix it yourself.
- **Never shelve** food that is open, home-canned or homemade, or past its "use by" date.
- **"Best by" items:** Shelf-stable items can be distributed up to six months past the date if in perfect condition.
- **Cans:** Discard any that are bulging, leaking, or dented on the seam. A small dent in the body, away from the seam, is fine.
- **Unsure?** Put the item on the gray cart marked "Ask" for the shift lead to review.
- **Allergies:** Peanut-free and gluten-free items have their own labeled shelf. Always return them there, and never mix them with other stock.

## Working with clients
- Never ask why someone needs food.
- Never photograph clients or the line outside.
- Don't share any client information with anyone, including other volunteers outside the pantry.
- If a client is upset, stay calm and kind, and get the shift lead.
- Say hello, smile, and offer to carry bags to the car.

## Safety and emergencies
- **Emergency exits:** the side door and the loading dock door at the back.
- **Fire extinguisher:** on the wall by the kitchen.
- **First aid kit:** on the shelf above the sign-in tablet.
- In an emergency, **call 911 first**, then tell the shift lead.
- Report every injury, however small, to the shift lead for the incident log.

## Quick checklist
Arrive early. Sign in at the side door. Wear closed-toe shoes. Wash your hands. Keep the cooler at 41°F or below. Ask when in doubt. Give 24 hours' notice if you can't come.

Thank you for joining the Tidewater family. We can't wait to meet you!
