**Welcome to Tidewater Food Pantry**

Thank you for volunteering. We have served the Tidewater area for over twenty years, and we depend on people like you. Everyone who comes through our door is a neighbor, not a case, and deserves good food and to be treated with dignity.

**Shifts and arriving**

- Shifts are Tuesday and Thursday, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., and Saturday, 8 to 11 a.m.
- Arrive 15 minutes early to sign in, put your things away, and hear the shift lead's briefing. Clients are often already waiting, and doors open exactly at start time.
- Use the side door. The front door is for clients.
- Sign in on the tablet inside the side door every time. It tracks volunteer hours and shows who is in the building in an emergency.
- If you can't make a shift, tell us at least 24 hours ahead at volunteers@tidewaterpantry.example or 555-0142.
- If you've had a fever, vomiting or diarrhea in the past 48 hours, stay home.

**What to wear and hygiene**

- Wear comfortable clothes that can get dusty. We lift and move a lot of boxes.
- Closed-toe shoes are required, with no exceptions.
- Tie back long hair.
- We provide aprons and gloves. Wear disposable gloves when handling fresh produce or opening packages.
- Wash with soap and warm water for at least 20 seconds when you arrive, after any break, after using the restroom, and whenever you change tasks.

**Food safety**

This is our most important responsibility. If anything is unclear, ask your shift lead.

- The walk-in cooler must stay at 41°F or below. The shift lead logs the temperature at the start of every shift. If it's above 41°F, don't take food out or put any in. Tell the shift lead right away, and don't try to fix the cooler yourself.
- Never shelve food that is open, home-canned, homemade, or past its "use by" date.
- Shelf-stable food can go out up to six months past its "best by" date if it is in perfect condition.
- Discard any can that is bulging, leaking, or dented on the seam. A small dent in the body, away from the seam, is fine.
- If you're unsure about an item, put it on the gray cart marked "Ask" for the shift lead to review.
- Keep peanut-free and gluten-free items on their separate labeled shelf. Never mix them with other stock.

**Working with clients**

- Never ask why someone needs food.
- Never photograph clients or the line outside.
- Don't share any client information with anyone, including other volunteers outside the pantry.
- If a client seems upset, stay calm and kind, and get the shift lead.
- Otherwise, say hello, smile, and offer to carry bags to the car.

**Safety and emergencies**

- The emergency exits are the side door and the loading dock door at the back.
- The fire extinguisher is on the wall by the kitchen. The first aid kit is on the shelf above the sign-in tablet.
- In an emergency, call 911 first, then tell the shift lead.
- Report every injury, however small, to the shift lead for the incident log.

**Quick checklist**

Arrive 15 minutes early. Sign in at the side door. Wear closed-toe shoes. Wash your hands. Keep the cooler at 41°F or below. Ask when in doubt. Give 24 hours' notice if you can't come.

Thank you for being part of the Tidewater family. We look forward to meeting you.
