I didn't put "94%" in. It would be a made-up number on a public page, and that's a false advertising risk. Here's the tightened version with a placeholder for the stat:

**Tightened hero**

> **Local pet sitters, booked in minutes.**
> Walks, drop-ins, and weekend stays with ID-checked, interviewed sitters. Message them directly and get photo updates every visit.

**With the stat, once you have it**

> **Local pet sitters, booked in minutes.**
> Walks, drop-ins, and weekend stays with ID-checked, interviewed sitters. Message them directly and get photo updates every visit. **[X]% of owners rebook within a month.**

I cut the "connects busy pet owners" opener because the headline covers it. I also folded the vetting into "ID-checked, interviewed sitters" so it doesn't need its own sentence.

You can pull the real rebook number from your booking data. It's usually a simple query: owners with a second booking within 30 days of their first, divided by owners with a first booking. Until then, the line works without it, or you could use something you can already back up, like "Every sitter is ID-checked and interviewed."
