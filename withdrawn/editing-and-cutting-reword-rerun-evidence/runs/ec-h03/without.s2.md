Nice note, warm and clear. A few things before you send:

**Fix**
- "recieve" → **receive**

**Worth a look**
- "You recieve boxes; we get to keep farming." This is the one line that reads a little off. I think you mean something like "You *receive boxes*; we *get to keep farming*," but the contrast is muddy and it can read as though members are getting something and you're getting something unrelated. If you want it clearer: "You take home a box; we get to keep farming." Or keep yours as is if it sounds like you.
- "Daniel's potatoes from the farm down the road." Fine, but members might wonder who Daniel is. "…from Daniel's farm down the road" is a touch smoother.
- "Cracked ones are the best, don't throw them out, just eat them first." Comma splice, but it works in a casual voice. I'd leave it.

**Optional**
- If anyone has allergies or wants to know how to store heirlooms, a one-liner ("keep tomatoes on the counter, not the fridge") would be a nice touch. Skip it if you want to keep it short.

Everything else I'd leave alone. The tomato-muttering and the crying over snap peas are what make it sound like you.
