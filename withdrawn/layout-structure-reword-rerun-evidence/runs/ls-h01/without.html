About

Marrow & Pine is a small architecture studio working across Oregon and Washington. We take on a handful of public and community projects at a time, so the people who start a building are the people who see it finished. We spend time with the teachers, librarians and neighbors who will use each place, and we build slowly, with durable materials and plenty of daylight.
