About

Marrow & Pine is a small architecture studio working across Oregon and Washington. We take on a limited number of public and civic commitments at a time, so the same people who draw the first sketch are on site when the building opens. We care about daylight, durable materials and rooms that people want to linger in, and we stay close to the communities we build for, from the first workshop to the final walkthrough.
