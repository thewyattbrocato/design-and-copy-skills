Yes, the system font stack plus the system monospace is fine for a laptop-reading developer audience, and you don't need to pay for a font. Use `system-ui` for prose and `ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas, monospace` for code, and spend any font budget on size, line height and code-block styling instead. If you want a distinct look later, a free open-licensed face like Inter or JetBrains Mono is the upgrade, still without paying.
