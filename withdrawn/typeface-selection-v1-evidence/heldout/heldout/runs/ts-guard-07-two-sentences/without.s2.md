A system font stack is fine for this audience: developers on laptops get crisp, well-hinted fonts like San Francisco, Segoe UI, and SF Mono or Consolas, and you get zero load time and no layout shift. A paid font is a branding choice, not a readability need, so if you want a distinct look, a free open-source option like Inter and JetBrains Mono (self-hosted) gets you most of the way without a license fee.
