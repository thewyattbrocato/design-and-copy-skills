Fernhollow Seed Library

Run by neighbours, for neighbours

Borrow a few seeds. Bring back a few more.

Fernhollow Seed Library is a volunteer-run collection of heirloom vegetable and flower seeds. Take home a packet, grow it in your own plot, and return seeds saved from what you grew, so the shelves keep filling up.

How borrowing works

  1. 1

    Choose your packets

    Come by during opening hours and browse the drawers. Our volunteers are happy to help you pick something that suits your soil and your sun.

  2. 2

    Sign the little ledger

    Write down what you took and your name. There are no fees and no cards, just a pencil and a bit of trust.

  3. 3

    Grow, save, and return

    Let a few of your best plants go to seed, dry them well, and bring them back in a labelled envelope. Even a small handful helps.
