Fernhollow Seed Library

Run by neighbours, for neighbours

Borrow a few seeds. Bring back a few more.

Fernhollow Seed Library is a volunteer-run collection of heirloom vegetable and flower seeds. Take home a packet, grow it in your own plot, and return seeds you save from the best of the crop. It's free, and everyone is welcome.

How borrowing works

  1. 1

    Choose your packets

    Come by during opening hours and browse the drawers. A volunteer will happily help you pick what suits your soil and space.

  2. 2

    Plant and tend them

    Take them home and grow them. Let a few of your healthiest plants go to seed at the end of the season.

  3. 3

    Return some seeds

    Dry your saved seeds, label them with the name and year, and bring them back so the next neighbour can borrow them.
