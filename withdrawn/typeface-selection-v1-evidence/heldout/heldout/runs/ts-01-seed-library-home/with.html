Fernhollow Seed Library

Run by neighbours, for neighbours

Borrow a few seeds. Grow something good. Bring some back.

We’re a small, volunteer-run library of heirloom vegetable and flower seeds, kept in drawers at the old grange hall. Take home a packet, plant it, and when your plants go to seed, return a handful from your own plot so the next neighbour can grow it too.

How borrowing works

  1. Pick your packetsCome by during opening hours and browse the drawers. Ask a volunteer if you’d like help choosing what suits your garden.
  2. Plant and tend themSow your seeds, look after them, and let a few of the best plants go all the way to seed.
  3. Save and returnDry your seeds, pop them in an envelope with the plant’s name, and bring them back to the library. No fees, no fuss.
