Fernhollow Seed Library

Borrow seeds. Grow food. Bring some back.

We're a small, volunteer-run library of heirloom vegetable and flower seeds, shared among neighbours. Take a few packets home, plant them, and return seeds saved from your own plot.

How borrowing works

  1. 1 Choose your packets Browse the drawers at the grange hall and pick a few varieties you'd like to grow. Our volunteers are happy to help.
  2. 2 Plant and tend them Take them home, sow them, and enjoy the season. Beginners are very welcome.
  3. 3 Save seeds and return some Let a few of your best plants go to seed, then bring a small envelope back so the next neighbour can borrow it.
