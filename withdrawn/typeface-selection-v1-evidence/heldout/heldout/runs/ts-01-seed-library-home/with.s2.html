Fernhollow Seed Library

Run by neighbours, for neighbours

Borrow seeds. Grow a garden. Bring some back.

We’re a small, volunteer-run library of heirloom vegetable and flower seeds. Borrow a few packets for your plot, and when the season turns, return seeds saved from what you grew.

No fees, no fuss. Beginners are very welcome.

How borrowing works

  1. Choose your packetsCome by and browse the drawers. A volunteer can help you pick what suits your soil and sun.
  2. Plant and tend themTake them home and grow them. Let a few of your best plants go to seed at the end of the season.
  3. Return some seedsBring back seeds you’ve saved, so the next neighbour can borrow them too.
