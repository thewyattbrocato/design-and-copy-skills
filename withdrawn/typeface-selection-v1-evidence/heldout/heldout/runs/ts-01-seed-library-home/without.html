Fernhollow Seed Library

Borrow a few seeds. Bring back some more.

We're a neighbourhood seed library, run by volunteers. Take home packets of heirloom vegetable and flower seeds, grow them in your own plot, and return seeds you've saved so the next gardener can start with them too.

How borrowing works

  1. 1

    Come and choose

    Drop by during opening hours and browse the drawers. Ask a volunteer if you're not sure what suits your soil.

  2. 2

    Take a packet home

    Sign the little notebook, take what you'll realistically plant, and enjoy growing it.

  3. 3

    Save seeds and return them

    When your plants have gone to seed, dry some, label them, and bring them back to the library.
