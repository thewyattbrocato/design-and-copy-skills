Use **Cormorant Garamond** (free on Google Fonts) in a light or regular weight, set large with a little letter-spacing, for the names. It's an elegant, high-contrast serif that reads as romantic without being a script. Pair it with a plain sans like Inter or Lato for the schedule and RSVP form, and make sure it supports the accent in "Inés" and "Tomás" (it does, but check it renders).
