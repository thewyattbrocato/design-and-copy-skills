## Revised guide (about 45 min)

**1. Intro (3 min).** Thank them. Say: "I'm learning how freelancers manage money. There are no right answers, and I'm not selling anything. Is it okay if I record for my notes? You can stop anytime."

**2. Background (5 min).**
- "Tell me about your work. What kinds of clients and projects do you have?"
- "How long have you freelanced?"

**3. Recent income story (10 min).**
- "Think about the last month where your income was higher or lower than you expected. Walk me through what happened."
- "What did you do when you noticed?"
- "Tell me more about that."

**4. Taxes (10 min).**
- "Tell me about the last time you paid taxes, or set money aside for them. Start from when you first thought about it."
- "How did you decide how much?"
- "What happened with the money in between?"
- "What, if anything, went differently than you wanted?"

**5. Current tools and habits (8 min).**
- "Show me, or describe, how you track money today. What do you open first?"
- "When did you last change how you do this, and what prompted it?"
- "What do you currently pay for to manage money or work? What have you cancelled?"

**6. Invoicing (6 min).**
- "Tell me about the last time you sent an invoice, from the moment the work finished."
- "What happened after you sent it?"
- "Tell me about a time a payment came late."

**7. Close (3 min).** Ask: "Is there something about managing money as a freelancer I didn't ask about that I should have?" and "Who else do you know who freelances and might talk to me?" Then thank them.

## Moderator notes
- Don't mention Ledgerleaf unless a later task test needs it. If they ask, say "I'll explain at the end."
- Use silence and "tell me more." Don't fill pauses with examples.
- If an answer is hypothetical ("I would probably…"), redirect with "When did you last do that?"
- Bring only 2 or 3 follow-up probes per section. Cut section 5 or 6 first if you run short.

- **Assumption:** This is a problem-discovery round, not a test of a built product. If you have a prototype, that needs a separate task-based session.
- **What you need to do:** Recruit people who did freelance work and handled their own taxes in the last year. A sample of only friends or fellow freelancers in your network will skew the results. Get recording consent. Report findings as "3 of 7," not percentages.
- **Pricing:** Interviews can't tell you what people will pay. They can show what people pay for today and what they've cancelled, which is better evidence than a stated price.
