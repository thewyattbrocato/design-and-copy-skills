What we heard

1. No visible way to reschedule (4)

Customers looked for a way to move their pickup day and found none. One noted there is no reschedule button after payment. One gave up, cancelled and rebooked.

"There's no reschedule button on the order after payment?"

2. Extra-bag fee is a surprise (2)

A $6 extra-bag fee appears at the end of checkout. Customers said they weren't told beforehand and that the total jumped.

"Nobody told me about that before I paid."

3. Late driver, no heads-up (1)

A driver arrived 40 minutes late without sending a text. This is an operations and notification issue more than an app bug.

4. Missing item and praise (1 each)

One missing sock in a delivery, handled as a normal service claim. One customer simply said they love the service.

Suggested next steps

  1. Add a reschedule option to the order screen, including after payment. Check first whether a cancel-and-rebook is the only path today.
  2. Show the extra-bag fee earlier, at booking and not only at the final total. Explain the bag allowance and the $6 charge up front.
  3. Send a delay text when a driver is running behind. Confirm whether one exists and why it didn't fire.
  4. Follow up on the missing sock through the normal claims process.

Based on 9 tickets from one week, so treat counts as directional. Ticket 7's cancel-and-rebook may also mean lost or duplicated orders, which is worth a look.
