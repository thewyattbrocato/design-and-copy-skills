What customers are saying

1. No way to reschedule Tickets 1, 2, 4, 7

Customers can't find a reschedule option. One says it's missing after payment. Another cancelled and rebooked instead, which is extra work for them and for us.

"There's no reschedule button on the order after payment?"

2. Extra-bag fee comes as a surprise Tickets 3, 6

A $6 extra-bag fee appears at the end of checkout. Customers say the total jumped and they weren't told beforehand.

"Nobody told me about that before I paid."

3. One-offs Tickets 5, 8, 9

One driver ran 40 minutes late without texting (5). One order was missing a sock (8). One customer sent thanks (9).

Suggested next steps

  1. Add a visible reschedule action to the order screen, including after payment. Check whether it exists today and is hard to find, or doesn't exist at all.
  2. Show the extra-bag fee before payment, with the bag allowance and the $6 rate on the booking and checkout screens.
  3. Check whether late-driver texts are being sent. Ticket 5 is a single report, so look at how often this happens before treating it as a pattern.
  4. Handle the missing sock through the normal lost-item process.

Based on 9 tickets from one week, so treat the counts as a signal rather than a measurement. Themes were assigned by reading each ticket's text.
