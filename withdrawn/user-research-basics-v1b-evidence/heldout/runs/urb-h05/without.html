Top themes

1. Rescheduling a pickup is hard or impossible4 tickets (#1, #2, #4, #7)

Customers can't find a way to change their pickup day. One reports no reschedule button after payment. Another gave up and cancelled and rebooked.

"Paid for a Tuesday pickup but I'm away. There's no reschedule button on the order after payment?"

2. Extra-bag fee surprises customers2 tickets (#3, #6)

A $6 extra-bag fee appears at the end of checkout and isn't disclosed beforehand, so the total jumps.

"Got charged a $6 extra-bag fee. Nobody told me about that before I paid."

3. Service issues (not app-specific)2 tickets (#5, #8)

A driver arrived 40 minutes late without texting, and a blue sock was missing from a delivery.

4. Positive feedback1 ticket (#9)

Recommended actions

  1. Add a reschedule option to the order screen, including after payment.
  2. Show the extra-bag fee up front (at booking and in the price estimate), not only at the end.
  3. Send late-driver text alerts automatically when a pickup is running behind.
  4. Follow up on the missing sock with the affected customer.

Based on 9 tickets from one week; a small sample, so treat counts as directional.
