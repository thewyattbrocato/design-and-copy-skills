Top issues

1. Customers can't find a way to reschedule (4 tickets)

Customers looked for a way to move their pickup day and couldn't find one. One noted there is no reschedule button after payment. Another gave up and cancelled and rebooked.

"There's no reschedule button on the order after payment?"

2. The $6 extra-bag fee comes as a surprise (2 tickets)

The fee shows up at the end of checkout or after payment, and customers say they weren't told beforehand. One said the total "jumped."

"Nobody told me about that before I paid."

Other items

  • Late driver (1): About 40 minutes late, with no text to the customer.
  • Missing item (1): One blue sock missing from a delivery.
  • Praise (1): "Honestly love the service, thank you!"

Suggested next steps

  • Add a reschedule option to orders, including after payment, or make the existing one easier to find.
  • Show the extra-bag fee and its conditions before checkout, so the total doesn't change at the end.
  • Look at sending an automatic text when a driver is running late.
  • Follow up with the customer who reported the missing sock.
