What we're hearing

1. Customers can't reschedule a pickup 4 tickets

Customers look for a reschedule option and don't find one. One noted it's missing after payment. One gave up and cancelled and rebooked instead, which is extra work for them and for us.

"Paid for a Tuesday pickup but I'm away. There's no reschedule button on the order after payment?"
"I tried to change my pickup day and gave up, had to cancel and rebook."

2. Extra-bag fee comes as a surprise 2 tickets

Customers say the $6 extra-bag fee wasn't disclosed before they paid, and the total jumped at the end.

"There was a bag fee at the end that I didn't expect, the total jumped."

3. One-off items 3 tickets

  • Late driver: 40 minutes late with no text. A proactive delay notice would have helped.
  • Missing item: one blue sock missing from a delivery. Needs a normal follow-up.
  • Praise: "Honestly love the service, thank you!"

Suggested next steps

  • Reschedule: Add a clear "Reschedule pickup" action on the order screen, including after payment. This is the highest-impact fix.
  • Bag fee: Show the extra-bag fee and its conditions before checkout, not just on the final total.
  • Late driver: Look at sending an automatic text when a driver is running behind.
  • Missing sock: Reply to the customer and follow up with the delivery team.
Based on 9 tickets from one week, so treat counts as directional rather than precise. Only the first ticket mentions the app by name. The others are inferred to be app-related from context. The suggested next steps are proposals for the team to discuss.
