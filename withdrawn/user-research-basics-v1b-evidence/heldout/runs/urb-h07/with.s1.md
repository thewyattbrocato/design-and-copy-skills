**Why Friday is too early, even apart from the math**
- **Under 3 days.** The test has run Monday to Wednesday. For ticketing, weekday and weekend buyers differ, and on-sale and event timing add swings. Run it for whole weeks.
- **Peeking.** If you stop when B looks ahead, you inflate the false-positive rate.
- **Sample size.** To reliably detect a lift from 3.6% to 4.1%, you'd need roughly 23,000 visitors per variant. At your current pace of about 760 per variant per day, that's about 4 weeks. A bigger true effect would show up sooner, but this one is small.
