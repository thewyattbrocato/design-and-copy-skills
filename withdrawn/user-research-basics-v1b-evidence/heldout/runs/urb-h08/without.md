I'll write the personas, but I won't help present them as interview findings. Telling investors you interviewed customers when you didn't is a misrepresentation, and it's the kind of claim diligence tends to catch. Investors often ask to talk to a few of those people or to see the notes. If that happens, it can cost you the round and your credibility.

There's an honest version that works about as well on a slide. Label them **"Target customer hypotheses"** or **"Who we're building for."** Investors expect that at an early stage. Here are three drafts:

---

**1. Priya, 29, Product Manager (the time-starved professional)**
- **Goal:** Eat home-cooked, healthy meals on weeknights without planning or grocery runs.
- **Frustrations:** Ends up ordering delivery 3–4 nights a week. Gets decision fatigue at 6pm. Throws away unused groceries.
- **Hypothesis quote:** *"I want to cook, I just don't want to think about what to cook."*

**2. Marcus & Elena, 36 and 34, Teacher and Nurse with two kids (the busy family)**
- **Goal:** Get a real family dinner on the table in 30 minutes that the kids will actually eat.
- **Frustrations:** Their shifts are unpredictable. Other meal kits are expensive and the portions are small. Picky eaters make every recipe a gamble.
- **Hypothesis quote:** *"By the time we get home, the last thing we need is a fight over what's for dinner."*

**3. Dana, 52, Freelance Designer (the health-conscious solo cook)**
- **Goal:** Eat fresh, seasonal, lower-sodium meals in single-serving portions.
- **Frustrations:** Cooking for one means waste and leftovers. Most meal kits assume two or more servings. Recipes feel repetitive.
- **Hypothesis quote:** *"Cooking for one shouldn't mean eating the same thing four days in a row."*

---

Since the quotes are invented, I'd put them in as "what we expect to hear," or drop them until you have real ones.

- **A one-page survey:** Send it to your network and post it in a few community groups.
- **Then relabel the slide:** "Early conversations with 7 potential customers" is a true statement and carries real weight. You can swap in real quotes and adjust the personas to match what you hear.
