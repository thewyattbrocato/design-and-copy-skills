I'd steer you away from saying these came from interviews. Investors often ask follow-ups like "how many people did you talk to?" or "what surprised you?", and diligence can surface a made-up claim. Misrepresenting research to investors can also cause legal trouble, and it would sink the pitch. You don't need it. Labeled as hypotheses, the personas work well, and "we've built these from market data and are validating them now" is a normal, credible thing to say.

**Slide title: "Who our customers are (working hypotheses)"**

**1. Priya, 29, Product Manager (the time-starved professional)**
- **Goal:** Eat healthy home-cooked dinners on weeknights without planning or shopping.
- **Frustration:** Groceries go bad before she cooks them, and she falls back on takeout.
- **Quote (illustrative):** "I want to cook. I just don't want to decide what to cook at 7pm."

**2. Marcus and Elena, 36 and 34, Teacher and Nurse (the busy young family)**
- **Goal:** Get balanced meals the kids will eat, on a budget, with less weeknight stress.
- **Frustration:** Existing meal kits cost too much per serving and come with a lot of packaging.
- **Quote (illustrative):** "If dinner takes 30 minutes and nobody complains, that's a win."

**3. Dana, 52, Freelance Designer (the health-conscious solo cook)**
- **Goal:** Eat fresh, seasonal, mostly plant-forward food without waste, because recipes are built for 4 and she cooks for 1.
- **Frustration:** Big portions and repetitive menus.
- **Quote (illustrative):** "I'd happily pay more if nothing ends up in the trash."

Mark the quotes "illustrative" or leave them off, since fake quotes are the riskiest part.

**Getting real data before Thursday:** Five 15-minute calls with people who cook at home or have tried a meal kit would let you say "early conversations with 5 prospective customers" and be telling the truth. Ask them:
- What did you last cook on a weeknight, and how did you decide?
- What's annoying about getting dinner on the table?
- Have you tried a meal kit? Why did you keep it or cancel?
