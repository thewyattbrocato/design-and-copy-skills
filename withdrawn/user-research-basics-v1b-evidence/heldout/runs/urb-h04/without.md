## Screener: rural clinic front desk interviews

**Intro (shown at top of the form)**
We're building a tool to help clinic front desks manage walk-in queues and missed appointments. We're looking for people who work at a clinic front desk to join a 30-minute video call. You'll get a [$X gift card] as thanks. This is research only, and nobody will try to sell you anything. Please don't include patient names or other patient information in your answers.

**Questions**

1. **What best describes your current role?** *(Must be a front desk or scheduling role.)*
   - Front desk / receptionist / patient registration ✅
   - Scheduler / appointment coordinator ✅
   - Office or practice manager who also works the front desk ✅
   - Office manager who does not work the front desk ❌ (or a backup pool)
   - Clinical staff (nurse, MA, provider) ❌
   - Other ❌

2. **How long have you worked in this role?**
   - Under 6 months ❌ (or a backup pool)
   - 6–12 months
   - 1–3 years ✅
   - 3+ years ✅

3. **What type of clinic do you work in?** *(Primary care, urgent care, community health center, or rural health clinic ✅. Specialty, dental, or hospital-only ❌, or note them as a mixed group.)*

4. **Where is your clinic located?** *(Ask for a ZIP code. Verify it's rural against the [USDA RUCA codes](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/rural-urban-commuting-area-codes) or the HRSA Rural Health Grants Eligibility Analyzer. Don't rely on self-description.)*

5. **Does your clinic accept walk-ins?**
   - Yes, regularly ✅
   - Occasionally ✅
   - No ❌

6. **How many appointments does your clinic book on a typical day?** *(Under 20, 20–50, 50+. Use this to get a mix of sizes.)*

7. **How do you currently track the walk-in queue and handle no-shows?** *(Open-ended, required. Look for specifics such as paper list, whiteboard, EHR, or phone calls. Reject one-word or vague answers.)*

8. **In the past month, how often did patients miss appointments without canceling?**
   - Rarely / never ❌
   - A few times a week ✅
   - Daily ✅

9. **Do you have a say in, or influence on, the tools your front desk uses?** *(Yes / Somewhat / No. Don't screen out on this. Use it to balance the group.)*

10. **Can you join a 30-minute video call on a computer or tablet with a stable connection and a quiet space?** *(Must be Yes. Rural bandwidth is a real risk, so ask which times work and offer a phone-call fallback.)*

11. **Which of these windows could you do between [date] and [date]?** *(Offer slots that fit clinic hours, such as early morning, lunch, and after close.)*

12. **Contact info:** first name, email, phone, time zone.

**Selection notes**
- Target 6 sessions and recruit about 9–10, since no-shows are likely, and the topic makes that ironic.
- Aim for a spread of clinic size, walk-in volume, and current tools (paper vs. EHR-only).
- Get approval from the participant for recording. Also check whether they need employer permission to talk about their workflow.
