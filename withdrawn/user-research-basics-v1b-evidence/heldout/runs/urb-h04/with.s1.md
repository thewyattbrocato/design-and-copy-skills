# Screener: front desk workflow study

**Study description shown to candidates (generic, no product named):** "A 30-minute remote conversation about how clinic front desks handle everyday tasks. [Incentive: amount and form to set] for your time, paid even if the study turns out not to be a fit."

**Quotas (targets, not guarantees):** about 6 completed interviews, with a mix of clinic sizes, at least 2 people who work alone at the desk, and a mix of walk-in-heavy and appointment-heavy clinics.

## Questions

**1. Which of these have you done at work in the past month? (select all that apply)**
- Checked patients in at a front desk or reception
- Scheduled or rescheduled appointments
- Handled walk-in patients
- Called or texted patients about appointments
- Verified insurance or collected payments
- Ordered supplies
- None of these → *end, thank*

*Must include "handled walk-ins" and "scheduled or rescheduled appointments". Both are required to continue.*

**2. What best describes where you work?**
- Clinic in a rural or small-town area (population under [threshold to set])
- Clinic in a mid-sized city
- Clinic in a large city or suburb
- Hospital department
- Other: ____

*Continue only on the first option. Don't use the word "rural" in the study description.*

**3. In a typical week, how much of your working time is spent at the front desk?**
- Most of it
- About half
- A small part
- None → *end*

*Continue on "most" or "about half".*

**4. Think about the last time a scheduled patient didn't show up. In a few sentences, what happened at your desk, from when you noticed to what you did next?** *(open text)*

*Screen the answer by hand. Accept a specific, recent account, even a short one. Decline vague or generic answers ("we just call them") and answers that read as invented to qualify.*

**5. Think about the last busy stretch with walk-ins. How did you keep track of who was waiting and in what order?** *(open text)*

*Same rule. Note the tools in their own words: paper, whiteboard, spreadsheet, EHR feature, memory.*

**6. Do you currently work for, or have you consulted for, any of the following?** *(select all that apply)*
- A company that makes scheduling, queue or practice-management software
- A UX, design or market research firm
- None of these

*Exclude if any of the first two is selected.*

**7. Have you been paid to take part in a research study in the past 3 months?**
- Yes → *exclude, or deprioritize if candidates are scarce*
- No

**8. Can you join a 30-minute video or phone call on a [weekday window to set], with a connection that holds up for a call?**
- Yes: video
- Yes: phone only
- No → *end*

**9. Contact details** *(last, optional until selected)*: first name, email or phone, best times, and time zone.

## Notes for the scheduler

- **Selection:** pick on questions 4 and 5 first, then on the quota mix. Tell people they've been selected, but don't tell them what the product is until the session materials show it.
- **Not a fit:** anyone who screens out after spending effort on questions 4 or 5 still gets the incentive.
- **Consent line for the invite:** say the call will be recorded only with their permission, who will see it, and that they can skip any question or stop. Ask them not to share any patient names or details.

## What the sample leaves out

- **Large clinics and hospitals** are excluded by question 2.
- **Staff who rarely work the desk** are excluded by question 3.
- **People without a reliable connection** are partly excluded by question 8. Offering phone-only keeps some of them in.
- **Volunteers who respond to an outreach post** skew toward people who are comfortable with technology.

## What a human has to do
