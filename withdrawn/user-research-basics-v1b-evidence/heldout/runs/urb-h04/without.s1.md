## How to find them by Friday

| Channel | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Your own network** | Ask colleagues, advisors, and clinic contacts for warm intros. This is usually the fastest route. |
| **Rural health associations** | State rural health associations, the National Rural Health Association, and state primary care associations can often forward a recruiting email to member clinics. |

| **Direct outreach** | Call or email clinics directly. Front desk staff are busy, so try early morning or late afternoon. |
