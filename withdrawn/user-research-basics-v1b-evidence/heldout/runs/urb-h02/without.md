A usability test watches a handful of real people try to complete tasks with your design, so you learn *why* things confuse them. You observe where they hesitate, misread, or get stuck. An A/B test shows two versions to large numbers of live users and measures which one performs better on a specific metric, like sign-ups or clicks. It tells you *which* version wins, but not why. Usability testing is qualitative and best for finding problems early. A/B testing is quantitative and best for choosing between options once you have traffic.
