**Pantrylog interviews: summary of 6 home cooks**

We interviewed 6 home cooks on Oct 6–9. All were recruited from our email list, so all already use the app.

**What we saw**
- 4 of 6 kept a separate phone note for what to buy, even though the app has a shopping list. Two of those four didn't know the list could be shared.
- 2 of 6 scanned barcodes when adding items. The other 4 typed names by hand.
- 1 of 6 mentioned expiry reminders unprompted. We didn't ask about them, so we can't say how many others care.

**What this suggests**
The shared shopping list may be hard to discover. This is a lead, not a conclusion: six existing users is a small, friendly sample.

**Proposed next step**
Watch 3 new users try to set up a shared list without help, and note where they stall.

**Not covered:** people who tried Pantrylog and left.
