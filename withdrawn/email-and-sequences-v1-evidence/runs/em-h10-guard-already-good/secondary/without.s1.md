Two real problems, plus one wording issue:

1. **Typo:** "recieve" should be "receive" (in "You'll recieve a receipt").

2. **Ambiguous refund window:** "If you cancel after that, email us within 14 days" doesn't say 14 days from what. Members could read it as 14 days from March 3, from when they notice the charge, or from the cancellation. If the policy is 14 days from the renewal charge, say so, e.g. "within 14 days of the March 3 charge."

3. **Possible confusion:** "cancel" and "refund the year" in the same sentence can read as two different outcomes. If cancelling after renewal ends the membership and returns the $48, a short clause like "and we'll refund the year and end your membership" would remove the question.

Everything else checks out: the {first_name} and {last4} merge fields, the $48 amount, the March 3 date, and the "reply to this email" line. The "before March 3" cancellation wording is consistent with the refund sentence.
