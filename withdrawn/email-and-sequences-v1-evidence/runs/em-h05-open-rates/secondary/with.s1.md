**What's going wrong**

The subject lines that worked each named something specific that a reader could act on: a place with a condition ("Snow still on Heather Ridge"), or a problem they might have ("Is your rain shell actually waterproof anymore?"). The ones that didn't are labels: an issue number, a month, or a category ("Newsletter: summer reading list"). A reader scanning a crowded inbox has no reason to open "Trail Notes #115," because the envelope doesn't say what's inside.

Six sends is a small sample, and topic, season and send day are all mixed in with the wording, so treat this as a strong pattern, not proof. Still, the direction is consistent: the three lowest are the ones that tell the reader nothing. The fix is to lead with the most useful, specific item each week and drop the issue number from the subject line. Keep the number in the body if you want it.
