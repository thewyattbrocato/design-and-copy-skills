## Email 2: send 2 days after sign-up

**Subject:** Test tiles: the nerdiest part of the box
**Preview:** Why we send a tile with every glaze set.

Hi [First name],

Did you read the tile-testing guide yet? If not, here's the short pitch: a glaze that looks great in the jar can surprise you in the kiln, and a tile lets you find that out before it happens to your best bowl.

That's why every box comes with a test tile and a printed card listing the firing range and application notes. The card tells you what we'd try. The tile tells you what actually happens in your kiln, with your clay and your habits.

Our tip: test every new glaze on a tile before you commit a finished piece to it. Write down what you did, too. Future you will be grateful.

Grab the guide again here if you need it: [link].

Marit & Dov
