## Email 2: send 2 days after sign-up

**Subject:** What's in your Kilnside box
**Preview:** Three jars, a firing card, one test tile. All cone 6.

Hi there,

Here's exactly what shows up in your box each month:

- 3 jars of tested glaze, 4 oz each
- A printed card with the firing range and application notes
- One test tile

Everything is cone 6, for mid-range electric kilns. We don't do cone 10 or raku, and we'd rather you know that now than find out at the kiln. Glazes behave differently at different temperatures, so a cone 6 glaze fired hot won't give you the result on the card.

One quick check: is that your kiln? If you fire at a different cone, or in a different kind of kiln, reply and tell us before the box ships.

Four ounces is a small jar, which suits testing: you get to try three glazes without committing to a bucket of any one of them.

Marit & Dov
