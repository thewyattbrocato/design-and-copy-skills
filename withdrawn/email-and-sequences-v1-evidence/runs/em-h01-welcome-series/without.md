## Email 2: What's in the box
**Send:** 2 days after sign-up

**Subject:** What's actually in the box
**Preview:** Three jars, one card, one tile, and one important note about cone 6.

Hi again,

Here's a quick tour of your monthly box, so nothing's a mystery when it lands.

The three jars are 4 oz each. That's enough to test a glaze properly without leaving you with a quart of something you didn't love. The printed card lists the firing range and application notes. The test tile is for trying the glaze before it goes near a finished pot.

Our nudge is to test first, then commit. The tile guide you downloaded walks through how.

One thing to know up front: every glaze we send is cone 6, for mid-range electric kilns. We don't do cone 10 or raku. If that's not what you fire, reply and tell us, or cancel anytime from your account page.

Marit and Dov
