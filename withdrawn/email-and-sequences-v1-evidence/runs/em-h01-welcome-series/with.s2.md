## Email 2: Kiln check
**Send:** 2 days after sign-up

**Subject:** Quick check: is your kiln cone 6?
**Preview:** Every Kilnside glaze is cone 6, made for mid-range electric kilns.

Quick compatibility check, so a box never lands with someone who can't fire it.

Every glaze we send is cone 6, made for mid-range electric kilns. We don't do cone 10 or raku. If that's how you fire, these won't suit you, and you can cancel anytime from your account page.

If you fire electric at cone 6, you're set. The card in each box lists the firing range for every glaze, so you can check it against your own schedule.

Either way, tell us about your setup. Just reply with your kiln and what you've been firing lately. A sentence is plenty. It goes straight to Marit or Dov, and we like knowing who we're glazing for.

Marit & Dov
