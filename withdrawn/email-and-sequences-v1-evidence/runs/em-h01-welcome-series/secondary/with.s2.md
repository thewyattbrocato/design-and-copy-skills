**Email 2: What's in the box**

*Subject:* Before your first box lands (32 characters)
*Preview:* The card, the tile and what cone 6 means for you.

Hi there,

Before your first box shows up, here's a quick tour of what's inside.

Three 4 oz jars of glaze, all tested and all formulated for cone 6. That's the range for mid-range electric kilns. We don't do cone 10 or raku, so if you fire something different, tell us and we'll help you figure out whether the box fits.

The printed card is the one to keep. It lists the firing range for each glaze and the application notes, so you know how to brush it on before you load the kiln.

The test tile is for trying before you commit. Glaze it, fire it alongside a piece you care about, and see what happens before the good bowl goes in.

Tell us what kiln you fire in. Just reply to this email, and it goes straight to Marit or Dov.

Marit and Dov
