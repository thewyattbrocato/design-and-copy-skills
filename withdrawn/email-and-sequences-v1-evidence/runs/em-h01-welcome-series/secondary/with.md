Hi [first name],

Your first box ships on [ship date], so here's what to expect.

Inside you'll find three 4 oz jars of tested glaze, one test tile, and a printed card with the firing range and application notes for each glaze. Read the card before you open a jar. It tells you where each glaze wants to land, and how thick to put it on.
