## Email 2: Tile Testing and Reply Invite
**Send:** 3 days after sign-up

**Subject:** Your test tile is a tiny science experiment
**Preview:** A quick way to get more out of every test.

> Hi [First name],
>
> Quick nerd moment: the test tile is the most useful thing in your box. A glaze looks one way in the jar, another on the tile, and something else again once it's been through your kiln. Treat every tile as a small experiment and you'll learn fast.
>
> Label the back with the glaze name and date. Note how many coats you applied. Keep one tile plain so you can compare.
>
> Got a question about the firing range, or a glaze that did something weird? Just hit reply. It goes straight to Marit or Dov, not a support queue. The weird results are our favorite emails to read.
>
> For glaze talk with people who get excited about crawling and pinholing, join our members-only Discord. Around 30 potters are already in there.
>
> Happy testing,
> Marit and Dov

## Email 3: Box Is Coming
**Send:** 2 days before each member's ship date (the 3rd, for a box shipping the 5th)

**Subject:** Your box ships on the 5th
**Preview:** What's inside, and how to get the most from it.

> Hi [First name],
>
> Your box ships on the 5th, so it's almost on its way. Here's what to expect: three 4 oz jars of tested cone 6 glaze, a printed card with the firing range and application notes, and one test tile.
>
> Read the card before you open anything. The firing range and application notes are where the useful stuff lives.
>
> Stir each jar well before you use it, since glaze settles. Then start with the test tile so you know how the glaze behaves before it goes on a piece you love. Think of it as a dress rehearsal.
>
> Questions before it arrives? Reply to this email and it reaches Marit or Dov directly.
>
> Happy firing,
> Marit and Dov

## Things to check before sending

- **Placeholders:** Replace `[First name]` and `[GUIDE LINK]`. If your email tool supports merge fields, use one for the first name.
- **Ship date:** Email 1 says "the 5th of next month," which only works for sign-ups before the 28th cutoff. If you can, have it pull the member's actual ship date. Email 3 should also be triggered from each member's ship date, not a fixed calendar day, so late sign-ups don't get the wrong timing.
- **Discord count:** "Around 30" will go stale quickly. Drop the number once the community grows.
- **Cutoff rule:** I assumed a sign-up before the 28th gets the box shipping on the 5th of the following month. If that's wrong, tell me and I'll adjust Email 1.
