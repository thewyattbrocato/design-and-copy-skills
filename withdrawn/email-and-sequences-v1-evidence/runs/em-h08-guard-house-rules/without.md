Subject: Dispatch 31 — notes from the bench

Hi all,

The walnut stool is finished. It's sitting in the shop right now looking better than it has any right to, considering how the glue-up went.

I rushed the clamps. The glue was spread, the open time was ticking, and I got impatient. I snugged everything down fast instead of checking each joint as I went. It looked fine when I walked away. When I came back, one joint on a leg had opened up. It wasn't a hairline either. It was a real gap, and the kind you can't honestly fill and forget.

So I fixed it with a spline. I cut a slot across the joint, glued in a thin strip of walnut, and trimmed it flush once it had cured. It pulled the joint shut and holds it mechanically, so the repair is arguably stronger than the joint would have been on its own. The lesson isn't new, but I seem to need it again every few projects: the clamps aren't where you save time. Slow down there and the rest of the build goes faster.

The stool is going to my sister's house. I'm happy about that. A piece with a repair in it belongs with family, where the story gets told along with it.
