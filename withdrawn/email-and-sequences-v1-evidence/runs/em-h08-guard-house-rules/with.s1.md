Subject: Dispatch 31 — notes from the bench

The walnut stool is finished.

I'll tell you how it got there, because it didn't go smoothly. The glue-up went wrong, and it was my own doing. I rushed the clamps. I wanted to be done, so I didn't set them properly, and by the time I looked again a joint on one of the legs had opened up.

There's a moment after something like that when you stand there deciding whether to be annoyed or to fix it. I was annoyed for a while. Then I fixed it.

The fix was a spline. I cut a slot across the failed joint and glued a thin piece of wood into it. It closed the gap and it's stronger than the joint was. It also leaves a small line you can see if you look for it. I could have hidden it better. I decided I didn't want to. It's part of how this stool got made, and I'd rather have the honest line than a perfect surface that pretends nothing happened.

The real lesson is an old one: the clamps are not the part of the job to hurry. Everything before them took hours, and I shortchanged the one step that holds it all together. I'll try to remember that next time, though I've said that before.

The stool is going to my sister's house. I'm glad it's going somewhere it'll get used.

Next issue I start on a workbench vise. That's a project I've wanted to get to for a long time, so I'll show you how it goes, mistakes included.
