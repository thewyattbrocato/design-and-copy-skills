The walnut stool is finished, but the road there was rougher than I'd like. I rushed the glue-up and didn't give the clamps the attention they needed. When I came back to the assembly, a joint on one leg had opened up.

The fix was a spline. I cut a slot across the open joint, fit a thin strip of walnut, glued it in, and trimmed it flush. Once it was finished, the repair almost disappears. I still know exactly where it is, and I think that's the right way to carry it. A stool with a visible repair is more honest than one that pretends nothing happened.

The stool is going to my sister's house. She's been patient about it, and I'm glad it's ending up with someone who will use it every day.
