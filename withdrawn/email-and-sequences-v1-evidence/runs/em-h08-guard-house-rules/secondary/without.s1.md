**Subject:** Dispatch 31 — notes from the bench

Hello friends,

This issue is about the walnut stool, which is finished, and a mistake I'd rather not repeat.

The glue-up went wrong because I rushed the clamps. One joint on a leg opened up under pressure, and I only caught it once things were already tightening. Rushing a glue-up is the fastest way I know to turn a good afternoon into a bad one.

The fix was a spline. I cut a slot across the open joint, fit a thin strip of walnut, glued it in, and cleaned it up once it cured. It's not invisible, and I'm fine with that. The leg is solid, and I know exactly where the repair is. Walnut is unforgiving when a joint is off, and it taught me to slow down before the first clamp goes on.

The stool is going to my sister's house. She knows the story, including the part where I had to fix it.

Next issue I start a workbench vise. A bench without a good vise is just a table, so this is where the real work begins. I'll cover the hardware, the layout, and the first cuts.
