Subject: Dispatch 31 — notes from the bench

The walnut stool is finished.

It almost wasn't, at least not cleanly. I rushed the glue-up. I had the clamps out, but I didn't set them properly before the glue started to grab, and I was hurrying to catch up. When I came back to check, one joint on a leg had opened up. It was a small gap, but a gap in a leg joint doesn't stay small once someone sits on it.

I could have taken the leg apart and started again. Instead I fixed it with a spline. I cut a slot across the joint and glued in a thin piece to bridge it. That closed the joint and made it stronger than it was before. It's also a repair I can see if I look for it, and I'm fine with that. A stool that has been fixed properly is better than one that got lucky.

The lesson is an old one, and I learned it again anyway: glue gives you a short window, and the clamps need to be ready before it opens. Dry-fit, set the clamps to size, and keep the cauls within reach. The extra five minutes of preparation costs far less than the repair.

The stool is going to my sister's house, so it will get used daily, which is what I made it for.
