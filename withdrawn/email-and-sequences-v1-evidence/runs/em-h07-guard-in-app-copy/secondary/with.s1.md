**1) Banner: carrier feed delayed** (about two lines)

> **Carrier updates are delayed**
> [Carrier] is running behind. Tracking info may be up to 30 minutes out of date.

If the banner has a tap target, the link text can be "Learn more" or nothing. The body already says what the user needs.

**2) Toast: refresh failed** (one line)

> Couldn't refresh. Try again.

This covers both no network and a server that didn't respond without guessing which one happened. It's about 28 characters, so it fits on one line on a phone.

Alternates if you want a different tone:
- Banner: "[Carrier] updates are running late. Tracking may be up to 30 minutes behind."
- Toast: "Refresh failed. Try again."
