**1) Banner (carrier feed delayed)**

> Carrier updates can be delayed up to 30 minutes. Tracking info may be out of date.

About 78 characters, so it should wrap to two lines on a phone.

**2) Toast (refresh failed)**

> Couldn't refresh. Try again.

This covers both no network and no server response without naming a cause the app may not know. It's short enough for one line. If you want to mention the connection, use "Couldn't refresh. Check your connection and try again." That's about 55 characters and may wrap on small screens.
