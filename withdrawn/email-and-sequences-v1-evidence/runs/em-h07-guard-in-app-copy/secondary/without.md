**1) Banner: carrier feed delayed** (about two lines)

> **Some tracking updates are delayed.** Carriers can lag by up to 30 minutes, so what you see may be out of date.

Shorter alternative:

> **Tracking may be behind.** Carrier updates can be delayed by up to 30 minutes.

**2) Toast: refresh failed** (one line)

> **Couldn't refresh. Try again.**

I left out "check your connection" because the failure can also be the server not responding, and "try again" covers both cases. If you want the cause in the message, use "Couldn't refresh. Check your connection and try again," but that's likely to wrap to two lines on a phone.
