**1) Banner (delayed carrier feed, about two lines)**

- Carrier updates are running up to 30 minutes behind. Tracking info on this screen may be out of date.
- Some carriers are delayed by up to 30 minutes, so the status you see may not be current.
- Tracking updates may be up to 30 minutes late for some carriers. What you see here may be out of date.

**2) Toast (refresh failed, one line)**

- Couldn't refresh. Try again.
- Couldn't refresh. Check your connection and try again.
- Refresh failed. Try again in a moment.

My picks: "Carrier updates are running up to 30 minutes behind. Tracking info on this screen may be out of date." for the banner, and "Couldn't refresh. Try again." for the toast. The toast works for both causes (no network or no server response) without naming either, and it's short enough to stay on one line at phone widths. If you want the connection hint, use "Couldn't refresh. Check your connection and try again," but check it on a small device, since it's close to the one-line limit.
