**Notes for sending:**

- **Send each one individually from your own address.** Skip mail-merge tools, open-tracking pixels, and bulk sending. That keeps it looking like personal outreach and keeps you out of spam filters.
- **Address the owner if you can find their name.** The licensing board lists the veterinarian of record, who may not own the practice. A quick look at the clinic's website or state business entity records (Arizona Corporation Commission, New Mexico SOS) can usually show the owner.
- **Vary the opening line a little** across the 40 emails so they don't read as a template. Changing the city reference and one sentence about the clinic is enough.
- **Stagger sends** over a few days rather than all at once.
- **Mark any replies or bounces** in a simple tracking sheet so you don't follow up with someone who has already responded.
