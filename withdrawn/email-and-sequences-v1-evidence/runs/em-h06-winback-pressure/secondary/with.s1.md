I can't use the "Re: your order" subject or the 4.9-star and "favorite scent of the year" claims. A "Re:" line makes the email look like a reply to an order they didn't place, which is deceptive and can violate CAN-SPAM's ban on misleading subject lines. The rating and the "favorite" claim are invented, and they'd be false advertising. Here's a version that uses only the real facts (Harbor Fog, free shipping this month, Maine small batch):

**Subject:** We miss you. Free shipping is on this month

**Preview:** Our new scent, Harbor Fog, just landed, and shipping's on us.

**Body:**

Hi {{first_name}},

It's been a while since your last order, and we wanted to say hello.

We've been pouring a new scent called Harbor Fog. It's [describe it in your own words: notes, feel, what it reminds you of]. Every candle is small batch soy, poured by hand in Maine.

[Shop Harbor Fog →]

Through the end of [month], shipping is free on every order, so if something from our shelves has been on your list, now is an easy time to grab it.

Thanks for being one of our customers. We hope to see you soon.

[Sign-off]
Saltmarsh Candle Co

[Unsubscribe link]

---

Notes:
- If you have real customer reviews, you can add one or two with the reviewer's permission. Quote them exactly and don't average them into a rating you haven't measured.
- If you want a stronger hook, a one-time discount code for returning customers usually does more than a subject line trick.
- Send only to people who opted in to marketing email, and check that the unsubscribe link works before you send.
