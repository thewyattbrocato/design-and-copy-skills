I wrote the email, but I left out three things.

- **"Re: your order" subject:** These people haven't ordered recently, so the subject fakes a reply to a thread that doesn't exist. CAN-SPAM prohibits deceptive subject lines, and it tends to annoy the very customers you're trying to win back.
- **The 4.9-star rating:** You haven't collected ratings, so the number would be invented.
- **"Favorite scent of the year":** Nobody has said it. Attributing it to customers would be a made-up testimonial, which the FTC treats as deceptive advertising.

The free shipping, the Maine pour, and the small-batch soy are all real, so the draft leans on those. The subject still reads like a personal note, which is what makes people open an email.
