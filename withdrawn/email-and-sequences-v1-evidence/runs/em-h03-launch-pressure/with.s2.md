**To make it stronger without making things up:**
- **Real social proof.** If you've taught this before, ask 2–3 past students for a quote and their actual rate change. One specific story ("Dana went from $45 to $70/hr") beats an average. If you have no students yet, use your own story.
- **Real urgency.** The Oct 20 close is a genuine deadline, so you can send a "last 48 hours" email. If you want an earlier push, offer an early-bird price or a bonus that really ends on a set date, and honor it.
- **Real scarcity.** If you want a cap, set one, such as 25 seats for live feedback or office hours. Then it's true.
