I wrote the email, but I left out the fake Friday deadline, the made-up 25-seat cap, and the invented 40% figure. **Making the urgency real**
- **Early-bird deadline:** if you want a deadline before the 20th, pick a real one, like $199 through Oct 13, then $249. Keep to it and your list will believe your next deadline.
- **Cap on seats:** if you want one, set it, for example 40 seats, so you can close it when it fills. A small cohort is also a real selling point if you'll give feedback to each student.
- **Results:** if you have past students, ask two or three for their real numbers and use those. If this is the first cohort, "I'll work through your pricing with you in week 2" is a stronger promise than an invented stat.
