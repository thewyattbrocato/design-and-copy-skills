# Discovery call prep: Ines Varga (20 min)

## What we know
- Solo brand designer who works with small businesses.
- Found us through a newsletter and booked the call herself, so she has some intent.
- Sole decision-maker, so there's no committee and no accountant. We can aim to close or agree a trial on this call.
- Price is $19/month. For a solo freelancer that's an easy purchase, so the real obstacle is switching effort or "my current setup is fine," not budget.

## Goal for the call
Learn how she invoices and tracks time today, find out what's painful enough to switch, and leave with a concrete next step: a trial started, or a dated follow-up.

## Suggested agenda
| Time | Segment |
|---|---|
| 0-2 min | Open: thank her, confirm 20 min, set the agenda |
| 2-5 min | Her context: studio, clients, how work is scoped |
| 5-14 min | Current workflow and pain (most of the call) |
| 14-17 min | Short, tailored demo or description of the fit |
| 17-20 min | Next step and close |

**Opening line:** "I saw you booked through the newsletter. What caught your eye there? I'd like to spend most of the time on how you handle this today, then show you only the parts that matter to you."

## Questions to ask

**Trigger and context**
- What made you book this call now?
- How do you price your work: project fees, hourly, retainers, or a mix? (This decides whether time tracking matters to her at all. Many brand designers charge per project.)

**Current workflow**
- Walk me through what happens from "project signed" to "invoice paid."
- What tools do you use for invoicing and time tracking today, if any? Spreadsheets, Wave, QuickBooks, FreshBooks, Notion?
- How much time a month do you spend on invoicing and admin?

**Pain and stakes**
- What's the most annoying part of that process?
- Have you had late payments, forgotten billable hours, or messy invoices? What did that cost you?
- What have you already tried, and why did you stop using it?

**Decision**
- If this solved the problem, what would you need to see to start using it?
- Is there anything that would stop you, such as migration effort, client-facing look, or tax or bookkeeping needs?

## Hypotheses to test (not assumed)
- She may care a lot about how invoices look, since she's a brand designer. Check whether customizing them with her branding matters.
- She may not track hours at all if she bills per project. If so, lead with invoicing, payment reminders, and project profitability, not timers.
- She may be using free or cheap tools and see $19 as an extra cost to justify. Anchor on time saved and faster payment, not features.

## Pitfalls to avoid
- Don't demo everything. Show two or three things tied to pains she names.
- Don't pitch before you understand how she bills.
- Don't assume she needs accounting features. She said no accountant is involved, but ask how she handles taxes and bookkeeping before you promise anything.

## Closing
Since she decides alone, ask directly: "Based on what you've told me, does it make sense to start a trial today? I can help you set up your first invoice right now." If she hesitates, find out the specific blocker and book a dated follow-up (for example, 3 days out) so it doesn't just drift.

## Before the call (5 min)
- Look at her studio website and portfolio, so you can say something specific about her clients and style.
- Check which newsletter referred her and what it said about us, so you can reference it.
- Have the trial or signup link and a sample branded invoice ready.
- Check how you handle migration or import from common tools, because she may ask.

If you tell me which newsletter it was, what your trial or free-plan terms are, or which competitors come up most, I can tailor the pitch and objection handling.
