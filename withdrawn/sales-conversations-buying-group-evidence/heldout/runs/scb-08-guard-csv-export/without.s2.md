**Tips for the follow-up:**

1. **Lead with the yes.** They asked a yes/no question, so give the yes first and skip the preamble.
2. **Ask what they're trying to do.** The CSV question usually means one of three things: they want to get data into Excel or Sheets, they want to feed another system (BI tool, warehouse), or they're checking for lock-in. A follow-up like "What would you do with the export?" lets you tailor the answer.
3. **Offer to show it.** If you're on a call, a 10-second demo (open a report, click export) is more convincing than describing it.
4. **Only mention other options if they're real.** If you also have an API, scheduled exports, or integrations and they need automation, bring those up. If they don't need them, don't oversell.

**Caveat:** Before the follow-up, double-check a few details so you don't overpromise: whether there are row limits, whether the export respects current filters, and whether all report types (including dashboards or custom reports) behave the same.
