## Running the meeting

1. **Open (2 min).** State the purpose: "By the end I'd like us to agree whether the problems the ops teams are hitting are worth solving, and if so, what the smallest next step is. I'm not asking for a decision today."
2. **Problem first (10 min).** The ops managers describe it in their own words and numbers. You ask what each role is held to. Check the statement with the whole room: "Is this the problem?" If people describe different problems, stop and reconcile them. Don't demo past it.
3. **Switching risk, on the agenda by name (10 min).** Raise it before Rachel does and credit her for it: "The legitimate worry is migration. Here's what moves, who does the work, what could go wrong, and what you'd have if it did." Use only what you can truthfully commit to: data import, timeline, who on your side owns it, any parallel-run or exit terms. Say plainly what you can't do. 6. **Close (5 min).** Restate the shared statement and the open items. Propose the next step aloud, get a yes from the room, and fix the owner and date. ## With Rachel in the room

- Give her a role that fits her job: she owns the migration-risk criteria, and her sign-off conditions become the plan.
