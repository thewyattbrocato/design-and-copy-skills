Send the form today. Your manager's process is built for a different kind of deal.

**Why:**
- **He's already decided.** Gus is the owner, he's the sole signer, and he told you "this is what I need." A mutual action plan and business case exist to build and prove a case to a buyer who hasn't committed or who has to sell internally. He doesn't have either problem.
- **Delay is the real risk.** He wants to start the 1st. Every day you wait gives him time to cool off, get busy, or talk to a competitor. Adding a business case doc to a 4,200/yr purchase can also signal that the deal is bigger or riskier than it is, and he may start second-guessing.
- **Bringing in the office manager and techs is asking for a veto.** They didn't ask to be involved. Pulling them in before he signs can create objections that don't exist now.

**What I'd do:**
1. Send the order form today, with a short note confirming the start date of the 1st.
2. Include a simple next-steps list with onboarding date, data or account setup you need from him, and a kickoff call. That's a light version of a mutual action plan, and it keeps the start date on track.
3. After he signs, ask him to bring the office manager and one senior tech to the kickoff or onboarding. They're the ones who'll use the app daily, so involve them for adoption, not for approval.

**Where your manager has a point:** if the deal fails, it'll be at adoption, not signing. Dispatchers and techs who weren't involved can quietly stop using the app. That's a reason to run onboarding well, not to hold up the signature.

**How to put it to your manager:** "He's the sole decision-maker and asked for the form. I'm sending it with an onboarding plan, and I'll get the office manager and techs involved at kickoff so adoption is covered." That gives them the structure they want without putting the deal at risk.

One caveat: if there's something I don't know, like a contract that needs legal review, an integration, or Gus mentioning that his office manager runs the current system, adjust accordingly. Based on what you've told me, send it.
