**Send the form today.**

Gus is the signer, he's seen the product, and he told you what he wants: "this is what I need, send me the order form, we'll start the 1st." A mutual action plan, a business case, and a review with his office manager and two senior techs are all ways to help a buyer reach a decision. He's already made his. At $4,200/yr, a doc he didn't ask for gives him more to read and more reasons to put it off. Sending anything other than the form after he asked for it is the likeliest way to turn a yes into a stall.

**What to send**
- The order form, with a short note: "Here's the form. Once it's signed, I'll book setup so you're live on [start date]."
- Confirm which "1st" he means. Today is Nov 4, so I assume Dec 1. Put the exact date in the note and on the form.

**What to keep from your manager's instinct**
Their concern is real: dispatch software fails when the people using it aren't ready, not when the owner doesn't sign. You can cover that after the signature, without making it a condition of signing. In the same note, or on the signing call, ask for one thing:
- A 20-30 minute kickoff with his office manager and the senior techs, scheduled for a specific day before the start date. Name who will run it and what it covers (setup, how dispatch works day to day).

That gets you the people and the plan, and he's still the one who decides.

**When I'd change this**
- Gus says he needs to loop in his office manager or techs before signing. Then do it, because he's asked for it.
- The form isn't back by `[date you set with him]`. Then ask what's in the way. Don't send a business case or push a deadline.
- Anything from the demo suggests he hasn't agreed on the problem. You said he's sure, so I'm assuming he has.

**For your manager:** "He asked for the form and he's the signer. I'm sending it now and putting the kickoff with his team on the calendar before go-live. A business case for a buyer who's already decided adds delay." If they still want a business case, write it after he signs, as the onboarding and success doc.

I'm assuming the 11-van, $4,200/yr figures and the owner-signs-everything detail are right. I have no information on anything else about the account.
