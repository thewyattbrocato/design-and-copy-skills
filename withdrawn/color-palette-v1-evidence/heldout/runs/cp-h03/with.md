**Pick the deep indigo, #3a2e8c, and use the orange-red as a small accent.**

**Why indigo leads**
- **Website:** it has 10.8:1 contrast against white, so it works for text, links and buttons. Teal gets 3.3:1 and orange-red 3.1:1, and both fail for body-size text on white.
- **Flyers:** deep colors survive cheap school-run photocopying and printing better than mid-tones. Bright orange-red tends to dull when converted to CMYK.
- **Patches:** one dark thread color with white or orange stitching stays legible at small sizes. Mid teal gets muddy next to fine detail.
- **Kids' program, parents' trust:** indigo reads as capable and steady. The orange adds the energy, so the program doesn't look stern.

**What sits alongside it**

| Role | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lead (logo, headers, primary buttons) | `#3a2e8c` | White text on it is 10.8:1. |
| Accent (highlights, badges, icons, one call-to-action band per page) | `#ff5a36` | Roughly a kestrel's rusty plumage. It's 3.5:1 on indigo, so use it for graphics and large text, not small text. |
| Text | `#1d1a33` | Indigo-tinted near-black, around 15:1 on the off-white. Use it for body copy, and for any text that sits on orange (about 5.5:1). Don't put white on orange. |
| Page background | `#f7f6fb` | Off-white leaning slightly toward indigo. |
| Soft panel | `#e9e6f7` | A light indigo tint for cards and callouts. |

- **Proportions:** mostly off-white, indigo as the repeating color, and orange in small amounts. If orange covers more than about 10% of a page, it's too much.
- **Errors:** orange-red is close to a warning color, so don't use it for form errors. Use a separate deeper red (`#b3261e`) with an icon and a plain-language message.
- **Bird logo:** use it in indigo on light grounds and in white on indigo. Reserve an orange version for one-off uses such as stickers.

**By medium**
- **Website:** use these values as given. Buttons should be indigo with white text. Orange can be a highlight stripe or badge, not a button that holds small white text.
- **Flyers:** limit them to indigo, orange and white. Ask the printer for a proof of the orange before the full run, because it will shift from screen.
- **Patches:** limit them to three thread colors (indigo, orange, white). Have the embroiderer match each to the nearest thread chart color, and expect a close match rather than an exact one.

**Assumptions**
- The logo is a single flat color and can be recolored.
- Flyers are printed in CMYK, not spot inks.
- The contrast ratios for the near-black text and the soft panel are my estimates. The indigo, teal and orange ratios were calculated from the hex values.
