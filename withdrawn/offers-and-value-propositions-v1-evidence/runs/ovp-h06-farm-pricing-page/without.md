# Pricing built for small farms

Ledgerleaf is bookkeeping software for small farms. It tracks sales by market stall or CSA, expenses by field or crop, and mileage. At year end it produces a summary your accountant can open, with CSV and PDF export. Choose the plan that fits your farm, and try it free for 30 days before you pay anything.

## The plans

### Seedling: $15/month
For a single farm getting its books in order.
- 1 farm
- Up to 200 transactions a month
- Sales and expense tracking

### Harvest: $34/month
For farms that want to know which crops earn their keep.
- Unlimited transactions
- Profit reports per crop
- Mileage log
- 2 users

### Co-op: $79/month
For farms with a bigger team, CSA members to bill, or both.
- Up to 10 users
- CSA member billing
- A login for your accountant

## Pay for 10 months, get 12

Choose annual billing and you pay for 10 months and get 12. That works out to two months free on any plan:

- Seedling: $150 a year
- Harvest: $340 a year
- Co-op: $790 a year

If you know you'll keep your books through the whole season, annual billing is the better deal.

## Try it free for 30 days

Start with a 30-day free trial. You don't need a credit card. During the trial you get every Harvest feature: unlimited transactions, profit reports per crop, the mileage log, and two users. You can enter real sales and expenses and see how Ledgerleaf fits the way you run your farm before you choose a plan.

## Frequently asked questions

### How does billing work?
Monthly plans are billed at the monthly price shown above. Annual plans are billed as one payment covering 12 months for the price of 10. The free trial needs no credit card, so you won't be charged unless you decide to continue.

### Can I cancel?
Yes, any time. There are no contracts to wait out and no cancellation hoops to jump through.

### What happens to my data if I leave?
It's yours. You can export all of your data at any time, whether you're staying, switching plans, or leaving.

### Do you offer refunds?
Monthly payments are not refundable. If you're unsure, use the 30-day trial first. It's free and has all the Harvest features, so you can see exactly what you'd be paying for.

### What's not included?
Ledgerleaf is bookkeeping software, and there are three things it doesn't do:

- **Payroll.** Ledgerleaf doesn't run payroll.
- **Tax filing.** We prepare your year-end summary, but we don't file your taxes. That's a job for you or your accountant.
- **Phone support.** Support is by email, and we answer within one business day.

### Can my accountant see my books?
Every plan can export a year-end summary as CSV or PDF, which any accountant can open. On the Co-op plan, your accountant also gets their own login.
