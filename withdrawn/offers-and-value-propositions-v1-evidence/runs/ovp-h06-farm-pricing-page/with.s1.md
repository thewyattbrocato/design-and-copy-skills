# Bookkeeping that keeps up with your farm

Ledgerleaf is bookkeeping software for small farms. It tracks sales by market stall or CSA, expenses by field or crop, and your mileage. At year end it makes a summary your accountant can open, with CSV and PDF export. You keep your records as you go, so you don't have to rebuild them from receipts and a spreadsheet each winter.

Try everything free for 30 days. You don't need a credit card.

## Pick your plan

### Seedling: $15/month
**For one farm with a modest number of sales and expenses.**
- 1 farm
- Up to 200 transactions a month
- Sales and expense tracking

Seedling doesn't include per-crop profit reports, the mileage log, or a second user. Those start with Harvest.

### Harvest: $34/month (our pick for most farms)
**For farms that pass 200 transactions a month, or want to know which crops earn their keep.**
- Unlimited transactions
- Profit reports per crop
- Mileage log
- 2 users

We recommend Harvest because it removes the monthly transaction cap and adds the reports that show profit crop by crop. It's also the plan you'll try during your free trial, so you can see whether you use those features before you pay.

### Co-op: $79/month
**For farms with a bigger team, CSA members to bill, or an accountant who wants to look in directly.**
- Up to 10 users
- CSA member billing
- A login for your accountant
