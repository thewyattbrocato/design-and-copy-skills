## FAQ

**How does billing work?**
Monthly plans renew on the same day each month. Annual plans renew once per year and give you two extra months of access. Payments are processed securely; you can update your payment method anytime in your account settings.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. Cancel whenever you want—no penalties, no questions. Your access continues through the end of your billing period. You can also export all your data at any time, whether you're leaving or just backing up your records.

**What's not included?**
Ledgerleaf handles bookkeeping, not payroll processing or tax filing. We also don't offer phone support, but our email support team typically responds within one business day. If you need help with tax strategy or complex deductions, you'll want to loop in your accountant—that's what the year-end exports are for.

**What if I'm not happy?**
Cancellations on monthly plans are final—we don't offer refunds. But that's exactly why we include a full 30-day free trial with Harvest features. Use it to test whether Ledgerleaf fits your workflow. If you switch plans during your billing cycle, we'll prorate any difference.
