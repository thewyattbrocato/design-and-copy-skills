## Questions About Billing & Plans

**Can I change plans anytime?**

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade any time. Changes take effect on your next billing date. If you upgrade mid-month, we'll prorate the difference.

**What if I need to cancel?**

You can cancel anytime. Your access continues through the end of your billing period. You can export all your data anytime—before you cancel, during your subscription, or after.

**Are there refunds?**

No refunds on monthly payments. Annual plans renew automatically unless you cancel beforehand.

**What's not included?**

We don't handle payroll or tax filing—those are jobs for specialists. We also don't offer phone support; we answer email within one business day. What we *do* handle is clean bookkeeping that makes tax time easier.

**Do I need all Harvest features?**

The trial comes with everything in Harvest so you can test the full experience. If you only need Seedling features, you can downgrade after the trial ends.

**Can my accountant see my data?**

Yes. With the Co-op plan, you can create a login for your accountant—they'll have read-only access to all your records. If you're on Seedling or Harvest, you export your data and send it to them.
