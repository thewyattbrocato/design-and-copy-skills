# Bookkeeping that grows at the pace of your farm

Ledgerleaf is bookkeeping software for small farms. Track what you sell, what you spend, and how far you drive. Come tax time, hand your accountant a clean summary instead of a shoebox of receipts.

Every plan includes simple pricing, a 30-day free trial, and the freedom to leave whenever you like.

## What Ledgerleaf does

- **Sales**, tracked by market stall or CSA
- **Expenses**, tracked by field or crop
- **Mileage**, so the trips to market and the feed store count
- **A year-end summary** your accountant can open, with CSV and PDF export

## Frequently asked questions

**How does billing work?**
You can pay monthly or annually. Monthly plans are billed each month at the plan price. Annual billing covers twelve months for the price of ten. Please note that we don't offer refunds on monthly payments.

**Can I cancel?**
Yes, any time. You're never locked in. You can also export all of your data at any time, whether or not you're cancelling, so your records are always yours.

**What's not included?**
Ledgerleaf doesn't do payroll or tax filing. It's built to keep your farm's books and give your accountant what they need, not to replace them. There's also no phone support. Support is by email, and we answer within one business day.

**Which plan should I start with?**
The free trial gives you all Harvest features, so you can try the fullest everyday setup first. Then pick the plan that fits.

**Can my accountant get in?**
On the Co-op plan, your accountant gets their own login. On any plan, you can export the year-end summary as a CSV or PDF and send it along.
