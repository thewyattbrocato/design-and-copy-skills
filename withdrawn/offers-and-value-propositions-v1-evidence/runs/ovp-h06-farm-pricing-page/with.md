# Bookkeeping for small farms, priced by the month

Ledgerleaf tracks your sales by market stall or CSA, your expenses by field or crop, and your mileage. At year's end it makes a summary your accountant can open, as CSV or PDF. Try it free for 30 days with no credit card, then pick the plan that fits your farm.

## Three plans

**Seedling: $15/month**
For one farm with a modest number of sales and expenses. You get sales and expense tracking for 1 farm, up to 200 transactions a month. It doesn't include per-crop profit reports, the mileage log or a second user. Those start on Harvest.

**Harvest: $34/month**
For a farm that wants to know which crops pay. You get everything in Seedling, plus unlimited transactions, profit reports per crop, a mileage log and 2 users. We suggest starting here: the free trial includes all Harvest features, so you can see what the crop reports show about your own numbers before you decide.

**Co-op: $79/month**
For farms with a team, a CSA, or both. You get up to 10 users, CSA member billing and a login for your accountant, so they can look at the books themselves.

All plans export to CSV and PDF, and you can export all your data at any time.

## The annual deal

Pay yearly and you pay for 10 months and get 12.

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly (pay for 10, get 12) |

The yearly price is the monthly price times ten. That's the whole reason for the discount: you pay for the year up front, so we give you two months.
