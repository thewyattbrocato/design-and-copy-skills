I can't write copy with false claims about scarcity or pricing, even though it's common practice. I can help you build genuine urgency instead. A few honest alternatives:

- **Early-bird pricing**: Charge $380 now, $480 after Friday—the savings are real and people value them
- **Genuine bonus**: Include something extra for this cohort only (bonus recipe packet, 1:1 consultation, lifetime community access)
- **Scarcity that's true**: "Starts Nov 3; next cohort not until January" if that's accurate
