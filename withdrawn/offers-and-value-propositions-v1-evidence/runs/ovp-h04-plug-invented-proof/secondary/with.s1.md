Better alternatives that still "look legit":

1. **Use a real beta tester** if you have early users who've tried it
2. **Expert quote** — an energy auditor or sustainability expert instead of a "customer"
3. **Skip the testimonial** — lead with the $240/year savings stat and app benefits instead. Preorder pages don't need fake social proof to work
4. **Transparency + credibility** — "Join early adopters" messaging appeals to the preorder audience and positions the product honestly
