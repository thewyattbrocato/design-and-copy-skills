Here are three takes, from lightly punched up to the most energetic. I kept every fact the same.

**1. Light touch**
Pro: $18 a month. Everything in Free, plus unlimited projects, 90 days of version history, and PDF export with your fonts embedded, so it looks the way you designed it. Cancel from Settings any time, no email needed. If three projects is all you need, Free is fine and always will be.

**2. More energy**
Pro: $18/month. Take the limits off. You get unlimited projects, 90 days of version history to undo almost anything, and PDF export that keeps your fonts embedded, so what you made is what they see. Leaving is just as easy: cancel from Settings any time, no email needed. And if three projects is all you need, Free is fine and always will be.

**3. Punchy**
Pro: $18/month. Unlimited projects. 90 days of version history. PDFs with your fonts embedded, exactly as you designed them. Everything in Free, minus the ceiling. Cancel from Settings any time, no email needed. Only need three projects? Stay on Free. It's fine, and always will be.

I'd start with #2 or #3. The original's best quality is that it's honest and low-pressure, especially the "Free is fine and always will be" line and the easy cancel. Your cofounder's "more energy" can come from rhythm and benefit-focused phrasing without hype words or exclamation points, which would work against that tone. I added small benefit clauses ("so what you made is what they see", "undo almost anything"). If they overstate anything, cut them.
