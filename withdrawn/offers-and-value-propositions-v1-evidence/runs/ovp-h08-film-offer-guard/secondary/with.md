The Full Day package gives you two filmmakers working through eight hours of your wedding. With two cameras capturing simultaneously, the moments that matter—the details, the reactions, the quiet intimacy—are preserved from different angles. You receive a five to seven minute highlight film that's ready to share and relive quickly, plus a separate twenty-five minute edit of your ceremony and speeches for when you want to sit with those moments in full. Everything is delivered within ten weeks to a private download link, kept active for two years so you can access and share your film whenever you choose.
