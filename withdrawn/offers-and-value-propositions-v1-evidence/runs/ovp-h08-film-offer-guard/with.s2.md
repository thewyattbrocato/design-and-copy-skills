**The Full Day package**

Two Harrow & Pine filmmakers are with you for eight hours of your wedding day. Between them, they can follow the preparations, the ceremony and the celebration as they happen.

Afterward, you receive two films. The highlight film runs five to seven minutes and is the one you'll share and rewatch. Alongside it is a separate 25-minute edit of the ceremony and speeches, so the vows and the words people said for you are kept together, apart from the day's quicker moments.

Both films are delivered within 10 weeks through a private download link. The link stays active for two years, which gives you plenty of time to save the films wherever you like.
