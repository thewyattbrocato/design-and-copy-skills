I can help. Quick clarifying question: **what's the main problem you're solving?** Are bookstores struggling to:
- Organize and manage the logistics of events?
- Get people to *know about* their events?
- Make events profitable/drive book sales?
- Something else?

The answer shapes the best pitch. For now, here are two directions:

**If it's about ease/management:**
- Spoken: "We make event management software for independent bookstores — handles the scheduling, promotion, and logistics of readings, book clubs, and author events."
- Site: "Event management software built for indie bookstores."

**If it's about discovery/community:**
- Spoken: "We help independent bookstores promote their events so they can build loyal reading communities and sell more books."
- Site: "Help indie bookstores grow their author events and book clubs."

The first is feature-clear; the second leads with outcome. Which is closer to what you actually do and why people should care?
