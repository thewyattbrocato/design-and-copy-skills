**One-liner (to say at a meetup):**

"We make event management software for independent bookstores. It handles registrations, attendee coordination, and logistics for author readings, book signings, and book clubs—so your staff can focus on hosting great events instead of managing spreadsheets."

**For your website:**

"Event management for independent bookstores"

---

To sharpen this further: the positioning works, but it currently differentiates by *who* it's for rather than by a specific problem solved better than alternatives. When someone at a meetup asks "wait, what problem does this actually solve?"—is the core issue that bookstore staff are spending hours on spreadsheets and email chains, or is it that general event platforms like Eventbrite are built for massive venues and don't understand bookstore-scale events, author coordination, or recurring book clubs?

That distinction would tighten both lines. If it's the latter, you'd emphasize "built for bookstore events" over generic "event management." If it's the first, you'd lead with time savings.
