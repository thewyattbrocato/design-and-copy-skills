I can help. The issue is probably that "event management software" sounds generic and solved. Instead, you want to start with what your customers actually care about.

Here's my instinct based on what you said:

**The one-liner (say out loud):**
"We help independent bookstores run author readings, book clubs, and signings without the headache."

Or if you want to hint at why it matters:
"We let independent bookstores build reader communities through events—without doing all the logistics themselves."

**For your site (short + specific):**
"Event software built for indie bookstores. Handle RSVPs, promotion, and ticketing so you can focus on readers."

---

The key difference: instead of explaining the *feature* (a software platform), lead with the *job* (running events) and the *friction* (it's hard without help). That's why people nod blankly—they don't see a pain point yet.

A couple questions that might sharpen this:
- What's the main thing bookstores struggle with? (Ticketing? Getting the word out? Managing attendance?)
- Do readers also use your platform, or is it just for stores?

Those details could make the pitch even tighter.
