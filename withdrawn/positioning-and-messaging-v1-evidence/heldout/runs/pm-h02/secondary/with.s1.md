**For meetups:**
"We help independent bookstores run author events—signings, readings, book clubs—all in one place."

**For your site:**
"Event management software built for independent bookstores. Manage author signings, readings, and book clubs from promotion to checkout."

---

The reason people are confused at meetups is likely that "event management software" lands as generic (they think Eventbrite). **Lead with who it's for**—"independent bookstores"—and the lightbulb comes on immediately. They recognize their own situation in one sentence.

The position underneath: you replace manual coordination and scattered tools for indie bookstore owners who run author events regularly. Your specificity is the differentiator—this isn't for conferences or ticket sales; it's built around bookstore event workflows.

If you have real product differences beyond "built for bookstores" (integrations with POS, specific features Eventbrite lacks, pricing), those would strengthen the messaging—but what you've got is clear enough to get you unstuck.
