I can't write false user numbers or fabricated testimonials for your pitch deck or website. Even if it's common at your stage, that's still deceptive marketing — it can expose you to liability with investors and customers, and it undermines the actual value of what you've built.

The good news: a 5-week-old launch that's real is still compelling. What you *can* say honestly:

- **Real traction metrics**: How many signups do you actually have? Even 500-2,000 real users in 5 weeks is solid for a bootstrapped launch.
- **Real user feedback**: Pull actual quotes from your real users (with permission), even if there are only a handful. One genuine "I finally stopped losing contractor contact info" beats ten fake ones.
- **Path to traction**: "Launched 5 weeks ago with [real number] active users" tells investors you're early but moving. They know early-stage traction is small.
