# Voice and Tone Guide for Marrow & Co

**Our voice reflects who we are: former restaurant people helping restaurant people with their numbers. We're knowledgeable without being academic, direct without being cold, and clear about what we actually do.**

## Practical, Not Theoretical

**The habit:** We talk about what works in restaurants, not abstract principles. We know your constraints—tight margins, seasonal swings, staff turnover, the rush before a big event. We speak to your situation, not a textbook one.

- **Say:** "Your January was quiet. March spiked. That's normal. Here's how to plan for it."
- **Don't say:** "Seasonal variance requires proactive cash flow modeling to optimize liquidity."

## Plain-Spoken, Not Fancy

**The habit:** We explain our thinking in words you'd use with your manager or your business partner. No jargon. No "solutions" or "leverage." When we need a technical term, we define it on first use or we find a plainer one.

- **Say:** "This is where you're losing money. We'll show you why and what to do about it."
- **Don't say:** "We'll provide granular visibility into margin compression across cost centers."

## Respectful, Not Condescending

**The habit:** We remember that you know your business better than we ever will. We're here to make sense of the numbers, not to tell you how to run your restaurant. We assume you're smart; we just aren't sure accounting makes sense yet.

- **Say:** "Walk me through how you count that. I want to make sure we're tracking it right."
- **Don't say:** "Most restaurant owners don't understand their P&L. Let us explain."

## Direct, Not Clever

**The habit:** We tell you what we do and why you need it. We don't dress it up in trends or hype. A short sentence beats a long one. A real number beats a vague claim.

- **Say:** "Bookkeeping takes time you don't have. We do it. Then we walk you through what the numbers mean."
- **Don't say:** "We empower data-driven decision-making for hospitality leaders."

## On Tone Shifts

Our voice stays the same across channels. But we adjust the tone:
- **Errors and problems** → more serious and clear (never blame you; help you fix it)
- **Updates and regular reports** → slightly warmer, because you know us by then
- **Explaining a tough number** → slower, more patient, brief sentences
