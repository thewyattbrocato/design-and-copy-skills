I'll write the positioning and the headline, but not the "only 3 desks left" line, the Friday deadline, or "#1 in the city." Since you know there are about 20 open desks and the rate doesn't actually end, those claims are false. Fake scarcity and fake deadlines are deceptive advertising, and they backfire with this audience. Freelancers talk to each other, and one person noticing the "3 left" banner never changes will cost you more than the urgency gains. "#1" with no ranking behind it is a claim a chain two blocks away can challenge.

**Urgency that's true**

- **Make the founding rate real.** For example: "Founding rate: lock in $X/month for as long as you stay. Available to the first 25 members." Then actually cap it, or set a real end date. That version is honest and still pushes people to act.
- **Use the real desk count.** "20 desks open, and they fill by the month" works if that's true. Real numbers read as confident.
- **Lower the cost of acting.** "Book a free day pass this week" or "Tour the space Thursday" gives people a reason to act now without a false deadline.
