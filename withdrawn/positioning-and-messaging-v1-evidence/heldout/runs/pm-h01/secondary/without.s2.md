## Tidewell: Positioning Brief

**What We Are**

Tidewell is operations software for small commercial laundries. We make it fast and simple to track customer loads from drop-off to delivery, text customers when their order is ready, and bill accurately—whether by weight, by piece, or with custom rates. The software runs on a tablet at the counter and a phone in the delivery van, works offline, and syncs automatically when you're back online.

**Who It's For**

Small commercial laundries—wash-and-fold shops, hotel and spa linen contractors, linen services for restaurants and medical offices—typically 3 to 25 employees. These are often owner-operated or owner-managed businesses where the person running operations is also making deliveries, troubleshooting customer issues, and handling the counter. They might have one or two staff members. They're growing steadily but don't need enterprise software.

**What They're Up Against Today**

Most of these shops still operate on paper tickets stacked by customer name, with customer data scattered across a spreadsheet and pricing stored in someone's head or a printed rate card. A few use a point-of-sale system built for retail—so you get inventory tracking you don't need and delivery tracking you do need but have to hack together. The result: customers don't know when their order is ready, drivers waste time hunting for bags, pricing errors slip through, and the owner gets a call at 7pm asking "where's my order?"

**Why Tidewell Is Different**

We built this for laundry operations, not retail checkouts. Every feature exists because we watched it matter in real shops.

Barcode tracking means no more guessing which bag belongs to which customer. One scan at drop-off, one scan at delivery—the system knows where every load is. Customers get a text when it's ready. Drivers get a phone app showing them exactly what's packed in the van and where to drop it off, and they work offline so a spotty connection doesn't stop the route.

Flexible pricing means you can bill the way your shop actually works: by weight for standard loads, by piece for specialty work, with different rates for different customers. The system calculates it so you don't.

And it syncs later. You don't need perfect internet at the counter or in the van. You work offline, and when you're back at WiFi, Tidewell catches up. That's built in from the ground up.

**Why We're Positioned to Do This Right**

Tidewell was founded by two people who ran a commercial laundry for six years. They weren't software engineers first; they were laundry operators first. They know the shape of the problem because they lived it. They built this to solve the problems they actually had, and they're still talking to customers every week to understand what comes next. That founder-operator perspective matters. You're not talking to salespeople or consultants. You're talking to people who've been you.

**Key Things We Say About Ourselves**

- **Built for offline work.** Laundries operate in the physical world. We assumed flaky internet from day one.

- **Barcode tracking isn't fancy—it's essential.** A barcode tag solves the central problem: which bag is which. Everything else is layered on top of that.

- **Operator-friendly, not engineer-friendly.** Menu hierarchies are shallow. Common tasks take two taps. If it takes training, it's broken.

- **You own your data.** We export everything to CSV so you can move it anytime. This is your business.

- **This is the beginning.** We're launching with the core: tracking, pricing, notifications. Payroll and accounting integrations are on the roadmap because we hear it repeatedly. We're not trying to boil the ocean day one.

- **Small is the point.** We're building for 3-to-25-person shops, not trying to scale to 500. Different business, different software.
