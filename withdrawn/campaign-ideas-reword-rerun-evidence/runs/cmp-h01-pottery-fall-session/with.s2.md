**The pick: "Not creative? Perfect. That's who this class is for."**

Most people who email you open with "I'm not creative," so the campaign says it first and treats it as the starting point. Beginners recognize themselves in it, and the usual "unleash your creativity" ad doesn't speak to them.

**Three forms (each works alone)**
1. **Post or poster:** A big line reading "I'm not creative." with "Tuesdays, 6:30 to 9. Six weeks. Wheel throwing for people who've never touched a wheel." underneath. Then the facts: 8 seats, $240, clay and firing of 4 pieces included, starts Oct 21.
2. **Email reply:** A short template for the "I'm not creative" messages you already get. It says that's what most people write before they sign up, and that two in three of last spring's students (31 of 46) signed up again. Send it as a reply and add it to the registration confirmation.
3. **In the studio:** On the first night, everyone's first pot goes on one shelf with no names. A photo of that shelf is the spring-to-fall follow-up post: [add: photo, with students' OK].

**Needs from you**
- Ask the owner: can you photograph the first-night shelf, and will students agree to appear?
- The "most people say this" line is true by your account, so keep it as you told me. Don't add specific quotes unless you have each writer's permission.

**Other ways to go (one line each)**
- **Two-in-three came back:** Lead with "31 of 46 signed up again." It's strong proof, but it speaks to people already sold on the idea, not the hesitant.
- **A week, not three:** Lead with the second kiln: "Your pot comes back in about a week." It's practical, but it reads as a feature.
- **Eight stools:** Portray the small room and the eight seats. It's warm and true, but a small class is the only hook.
- **Upstairs from the hardware store:** A Tuesday-night, Dunmore Street, park-behind-the-building tone. It's charming and local but doesn't give a reason to sign up.

**Plain fallback:** A clear info post using the facts above, with "31 of 46 came back" as the proof line. It needs nothing new from you.

**Two things to check before you launch**
- Oct 21, 2026 is a Wednesday, not a Tuesday. Confirm the start date or the day.
- Registration opens next week, and Oct 21 is also next week. Make sure people get enough lead time to sign up.
