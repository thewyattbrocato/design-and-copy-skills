The concept is sound. The headline carries the "pleasure of trading" idea without mentioning money, and "No seeds to trade? Come anyway" keeps newcomers from feeling shut out. But check the date before printing.

**Date problem**
- Today is Oct 15, 2026, and Saturday, March 14 was in 2026, so that date has already passed.
- If the swap is March 2027, March 14 is a Sunday. The Saturday would be March 13.
- Either the day or the date is probably wrong. Confirm which is right and add the year, or at least check it against the venue booking.

**Smaller notes**
- **"Free" in the subline:** It works against "not about saving money" only slightly, and people need to know there's no cost. I'd keep it, but move it later, e.g. "Alder Park Seed Swap: Saturday, March 13, 10 am to 1 pm, by the pavilion. Free." That lets the headline's tone lead.
- **"Seeds you saved" vs. "Seed packets from last year":** These could read as two different things. Something like "Bring saved seeds or leftover packets from last year, labeled if you can" would be clearer.
- **"Pocketful":** It's charming, but it implies small amounts. If people might bring larger lots, the small print could say "any amount is welcome."
- **Location:** "By the pavilion" is fine for regulars. If the park has more than one pavilion, or the flyer will go outside the neighborhood, add a street or cross-street.
- **Contact:** There's no website, email, or social handle for questions or weather changes. If the swap is outdoors in March, a rain plan or a place to check for updates would help.

The headline, tone, and footer need no changes. Fix the date, and the rest is optional polish.
