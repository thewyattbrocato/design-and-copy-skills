I won't write the "only 200 exist, sells out by Friday" claim or the per-visitor restarting timer. Neither is true, and both are the kind of fake scarcity and fake deadline the FTC treats as deceptive. They also tend to backfire for a small brand. Customers notice when the timer resets, and a candle company runs on trust and repeat buyers.

You have two real levers instead:

- **A real deadline.** Dec 10 is a real order-by date. Put a countdown to it on the page, with the same end time for every visitor.
- **A real cap.** If you want "200 boxes," set 200 as an actual limit and close orders when you hit it. You can raise it later and say so honestly ("we added 100 more").

The copy below uses the real Dec 10 deadline. Brackets are for you to fill in.

## Campaign idea: "Light the Season"

Position the box as the gift that's already done: a made-to-order, hand-poured box, so the deadline is about pouring time. Real urgency comes from the fact that each box is made after you order it, and Dec 10 is the last day that guarantees delivery before the holidays.

## Landing page hero

**Eyebrow:** Holiday Gift Box · Poured to order

**Headline:** Poured for you. Wrapped for them.

**Subhead:** Each Hollow & Hill holiday box is hand-poured after you order it. [Describe: 2 candles + match strike + ribbon, scents]. Order by **December 10** and it arrives before the holidays.

**Countdown (same for everyone):** Order-by deadline: 56 days · 04 hrs · 12 min

**Primary CTA:** Order your box
**Secondary line:** Made to order. Ships within [X] days. Free gift note.

**Optional honest scarcity line:** We pour in small batches, so late-season orders may ship later. Dec 10 is our guarantee date.
