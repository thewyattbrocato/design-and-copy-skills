Here are three versions that keep "Rent less, roam more" intact. I counted each one including spaces.

1. **Rent less, roam more: weekend deals** (35)
2. **Rent less, roam more. Deals Saturday!** (37)
3. **Rent less, roam more: Sat. car deals** (37)

I'd go with #1. It's the cleanest and leaves 5 characters of headroom in case a platform adds a prefix or truncates. The cost is that it drops the "this Saturday" timing. If the date matters, #2 keeps it, but it loses "weekend" and "car."
