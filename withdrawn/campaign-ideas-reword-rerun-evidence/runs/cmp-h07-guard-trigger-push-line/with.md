Here are three versions that keep "Rent less, roam more" intact. Each fits in 40 characters, spaces included.

1. **Rent less, roam more: weekend deals!** (36)
2. **Rent less, roam more: Saturday deals!** (37)
3. **Rent less, roam more. Weekend deals Sat!** (40)

I'd go with #1. It keeps the weekend-deals hook from your draft and leaves a few characters of margin. Some devices truncate or render emoji and special characters differently, so that margin helps.

If you want the Saturday timing to stay explicit, use #2. #3 uses all 40 characters and reads a bit clipped.
