**The idea: "Bank first, then budget."**
Tally Nest launches by saying what every freelancer already knows: a monthly budget is a fiction when your income isn't monthly. The campaign shows the app's one different move, which is that you budget only what's already in the bank.

Sample line: *"Budget what you've banked, not what you're hoping for."*

**Three forms** (each works if the others never run)

1. **Short demo video (15–30 sec, founders on camera or screen recording).** Start with a normal budgeting app showing "$4,000 income this month" on a month where the client hasn't paid. Cut to Tally Nest showing "$0 banked so far, here's what's safe to spend." Close with: "A budget for the money you actually have." Needs: a screen recording of the real app, using only real features.

2. **"Banked so far" counter as a shareable.** A simple web page or in-app share card where a freelancer enters what they've banked this month and gets a plain read: covered rent, groceries, and what's left. It works without the app, and the share card carries the Tally Nest name. Needs: the owner to confirm the app's logic can be mirrored on a simple page. If not, use a static screenshot template.

3. **Direct outreach to freelancer communities (Nov 3).** A short, honest post for the places freelancers already talk about money: "We're two people who built this because monthly budgets kept lying to us. Free for the first month, $6/mo after." Needs: [add: the real origin story, one or two sentences] and posting only where community rules allow it, as yourselves and disclosed as the makers.

**What it needs from you**
- Ask the owner: do the beta testers include freelancers who'd let you quote them or show their story, with consent and credit? One real line beats any claim from us. I've left that as [add: real beta tester line].
- Ask the owner: what's the founder story? Is at least one of you a freelancer? That's the strongest hook if true. [assumed: it is]
- No numbers are used. Once you pull beta data (for example, how many people stuck with it past week one), it can go in, but only if it's real.

**Plain fallback**
Skip the shareable and run only forms 1 and 3: one demo video and one honest founder post, with the same line, "Budget what you've banked." It needs a screen recording and about an hour of writing.

**Two notes**
- The buyer truth here is [assumed]: freelancers with lumpy income find monthly budgets don't fit. Check it against what beta users actually said before leaning on it.
- Keep the pricing plain everywhere: "Free for a month, then $6/mo." Don't use urgency or launch-day scarcity unless there's a real deadline.
