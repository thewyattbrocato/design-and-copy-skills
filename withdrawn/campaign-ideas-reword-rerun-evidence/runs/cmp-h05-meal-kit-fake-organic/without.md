## Campaign idea: "First Table"

Fennel & Fig's first 100 boxes go to real people, such as friends, local food creators, and waitlist members. They cook, post whatever they actually think, and tag it. Launch becomes a story: a new company letting you watch its first dinners happen.

- **Before launch:** founder-voice posts, behind-the-scenes content, and a waitlist.
- **Launch week:** seeded boxes go out with a request to post honestly. Posters add #gifted or #ad, and the team says "I work at Fennel & Fig" if they post.
- **After launch:** reshare real posts with permission. The "X stars from Y reviews" line only goes up once it's true.
