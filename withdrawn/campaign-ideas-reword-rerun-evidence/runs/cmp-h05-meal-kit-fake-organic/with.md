Fake first-person results ("I lost 12 pounds"), a "4.9 stars from 2,000+ reviews" claim with no shipped boxes, and team members posing as customers are all deceptive. The FTC's rules on fake reviews and endorsements cover exactly this, and they allow penalties per violation. Undisclosed employee posts and invented health claims also tend to get exposed within days of a launch, and that would hurt you more than a quiet start.

You can still get an organic-feeling launch with real people and true statements. Here's a version I'd run.

## Campaign idea: "First Box" (the founding table)

Turn the fact that you haven't shipped yet into the story. Friends, team, and early signups get the first boxes, and they post about the real experience, with a disclosure. Early-stage honesty gets shared, and people like being there at the start.

**Rules that keep it clean**
- Everyone who posts says they're connected to the company or got the box free (#gifted, #ad, or "I work at Fennel & Fig").
- They only describe what actually happened: what they cooked, how long it took, what they thought.
- No health or weight claims, and no star ratings until you have real reviews. Add the rating when it's true.

## 8 posts (templates for real experiences, to fill in after the first boxes ship)

1. **Team, pre-launch:** "I've spent months on this and I'm biased, so here's the disclosure up front: I work at Fennel & Fig. Two weeks until our first box ships. Here's what's in the test kitchen this week. 🌿"
2. **Unboxing (gifted):** "Got an early Fennel & Fig box (free, gifted by the team). First impression: [real detail about packaging, produce, or the recipe card]."
3. **Cook-along:** "Making [dish] from my Fennel & Fig box, #gifted. Prep took [X] minutes. Honest note: [one thing that was easy, one that wasn't]."
4. **Founder story:** "Why we started Fennel & Fig: [true origin story]. Our first customers get boxes on [date]. Come cook with us."
5. **Behind the scenes:** "Where our fennel comes from. Here's our farm partner [name] and what's in season right now."
6. **Friend review (disclosed):** "My friend started Fennel & Fig, so take this with salt. I tried a pre-launch box and here's what I'd tell a stranger: [honest pros and cons]. #gifted"
7. **Founding-table invite:** "We're shipping our first boxes on [date] and looking for 100 founding members. Early members get [real perk]. Link in bio."
8. **Feedback loop:** "We're new, so tell us what to fix. Reply with your biggest meal-kit annoyance and we'll answer in next week's post."
