# Spoke Yard Fall Campaign: "Bring Us Your Flat"

## The idea

The most common first visit to Spoke Yard is a flat tire, according to our volunteer sign-in sheet. The campaign starts from that. A flat is the smallest repair, the most familiar one, and the one that most often strands someone with a bike they like but can't ride.

**"Bring us your flat."** is the headline. The follow-up line is **"Learn to fix it. Then keep going."**

Our fall goal is 100 members by December 31. We have 64, so we need **36 more**. The campaign uses that number openly. Asking people to help close a specific gap is more concrete than asking them to "join us," and it lets members and followers see progress.

The call to action is always the same: come in on a Wednesday or Saturday, bring the bike, and sign up for $9 a month.

## Why it fits us

- **It's true to how people actually find us.** The flat-tire line comes from our own sign-in sheet, not from a slogan we invented.
- **It lowers the stakes.** A flat is an easy first problem to bring. Beginners get a 30-minute walkthrough on their first visit, so someone who has never touched a tool is covered.
- **It shows what $9 buys.** Membership includes the stand and tools during open hours, plus free pick of the donated used parts shelf. The flat-tire story naturally leads to "and here's the rest of what you get."
- **It's honest about who we are.** Every instructor is a volunteer and there's no staff. We say so plainly and treat it as a reason to trust us: the people helping you are here because they want to be.
- **It uses the goal as a hook.** "36 to go" gives the campaign a visible finish line, and we can update it as members join.

## How it shows up

### Poster

- **Headline:** Bring us your flat.
- **Image:** A single bike wheel on our repair stand, with the tire off. A simple photo or bold illustration works. Use a real shot from the shop if we have one.
- **Body copy:** *Learn to fix it yourself. $9 a month gets you the stand, the tools, and a free pick from our shelf of donated parts. First visit includes a 30-minute walkthrough.*
- **Footer:** Wednesdays & Saturdays, 10–4 · Back of the old laundromat on Pell Street · 64 members. Help us reach 100 by Dec 31.

Keep it to one image and one message so it reads from across a street or on a crowded bulletin board.

### Email

- **Subject line options:** "Bring us your flat" or "36 more members by December 31"
- **Opening:** Name the most common first visit and say that's why we're writing.
- **Middle:** Three short points: what $9 a month includes, that beginners get a 30-minute walkthrough, and that everyone helping is a volunteer.
- **Close:** State the goal, 64 members now and 100 by December 31, then ask the reader to come by on a Wednesday or Saturday. Invite current members to bring a friend.
- **One link or reply prompt** only, so the next step is obvious.

We have no member quotes cleared for use, so the email carries no testimonials. It relies on the facts above.

### Instagram post

A carousel works well here:

1. **Slide 1:** Bold text, "Bring us your flat."
2. **Slide 2:** "The most common first visit at Spoke Yard? A flat tire. (We checked the sign-in sheet.)"
3. **Slide 3:** What $9 a month gets you: the stand, the tools, free used parts.
4. **Slide 4:** "First visit? You get a 30-minute walkthrough."
5. **Slide 5:** "64 members. We want 100 by Dec 31. Wednesdays & Saturdays, 10–4, Pell Street."

Caption: a short, friendly version of slide 5 with the hours and location. Later posts can update the count ("Now at __ of 100"), using real numbers only.

### Shop window

The window is where the campaign becomes physical. A real bike wheel with a flat tire hangs or leans in the window under the headline **"Bring us your flat."** Beside it, a hand-lettered or printed tally reads **"64 / 100"**, and we update it by hand as members join. People walking past can watch the goal get closer.

Below the tally, in smaller type, we add the hours and "$9 a month · Stand, tools & free used parts."

Because we're in the back of the old laundromat, the window and a clear sign pointing people to the entrance matter most. Anyone who stops to look should know where to go next.

## What we'd need from the board

- **A decision on the name and headline.** "Bring us your flat" is the working version.
- **A photo of the shop or stand,** or a volunteer willing to take one.
- **Someone to update the tally** in the window and online.
- **Permission to collect member quotes later.** None are cleared yet, so the campaign doesn't use any. If we get some before December, they'd strengthen the email and Instagram.
