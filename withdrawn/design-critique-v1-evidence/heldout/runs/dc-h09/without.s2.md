The page has no clear hierarchy. Almost every choice gives the same weight to everything, and where you do vary things, the variation looks accidental.

**Type: three headings that compete**
- The 28px headline, 26px date and 24px bold first sentence are nearly the same size and all bold. The eye has no clear first, second and third stop.
- The jump from 24px bold to 16px regular in one paragraph reads as a styling glitch, not an emphasis choice.
- Fix: make the headline much bigger (40–56px). Make the date a clear second tier, such as a lighter weight, a different size or an accent color. Set the intro as a normal 18–20px lead paragraph with no bold.

**Color: one color for everything**
- Dark red text, borders and buttons on pale pink is a single hue throughout. Nothing signals "this matters" and the page looks flat.
- Fix: use a near-black or dark neutral for body text. Keep the red as an accent for the date, links and the main call to action. Use a neutral or off-white background, or a much subtler tint.

**Layout: centered everything in identical boxes**
- Centered text is hard to read in paragraphs or lists. It works for a headline and a short tagline, not for parking directions.
- Five boxes, each with a border and a drop shadow, give every section the same importance. "Tickets" looks no more important than "parking." The border and shadow together are also redundant, and the effect looks dated.
- Fix: left-align the body copy. Drop the boxes and use spacing and section headings to separate content. If you keep cards, use one treatment (a light fill or a border, not both plus a shadow) and give tickets a distinct, prominent spot.

**Buttons: four styles and no primary action**
- Outlined, filled, text-in-a-pill and filled-in-another-red make four visual languages for similar actions.
- "Get tickets" is probably your main goal, but it's the outlined button, which is the weakest style. "Vendor list" is filled, so it looks more important.
- The second red on "Subscribe" looks like a mistake, not a decision.
- Fix: use one primary style (filled) for "Get tickets" and one secondary style (outlined) for everything else. Make "Map" a button or a plain link, but not its own variant. Use one red.

**Photos: inconsistent sizes**
- Photos of different sizes break the grid and make the page look unplanned.
- Fix: crop them all to one aspect ratio (for example 4:3 or square) and one size, in an even grid with consistent gaps.

**Order of fixes**
1. Fix the type scale.
2. Fix the buttons.
3. Remove the boxes and shadows.
4. Fix the photos.
5. Fix the colors.

The first three will do the most to make it look deliberate.
