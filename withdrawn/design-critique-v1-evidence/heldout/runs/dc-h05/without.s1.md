**What I'd check in a sauna booking flow**
- **Clicks to first availability:** can someone see open slots within 1–2 clicks of the homepage? Anything more is a leak.
- **Account wall:** requiring signup before showing price or availability is the most common killer.
- **Price clarity:** is the cost per person or per session? Are there hidden fees at checkout? Surprise totals cause abandonment.
- **Mobile:** most sauna bookings happen on phones. Check tap targets, calendar widget usability, and whether the keyboard covers form fields.
- **Third-party widget:** Mindbody, Acuity, and similar embeds often look off-brand, load slowly, and break on mobile.
- **Capacity and format confusion:** private vs. shared sessions, group size limits, and what to bring.
- **Trust signals at the payment step:** cancellation policy, reviews, and the address and parking details.
- **Confirmation:** does it send an email or text with directions and reminders? Weak confirmation drives no-shows.

**On your hunch:** the booking flow is the right place to look first, but you can test it before assuming. Compare the drop-off between "visited site" and "started booking" with the drop-off between "started booking" and "paid." If most people leave before they start booking, the problem is your homepage, messaging, or traffic quality. If they leave mid-flow, it's the flow.
