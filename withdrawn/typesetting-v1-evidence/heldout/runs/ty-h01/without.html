Smoked Lentil Stew

Simmer: 35 minRest: 10 minVegetable-forward

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups brown lentils
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 onion
  • 4 cups stock
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. SautéHeat the olive oil in a large pot. Sauté the onion and carrot until softened.
  2. Add paprikaStir in the smoked paprika and cook briefly until fragrant.
  3. Add lentils and stockAdd the lentils and stock, and bring to a boil.
  4. SimmerReduce the heat and simmer for 35 minutes, until the lentils are tender.
  5. Season and restSeason with salt, then let the stew rest for 10 minutes before serving.
