Smoked Lentil Stew

  • Simmer 35 min
  • Rest 10 min

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups brown lentils
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 onion
  • 4 cups stock
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  •  Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Sauté the onion and carrot in the olive oil until softened.
  2. Add the paprika and stir to coat the vegetables.
  3. Add the lentils and stock and bring to a boil.
  4. Simmer for 35 minutes.
  5. Season and rest with salt, then let stand for 10 minutes before serving.
