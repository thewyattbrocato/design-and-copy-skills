Emberfall · Patch Notes

1.4: The Salt Marsh Update

Released March 11

New

  • The Salt Marsh region, with three new enemy types: Bog Wardens, Reed Stalkers, and the Tidecaller boss.
  • Lantern crafting now needs two oil flasks instead of three.
  • Added a rest-at-campfire emote.

Changed

  • Torch duration is now 6 minutes (was 4).
  • Rope arrows now stick to wooden walls.
  • Party size for the Hollow Keep raid is capped at 4 players.

Fixed

  • Players could fall through the floor of the Mill after reviving on the stairs.
  • The map no longer resets zoom when a teammate joins.
  • Fixed a crash when two players loot the same chest on the same frame.

Known Issues

  • Reed Stalkers occasionally freeze in place near water.
  • Voice chat can drop for a few seconds after a region change.

We are working on both for 1.4.1.
