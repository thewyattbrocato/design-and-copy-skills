Caring for your dog after a dental cleaning

Your dog had a dental cleaning under anesthesia today. Most dogs are back to normal by tomorrow morning. This page covers what to expect tonight, the next few days, and when to call us.

Tonight

  • Offer a small, soft meal about two hours after you get home.
  • Expect some grogginess, a husky bark, and a little drooling.
  • Keep your dog indoors and away from stairs until the sedation has fully worn off.
  • Water is fine right away, in small amounts.

The next five days

  • Feed soft food or softened kibble for five days.
  • Skip hard chews, bones and rope toys until the follow-up visit.
  • If your dog was prescribed pain medication or antibiotics, give the full course even if they seem fine.
  • Brushing can restart gently after day seven.

Call us if you notice

  • Bleeding that does not slow after a few hours
  • Refusing food for more than 24 hours
  • Swelling of the face or jaw
  • Vomiting more than twice
  • A foul smell from the mouth that was not there before
