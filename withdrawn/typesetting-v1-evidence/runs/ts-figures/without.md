**Tabular figures** (every digit the same width) suit anything where numbers line up or change:

- Tables, financial reports, and spreadsheets
- Prices in lists
- Timers, counters, and live-updating values
- Dashboards and code

They keep decimals aligned in columns, and they stop text from jittering when digits change.

**Proportional figures** (each digit sized to its shape) suit numbers inside running text:

- Body copy, headlines, and captions
- Standalone numbers that don't need to align, such as dates in prose, addresses, and pull quotes

They give more even spacing and read more naturally next to letters. The "1" no longer sits in a wide gap.

**Rule of thumb:** use tabular if numbers stack or update, and proportional if they sit in a sentence.
