**Use tabular figures** when numbers need to line up or change in place. That covers prices in lists, columns, totals, tables, dashboards, timers and counters. Right-align them, or align on the decimal, and use the same number of decimals on every row. In CSS: `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.

**Use proportional figures** in running prose, headings and standalone numbers. Their spacing looks more natural there, and nothing needs to align.

**If the font has no tabular figures,** set the column in a monospace or system face that does. Don't force the alignment.
