**(a) Dense admin dashboard: Major Third (1.25)? No: Minor Third (1.2), 6 sizes.**
Base 13–14px, ranging roughly 11 to 28px. A tight ratio keeps the steps close, so tables, labels, and controls stay compact and clearly related. Dashboards need few distinct levels: caption, body, table header, section title, page title, and a KPI number. More sizes would blur hierarchy.

**(b) Marketing site: Perfect Fourth (1.333), 8 sizes.**
Base 16–18px, ranging roughly 14 to 90px or more. The larger ratio gives the dramatic contrast between hero headline, subhead, and body that a design tool's site needs to look confident and visually designed. The extra sizes cover display, hero, H1–H3, lead paragraph, body, and small text. On mobile, drop to 1.2 or 1.25 so the largest sizes don't overflow.
