**(a) Dense admin dashboard: ratio 1.2 (minor third), 5 sizes**
Roughly 12 / 14 / 16 / 19 / 23px, or 12 / 14 / 16 / 20 / 24px if you round to tidy values. Use 14px for table and UI text, 16px for anything a person must act on, 12px for metadata, and the top two for page and section titles. A gentle ratio keeps steps small, so more information fits on screen. Five sizes is enough. Use tabular figures in data cells.

**(b) Marketing site: ratio 1.25 for text, about 1.5 for display, 6 sizes**
Roughly 14 / 16 / 20 / 31 / 47 / 70px (the 70px size scales with `clamp()`). Body stays at 16px and the larger sizes jump more steeply. The steep display steps give a hero and section headlines drama, which suits a design tool selling craft. The text sizes stay close together for comfortable reading.

**Why:** Dashboards reward density and consistency. Marketing pages reward contrast and a clear first read.
