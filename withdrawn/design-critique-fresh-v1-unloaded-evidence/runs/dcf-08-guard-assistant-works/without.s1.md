Based only on your description (I haven't seen the code), the design is sound: nothing is written without Insert, claims are sourced, and the panel can be switched off. Before launch, I'd check two things:

1. **Flags and citations on Insert:** Confirm the uncertainty flag and source link survive Insert, or that the user is warned when they don't. Otherwise the doubt disappears the moment text enters the document.
2. **Source links and the dotted underline:** Test that each link lands on a passage that really supports its claim, since a wrong link makes a claim look verified. Also check that dotted underlines read as "unsure" and not as "link" when every claim is also linked.
