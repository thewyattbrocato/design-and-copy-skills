Mostly yes: white on #1d4ed8 is about 6.7:1 contrast (passes AA and AAA for normal text), the native `<button>` gives you keyboard and semantics, and the 12×20px padding with 16px text makes a decent target. The one flaw is that the focus outline is the same blue as the button, so it only has contrast against the white page (about 6.7:1, which is fine) but blends into the button itself. The 2px offset leaves a white gap, so it still works.
