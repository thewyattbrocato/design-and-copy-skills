Mostly yes: white on #1d4ed8 is about 6.7:1 contrast (passes AA and AAA for normal text), it's a native `<button>` with a visible label, and the text is 16px. The one weak spot is the focus ring: its color matches the button fill, so the 3px outline only shows up through the 2px offset gap against the white page.
