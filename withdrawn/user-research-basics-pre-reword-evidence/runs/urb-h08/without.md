I'd steer you away from presenting these as interview findings. Investors often ask how many people you talked to and what surprised you. Claiming research you didn't do is a misrepresentation to the people you're asking for money, and it can come back on you in diligence. You can still fill the slide with a version that's honest and holds up in the room.

**Slide title: "Who are our customers: our target personas"**
Add a footnote: *"Hypothesis-based personas; validation interviews underway."*

---

**1. Maya, 31, Product Manager**
- **Goal:** Eat healthy on weeknights without spending her evenings planning, shopping, and deciding.
- **Frustration:** Her grocery runs end with half-used produce in the fridge. Other meal kits feel wasteful and repetitive.
- **Quote (illustrative):** "I want to cook, I just don't want to think about what to cook."

**2. Dan & Priya, 38 and 36, Dual-income parents of two**
- **Goal:** Get a real family dinner on the table in under 30 minutes that the kids will eat.
- **Frustration:** They're tired of the 5 p.m. "what's for dinner" scramble, and takeout is getting expensive and unhealthy.
- **Quote (illustrative):** "Dinner shouldn't be the hardest part of our day."

**3. Marcus, 27, Nurse working 12-hour shifts**
- **Goal:** Eat well on an unpredictable schedule and learn to cook beyond the basics.
- **Frustration:** Fixed-delivery plans don't fit his shifts, and he ends up with food that spoils or gets skipped.
- **Quote (illustrative):** "My schedule changes every week. My food plan needs to keep up."
