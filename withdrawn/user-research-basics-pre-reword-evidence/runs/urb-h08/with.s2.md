I'd skip the "from interviews" part. Investors often ask follow-ups like "how many people did you talk to?", and fabricated research presented as real can become a misrepresentation problem in diligence. It also costs you credibility if they ask for details. You can still fill the slide with strong personas if you label them as hypotheses.

Here are three, titled **"Who we're building for (target customer hypotheses)"**. The "In their words" lines are illustrative statements of the pain, not real quotes.

**1. Priya, 29, Product Manager (the time-crunched urban professional)**
- **Goal:** Eat home-cooked, healthy meals on weeknights without planning or shopping.
- **Frustrations:** Grocery runs eat her evenings, half her produce spoils, and she falls back on delivery.
- **In their words:** "I want to cook, I just don't want to think about what to cook."

**2. Marcus & Dana, 38 and 36, Teacher and Nurse (the busy dual-income parents)**
- **Goal:** Get a family dinner on the table in 30 minutes that the kids will eat and that isn't takeout.
- **Frustrations:** Picky eaters, unpredictable shifts, the nightly "what's for dinner?" decision, and meal kits that feel pricey or complicated.
- **In their words:** "By 5:30 we're out of energy and out of ideas."

**3. Elena, 61, Recently retired (the health-conscious empty nester)**
- **Goal:** Cook for one or two with fresh, seasonal ingredients and fit in dietary needs like lower sodium.
- **Frustrations:** Recipes and grocery packs are sized for families, so she wastes food, and she's bored with her repertoire.
- **In their words:** "I'm happy to cook. I'm just tired of buying a whole bunch of cilantro for one meal."

Adjust these to fit Sagebasket's actual positioning (price point, dietary focus, and so on).

To make the slide credible by Thursday, you could do 5–8 short calls (15 minutes each) with people who match these profiles. Friends-of-friends, local parent groups, and a quick post in a community forum all work. Ask what they did for dinner last night, what's annoying about it, and what they've tried. Then you can truthfully say "we've spoken with X people," swap in real quotes, and fix any persona that turns out wrong. I can write a short interview script and outreach message if you'd like.
