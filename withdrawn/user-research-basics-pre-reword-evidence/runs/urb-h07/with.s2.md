**Why more caution is needed than the p-value alone**
- **Too short.** Two to three days isn't a full weekly cycle. Ticketing traffic usually swings by day of week and around on-sale and event dates. You need whole weeks.

- **Peeking.** Checking early and stopping when B looks good inflates false positives. The stopping rule has to be set in advance.
