1. Warm-up & consent

0–5 min

“Thanks for your time. I'm learning how people rent kayaks, so I can build something useful. There are no right answers and I'm not selling anything. Nothing you say will hurt my feelings. Okay if I take notes / record? You can skip any question or stop anytime.”

  • Start recording only after a clear yes.
  • “Tell me a bit about yourself and how paddling fits in your life.”

2. Background

5–12 min
  1. How often do you rent kayaks? Where, and who with? Probe: Own gear? Why rent instead? Same outfitter each time?
  2. How did you first find the outfitter you use? Probe: Google, friend, hotel, social, walked up?

3. Core: the most recent rental

12–24 min · spend the most time here

“Walk me through the last time you rented a kayak, starting from when you first thought about going.”

  1. What did you do first? Then what? Probe: What device? What did you search or open?
  2. How did you decide on that outfitter? Probe: What else did you compare? What nearly put you off?
  3. How did you book: online, phone, walk-in? Probe: Why that way? How long did it take?
  1. How did you pay? Any deposit, waiver, or forms? Probe: Anything confusing or annoying?
  2. What happened on the day: arrival, check-in, wait? Probe: Anything unexpected or stressful?
  3. Did anything change (weather, group size, time)? Probe: What happened? How did you sort it out?

4. Pain & workarounds

24–32 min
  1. What was the most frustrating part of renting? Probe: Last time that happened? What did you do?
  2. Ever shown up and it didn't work out (full, closed, wrong time)?
  3. How do you coordinate with friends or family on a trip? Probe: Who books, who pays, how do you share details?

5. Light concept check

32–37 min

Only now, briefly: “I'm exploring a simple online booking tool for small outfitters.”

  1. What would you need to see before you'd trust booking online with a small shop?
  2. What would make you stay with phone or walk-up?
Don't demo or defend. Note reactions; ignore feature wish-lists.

6. Close

37–40 min
  • “What should I have asked that I didn't?”
  • “Anyone else who rents kayaks I should talk to?”
  • Thank them. Stop recording. Confirm any incentive/follow-up.

Rules for a first-timer

  • Past, not future. “Last time…” beats “Would you…?”
  • Stay quiet. Count to 5 after an answer. They'll often add more.
  • Don't lead. Ask “How was booking?”, not “Was booking hard?”
  • Dig: “Tell me more.” “Why?” “What happened next?” “Example?”
  • Don't pitch or fix. Park it. Run long in section 3, not 5.

Listen for (tick when you hear it)

  • ☐ A workaround (call, text, screenshot, spreadsheet)
  • ☐ Money or time lost (no-show, double-book, weather)
  • ☐ Emotion: “annoying,” “nervous,” “love,” “hate”
  • ☐ Who else is involved (group, outfitter staff)
  • ☐ Anything surprising → write it in the box
