## Screener: Rural Clinic Front Desk Interviews

**Study:** 30-minute remote interview about managing walk-ins and no-shows
**Target:** 6 participants, plus 2 backups
**Incentive:** [$X gift card, set before sending]

### Intro (shown on the form)
> We're building software to help clinic front desk staff manage walk-in queues and missed appointments. We're looking for people who work at the front desk of a clinic to share how they handle this today. It's a 30-minute video or phone call. You'll receive [$X] as thanks. We aren't selling anything, and there are no right or wrong answers.

### Questions

**1. Which best describes your current role?**
- Front desk / reception / patient check-in (**continue**)
- Office or practice manager who also works the front desk (**continue**)
- Clinical staff (nurse, MA, provider) (**screen out**, unless you want a secondary segment)
- Billing or back office only (**screen out**)
- Other (**screen out**)

**2. How long have you worked in a clinic front desk role?**
- Under 6 months (**screen out**, too little experience with no-show patterns)
- 6–12 months
- 1–3 years
- 3+ years
- *Aim for a mix, and cap the 3+ group at about 3 people.*

**3. How would you describe the area where your clinic is located?**
- Rural, with a small town or farmland and a long drive to larger services (**continue**)
- Small town (**continue**)
- Suburban (**screen out**)
- Urban (**screen out**)

**4. What type of clinic is it?** (select one)
- Primary care / family practice
- Community health center / FQHC
- Rural health clinic (RHC)
- Urgent care
- Specialty clinic
- Other: ____
- *Aim for mostly primary care, community health center, and RHC. Reach urgent care and specialty only if you need contrast.*

**5. Does your clinic take walk-in patients?**
- Yes, regularly (**continue**)
- Yes, occasionally (**continue**)
- No (**screen out**)

**6. Does your clinic also schedule appointments?**
- Yes (**continue**)
- No (**screen out**)

**7. In a typical week, roughly how many appointments are missed, meaning no-shows?**
- Almost none
- A few (1–5)
- Several (6–15)
- Many (16+)
- *Screen out "almost none". Prioritize "several" and "many".*

**8. How do you currently keep track of the walk-in line and no-shows?** (select all that apply)
- Paper list / sign-in sheet
- Whiteboard
- Spreadsheet
- EHR/practice management scheduling module
- A dedicated queue or waitlist tool
- Memory or informal
- Other: ____
- *Aim for a mix. Don't recruit only EHR users.*

**9. In 2–3 sentences, tell us about the last time a no-show or a busy walk-in day caused a problem at your desk.** (open text)
- *Use this to filter. Vague or one-word answers are a flag for low engagement or bots.*

**10. Are you comfortable joining a 30-minute video or phone call, with screen share optional?**
- Video call (**continue**)
- Phone only (**continue**, note it)
- Not able (**screen out**)

**11. Do you have reliable internet or phone service at work or home for the call?**
- Yes / Mostly / No (**screen out**)

**12. Do you, or does a close family member, work for a company that makes clinic scheduling or practice management software?**
- Yes (**screen out**)
- No (**continue**)

**13. May the clinic's policies permit you to talk about your workflow?** *(Do not collect patient information. We only want to hear about your process.)*
- Yes / Unsure, I'll check / No (**screen out**)

### Logistics (qualified respondents only)
- Name, email, and phone (optional)
- Best days and times, with time zone. Rural clinics often have limited slots, so offer early morning, lunch, and after close.
- Clinic type and state (for your records)
- Consent to recording for note-taking (yes/no)

### Selection guidance for 6
- Mix of tenure, clinic type, and tracking method (paper through EHR).
- Include at least 2 who rated no-shows as "several" or "many".
- Include at least 1 who uses a paper or whiteboard system.
- Confirm no patient information will be shared. Remind them at the start of each call.

## Where to find people (by next Friday)
Today is Oct 9, so you have about a week. Start now.
1. **Your own network.** Ask colleagues, friends, and advisors for warm intros to rural clinic staff. This is the fastest route.
2. **Rural health associations.** Examples include your state's Rural Health Association, the National Association of Rural Health Clinics, state Primary Care Associations, and Area Health Education Centers (AHECs). Many will share a recruitment post with their members.
3. **Community health center networks.** Ask for a newsletter blurb or an introduction through an operations contact.
4. **Professional communities.** Front desk and medical office groups on LinkedIn and Facebook, and subreddits like r/medicaloffice. Check each group's rules on recruiting.
5. **Research panels.** User Interviews, Respondent, or similar services can filter on job title and rural location. They cost more but work well on a tight timeline.
6. **Direct outreach.** Call clinic managers, since front desk staff are hard to reach by email.

## Notes
- Front desk staff are busy and often can't take calls mid-day. Offer flexible times, and expect about a 30–50% no-show rate on interviews, which is why I suggested 2 backups.
- Set the incentive before you send anything. At this audience's rates, about $50–75 for 30 minutes is typical.
- A clinic's employer policy may restrict staff from participating. Question 13 flags this early.
