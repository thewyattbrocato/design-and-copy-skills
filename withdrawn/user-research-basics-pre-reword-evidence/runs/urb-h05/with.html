Tidewell: App Support Tickets, Last Week

Takeaway

Two app issues account for 6 of 9 tickets. Customers can't find a way to reschedule a pickup (4 tickets), and the $6 extra-bag fee surprises them at checkout (2 tickets). Both look fixable in the app itself.

What customers told us

Rescheduling is hard to find, or missing Top issue

Customers look for a way to move their pickup and can't find one. One reported there is no reschedule button on the order after payment. Another gave up, cancelled and rebooked.

"I tried to change my pickup day and gave up, had to cancel and rebook."

The extra-bag fee comes as a surprise

A $6 fee appears late in checkout, and the total jumps. Customers say they weren't told before paying.

"Got charged a $6 extra-bag fee. Nobody told me about that before I paid."

Late driver with no communication

One driver arrived 40 minutes late without sending a text. The complaint is as much about the missing heads-up as the delay.

Missing item

One customer reported a missing blue sock in their delivery. It's a one-off, but it needs a response.

Praise

"Honestly love the service, thank you!"

Suggested next steps

  1. Add a clear "Reschedule pickup" action on the order screen, including after payment. If rescheduling is deliberately limited, say so in the app.
  2. Show the extra-bag fee before payment, either on the booking screen or as a line item in the order summary.
  3. Text customers when a driver is running late, or when the ETA slips past a set threshold.
  4. Follow up on the missing sock through the usual lost-item process.

Based on only 9 tickets from one week, so treat the counts as directional rather than precise. Recheck next week to see whether the reschedule and bag-fee themes persist.
