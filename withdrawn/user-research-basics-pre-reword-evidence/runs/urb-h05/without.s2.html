Key themes

1. Rescheduling a pickup is hard to find or missing 4 of 9 tickets (#1, #2, #4, #7)

Customers can't find a way to change their pickup day. One notes there is no reschedule button after payment. Another gave up and cancelled and rebooked instead.

"There's no reschedule button on the order after payment?"

2. Extra-bag fee isn't disclosed before payment 2 of 9 tickets (#3, #6)

Customers were charged a bag fee (one cited $6) that they did not see until the total changed at the end of checkout.

"Nobody told me about that before I paid."

3. One-off service issues 2 of 9 tickets (#5, #8)

A driver arrived 40 minutes late without texting, and a customer reported a missing sock in their delivery. These are not app-related patterns on their own, but are worth routing to operations.

4. Positive feedback 1 of 9 tickets (#9)

"Honestly love the service, thank you!"

Suggested actions

  1. Add a visible "Reschedule pickup" option on the order screen, including after payment. This is the biggest source of tickets.
  2. Show the extra-bag fee before checkout, ideally with a line item in the estimate and a note on the booking screen.
  3. Send an automatic delay text when a driver is running late. Follow up with the affected customer.
  4. Handle the missing sock through the usual lost-item process.
