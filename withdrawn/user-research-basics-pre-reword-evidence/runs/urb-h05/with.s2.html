What customers said

1. Rescheduling a pickup 4 of 9 tickets

Customers want to move a pickup day and can't find how. One reported no reschedule button after payment, and another gave up and cancelled and rebooked.

"Paid for a Tuesday pickup but I'm away. There's no reschedule button on the order after payment?"

2. Extra-bag fee surprise 2 of 9 tickets

The $6 extra-bag fee wasn't disclosed before payment, so the total jumped at the end.

"Got charged a $6 extra-bag fee. Nobody told me about that before I paid."

3. One-offs 1 ticket each

  • Late driver: 40 minutes late with no text.
  • Missing item: one blue sock missing from a delivery.
  • Praise: "Honestly love the service, thank you!"

Suggested next steps

  • Reschedule: Check whether the app has a post-payment reschedule option. If it does, make it easy to find on the order screen. If not, scope adding one. Cancel-and-rebook is the current workaround, and one customer used it.
  • Bag fee: Show the extra-bag fee and its trigger before payment, not on the final total.
  • Late pickups: Consider an automatic delay text to customers. This is a single report, so check driver or dispatch data before treating it as a pattern.
  • Missing item: Route to the usual lost-item process. No action beyond that is needed from one ticket.

Keep in mind: This is 9 tickets from one week, so the counts show where to look, not how big the problems are. Ticket text doesn't tell us whether reschedule is missing or just hard to find. Confirm that in the app before deciding on a fix.
