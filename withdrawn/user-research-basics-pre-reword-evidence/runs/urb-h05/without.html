Key themes

1. Customers can't find how to reschedule a pickup 4 of 9 tickets

Customers look for a way to move their pickup day and don't find one. One noted there is no reschedule button on the order after payment. Another gave up and cancelled and rebooked instead.

"Paid for a Tuesday pickup but I'm away. There's no reschedule button on the order after payment?"

2. Extra-bag fee comes as a surprise 2 of 9 tickets

Customers were charged a $6 extra-bag fee they weren't told about before paying, and the total rose at the end of checkout.

"Got charged a $6 extra-bag fee. Nobody told me about that before I paid."

3. Individual service issues 2 of 9 tickets

  • Driver arrived 40 minutes late and sent no text.
  • One blue sock missing from a delivery.

These are one-offs this week, with no pattern yet.

4. Positive feedback 1 of 9 tickets

"Honestly love the service, thank you!"

Suggested next steps

  • Add a visible reschedule option in the app, including on orders that are already paid. This is the most common complaint.
  • Show the extra-bag fee before payment, on the booking screen or in the order total, so the final price doesn't change.
  • Send a late-arrival text to customers when a driver is running behind.
  • Follow up on the missing sock and the late driver with the customers involved.

Based on 9 support tickets, so treat the counts as directional. Each ticket was assigned to one theme.
