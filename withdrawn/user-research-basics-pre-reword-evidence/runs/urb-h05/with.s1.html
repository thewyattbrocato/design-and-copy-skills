App Support Tickets: Last Week

Tidewell support summary · 9 tickets reviewed

The short version

Two issues came up in the app. Customers can't find a way to reschedule a pickup (4 of 9 tickets), and the $6 extra-bag fee surprises people at checkout (2 of 9). The other tickets were one-offs: a late driver, a missing sock, and a thank-you.

Themes

  • 1. Can't reschedule a pickup4 tickets (#1, #2, #4, #7)

    Customers look for a reschedule option and can't find one. One noted there is no button on the order after payment. Another gave up and cancelled and rebooked.

    "Paid for a Tuesday pickup but I'm away. There's no reschedule button on the order after payment?"

  • 2. Surprise extra-bag fee2 tickets (#3, #6)

    A $6 extra-bag fee appeared at the end of checkout and customers say they weren't told earlier. One said the total "jumped".

    "Got charged a $6 extra-bag fee. Nobody told me about that before I paid."

  • 3. Late driver, no text1 ticket (#5)

    A driver was 40 minutes late and the customer got no message.

  • 4. Missing item1 ticket (#8)

    One blue sock was missing from a delivery.

  • 5. Positive feedback1 ticket (#9)

    "Honestly love the service, thank you!"

Suggested next steps

  1. Reschedule: Decide whether customers should be able to change a pickup day after paying. If they should, add a visible option on the order screen. If not, say so clearly and explain the cancel-and-rebook path.
  2. Bag fee: Show the $6 extra-bag fee before the payment step, for example next to the bag selection, so the total doesn't change at the end.
  3. Late driver: Check whether a delay message exists and why it didn't send. This is a single report, so confirm it before treating it as a pattern.
  4. Missing sock: Follow up with that customer directly.

Based on 9 tickets from one week, so treat the counts as a signal rather than a measure. Each ticket was assigned to one theme. Tickets #5, #8 and #9 don't refer to the app itself, so they may belong in an operations or service review rather than a product one.
