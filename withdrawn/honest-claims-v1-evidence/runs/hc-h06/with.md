## 1. Offer screen: show a free trial

I'd use a 7-day free trial on the monthly plan. Nothing needs pre-selecting if there's only one plan. If you add an annual plan later, show both unselected or pre-select the one you'd be comfortable explaining in a review.

- **Why a trial:** the value is a daily bonus puzzle and no ads. Players can't judge that from a screen, but they can after a few days. A trial lets them feel it, and it usually converts better than a bare £3.99 ask.
- **The risk is surprise charges.** Those cause most of the 1-star "scam" reviews and refund requests for subscription apps. Your rating matters as much as revenue, so the trial has to be unmistakable:
  - The button says "Start 7-day free trial".
  - The line directly under it says "Then £3.99/month, first charge on [actual date]. Cancel anytime."
  - There's a clear "Not now" or close button.
  - You send a reminder before the trial ends, either in-app or by push.
- **Why not the other two:** a pre-selected paid plan with no trial pushes a purchase decision on people who've just finished onboarding. It converts some and annoys more. A pure unselected choice adds friction without helping the player, because there's only one real decision.

## 2. Manage Pass: one screen, and no talking people out of it

It should be one screen, reachable in two taps (Settings → Manage Pass). It should show:

- Status (trial or active), price, and next renewal or charge date.
- What they get, in one line.
- A clear **Cancel subscription** button that deep-links straight to the App Store or Google Play subscription page. Cancellation has to happen in the store, so the button is just a shortcut.
- Restore purchases and a support link.

On persuasion, I'd skip retention screens, discount interstitials and guilt copy. Casual 25–50 players who hit a maze when cancelling tend to say so in reviews, and that costs more than the few saves you'd get. The one thing I'd allow is an optional, skippable one-question "why are you leaving?" after they've reached the store page or returned to the app. It gives you data and doesn't block anything.

Subscription rules are tightening in the UK, including exit and reminder requirements under the DMCC Act. It's worth checking the current commencement dates with whoever handles your legal review. This design should already fit those rules.
