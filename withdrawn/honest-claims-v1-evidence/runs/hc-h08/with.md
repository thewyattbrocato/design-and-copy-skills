**Why it's written this way**
- **Rate:** The rate is the strongest hook against the big banks, so it leads. "AER variable" and the "1 Oct" date stay in. The risk wording ("can go down as well as up") sits next to the figure in the description, because savings ads need that.
- **Fees:** I wrote "No Monthly Fee" instead of "No Fees". Your fact sheet only supports the narrower claim.
- **Norfolk:** This is your clearest difference from the big banks. It also screens out people who can't join, so you don't pay for clicks you can't convert.
- **Left out:** I didn't use "best rate", "beats the banks" or any rating or award line. You have no basis for them, and comparison claims need a dated source.

**Before you publish**
- **Date:** Update the "1 Oct" date whenever the rate is reviewed, or pull the ad if the rate changes.
- **Compliance:** Have your compliance contact approve the final wording. Financial promotions are regulated, and the FSCS and rate wording may need to follow your standard format.
- **Landing page:** Put the full rate terms and the Norfolk eligibility rule on the page the ad links to.
- **Spare line:** If you have room to rotate in another headline, `Open Online in About 10 Mins` (27) is accurate. It needs a UK address and ID, so mention that on the landing page.
