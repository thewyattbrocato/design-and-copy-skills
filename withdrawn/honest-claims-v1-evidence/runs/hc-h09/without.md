Open-tracking shows when the invoice link or email is opened, but email opens aren't always reliable. If you want to stay safe, use "see when your client opens it" and drop "the moment."
- **The support line uses your real number.** "Usually within a few hours" is now "median reply time of 3 hours on weekdays." It's more specific and more credible. I also cut "we answer every support email personally," since you can verify the 3-hour median but "every email, personally" is a stronger claim than the fact sheet supports. Restore it if it's true.
- **I dropped the "no big customer list" line.** It's honest, but it works against you on a homepage. I didn't replace it with anything invented, such as a user count or testimonials. Since you're not publishing the account number, I left it out entirely.
- **"Team of five" is now "the five of us."** It's warmer, and it fits the founder-story beat.
