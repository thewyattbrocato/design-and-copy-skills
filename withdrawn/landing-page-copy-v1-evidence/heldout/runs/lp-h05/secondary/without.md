- **Simplified the product description** — One sentence that explains what it does, not what it's "cutting-edge" about
- **Let the pricing breathe** — People can read it clearly without distraction
- **Removed the social proof that isn't there** — "Thousands of happy teams" is vague; a real testimonial or case study would be better, but generic claims backfire
