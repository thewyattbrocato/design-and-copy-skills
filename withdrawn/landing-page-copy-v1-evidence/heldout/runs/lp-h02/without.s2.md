# Quiet Books
## Bookkeeping for freelance illustrators and designers

You went freelance to make things. You didn't go freelance to wonder whether an invoice got paid, to sort personal and business charges by hand, or to dig through a pile of receipts when tax time comes.

Quiet Books is a five-week, self-paced course that walks you through the bookkeeping side of freelancing. It's built for illustrators and designers, and it's taught by someone who has done this work for a living.

## What you'll learn

The course covers the five parts of freelance bookkeeping that tend to cause the most stress:

**Separating personal and business money.** Set up your accounts so the two stop mixing. Everything after this step gets easier.

**Tracking invoices and who owes you.** Keep one clear view of what you've billed, what's been paid, and what's overdue, so you know when to follow up.

**Setting aside money for quarterly estimated taxes.** Build a habit of putting money aside as you earn it, so quarterly payments don't catch you off guard. This section is US only.

**Handling receipts.** Set up a routine for capturing and filing receipts that takes little time and doesn't depend on your memory.

**Handing a clean year to an accountant.** Finish the year with records an accountant can use, so you aren't scrambling to explain what happened in March.
