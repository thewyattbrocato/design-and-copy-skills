## Five habits that keep your books quiet

**Separate personal and business money.** Set it up once, and every transaction lands where it belongs.

**Track invoices and who owes you.** See what's been paid and what's still outstanding.

**Set aside money for quarterly estimated taxes.** Build the habit of putting money away ahead of each payment. (US only.)

**Handle receipts.** Keep them in a way you can find later.

**Hand a clean year to your accountant.** Give them records that are organized and ready.
