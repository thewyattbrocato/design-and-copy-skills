# Quiet Books: Sales Page

## Your Books Don't Have to Be a Black Hole

You landed a great project. Your client loved the work. You invoiced them and moved on. Six months later, when you're trying to file taxes, you realize you can't find half your receipts, you're not sure what you actually earned, and you have no idea if a client paid you or not.

Sound familiar?

Bookkeeping doesn't have to be this way. It doesn't require fancy accounting software, a degree in finance, or hours of frustrating admin every week. But it does require knowing what you're doing—and that's exactly what Quiet Books teaches you.

## What Is Quiet Books?

Quiet Books is a 5-week, self-paced course designed for freelance illustrators and designers who want to handle their own bookkeeping without the overwhelm. Created by Dana Okafor-Reyes, who's spent 9 years working as a bookkeeper, the course cuts through the jargon and teaches you exactly what you need to know.

You'll get:

- **18 video lessons** (about 12 minutes each) that break bookkeeping down into digestible, actionable steps
- **6 customizable spreadsheet templates** available in both Google Sheets and Excel, so you can start organizing your finances immediately
- **A monthly group Q&A call** (45 minutes, held on the last Thursday of each month) where you can ask Dana your questions in real time
- **Lifetime access** to the course materials (at the current version)

## What You'll Learn

The course covers five core topics that actually matter to your work:

- **Separating personal and business money**: How to set up your finances so you know what's yours and what belongs to the business
- **Tracking invoices and who owes you**: A system for knowing exactly who you're waiting to be paid by and how to follow up without being awkward
- **Setting aside money for quarterly estimated taxes**: The thing nobody tells you about freelancing until it's too late, explained clearly
- **Handling receipts**: A simple method so you're not scrambling through old photos and emails when tax time arrives
- **Handing a clean year to an accountant**: How to organize everything so if you work with a tax professional, you're actually prepared

By the end of the course, you'll have systems in place. Not complicated systems. Just ones that work.
