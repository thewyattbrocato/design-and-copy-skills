**Notes:**
- I left out "Trusted by 5,000+ small businesses," "4.9/5 from 2,300 reviews," and the reset countdown. They'd be false claims on a live page, and fake reviews and fake deadlines are what regulators like the FTC go after. They also cost you trust the first time someone notices the timer resetting. Use real proof as you get it. Examples are a beta-user quote ("[add a real customer quote]"), a count like "[X] contractors in the beta", or a stat such as the average time to send an invoice. If you want urgency now, "closed beta, limited access" works as long as access really is limited.
- I assumed the action is requesting beta access. Change the button text if it's actually a waitlist or an invite code.
