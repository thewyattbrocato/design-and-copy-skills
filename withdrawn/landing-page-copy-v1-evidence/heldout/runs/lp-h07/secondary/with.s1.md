## Why the time and the cost

When we reupholster a piece, we rebuild it from the ground up. We strip it to the frame, assess what's worth keeping, and start over. New webbing goes down under tension. Springs are hand-tied, not stapled into a foam base. New stuffing goes in—materials that don't compress into nothing after a few years. New fabric wraps it. Everything is constructed to last. Yes, it takes longer. Yes, it costs more. The difference shows the first time you sit down.

---

## What we do

We reupholster armchairs, sofas, and dining chairs. We take them apart, inspect the frame, and rebuild everything underneath the fabric: the webbing, springs, stuffing, and internal structure that determines how long the piece will hold up. If the frame is solid, we keep it. If it's damaged, we repair or replace it. You choose the fabric. We do the work the way it was done before the shortcuts.
