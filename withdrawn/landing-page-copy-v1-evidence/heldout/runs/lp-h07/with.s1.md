## Why people choose us anyway

Fenwright is two people, and the owner works on your furniture. When you send us a chair, the same hands that quote it also rebuild it. Nothing goes to a back room you've never seen or gets passed down a line of people who've never met you.

We do the work the traditional way. Springs are hand-tied, and the seat is supported with traditional webbing. We don't use the staples-and-foam shortcuts that make a piece cheaper and faster to turn around. Those shortcuts are a big part of why the chains can be quicker and cheaper, and we'd rather tell you that than pretend otherwise.

That is also why the job takes 8 to 10 weeks. Hand-tying springs and building a seat properly can't be hurried, and with two of us we only take on what we can do well. So we don't offer rush jobs. If a deadline is tight, the honest answer is that we're the wrong choice, and we'd rather you hear that now than in week three.
