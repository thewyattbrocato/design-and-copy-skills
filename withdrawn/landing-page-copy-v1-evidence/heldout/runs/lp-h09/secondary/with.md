## What it needed

Your current copy works *functionally*—it has all the right facts. But it reads like a Wikipedia entry, not a person. Three things hold it back:

1. **No voice.** "I'm Wendell Prasad and I tune pianos" is just a statement. Reframing that 22 years as *proof he knows what he's doing* makes it matter. "Been doing it for 22 years" feels like lived experience; "I've done it for 22 years" sounds like you're reading a resume.

2. **The honesty is buried.** Your biggest strength—you won't upsell, won't hide costs, tell people upfront—is buried in logistics. It should be *the point*, framed as what the reader gets (peace of mind, no surprises) not what you don't do (don't sell, don't commission). Someone calling you is already scared of being ripped off; that fear drives half the decision.

3. **No sense of relationship.** Piano tuning is craft work and local. The reader wants to know they're dealing with someone who cares about their piano, not a transaction. "I'll tell you the truth" and "you get the truth" signals that you're on their side.
