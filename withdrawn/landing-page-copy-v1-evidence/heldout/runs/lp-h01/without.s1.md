## What we make
We bake sourdough, and only sourdough. Each loaf is made from flour, water and salt, then left to ferment for about 36 hours. That's the whole recipe, and the long ferment does the rest.

**Country White**
Our everyday loaf. It has a crisp crust and an open, chewy crumb, and it's good for toast, sandwiches or tearing at the table.

**Rye-Caraway**
Dense, tangy and fragrant. It's good with butter, sharp cheese or a bowl of soup.

**Seeded Spelt**
Nutty and a little sweet, with a good crunch from the seeds. It's the one to have with breakfast.

## About us
We're two people baking out of a converted garage in Kestrel Falls, Oregon. We started this spring because we wanted bread made slowly, with a short ingredient list, and brought to your door while it's still fresh.

We're small on purpose. We make a limited number of loaves each week, and we bake every one of them ourselves.
