# Halloway Loaf Co.

## Home

**Real sourdough, made simple.**

We're two people baking sourdough out of a converted garage in Kestrel Falls, Oregon. We started this spring, and we're doing one thing: sourdough, the way it should be made.

### Our Loaves

We make three kinds, all with the same philosophy: flour, water, salt, nothing else. Long ferment (about 36 hours). Real bread.

- **Country White** — Our classic. Tangy, open crumb, golden crust.
- **Rye-Caraway** — Earthy and warm, with a bright caraway finish.
- **Seeded Spelt** — Nutty, hearty, and nourishing.
