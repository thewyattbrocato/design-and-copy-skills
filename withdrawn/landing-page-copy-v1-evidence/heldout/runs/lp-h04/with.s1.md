**What's in it**
- The hero says who you are, what you treat, the price and the action. The $95 and 60 minutes appear before the final booking button.
- Everything in `[brackets]` is something you haven't given me yet: the booking URL, therapist details, hours and phone. I didn't invent any of it.

**What I left out, and what to do instead**
- **Testimonial:** the quote section is a placeholder. Ask your first few patients for a quote and permission to use it, then paste in exactly what they said. Until then, delete the section. Real quotes are usually less polished than invented ones, and that's part of why people trust them.
- **"Pain-free in 14 days or your money back":** I didn't use it. If you advertise a refund and don't pay it, that's false advertising, and a guaranteed outcome is a claim health regulators look at closely. If you want a guarantee, make it one you'd honor, such as "If your first visit doesn't feel worth $95, tell us and we'll refund it." I can add that if you're willing to stand behind it.
