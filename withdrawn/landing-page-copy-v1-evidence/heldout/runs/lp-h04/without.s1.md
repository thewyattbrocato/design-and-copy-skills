**Honest ways to get the same lift**
- **Real testimonials:** After your first few patients, ask them for a short quote and written permission to use it. Two or three real quotes with a first name will do the job. Until then the page works fine without them.
- **A guarantee you'd actually honor:** For example, "If you don't feel your first visit was worth it, you don't pay." That's a small, bounded risk and it's easy to stand behind. Avoid promising specific outcomes like "pain-free in 14 days", because recovery varies and the claim can't be backed up.
