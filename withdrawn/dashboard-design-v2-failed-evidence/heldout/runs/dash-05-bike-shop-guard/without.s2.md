The layout is already good, so I'd make only a few changes. Most of them are about the text making claims the data doesn't back up.

**Fix before sending**

1. **The headline contradicts the stock-outs.** It says the repair queue is the only thing needing attention, but two items are out of stock, and tubes and chain lube are fast movers in a bike shop. Either soften it ("…the repair queue is the main thing to watch; two stock-outs are on the reorder list") or make sure it's true. Check that both stock-outs are in the reorder table and marked there. If they aren't, the owner will wonder why.
2. **Check that the week-over-week comparison is like for like.** The data runs through Friday 5pm. If this week is Mon–Fri 5pm and last week's $16,280 includes Saturday and Sunday, the +13% is wrong. If it's the same window for both weeks, say so in the footer. Also, Friday's bar is a partial day, so label it as one.
3. **Say "in-store" next to the sales number.** The online-orders exclusion is only in the small footer, but "Sales $18,420" is the number the owner will quote. Label it "In-store sales this week" and keep the footer note too.

**Smaller improvements**

4. **Don't let amber carry the repair status alone.** Print the reason in the tile: "9 bikes · oldest 6 of 7 days". The owner then sees how close it is to the promise without decoding the color. Check that the amber text has enough contrast on your background. If it doesn't, use a dark text color with an amber marker.
5. **Label the table columns clearly.** "Quantity" is ambiguous, so use "Qty to order" or "On hand". Add the supplier name, since you mention suppliers. Flag the two stock-outs with a small text tag, not a new color.
6. **Consider the sort order.** Longest lead time first is defensible. But if two items are already out of stock, the owner may prefer stock-outs first, then lead time. Pick one and say which in a short caption.
7. **Keep the chart's bar labels readable.** Fourteen value labels are fine if you drop the cents and the dollar signs, and keep the gray and blue consistent. Label "This week" and "Last week" in the same colors as the bars. Start the axis at zero.
