**Use a horizontal bar chart, sorted largest to smallest.** A pie would work with three slices, but a bar is easier to read from the back of a room. Viewers can compare 62% against 25% and 13% by length, and the labels can go right on the bars.

Suggested labels:

- **Hardware: 62% (about 769 tickets)**
- **Software: 25% (about 310 tickets)**
- **Other: 13% (about 161 tickets)**

Make Hardware the highlighted bar and grey out the other two. The slide's point is that hardware dominates, so the color should say that. Put a headline above the chart like "Hardware drove most of our 1,240 repair tickets this year."

A donut is the fallback if you want a pie-style look. Keep the 62% large in the center and remove the legend, labeling each slice directly.
