**Use a horizontal bar chart**, sorted largest to smallest, with the hardware bar in your accent color and the other two in gray.

- **Bars are easier to read from the back of a room.** Pie slices are hard to compare at a distance, and 25% vs. 13% is a close call in a pie.
- **Labels stay readable.** Each bar can carry its name, percentage, and count directly, so no legend is needed.
- **The main point is obvious.** Hardware at 62% is far longer than the rest, so the highlight does the work.

Label each bar like this:

- Hardware: 62% (~769 tickets)
- Software: 25% (~310 tickets)
- Everything else: 13% (~161 tickets)

The counts add up to 1,240. Put a headline above the chart such as "Hardware drove 6 in 10 repair tickets this year."

A donut chart with 62% in the center would also work if you want something more visual, but it's a close second. Avoid a pie with a legend, since the audience will have to look back and forth to decode it.
