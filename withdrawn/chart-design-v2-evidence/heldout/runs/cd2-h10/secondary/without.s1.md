A pie chart works for three parts that add to a whole, but I'd use a **horizontal bar chart** sorted largest to smallest. Bars are easier to read from the back of a room, and the labels fit without a legend.

- **Hardware: 62%** (about 769 tickets)
- **Software: 25%** (about 310)
- **Other: 13%** (about 161)

Put the value and percentage on each bar. Renaming "everything else" to "Other" makes the slide cleaner, and if you can name the largest subcategories of "Other" it may be worth a footnote.

If the slide's point is "most of our work is hardware," a single large "62%" callout with the bar chart beneath it will make that clearer than either chart alone.
