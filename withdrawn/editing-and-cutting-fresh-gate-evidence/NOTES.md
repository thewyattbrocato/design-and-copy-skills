# editing-and-cutting, fresh held-out set: thin evidence, labelled marginal

Evidence only. The skill was tested once on a freshly written held-out set and ships in the first release labelled marginal, because the evidence is thin.

- Generator Sonnet, judge Opus, installed mode, 3 samples per arm, 10 tasks, 2026-10-15.
- All samples: with 8 wins, without 1, 1 tie.
- Loaded-only: with 3 wins, without 1, 1 tie over 5 tasks; mean rubric 4.61 with, 4.22 without (+0.39). The skill loaded in 15 of 30 with-skill samples.
- The gate needs 6 tasks where the skill loaded; it loaded on 5 of 10. It did not load on any of the five write tasks, which is expected for an editing skill that should stay out of tasks that write new text.
- This is thin evidence, so editing-and-cutting is labelled marginal.
