Some ways to bring in the "everybody loves us" feeling honestly:
- **Quote a real customer:** "Everyone keeps telling us we're the best bakery around" works if you phrase it as "the kind words from our neighbors have been unreal."
- **Run a real vote:** Ask customers to vote for their favorite bun in the first month, then announce the winner.
- **Enter a real contest:** Local "best of" polls often open in the new year, so you could earn the title.
