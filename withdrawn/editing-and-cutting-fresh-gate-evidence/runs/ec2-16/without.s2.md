A few notes on what I changed:
- **Gave a specific deadline.** "Sometime this week" lets it slip. I used Friday as a placeholder, so change it to the date you need.
- **Cut the hedges** ("possibly," "sort of," "no worries if it's tough"). They signal that the deadline is optional.
- **Stated the reason once.** The launch date explains the urgency without sounding like blame.
- **Asked for confirmation and left room for problems.** That keeps the tone cooperative and gets you an answer even if he can't deliver.

If this is a repeat issue, you could add a line like "Since the last batch was also delayed, I want to make sure we're aligned on timing." I left it out to keep this version friendly.
