Here's a more direct version:

> Hi Marcus, I need the product photos by Friday to keep the launch on track for the 24th. Can you confirm you'll have them to us by then? If something is getting in the way, let me know today so we can figure out next steps.

A few notes on what I changed:

- **Specific deadline:** "Sometime this week" became "by Friday." Swap in whatever day works for you. A concrete date is the biggest thing that makes this read as firm.
- **Reason for the deadline:** The launch date stays, since it explains the request without sounding like blame.
- **Hedges removed:** I cut "quickly," "possibly," "sort of," "no worries at all," and "if it's not doable." Those phrases give Marcus an easy way to let it slide.
- **Confirmation requested:** Asking him to confirm gets you a response. If he's going to miss the date, you'll hear about it now instead of on the 23rd.

If you want it a bit warmer, you could add "Thanks, I appreciate it" at the end. If you want it firmer, since this has happened before, you could change the last sentence to: "Since this has come up before, I'd like to lock in a delivery date today."
