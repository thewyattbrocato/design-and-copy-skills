---

**Subject: This Saturday! Seedling Swap, 9 to noon at Eastfield Library**

Hi everyone,

A quick reminder that our seedling swap is **this Saturday, October 17th, from 9:00 AM to noon** in the **Eastfield Library parking lot**.

Bring any extra seedlings, starts, or cuttings you have, and take home something new. You don't need to bring something to take something. Label your plants if you can, and bring a box or tray for carrying your finds.

We've also invited local news to stop by, so you may see a camera or two. It's a great chance to show off what our community is growing.

Please share this with friends and neighbors who garden, or who might want to start. The more variety, the better the swap.

See you Saturday!

[Your name]
