The only space between the last section and the footer is `main`'s 24px bottom padding. The last section's 8px margin sits inside that padding, and the footer has `margin: 0`, so nothing adds to it. That 24px is the same as the page's edge padding, and the footer's thin `#ddd` rule and 8px of inner padding make it read as part of the content block. The single fix is to give the footer its own top margin, such as `footer { margin-top: 48px; }`, so the gap above it is clearly larger than the gaps between sections.
