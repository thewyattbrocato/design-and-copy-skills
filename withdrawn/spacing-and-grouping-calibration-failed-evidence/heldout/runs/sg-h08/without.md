The footer has `margin: 0` and only 8px of its own padding, and the last `section` contributes just an 8px bottom margin. So there's only about 16px between the content and the footer's top border, and the `main` padding doesn't help much because the footer sits outside it. The single fix is to give the footer breathing room, for example `footer { margin-top: 32px; }`. You could also raise the section's bottom margin, but the footer rule is the more reliable place since it applies no matter which section comes last.
