A few notes:
- I put the social proof in the body line, so the headline stays an invitation. The body also says what they get (care reminders), which gives the button a reason to be clicked.
- I'd pull the number from a config value or constant instead of hardcoding it in the string, e.g. `Join {userCountFormatted} happy plant parents…`. Then it's one change at launch, and the copy can't go stale later.
