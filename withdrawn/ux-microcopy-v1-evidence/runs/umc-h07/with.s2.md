**Save-failed toast (recommended)**
We couldn't save. Your changes are still here, so try again.

**If the designer wants it warmer than that**
We couldn't save your note, but your changes are still here. Try again in a moment.

- You can push back, and here's the reason: the current string already does what a failure message should. It says what happened, that nothing was lost, and what to do next. The reassurance is the warm part. Warmth in an error comes from that, not from "Oops!" or exclamation points. Adding "We" to the start is the one small change I'd make, because it puts the failure on the app rather than on the user.
- A friendlier, longer line costs more reading time in a toast that disappears. Skip anything jokey, since the person is worried about losing work.
