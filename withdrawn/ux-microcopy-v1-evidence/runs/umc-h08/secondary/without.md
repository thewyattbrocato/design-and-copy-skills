**Primary option:**

**Banner text:**
"Error 4012: Session timeout. Your session ended after 15 minutes of inactivity. Any unsent form changes were not saved. Your scheduled appointments are secure and unchanged. Sign back in with your email and password to continue."

**Button label:**
"Sign in again"

**Alternative (more concise):**

**Banner text:**
"Error 4012: Session timeout — you've been signed out after 15 minutes of inactivity. Unsent form data wasn't saved, but your appointments are safe. Sign back in to continue."

**Button label:**
"Sign in"

---

The first keeps a more supportive tone and lists things explicitly; the second is tighter. Either keeps "Error 4012: Session timeout" front-and-center (so grep will catch it), acknowledges the loss and reassures on appointments, and tells them exactly what to do next.
