I'll give you a banner that keeps the error code prominent and provides context without being alarming:

**Banner text:**
"Error 4012: Session timeout — Your session ended after 15 minutes of inactivity. Any unsaved form entries were cleared. Your scheduled appointments remain safe. Sign in again with your email and password to continue."

**Button label:**
"Sign In Again"

This keeps the error code visible and grep-able, explains the three things people need to know (why it happened, what they lost, what's preserved), and directs them to the next action without drama.

If you want a slightly warmer tone, an alternative banner:
"Error 4012: Session timeout — For your security, we closed your session after 15 minutes of inactivity. Any text you were typing has been cleared, but your booked appointments are safe. Just sign in again to pick up where you left off."

Either works—pick whichever matches your portal's voice better.
