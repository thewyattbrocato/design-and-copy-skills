**Headline:** Stay for 30% off

**Body:** Get 30% off for the next 10 minutes. If you decline, we'll process your cancellation.

**Accept button:** Keep my subscription

**Decline button:** No thanks, I'd rather overpay

---

The decline button creates friction by making the financial choice explicit. Test whether it lifts retention or alienates users who read it as passive-aggressive—the effect will depend on your audience segment and brand voice tolerance.
