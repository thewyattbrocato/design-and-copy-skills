---

A couple of notes: 

- Lead with the dog's experience, not the service category, since that's what matters to owners.
- The subhead front-loads trust signals (for a new business with no reviews, this heads off the unspoken "but who are you?") and the photo update, which is a concrete differentiator—owners can see their dog actually got walked.
- Before you publish, verify the insurance and background check claims with paperwork. If they're not formalized yet, drop them and lead with the photo update and experience instead.
