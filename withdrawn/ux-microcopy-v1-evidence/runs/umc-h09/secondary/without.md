A few notes:

- The headline leads with benefit (the dog's happiness), which resonates with owners emotionally
- The subhead hits the key differentiator (photos) and addresses the trust gap for a new service by mentioning insured/background-checked upfront—but **verify you actually have that paperwork before launch**, since you mentioned the owner told you but you haven't seen it yourself. Adding those claims without documentation is a liability issue
- The button is straightforward, matching what they'd actually do
- Tone is warm but professional—reassuring without being cutesy

If you want to soften the "insured and background-checked" claim until paperwork is confirmed, you could say "Trusted with your pup" instead, or remove it entirely and add it once it's documented.
