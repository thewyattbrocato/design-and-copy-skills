Here's a simple confirmation dialog that acknowledges what they're losing and what they can do next:

**Title:** Cancel your booking?

**Body:** You'll release your seat for this 2-hour class. You can rebook any available class anytime from this screen.

**Buttons:**
- Keep booking
- Cancel booking

---

Once you know the refund policy, you might add a sentence to the body like "Your refund will be processed according to [policy summary]" — but this works as-is without it. The dialog clarifies the seat loss (most urgent) and the rebook path (most useful), so people understand the consequence and their next step.
