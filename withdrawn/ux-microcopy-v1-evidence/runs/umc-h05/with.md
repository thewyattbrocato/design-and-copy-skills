**Headline**
See bikes near you

**Body**
Wheelhaus uses your location to show available bikes on the map and to start your ride when you scan a bike. You can change this anytime in Settings.

**Buttons**
- Primary: Turn on location
- Secondary: Not now

Notes:
- The copy says nothing about what happens to location data afterward or how long you keep it. Once legal decides, add a line here or link to the privacy policy. Don't write "we never store your location" or similar until legal confirms it.
- "Not now" should lead to a map where people can still look for bikes by searching an address. The "start a ride" half of the promise also needs a fallback, such as entering the bike's code, so decide what that looks like.
