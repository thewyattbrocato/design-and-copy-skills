Here are a few approaches, since the data retention story isn't locked in yet:

**Option 1: Straightforward & Specific**
- Headline: "We need your location"
- Body: "To show available bikes near you and let you start rides by scanning a bike."
- Buttons: "Allow Location" / "Not Now"

**Option 2: Slightly Warmer**
- Headline: "Find bikes near you"
- Body: "We use your location to show available bikes on the map and start rides when you scan a bike."
- Buttons: "Turn on Location" / "Skip"

**Option 3: Extra Cautious** (if you want breathing room for legal decisions)
- Headline: "Location helps you ride"
- Body: "We use your location to show nearby bikes and start rides. We don't share your location with others."
- Buttons: "Allow Location" / "Not Now"

The key things I'd suggest:
- **Don't promise data minimization or deletion timelines** until legal signs off (e.g., avoid "we delete it after 30 days" if that's not decided)
- **Be specific about the *use cases*** (map, ride initiation) — users are more likely to grant permission when they understand the benefit
- **Avoid "Required"** language if "Not Now" is actually an option
- **Skip the privacy lecture** — link to full privacy policy elsewhere if needed, but the pre-prompt should be quick
