Assumed: desktop accountants, daily use.

- **Checkboxes:** Show a checkbox on every row, with shift-click range select. A header checkbox selects the current page.
- **Select all:** After a page select, show "Select all 4,000 matching" in the selection bar. Selection then means the filter, not the loaded rows.
- **Persistence:** Selection survives paging, sorting and filtering, and the bar always shows the count.
- **Actions:** The bar offers Mark paid and Send reminders. Skip ineligible invoices and say how many were skipped.
- **Recovery:** Mark paid gets an Undo toast. Reminders are sent to customers, so confirm them once, naming the count.
- **Keyboard:** Every selection action has a keyboard route and a screen-reader announcement.
