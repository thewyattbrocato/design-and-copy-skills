Assumption: desktop, daily bookkeepers.

- **Checkboxes:** Show them on every row, with a header checkbox that selects the current page.
- **Select all:** After a page select, offer "Select all 4,000 matching filter". Selection then follows the filter, not the pages.
- **Persistence:** Selection survives paging, sorting, and filtering. A bar shows the count and the actions.
- **Mark paid:** Do it immediately and show an Undo toast. Money is booked, so it needs one specific confirmation naming the count and total.
- **Reminders:** Emails can't be recalled, so confirm once with a recipient count, or add a short send delay.
- **Keyboard:** Shift-click selects a range. Space toggles a row.
