**Assumptions:** a household of 2 to 6 people, mostly on phones, glancing at it daily. One person sets it up and the others join later and rarely open settings. Every number below is a starting value I made up, not something I measured, so change it freely.

The main problem with 24 options that all start blank is that the app does nothing useful until someone fills them in. Flip that. Setup picks chores and people, and everything else is already decided.

## What should just happen (no setting)

- **Rotation:** Round-robin, skipping anyone marked "away." A new member joins at the end of the order. Finishing a chore early doesn't shift anyone else's turn. This replaces the rotation-rule options. Offer one per-chore choice, "rotate / always the same person," on the chore itself.
- **Overdue chores:** They stay with the assigned person and are flagged. Nothing is auto-reassigned, because that causes arguments.
- **Swaps:** Anyone can propose one, and the other person accepts with one tap. An unanswered swap expires on its own (start with 48h), and accepting has Undo. Nothing is configurable.
- **Theme, time zone, week start, language:** Follow the device.
- **Quiet hours:** Follow the phone's Do Not Disturb. Add an app-level default of 10pm to 7am so no one is pinged at night before they've set anything.
- **Reminders:** Each chore gets one reminder at its due time, plus one nudge if it goes overdue. No per-chore reminder setup.
- **Points:** Chores come from templates with points already assigned (small, medium or large, for example 1, 2 or 3). Edit points inline on the chore, not in settings. Weekly totals reset automatically and history is kept.
- **Permissions:** Everyone can complete, add, edit and swap chores. Only the creator can remove members or delete the household.

## Settings that should exist (about 6, prefilled)

| Setting | Scope | Default |
|---|---|---|
| My reminder time | Per person | 9am (assumed), changeable from the first reminder |
| My quiet hours | Per person | Prefilled as above |
| Points on or off | Household | On. Some households won't want a scoreboard. |
| "Only organizers can edit chores" | Household | Off. This is one toggle in place of the whole who-can-edit matrix, for households with kids. |
| Away / vacation | Per person | Not away. It's an action on your profile, not a buried setting. |
| Notification types (reminders, swaps, nudges) | Per person | All on |

Label each as "this household" or "just you." Settings apply immediately, with a reset-to-defaults option. Notification and quiet-hour changes need no confirmation.

## Cut or defer

- **Per-person reminder times for everyone:** Each person sets only their own. Don't make one person configure four people's phones.
- **Per-chore point values, rotation rules and reminder times in settings:** These belong on the chore, where people are already looking.
- **Fine-grained "who can edit what":** Cut it. The one toggle covers the real need. Add granularity only if people ask.
- **Anything that looks like an internal option:** Keep it out of v1.

## First run

1. Name the household and add people by invite link.
2. Tick chores from templates.
3. Done. Show the assumptions in place ("Reminders at 9am. Change") instead of asking about them up front.
