Notes and open questions

  • Merges are proposals. I rounded each odd value to its nearest token. Check any place where a 5px or 14px value was doing something deliberate before changing it.
  • Font weights, line heights and button sizing are assumed. The extracted list had no weights. I used 400, 600 and 700, a 1.5 line height for body text and 44px minimum tap targets. Adjust if the real CSS differs.
  • Badges use dark text on a light tint with a colored dot. White text on the green or amber fills would fail contrast at small sizes.
  • Muted text belongs on white. On Mist it drops just under 4.5:1.
  • Not covered yet: shadows, breakpoints, icons, motion, and dark or high-glare modes for the deck tablet.
