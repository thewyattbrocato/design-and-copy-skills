Notes for the director

  • Halden values: main color #F5C842 (yellow), headings #1F4D3A (dark green).
  • Text on yellow buttons is dark green rather than white, because white on this yellow is hard to read.
  • Yellow is too light for thin outlines and text on white, so the outline button and its label use dark green. Yellow stays on filled buttons, the banner accent and the tint.
  • Everything else (page background, card surface, borders, body text, corner radius) is unchanged from the default.
