What changes for Halden

  • Main color #F5C842 (brand yellow): primary button fill, banner tint and accent bar.
  • Headings #1F4D3A (dark green).
  • Text on yellow: dark green instead of white. White on this yellow is too low-contrast to read. Dark green on yellow is about 6:1.
  • Outline buttons (secondary, Reschedule): dark green outline and text. A yellow outline on white would be nearly invisible.
  • Everything else (page background, card surface, borders, body text, corner radius) is unchanged from the Kestrel default.
