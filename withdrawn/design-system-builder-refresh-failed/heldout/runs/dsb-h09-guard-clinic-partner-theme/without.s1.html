Notes for approval

  • Text on the yellow buttons is dark green (#1F4D3A) instead of white. White on #F5C842 is too faint to read; dark green on yellow has a contrast ratio of about 6:1.
  • Outline buttons and their labels use dark green rather than yellow, because yellow lines and text on a white background are hard to see.
  • Yellow is used for fills, the banner accent, and the banner tint. Headings use dark green (#1F4D3A).
  • Page background, card surfaces, borders, body text, and corner radius are the same as the Kestrel default.
