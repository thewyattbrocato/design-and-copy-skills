What changes for Halden

  • Brand #F5C842: primary button fill, outlines and banner accent.
  • Headings #1F4D3A: titles and card headings.
  • Text on yellow is dark green (#1F4D3A) rather than white, because white on this yellow is hard to read. The same green is used for secondary button text and outlines, since yellow text on white would be unreadable.
  • The banner tint is a light yellow (#FDF5DA). All other values (surface, page, border, body text, radius) are unchanged.
