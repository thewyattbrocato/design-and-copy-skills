Notes for approval

  • Yellow #F5C842 is the main color: primary button background, banner tint and accent edge. Headings use dark green #1F4D3A.
  • Text on yellow buttons is dark green rather than white, because white on this yellow is too low-contrast to read comfortably (about 1.6:1 versus about 6:1 for green).
  • Outline button text and borders use dark green, since yellow lines and text on a white background would be hard to see.
  • Everything else (backgrounds, borders, body text, corner radius) stays the same as the Kestrel default.
