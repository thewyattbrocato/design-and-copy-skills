A blank cell is ambiguous. It could mean "unknown," "not applicable," or "someone forgot to fill it in." Readers can't tell which.

Defaulting to 0 is worse, because it states a false fact. A 0 reads as a real measurement, so it skews sums and averages and hides gaps in the data.

A dash or "n/a" shows that someone deliberately recorded the value as unavailable. It's also easy to filter on, and it keeps the unknowns out of calculations. Agree on one convention, for example "n/a" for not applicable and "–" for unknown, so everyone reads the data the same way.
