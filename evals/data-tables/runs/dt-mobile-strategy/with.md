**Use a card list.** Support staff look up one order at a time, so a card suits that job better than a nine-column grid on a phone.

- **Card layout:** Put order number and status in the header, with customer and placed-on date beneath. Show the total prominently. Put items, subtotal, tax and shipping in a compact label/value list. Right-align the amounts so they're easy to compare.
- **Why not horizontal scroll:** It hides the columns people need, and staff lose the order number as they scroll. It works only if you pin the first column, and that's still awkward with nine columns.
- **Why not hiding columns:** Staff can't know in advance which field a given ticket will need. Hidden data forces extra taps.
- **Keep the table on desktop:** Switch to cards below a breakpoint (about 640px). Both views can render from the same data.
- **Add search:** Put order-number and customer search at the top, since lookup is the main task.
