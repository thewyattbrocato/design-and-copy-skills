## Reset your password

1. **Go to the sign-in page and select "Forgot password?"**
   Enter the email address associated with your account, then select **Send reset link**.

2. **Open the reset email.**
   Check your inbox (and spam folder) for a message from us, then select **Reset password**. The link expires after a set time, so use it soon after it arrives.

3. **Create a new password.**
   Enter a new password, confirm it, and select **Save**. You'll be signed in or returned to the sign-in page, where you can use your new password.
