## How to reset your password

1. **Go to the sign-in page and select "Forgot password?"**
   Enter the email address associated with your account, then submit.

2. **Check your email for a reset link.**
   Open the message and click the link. It usually arrives within a few minutes. If you don't see it, check your spam folder. The link typically expires after a set time, such as 24 hours.

3. **Create and confirm your new password.**
   Enter a new password, re-enter it to confirm, and save. You can then sign in with it.
