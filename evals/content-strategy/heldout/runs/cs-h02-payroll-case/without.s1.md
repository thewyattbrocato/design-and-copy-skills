# Proposal: Fund a Content Program at Tallowby

**Ask:** Two full-time writers and an annual program budget of **$[X]**.

**Why now**
- Restaurant owners search for answers to payroll questions, such as tip reporting, overtime rules and seasonal staffing. Right now we mostly aren't there when they do.
- Our two largest competitors, [Competitor A] and [Competitor B], each publish about 5x as much as we do. Every month we wait, they build more search visibility and trust with the buyers we want.

**What the money buys**
- Two writers producing about [N] pieces a month: guides, state-by-state compliance explainers, templates and calculators.
- A small budget for [SEO tooling, design, freelance review by a restaurant or payroll expert].
- Content aimed at owners and managers of small restaurants, tied to demo and trial signups.

**Expected return**
- Industry benchmarks often cite roughly **3x return** on content marketing spend. At a $[X] budget, that implies about $[3X] in attributable revenue.
- Content compounds. Articles keep bringing in leads long after they're published, so returns usually build over 6–12 months rather than arriving immediately.

**How we'll measure it**
We have no content tracking today, so the first 30 days go to setting up a baseline: organic traffic, demo and trial signups by source, and a count of competitor output. We'll report quarterly on:
- Organic visits
- Content-sourced leads
- Pipeline and revenue influenced

**Checkpoint:** At 6 months, we review results against these metrics and decide whether to continue, expand or cut back.

**Decision needed:** Approve two headcount and $[X] annually, starting [date].
