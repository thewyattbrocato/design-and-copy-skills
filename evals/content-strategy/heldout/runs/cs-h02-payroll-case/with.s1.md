# Proposal: Fund a Content Program at Tallowby

**Ask:** Two full-time writers and an annual budget of **[$___]** (salaries plus tools, freelance support, and distribution).

**Why now**
- Our two biggest competitors publish roughly 5x as much content as we do. Every search a restaurant owner makes about payroll, tip reporting, or scheduling is a chance for them to be found instead of us.
- Small-restaurant owners research payroll software on their own, mostly through search, before they talk to anyone. Right now we're largely absent from that stage.

**Expected return**
- Industry benchmarks commonly cite about 3x return on content marketing spend. We've never tracked our own content results, so we don't yet have a Tallowby number to put next to that benchmark.
- That's why the plan includes measurement from day one (below). By the end of Q2 we'll have our own numbers instead of an industry average.

**What the writers will do**
1. Publish content restaurant owners search for: payroll compliance, tipped-wage rules by state, labor cost guides, and software comparisons.
2. Build a small library of high-value guides and tools, such as a tip-pool calculator, that earn links and leads over time.
3. Close the volume gap with competitors, prioritizing the topics closest to buying decisions.

**How we'll measure it** (set up in the first 30 days)
- Baseline: current organic traffic, content-attributed signups, and demo requests.
- Track monthly: traffic, leads, and pipeline and revenue attributed to content.
- Review at 6 and 12 months against cost. If results fall well short of the benchmark, we adjust or cut.

**Timeline**
- Month 1: hire and set up tracking.
- Months 2–3: first wave of priority content.
- Month 6: first read on leads and pipeline.
- Month 12: full ROI review.

**Decision needed:** Approve two headcount and **[$___]** to start **[date]**.

---

**Before you send it:**
- **The 3x figure:** I framed it as an industry benchmark, not something we'll achieve. "Everybody uses it" won't survive a CFO asking where it comes from, and the figure varies widely by source and company. If you have a specific source, name it in that line.
- **The 5x competitor gap:** I kept it as you stated it. Spend 20 minutes counting each competitor's blog posts over the last 12 months and compare with ours. A real count ("they published 120 posts, we published 24") is stronger than a multiple, and it protects you if the CEO checks.
