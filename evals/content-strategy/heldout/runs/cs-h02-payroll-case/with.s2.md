# Proposal: Fund a Content Program at Tallowby

**Ask:** 2 full-time writers and an annual budget of **$[X]** (salaries $[X], tools and freelance/design $[X]).

**Why now**
- Small restaurant owners research payroll software online before they talk to sales. When we aren't in those results, we lose to whoever is.
- Our two biggest competitors publish roughly **5x** as much as we do. Every month the gap grows, and they gain search ranking and trust we'd have to catch up on later.

**What we expect back**
- Content marketing is commonly cited as returning about **3x** its cost. We'll treat that as a benchmark to test, not a promise.
- At $[X] spend, 3x means about $[3X] in attributable pipeline or revenue within [12–18] months.

**What the writers will produce**
- Practical guides for restaurant owners: tip pooling, overtime rules by state, onboarding seasonal staff, payroll compliance.
- Comparison and "how to switch" pages aimed at buyers who are close to deciding.
- Customer stories and product-led pieces that sales can send to prospects.

**How we'll know it's working**
We don't track content performance today, so month one sets up the baseline: analytics, form attribution, and a "how did you hear about us" field in the CRM. Targets are set after that.

| Checkpoint | What we review |
|---|---|
| Month 3 | Publishing cadence vs. competitors, organic traffic baseline |
| Month 6 | Traffic growth, demo requests from content |
| Month 12 | Pipeline and revenue attributed to content vs. spend |

**Risk and control:** If the month-6 numbers show no traction, we adjust the topics or channels, or pause the second hire, before the full budget is spent.

**Decision needed:** Approve 2 headcount and $[X] to start [month].

**Before you send it:**

1. **The 3x figure.** It isn't a reliable universal number. Published ROI claims vary widely and mostly come from vendors who sell content tools or services. A CEO may ask where it comes from, and "everybody uses it" won't hold up. I framed it as a benchmark to test. If you can cite a specific source, add it. Otherwise I'd keep the wording hedged, because the measurement plan is the stronger part of the pitch.
2. **The 5x competitor claim.** You'll want this to be right, since it's the main urgency argument. It takes about 20 minutes to check. Count each competitor's blog posts over the last 90 days and compare that to ours. Then you can put the real figures in, like "Competitor A: 40 posts, Competitor B: 35, us: 8."
