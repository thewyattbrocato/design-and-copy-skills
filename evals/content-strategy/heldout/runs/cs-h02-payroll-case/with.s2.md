# Proposal: Fund a Content Program at Tallowby

**Ask:** Two full-time writers and an annual program budget of **[$___]**.

**The problem**
Our two biggest competitors publish about 5x more content than we do. Restaurant owners researching payroll software find them first, and we're not in the conversation. The gap grows every month we wait, because content compounds.

**Why content**
- Small restaurant owners search for answers to specific problems: tip pooling, overtime rules, state-by-state compliance, running payroll with high turnover. We can be the answer.
- Industry benchmarks commonly put content marketing returns around 3x spend. We should treat that as a planning assumption, not a promise, and replace it with our own numbers within two quarters.
- Content keeps working after it's published. Paid ads stop when the budget does.

**What we'd do with two writers**
- **Writer 1:** Search-driven guides and tools (compliance, tip rules, payroll how-tos) aimed at owners already looking for a solution.
- **Writer 2:** Customer stories, comparison pages, and sales enablement content that help close deals.
- Together they'd produce roughly **[__] pieces per month**, closing most of the volume gap within 12 months.

**How we'll measure it**
We've never tracked content performance, so we'd start from zero. In the first 30 days we would:
- Set up attribution from content to signups and demos.
- Record baselines for organic traffic, leads, and pipeline.
- Report quarterly to you on traffic, leads, and pipeline influenced.

**Checkpoints**
- **Month 6:** Output at target, and organic traffic and lead trends visible.
- **Month 12:** Measured return against spend. If we're well short of the 3x benchmark, we adjust or scale back.

**The decision**
Approve two hires and **[$___]** per year. The risk is bounded, because we'll know by month 12 whether it works. The risk of waiting is that competitors keep widening their lead.

**Before you send this:**
1. **Verify the 5x claim.** Count each competitor's posts over the last 12 months (blog archive or sitemap). It takes about 15 minutes, and the CEO may ask. If the real ratio is 3x or 8x, change the number.
2. **Soften or source the 3x.** I framed it as a "commonly cited benchmark" because it's a rule of thumb, not a verified figure, and it varies a lot by company and by how return is counted. If the CEO asks "3x of what?", you want an answer. The measurement plan is your strongest protection here, since it shows you aren't betting everything on that number.
3. **Fill in the blanks.** I left the budget and monthly output open because I don't have your numbers. If you tell me the salary range and tool costs, I can draft the budget line.
