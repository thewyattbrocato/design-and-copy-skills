## Content tiers

Your holiday-hours mistake was a high-risk edit that got treated like a blog post. So the process should depend on what's being changed.

| Tier | Content | Who writes | Who checks | Who publishes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A: Critical** | Hours, holiday hours, phone numbers, addresses, emergency info, appointment and cancellation policies, prices | Clinic lead or you | A second person (not the author) | You |
| **B: Clinical** | Blog posts and pet care advice | Anyone, including vets | The vet reviewer, for medical accuracy | You |
| **C: Minor** | Typos, photos, team bios | You | No review needed | You |

## Roles (two people to start)

- **You (practice manager)** are the publisher and owner. You're the only one who can publish Tier A and B changes.
- **The vet** is the clinical reviewer for blog posts. She checks accuracy only. She doesn't proofread or format.
- **Clinic leads** (one per clinic) propose changes to their own clinic's page but can't publish them.
- Everyone else gets draft-only access. If your platform can't do that, ask people to send changes to you instead of editing live.

## The process

1. **Request.** Changes come in through one shared inbox or a shared doc. Each entry says what to change, which clinic, and the effective dates.
2. **Draft.** The author writes the change as a draft and doesn't publish it.
3. **Check.** For Tier A, a second person compares the draft against the source of truth: the clinic's own calendar or the lead's written confirmation. For Tier B, the vet reads it.
4. **Publish.** You publish and note the date and who approved it.
5. **Spot-check.** Look at the live page once after publishing.

## Holiday hours

This is what actually failed, so it gets its own routine:

- **Calendar reminder:** Six weeks before each holiday, you ask each clinic lead to confirm their hours in writing.
- **One shared sheet** lists all four clinics' hours and holiday exceptions. This is the source of truth, and the website is copied from it.
- **Two-person check:** Someone other than the author compares the sheet to each live page.
- **Quarterly sweep:** Spend 15 minutes checking that all four pages still match the sheet.

## Permissions

Cut login access down to match the roles above. Most platforms have Editor/Contributor/Admin levels, and Contributor (can draft, can't publish) is usually what you want for most people. Remove any accounts for people who've left, including your former marketing person.
