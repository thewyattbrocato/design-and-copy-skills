## 1. Product Page Brief

**Writer:** Tobias | **Fact-checker:** Ines | **Photos:** freelance photographer

**Product:** [name, SKU, supplier]
**Category:** [clay / glaze / tool / kiln accessory]

**Goal:** Give a buyer enough accurate detail to choose this product and order it without emailing us.

**Reader:** A hobbyist or studio potter who knows basic ceramics terms but may not know this product.

**Sources (in this order):**
1. The supplier spec sheet (attach it)
2. Ines's notes on the product (attach them)
3. Nothing else. If a detail isn't in these, ask Ines. Don't fill gaps from other websites.

**Page must include:**
- Short description (2-3 sentences: what it is and who it suits)
- Key specs: [cone range / firing temperature, size or weight, colour or finish, food-safe status, as applicable]
- How to use it, in plain steps or bullets
- Any safety notes from the spec sheet
- Pack sizes and what's in the box

**Tone:** Plain, practical, friendly. No hype, no claims we can't back up.

**Don't include:** Customer quotes, review language, or "best-selling" claims. We have no reviews or sales data on the site yet.

**Photos:** If the photographer hasn't shot this product yet, upload with the supplier image if we have permission to use it, and flag it for the next shoot. Write alt text for every image.

**Who owns what**
| Step | Owner |
|---|---|
| Draft from spec sheet and notes | Tobias |
| Check every firing temperature, cone range, and safety statement | **Ines (required before publishing)** |
| Upload, pricing, stock | Tobias |
| Photos | Photographer, booked by Ines |

**Done when:** Ines has signed off on temperatures and safety, every spec matches the spec sheet, and the page is live.

## 2. Glaze How-To Article Brief

**Writer:** Tobias | **Tester and fact-checker:** Ines

**Glaze:** [name, supplier, product link]
**Working title:** [e.g. "How to Get the Best Results with [Glaze] at Cone 6"]

**Goal:** Help a potter apply and fire this glaze successfully, and point them to the product page.

**Reader:** A potter who has the glaze (or is considering it) and wants reliable, repeatable results.

**Do not start writing until Ines has test-fired the glaze in the shop kiln.** Tobias writes only from Ines's test results.

**Ines provides (before drafting):**
- Cone and firing schedule used
- Clay body and bisque temperature
- Number of coats, application method, and thickness
- What worked, what didn't (running, crawling, pinholing, colour shifts)
- Photos of test tiles or pieces, if available
- Any safety notes

**Article structure:**
1. What this glaze is and what it looks like fired (2-3 sentences)
2. What you'll need (glaze, tools, clay body, kiln)
3. Step-by-step: prep, application, firing
4. Results from our test: what we did and what we saw
5. Common problems and fixes
6. Link to the product page

**Tone:** Practical and first-person plural ("we tested…"). Be clear that results come from our kiln and may vary in yours.

**Don't include:** Results we haven't tested, claims about other clay bodies or cones unless Ines tested them, or reader testimonials (no reviews yet).

**Who owns what**
| Step | Owner |
|---|---|
| Test firing and results notes | Ines |
| Draft | Tobias |
| Check temperatures, schedules, and safety | **Ines (required before publishing)** |
| Publish and link to product page | Tobias |

**Done when:** Every firing detail matches Ines's test notes, Ines has signed off, and the article links to the product page.
