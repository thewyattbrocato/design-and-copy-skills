# Brief 1: Product page

**Owner:** Tobias writes and uploads. Ines supplies notes and checks safety and temperature claims.

**Inputs Tobias needs before starting**
- Supplier spec sheet (Ines forwards it when she buys the stock)
- Ines's notes: how it behaves, who it suits, anything the spec sheet gets wrong
- Photos. The photographer shoots a few times a year, so new products may not have any yet. Ask Ines whether to wait for the next shoot or publish with supplier images (if their terms allow).

**Page contents**
1. **Title:** product name plus type (e.g. "Speckled Buff Stoneware Clay, 12.5 kg").
2. **Opening line:** what it is and who it's for, in plain words.
3. **Key details:** size or weight, cone range or firing temperature, body or colour, and food-safe status. Copy these from the spec sheet exactly.
4. **How it works in practice:** two to four sentences from Ines's notes. This is what separates us from the supplier's own page.
5. **Pairs well with:** one or two products we stock.
6. **Care and safety:** any handling, dust or ventilation notes from the spec sheet.

**Tone:** Plain and practical, like a knowledgeable person at the counter. No hype and no claims we haven't verified.

**Rules**
- Never write a firing temperature, cone, or food-safe or toxicity claim from memory. Take it from the spec sheet and flag it for Ines.
- If the spec sheet and Ines's notes disagree, ask Ines. Don't pick one.
- Don't add review or rating sections. We have no reviews yet.

**Sign-off**
- Tobias drafts, then highlights every temperature, safety and food-safe line.
- Ines checks the highlighted lines only and replies "ok" or with corrections.
- Tobias fixes, uploads and checks the live page on phone and desktop.

**Done when:** every spec field is filled, Ines has cleared the highlighted lines, and the page is live with a photo or a deliberate placeholder.

# Brief 2: Glaze how-to article

**Owner:** Tobias writes and uploads. Ines tests the glaze and owns the facts. Tobias doesn't start until Ines has tested.

**Inputs Tobias needs before starting**
- Ines's test notes from the shop kiln: glaze and clay body used, application method and number of coats, cone and firing schedule, results, and any problems
- Test tiles or pieces. If Ines wants photos, she or Tobias shoots them simply in the shop, since the photographer only does product shoots.
- The product pages for the glaze and any related items we sell

**Article structure**
1. **Title and one-line summary:** which glaze, what result, what firing.
2. **What you'll need:** glaze, clay body, tools and kiln accessories, each linked to our product page where we sell it.
3. **Steps:** surface prep, mixing or stirring, application, drying, firing. Use numbered steps and put quantities, coats and times in each step.
4. **Firing:** cone, schedule and atmosphere, taken only from Ines's test notes.
5. **What to expect:** the result, plus what varies with thickness, clay body or kiln.
6. **Troubleshooting:** the problems Ines actually saw and how she fixed them. Don't add problems she didn't test.
7. **Safety:** glaze dust, ventilation, and food-safe status. Ines supplies or approves every line.

**Tone:** Same as product pages. Write for a hobby or studio potter who is competent but new to this glaze. Explain a term once, then use it.

**Rules**
- Everything about results must come from Ines's test. If we haven't tested a clay body or cone, the article doesn't claim it. Say "tested on X at cone Y" instead.
- No food-safe claim unless Ines confirms it for this exact glaze and firing.
- Don't mention class dates or sign-ups. Classes are handled separately.

**Sign-off**
- Tobias drafts and highlights the firing and safety sections.
- Ines reads the whole article against her test notes, since a wrong step can ruin a firing. She marks changes.
- Tobias revises, uploads, and confirms product links work.

**Done when:** Ines has approved the firing and safety sections, all links work, and the article has at least one photo of the tested result.
