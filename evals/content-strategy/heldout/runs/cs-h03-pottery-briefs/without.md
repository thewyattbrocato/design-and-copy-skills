# Moss Hollow Ceramics Supply: Writing Briefs

## Brief 1: Product page

**Product:** [name, SKU]
**Category:** Clay / Glaze / Tool / Kiln accessory
**Writer:** Tobias | **Reviewer:** Ines | **Due:** [date]

**Inputs (gather before writing)**
- Supplier spec sheet (Tobias saves a copy in the product folder).
- Ines's notes on the product: why we stock it, who it suits, quirks.
- Photos. If the photographer hasn't shot it yet, Tobias uses supplier images only if the supplier permits it. Otherwise he marks the page "photos pending" and doesn't publish.

**Page structure**
1. **Title:** product name plus size or weight, in plain words.
2. **Opening line:** what it is and who it's for, in one sentence.
3. **Key details:** a short list covering size, weight, cone range or firing range, food-safe status, and colour or finish. Copy figures exactly from the spec sheet.
4. **Description:** 100–150 words on how it behaves and what to use it for. Use Ines's notes for practical detail. Don't add claims that aren't in the spec sheet or the notes.
5. **Care and safety:** only what the spec sheet states, such as dust precautions or storage.

**Tone:** Plain and practical, like a knowledgeable person at the counter. No hype and no "perfect for every potter."

**Ines must check before publishing**
- Every firing temperature or cone number.
- Every food-safe, toxicity or safety statement.
- Anything Tobias is unsure of. Tobias flags these in the draft with [CHECK].

**Leave out:** Review quotes and star ratings, because we have no reviews yet. Don't add placeholder sections for them.

**Done when:** Ines has signed off on the checked items, photos are in place, and Tobias has uploaded and previewed the page on desktop and phone.

## Brief 2: Glaze how-to article

**Glaze:** [name, supplier, cone]
**Writer:** Tobias | **Tester and reviewer:** Ines | **Due:** [date]

**Order of work**
1. **Ines tests the glaze in the shop kiln.** Tobias doesn't start writing until she has finished. She records:
   - Clay body used.
   - Application method, number of coats, and thickness.
   - Firing schedule and cone.
   - Results, including the good, the bad, and any surprises such as running, crawling or colour shift.
   - Photos of the test tiles, taken by her or Tobias. The freelance photographer isn't involved in articles.
2. **Tobias drafts** from those notes, plus the supplier's data sheet for background.
3. **Ines reviews** the full draft, not just the technical parts. Tobias revises.
4. **Tobias uploads** and previews.

**Article structure**
1. **What this glaze is:** one short paragraph.
2. **What we tested:** clay body, cone and kiln. State that these results come from our shop kiln and that other kilns may differ.
3. **Step-by-step application:** numbered steps covering mixing, coats, drying time and firing.
4. **What to expect:** results in our test, with photos.
5. **Troubleshooting:** the problems Ines saw or the supplier warns about, and what to try.
6. **Safety:** mixing and handling precautions, and food-safe status, taken from the supplier's data sheet.
7. **Where to buy:** link to the product page.

**Tone:** Instructional and honest. If the test went badly, say so. Write only what Ines observed. If something wasn't tested, say "we haven't tested this" and don't guess.

**Ines must check:** Every temperature, cone, schedule and safety statement, and that the described results match what she saw.

**Leave out:** Customer results or quotes, because we have no reviews yet. Also leave out performance claims, such as "our most-read guide," because we have no analytics yet.

**Done when:** Ines has approved the draft, the photos are labelled with what each one shows, and the article is live and linked from the product page.
