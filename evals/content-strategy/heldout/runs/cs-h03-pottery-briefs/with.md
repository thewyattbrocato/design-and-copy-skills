## Brief 1: Product page

**Job:** Help a buyer decide whether this is the right clay, glaze, tool or kiln accessory. Then help them add it to the cart without emailing us.

**Reader:** A potter who knows their own kiln and clay body but not this product. They are likely to compare it with something they already use.

**Order on the page:**
1. Name, price, size/weight, the add-to-cart button.
2. A two-sentence "what it is and who it's for".
3. Specs a potter filters on.
4. Safety and handling.
5. Longer detail, only on request.

**Facts that must appear:**

| Fact | Source | Confirmed by |
|---|---|---|
| Name, size, price, stock | Supplier sheet, our price list | Tobias |
| Firing range or cone (clay, glaze) | Supplier sheet | **Ines** |
| Max temperature (kiln accessories) | Supplier sheet | **Ines** |
| Safety/handling notes | Supplier safety data sheet | **Ines** |
| "Tested in our kiln" | Ines's notes | Ines |

If the supplier sheet and Ines's notes disagree, don't pick one. Write `[CHECK: Ines]` in the draft and leave the page unpublished.

**Don't:** Paste supplier marketing lines unchanged. Say "food safe" or give a temperature that isn't in a source above. Describe how a glaze looks when fired unless there's a photo or Ines has tested it.

**Images:** Use photographer shots when we have them. Until then, Tobias's own photo is fine. Add the product to a "reshoot" list for the photographer's next visit.

**Before launch:** No placeholder text, no `[CHECK]` tags, and Ines has okayed the temperature and safety lines.

**Goes stale when:** The supplier changes the spec sheet, price or formulation. Stock stops being carried. A customer asks the same question twice. Tobias checks when a stock delivery or supplier price update arrives. Ines checks pages that state a temperature or safety line at least once a year `[confirm cadence]`.

## Brief 2: Glaze how-to article

**Job:** Show a potter how to get a specific result with a glaze we sell. It should also lead them to the glaze and any tools they need.

**Reader:** A potter who has bisque-fired work and wants a repeatable result. They may be using a different kiln from ours.

**Rule:** Ines tests first, then Tobias writes. No test means no article.

**Ines hands over (before Tobias starts):**
- Glaze and clay body used.
- Application method, number of coats, thickness.
- Firing schedule and cone, and which kiln.
- What worked, what didn't, any surprises.
- Test tiles or fired pieces to photograph.

**Order in the article:**
1. The result, with a photo.
2. What you need (linked to our products).
3. Steps.
4. Firing.
5. Troubleshooting.
6. Safety.

**Must appear:**
- Every step comes from Ines's test, not from the supplier sheet.
- Say plainly that results come from our shop kiln. Other kilns vary.
- Link each product used to its product page.

**Don't:** Promise a result outside what Ines tested. Add steps Tobias hasn't seen in her notes. Give a firing schedule that wasn't used.

**Review:** Ines reads the full draft against her notes and either approves or sends back specific corrections. Agree on a turnaround with Tobias `[days]`, since he works three days a week.

**Goes stale when:** We change the glaze supplier or formulation. Ines re-tests and gets a different result. We stop carrying a linked product. Tobias checks links when stock changes. Ines decides whether a re-test is needed.
