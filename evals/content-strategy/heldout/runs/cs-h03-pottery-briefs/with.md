## Brief 1: Product page

**Job:** Someone considering a clay, glaze, tool or kiln accessory can tell whether it fits their work and add it to the basket. We want the sale without a returned item or a question we could have answered on the page.

**Reader:** A potterer, from hobbyist to small studio. They know their own kiln and clay body, but not necessarily this product.

**Order on the page:**
1. What it is and what it's for, in one plain line.
2. Key specs: size, weight, cone range, clay body or kiln compatibility, as they apply.
3. Price and stock.
4. Longer detail on request: how to use it, how to store it, and anything unusual.

**Facts that must appear**

| Fact | Source | Confirmed by |
|---|---|---|
| Name, size, weight, pack size | Supplier spec sheet | Tobias |
| Firing range or cone | Spec sheet, plus Ines's notes | **Ines, always** |
| Safety notes (dust, food-safe claims, handling) | Spec sheet or safety data sheet | **Ines, always** |
| Price | Ines's stock list | Ines |
| Our own advice ("works well with…") | Ines's notes | Ines |

**Rules**
- Tobias uses the supplier's figures as given. If the spec sheet and Ines's notes disagree, he stops and asks. He doesn't pick one.
- Don't write "food-safe" or "non-toxic" unless Ines has confirmed it for that product.
- Don't write testimonials, star ratings or "customers love…" lines. We have no reviews on the site yet.
- Don't promise results Ines hasn't seen. Say what the supplier states and mark it as theirs.

**Photos:** Use the photographer's shots when we have them. If a product has none, publish with a placeholder note to Ines, and don't use supplier images without Ines's okay. `[Photographer's usage terms: Ines to confirm]`

**Handoff:** Tobias drafts. Ines checks the temperature and safety lines within `[agreed turnaround, e.g. her next shop day]`. Then Tobias uploads.

**Goes stale when:** the supplier changes the formulation or spec sheet, the price changes, or the item is discontinued or replaced. Tobias checks pages when a new delivery or price list arrives. Ines tells him about any change in stock.

## Brief 2: Glaze how-to article

**Job:** Help a reader get a good result with a specific glaze, and show them the glaze in our shop. The article should lead naturally to the product page.

**Reader:** Someone who has bought or is considering the glaze. They can fire a kiln but may not have used this glaze.

**Start condition:** Tobias doesn't start until Ines has tested the glaze in the shop kiln and handed over her notes. No test, no article.

**What Ines hands over**
- Clay body used, firing schedule and cone, and the kiln type
- Number of coats, application method and thickness
- What went wrong or surprised her (running, crawling, colour shift)
- Photos or fired test tiles `[who photographs them: Ines on her phone, or the photographer]`

**Order in the article**
1. What the glaze does, with a fired result shown.
2. What you need: glaze, clay body, cone range.
3. Step by step: prep, application, firing.
4. Common problems and fixes, from Ines's tests only.
5. Link to the product page.

**Rules**
- Every firing temperature, schedule and safety line is Ines's wording or checked by her before publishing.
- Say "in our kiln" for what we tested. Kilns vary, so don't promise the same result elsewhere.
- Don't describe results we didn't test. If we only tried one clay body, say so.
- Keep a short safety line where relevant (ventilation, dust masks, food contact). Ines writes or approves it.

**Handoff:** Ines tests, then gives Tobias her notes. Tobias drafts, Ines checks the firing and safety content, and Tobias uploads and links the product page.

**Goes stale when:** the supplier reformulates the glaze, we stop stocking it, or Ines retests with a different result. Tobias re-reads the article whenever its product page changes.
