Last week, our crew of 9 at Aldercreek Solar wrapped up a rooftop solar install on the Hollis Valley grange hall. It's our first community hall project, and it meant a lot to us.

The best part came this past Sunday. The hall's kitchen, where the monthly pancake breakfast gets cooked, ran on the new panels for the first time. Seeing the griddles heat up on sun-powered electricity was a great way to close out the job.

Thank you to the grange volunteers, who held ladders and made lunch for the whole crew. We couldn't have asked for a better team on the ground.

More details on the system are coming soon. For now, we're just glad the pancakes are in good hands.
