Last week, the Aldercreek Solar crew of 9 wrapped up a rooftop solar install on the Hollis Valley grange hall. It's our first community hall project, and it meant a lot to us.

The best part came this past Sunday. The hall's kitchen is where the monthly pancake breakfast gets cooked, and this month's pancakes ran on the new panels.

The grange volunteers were part of the job, too. They held ladders and made lunch for our crew.

Thank you, Hollis Valley Grange, for trusting us with your hall. We're proud of this one.
