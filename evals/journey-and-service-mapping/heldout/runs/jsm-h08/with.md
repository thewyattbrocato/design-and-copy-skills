**Frame:** A dog owner on a monthly pet-food subscription, from the first box arriving to the point they cancel or settle in past month 4. The map is meant to help the team choose which of three moments to fix first.

**Status:** Built from 31 cancellation survey answers and the common support-ticket themes. The tickets have no counts, so I can't rank them. Cells marked *(assumed)* are my inference and need correcting by support and CX. The survey only covers people who cancelled, so it says nothing about why others stay.

**Map type:** A journey map, because the trouble sits between product, billing, logistics and support, and no one team owns it.

| | 1. Try the first box | 2. Wait for the box | 3. Switch to a new recipe | 4. Food piles up | 5. Discount ends | 6. Stay, skip or cancel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Does** | Mixes new food in | Plans around delivery day | Swaps the recipe *(assumed)* | Stacks bags in storage | Sees the full price charged | Hunts for skip, then cancels |
| **Asks** | Will my dog eat it? | Will I be home? | Why won't she eat this? | Do I really need this much? | Is this worth the price? | Where's the skip button? |
| **Feels** *(assumed)* | Hopeful | Mildly anxious | Worried, annoyed | Overstocked | Surprised, resentful | Stuck, then done |

| **Goes wrong** | Nothing reported | Missing the delivery | Refusing the new food | Receiving more than needed | Paying more than expected | Failing to find skip |

**Marked moments**

1. **Phase 3, the recipe change.** This is the largest single cancel reason, at 11 of 31. Open question: is the recipe change something we trigger, or something the customer chooses? The fix depends on the answer.
2. **Phase 5, the discount ends.** 9 of 31 cite price after the intro discount. This is where an earlier promise meets the real charge. Check whether the discount end lines up with months 2 to 4, since that would explain the timing.
3. **Phases 4 to 6, piling up with no visible skip.** 6 of 31 cite too much food, and "how do I skip" is a common ticket. I suspect these are one story: people who would skip end up cancelling instead. That link is a hypothesis. To test it, check how many cancellers viewed the skip page or contacted support first.

"Box came when I'm not home" shows up in tickets but not in the survey. It looks like a support cost and a risk to the experience more than a cancel driver. It sits in phase 2 and isn't marked.

**Gaps to close first**
- "Other" is 5 of 31 and I don't know what's in it.
- I have no ticket volumes and no data on which month each person cancelled.
- It's unclear whether the recipe change is customer-chosen or company-driven.
