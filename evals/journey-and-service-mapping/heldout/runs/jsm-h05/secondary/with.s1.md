**Subject: Parent move-in journey: what we found, and what I need from you Tuesday**

We followed 5 families (adult children) from first tour through week six, and talked to 4 staff. All 5 families said the tour was great, and the sales coordinator is the reason. The problems come after the tour:

1. **No clear contact after signing.** 4 of 5 families didn't know who to call during the roughly three weeks between signing and move-in.
2. **Medication lists are slow.** Getting each resident's medication list from their family doctor takes our nurses 2 to 3 days of phone chasing. We had never measured this.
3. **Dining doesn't know who has arrived.** In week one, 3 of 5 parents ate most meals alone in their room because the dining team didn't know they had moved in.
4. **The move-in checklist is out of date.** Two staff said it is a Word file last updated two years ago.

**For Tuesday:** I want one named owner for each of gaps 1, 2, and 3. Please come with a candidate for each.
