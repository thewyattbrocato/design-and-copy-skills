**Subject:** Move-in journey: what we found and what I need Tuesday

Team,

We followed 5 families (adult children) from first tour through week six of their parent's move-in, and talked to 4 staff.

**What we found**
- All 5 families said the tour was great. The sales coordinator is the reason.
- 4 of 5 families didn't know who to call between signing and move-in, about three weeks.
- Getting a medication list from a family's doctor takes our nurses 2 to 3 days of phone chasing per resident. We had never measured this.
- In week one, 3 of 5 parents ate most meals alone in their room. The dining team didn't know the new resident had arrived.
- Two staff said the move-in checklist is a Word file last updated two years ago.

**What I need Tuesday**
Name one owner for each of the first three gaps:
1. Who families call between signing and move-in
2. Medication list collection from family doctors
3. Notifying dining when a new resident arrives
