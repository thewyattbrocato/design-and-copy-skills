**Subject: Move-in journey review: findings and Tuesday ask**

Team,

We followed 5 families (adult children) from first tour through the end of week six, and we talked to 4 staff.

**What worked:** All 5 families said the tour was great. The sales coordinator is the reason.

**What broke:**

1. **No clear contact after signing.** 4 of 5 families didn't know who to call between signing and move-in, about three weeks.
2. **Medication lists take too long.** Getting the list from a family's doctor takes our nurses 2 to 3 days of phone chasing per resident. We had never measured this before.
3. **New residents eat alone.** In week one, 3 of 5 parents ate most meals alone in their room. The dining team didn't know they had arrived.
4. **The move-in checklist is out of date.** Two staff said it's a Word file last updated two years ago.

**Ask:** Please come to Tuesday's leadership meeting ready to name one owner for each of the first three gaps.
