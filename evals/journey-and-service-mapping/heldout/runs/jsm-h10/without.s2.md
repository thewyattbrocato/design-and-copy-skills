**Item 6 is unsupported.** Nothing on the wall backs "more personalised care". Naming the dentist in the reminder is a small step toward it, but that already sits in item 4. Before you pitch it, go back to the team and ask what personalisation would look like, such as remembering preferences or anxiety notes. Otherwise it's best parked as "to define".

**Item 1 should lead.** It's the vaguest line, and items 4, 5 and 8 already cover it concretely. Presenting it as the heading over those three keeps the team's wording and gives the owner specific things to fund.

- Items 2, 3 and 7: average first-visit wait, and how many minutes the form takes now.
- Item 4: no-show rate and "which location?" calls.
- Item 5: billing questions at the desk and unpaid balances.
- Item 8: how many follow-up calls are made, and any complaints or returns after treatment.
