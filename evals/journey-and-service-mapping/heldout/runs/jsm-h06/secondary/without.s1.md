Overall the story is strong: the "is it free?" finding is clear, and the songs as the high point gives the board something to remember. These are the changes I'd make before you walk in.

**1. Fix the "free, no sign-up" wording.** Borrowing does require a card, so "no sign-up" is only true for story hour. Use "Free to attend, no card needed." Before you print it, confirm with the branch that non-members can attend and that the website and flyer will say the same thing.

**2. Say what the sample can and can't show.** Eight exit interviews over three Saturdays is enough to find problems, not to size them. Put n on the slide and label the findings as "what we heard and saw," not as rates. Also state who the interviews missed: parents who turned around at the locked door or left before story hour never reached an exit interview, so the arrival problem is probably understated.

**3. Give the denominators.** "5 parents tried the front door" needs a total. How many arrivals did you watch in those three Saturdays? Without it, the board will read 5 as a large number or a small one.

**4. Show that the ID barrier caused the 3 non-borrowers to leave.** Did those three say the ID requirement was why? If they did, quote one. If not, say "3 of 8 left without borrowing; ID was the barrier we heard most." Don't claim more than the interviews show.

**5. Address the timing.** The fieldwork was April and May, and it's now late October. The board will ask what has changed since then. If any of the three fixes is already done, say so. If none are, say that plainly and treat the work as current.

**6. Check the June date.** If the first check-in is June 2027, that's seven months out, and some fixes may not show results in that window. Give the board one or two measures to watch at each check-in, such as the number of "is it free?" questions to staff, the number of people who try the front door, and the number of attendees who borrow. Set a target for each if you can.

**7. Fill the ownership gap.** Your strongest root-cause finding is that nobody is assigned to the Saturday door and front desk staff are trained only for members. None of the three owners you listed covers that. Name an owner for staff training and Saturday coverage, or the arrival and borrowing fixes won't stick.

**8. Explain why this order.** Say why the flyer comes first: it's the cheapest change and it reaches people before they arrive. Note that the ID policy is the hardest and needs a policy decision, so it's last in sequence but may matter most for new residents.

**9. Label the "Feels" lines.** Say whether they were reported in the interviews or inferred from observation. Otherwise a board member can ask where they came from, and you won't have a clear answer.

If you tell me the date you're presenting and whether any fixes are already in place, I can help you reword the opening slide and the close.
