The map is clear and the evidence is tied to each stage. Here is what I'd change before you walk in.

**Fix before tomorrow**
1. **Lead with the ask.** The board will remember one thing. Opportunity 3 is the only one that needs their decision, because it's a policy change. Opportunities 1 and 2 are small fixes the staff can make now. Say that up front: "Two fixes we're doing now, one decision we need from you."
2. **Make the denominators match.** "8 of 8" and "3 of 8" are interview counts, but "5 parents tried the locked door" comes from the door watching. Say "5 of N parents observed over 3 Saturdays", or someone will ask 5 out of how many.
3. **Say what happened to the other 5.** "3 of 8 left without borrowing" invites the question of whether the rest borrowed or just didn't want to. Have that answer ready.
4. **Check the timeline.** The research ran April to May and the first check-in is the June board meeting. If June has already passed, or this is next June, make sure the slide dates say what you mean.

**Strengthen**
- **Name the limits.** You interviewed people who came. The parents who saw the flyer and stayed home over the "is it free?" question aren't in your data, and they may be the bigger group. Eight interviews is fine for a qualitative story, but present it as one, not a measured rate.
- **Add a success measure for each opportunity.** Right now June has nothing to check against. Candidates:
  - Fewer "is it free?" questions at the desk.
  - Fewer parents trying the locked front door.
  - More first-time families leaving with a book.
- **Match owners to opportunities.** Marketing, facilities and branch manager presumably map 1, 2 and 3. Say so on the slide. Opportunity 3 probably needs the board's sign-off, not only the branch manager's.
- **Consider a Saturday greeter.** Your "behind the scenes" row says nobody is assigned to the Saturday door, but the door sign only works around that gap. A greeter or a trained volunteer would also fix the staff-trained-for-members problem. You could offer it as a follow-up if the sign isn't enough.
- **Say why the order is what it is.** I'd justify it as "cheapest first", but opportunity 3 fixes the lowest emotional point (embarrassed). Tell the board that explicitly so it doesn't read as the lowest priority.

**Keep**
The songs are your strongest finding, with 7 of 8 naming them unprompted. Add a line that says the fixes are meant to get more families to that moment and don't change it. That reassures staff and the board.
