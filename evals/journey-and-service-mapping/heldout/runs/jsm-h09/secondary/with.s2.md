## Thursday: 12 people, support, product, sales, onboarding

**Format:** A large printout (A0 or bigger, or several sheets taped together) on a wall, plus blank sticky notes and a printed list of the 11 pain points and 9 ideas, each with an ID (P1–P11, I1–I9).

**What goes on the sheet:**
- **Framing line at the top:** who the person is (a new restaurant owner), where the story starts (signed contract) and ends (first successful payroll run), and the decision it serves (which pain points to fix first).
- **Status line under the framing:** what it's built on. If it's a draft from internal knowledge, say so. Don't let anyone mistake a guess for research.
- **The 7 stages** as a strip across the top, in the owner's order.
- **The 4 lanes:** owner, onboarding specialist, software, backstage (bank verification and similar).
- **The emotion line,** labeled as the team's estimate unless it was measured.
- **P1–P11 marked on their cells**, and I1–I9 tied to the pain points they answer.

**What you ask the room to do:** Correct the map. Have each function check its own lanes first, then add missing steps and mark wrong feelings with stickies. Support and onboarding will likely know the backstage steps better than anyone, so have them review those first.

**Doc:** Send the same content as a page or two afterward, with the corrections folded in.

## Exec team: 10 minutes

**Format:** 3–4 slides, with the one-page doc as a leave-behind. Don't walk the wall.

**What goes on each slide:**
1. **Frame and status:** the person, start and end, the decision, and what the map is built on.
2. **The journey in one picture:** the 7 stages, the emotion line (simplified), and 2–3 marked moments with a one-line reason each.
3. **The top 2–3 pain points:** where each occurs, what it costs (only with a number you have), and the evidence.
4. **The proposed next step:** up to three hypotheses, each with an owner and a date, plus the ask.

**Timing:** About 1 minute on the frame, 4 on the story, 3 on the moments and pain points, 2 on the decisions and the ask. Leave the other pain points and ideas in the doc for questions.

## Things to check before either session

- **Status line:** Every version must say what the map is built on. If the pain points and emotion line come from staff opinion, say that.
- **Emotion line:** Don't show a smooth curve to execs unless it was measured. Use labeled words per stage instead.
- **Ideas:** Show only the ones tied to a decision. Shelve the rest with a reason, so nobody thinks they were ignored.
