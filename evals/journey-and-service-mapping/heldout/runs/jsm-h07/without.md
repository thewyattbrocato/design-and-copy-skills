# Changing your delivery address

Follow these steps to update a delivery address in the app.

1. Open the app and tap **Account**.
2. Tap **Addresses**.
3. Tap the address you want to change.
4. Edit the address details.
5. Tap **Save**.

**Note:** If your order is already being picked, the change will apply to your next order, not the current one.
