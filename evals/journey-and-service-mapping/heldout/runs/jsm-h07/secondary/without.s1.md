## How to change your delivery address

1. Open the **Account** tab.
2. Tap **Addresses**.
3. Tap the address you want to change.
4. Edit the address details, then tap **Save**.

**Note:** If an order is already being picked, the new address applies to your next order. The order in progress will still be delivered to the original address.
