## How to change your delivery address

1. Open the app and tap **Account**.
2. Tap **Addresses**.
3. Tap the address you want to change.
4. Edit the address details, then tap **Save**.

**Note:** If an order is already being picked, your change applies only to your next order. The current order will still go to the original address.
