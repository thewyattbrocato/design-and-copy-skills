## How to change your delivery address

1. Open the **Account** tab.
2. Tap **Addresses**.
3. Tap the address you want to change.
4. Edit the address details, then tap **Save**.

**Note:** If we've already started picking an order, the new address applies only to your next order. Your current order will still go to the original address.
