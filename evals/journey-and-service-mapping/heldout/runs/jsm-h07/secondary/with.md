## How to change your delivery address

1. Open the **Account** tab.
2. Tap **Addresses**.
3. Tap the address you want to change.
4. Edit the address details, then tap **Save**.

Your new address will be used for your next order.

**If an order is already being picked:** Changes you make while an order is being picked don't apply to that order. The new address will be used for your next order.
