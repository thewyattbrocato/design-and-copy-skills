## Change your delivery address

1. Open **Account**.
2. Tap **Addresses**.
3. Tap the address you want to change.
4. Edit the address details.
5. Tap **Save**.

**Note:** If your order is already being picked, the change won't apply to it. Your new address will be used for your next order.
