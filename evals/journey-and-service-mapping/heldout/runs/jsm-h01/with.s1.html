Two moments that matter

1 · After the trial, choosing a plan

Why it matters: this is the decision point, and it is where we lose control of the outcome. The page confused 4 of 6. Two joined on the spot. Four went home to think. One of those got no follow-up email and joined only because a friend nudged her.

Evidence: 6 conversations. Unknown: how many trial-takers never join. [to measure]

2 · Waiting for firing

Why it matters: a wait with no status. Firing takes 2–3 weeks and nobody told 2 of 6, so they thought their pieces were lost. This is also the first moment the work they made is out of their hands.

Evidence: 6 conversations. Unknown: whether anyone left over this. [to collect]

Watch, not yet marked: month three. Three of 6 were wavering (time ×2, feeling no progress ×1), but we only have 6 voices and no renewal data.
