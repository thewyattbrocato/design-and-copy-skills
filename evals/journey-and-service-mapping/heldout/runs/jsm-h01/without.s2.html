Why they might leave

Try

A month-three check-in. Offer a skills goal or a pause option.

Where to start on Thursday

  1. Fix the basics: up-to-date class times, and what's included in each plan (glaze and firing).
  2. Follow up with every trial student who doesn't join on the spot.
  3. Set expectations early: firing time, shelf system and studio etiquette, ideally in an orientation.
  4. Check in around month three to help people who feel stuck or short on time.

Keep doing

The trial class and the teacher's welcome. Everything after it should feel as easy.
