The two moments to look at

A. After the trial, deciding whether to join

A decision point, and where our promise (a great trial) meets the paperwork. Four of six were confused by the plans, mainly whether glaze and firing are included. Two joined at the desk. Four went home, and one of those got no follow-up and joined only because a friend nudged her.

Why it matters: the people who left to think were won over by the class, then slowed by us.

B. Waiting for the first firing

A wait with no status. Firing takes 2 to 3 weeks and two members were never told, so they thought their pieces were lost. Their first finished work is the proof that membership is worth it.

Why it matters: a member who thinks their work is gone is at risk before month three.

Smaller fixes, no map needed

  • Update the class times on the website. Two people almost didn't come because of stale times (phase 2). Ticket-sized, [owner], [date].
