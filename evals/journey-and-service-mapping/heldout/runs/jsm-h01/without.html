What it's like to become a member

From the first Google search to around month three, based on conversations with six new members over the last month.

Stage 1

Finding us

Good reach, shaky first impression
What works
  • Most found us through a friend's Instagram post or a local search.
Where it hurts
2 of 6almost didn't come because class times on our site were out of date.
Stage 2

Trial class · $35

Our strongest moment
What works
  • Nearly everyone loved it.
  • The teacher made them feel it was okay to be bad at it.
"Okay to be bad at it" is the feeling to protect.
