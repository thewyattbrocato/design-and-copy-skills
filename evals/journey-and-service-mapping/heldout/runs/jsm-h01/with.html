Most found us via a friend's Instagram post or local search. Two joined at the front desk straight after the trial. One of the four who went home only joined because a friend nudged her.

Two moments to mark

A. Choosing a plan after the trial

The trial is the strongest thing we do, then the page and the follow-up let people drift.

Why it matters: it's a decision and a hand-off. 4 of 6 were confused about glaze and firing. 4 of 6 went home to think. One heard nothing and joined only through a friend. Some members were lost without our noticing.

B. Waiting for the first firing

A new member leaves their work with us and hears nothing for 2–3 weeks.

Why it matters: it's a wait with no status. 2 of 6 thought their pieces were lost. It lands in the first month, when they decide whether this is for them.

Suspected, not yet evidenced: month 3. Time and "not getting better" are the stated reasons for leaving, but only three people mentioned them. Worth asking more members before acting.
