Becoming a member

From the first search to month three, as told by six new members from the last month.

4 of 6
couldn't tell if the monthly plan includes glaze and firing
4 of 6
went home to think about joining; 1 of them never got a follow-up email
2 of 6
thought their pieces were lost during the 2–3 week firing wait
3 of 6
were considering not renewing at month three
  1. 1

    Discover

    Found us through a friend's Instagram post or a local search.

    Snag · 2 of 6 almost didn't come

    Class times on our website were out of date.

  2. 2

    Trial class

    $35 intro class.

    Went well · nearly everyone

    The teacher made them feel it was okay to be bad at it. Most loved it.

  3. 3

    Choose a plan

    Looking at the membership options page after the trial.

    Snag · 4 of 6 confused

    Unclear whether the monthly plan includes glaze and firing.

  4. 4

    Decide

    2 joined at the front desk. 4 went home to think.

    Snag · the 4 who went home

    1 never got a follow-up email and only joined because a friend nudged them.

  5. 5

    First month

    Open studio hours are the big draw.

    Went well · open studio

    Members love the freedom of open hours.

    Snags

    3 of 6 felt in the way of regulars.
    2 of 6 couldn't work out the shelf system for unfinished pieces.

  6. 6

    First firing

    Pieces go into the kiln and come back later.

    Snag · 2 of 6 thought pieces were lost

    Nobody told them firing takes 2–3 weeks.

  7. 7

    Month three

    The point where renewal gets decided.

    Snag · 3 of 6 thinking of not renewing

    2 cited time. 1 felt they weren't getting better.

Source: conversations with six new Kiln & Co. members over the past month. Small sample; treat as directional.
