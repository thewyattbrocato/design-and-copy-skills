Stage 1

Discover

First search or friend's post

What they do

Find us through a friend's Instagram post or a local search.

What they feel

Curious, but unsure whether the class fits their schedule.

Where it breaks

2 of 6 nearly didn't come: class times on the site were out of date.
Opportunity Make class times on the site a weekly check, not a yearly one.
Stage 2

Trial

$35 trial class

What they do

Book the trial and show up as beginners.

What they feel

Nearly everyone loved it. The teacher made them feel it was okay to be bad at it.

Where it breaks

Nothing reported. This is our strongest stage.

Opportunity Protect what the teacher does here and make it the model for every class.
Stage 3

Decide

Membership options page and front desk

What they do

Compare plans on the website, then decide at the desk or at home.

What they feel

Confused about what they're paying for.

Where it breaks

4 of 6 couldn't tell if the monthly plan includes glaze and firing.
4 of 6 went home to think instead of joining on the spot.
1 never got a follow-up email and joined only after a friend nudged her.
Opportunity State what each plan includes in plain words, and send a follow-up email to everyone who took a trial.
Stage 4

First month

Open studio and shelves

What they do

Use open studio hours, make work, and store unfinished pieces on shelves.

What they feel

They love the open studio hours.

Where it breaks

3 of 6 felt they were in the way of regulars.
2 of 6 couldn't work out the shelf system for unfinished pieces.
Opportunity Add a short orientation for new members and label the shelves so the system is clear without asking.
Stage 5

Month three

Renewal decision

What they do

Decide whether to renew, based on time and progress.

What they feel

Tired of the time commitment, or unsure they are improving.

Where it breaks

2 of 6 were thinking of not renewing because of time.
1 of 6 felt she wasn't getting better.
2 of 6 weren't told firing takes 2 to 3 weeks and thought their pieces were lost.
Opportunity Tell members about the firing timeline when they start, and check in on progress before month three.

What to take into Thursday

  1. The trial works. The problems start after it, when people try to decide and then to settle in.
  2. Three fixes cost little: accurate class times, plain plan details, and a follow-up email to every trial member.
  3. Set expectations early: what the plan covers, how the shelves work, and that firing takes 2 to 3 weeks.
  4. Month three is a progress and time question. Check in before the renewal date, not after it.
Based on conversations with six new members over one month. A small sample, so treat the numbers as signals to test, not final measures.
