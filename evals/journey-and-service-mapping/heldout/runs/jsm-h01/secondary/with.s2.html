Becoming a Kiln & Co. member

From the first search to month three. Based on conversations with six new members over the last month.

The journey

Stage 1

Find us

What happens

A friend's Instagram post, or a local search.

2 of 6 snag

Class times on the website were out of date. Two almost didn't come.

Stage 2

Trial class

What happens

$35 trial class with a teacher.

Bright spot

Nearly everyone loved it. The teacher made them feel it was okay to be bad at it.

Stage 3

Choose a plan

What happens

Reads the membership options page.

4 of 6 snag

Unsure whether the monthly plan includes glaze and firing.

Stage 4

Decide

What happens

Two join at the front desk. Four go home to think it over.

1 snag

One never got a follow-up email. She joined only after a friend nudged her.

Stage 5

First month

What happens

Open studio hours, and a shelf for unfinished pieces.

Bright spot

They love the open studio hours.

3 felt in the way of regulars
2 couldn't work out the shelf system
Stage 6

Waiting on the kiln

What happens

Pieces go in to be fired.

2 of 6 snag

Nobody told them firing takes 2 to 3 weeks. They thought their pieces were lost.

Stage 7

Month three

What happens

Deciding whether to renew.

2 thinking of not renewing: time
1 not renewing: feels no improvement
