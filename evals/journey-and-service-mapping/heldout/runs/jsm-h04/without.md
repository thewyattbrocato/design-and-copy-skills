Mapping the full return journey, from the customer's click to the warehouse, refund and restocking steps, shows us where money is leaking today: refund delays, lost or unsellable stock, repeat support contacts, and customers who don't come back. Two weeks of work produces a ranked list of fixes with estimated savings, so we can decide where to invest based on evidence rather than guesswork. It's a cost-finding exercise, not a design exercise.
