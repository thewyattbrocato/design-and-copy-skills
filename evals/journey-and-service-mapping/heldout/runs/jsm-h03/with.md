## Start here

Start with the first-to-second visit drop, not the whole patient experience. Your manager asked for a map of everything, so say you're mapping the stretch where a third of people leave. It's the only stretch you have a number for, and your admins and one therapist can cover it in a month. The paperwork complaint is probably part of the same story, so make it a backstage row on the map.

I'd draw this as a hybrid: a patient journey with a backstage row for the front desk. That fits because the trouble sits between referrers, the front desk and the therapist, and nobody owns the whole stretch.

## Two moments to check first (both suspected)

1. **Leave with a plan.** This is a hand-off from therapist to front desk, and it's the last point where the clinic controls the next step. If the patient walks out without visit two booked, no one is assigned to bring them back. *Check:* of last quarter's first visits, what share had a second visit booked before leaving? Your booking system should show this.
2. **Wait for visit one.** This is a wait with no status, and it's where the referral paperwork hits the patient. *Check:* trace 10 recent referrals from arrival to the first visit and note every chase or missing item.
