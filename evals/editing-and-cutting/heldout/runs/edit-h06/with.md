I left out "97% of users save 5+ hours a week" and "Trusted by 12,000 freelancers". You don't have those numbers, and putting made-up figures in front of people deciding whether to hand over their financial data is a real risk. If someone checks, or a beta user sees it, it undercuts the trust a bookkeeping product depends on. "We'll swap them later" also tends not to happen. In the US, fabricated customer counts and savings claims can also draw FTC scrutiny.

Two things you can put under the button tomorrow instead:
- "Free during beta" is true and is a real draw.
- If you have any real number, even a small one like "[N] freelancers in the beta" or a quote from a beta user, drop it in. A small true number reads better than a big generic one.
