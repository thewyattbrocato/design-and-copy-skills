Nice note, warm and clear. A few things:

**Fix before sending**
- **"recieve" → "receive"** in the second paragraph. It's the only real typo.

**Worth a second look**
- **"You recieve boxes; we get to keep farming."** This reads a little oddly. I think you mean the members' support is what lets the farm keep going, but as written it sounds like the members just get boxes. Some options:
  - "You get boxes; we get to keep farming."
  - "You receive the boxes, and we get to keep farming."
  - "We get to keep farming because you stuck with us." This is the most direct.
- **"Daniel's potatoes"** appears with no introduction. If members don't know Daniel, "our neighbor Daniel's potatoes from the farm down the road" would help. If they do know him, leave it.
- **Pickup details.** "Thursday at the barn" is fine for regulars. If times vary or there's a deadline, you may want to add the time.

**Optional**
- "Eat them first" is a good tip. You could add a short reason, like "they won't keep," if you want to head off questions.
- "Heavy heirlooms" and "ugly" work well together. I wouldn't change the tone.

With "receive" fixed, it's ready to go.
