- Split the long third sentence in two so the fee information stands on its own.
- Swapped "members who make deposits" for "those who make deposits" to avoid repeating "members."

Unchanged: "variable rate," "up to," "may," "typically," and "Pre-Authorized Debit" are all exactly as they were, in the same positions.
