**Welcome to Tidewater Food Pantry**

Thank you for volunteering. We've served the Tidewater area for over twenty years, and we couldn't do it without you. We believe everyone deserves good food and to be treated with dignity. Everyone who comes through our door is a neighbor, not a case. Keep that in mind in everything you do, from stocking shelves to saying good morning.

**Shifts and arriving**

- Shifts are Tuesday and Thursday, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., and Saturday, 8 to 11 a.m.
- Arrive 15 minutes early to sign in, put your things away, and hear the shift lead's briefing. Clients are often already waiting, and we open exactly at shift start.
- Use the side door. The front door is for clients.
- Sign in on the tablet inside the side door every time. It tracks volunteer hours and shows who is in the building in an emergency.
- If you can't make a shift, tell us at least 24 hours ahead: volunteers@tidewaterpantry.example or 555-0142. The more notice, the better, because we depend on having enough people.
- Don't volunteer if you've had a fever, vomiting, or diarrhea in the past 48 hours.

**What to wear and hygiene**

- Closed-toe shoes are required, no exceptions. Wear comfortable clothes that can get dusty, and tie back long hair.
- We provide aprons and gloves. Wear disposable gloves when handling fresh produce or opening packages.
- Wash your hands with soap and warm water for at least 20 seconds when you arrive, after any break, after using the restroom, and whenever you change tasks.

**Food safety**

This is your most important responsibility. Ask your shift lead if anything is unclear.

- The walk-in cooler must stay at 41°F or below. The shift lead logs the temperature at the start of every shift. If it's above 41°F, don't take food out or put any in. Tell the shift lead right away, and don't try to fix the cooler yourself.
- Don't shelve food that is open, home-canned, homemade, or past its "use by" date.
- Shelf-stable items may be given out up to six months past their "best by" date if in perfect condition.
- Discard any can that is bulging, leaking, or dented on the seam. A small dent in the body, away from the seam, is fine.
- If you're unsure about an item, put it on the gray cart marked "Ask" for the shift lead to review.
- Return peanut-free and gluten-free items to their labeled shelf. Never mix them with other stock.

**Working with clients**

- Never ask why someone needs food.
- Never photograph clients or the line outside.
- Don't share client information with anyone, including other volunteers outside the pantry.
- If a client seems upset, stay calm and kind, and get the shift lead.
- Say hello, smile, and offer to carry bags to the car.

**Safety and emergencies**

- Emergency exits: the side door and the loading dock door at the back.
- The fire extinguisher is on the wall by the kitchen. The first aid kit is on the shelf above the sign-in tablet.
- In an emergency, call 911 first, then tell the shift lead.
- Report any injury, however small, to the shift lead for the incident log.

**Quick checklist**

Arrive 15 minutes early. Sign in at the side door. Wear closed-toe shoes. Wash your hands. Keep the cooler at 41°F or below. Ask when in doubt. Give 24 hours' notice if you can't come.

We're glad to have you with us.
