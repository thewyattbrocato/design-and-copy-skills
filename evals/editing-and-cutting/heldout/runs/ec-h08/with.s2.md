Welcome to Tidewater Food Pantry

Thank you for volunteering. We've served the Tidewater area for over twenty years, and we couldn't do it without you. Keep this page handy for your first shift.

**Our values**
Everyone deserves good food and deserves to be treated with dignity while getting it. Everyone who comes through our door is a neighbor, not a case.

**Shifts and arriving**
Tuesday and Thursday, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. Saturday, 8 to 11 a.m.
- Arrive 15 minutes early to sign in, put your things away, and hear the shift lead's briefing. Clients are often already waiting, and we open exactly at the start of the shift.
- Use the side door. The front door is for clients.
- Sign in on the tablet inside the side door every time. It tracks volunteer hours and shows who is in the building in an emergency.
- If you can't make a shift, tell us at least 24 hours ahead: volunteers@tidewaterpantry.example or 555-0142.
- If you've had a fever, vomiting or diarrhea in the past 48 hours, stay home.

**What to wear and hygiene**
- Closed-toe shoes are required, with no exceptions. Tie back long hair. Wear comfortable clothes that can get dusty, since we lift and move boxes.
- We provide aprons and gloves. Wear disposable gloves when handling fresh produce or opening packages.
- Wash your hands with soap and warm water for at least 20 seconds when you arrive, after any break, after the restroom, and whenever you change tasks.

**Food safety**
This is our most important responsibility. Ask your shift lead if anything is unclear.
- The walk-in cooler must stay at 41°F or below. The shift lead logs the temperature at the start of every shift. If it's above 41°F, don't take food out or put any in. Tell the shift lead right away, and don't try to fix the cooler yourself.
- Don't shelve food that is open, home-canned or homemade, or past its "use by" date.
- We can distribute shelf-stable items up to six months past their "best by" date if they're in perfect condition.
- Discard any can that is bulging, leaking, or dented on the seam. A small dent in the body, away from the seam, is fine.
- If you're unsure about an item, put it on the gray "Ask" cart for the shift lead to review.
- Peanut-free and gluten-free items have their own labeled shelf. Always return them there and never mix them with other stock.

**Working with clients**
- Never ask why someone needs food.
- Never photograph clients or the line outside.
- Don't share client information with anyone, including other volunteers outside the pantry.
- If a client seems upset, stay calm and kind, and get the shift lead.
- Otherwise, say hello, smile, and offer to carry bags to the car.

**Safety and emergencies**
- Emergency exits: the side door and the loading dock door at the back.
- The fire extinguisher is on the wall by the kitchen. The first aid kit is on the shelf above the sign-in tablet.
- In an emergency, call 911 first, then tell the shift lead.
- Report every injury, however small, to the shift lead for the incident log.

Thank you for being part of the Tidewater family.
