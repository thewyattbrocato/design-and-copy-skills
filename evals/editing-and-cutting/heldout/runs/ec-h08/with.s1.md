**Welcome to Tidewater Food Pantry**

Thank you for volunteering. We've served the Tidewater area for over twenty years, and we couldn't do it without you. Everyone who comes through our door is a neighbor, not a case, and deserves good food and dignity. Keep that in mind in everything you do.

**Shifts and arriving**
- Shifts are Tuesday and Thursday, 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m., and Saturday, 8:00 to 11:00 a.m.
- Arrive 15 minutes early to sign in, put your things away, and hear the shift lead's briefing. Clients are often already waiting, and doors open exactly at shift start.
- Use the side door. The front door is for clients. Sign in on the tablet just inside every time. It tracks volunteer hours and shows who is in the building in an emergency.
- If you can't make a shift, tell us at least 24 hours ahead: volunteers@tidewaterpantry.example or 555-0142. We depend on having enough people each week.
- Stay home if you've had a fever, vomiting or diarrhea in the past 48 hours.

**What to wear and hygiene**
- Closed-toe shoes are required, no exceptions, because of dropped cans and crates. Wear comfortable clothes that can get dusty, and tie back long hair.
- Aprons and gloves are provided. Wear disposable gloves when handling fresh produce or opening packages.
- Wash your hands with soap and warm water for at least 20 seconds when you arrive, after any break, after using the restroom, and when changing tasks.

**Food safety**
This is our most important responsibility. Ask your shift lead if anything is unclear.
- The walk-in cooler must stay at 41°F or below. The shift lead checks it at the start of every shift and logs the temperature. If it's above 41°F, take nothing out and put nothing in, tell the shift lead right away, and don't try to fix the cooler yourself.
- Don't shelve food that is open, home-canned or homemade, or past its "use by" date.
- Shelf-stable items can be distributed up to six months past their "best by" date if in perfect condition.
- Discard any can that is bulging, leaking, or dented on the seam, as these can signal contamination. A small dent in the body, away from the seam, is fine.
- If you're unsure about an item, put it on the gray cart marked "Ask" for the shift lead to review.
- Keep peanut-free and gluten-free items on their labeled shelf, and never mix them with other stock.

**Working with clients**
- Never ask why a client needs food.
- Never photograph clients or the line outside.
- Don't share client information with anyone, including other volunteers outside the pantry.
- If a client is upset, stay calm and kind, and get the shift lead.
- Otherwise, say hello, smile, and offer to carry bags to the car.

**Safety and emergencies**
- Emergency exits are the side door and the loading dock door at the back. The fire extinguisher is on the wall by the kitchen. The first aid kit is on the shelf above the sign-in tablet.
- In an emergency, call 911 first, then tell the shift lead.
- Report every injury, however small, to the shift lead for the incident log.

Thank you for being part of the Tidewater family.
