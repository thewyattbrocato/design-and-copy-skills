**Welcome to Tidewater Food Pantry**

Thank you for volunteering! We've served the Tidewater area for over 20 years, and we couldn't do it without people like you. Everyone who comes through our door is a neighbor, not a case. Please treat everyone with dignity, respect, and kindness, whether you're stocking, packing, or greeting.

**Shifts and arriving**
- Shifts are Tuesday and Thursday, 9:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m., and Saturday, 8:00–11:00 a.m.
- Arrive 15 minutes early to sign in, store your belongings, and hear the shift lead's briefing. Doors open to clients exactly at start time, and clients are often already waiting.
- Use the side door. The front door is for clients.
- Sign in on the tablet inside the side door every time. It tracks volunteer hours and shows who's in the building in an emergency.
- If you can't make a shift, tell us at least 24 hours ahead at volunteers@tidewaterpantry.example or 555-0142. We understand things come up, but we depend on having enough people.
- Stay home if you've had a fever, vomiting, or diarrhea in the past 48 hours.

**What to wear and hygiene**
- Wear comfortable clothes you don't mind getting dusty. We lift and move a lot of boxes.
- Closed-toe shoes are required, with no exceptions, because of dropped cans and crates.
- Tie back long hair.
- We provide aprons and gloves. Wear disposable gloves when handling fresh produce or opening packages.
- Wash your hands with soap and warm water for at least 20 seconds when you arrive, after any break, after using the restroom, and whenever you change tasks.

**Food safety**
This is our most important responsibility. Ask your shift lead if anything is unclear.
- The walk-in cooler must stay at 41°F or below. The shift lead checks it at the start of each shift and logs the temperature. If it's ever above 41°F, don't remove or add any food. Tell the shift lead right away, and don't try to fix the cooler yourself.
- Don't shelve food that is open, home-canned or homemade, or past its "use by" date.
- Shelf-stable items may be given out up to six months past their "best by" date if they're in perfect condition.
- Discard any can that is bulging, leaking, or dented on the seam. A small dent in the body, away from the seam, is fine.
- If you're unsure about an item, put it on the gray "Ask" cart for the shift lead to review.
- Keep peanut-free and gluten-free items on their labeled shelf, and never mix them with other stock.

**Working with clients**
- Never ask why someone needs food.
- Never photograph clients or the line outside.
- Don't share any client information with anyone, including other volunteers outside the pantry.
- If a client is upset, stay calm and kind, and get the shift lead.
- Otherwise, say hello, smile, and offer to carry bags to the car.

**Safety and emergencies**
- The emergency exits are the side door and the loading dock door at the back.
- The fire extinguisher is on the wall by the kitchen. The first aid kit is on the shelf above the sign-in tablet.
- In an emergency, call 911 first, then tell the shift lead.
- Report every injury, however small, to the shift lead so it goes in the incident log.

**Quick checklist**
Arrive 15 minutes early. Sign in at the side door. Wear closed-toe shoes. Wash your hands. Keep the cooler at 41°F or below. Ask when in doubt. Give 24 hours' notice if you can't come.

We're so glad you're here!
