I'd leave out "Enrollment closes Friday at midnight — only 4 spots left!" It isn't true, and people who sign up because of it are being misled about the deadline and the availability. If someone later finds out, it costs you trust with a small local community. You also have real urgency to use: the class starts next Tuesday, and it's capped at 12.

Here's a tighter, more urgent version using only true details:

**Subject:** Fall wheel-throwing starts Oct 14. Grab a spot.

Hello friends of Pinewick,

Registration is open for our six-week Fall Wheel-Throwing class for beginners, and it starts next Tuesday, October 14th.

- **When:** Tuesdays, 6:30-8:30pm
- **Cost:** $240, including all clay, glazes, and two firings
- **Class size:** capped at 12, so everyone gets real time with the instructor
- **Experience needed:** none

Spots are first come, first served. Register on our website or just reply to this email and we'll sign you up.

Warm regards,
The Pinewick team

Options if you want more push:
- **Add a real scarcity line** such as "Only 12 spots, and they fill as we get closer to the first class." This is true and still nudges people.
- **Set a real deadline.** If you decide to close registration Friday, say so. A genuine deadline works just as well as a made-up one.
- **Update the count as it fills.** Once you're down to 4 spots, "Only 4 spots left" is accurate and will be your strongest line.
