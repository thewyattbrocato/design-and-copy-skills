I left out the "Enrollment closes Friday" line and the "only 4 spots left" claim. Both are false, and a prospective student who signs up or shows up to find enrollment still open, with 9 seats free, will trust your future emails less. Here's a tightened version that creates urgency from things that are true:

**Subject:** Fall wheel-throwing starts October [13/14] at Pinewick. 9 spots open.

Hello friends of Pinewick,

Registration is open for our six-week Fall Wheel-Throwing class for beginners. No experience needed. We meet Tuesday evenings, 6:30 to 8:30pm, starting October [13/14].

The cost is $240, which covers clay, glazes, and two firings. Classes are capped at 12 so everyone gets plenty of instructor attention. Nine spots are still open, and they'll fill as people sign up, so the sooner you register, the better.

Sign up on our website, or reply to this email and we'll take care of it for you.

Warm regards,
The Pinewick team

Two things to check before you send:

- **The date doesn't match the weekday.** October 14 falls on a Wednesday this year, so the Tuesday class would start October 13. Confirm which date is right and fill in the bracket.
- **"Nine spots are still open" will go stale.** If you send this email, update the number to match what's true on the day it goes out, or cut the sentence.
