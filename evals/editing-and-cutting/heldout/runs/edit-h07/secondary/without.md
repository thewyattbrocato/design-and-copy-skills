I left out "Enrollment closes Friday" and "only 4 spots left." Enrollment stays open until the first class, and those lines would tell people something that isn't true. Readers who check and find the class still open, or who see the spot count change, are likely to trust you less, and it could create refund complaints. The one-week start date and the 9 open spots are real, and they still give people a reason to act now.

If you want a real Friday cutoff, you could set one and enforce it. Otherwise, I'd send this soon, since the class starts in a week.
