Two things to check before sending:

- **Date and weekday:** October 14, 2026 is a Wednesday, not a Tuesday. If classes meet Tuesdays, the first session is probably October 13. Please confirm the start date.
- **"One week away":** This assumes the email goes out today, October 7. If it goes out later, change that line or drop it.
