I'd drop that line. "Closes Friday" and "only 4 spots left" are both false, and fake scarcity can cost you with a small studio's community. If someone signs up in a panic and later sees the class still open, or sees it wasn't full, you lose trust. The email can still be urgent with true facts: the class starts next Tuesday, and the class is capped at 12.

**Subject:** Fall wheel-throwing starts next Tuesday: join us

Hello friends of Pinewick,

Registration is open for our six-week Fall Wheel-Throwing class for beginners, and the first session is **next Tuesday, October 14th**.

- **When:** Tuesdays, 6:30 to 8:30pm
- **Cost:** $240, including all clay, glazes and two firings
- **Experience:** None needed
- **Size:** Capped at 12, so you get real one-on-one time with the instructor

Spots are filling, and you'll want to be signed up before we start. Register on our website, or just reply to this email and we'll take care of it.

Warm regards,
The Pinewick team

If you'd like to push harder, there are a few true options:
- "Sign up by Sunday so we can have your apron and clay ready." This works if it's real, and it's a genuine reason to register early.
- "Only 12 seats, and it's the only beginner wheel class this fall." Use this only if it's true.
- Once you have more sign-ups, say so: "Over half full" is accurate and still creates momentum.

I wrote "Spots are filling" assuming at least a few have gone. If 3 of 12 is the real count, that's fair. If none have, change it to "Space is limited to 12."
