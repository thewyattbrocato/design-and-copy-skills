I'd leave out "Enrollment closes Friday at midnight — only 4 spots left!" The deadline and the spot count are both false, and you'd be using them to push people into paying $240. Even if nobody checks, someone who signs up because of it and later learns the truth is likely to feel misled. The email also has real urgency you can use. The class starts in one week, and the 12-person cap is real.

Here's a tighter version:

**Subject:** Fall wheel-throwing class starts next Tuesday: 12 spots

Hello friends of Pinewick,

Our six-week Fall Wheel-Throwing class for beginners starts **Tuesday, October 14th**, one week from today. Spots are limited to 12 so everyone gets real time with the instructor.

- **When:** Tuesdays, 6:30–8:30pm
- **Cost:** $240, including all clay, glazes and two firings
- **Experience:** None needed

Reserve your spot on our website or just reply to this email and we'll take care of it.

Warm regards,
The Pinewick team

If you want more pressure that's still true, you could:
- Set a real deadline, such as "register by Friday to get your spot confirmed before we start." Only do this if you'll actually enforce it.
- Say "Only 12 spots, and they fill on a first-come basis." This is accurate.
- Update the count honestly as spots fill, for example "Down to 5 spots" once that's the case. That claim gets stronger over time.
