# First call cheat sheet (30 min)

**GOAL: find out if a close/books problem exists and is worth solving. Nothing else.**
**END ON: a named next meeting, with a date, time, and who attends, said aloud.**

## 0:00–3:00 Open
- "I'll ask some questions about how your books work today, and you can ask me anything. At the end we'll decide together if a next step makes sense, or if it doesn't. Fair?"
- Then ask: **"What made you take this call?"** Then listen.

## 3:00–8:00 Situation (get the facts)
| Ask | Number you want |
|---|---|
| Who does the books today? | Person, hours/week |
| What software? | Tool name |
| How many people and active jobs? | Headcount, jobs |
| When did you last close a month, and how long did it take? | Days after month-end `[to learn]` |

## 8:00–22:00 Problem (size it in their numbers)
Pick 3–4. Don't run the whole list.
1. **"How late is the month usually closed?"** → days late vs. what they need
2. **"Do you know profit by job, or by company only?"** → yes or no, and how they know
3. **"Last time numbers were wrong or late, what happened?"** → the specific miss and what it cost
4. **"Who chases receivables, retainage, and pay apps?"** → hours/week, $ outstanding `[to learn]`
5. **"Does a bank, bonding company, or CPA ask for financials?"** → what they need, how often, how late it is
6. **"How much of your time goes to books?"** → owner hours/week
7. **"What have you tried to fix it?"** → past attempts and why they failed

**Vague answer?** Say: "How many, against what you need?"
**Small problem?** Say so. Shrink the offer or point them elsewhere.

## 22:00–26:00 Gate check (all three before any proposal)
- [ ] **A felt problem** they can size
- [ ] **Signer** is on the call, or I know the route to them (partner, spouse, CPA?)
- [ ] **What they'll put in:** access to the books, 30 minutes, bank/payroll logins

If one is missing, that becomes the next step. Don't pitch past it.

## 26:00–30:00 Close
1. Recap their problem in their words and their numbers.
2. If asked about price: give one figure and what it covers, then **stop talking**. Don't discount on the first reaction. `[your price]`
3. **Next step, aloud:** "Thursday at 10, you and [signer], I'll bring [X]. I'll send the invite and a recap today."
4. **If they say no:** confirm it, ask for the reason in their words, and stop.

## If you lose the thread
- Pause. Say: **"Let me make sure I have this right."** Repeat their last point.
- Then ask: **"What's the hardest part of that?"**
- Silence is fine. Count to five before you fill it.

## Never
- Demo, quote, or propose before the gate is met.
- End with "let me know" or "I'll follow up." Use a date and an owner.
- Invent a savings figure. Use theirs or `[to learn]`.
