# First-Call Cheat Sheet (30 min)

**GOAL: find out if there's a problem worth solving. Don't pitch.**
**END ON: a dated next step. Who, what, when, said out loud.**

---

### 0:00–3:00 | OPEN
- "Thanks for the time. I'll ask about how your books work today, tell you if I think I can help, and if so we'll pick a next step. Sound okay?"
- "Anything you want to be sure we cover?"
- Before the call, fill in what you already know from their site or referral. Say it back ("I saw X, still right?") and don't ask for it again.

### 3:00–20:00 | ASK (listen, jot the number)
| Ask | Number you want |
|---|---|
| "Walk me through how the books get done today. Who does it?" | Who / hours per week |
| "When do you usually know last month's numbers?" | Days after month-end `[to learn]` |
| "How do you track profit per job?" | Job costing: yes / no / spreadsheet |
| "How do progress billing, retainage, and change orders get tracked?" | Where it breaks |
| "Last time the books were wrong or late, what happened?" | The specific miss and its cost, in their words |
| "Who needs the financials? Bank, bonding, CPA, you?" | Outside deadlines |
| "What would 'fixed' look like in 6 months?" | Their target and why |

**Vague answer?** Ask: "How many? Against what you need?"
**Stuck?** Ask: "Tell me about the last time that happened."

### 20:00–25:00 | GATE CHECK (all 3 before any proposal or price)
- [ ] **Felt problem they can size:** ______
- [ ] **Who signs?** (Name, or how it gets decided): ______
- [ ] **What they'll put in:** time, access to bank/QuickBooks, a partner or spouse on the next call: ______

Missing one? Don't pitch. The next step is to get it.

**Small problem or poor fit?** Say so and say why. Shrink the offer or point them elsewhere.

### 25:00–30:00 | CLOSE
1. Recap in their words: "So the issue is ___, it cost you ___, and you want ___."
2. Give a recommendation, or say what you still need.
3. **Book it before hanging up:** "Let's do ___ on [date/time]. I'll send the invite now. Can [signer] join?"
4. "I'll email a recap today."

**If it's a no:** confirm it, write down the reason in their words, and stop.

---

### IF YOU LOSE THE THREAD
- Say: "Let me make sure I have this right…" and repeat the last thing they said.
- Ask: "What's the biggest headache in the books right now?"
- Pausing to look at your notes is fine.

### IF THEY ASK PRICE EARLY
Give a range and what moves it (headcount, number of jobs, backlog cleanup). Then say: "I'd rather confirm it fits before I quote a firm number."

### IF THEY SAY "SEND ME INFO"
Ask: "Happy to. What would you want to see in it to decide?" Then book the next call.

---

**Two things to do before you use this:**
- Pre-fill the "already known" line from your CRM or their site.
- Don't quote savings or results from other clients unless you have a real number you can back up. Use theirs.
