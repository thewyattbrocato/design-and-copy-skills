# 30-Minute First Call Cheat Sheet

**Goal of the call:** Learn if they have a real problem, then book a next step (proposal call or a look at their books). You are not trying to close today.

---

## ⏱ Flow

| Min | Step | Your move |
|---|---|---|
| **0–3** | **Open** | Set the agenda and get permission |
| **3–18** | **Discover** | Ask questions and listen. They should talk 70% of the time |
| **18–23** | **Reflect + fit** | Summarize their pain and check you've got it right |
| **23–27** | **Pitch (short)** | Tie what you do to *their* words |
| **27–30** | **Next step** | Book it before hanging up |

---

## 🟢 Open (say this)
> "Thanks for the time. I'd like to spend most of it learning how you handle the books today, then tell you quickly what I do, and we'll decide if a next step makes sense. Fair? Anything you want to make sure we cover?"

---

## 🔍 Discover: Ask in This Order
1. **Who does the books now?** (Owner? Spouse? Office manager? Part-time bookkeeper? CPA only at tax time?)
2. **When do you see accurate numbers each month?** (Day 10? Day 45? Never?)
3. **What system?** (QuickBooks? Desktop or Online? Spreadsheets?)
4. **Do you know which jobs made money?** (Job costing, WIP)
5. **What's the headache?** (Pay apps, retainage, change orders, payroll, 1099 subs, lien waivers, sales tax)
6. **What happens when the numbers are late or wrong?** (Cash surprises, bonding or bank asks, tax mess, owner up at 10pm)
7. **Have you tried to fix this before?** What happened?
8. **If this were solved, what changes for you?**

**Follow-ups to keep them talking:**
"Tell me more." • "What did that cost you?" • "How long has that been going on?"

---

## 🚩 Pain Signals to Listen For
- Books **30+ days behind**, or "my CPA fixes it in March"
- Owner or spouse doing the books at night
- **No job-level profit** ("I think we did OK on that one")
- Cash tight despite being busy
- Retainage or billings not tracked
- Bank or **surety/bonding** wants financials and they scramble
- Messy payroll, 1099s, or **workers' comp audits**
- Got burned by a past bookkeeper

---

## ✅ Qualify (get these by min 18)
- [ ] **Pain** is real and costs them money or time
- [ ] **Decision-maker** is on the call (or who else decides?)
- [ ] **Timing**: why now?
- [ ] **Budget comfort**: "Have you set aside a budget for this, or would this be new spend?"
- [ ] **Size/fit**: 5–30 employees, uses or will use a real accounting system

---

## 🪞 Reflect (min 18)
> "So what I'm hearing is: [books are X days late], [you can't see job profit], and it's costing you [specific thing they said]. Did I get that right? Anything I missed?"

---

## 🎯 Pitch (keep under 4 min)
> "We take bookkeeping off your plate and deliver a **monthly close by day [X]**, with **job-level profit** and clean books you can show a bank or bonding company. You stop chasing the numbers and just read them."

Then tie it to *their* pain: "You said ___. Here's how that changes."

Don't walk through every service. Pick the 1–2 things they care about.

---

## 🛡 Objections: One-Line Responses

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| **"Too expensive."** | "Compared to what? What's it costing you now in your time, late numbers, or surprises?" |
| **"My CPA / wife / nephew does it."** | "Great. How's that working for getting numbers on time? We often work alongside your CPA." |
| **"We're fine."** | "Good to hear. When do you see last month's numbers, and do you know which jobs made money?" |
| **"Send me info."** | "Happy to. What specifically should it answer? Can we also put 20 minutes on the calendar to go through it?" |
| **"Need to think about it."** | "Of course. What would you be weighing?" |
| **"Need to talk to my partner."** | "Makes sense. Can we get them on a quick call this week?" |
| **"Busy season."** | "That's usually when books slip. What would a light first step look like?" |

---

## 🏁 Close: Book the Next Step
> "Here's what I'd suggest: [a 20-min follow-up where I send a scope + price / you share a read-only QuickBooks login so I can look at your books]. Does **Tuesday at 10** or **Thursday at 2** work?"

Get a **date and time on the calendar before you hang up.**

Confirm: "I'll send a recap by end of day, including [X]."

---

## 🧭 If You Lose the Thread
- **Pause and breathe.** A 3-second silence feels long to you and normal to them.
- Say: *"Let me make sure I've got this right…"* and summarize. It buys you time and looks attentive.
- Ask: *"What else is on your mind about this?"*
- Fall back to the **#6 question**: *"What happens when the numbers are late or wrong?"*
- Check the time. Behind? Skip to **Reflect**.

---

## 📝 After the Call (5 min)
- Their exact words on pain
- Decision-maker and timing
- Next step and date
- Send recap email same day
