# First-Call Cheat Sheet: Construction Sub Bookkeeping & Monthly Close

**Goal of the call:** Learn their numbers pain, confirm fit, and book the next step. Don't pitch until minute 20.

## Clock
| Min | Phase | Say / Do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Open | "Thanks for making time. I'll ask questions for about 15 minutes, then tell you what I'd suggest. Sound good?" |
| 2–14 | Discover | Use the questions below |
| 14–20 | Pain & impact | "What does that cost you?" |
| 20–25 | Your fit | 2 min max. Tie to what they said |
| 25–30 | Next step | Book it. Don't leave without a date |

## Discovery (ask in order, skip what's answered)
1. **Snapshot:** "Walk me through your business. Trades, crew size, how many active jobs?"
2. **Close today:** "When does your month close now, and who does it?"
3. **Days to close:** "How many days after month-end before you see real numbers?"
4. **Job costing:** "Can you see profit by job while it's running, or only at the end?"
5. **WIP & billing:** "How do you track over/under billings and % complete?"
6. **Retainage & lien waivers:** "How are retainage and lien waivers tracked?"
7. **Payroll:** "Do you do certified payroll? Any union or prevailing wage work?"
8. **Software:** "What runs your books? QuickBooks, Sage, Foundation, something else?"
9. **Tax & 1099s:** "Who handles sales tax, 1099s, and year-end with your CPA?"
10. **Cash:** "Do you ever get surprised by cash position or a bad job?"

## Pain → Impact (the money question)
- "What happens when a job is over budget and you find out late?"
- "What did the last surprise cost you?"
- "If you had clean job numbers every month, what would you do differently?"
- Write down their **exact words** for the pain. Reuse them in your close.

## Your Fit (2 min, only after pain is clear)
- Restate their top 2 pains in their words.
- One sentence on how you solve them: "We close monthly, with job-level reporting, so you know margin before the next bid."
- Name one proof point: [insert client result or case].
- Ask: "Does that match what you need?"

## Objections
| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We already have a bookkeeper." | "Great. Is that person giving you job-level margin each month, or just the books?" |
| "We do it ourselves." | "What does your close look like on a busy month?" |
| "Send me info." | "Happy to. What's the one thing you'd want to see in it?" |
| "Too expensive." | "Compared to what? What did the last bad job cost?" |
| "Not right now." | "What would have to change for it to be the right time?" |

## Close (last 3 min)
- **Recap:** "So: [pain 1], [pain 2]. Next step is [X]."
- **Ask:** "Can we book 45 minutes with [person] next [day]?"
- **Confirm:** Who attends, what you'll send, and when.

## Don't Lose the Thread
- If lost: "Let me make sure I have this right..." then restate.
- Glance at the clock every 5 minutes.
- Use silence. Ask "Anything else on that?" before moving on.
- Jot 3 notes max: **pain**, **number** (jobs/crew/close days), **next step**.
