# Discovery Call Cheat Sheet (30 min)

**Goal:** Learn whether month-end close is costing you real money or time, and book a named next call. Don't pitch yet.

**End on:** "[Name] joins a 20-min call [day, time]. I'll send the invite and a short recap today."

---

**Open (2 min)**
- "I know you run [#] crews in [trade]. Is that still right?"
- "I'd like to understand how month-end works so I don't waste your time."

**Core questions (20 min). Each one gets a number.**
1. How long does close take now? *(days)*
2. Who does it, and how many hours a week? *(people, hours)*
3. Where does it get stuck? *(job costing, WIP, billing, payroll, sub paperwork)*
4. Last time it went wrong, what did it cost? *(money, weeks, a missed bid or bank issue)*
5. What's the target, and why that one? *(motive)*
6. What have you tried? What happens if nothing changes? *(urgency)*

**Vague answer?** "How many, compared to what you need?"
**No figure?** Say "[to learn]" and make pulling it the next step.

**Who decides (4 min)**
- "Who else looks at these numbers? Who would push back?"
- "How did you bring in outside help last time?"
- "What date is driving this?"

**Gate check. Before any proposal or demo, all three must be true:**
- [ ] They feel the problem and can size it
- [ ] A named person signs, or I know the route to a signature
- [ ] I know what they'd put in (time, data, a second person)

Missing one? The next step is to get it. Don't show.

**Close (4 min)**
- "What should I have asked that I didn't?"
- Repeat their outcome in their words.
- Lock the next step. Say it out loud, write it down.

---

**If they stall**
- Don't re-argue the cost of waiting.
- Give one recommendation and why: "I'd start with [small step] because [reason]. I'd change that if [condition]."
- "Send more info?" → "Which piece would help most? I'll send that one."

**Price** (only after value): one number, what it covers, then stop talking. No discount on a first reaction.

**Never**
- Pitch before the gate is met
- End with "let me know"
- State a savings figure you don't have
- Push after a no: confirm it, ask the reason, stop

**Kind decline:** "From what you've described, [volume] is below where we'd pay back. I'd rather say that now. If [threshold] changes, I'd be glad to talk again."

---

*Before you dial, fill in what you already know about the prospect (size, trade, source), so you can confirm it instead of asking for it.*
