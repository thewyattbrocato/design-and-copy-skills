**GOAL:** Learn if the month-end close problem is real and sized. End with a named next meeting, or a clean no.

**0:00-2:00 Open**
- "Thanks for the time. I'd like to learn how you close the books now, and see if we're a fit. Is 30 minutes still good?"
- Confirm the agenda and that they can decide or bring the decider.

**2:00-5:00 Confirm what you know**
- "I understand you run [trade] with about [X] employees. Still right?"

**5:00-22:00 Ask, and write the number each answer gives you**

| # | Ask | Number it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Walk me through your last month-end close. Who does it, and how many days did it take?" | Days to close, hours, people |
| 2 | "How many jobs are open at once, and do you track cost by job?" | Active jobs, job-cost visibility |
| 3 | "When were you last surprised by a job's numbers? What did it cost?" | $ or weeks lost [to learn] |
| 4 | "What do you need from the books by a set date: bank, bonding, lender, payroll?" | Deadline and who needs it |
| 5 | "Are retainage, lien waivers, or certified payroll adding work?" | Extra hours per month |
| 6 | "What have you tried already, and what happened?" | Prior spend, past failure |
| 7 | "Who else has to agree before anything changes?" | Signer, route to yes |

If an answer is vague, say: "How many, compared with what you need?"

**22:00-26:00 Gate check (before any demo, proposal or trial)**
- [ ] A problem they feel, with a size
- [ ] A signer, or a named route to signature
- [ ] What they'll put in: time, data, a second person

Any box empty = the next step is to fill it, not to show anything.

**26:00-28:00 Price (only if asked, or after value is clear)**
- State value in their number first, then: "Our monthly close is [your price], and it covers [scope]."
- Stop talking. Don't discount on a first reaction.

**If they go quiet or say "I need to think"**
- Give one recommendation and why: "Based on [their number], I'd start with [small step]."
- Name what would change your view.
- Offer a small, reversible first step. No new deadline, no new fear.

**28:00-30:00 Next step (non-negotiable)**
- "Here's what happens next: [who] will [what] by [date/time]. Does that work?"
- Put it on both calendars. Send a written recap the same day.

**Never**
- Invent a savings figure, customer, or reference.
- Demo before the gate is met.
- End on "let me know" or "I'll follow up."
