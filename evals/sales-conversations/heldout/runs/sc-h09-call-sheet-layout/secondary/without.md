# First-Call Cheat Sheet (30 min)

**Goal:** Learn 3 things: what hurts, what it costs them, and whether they'll decide soon. Book a next step before hanging up.

## Clock
- **0–3 min** Open: purpose + agenda
- **3–20 min** Discover (talk ratio ~70% them)
- **20–25 min** Map pain to your offer
- **25–30 min** Next step, date, time

## Open (say this)
> "Thanks for making time. Today I'd like to learn about your books and close process, see if we're a fit, and if so, sketch next steps. Sound good?"

## Discover: ask, then stop talking
1. Walk me through how a month-end close works for you today.
2. How many days does it take? What's the slowest part?
3. Who touches the books? Are you involved in them yourself?
4. How do you track job profitability? Do you know margins before a job ends?
5. How are you handling WIP schedules, pay apps, and retainage?
6. How do sub invoices, lien waivers, and 1099s get handled?
7. What happened the last time the numbers surprised you?
8. What have you tried already? What did you like or hate about it?
9. What would change for you if this were fixed?
10. Who else is involved in a decision like this?

## Pain signals (note them)
- Close takes more than ~10 business days
- Owner pulls numbers at night or on weekends
- Bids made without knowing true job costs
- Overbillings/underbillings surprise them
- Missed or late lien waivers, certified payroll, or 1099s
- Bank reconciliations pile up
- Current bookkeeper or tool is leaving them

## Fit check (tick mentally)
- [ ] Construction subcontractor
- [ ] 5–30 employees
- [ ] Has a monthly close pain
- [ ] Decision-maker present or reachable
- [ ] Can start within [timeline]

**Not a fit?** Say so kindly and refer if you can. Don't force it.

## Map pain to offer (20–25 min)
> "So what I'm hearing is [pain in their words], which costs you [time/money/sleep]. We [specific thing you do] so that [outcome]. Is that the kind of fix you're looking for?"

Then price only if asked, or use: "Pricing depends on [volume/jobs/complexity]. Can I ask a couple more questions so I quote accurately?"

## Lost? Lifelines
- "Let me make sure I've got that right..." (summarize, then re-ask)
- "Can you say more about that?"
- "What's the most important part of that for you?"
- "Let me pause and check: what's the one thing you want fixed first?"

## Close (25–30 min)
- "Based on this, here's what I'd suggest: [next step]. Does [day/time] work for [follow-up / proposal review / a walkthrough of your books]?"
- "Who else should be on that call?"
- "What would need to be true for you to say yes?"

**Never end without a dated next step.**

## Vocabulary (for credibility)
WIP schedule · job costing · cost codes · over/underbillings · retainage · pay apps (G702/G703) · lien waivers · certified payroll · sub 1099s · accrual vs. cash · month-end close

## After the call (5 min)
- Write pain points in their words
- Note the decision-maker, timeline, and next step
- Send a recap email within 24 hours
