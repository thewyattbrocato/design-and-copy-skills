# Mid-Call Cheat Sheet: 30-Min Discovery, Subcontractor Bookkeeping and Monthly Close

**Goal:** Learn whether a late or messy month-end is costing them enough to fix, then book a named next call with whoever signs.

**End on:** "[Name], can we book 20 minutes with [decision-maker] on [day, time]? I'll send the invite and a summary today." Get a yes out loud.

---

**0:00–3:00 Open**
- "Thanks for the time. I have 30 minutes. My goal is to understand how month-end works for you and whether it's worth fixing. If not, I'll say so."
- Confirm what you already know: "I saw [fact]; is that still right?"

**3:00–12:00 Situation (numbers)**
- "Walk me through how your month closes, from the last invoice to books you trust." → *days to close [to learn]*
- "How many people touch that, and how many hours a week?" → *people, hours*
- "How do you know a job is making money mid-month?" → *lag in job cost visibility*

**12:00–20:00 Consequence and cause**
- "When the books are late or wrong, what gets hit? Draws, bonding, payroll, a lender?" → *money, deadline*
- "What have you tried? What happened?"
- "Why that target? Who asked for it?" → *motive, who cares*

**20:00–25:00 People and fit**
- "Who else sees these numbers? Who would object to a change?"
- "What date is driving this?"
- If the problem is small or we don't touch its cause: **say so**, shrink the offer, or point elsewhere. Don't inflate it.

**25:00–30:00 Close**
- "What should I have asked that I didn't?"
- Next step (above). Don't leave it at "let me know."

---

**Before any proposal or trial, check all three:**
- [ ] A felt problem with a size (*[to learn]*)
- [ ] The signer, or a named route to the signature
- [ ] What they'll commit (time, system access, a second person)

Missing one? Next step is to get it, not to show or price.

**If they go quiet or say "I need to think":**
"Based on what you've told me, [one recommendation] makes sense, because [reason]. It would change if [condition]. Want to start with [small, reversible step] by [date]?"
No new fear, no deadline, no discount.

**If they ask price:** Value first. "[Price] covers [scope]. What's your reaction?" Then stop talking.

**If it's not a fit:** "From what you've described, [reason]. I'd rather say that now. If [condition] changes, I'd be glad to talk again."

---

*Notes: "Days to close" and "hours a week" are blanks, not facts. Fill them from the buyer, never from a guess. Replace [price] and [scope] with your actual offer before the call.*
