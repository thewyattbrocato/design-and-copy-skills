# First-Call Cheat Sheet

**Goal:** Leave with (1) their real pain in their words, (2) the numbers behind it, (3) a agreed next step.

## Clock
- **0–3 min** Open: agenda + permission
- **3–13 min** Their business
- **13–23 min** Pain, numbers, cost of the problem
- **23–27 min** Your recommendation (only what fits)
- **27–30 min** Next step + owner + date

## Open (say this)
> "Thanks for making time. Here's my plan for the next 30 min: learn how you run the books today, dig into what's not working, and then I'll tell you if and how we could help. Sound good?"

## Discovery Questions
**Business**
- How many active jobs right now? Typical job size?
- Who does the books today, in-house, a bookkeeper, or a CPA?
- What software? (QuickBooks, Sage, Viewpoint, Procore?)

**Month-end close**
- When do you get your books closed each month? (Day 5? Day 20?)
- When do you see your job-level P&L?
- How often are you surprised by a job being over budget?

**Job costing & billing**
- How do you track WIP? Over/under billings?
- How do you handle retainage? Who's chasing it?
- Lien waivers and draws: who tracks them, and what slips?

**Payroll & compliance**
- Certified payroll or prevailing wage jobs?
- Who handles 1099s for subs? Workers' comp audits?
- Sales tax on materials: how is that handled?

**Cost of the problem**
- What does a bad close cost you (decisions, bonding, bank lines)?
- What have you tried before? What happened?
- If this were fixed in 90 days, what would be different?

## Listen For (Signals)
- Bonding company or bank asking for financials they can't produce fast
- Owner guessing at job margins
- Books "always behind," tax surprises at year-end
- Frustration with a bookkeeper who doesn't understand construction
- Growth coming, but the back office can't keep up

## Mirror & Confirm
> "So what I'm hearing is [pain] costs you [impact], and you'd like [outcome] by [timeframe]. Is that right?"

Don't pitch until they say yes.

## Recommend (Only If Pain Is Confirmed)
1. **Restate their top 2 pains** in their words
2. **One solution** mapped to each pain
3. **One proof point** from a similar sub *(insert yours)*: _______________
4. **Price anchor:** _______________ (or "I'll put numbers together after I see your books")

## Handle Objections
- **"We're fine with our bookkeeper."** → "What would it take for you to *know* your job margins without asking?"
- **"Too expensive."** → "What does one job going 10% over cost you?"
- **"Let me think about it."** → "What specifically would you need to think through?"
- **"Send me something."** → "Happy to. What three things should it answer?"

## Close the Call
- [ ] Pain confirmed in their words
- [ ] Numbers captured (jobs, revenue, close timing)
- [ ] Decision-maker identified: _______________
- [ ] Next step booked with date/time
- [ ] Recap email sent within 24 hrs

**Next step ask:** "Can we get your last two months of financials and a job list on [date] so I can show you what I'd change?"
