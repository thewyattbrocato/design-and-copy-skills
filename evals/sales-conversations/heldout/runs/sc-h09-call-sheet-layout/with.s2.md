# First Call Cheat Sheet (30 min)

**GOAL: Find out if the books/close problem is real and worth fixing.**
**END ON: a dated meeting with the signer, on both calendars, e.g. "[day/time], you + [partner/bookkeeper/CPA if any]."**

---

### 0–3 min | OPEN
- "Thanks for the time. I'll ask a few questions about how your books work now, then tell you honestly whether I can help. If not, I'll say so. Okay?"
- Confirm what you know: "I saw [trade, size, source]. Still right?"

### 3–18 min | ASK (what number does it give me?)
| Ask | Gets you |
|---|---|
| "Who does the books today, and how many hours a week?" | **Hours and who** |
| "When do you close a month? Day 10? Day 30? Never?" | **Days to close** |
| "What did you last want to know and couldn't? Job profit? Cash?" | **A felt problem** |
| "Do you know margin per job *while* it's running, or after?" | **Job costing gap** |
| "How far behind are AR, retainage, and progress billing?" | **$ stuck / days late** |
| "Last time the books were wrong, what happened? (bank, bonding, tax, a bad bid)" | **Cost of a miss, in their words** |
| "What does your CPA/bank/surety need from you, and when?" | **Deadline and outside pressure** |
| "What have you tried? Why didn't it stick?" | **Past fixes, objections** |
| "What would 'fixed' look like in 90 days?" | **Their target** |

**Vague answer?** "How many, against what you need?"
**Dollar figure?** Only theirs. Write `[to learn]` if you don't have it.

### 18–24 min | GATE CHECK (all 3 before you pitch anything)
- [ ] **Felt, sized problem** (they said it, with a number)
- [ ] **Signer** (who decides? Is that person on this call?)
- [ ] **What they'll put in** (time, access to bank/QuickBooks, 1 more person)

Missing one? Your next step is to get it. Don't propose yet.

### 24–28 min | IF GATE IS MET
- Reflect back: "So: [their problem] costing [their number], and you want [their target]. Right?"
- Fit check: if it's small or not what you fix, say so and point elsewhere.
- Price (only after value): **[your price] covers [scope]**. Then **stop talking.**
- Pushback? Ask: "Where does that sit against [their target]?" **No discount.**

### 28–30 min | CLOSE
Say it aloud: **"Who, what, when?"**
- "Let's meet [day/time] with [signer]. I'll send [what] by [day]. You'll send [access/doc] by [day]."
- Same day: send a short written recap.
- If they say no: "Thanks, what's the main reason?" Write it down. Stop.

---

**IF YOU LOSE THE THREAD:** "Let me make sure I have this right…" and repeat their last number. Then ask the next question.

**DON'T:** pitch before the gate · say "let me know" or "I'll follow up" · invent savings or customer names · push after a no.

---

Two things to fill in: your price and scope line, and the exact words for your opener. The questions assume QuickBooks-style books and job costing. If you work in other tools, swap the wording.
