# First-Call Cheat Sheet (30 min)

**Goal:** Learn their pain, see if there's a fit, and book a next step. You're not trying to close today.

## Timeline
| Min | Phase | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | **Open** | Set the agenda and get permission |
| 3–18 | **Discover** | Ask and listen (they talk 70%) |
| 18–23 | **Position** | Connect 1–2 of their pains to what you do |
| 23–27 | **Qualify** | Budget, decision-maker, timing |
| 27–30 | **Close** | Book a specific next step |

## Open (say this)
> "Thanks for making time. I'd like to spend about 15 minutes learning how your books work today, then tell you if and how I can help. If it's not a fit, I'll say so. Sound okay?"

## Discover: ask in this order
1. **Now:** "Walk me through how the books get done today. Who does it?"
2. **Timing:** "How many days after month-end before you see real numbers?"
3. **Pain:** "What's the most annoying part?"
4. **Job costing:** "Can you tell which jobs made money? How do you know?"
5. **Cash:** "Ever surprised by cash, taxes, or a bank/bonding request?"
6. **Cost:** "What happens when the numbers are late or wrong?"
7. **Magic wand:** "If this were fixed, what would be different?"

**Follow-ups when stuck:** "Tell me more." • "Can you give me an example?" • "What did that cost you?"

## Listen for (pain signals)
- Owner or spouse does the books at night
- Numbers 30+ days late
- WIP, retainage, or progress billing is a mess
- No job-level profit visibility
- Bonding, bank, or tax-time scrambles
- Cleanup or catch-up backlog
- Payroll, 1099, or sales tax headaches

## Position (one sentence)
> "I take bookkeeping and monthly close off your plate, so you get accurate, job-level numbers by day ___ every month and stop doing it yourself."

Tie it to **their words**: "You said ___, that's exactly what we fix."

## Qualify
- **Size:** headcount, number of active jobs
- **Software:** QuickBooks, Sage, or something else?
- **Decision:** "Who else weighs in on this?"
- **Budget:** "What are you spending now, in money or time?"
- **Timing:** "What would make this a priority now?"

## Objections (short replies)
| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive" | "Compared to what? What does a late or wrong close cost you now?" |
| "My cousin/spouse does it" | "Makes sense. What happens when they're busy?" |
| "Need to think about it" | "Of course. What would you need to know to decide?" |
| "Send me info" | "Happy to. What would you look for in it? Can we talk Thursday after you've read it?" |
| "Not right now" | "Fair. What would need to change? Can I check back on ___?" |

## Close (pick one and name a date)
> "Next step is ___. Does **[day/time]** work?"
- Fit: book a proposal or deeper-dive call, and ask them to bring a recent P&L and the bank/software login details
- Unsure: book a short follow-up
- No fit: say so, and ask for a referral

## If you lose the thread
- Pause and breathe. Silence feels longer to you than to them.
- "Let me make sure I've got this right…" then summarize
- "Can I ask a quick question about ___?" then go back to the Discover list
- "What else should I be asking?"

## Before you hang up
☐ Next step is booked with a date
☐ You know their pain, software, and decision-maker
☐ You sent a recap email within 24 hours
