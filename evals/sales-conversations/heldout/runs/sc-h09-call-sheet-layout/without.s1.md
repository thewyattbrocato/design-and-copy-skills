# First Call Cheat Sheet: 30 min

**Goal of the call:** find out if there's real pain, and book a next step. You don't have to close anything.

---

## 0:00–3:00 | OPEN
- "Thanks for making time. I'll ask a few questions about how your books work today, tell you briefly how I help, and we'll decide together if a next step makes sense. Sound good?"
- Ask: "Anything specific you wanted to get out of this call?"

## 3:00–18:00 | DISCOVERY (they talk ~70%)
**Start here:**
1. "Walk me through how the books get done today. Who does it?"
2. "When do you usually see last month's numbers?"

**Dig into the pain:**
- **Timing:** Close takes how many days? Ever months behind?
- **Job costing:** Do you know profit per job *while* it's running, or only after?
- **Billing:** Progress billing / pay apps / retainage. Who tracks what's owed?
- **Cash:** Ever surprised by cash being tight? Waiting on receivables?
- **Compliance:** Payroll taxes, workers' comp audits, 1099s, sales tax. Any scrambles or notices?
- **Outside needs:** Bank, bonding/surety, or tax preparer asking for financials?
- **Tools:** QuickBooks? Other software? Who has access?

**Follow-ups (use often):**
- "Tell me more about that."
- "What does that cost you: time, money, stress?"
- "How long has that been an issue?"

## 18:00–23:00 | CONFIRM + PITCH (short)
- Recap: "So what I'm hearing is **[1]**, **[2]**, **[3]**. Did I get that right?"
- Tie it to what I do: "Monthly close by day ___, clean job-level numbers, retainage and receivables tracked, so you can ___."
- Stop after 2–3 sentences. Go back to them.

## 23:00–27:00 | QUALIFY + OBJECTIONS
- "Who else weighs in on a decision like this?"
- "If this worked well, what would that change for you?"
- "Have you looked at other options?"

| They say | I say |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive" | "Compared to what? Let's look at what the current mess costs you." |
| "My cousin/wife does it" | "Makes sense. What happens if they get busy or leave?" |
| "Need to think about it" | "Of course. What specifically would you want to think through?" |
| "Send me info" | "Happy to. Can we also set 15 min Thursday to go over it?" |

## 27:00–30:00 | CLOSE THE NEXT STEP
- "Here's what I suggest: **[review their books / send proposal / short follow-up]**."
- **Get a date and time on the calendar before hanging up.**
- Confirm: "I'll send a recap by end of day. Anything I missed?"

---

## IF YOU LOSE THE THREAD
- Pause. Take a breath. Silence feels longer to you than to them.
- Say: "Let me make sure I understood that. You said ___?"
- Or: "Tell me more about that." Then take notes while they answer.
- Fallback question: **"What's the most frustrating part of the books right now?"**

## DON'T
- Pitch before you've heard the pain.
- Talk price before they've said what the problem costs.
- Hang up without a dated next step.
