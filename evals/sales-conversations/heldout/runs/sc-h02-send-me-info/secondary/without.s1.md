1. **He's not the decision-maker.** Facilities usually owns the physical security side, but badge and door systems at a hospital often need sign-off from IT or security, sometimes clinical operations, and a budget owner. Your deck may be sitting in someone's inbox waiting for a meeting that hasn't been scheduled.
2. **It's not a priority yet.** "Sounds interesting" often means "I'm open to hearing more when I have bandwidth," not "I'm evaluating now." Hospitals also run on capital planning cycles, so a project may not be funded until next budget year.
3. **Your email got buried or filtered.** Decks and one-pagers sometimes trip spam filters or get stuck behind hospital email security. Hospital IT is strict about attachments and external links.
