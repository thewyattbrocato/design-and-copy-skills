**I wouldn't lead with 40% off.** I'd first find out whether she's actually a fit.

**Why the discount probably won't work**
- 40% off is still $288/month, or about $3,450 a year. For a one-person studio with 2 wholesale accounts, that's still a big expense, and the product is built for 10+ accounts. "The price is a lot for me right now" may really mean "I don't need this much yet."
- If she signs because of a discount on a tool that's too big for her, she'll likely churn within 3-6 months. That hurts your retention numbers, and she'll leave annoyed. A deal that churns early is worse for you than a lost deal.
- A big discount also sets the price anchor. At renewal she'll see $480 and balk again.
- She says she loves it, but she hasn't said what problem it solves for her. After 3 calls, that's the real gap.

**What I'd do before discounting**
1. Ask a direct question: "What's the cost of not having this? Where are you losing time or money today with 2 wholesale accounts and about 40 orders a month?" If you can't find a concrete answer, she isn't ready to buy.
2. Check for a smaller plan, a startup or small-business tier, or a deferred start. Any of these is better than a deep discount on the full plan.
3. If she does have a real need, such as growing wholesale or inventory chaos, a smaller discount tied to something works better. For example, 15-20% off for an annual commitment, or a lower price for 3-6 months until she reaches 5+ accounts.

**What I'd say to her**
> "I want to be straight with you. You've told me you love it, and I believe you. But our platform is built for brands with 10 or more wholesale accounts, and I don't want you paying for more than you need. With two accounts, I'm not sure it's the right fit today. Here's what I can do: [smaller plan / start date when you're ready / discount for annual]. And if it's better to revisit when you've added a few more wholesale accounts, I'm happy to do that, and I'll check back in [timeframe]."

This builds trust. If she is a fit, she'll often step up. If she isn't, you keep a warm lead who may come back at full price.

**With your manager**
Don't refuse. Show the math: "A 40% discount on a bad-fit account likely churns in a few months. I'm offering a right-sized option, or I'm parking her and moving to better-fit deals." If your quota is behind, your time is probably better spent on prospects with 10+ accounts, where $480 is easy to justify.

If you tell me what plans or discount limits you actually have, I can help tailor the offer.
