# 20-Minute Check-In Agenda

**1. Thank them and catch up (3 min)**
- Thank them for three years. Name something specific, like how long they've been with you or a milestone you know about.
- Ask how the business is going: season, hiring, new service lines, crew growth.

**2. How they use the product (5 min)**
- "What's working best for you right now?"
- "Is there anything you've worked around or wish took fewer clicks?" This works better than "any problems?" because happy customers rarely volunteer small annoyances.
- Ask how their dispatching has changed since they started. Their answer may show whether they use features they haven't discovered.

**3. What's new and what's coming (5 min)**
- Show or mention 1-2 recent features that fit a landscaping crew, such as route planning, recurring jobs, customer notifications, or mobile updates.
- Ask for their reaction to one upcoming idea. Customers like being asked.

**4. Renewal heads-up (3 min)**
- Keep it low-key: "Your renewal is coming up next month at the same price. Anything you need from us before then?"
- Confirm the right billing contact and whether they want to renew as-is.

**5. Small asks, only if the mood fits (3 min)**
- A referral to another local business owner.
- A short testimonial or case study.
- A review on G2 or Capterra.
- Pick one at most. Three asks would make it feel transactional.

**6. Wrap up (1 min)**
- Recap any follow-ups and give them a direct contact for questions.

**Prep tips**
- Pull their usage data beforehand. Look for features they don't use, or recent drops or spikes in activity. A drop could be a quiet warning sign, despite the positive feedback.
- Check their support ticket history so you can mention anything you resolved recently.
- Decide your single most important takeaway. I'd pick one real insight into how they use the product or what they'd want next.

If they'd benefit from a plan upgrade or add-on, don't pitch it in this call. It would undercut the "just checking in" tone. Note it and follow up separately if it's relevant.
