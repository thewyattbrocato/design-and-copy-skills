# 20-minute check-in agenda

**1. Thank them and catch up (2–3 min)**
- Thank them for three years. Be specific, e.g. "You've been with us since [year], and your feedback has shaped X."
- Ask how the season is going.

**2. Their business (5–7 min)**
- Ask what's changing: hiring, new crews, new service lines, bigger commercial contracts, seasonal swings.
- Ask what's taking up most of their time right now.
- This is the most useful part of the call. A 14-person company that grows will outgrow how it uses your app, and you want to hear that early.

**3. How the app fits (5 min)**
- Ask what they use most and what they've stopped using.
- Ask if anything is clunky or if there's something they work around.
- Show or mention one new feature that fits what they just told you. Pick one, not a tour.

**4. Renewal (2 min)**
- Mention it's coming up next month at the same price. Ask if anything about their setup or needs should change, and who on their side handles it.
- Keep it to a quick confirmation. Don't make it a pitch.

**5. Small ask and wrap (2 min)**
- Optional: ask for a referral to another local service business, a short review, or a quote for a case study. They've always said they love it, so this is the natural time.
- Recap any follow-ups and say who will do each.

**Tips**
- Let them talk for most of the call. If the conversation is going well, cut your own agenda items.
- Don't upsell on this call. If step 2 shows a real need, such as more users or a new feature, offer a separate follow-up.
- Send a short thank-you email afterward with any promised follow-ups.
