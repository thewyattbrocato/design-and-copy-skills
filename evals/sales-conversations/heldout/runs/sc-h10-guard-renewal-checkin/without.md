# 20-minute check-in agenda

**1. Warm open (3 min)**
- Ask how the season has gone. For a landscaping company that's a natural opener, and it shows you care about their business, not just the account.
- Ask about anything new: crew growth, new service lines, big jobs.

**2. What's working (5 min)**
- Ask what they use the app for most and what they'd hate to lose.
- Ask if there's anything they do outside the app, like spreadsheets, texts, or a whiteboard. That's the easiest place to spot a gap you could help with.
- Capture any quotable praise. Ask if they'd be open to a testimonial or a case study, or to referring other local companies.

**3. Quick wins and updates (5 min)**
- Show one or two features released in the past year that fit their workflow, such as route optimization, crew notifications, or invoicing integrations. Pick things they likely haven't tried.
- Offer a short training session for the newer or seasonal crew if that would help.

**4. Their wishlist (4 min)**
- Ask what one thing would make scheduling easier next season. Even happy customers have a pet peeve.
- Tell them you'll pass it to the product team, and follow up afterward.

**5. Renewal and wrap-up (3 min)**
- Mention the renewal in one line: "Your renewal is coming up next month at the same price. Anything you need from us before then?"
- Thank them for three years. Confirm the next steps and when you'll follow up.

**Tips**
- **Listen for signals.** Growth means they may need more seats or tiers. Mentions of a competitor or a rising cost mean you should dig in.
- **Don't turn it into an upsell.** If they raise a need, note it and follow up later.
- **Send a short recap email within a day.** Include the wishlist items and any resources you promised.
