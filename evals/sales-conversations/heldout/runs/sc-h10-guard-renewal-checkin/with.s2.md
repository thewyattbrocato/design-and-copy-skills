# 20-minute check-in agenda

**1. Thank them and catch up (3 min)**
- Thank them for three years. Be specific if you can, like a season they got through or a milestone like the crew growing.
- Ask an open question: "How's the season been? What's changed since last year?"

**2. How they use the app today (5 min)**
- Ask what's working well and which features the crew relies on most.
- Ask what's still clunky or manual, even if it's small. Happy customers often don't mention workarounds unless you ask.
- Ask whether their needs have changed. For example, more crews, new service lines, or different seasonal patterns.

**3. What's new and relevant (4 min)**
- Show or mention only 1-2 recent features that fit a landscaping business. Skip the full release list.
- Ask whether each would help them, rather than pitching it.

**4. Their feedback and wish list (4 min)**
- Ask, "If you could change one thing about the app, what would it be?"
- Ask, "Is there anything you've wished it did that we haven't talked about?"
- Take notes and promise a follow-up on anything you can't answer on the spot.

**5. Renewal and wrap-up (4 min)**
- Mention the renewal briefly and without pressure: "Your renewal is coming up next month at the same price. Anything you need from us before then?"
- Confirm who handles the paperwork or invoice on their end.
- Close with a clear way to reach you and a thank-you.

## Tips
- **Do more listening than talking.** The aim is for them to feel heard, not for you to present.
- **Don't add an upsell.** If something does come up naturally, offer to follow up separately.
- **Send a short recap email afterward** with any action items and a thank-you. It takes five minutes and reinforces the relationship.
- **Pass the feedback on** to your product team, and tell the customer you did.
