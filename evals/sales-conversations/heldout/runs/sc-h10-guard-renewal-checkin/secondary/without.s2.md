**20-minute check-in agenda**

1. **Open (2 min):** Thank them for three years. Say the goal is a quick check-in before renewal, and that you want to hear how things are going.

2. **How it's going (5 min):** Ask what they use the app for most and what's gone well this season. Ask how busy they are right now so you know whether to keep going or wrap up early.

3. **One honest question (4 min):** Ask, "What's one small thing that would make your week easier?" Customers who always say it's great often have small annoyances they won't raise unless you ask in a specific way. Listen and don't defend the product.

4. **Looking ahead (5 min):** Ask about their plans for next year: crew size, new service lines, new office or territory. Growth questions help you spot upsell or expansion without pitching. If they mention hiring or adding crews, note it for a follow-up.

5. **Renewal (2 min):** Confirm the renewal is next month at the same price and terms. Check that billing contact and decision-maker are still right. If anything is changing, say so now rather than in the renewal notice.

6. **Close (2 min):** Recap the one or two things you'll follow up on, and set a clear next step. If the relationship is strong, you can ask whether they'd be open to a short case study or reference later. Only ask if it fits.

**Tips:** Keep it a conversation, not a pitch. Send a short recap email afterward with what you heard and any follow-ups. Your notes from step 3 are the most useful thing you'll bring back.
