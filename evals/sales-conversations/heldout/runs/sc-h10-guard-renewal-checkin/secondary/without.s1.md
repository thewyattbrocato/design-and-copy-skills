Here's a 20-minute agenda that keeps it warm and light:

**1. Open and reconnect (3 min)**
- Thank them for three years and ask how the season is going.
- Ask what's keeping them busy right now (crew size, jobs booked, any new service lines).

**2. Quick value recap (4 min)**
- Share one or two concrete wins from their account, like jobs scheduled, hours saved, or on-time rates, if you have the numbers.
- Ask: "What does the app save you most time on?" Let them talk.

**3. Listen for what's changing (6 min)**
- "Any changes coming up for the business: hiring, new equipment, new territory, a bigger crew?"
- "Is there anything you do manually or in a spreadsheet that you wish the app handled?"
- Don't pitch. Note anything that sounds like a future need.

**4. Renewal, handled gently (3 min)**
- Mention it plainly: "Your renewal is coming up next month. Nothing changes on pricing, and I wanted to make sure you're set for the next year."
- Ask if there's anything they want added or adjusted before then.

**5. Close and next step (4 min)**
- Ask how they'd like to hear from you (phone, text, email) and when the next check-in makes sense.
- Ask for a referral or a reference only if it feels natural. Skip it if it doesn't.
- Thank them again.

**Notes to keep for yourself**
- Their answers to the "changing business" question are your best signal for upsell or churn risk, even when everything looks fine.
- If they mention a feature they'd like, log it and follow up after the call.
