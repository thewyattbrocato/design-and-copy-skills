Here's a 20-minute agenda that keeps it warm and still gets you useful information before the renewal.

**1. Open and reconnect (3 min)**
- Thank them for three years and ask how the season is going.
- Ask what's changed in their business since you last talked: new crews, new services, new equipment.

**2. Walk through how they use the app (5 min)**
- Ask what they rely on most day to day, and what a typical scheduling week looks like.
- Ask whether anyone on the team has become a power user, or whether someone is still doing things the long way.

**3. Look ahead (5 min)**
- Ask about growth plans: more crews, more clients, new service lines, or hiring. Those may change what they need from the app.
- Ask what they expect to be busy with over the next year.

**4. One improvement question (3 min)**
- Ask: "If you could change one thing about the app, what would it be?" Even happy customers usually have one friction point, and hearing it now is much better than hearing about it at renewal or after they've left.
- Thank them for the answer and say you'll look into it, then follow up with a clear answer.

**5. Renewal and next steps (3 min)**
- Confirm the renewal date and that the terms are unchanged, so there are no surprises.
- Offer one useful thing: a feature they haven't tried, a training session for new hires, or an account review before renewal.
- Ask whether they'd be open to a short case study or referral. Only ask if the relationship feels strong, and make it easy to say no.

**Close (1 min)**
- Recap any follow-ups and confirm the next touchpoint.

Two things to check before the call:
- **Price:** If there is any chance of a price change at renewal, decide now and tell them in this call or in writing before the renewal date. Long-term customers feel blindsided by increases that show up on an invoice.
- **Account context:** Look at their recent support tickets and usage data beforehand. If something is quietly declining, you'll know what to ask about.
