# 20-minute check-in agenda

**1. Thank them and set the tone (2 min)**
- Thank them for three years. Be specific, like "you've been with us since [year], and you were one of our early customers."
- Say the goal up front: "No agenda from our side. I wanted to hear how things are going."

**2. How they use it today (5 min)**
- "What does a typical week look like in the app?"
- "What's the one thing you'd miss most if it disappeared?"
- Their answers show what they value and which features they rely on, so you can describe it in their words later.

**3. What's changed in their business (5 min)**
- Ask about crew size, new services, seasonal swings, and whether they're growing or planning to.
- Ask: "Is there anything you're doing outside the app, like spreadsheets, texts, or a whiteboard, that you wish it handled?"
- Don't skip this one. Happy customers rarely volunteer small workarounds, and it's where you'll find expansion ideas or quiet churn risk.

**4. Quick product share (4 min)**
- Mention one or two recent or upcoming features that fit what they just told you. Skip the rest.
- Ask for their reaction instead of pitching.

**5. Close (3 min)**
- Ask: "Anything we could do better?" and "Anything you'd want on the roadmap?"
- Mention the renewal lightly: "Your renewal's coming up next month at the same price. Anything you need from us on that?" Don't make it a negotiation or a sales moment.
- Confirm who handles the renewal on their side.
- Offer a direct contact for questions, then follow up in writing with anything you promised.

**Tips**
- Aim to listen about 70% of the time.
- Ask who else on their team uses the app daily, since you may be hearing only from the owner.
- If they offer a testimonial, case study, or referral, say yes. Don't ask for one cold in this call.
