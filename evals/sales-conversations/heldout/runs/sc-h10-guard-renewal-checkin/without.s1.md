# 20-minute check-in agenda

**1. Thank them and catch up (3 min)**
- Thank them for three years. Be specific, like "you were one of our earliest customers."
- Ask how the business is going: season, crew growth, anything new they're excited about.

**2. How they use the app today (5 min)**
- "What's working best for your crew right now?"
- "Is there anything you do outside the app that you wish you could do inside it?"
- Happy customers often have workarounds they've stopped noticing. These are useful product feedback and can point to expansion later.

**3. What's coming (4 min)**
- Show one or two recent or upcoming features that fit landscaping, such as seasonal scheduling, route planning, or crew notifications.
- Ask which would be useful to them. Don't pitch.

**4. Their input (3 min)**
- "If you could change one thing, what would it be?"
- "Is there anything on your wish list for next year?"

**5. Renewal and wrap-up (3 min)**
- Mention it plainly: "Your renewal is coming up next month at the same price. Anything you need from us before then?"
- Confirm who the right contact is for renewal paperwork.
- Thank them again.

**6. Optional ask (only if the conversation goes well)**
- A short testimonial or case study, or a referral to another local landscaper.
- Keep it low-pressure and separate from the renewal talk. If it doesn't fit, skip it.

**Tips**
- Aim to talk about 30% of the time. The goal is to listen.
- Take notes and send a short thank-you email within a day. Include any follow-ups you promised.
- If they bring up a problem or a feature request, don't commit on the spot. Say you'll follow up by a specific date.
- After three years, a small gesture like a handwritten note or a gift card for the crew can say more than the meeting.
