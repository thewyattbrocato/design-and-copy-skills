## Gate check (before anything else on the call)

Do not demo, quote, or propose unless all three are true:
- A problem she feels and can size (from questions 2 to 4).
- A named person who signs, or a clear route to one (question 7).
- What they would put in: time, invoices, a second person on the next call.

If any is missing, the next step is to get it, not to pitch. If the numbers are small (say, one failure a year with a same-day fix), say so kindly, point to a better fit or a smaller plan, and stop. Do not inflate the problem to justify the call.
