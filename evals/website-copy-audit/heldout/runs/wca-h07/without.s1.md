Top problems, ranked:

1. **The hero is generic and doesn't differentiate.** "A smarter way to manage your team" could describe any scheduling tool, and "all in one place" is filler. It never says what makes Orchard Rota different from competitors or from a spreadsheet, and it doesn't speak to a restaurant manager's real pain, such as last-minute no-shows or juggling shifts around availability.

2. **There's no proof or credibility anywhere.** There are no customer names, quotes, numbers or logos, and no sign of how many restaurants use it. Claims like "in minutes" and "stay on top of labor costs" are unsupported, so a skeptical manager has little reason to believe them.

3. **The call to action is weak and the next step is unclear.** "Request access" suggests a waitlist or gatekeeping, and it doesn't say what happens after clicking (a demo, a trial, a reply within a day). Combined with "Pricing on request" in the footer, it adds friction for a busy manager who just wants to know cost and effort.

4. **The section copy stays at the feature level, with little concrete benefit.** "Know your numbers" and "Swaps without the chaos" don't say what the outcome is, such as hours saved per week or labor cost reduced. The "Know your numbers" section is also thin, with a single sentence and nothing on how cost is calculated or which data it uses.
