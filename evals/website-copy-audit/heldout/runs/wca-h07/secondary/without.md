3. **The benefit claims have no proof.** "Schedules your staff will actually follow" and "in minutes" are promises the page doesn't support with a number, a customer, or a visual. Skeptical managers who've tried rota tools before will discount them.

4. **The strongest differentiator is buried under the weakest heading.** Seeing projected labor cost before publishing is the most concrete value on the page, but "Know your numbers" is vague and the body doesn't say what decision it helps the manager make.
