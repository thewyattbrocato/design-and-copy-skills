Top problems, ranked:

1. **The hero is generic.** "A smarter way to manage your team" could describe any workforce tool, and it never says who this is for or what makes it different. The second sentence (schedules, swaps, labor costs, "all in one place") is a feature list, not a reason to choose Orchard Rota.

2. **The page has no proof.** There are no customer names, quotes, numbers, or screenshots. Claims like "in minutes" and "your staff will actually follow" are unsupported, so a skeptical restaurant manager has nothing to trust.

3. **The call to action is vague and high-friction.** "Request access" doesn't say what happens next, how long it takes, or whether there's a demo or trial. Combined with "Pricing on request," visitors are asked to commit before they know the cost or the experience.

4. **Pricing and availability are buried in the footer.** The UK and Ireland restriction and "Pricing on request" are important qualifiers that appear only at the very end. Visitors elsewhere, or anyone price-sensitive, will find out late and may feel misled.
