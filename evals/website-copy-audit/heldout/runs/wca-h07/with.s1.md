Top 4 problems, ranked. I've skipped the tagline and written no rewrites.

1. **Hero: "A smarter way to manage your team."** This is generic enough that any rival could sign it. "Smarter" says nothing, and "manage your team" doesn't tell a stranger this is shift scheduling. The one specific sentence after it ("build schedules, handle swaps and stay on top of labor costs") does the real work, and it sits below the vague line. With the tagline also not naming the category, a newcomer's first screen never says what the product is.

2. **Sections 1 and 3 make promises with no detail behind them.**
   - "Build next week's rota in minutes" gives no sense of how.
   - "Know your numbers" offers only "projected labor cost" and doesn't say what it's projected from.
   - Neither section says what the manager actually does or sees, so the claims are easy to skim past and hard to believe.
   - Only the swaps section is concrete: "approve it with one tap".

3. **Sections 1 and 3 mix up vocabulary and audience.**
   - The copy uses "rota" (UK), "labor" (US spelling) and "your team's phones" together.
   - The footer says UK and Ireland, so "labor" reads as an American-company slip. "Labour" would be expected by the people you serve.
   - This is small, but it undercuts trust at the exact spot where you're saying "built for you".
   - Section 1's headline is also about staff following the schedule, while the body is about the manager's speed. The headline and body make different points.

4. **The path to the button is weak, and the footer hides the key terms.**
   - "Request access" doesn't say what happens next: a demo, a waitlist, a trial?
   - "Pricing on request" appears only in the footer, after the decision point.
   - Together, a visitor can't tell what they're signing up for or whether it's affordable until they've already committed to the ask.
   - Availability ("UK and Ireland") is also in the footer, so someone outside those regions finds out last.

**Not reviewed:** only this copy, with no layout, other pages, or the sign-up flow.
