1. **Hero headline: "A smarter way to manage your team."** Any scheduling, HR or payroll tool could use this line. A newcomer doesn't learn it's shift scheduling for restaurants until the second sentence, and "smarter" makes no claim they can check.

2. **No proof anywhere.** "Build next week's rota in minutes," "Schedules your staff will actually follow," and "stay on top of labor costs" are all unbacked. There's no customer, restaurant name, number or quote, so a manager has to take your word for it. I'd only fill this gap with real material, such as a customer quote or a measured time-to-build.

3. **The next step and the terms are vague.** "Request access" doesn't say whether it's a demo, a waitlist or a trial. "Pricing on request" and "available in the UK and Ireland" sit in the footer, after the reader has already decided whether to click. A manager outside those countries, or one who needs a price, finds out last.

4. **"The app" is never introduced, and the terms drift.** Section 2 says "Staff request a swap in the app," but nothing earlier says there is an app, only "your team's phones." The page also switches between "rota" and "schedule(s)" for the same thing, which is a minor inconsistency but one to settle.

I only had the text, so I haven't judged layout or any other pages.
