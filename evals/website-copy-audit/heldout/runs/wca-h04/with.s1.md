I'll fix the copy, but I'm leaving out two things. I won't write invented customer quotes or a "Sale ends tonight at midnight" banner for a discount that runs all year. Both are deceptive: the FTC prohibits fake reviews and fake urgency, and either one can get you fined or pulled by payment processors and ad platforms. If a customer notices the "ends tonight" sale is still on tomorrow, it also costs you trust right at launch.

Here's what I'd use instead.

**Revised copy**

**Title:** The Nightmoor Weighted Blanket

**Intro:** Gentle, even pressure to help your body settle and your mind slow down. Nightmoor is built for deeper, calmer nights.

**Details:**
- Weights: 12 lb, 15 lb or 20 lb
- Fill: glass beads, evenly distributed
- Cover: cotton, removable and machine washable
- Size: Queen (more sizes coming)

**Price:** ~~$129~~ **$109.65** with 15% off

**Button:** Add to cart

**Small print:** 30-day returns. Try it at home and send it back if it's not for you.

I cut "ultimate," "premium," and "crafted with care" because they're generic claims. I also avoided promising that it will make you "drift off effortlessly," since that's a sleep claim you'd need to support.

**Banner options that are true and still create urgency**
- "Launch offer: 15% off Nightmoor Weighted Blankets". This is accurate as long as you phrase it as a launch offer and don't give an end date you won't keep.
- "Launch week: 15% off, free returns for 30 days". Only use this if you'll actually end or change the discount after launch week.
- If you want a real deadline, set one and honor it. For example, end the 15% on a date, then raise the price.

**Getting real reviews by Friday**
- Send the blanket to 5–10 friends, family members or beta testers this week and ask for honest feedback. If you give them free product, a short "received a free product" disclosure keeps it compliant.
- Launch with a "Be one of our first reviewers" prompt or an email follow-up 2–3 weeks after delivery.
- Until then, lean on concrete facts: the 30-day return policy, the washable cover, and the weight options. For a new product these build more trust than a thin set of quotes.
