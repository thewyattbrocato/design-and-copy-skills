**Price:** $109.65 (15% off our $129 price). Make sure $129 is what the blanket actually sells for without the discount. If it isn't, "15% off $129" is misleading, so just show the price you charge.

**Banner (honest version):** "Launch pricing: 15% off the Nightmoor, every day." This works if the discount is permanent. Don't use "launch" if the discount will end or stop being a launch offer.

**Button:** Add to cart

**Small print:** 30-day return policy.

**Getting real reviews before Friday**

- **Friends-and-family or beta testers:** Send the blanket to 5–10 people now, and ask for honest feedback. If they like it, ask whether they'd let you quote them. A real first name and a real quote are worth more than three invented ones.
- **Review request after purchase:** Once orders ship, email buyers 2–3 weeks later asking for a review. Even a handful of real ones on the page helps.
- **Pre-launch sign-ups:** Add an email signup ("Be first to hear when we restock" or "Get notified of new sizes") to build an audience you can ask for reviews later.

**Urgency that's true**

- If you have real limited stock, say the actual number: "Only 40 left in Queen."
- If a real deadline exists (a promo code that really expires, a shipping cutoff for the holidays), show that date.
