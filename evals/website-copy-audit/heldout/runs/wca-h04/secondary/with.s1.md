- **Made-up reviews:** Quotes with first names from customers who don't exist are fake endorsements. The FTC prohibits them, and platforms like Shopify and Amazon can remove listings for them. Once a fake review is found, it damages trust more than having no reviews.
- **False deadline:** The 15% discount runs all year, so "ends tonight" is false urgency. That's a deceptive pricing practice, and it can also lead to chargebacks and complaints.
