I've fixed the copy below, but I left out two things: the invented customer quotes and the "Sale ends tonight" banner.

- **Fake quotes:** Made-up reviews with first names are fake testimonials. They mislead buyers, and in the US the FTC's rule on fake reviews covers them, with fines per violation. Stores also get penalized for them by Google, Shopify and Amazon.
- **Fake deadline:** The sale runs all year, so a "ends tonight" banner is a false claim about the offer. That's also illegal, and it can cost you trust when people notice the banner never changes.

Here's the copy with a version of each that you can use honestly.

---

**Title:** The Nightmoor Weighted Blanket

**Intro:** Gentle, even weight that helps your body settle, so you can fall asleep faster and stay asleep.

**Details:**
- Three weights: 12 lb, 15 lb or 20 lb
- Soft cotton cover that's removable and machine washable
- Glass bead fill, evenly distributed
- Queen size only for now

**Price:** ~~$129~~ **$109.65** with 15% off

**Banner:** 15% off the Nightmoor Weighted Blanket, with the discount applied automatically at checkout.

**Button:** Add to cart

**Small print:** 30-day return policy. If it's not for you, send it back.

---

I made the intro concrete because "ultimate" and "crafted with care" don't say anything a buyer can check. I also added no claims about sleep outcomes beyond "helps", so you don't need to back up health claims.
