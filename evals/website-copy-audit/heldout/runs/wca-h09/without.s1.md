## Revised copy

**Hero**
- Headline: One grocery list for the whole household
- Subhead: Everyone adds items, and everyone sees them the moment they're added. Tidewell is a shared list for people who live together.
- Buttons: Download for iPhone · Download for Android

**How it works**
1. **Make a household.** Invite the people you live with by link or code.
2. **Add things as you think of them.** Everyone's list updates straight away.
3. **Tick things off in the shop.** They disappear from everyone's list, so nobody buys the milk twice.

**Price**
- Free for households of up to 4.
- Larger households: $2 a month for the whole household. *(Confirm this is per household. See below.)*

**FAQ**
- **Can I use it on my own?** Yes. A household can be just one person, and it's free.
- **Is there a web version?** Not yet. Tidewell works on iPhone and Android.
- **How do I invite people?** Send a link or share a code from inside the app.

## What I changed and why

1. **The hero subhead was doing too many jobs.** The platform and "no web version" caveats sat between the pitch and the button. I moved the platform info onto the buttons and the caveat to small print, so the subhead now only explains the product.
2. **One generic download button became two.** "Download the app" makes someone on a laptop wonder where it goes. Naming iPhone and Android answers that and matches the "no web version" note.
3. **I added the price near the hero.** "Free for up to 4" is your best objection-killer, and it was buried below the fold.
4. **"You see them as they're added" is now "the moment they're added."** The original sentence switched subject mid-way ("Everyone adds… you see…"). The new one keeps "everyone" as the subject and is a little more concrete.
5. **The FAQ answer is a bit stronger.** It now says solo use is free. The FAQ was also a single question, so I added two that visitors are likely to ask. Swap them for your real support questions if you have any.
