## What I'd change

1. **The subhead does two jobs.** It describes the product, then lists platform availability and a limitation. Availability belongs next to the button, where someone deciding to download will look for it.
2. **"Everyone adds items, and you see them as they're added"** says the same thing as step 2 of How it Works ("Everyone sees the list update straight away"). I've trimmed the duplication.
3. **"Download the app" doesn't say which app or where.** A visitor on a laptop can't tell whether it will work for them. Naming the platforms on the button fixes that.
4. **The price line is ambiguous.** "Larger households are $2 a month" could mean $2 per household or $2 per person. It also doesn't say whether "4" counts people or devices.
5. **The FAQ answer is correct but flat.** It can say more about who the product is for.

## Revised copy

**HERO**
- Headline: One grocery list for the whole household *(unchanged, it's strong)*
- Subhead: Everyone adds items, and the list updates for everyone as it happens. Tidewell is a shared list for people who live together.
- Button: Download for iPhone or Android
- Small text under the button: No web version yet.

**HOW IT WORKS**
1. **Make a household.** Invite the people you live with by link or code.
2. **Add things as you think of them.** Everyone sees the list update straight away.
3. **Tick things off in the shop.** They disappear from everyone's list, so nobody buys the milk twice.

(The only change is a comma in step 3.)

**PRICE**
Free for households of up to 4 people. Larger households pay $2 a month.

**FAQ**
**Can I use Tidewell on my own?**
Yes. A household can be just you.

## Questions for the founder

- Is the $2 per household or per person? Is it billed monthly only, or is there an annual option?
- Does "up to 4" count people? What happens when a fifth person joins, and is there a prompt, a block, or a grace period?

- Is there a waitlist or notify-me option for the web version? "No web version yet" with no follow-up is a dead end.
