## Audit

**What's working:** The headline is clear and specific. "Milk twice" is a concrete example that makes the value obvious. The three steps are short and in the right order.

**What to fix:**

1. **The subhead repeats itself.** "Everyone adds items, and you see them as they're added" is said again two sentences later in the How it works section. Cut one.
2. **The platform note is buried and negative.** "There is no web version yet" leads with what you don't have. Put platform availability in its own line and state it plainly.
3. **The button doesn't say where the app runs.** With iPhone and Android, store badges next to the button will convert better than a generic label.
4. **The price is ambiguous.** "Larger households are $2 a month" doesn't say whether that's per household or per person, or what the billing period is. Someone reading this can't tell what they'll pay. This is the most important thing to confirm before Friday.
5. **Regional spelling is mixed.** "Grocery" is common in the US and Australia, but "shop," "tick," and "straight away" are more British or Australian. Pick one register and use it everywhere.
6. **The FAQ answer is clunky.** "Yes, a household can be one person" works, but it reads backwards.
7. **The page is thin for a launch.** There's one FAQ and no heading on the price section. That's fine for a soft launch, but you may want two or three more questions (see below).

## Suggested copy

**Hero**

> **One grocery list for the whole household**
>
> Tidewell is a shared list for people who live together. Anyone in the house can add an item, and everyone sees it right away.
>
> Available on iPhone and Android. Not on the web yet.
>
> [Download the app] [App Store] [Google Play]

**How it works**

1. **Create a household.** Invite the people you live with by link or code.
2. **Add things as you think of them.** Everyone sees the list update right away.
3. **Check things off at the shop.** They disappear from everyone's list, so nobody buys the milk twice.

**Price**

> Free for households of up to 4. Households of 5 or more pay $2 a month.

I've assumed the $2 is a flat monthly fee per household. Change this if that's wrong.

**FAQ**

> **Can I use Tidewell on my own?**
> Yes. A household can be just you.

## Questions for the founder before Friday

1. **Price:** Is $2/month per household or per person? Is it billed monthly or yearly, and in which currency? Is there a free trial?
2. **Joining:** Do invited people need the app installed to join, or can they accept a link in a browser? This affects the "not on the web yet" line and the invite copy.
3. **Spelling:** Are you writing for US, UK, or Australian readers? I'd pick one and I can rewrite the copy to match.
4. **Store listings:** Will the App Store and Google Play listings be live on Friday? If not, the badges and the download button need a different treatment.
