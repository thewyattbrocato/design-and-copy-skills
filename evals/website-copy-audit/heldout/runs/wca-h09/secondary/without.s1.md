Here's a tightened version, followed by the issues I'd check before Friday.

**HERO**
- **Headline:** One grocery list for the whole household
- **Subhead:** Tidewell is a shared list for the people you live with. Anyone can add an item, and everyone sees it the moment it's added. Available on iPhone and Android. No web version yet.
- **Button:** Download the app

**HOW IT WORKS**
1. **Make a household.** Invite the people you live with by link or code.
2. **Add things as you think of them.** Everyone sees the list update straight away.
3. **Tick things off at the shop.** They disappear from everyone's list, so nobody buys the milk twice.

**PRICE**
Free for households of up to 4. Households of 5 or more pay $2 a month.

**FAQ**
**Can I use Tidewell on my own?** Yes. A household can be just you.

**Issues to check**

1. **Pricing is ambiguous.** "Larger households are $2 a month" doesn't say whether that's per household or per person, or whether it's a subscription. The currency symbol also isn't clear for the market. Confirm all of this before launch, since it's the line people are most likely to dispute.
2. **Claims need to match the app.** Step 3 says items "disappear." Confirm that's what happens, and whether an accidentally ticked item can be restored. Step 2 promises instant updates, so confirm that holds too, including what happens offline.
3. **Spelling and dialect are mixed.** "Grocery" is American, while "shop," "tick," and "straight away" are British or Australian. I kept the founder's wording, but pick one market. If it's UK or AU, "shopping list" reads more naturally.
4. **The subhead repeated itself.** The original said "everyone adds items" and then "shared list for people who live together" in the same paragraph. I merged them.
5. **The web note reads as a disclaimer.** I kept it as a plain fact. If web is on the roadmap, don't promise a date on the page unless you have one.
6. **The button could be clearer.** Since the app runs on two platforms, store badges that link to the right store usually convert better than a generic "Download the app."

**Gaps I'd raise before Friday.** I haven't invented answers for these:
- A privacy or data line. People are sharing a list with housemates, so they'll want to know who can see it and whether an account is needed.
- What happens when someone leaves a household or an invite link is shared.
- Some pricing FAQ, such as whether there's a trial or how to cancel.
- A screenshot or short preview of the list in use.
- A closing call to action at the bottom of the page.
