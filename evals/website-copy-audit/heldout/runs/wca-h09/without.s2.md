The copy is already clear and plain. The changes below fix a few things that could cost you at launch.

## What I'd change

1. **The hero subhead does too much.** It repeats itself ("adds items… as they're added"), and it ends on "There is no web version yet", which is the weakest note to finish on.
2. **The button doesn't say where to download.** Visitors on a laptop can't use it, and you've just told them there's no web version. Make the button platform-aware, or add a line under it.
3. **The price is ambiguous.** "$2 a month" could be per household or per person over 4. Who pays, and is it billed monthly? Customers will ask this before they download.
4. **The spelling mixes US and UK.** "Tick things off", "in the shop" and "straight away" are British, but the price is in dollars. Pick one to match your audience. I've used US below. Say if you want UK and I'll switch it.
5. **"They disappear from everyone's list" needs checking.** If ticked items move to a "done" section instead of vanishing, this is inaccurate. Confirm against the app.
6. **The FAQ answer is thin.** "Yes" is fine, but it's an easy place to say who it's for.

## Rewrite

**HERO**
- Headline: One grocery list for the whole household
- Subhead: Everyone adds items, and everyone sees them the moment they're added. Made for people who live together.
- Button: Download the app
- Line under the button: Available for iPhone and Android. A web version is coming later.

"Coming later" promises something. If you can't commit to it, use "Web version not available yet."

**HOW IT WORKS**
1. **Make a household.** Invite the people you live with by link or code.
2. **Add things as you think of them.** The list updates for everyone right away.
3. **Check things off at the store.** They clear from everyone's list, so nobody buys the milk twice.

**PRICE**
Free for households of up to 4 people. Larger households pay $2 a month **[per household? confirm]** in total.

**FAQ**
**Can I use Tidewell on my own?**
Yes. A household can be just one person, so it works as a personal list too.

## Questions for the founder before Friday
- Is the $2 per household or per person? Is it billed monthly only?
- Do checked items vanish or move to a done section?
- US or UK spelling?
- Is a web version actually planned? That decides whether "coming later" is honest.

The page also has no FAQ on privacy or accounts, and nothing on what happens when a free household goes past 4 people. I'd add both if you have the answers, but I didn't want to invent them.
