## Why Kettlebridge?

- "Best-in-class," "industry-leading," and "trusted by teams everywhere" are phrases buyers have learned to skip. They are claims without evidence.
- Replace them with proof: a number ("X jobs dispatched per month"), a named customer with trade and size ("Smith Heating, 14 techs"), a review quote, or a logo strip. A buyer with 5 to 30 techs trusts a similar company far more than an adjective.
- "Powerful integrations" should name the integrations. If you connect to QuickBooks, a phone system, or Google Calendar, that's a reason to buy. "Powerful" isn't.

## Features

- **Smart Dispatch:** "Our intelligent engine optimizes your routes" describes the technology, not the result. Say what the owner gets, such as fewer drive-time gaps or fewer late arrivals. Quantify it if you can.
- **Mobile App:** "Work from anywhere" isn't what a tech cares about. Techs care about seeing the address, the job history, and the equipment on site, and about closing out a job without going back to the office.
- **Invoicing:** "Get paid faster" is the right outcome, but show the mechanism: "Invoice from the truck and take card payment before the tech leaves."

## Footer banner

"Ready to transform your business?" is the most generic line on the page. Make the offer specific, for example "See your own dispatch board in a 20-minute demo," and use the same wording as the hero button.

## What's missing for search visitors

Someone who searched a trade-specific term wants to confirm fit quickly. The page currently has none of these:

- **Who it's for.** You already know it's plumbing and HVAC companies with 5 to 30 techs. Say so in or directly under the hero. This also filters out people you can't serve well, which helps your conversion rate.
- **The problem.** Missed callbacks, dispatch by text message, paperwork at night, invoices going out a week late. Owners recognize their own pain when you name it.
- **How it works** in two or three steps. This lowers the perceived effort of switching.
- **Objection handling.** "Will my techs actually use it?" "How long does switching take?" "What happens to my data?" A short FAQ or a sentence on onboarding covers these.
- **Matching search language.** The H1 and title tag should include the terms people search, such as "HVAC dispatch software." Check that they do.
