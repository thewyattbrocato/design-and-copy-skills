## Findings, ranked

**1. Hero: a stranger can't tell what Hollowpaw is** (Blocker)
> "Hollowpaw connects loving pet parents with caring, passionate sitters in a community built on trust."

A newcomer can't tell if this is a sitting service, an app, a rescue or a social network. It never says Portland, never says marketplace, and never says overnight care. "Loving," "caring," "passionate" and "community built on trust" could be on any rival's page.
**Rewrite:**
> **Where pets feel at home.**
> Hollowpaw is a Portland marketplace for overnight pet sitting. Book a background-checked sitter by the night. Your pet stays at their place, or they stay at yours.

**2. "The best in the business" is unsupported** (Blocker)
> "Our sitters are the best in the business and treat every pet like family."

You can't back a superlative, and readers discount it. It also leaves out your real trust points: the background check and the ability to message first.
**Rewrite:**
> Every sitter is background checked through a third-party screening company. Message them before you book to see if they're the right fit for your pet.

If you want to name the screening company, add it. It makes the claim more credible.

**3. "Find. Connect. Relax." names no steps** (Major)
Nobody learns how booking works, what it costs or what the choices are. The fact that sitters set their own prices appears nowhere on the page.
**Rewrite:**
> **How it works**
> 1. **Find a sitter.** Browse Portland sitters. Each one sets their own nightly price.
> 2. **Message them first.** Ask questions and check the fit before you book.
> 3. **Book by the night.** Choose to have your pet stay at the sitter's home, or have the sitter come to yours.

Check that step 1 matches what the site lets people do. I only used what you told me.

**4. "Learn more" button** (Major)
It doesn't say what happens next. For a marketplace, the main action is finding a sitter.
**Fix:** Use "Find a sitter" if that's where the button goes. If it only scrolls to the explanation, "See how it works" is more honest.

**5. Footer: "Join the Hollowpaw family today!"** (Minor)
It doesn't say what to do, and "family" repeats the sitter line. It also reads as if you're recruiting members, when you want owners to book.
**Rewrite:** "Find a sitter in Portland." If you also want to recruit sitters, give them their own line and button. The current copy doesn't address them.

## What works
- "Where pets feel at home." It's warm, and it fits both the sitter's-home and your-home options. Keep it as the headline.
- The name and the "Find / Connect / Relax" rhythm are fine as a short summary. Put it above the concrete steps if you like it.

## Fix order
1. Rewrite the hero subline to say what you are, where, and how booking works (small).
2. Replace the "best in the business" line with the background check and message-first facts (small).
3. Turn "How it works" into three real steps that include per-night booking and sitter-set prices (small).
4. Change the button and footer to name the action (small).
5. Add a sitter-facing path and line if you're recruiting on this page (medium).
