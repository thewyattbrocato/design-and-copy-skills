## Findings, ranked

**1. Blocker. Our sitters section**
> "Our sitters are the best in the business and treat every pet like family."

"Best in the business" has no basis, and a new marketplace with no track record can't back it. Readers skim past it as boilerplate. The line also leaves out your one real, checkable trust fact, the background check.

Rewrite: *"Every Hollowpaw sitter is background checked through a third-party screening company. Sitters set their own nightly prices, and you can message them before you book."*

**2. Major. Hero**
> "Where pets feel at home. Hollowpaw connects loving pet parents with caring, passionate sitters in a community built on trust."

A stranger can't tell what this is. It never says pet sitting, Portland, overnight stays, or that it's a marketplace. Any pet brand could sign it. "Loving," "caring," "passionate," and "community built on trust" are stacked adjectives that say nothing specific.

Rewrite (headline kept): *"Where pets feel at home. Hollowpaw is a Portland marketplace for pet sitting. Choose a background-checked sitter who hosts your pet at their place or stays at yours. Booked by the night."*

**3. Major. How it works**
> "Find. Connect. Relax."

The rhythm is good, but the section tells the reader nothing about the process. The two things that set you apart, messaging before booking and the choice of where the pet stays, aren't mentioned. "Relax" is an outcome you can't promise, and it takes the place of the booking step.

Rewrite:
- **Find.** Browse Portland sitters and see their nightly prices. *(This assumes the site has a browse view. Adjust if it doesn't.)*
- **Connect.** Message a sitter before you book to ask questions and see if it's a fit.
- **Book.** Book by the night. The sitter hosts your pet at their place or comes to your home.

**4. Major. Button**
> "Learn more"

It doesn't say what happens next, and the hero already explains the basics. I assumed the main action is finding a sitter.

Rewrite: *"Find a sitter"*. Use a different label if the first step on your site is something else.

**5. Major. Price and terms appear nowhere**
Per-night booking and sitter-set pricing are the first things an owner wants to know, and neither appears in the copy you sent. Owners can't tell whether this is a flat rate, a subscription, or quotes. The fixes in #2 and #3 cover it. Also put "Sitters set their own nightly prices" near wherever prices are shown.

**6. Minor. Footer**
> "Join the Hollowpaw family today!"

"Family" echoes "treat every pet like family" and is vague. It doesn't say who should join (owners or sitters) or what joining involves.

Rewrite: *"Ready to find a sitter? Browse Portland sitters on Hollowpaw."* If sitters are a second audience, give them their own line, e.g. `[sitter sign-up line]`, once you tell me how they join.

## What works
- "Where pets feel at home." It's warm and clear, so keep it as the headline and let the subline carry the specifics.
- The three-beat step format ("Find. Connect. …") is easy to scan. Keep the format and only change the content.

## Fix order
1. Replace the "best in the business" line with the background-check line (small).
2. Rewrite the hero subline so it names pet sitting, Portland, per-night booking, and host-or-visit (small).
3. Rewrite "How it works" with the real steps (small).
4. Change the button and footer to name the action (small).
5. Make sure per-night, sitter-set pricing shows wherever sitters or prices appear (medium, since it touches other pages).
