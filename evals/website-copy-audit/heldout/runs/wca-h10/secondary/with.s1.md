**Ember & Rye homepage vs. Quillbean and Northpour (copy as supplied, 2026-10-28)**

**Findings, ranked**

1. **Major · Headline.** "Great coffee, delivered." Any coffee company could sign this. It doesn't say who it's for or why it's different, so a visitor can't tell if it's for their office. Quillbean's headline names the buyer's problem. **Fix:** "Coffee roasted this week, delivered to your office."

2. **Major · Sub, first screen.** "Choose your beans, choose your frequency, and we take care of the rest." This describes a generic subscription. Our one real difference, that beans are roasted that week, appears only in a separate section lower on the page. **Fix:** "We roast on Tuesdays and ship on Wednesdays, so every bag was roasted that week. Pick your beans and how often you want them, and we take care of the rest."

3. **Major · Price, next step.** No price appears anywhere in the supplied copy, and the button says "Start a subscription," so a buyer has to commit before learning the cost. Quillbean states a plan threshold and sends people to "See plans and prices." **Fix:** add a line under the sub: "Plans from [price per month]. [Renewal and cancellation terms in one line.]" Placeholders are needed because I don't have your prices or terms. If the plans are on another page, change the button to "See plans and prices."

4. **Major · Trust line placement.** "We're a small team in Leeds and every order is packed by one of us" is the most credible thing we say, and it sits mid-page under a section label. **Fix:** move it directly below the sub, unchanged. It needs no rewrite.

5. **Minor · Footer.** "Questions? Email hello@emberandrye.example" is a direct, human contact point that suits a small team. Leave it.

**What works (keep as written)**

- "We roast on Tuesdays and ship on Wednesdays, so what you get was roasted that week."
- "We're a small team in Leeds and every order is packed by one of us."
- "Start a subscription" is a clear action, even if it should change once prices are visible.

**Competitor comparison**

- **Who it's for:** all three target offices.
- **Main promise:** Quillbean promises less hassle. Northpour promises premium quality. We promise freshness and a named local team.
- **Proof:** Quillbean offers roast-to-order, next-morning dispatch and a grinder loan. Northpour's "ethically sourced" has no detail behind it. Ours is a fixed weekly roast, Leeds and hand-packing, which is more specific than either rival.
- **Price visibility:** Quillbean has a stated threshold and a plans link. Northpour has none. We have none.
- **Tone:** Quillbean is plain and a little playful. Northpour is abstract ("discerning office"). Ours is plain, but only in the lower section.

Quillbean does the first screen better: it names the buyer's annoyance, shows the choices and points to price. Northpour is the weakest of the three, and we beat it on specifics. The distinction worth keeping is the fixed weekly roast and the named team. Don't copy Quillbean's roast-to-order framing. The gap to close is price visibility. If you have a grinder loan or similar perk, say so on the page; I haven't added one because it isn't in what you gave me.
