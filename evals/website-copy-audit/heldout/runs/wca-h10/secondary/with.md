# Homepage copy: Ember & Rye vs Quillbean and Northpour

*Based on the copy you pasted, not the live pages. Reviewed 28 Oct 2026.*

**Bottom line:** Quillbean is the clearest of the three. Our copy has the most checkable facts, but they sit below a headline that could describe any coffee company, and the copy never says what the subscription costs before it asks for a commitment.

## Where we're losing

**1. Major · Headline, "Great coffee, delivered."** A stranger learns that we sell coffee and deliver it, but not that it's for offices or what makes us different. "Great" is a quality claim with no basis on the page. Quillbean's "Office coffee without the faff" names the buyer and the frustration in six words. Fix: use the headline below, or keep "Great coffee" only if you can point to a basis for it.

**2. Major · Button, "Start a subscription."** Nothing before this button in the copy you sent states a price, renewal or cancellation terms. Quillbean's button says "See plans and prices", so its reader expects the cost. Asking for a subscription before the cost appears loses people who would otherwise stay. Fix: add the price line below.

**3. Major · Your best line is the third block.** "We roast on Tuesdays and ship on Wednesdays, so what you get was roasted that week" is the strongest thing on the page: specific, checkable and yours. Quillbean makes a similar freshness claim in its sub ("We roast to order in Manchester and post it the next morning"). Ours is more concrete, but only a reader who scrolls that far will see it. Fix: move the schedule into the sub.

**4. Minor · Sub, "we take care of the rest."** This tells the reader they'll get less effort without saying what they get. Northpour's "Elevate your workplace coffee experience" has the same problem, with even less to go on.

**5. Minor · Footer, "hello@emberandrye.example."** `.example` is a reserved placeholder domain. Confirm the real address works before Monday.

## Competitor read

Same dimensions for each:

- **Who it's for:** Quillbean says "office" in the headline. Northpour's "the discerning office" flatters the reader without describing the service. Ours says "your office" in the sub, which is fine, but the headline doesn't.
- **Main promise:** Quillbean: pick a bag size and a delivery day. Northpour: "premium specialty beans." Ours: roasted that week, packed by one person in Leeds. Ours is the most checkable of the three.
- **Proof:** None of the three shows customers in this copy. Northpour's "ethically sourced" is a claim with no detail. We can't match reviews yet, so our schedule and packing facts have to carry the proof.
- **Price visibility:** Quillbean signals it through its button. Northpour and we don't show it in the copy supplied.
- **Tone:** Quillbean is plain and local ("faff"). Northpour is generic premium. Ours is plain too, which suits us.

Quillbean does the job best of the three. The gap to close is price visibility. The distinction to keep is the roasting-week promise and one-person packing.

## What works

- "We roast on Tuesdays and ship on Wednesdays, so what you get was roasted that week."
- "every order is packed by one of us."
- "Fresh-roasted beans for your office, on your schedule."

Leave these as they are.
