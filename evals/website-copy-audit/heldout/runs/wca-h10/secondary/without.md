# Homepage copy: where we stand against Quillbean and Northpour

**Bottom line:** Quillbean is the real competitor. Its copy says who it's for, what the offer is, and what it costs to start. Northpour's copy is mostly adjectives, so it isn't the benchmark. We have the best material in the set (weekly roasting, a Leeds team that packs every order), but a generic headline and a buried process section hide it.

## How the three compare

| | Ours | Quillbean | Northpour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | "Great coffee, delivered." Could be anyone. | "Office coffee without the faff." Names the buyer and the pain. | "Elevate your workplace coffee experience." Jargon. |
| Specifics | Weekly roast, Wednesday dispatch, Leeds, hand-packed | Bag sizes, delivery day, next-morning dispatch, free grinder over 3kg/month | "Premium," "ethically sourced," "expertly roasted." No proof. |
| CTA | "Start a subscription." Asks for commitment before showing anything. | "See plans and prices." Low friction. | "Learn more." Vague. |

## Where we're losing

1. **The headline says nothing specific.** A visitor can't tell what makes us different from the first line.
2. **Our best facts are below the fold.** The Tuesday roast, Wednesday dispatch, and packed-by-one-of-us story is our strongest proof, and it sits in a secondary section.
3. **Quillbean makes a concrete offer and we don't.** Bag sizes, delivery days, and a grinder loan are reasons to click. We show none of that.
4. **Our CTA asks the most.** "Start a subscription" comes before any prices or options. Quillbean's "See plans and prices" lets people qualify themselves first.
5. **Freshness is contested.** Quillbean also claims roast-to-order. We need to win on what they can't copy: a fixed weekly cadence, a Leeds team, and a person who packs the box.

Don't borrow Northpour's style. "Premium" and "ethically sourced" with no evidence read as filler.

## What to change

**1. Headline and sub**
- Current: *Great coffee, delivered*
- New: **Roasted Tuesday. Shipped Wednesday.**
- Sub: *Fresh-roasted beans for your office, every week. Choose your beans and your frequency. We roast and pack every order ourselves in Leeds.*

This puts the freshness promise in the first line and names the local, human difference. It keeps the choice-of-beans and frequency points from the original sub.

**2. Move the process section up**
Place it directly under the hero, tightened:
*Every bag is roasted the week it ships. We're a small team in Leeds, and one of us packs your order before it leaves.*

**3. Pair two CTAs**
- Primary: **Choose your beans**
- Secondary: **See plans and prices** (links to pricing, matching Quillbean's low-friction entry point)

**4. Use the proof we have**
With no reviews yet, show process instead:
- Print the roast date on every bag and show it on the homepage.
- Add a photo of the team member who packs orders.
- Name the roaster if they're willing to be named.

**5. Make the help line personal**
- Current: *Questions? Email hello@emberandrye.example*
- New: *Questions? Talk to the team in Leeds: hello@emberandrye.example*

## Open questions before Monday

- **Can we show prices on the homepage?** Quillbean's CTA depends on visible plans. Without prices, the secondary CTA has nowhere to go.
- **Do we have any offer?** A first-order discount or a grinder-style perk would give visitors a reason to act now. If we don't, I'd rather not invent one. Clear pricing does more than a fake incentive.
- **Do we know our bean origins and roasters?** Specific sourcing would beat Northpour's vague "ethically sourced" with actual proof.
- **Are we comfortable showing the people behind the roasting and packing?** With no reviews, that's our strongest proof.
