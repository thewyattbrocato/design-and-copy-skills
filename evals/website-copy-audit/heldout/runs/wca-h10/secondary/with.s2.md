**Ember & Rye homepage vs. Quillbean and Northpour**

Source: the three homepage extracts as supplied in the roundup (undated). Homepage copy only; no screenshots, sign-up flow or emails reviewed.

**Summary:** We have the most checkable facts of the three, and we hide them. Quillbean tells the buyer what they pick and what to click next. Northpour says nothing that can be checked. We have no blocker. We lose on a generic headline, a buried proof point, and a subscribe button with no price.

**Findings**

1. **Major · Headline.** "Great coffee, delivered." This could be any coffee seller's headline. It names neither the office buyer nor the weekly rhythm, so a stranger has no reason to choose us over Quillbean's "Office coffee without the faff," which names the buyer and the problem. Fix: "Office coffee, roasted the week it ships."

2. **Major · Our best fact is buried.** "We roast on Tuesdays and ship on Wednesdays, so what you get was roasted that week." sits in the second section, after the sub. A reader who leaves after the hero never learns our main difference. Fix: move the line into the sub (rewrite below).

3. **Major · Subscribe with no price or terms.** "Start a subscription" asks for a commitment before the reader sees a price, a renewal rule or how to stop. Quillbean's button, "See plans and prices," sets the expectation that a price is coming. Fix: change the button to "See plans and prices" and add the real price and terms beside the sub (placeholders below). Do not write "cancel anytime" unless it is true and on file.

4. **Minor · "On your schedule" promises without specifics.** A buyer cannot tell what the schedule options are. Quillbean says what the buyer picks: "Pick a bag size and a delivery day." Fix: list the actual choices in the sub.

**What works (keep these)**

- "We roast on Tuesdays and ship on Wednesdays, so what you get was roasted that week." Specific and checkable. It is our best line.
- "every order is packed by one of us." Distinctive and true. Quillbean and Northpour make no comparable claim in the supplied copy.
- "Fresh-roasted beans for your office." A good start; keep "for your office."
- "Questions? Email hello@emberandrye.example." A real contact. Neither rival shows one in the supplied copy.

**Rewrites**

- Headline: "Office coffee, roasted the week it ships."
- Sub: "Fresh-roasted beans for your office. Choose your beans and delivery frequency. We roast on Tuesdays and ship on Wednesdays, so every order was roasted that week."
- Terms line under the sub: "[price per bag or per month]. [Renewal and cancellation, as they actually work.]"
- Button: "See plans and prices." This only works if the link goes to a page that shows prices.

**Competitor comparison**

- *Who it's for:* Quillbean and we both say office. Northpour's "the discerning office" is vague.
- *Main promise:* Quillbean is concrete about mechanics. We are concrete about timing. Northpour uses adjectives.
- *Proof:* We have the most checkable facts. Northpour's "ethically sourced" has no source on the page, so don't copy the phrase.
- *Price visibility:* Quillbean points to prices. We don't. Northpour doesn't either.

The one gap to close is price and terms visibility. The one distinction to keep is the Tuesday roast, Wednesday dispatch and one-person packing.

**Also noticed**

- Quillbean offers a free grinder loan over 3kg a month. If we have an equivalent perk, put it in the sub. If we don't, don't invent one.
- We have no reviews. Don't add counts or quotes we can't back. If you have a real photo of the people who pack the orders, that is an honest substitute.

**Fix order**

1. Add price and terms beside the sub. **Small** if the figures exist, **medium** if pricing needs settling first.
2. Move the Tuesday/Wednesday line into the sub. **Small.**
3. Replace the headline. **Small.**
4. Change the button to "See plans and prices" and make the linked page show prices. **Medium.**
5. Replace "on your schedule" with the actual options. **Small.**
