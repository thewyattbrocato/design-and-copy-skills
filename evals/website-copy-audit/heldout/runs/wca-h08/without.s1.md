- **Home:** The old title was about 130 characters, so Google was cutting it. It had no description, so Google was picking its own text.
- **Whitening:** I dropped "the best teeth whitening available". It's an unsupported claim, and Google tends to ignore generic copy like that.
- **Emergency page:** The title uses "Brightwater" alone to stay under the limit. The description names the full practice.
- **Title lengths:** Google actually truncates by pixel width, not character count. All four titles have some room, but wide letters like "W" and "M" take more space.
