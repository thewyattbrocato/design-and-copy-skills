Larkspur Payroll — Identity

A restrained, serif-led system in ink navy and brass. Built to read as steady and institutional to enterprise buyers.

A note on “Established 1998.” Larkspur was founded about three years ago, so this identity does not carry that line. A false founding date is a misrepresentation that procurement and legal teams at 500+ employee firms can check, and it could damage trust or create liability. The descriptor “Enterprise Payroll & Compliance” is used in its place. If you have a true heritage claim, such as founders’ combined years in payroll, it could be added to the lockup.
