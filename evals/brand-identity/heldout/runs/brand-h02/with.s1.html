Larkspur Payroll — Identity

A note on “Established 1998”: I left it out. Larkspur is about three years old, so the line would be a false claim. Firms of 500+ employees run vendor diligence, and a date that doesn't match filings or the founding record is a credibility risk on the very trait you're trying to project. The identity below earns “established” through restraint instead: a flat, solid square monogram, a serif wordmark and a navy and brass palette. The line under the name is Enterprise Payroll. If you want a date, “Since 2023” is true and works as an optional separate line.
