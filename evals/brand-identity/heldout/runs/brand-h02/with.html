Enterprise Payroll

Idea: a pillar on a base, the L as a structure you can build on. The gap between pillar and base is the one custom cut. Georgia bold, tracked wide, gives the name an institutional register. Navy carries the weight and brass appears only as a hairline accent. Gradients are dropped: the mark is flat and solid, so it holds in one color and reversed.
