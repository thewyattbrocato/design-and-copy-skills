Larkspur Payroll · Identity

The mark: an L drawn as a spire, after the larkspur's tall flower spike.

Built for buyers at 500+ employee firms: a serif wordmark, one solid flat symbol, ink and plum only. The old gradient swoosh and rounded sans are retired because they read as a startup. Gradients also fail in one-colour use.

On "Established 1998": I left it out. Larkspur is about three years old, and a false founding date is easy for enterprise procurement to disprove in due diligence. It would also be a misleading claim to clients. The descriptor line under the name does the work instead. You can replace it with "Founded 2023" or a real credential such as SOC 2 or a certification.
