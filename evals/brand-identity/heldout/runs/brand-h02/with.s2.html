Design notes

Why not "Established 1998": Larkspur is about three years old, so that line would be a false claim. Enterprise buyers run vendor due diligence (registrations, references, financials), and a founding date that doesn't match would damage trust far more than a young company does. I used "Payroll for the enterprise" in that position instead. If you want a date line, "Established 2023" is accurate. Update the year if your incorporation date differs.

How it reads as established: a classical serif wordmark with wide tracking, a restrained navy and brass palette, a square monogram in place of the gradient swoosh, and a larkspur stem of three florets rising from the L. The mark is one flat shape set, so it works at small sizes and in one colour.

What would help more than a date: named enterprise customers, security attestations you actually hold, and the team's payroll-industry experience. Those are things a 500+ employee buyer can verify.
