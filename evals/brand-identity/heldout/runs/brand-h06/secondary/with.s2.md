## Lockups by placement

**Units.** Measure everything in **b**, the height of one book bar in the symbol. Set the name in two lines, "Southbank" over "Reading Room", in the same weight as the book bars. The sizes below are starting values to check on real output.

**Lockups to build:**
1. **Horizontal:** symbol left, two-line name right.
2. **Stacked:** symbol above the centered two-line name.
3. **Symbol only:** for small and square spaces.

Each comes in solid ink on light grounds and reversed (paper-colored) on dark grounds.

### 1. Wide website header
- **Lockup:** horizontal.
- **Layout:** symbol on the left. The name block is two lines, and its height equals the symbol's height: the top of "Southbank" aligns with the top book, and the baseline of "Reading Room" aligns with the bottom book.
- **Gap:** 1b between symbol and name.
- **Size:** symbol 40–56 px tall on desktop. Below 24 px tall, switch to symbol only.
- **Clear space:** at least 1b on all sides.
- **Position:** left-aligned, vertically centered in the header bar.
- **Color:** ink on a light header, reversed on a dark header. Never place the full mark over a photo without a solid panel behind it.

### 2. Square social avatar
- **Lockup:** symbol only. At 40–200 px the name would be unreadable.
- **Field:** a solid, full-bleed square in one color. No transparent corners, since platforms crop to a circle and show the image on dark backgrounds.
- **Size:** symbol about 50% of the canvas height, so it sits inside the central circle that survives a circle crop (about 70% of the diameter).
- **Centering:** center optically. Nudge the symbol if the stack looks low or high.
- **Export:** 512 px and 1024 px.
- **Variant:** light symbol on a dark field, or the reverse.

### 3. Narrow bookmark (about 50 × 150 mm)
- **Lockup:** stacked, with the name horizontal.
- **Layout:** symbol in the upper part, two-line name centered below it.
- **Symbol width:** about 60% of the bookmark's width.
- **Top margin:** keep the top 15 mm clear for a tassel hole or ribbon, then place the symbol.
- **Name width:** no more than 80% of the bookmark's width. Minimum 7 pt for print.
- **Rotation:** keep the name horizontal. Rotating it along the spine is a crop-test variant for later, not the default, since it is harder to read held in the hand.
- **Print:** one color. No hairlines or gaps narrower than about 1 mm at print size. Widen the gaps in the printed version if needed.

### 4. Canvas tote bag (front panel about 36 × 40 cm)
- **Lockup:** stacked, centered horizontally.
- **Vertical position:** top of the lockup at about 35–40% down the panel, so it sits clear of the handle seam and the hand when carrying.
- **Width:** about 55–60% of the panel width (roughly 20–22 cm).
- **Edge clearance:** at least 8% of the panel width from the sides.
- **Print:** one-color screen print, no gradients. Test a swatch of the actual canvas before printing the run.
- **Ink:** black on natural (light) canvas. Paper-colored ink on dark canvas.

## Shared rules
- **Files:** deliver SVG for the web, and vector PDF for the bookmark and tote printers.
- **Color:** default to one color. Full color is an optional extra for the header.

## Notes
- I assumed the name is set as two lines in one typeface. If the name stays on one line, the horizontal lockup changes: the name becomes a single line, and its cap height should still match the symbol's total height.
- I have not seen the actual artwork, so the numbers are proportions to test on a real printed bookmark and tote and a live header, not verified sizes.
