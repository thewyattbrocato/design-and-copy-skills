## Shared groundwork (build these first)

- **Three lockups from one master file:**
  - **Horizontal:** mark on the left, name on the right.
  - **Stacked:** mark centered above the name.
  - **Mark only:** the book-S by itself.
- **Name on two lines.** Set "Southbank" over "Reading Room" so the wordmark is compact and works in both lockups.
- **Clear space:** leave at least the height of one book spine on all sides.
- **Color versions:** full color, one-color dark, and one-color reversed (white). The tote and any small print need the one-color versions.
- **Convert all text to outlines** and keep a vector master (SVG).

## 1. Wide website header

- **Use the horizontal lockup,** left-aligned, with the mark about 40–48 px tall on desktop. The name sits to the right, vertically centered on the mark.
- **Name size:** cap height roughly one-third to one-half of the mark height. The two lines stay tight, with the stack's total height no taller than the mark.
- **Navigation goes right.** Leave generous empty space between logo and nav so the logo doesn't compete with it.
- **Background:** use the full-color version on a light header. If the header is dark or colored, use the reversed version.
- **Mobile:** drop to the mark plus a one-line or smaller two-line name at about 32 px tall. Below roughly 360 px wide, use the mark alone and put the name in text next to it or in the page title.
- **Export:** SVG for the header, with a 2x PNG fallback. Add alt text: "Southbank Reading Room."

## 2. Square social avatar

- **Use the mark alone,** centered, without the name. Most platforms show avatars very small, and the name would be unreadable at that size.
- **Safe area:** keep the mark within the central ~60–65% of the canvas. Circular crops (Instagram, Facebook, X) will cut the corners.
- **Background:** a solid brand color with the reversed mark, or a cream/white field with the full-color mark. Don't use a transparent background, because platforms fill it unpredictably.
- **Optical centering:** an S built from stacked books may look off-center even when it's mathematically centered. Nudge it until it looks balanced inside a circle.
- **Export:** 1080×1080 px PNG, and check that it reads at 48 px.
- **Name:** put it in the profile name and bio, not the image.

## 3. Narrow bookmark

- **Use the stacked lockup in a vertical layout.** A typical bookmark is about 2 × 6 in (5 × 15 cm).
- **Top third:** the mark, centered, at about 60–70% of the bookmark's width.
- **Name:** "Southbank / Reading / Room" on up to three short lines under the mark, centered, in a size that fits within the margins. Don't shrink the type below about 8 pt in print. If it doesn't fit, break the name into three lines.
- **Lower area:** leave open for a short line, such as the website, address, or hours.
- **Optional:** a thin rule or a repeating spine-stripe pattern along the bottom, echoing the stacked books.
- **Print:** include 1/8 in bleed. Keep text and the mark at least 1/4 in inside the trim. A hole or tassel at the top needs its own clearance.
- **Back side:** can use a solid brand color with the mark alone, reversed.

## 4. Canvas tote bag

- **Use the stacked lockup** with the mark above the name, centered on the bag, about 8–10 in wide. Place it in the upper-middle of the bag body, roughly 3–4 in below the top seam, so it clears the straps and sits above where the bag bulges.
- **Limit the colors.** Screen printing on canvas works best with one or two ink colors. Use the one-color version in a dark ink on natural canvas, or white ink on a dark bag.
- **Simplify fine detail.** Canvas texture swallows thin lines. Make sure any gaps between the books are at least ~1.5 mm wide, and thicken hairlines in the type. If the mark has gradients or shading, flatten them to solid shapes.
- **Name:** use a heavier weight than on screen, with a bit of extra letter spacing.
- **Production file:** supply a vector file (SVG or AI/PDF) with the text outlined, at actual print size. Check with the printer on their ink and file requirements.
- **Option:** a small mark alone on the front corner of a pocket, for a secondary, quieter variant.
