Assumptions: the mark is a flat, single-color stack of three books whose negative space forms an S. The name is "Southbank Reading Room". The brand color is one ink plus a paper color. Nothing here is based on seeing the actual artwork.

## Master files to build first
- **Symbol:** solid and flat, with no gradients or shadows. Check it in solid black on white first, then reversed (white on dark).
- **Small symbol:** a heavier redraw for 16–48 px. Make the gaps between books wider and the S-forming cuts thicker. Drop any spine details or hairlines.
- **Wordmark:** "Southbank Reading Room" in a sturdy serif or humanist sans that matches the symbol's stroke weight. Add one custom touch, such as a cut or a shared baseline with the bottom book. Read the name aloud and check the crops below for unintended words.
- **Lockups:** horizontal, stacked and symbol-only. Clear space is the height of one book on all sides. A tagline, if used, is a separate line and never part of the mark.

## 1. Wide website header
- **Layout:** the horizontal lockup, with the symbol at left and the name beside it, vertically centered on the symbol.
- **Name:** one line, "Southbank Reading Room". If the header is very tight, stack it on two lines ("Southbank / Reading Room") to match the symbol's height.
- **Size:** symbol about 40–56 px tall on desktop. On mobile, keep the horizontal lockup if the name stays legible (about 14 px minimum). Otherwise show the symbol alone with the name in text.
- **Ground:** the full-color or black version on a light header. Use the reversed version if the header is dark.
- **Spacing:** keep the clear space around it. Navigation sits to the right and doesn't touch the lockup.

## 2. Square social avatar
- **Layout:** the symbol only, with no name. The name is already shown beside the avatar on every platform, and it would be unreadable at about 40 px.
- **Size and crop:** center the symbol with generous padding, about 20% on each side. Many platforms crop avatars to a circle, so the symbol must fit inside the circle with room to spare.
- **Version:** use the heavier small symbol.
- **Ground:** a solid brand-color field with the symbol reversed, or the ink symbol on paper. Either reads well at thumbnail size.

## 3. Narrow bookmark
- **Layout:** the stacked or vertical arrangement. Put the symbol at the top, then the name on three short lines ("Southbank / Reading / Room"), centered and set horizontally.
- **Name direction:** keep the text horizontal if it fits. Rotating the name 90° to run down the spine is the alternative. It echoes a book spine, but check that it's readable and doesn't produce odd letter combinations in that orientation.
- **Option:** place the symbol large at the top or bottom, with the name smaller and a plain field between them.
- **Printing:** one or two flat inks (ink on paper stock, or reversed on a colored stock). Avoid fine detail and tints that print poorly on thin card.
- **Safe area:** leave at least 5 mm from the edges, and from any punched hole or tassel.

## 4. Canvas tote bag
- **Layout:** the stacked lockup, or the symbol large with the name below it, centered on the upper-middle of the bag. Keep it about 20–25 cm wide so it reads from a distance.
- **Ink:** one color, screen-printed. Use the solid black or reversed version. Dark ink goes on natural canvas, and light ink on a dark bag.
- **Production:** thicken thin gaps and joins so the ink doesn't fill them in on textured fabric. Use the heavier small symbol's proportions if the S cuts are fine.
- **Option:** a symbol-only large print on one side and the full name on a small tag or the reverse. This is simpler and more graphic, but the name is then less prominent.
