# Southbank Reading Room: four logo arrangements

**Assumptions:** the mark is a flat, single-color stack of three books that reads as an S. The name is "Southbank Reading Room." You have a light and a dark ink color, and there's no tagline. If the mark has gradients or fine lines, flatten it first. Everything below depends on a solid shape that works in one color.

## Parts to build first

Make four master pieces and use them everywhere:

1. **Symbol:** the book-S alone.
2. **Horizontal lockup:** symbol on the left, name on the right, on one line or two.
3. **Stacked lockup:** symbol above the name, centered.
4. **Small-size symbol:** a heavier redraw. Thicken the gaps between books and the spine lines, and drop any detail that disappears at 32 px.

**Clear space:** keep at least the height of one book spine free on all sides of every lockup.

**Wordmark:** set it in one weight that matches the symbol's stroke weight. Tune the spacing by eye. Check it upside down for accidental words. Add one custom touch, for example a book-spine-width cut in a letter, so it isn't plain stock type.

## 1. Wide website header

- **Use:** horizontal lockup, left-aligned. Put the name on one line: "Southbank Reading Room."
- **Proportions:** the symbol is about 40–48 px tall on desktop. The cap height of the name is roughly half the symbol height, and the gap between them is about one book-spine width.
- **Mobile:** the name becomes two lines ("Southbank / Reading Room") beside the symbol, or you switch to symbol only if the space is under about 160 px wide.
- **Color:** dark ink on a light header. Use the reversed (light) version if the header sits on a dark band or photo.
- **Don't:** center the logo in a wide bar with empty sides, put a tagline in the lockup, or stretch it to fill the width.
- **Favicon:** use the small-size symbol, not the full logo shrunk.

## 2. Square social avatar

- **Use:** symbol only. The name is unreadable at avatar sizes, and the platform shows the account name beside it anyway.
- **Layout:** center the symbol on a solid brand-color square, filling about 60–65% of the width. Platforms crop avatars to a circle, so keep everything inside a central circle with a margin all round.
- **Weight:** use the small-size symbol, since it will often display at 40 px or less.
- **Color:** a light symbol on a dark ground, or the reverse. Pick whichever stands out more in a feed.
- **Check:** view it at 32 px and in circle crop before approving.

## 3. Narrow bookmark

- **Use:** stacked lockup, or better, the symbol with the name turned vertical.
- **Option A (recommended):** a bookmark about 2 × 6 in. Put the symbol near the top, about 70–80% of the width. Below it, set the name on three short lines, "Southbank / Reading / Room," centered, with generous space beneath.
- **Option B:** the symbol at the top and the name running vertically along the length, reading bottom to top, in a smaller size. This suits a long, thin shape but is harder to read, so keep the name large enough to read comfortably.
- **Don't:** squeeze the horizontal lockup into the width, which makes it tiny.
- **Print:** one ink on colored or kraft stock is the cheapest and sturdiest option. Leave about 0.25 in. of margin from the edges, and punch any hole or tassel clear of the symbol.

## 4. Canvas tote bag

- **Use:** stacked lockup, printed in one color. Screen printing handles flat shapes well and not fine detail.
- **Placement:** center it on the upper-middle of the bag, about 6–8 in. wide on a standard tote. Lower down tends to get hidden by what's carried.
- **Color:** a single dark ink (navy or black) on natural canvas, or white ink on a dark bag. Use the solid, no-gradient version, and use the heavier small-size symbol if the print is small.
- **Detail:** keep the thinnest line at least about 1.5 mm, which survives fabric texture and ink spread. The print shop may need to thicken thin gaps.
- **Option:** for a second run, a large symbol only, cropped bold and partly off-center, can serve as a graphic pattern. Keep the full lockup on the main design so people can read the name.

## Quick rules for your designer

- Flat, solid color only. No shadows, gradients or textures in the logo itself.
- Test each version in black on white and reversed before choosing colors.
- Set a minimum size for each lockup and write it down. As a starting point, use 24 px tall for the horizontal lockup on screen and 0.5 in. for print.
- Save the files as SVG for web and print, plus PNGs for the avatar.

**Trade-off:** the stacked lockup is the most flexible, but it's wide in the bookmark and tote only if you keep it narrow, so check the name line breaks there first.
