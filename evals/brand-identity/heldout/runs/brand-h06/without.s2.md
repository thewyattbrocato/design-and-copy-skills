# Southbank Reading Room: logo layouts

I'm working from your description (a three-book stack forming an S, with the name beside it), not the artwork itself. Adjust the numbers below once your designer has the real proportions. Let **x** be the height of one book in the stack. Use x for clearance and sizing, so every version scales the same way.

## Two building blocks to prepare first

1. **Mark only:** the stacked-S books, with no text.
2. **Horizontal lockup:** the mark on the left and the name on the right, vertically centered on the mark. Set the name on two lines, "Southbank" over "Reading Room". The mark's height should match the two-line text block, or be slightly taller. A one-line name makes a very long lockup.

A **stacked lockup** (mark above, centered name below) is useful for the bookmark and tote.

Keep a clear space of at least x on all sides in every version.

## 1. Website header (wide)

- **Layout:** horizontal lockup, left-aligned, with navigation on the right.
- **Size:** about 40–56 px tall on desktop, and never smaller than 32 px.
- **Name:** keep the two-line name on desktop. On mobile, either shrink the lockup or switch to the mark plus a one-line "Southbank Reading Room" if the width allows. Below roughly 320 px, show the mark alone.
- **Color:** full-color on a white or light background. If the header is dark or photographic, use a one-color white version.
- **Build notes:** export as SVG. The logo should link to the home page and have alt text "Southbank Reading Room."

## 2. Social avatar (square)

- **Layout:** the mark alone, centered. Don't include the name, because it becomes unreadable at 40–60 px, and the platform shows the account name next to the avatar anyway.
- **Crop safety:** most platforms crop to a circle, so the whole mark must fit inside a circle covering about 80% of the canvas. Keep the corners empty.
- **Background:** a solid brand color, or a light tint with the mark in full color. Make sure the S shape still reads at small sizes. If the three books lose their gaps when small, thicken the gaps or simplify the spines for this version.
- **Size:** build at 1000 × 1000 px and let the platform scale it down.

## 3. Bookmark (narrow, tall)

- **Layout:** stacked, vertical. Put the mark near the top, large, at about 60–70% of the bookmark's width. Put the name below it, centered on two lines. Leave the lower area open for a tagline, hours, or the website address, or just leave it blank.
- **Alternative:** if you want a bolder look, rotate the name 90° to run vertically along the length, with the mark at the top. This works better with a short name line and gives the bookmark a spine-like feel.
- **Details:** keep everything at least 3 mm from the edges, and add 3 mm of bleed if the printer trims it. If there's a hole or tassel at the top, move the mark down. Check that the smallest text is at least 7 pt.
- **Color:** one or two ink colors keep printing cheap. Use a one-color version on a colored stock.

## 4. Canvas tote bag

- **Layout:** stacked lockup, centered on the front of the bag, about 20–25 cm wide on a standard tote. Place it in the upper-middle of the bag body, not at the very center, so it sits above where the bag sags.
- **Color:** one-color screen print, for example dark ink on natural canvas, or white on a dark tote. Convert the mark to a flat one-color version first. If it uses gradients or thin lines, simplify them, since fabric ink can't hold fine detail. Keep gaps between the books at least 1.5 mm at print size.
- **Option:** a small mark alone on the back or on a pocket makes a nice secondary detail, but it's not required.
- **Build notes:** supply vector art with text converted to outlines.
