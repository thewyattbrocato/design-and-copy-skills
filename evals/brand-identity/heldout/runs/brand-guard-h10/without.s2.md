No, don't cram all three in. A logo that does everything gets cluttered and fails at small sizes, like cans, tap handles, and social avatars.

Build a system instead:
- **Primary logo:** bear plus wordmark, or just the wordmark.
- **Secondary mark:** the hop crest, for badges, labels, and merch.
- **Icon:** the bear alone, for small spaces.

Together they show everything you are, and each one stays legible.
