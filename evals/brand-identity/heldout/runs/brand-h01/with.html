The idea: a single bore dropping from the surface to water. Flat, one color, and the shaft is thick enough to hold up at 16 px and on a truck door. Assumed: rural, family-run, trustworthy and plain-spoken, with a dark green and a cream as the base colors. Water blue (#2f7f9e) is held back as an optional accent.
