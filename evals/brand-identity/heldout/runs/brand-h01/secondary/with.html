Notes

Idea: a drop of water, cut by the creek line. It says water and it says place, in one shape. Form: flat and solid, no gradient or shine, so it works in one color, reversed, and at 16 px. Type: system sans-serif in bold caps with open tracking, so the name stays legible on a truck at highway speed. Color: ink and paper only for now. Choose a single accent later if the trucks need one. Assumptions: no existing mark was given, and the name is taken as the brand. A trademark search should be done before using it.
