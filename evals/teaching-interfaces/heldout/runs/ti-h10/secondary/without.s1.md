Here's a rewrite that groups the steps by purpose and puts them in the order they're easiest to do:

---

**Welcome to Stackwell**

Stackwell helps your clinic keep supplies stocked. You can track what's on hand, order from vendors, and receive deliveries. Work through these steps in order, or finish now and return to any of them later.

**Account basics**
1. **Set your currency and time zone.** These apply to costs, reports, and dates across your account.
2. **Add your locations.** List where supplies are kept, such as the front desk, exam rooms, or storage.

**Supplies**
3. **Import your item catalog.** Upload a spreadsheet of your supplies. Do this before setting par levels so you can see every item.
4. **Set par levels.** For each item, enter the amount you want to keep on hand.

**Ordering**
5. **Add vendors.** Include each vendor's payment terms.
6. **Set purchase order approval rules.** Decide which orders need sign-off before they're sent.

**Team**
7. **Invite your team and assign roles.** Roles control what each person can see and do.

**Connections and routines**
8. **Connect your accounting software.**
9. **Schedule weekly cycle counts.** Counting a section of stock each week keeps your inventory numbers accurate.

When you're done, click **Finish**. For help at any time, visit the Help Center.

---

**Choices I made:**
- **Reordered the steps.** The original asked admins to set par levels for 200+ items before importing the catalog. Importing first means they set par levels on a complete list.
- **Grouped the steps** under short headings so the list is easier to scan.
- **Moved the feature list out.** Listing every feature (cost reports, audit logs, and so on) before the steps made the modal feel dense. Those features will show up as the admin uses them.
- **Added "finish now and return later."** This is my assumption that setup steps can be completed after the wizard closes. Please confirm that's true before shipping, or I'll remove the sentence.
