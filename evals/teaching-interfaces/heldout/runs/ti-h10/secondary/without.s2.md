**Welcome to Stackwell**

Stackwell helps your clinic order, receive, and track supplies. Let's set up the basics. You can do these in order, and you can return to any step later from Settings.

1. **Set your defaults.** Choose your currency and time zone.
2. **Add your locations.** For example: front desk, exam rooms, storage.
3. **Add your vendors.** Include each vendor's payment terms.
4. **Invite your team.** Give each person a role.
5. **Import your items.** Upload your catalog from a spreadsheet.
6. **Set par levels.** Start with your most-used items and add the rest later.
7. **Set purchase order approval rules.** Decide which orders need sign-off.
8. **Connect your accounting software.** *(Optional)*
9. **Schedule weekly cycle counts.**

When you're done, click **Finish**. Need help? Visit the Help Center.

**What changed:**

- **Shorter opening.** It says what the app is for in one sentence instead of listing twelve features.
- **Steps in dependency order.** Items come before par levels, and locations come before cycle counts, so each step builds on the last.
- **Action-first wording.** Each step starts with a verb, so admins know what to do at a glance.
- **Par levels framed as a starting point.** Telling admins they don't need all 200+ items done at once makes the step less daunting.
