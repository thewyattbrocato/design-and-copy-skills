**Welcome to Stackwell**

Stackwell keeps your clinic's supplies stocked without overordering. Setup has four parts:

1. **Set up your clinic.** Choose your default currency and time zone. Then add your locations, such as the front desk, exam rooms, and storage.
2. **Add your items.** Import your item catalog from a spreadsheet. Then set a par level for each item, which is the amount you want to keep on hand. Finally, schedule weekly cycle counts.
3. **Set up ordering.** Create a profile for each vendor with its payment terms. Then set approval rules for purchase orders.
4. **Add your team and connections.** Invite your team and assign their roles. Then connect your accounting software.

**What changed and why**

- **Grouped nine steps into four.** Each group answers one question: where we are, what we stock, how we order, and who is involved.
- **Moved the catalog import ahead of par levels.** You can't set par levels for 200+ items until they're in Stackwell, so the import comes first.
- **Cut the feature list.** A new admin doesn't need to know about all 14 features on day one. The app can introduce them as people reach them.
- **Put the action in each step.** Each step now says what to do, not only what it is.

**Assumptions to check**

- I assumed every step is needed before Finish. If some can wait, the copy should say so, and the modal can offer "Finish" alongside a way to return later.
- I assumed admins can return to setup later. If so, the copy should name where it lives, such as Settings.
- The button labels are my guesses. Match them to your existing components.
