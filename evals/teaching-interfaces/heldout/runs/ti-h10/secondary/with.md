**Welcome to Stackwell**

Stackwell helps your clinic keep supplies stocked, order from vendors, and track what you spend. You don't need to finish setup today.

**Start here (about 10 minutes)**
1. **Set your currency and time zone.** Used for orders, costs, and reports.
2. **Add your locations.** For example: front desk, exam rooms, storage.
3. **Import your item catalog** from a spreadsheet.

**Do these when you're ready**
4. **Set par levels** for your items, so Stackwell knows how much of each to keep on hand.
5. **Add vendors** and their payment terms.
6. **Set approval rules** for purchase orders.
7. **Invite your team** and assign roles.
8. **Connect your accounting software.**
9. **Schedule weekly stock counts.**

You can come back to these steps anytime. Questions? The Help Center has guides for each one.

**What changed and why**

- **Cut the feature list.** It named 14 features but told the admin nothing to do. Each step now says what it's for.
- **Split the steps into "start here" and "later."** Nine required steps before you can leave is a lot. The first three get a clinic running, and the rest can wait.
- **Moved catalog import ahead of par levels.** The original had par levels (step 3) before the catalog import (step 8), but you can't set par levels for items that haven't been loaded yet.
- **Replaced "Click Finish when done" with "Finish for now."** The original implied setup had to be complete before exiting, which is discouraging and probably not true.
- **Plain wording.** "Cycle counts" became "stock counts," and "par levels" gets a short explanation the first time it appears.

**Things to check against the product:** the wording assumes the checklist stays available after the modal closes, and that the steps can be done in any order after step 3. If either isn't true, the "Finish for now" option and the "come back anytime" line need to change.
