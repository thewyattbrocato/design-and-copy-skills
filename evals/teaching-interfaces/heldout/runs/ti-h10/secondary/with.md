**Setup checklist (stays visible after the modal closes)**

Steps appear here as the previous ones are done, so admins see only what's next:

- **Add your vendors.** Enter each vendor's payment terms.
- **Set par levels.** Choose how much of each item to keep on hand. Start with the items you use most.
- **Set purchase order approval rules.**
- **Invite your team and assign roles.**
- **Connect your accounting software.**
- **Schedule weekly cycle counts.**

Each step links to its Help Center article. When the list is empty, the checklist shows a short "You're set up" message.

**Notes**

- I reordered the steps by dependency. Par levels need the catalog, and vendors and approval rules come before purchase orders. The feature list is gone; the Help Center covers each area when someone needs it.
- I added a visible "Skip for now" and a checklist, since the original modal was shown once and then gone. I used "[Start setup]" and "Skip for now" as placeholders; check them against your actual labels and where the checklist lives in your menus.
