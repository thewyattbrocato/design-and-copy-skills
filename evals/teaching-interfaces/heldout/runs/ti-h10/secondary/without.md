Here's a rewrite. I reordered the steps so each one comes after whatever it depends on. For example, the catalog is imported before par levels are set, since you can't set par levels for 200+ items that aren't loaded yet.

---

**Welcome to Stackwell**

Stackwell helps your clinic track supplies, order from vendors, and receive deliveries. You don't need to set everything up now. Start with the first three steps and come back to the rest from Settings.

**Start here**
1. **Set your basics:** Choose your default currency and time zone.
2. **Import your items:** Upload your item catalog from a spreadsheet.
3. **Add your locations:** Where supplies are kept, such as the front desk, exam rooms, or storage.

**Then set up ordering**
4. **Add vendors:** Create a profile for each supplier, including payment terms.
5. **Set par levels:** Choose how many of each item to keep on hand at each location.
6. **Set approval rules:** Decide who needs to sign off on purchase orders, and when.

**Then set up your team and tools**
7. **Invite your team:** Add people and assign their roles.
8. **Connect accounting:** Link your accounting software to sync costs and orders.
9. **Schedule cycle counts:** Set up weekly counts to check stock against the system.

Click **Finish** when you're ready. You can change any of this later in Settings. For help at any point, visit the Help Center.

---

**What changed and why**
- **Grouped into three stages** ("Start here," "Then set up ordering," "Then set up your team and tools") so new admins see a short path first, not a nine-item list.
- **Reordered by dependency.** Catalog import now comes before par levels, and vendors come before approval rules.
- **Replaced the feature list with a one-line summary.** The old list of 14 features read like a catalog and didn't tell the admin what to do. The features are now described by the steps that use them.
- **Used plain verbs** ("Add," "Set," "Invite") and short descriptions, and dropped jargon like "reorder points," "lot tracking," and "audit logs" from the welcome. Those can appear later in Help or in the screens where they matter.

**One assumption to check:** I wrote "come back to the rest from Settings" as if every step can be skipped and done later. If Finish is blocked until certain steps are complete, let me know which ones and I'll adjust the copy.
