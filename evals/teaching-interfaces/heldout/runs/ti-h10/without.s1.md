I made three changes:

- **Cut the feature list.** The 12-item list of features means nothing to someone who hasn't used the app yet, and the steps already introduce what matters.
- **Reordered the steps.** The original has you define par levels for 200+ items before you've imported them. Importing the catalog has to come first.
- **Split setup into "now" and "later."** Nine steps at once is a lot to take in. Only the first four are needed to place an order, so the rest can wait.

I'd also recommend turning this from a one-time modal into a persistent checklist, such as a "Setup" card on the dashboard. Nobody remembers nine steps from a modal they saw once. If that's not possible, the copy below still works as the modal.

---

**Welcome to Stackwell**

Stackwell helps your clinic keep supplies stocked and orders organized. Setup takes about [X] minutes. Start with these four steps:

1. **Set your basics.** Choose your clinic's currency and time zone.
2. **Add your locations.** For example: front desk, exam rooms, storage.
3. **Import your items.** Upload your item catalog from a spreadsheet.
4. **Add your vendors.** Include payment terms for each one.

After that, you're ready to place your first order.

**When you're ready, you can also:**

- Set par levels, the stock amount you want on hand for each item
- Invite your team and assign roles
- Set approval rules for purchase orders
- Schedule weekly cycle counts
- Connect your accounting software

You can find these anytime under **Setup** on your dashboard. Questions? Visit the **Help Center**.

Notes on the details:

- **"Par levels"** is defined inline because new admins may not know the term. If your users are mostly clinic staff rather than inventory people, define it the first time it appears.
- **"Finish when done"** is gone. With a checklist there's no single "done," and people can leave and come back.
- **"[X] minutes"** needs a real number. If you don't know it, drop the sentence rather than guess.
- **Accounting is in the "later" group** on the assumption that it isn't required to place orders. If it is for your customers, move it up.
