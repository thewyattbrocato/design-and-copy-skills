How kiln booking works

The part that trips up most new members.

Firing a piece takes three steps:
  1. Dry your piece fully, then drop it at the kiln shelf with your name on the tag.
  2. Book a firing slot. Bisque firings run Mon and Wed, glaze firings run Fri.
  3. Pick up your piece within 48 hours of the firing date.

Questions? Message the front desk at hello@hollowoakpottery.example or stop by Tue to Sun, 9 am to 6 pm.
