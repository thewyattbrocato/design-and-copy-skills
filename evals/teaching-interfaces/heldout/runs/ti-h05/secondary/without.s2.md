Screen 2 was the only real fix. "Draw a circle" doesn't match a circle that's already placed, so the new copy tells people to move it.

**Screen 3: You're ready**
> Your first walk is on the calendar. Tap it anytime to change or cancel.

"Anytime" is one word, and "Tap it any time" is fine as is. Keep the buttons as they are.

Two things to check before you ship:

1. **Skip on Screen 1 could make Screen 3 untrue.** If someone skips the first walk, "Your first walk is on the calendar" is false. Either remove Skip from Screen 1, or change Screen 3 to something like "Your calendar is ready. Add walks any time."
2. **Skip on Screen 2 leaves the walking area unset.** Check what the app does with no area: whether it uses the default circle or offers no walks at all. If it offers no walks, the Skip link on this screen could cause the support tickets you're trying to avoid.
