What your starter does after you feed it

After reading this, you can look at your jar and tell whether it's time to mix your dough, too early, or too late.

Mix at the peak, or just before

The yeast is at its strongest and plentiful, so your dough rises reliably.

Too early

Barely above the line? The yeast hasn't built up, and dough will be slow and dense.

Too late

Sunk and sour? It's still alive. Feed it again and wait for the next peak before baking.

Try it: after feeding, put a rubber band at the starter's level and check the jar every hour or so. The time to peak depends on your kitchen's temperature and how much you feed, so note yours once and you'll know it next time.
