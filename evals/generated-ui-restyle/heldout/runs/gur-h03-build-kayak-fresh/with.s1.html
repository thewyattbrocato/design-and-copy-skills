Saltrow Sea kayak log & tide windows · iPhone & Android · Spring

Know when to go out, and when to be back.

Saltrow is a trip log for sea kayakers. Record each paddle, then save your launch spots and see their tide and current windows before you load the boat.

Made by two of us who paddle out of Gannet Sound. Coming to iPhone and Android this spring.

What it does

01

Log every trip

Launch spot, route, distance, conditions, and who you paddled with. A few taps at the beach or at home with a coffee. Over a season it becomes the record you wish you'd kept.

launch · route · distance · conditions · crew
02

See the window for your launches

Save the spots you actually paddle from. Saltrow shows the tide and current windows for each, so it's clear when going out is sensible and when you need to be off the water.

03

Charts without signal Pro

Download charts to your phone so they work where coverage doesn't, which on a lot of coastline is most of it.
