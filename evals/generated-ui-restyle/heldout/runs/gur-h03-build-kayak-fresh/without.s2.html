The window, drawn out

For each saved launch, Saltrow lays out the day's tide and current so the plan is simple: the easy stretch to paddle, and the time you're off the water.

Three things it does

It's a logbook with tide and current windows. It's not a social feed or a leaderboard.

  1. 01

    Log every trip

    Launch spot, route, distance, conditions, and who you paddled with. Do it on the beach or at the kitchen table. A season later you'll be able to answer "when did we last do the east shore in a southwesterly?"

  2. 02

    See the tide and current windows for your launches

    Save the spots you actually use. Saltrow shows each one's tide and current windows, so you can see when it makes sense to go out and when you need to be back.

  3. 03

    Take your charts offline Pro

    Water and signal don't always come together. Saltrow Pro keeps your charts on the phone for the places where coverage drops out.
