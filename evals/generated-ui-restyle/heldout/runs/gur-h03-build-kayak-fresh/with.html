Saltrow Made in Gannet Sound · iPhone & Android, spring

Log the trip. Know the tide before you launch.

Saltrow is a trip log for sea kayakers. Write down where you put in, the route, the distance, the conditions and who came along. Save your launch spots and it shows the tide and current windows for each one, so you can see when it makes sense to go out and when you need to be back.

Two of us built it, and we paddle out of Gannet Sound.

What it does

Three jobs, done properly

  1. Logs every trip

    Launch spot, route, distance, conditions and who you paddled with. Over a season it becomes your own record of what the water was actually like.

  2. Remembers your launch spots

    Save the places you put in. Each one keeps its own tide and current information, so you aren't looking it up from scratch each time.

  3. Shows the window

    For a saved spot you see when it's sensible to go out and when you need to be back on the beach.
