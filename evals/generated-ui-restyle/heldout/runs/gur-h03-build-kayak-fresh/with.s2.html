Saltrow
Built in Gannet Sound · iPhone & Android, spring

Log the paddle. Know the water before you go.

Saltrow is a trip log for sea kayakers. Record where you launched, the route, the distance, the conditions and who you went with. It also shows the tide and current windows for the launch spots you've saved, so you can see when it makes sense to go out and when you need to be back.

Two of us wrote it because we paddle out of Gannet Sound and wanted it for ourselves.

Just an email address. Nothing else is collected.

What it does

Three jobs, no more.

  1. 01

    Log every trip

    launch · route · distance · conditions · crew

    Write the day down while you still remember it: where you put in, the line you took, how far, what the wind and water did, and who was out with you. Your history builds up into something you can look back on.

  2. 02

    Save your launch spots

    ramps · beaches · slips

    Keep the places you actually put in. Each one carries its own tide and current times, so you aren't hunting through a table for the right station every time.

  3. 03

    See when to go and when to be back

    tide · current · windows

    For each saved spot, Saltrow shows the tide and current windows so you can tell when it's sensible to head out and when you need to be off the water. It's information to inform your call. The decision stays with you.
