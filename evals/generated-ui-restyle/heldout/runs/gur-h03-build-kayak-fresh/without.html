Know when to go out. Know when to be back.

Saltrow is a trip log for sea kayakers. Save your launch spots, see their tide and current windows, and keep a record of every paddle: where, how far, what it was like, and who came along.

Why it exists

The log in your head stops working after a few seasons.

We paddle out of Gannet Sound and kept running into the same two problems. Notes about a trip ended up scattered across texts and photos, and the tide and current checks were a separate chore before every launch.

Saltrow puts both in one place. It's small on purpose, and it's built by the two of us.

What it does

Three things.

  1. 01

    Log every trip

    Launch spot, route, distance, conditions, and who you paddled with. Free, and it will stay free.

  2. 02

    See tide and current windows

    Save the launches you use and see when the water is workable and when you need to be back on the beach.

  3. 03

    Take charts offline Pro

    Download charts for your areas so they work where signal doesn't, which is a lot of the coast.
