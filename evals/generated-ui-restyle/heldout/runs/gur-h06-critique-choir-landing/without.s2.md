## Two problems that matter more than the look

1. **"Trusted by 1,800+ choirs worldwide" next to a 38-school pilot.** If that number isn't literally true, remove it today. Directors talk to each other, and one caught inflated claim costs more than a plain page ever would. "Piloting in 38 schools" is smaller but believable.
2. **"AI-powered."** If nothing in the product is AI, cut it. Even if something is, many music educators are wary of AI right now. Lead with what the product does and mention AI later, if at all.
