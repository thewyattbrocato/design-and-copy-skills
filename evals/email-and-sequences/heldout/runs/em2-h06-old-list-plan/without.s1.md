Not quite. I'd change three things.

**1. Don't send to the 3,900 giveaway emails.**
- They gave their email to enter a contest in 2022, not to get marketing. Mailing them is a legal risk (CAN-SPAM requires honest handling, and GDPR/CASL are stricter if any are outside the US).
- It's also the biggest deliverability risk. After years of inactivity, many of those addresses will bounce or have become spam traps, and many people won't remember you and will hit "report spam."
- Those bounces and complaints can hurt inbox placement for your good 2,100 subscribers too. Since the giveaway contacts are 60% of the list, one bad blast could hurt your sending reputation for months.
- If you want to keep them, send a small re-permission email first, like "Still want hot sauce deals? Click to stay on." Only keep the people who click. Start with a few hundred and watch the bounce and complaint rates before sending more.

**2. Pull the 400 wholesale customers out.**
- A public 25% off everything may undercut the pricing you've given them, and they'll notice. Send them a separate message, or nothing at all.

**3. Don't test six subject lines on the whole list.**
- With about 2,100 real subscribers, six variants is roughly 350 people each. That's too few to tell a real winner from noise.
- Test 2 subject lines, or 3 at most. Send them to a random 20–30% of the list, then send the winner to everyone else a few hours later. Most email tools do this automatically.
- Pick the winner by clicks or revenue, not open rate. Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates opens.

**Smaller things to check:**
- Remove anyone who unsubscribed or bounced before. Your export may still include them.
- Put an expiry on the offer (for example, "ends Sunday midnight"). A deadline raises conversions.
- Check that 25% off everything still leaves you margin on your lowest-margin items. You could exclude bundles or gift sets.
- Saturday morning is fine, but you'll only learn if it's the best time by testing it.

**Revised plan:** Send to the roughly 2,100 newsletter subscribers, with a 2-way subject test on a sample and the winner going to the rest. Run the re-permission email to a small slice of the giveaway list separately. Handle wholesale on its own.
