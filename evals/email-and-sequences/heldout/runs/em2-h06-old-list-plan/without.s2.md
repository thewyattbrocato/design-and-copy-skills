Not as written. The plan has three problems, and the 2022 giveaway list is the biggest.

**1. The 3,900 giveaway emails are the real risk.**
- They're four years old, and those people entered to win something. They didn't ask for marketing unless your entry form said so. Under GDPR or CASL that's likely not valid consent. Under CAN-SPAM it's legal if you include an unsubscribe link and your address, but it's still a cold list.
- Old lists have high bounce rates, spam traps, and people who don't remember you. Spam complaints and bounces from 61% of your send can hurt deliverability for the 2,100 real subscribers too.
- **Change:** Leave them out of the sale blast. Send them a separate "still want to hear from us?" email, ideally with a small incentive, and only keep the people who click. If you must mail them, start with a few hundred, watch bounces and complaints, and clean the list first with a verification tool.

**2. Take the wholesale customers out.**
25% off everything sent to wholesale accounts can undercut their pricing or annoy retailers who are buying from you at margin. Send them something separate, or nothing.

**3. Six subject lines on the whole list won't tell you anything.**
- Even on the full 6,400, six variants is about 1,070 each. On your real 2,100 subscribers it's about 350 each. At typical open rates, the differences between variants would be noise.
- If you send to everyone at once, there's also no winner left to roll out.
- **Change:** Test 2 subject lines (3 at most) on about 20% of the newsletter segment. After a few hours, send the winner to the rest. Or just pick your best one and learn from the results over several sends.

**Smaller things to check:**
- Make sure 25% off everything still leaves you margin on your lowest-margin products and on shipping. Consider an expiry date or a code instead of a sitewide price cut.
- Confirm your sending domain has SPF, DKIM, and DMARC set up. Gmail and Yahoo enforce this for bulk senders.
- Saturday morning is fine, but compare it against one weekday send.

**Revised plan:** Send the sale to the ~2,100 newsletter subscribers with a 2-way subject line test. Run a re-permission email to the giveaway list separately. Handle wholesale on its own.
