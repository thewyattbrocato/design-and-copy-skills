Not as planned. I'd change four things.

**1. Drop the 2022 giveaway list (3,900 addresses) from this send.**
- They entered a contest. They didn't ask for marketing email, and they haven't heard from you in about four years.
- Expect high bounce and spam-complaint rates. Complaints above roughly 0.3% can hurt deliverability for everyone, including your real subscribers.
- Your email platform's terms may also prohibit this, and if any of them are in the EU, UK, or Canada, it can be a legal problem.
- If you want to try to keep them, send a separate "want to hear from us?" email to a small slice. Add only those who click, and let the rest go.

**2. Pull wholesale (400) out and message them separately.** A 25%-off-everything blast can undercut their pricing and annoy accounts you depend on. Send them their own offer, or nothing.

**3. Don't test six subject lines.**
- The newsletter list is about 2,100 people. Six variants leaves about 350 per group.
- At typical open rates, differences of a few points between groups would be noise, so you wouldn't learn anything.
- Testing on the whole list also means nobody gets the winning version.
- Use two subject lines, and think of it as a rough gut check, not proof. Or send the two to about 20% of the list, then send the better one to the remaining 80%.
- Change only the subject line. If you also change the send time or the offer, you won't know what caused the difference.

**4. Check the offer itself.** Make sure 25% off everything leaves you a margin on your lowest-margin products and on shipping. Consider a code with an end date, or excluding bundles and sale items.

**Revised plan:**
- Send to the roughly 2,100 newsletter subscribers, after removing past unsubscribes and bounces.
- Use a two-way subject line split, ideally with the winner going to the remainder.
- Send wholesale their own message.
- Handle the giveaway list as a separate re-permission experiment, or leave it out.

Saturday morning is fine, but you can't evaluate it from one send. If you want to learn about timing, test that in a later campaign.
