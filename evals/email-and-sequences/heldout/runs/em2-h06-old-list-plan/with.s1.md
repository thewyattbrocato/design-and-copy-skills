**Not as it stands. Two things would change, and the A/B test needs trimming.**

**1. Don't blast the 3,900 giveaway emails.**
- They entered a contest in 2022. They didn't ask for marketing, and it has been about four years.
- Cold addresses give you bounces, spam complaints and dead addresses. Those hurt deliverability for the whole list, including the 2,100 people who want your emails.
- Whether giveaway entry counts as consent depends on where your customers live, and the rules change. Check yours, or have your email platform's compliance docs confirm it.
- A safer route is a separate, one-time "Do you still want hot sauce news from us?" email to that group. It would have a single button to stay on the list, and anyone who doesn't click gets dropped. A sale offer could sweeten it, but the question should come first.
- At minimum, run the group through your platform's list-cleaning or verification step first. If you send to them anyway, do it after the main send, in small batches. Watch bounces and unsubscribes, and stop if they spike.

**2. Pull the 400 wholesale customers out.**
- A 25%-off-everything consumer sale could undercut their pricing or annoy them. If they get anything, it should be a separate message, or nothing.

**3. Cut the A/B test to two subject lines.**
- Six variants across 2,100 real subscribers is about 350 people each. Differences that small are mostly noise.
- Open rates are also unreliable now because of Apple's mail privacy features. Judge the winner on clicks and orders, not opens.
- A cleaner test is two subject lines, one difference between them (for example, "25% off all sauces through [end date]" versus a flavor-led line), split evenly across the 2,100. Or send to about 20% of them, wait a few hours, and send the better one to the rest.

**Smaller points**
- **Real end date:** put one in the email, for example "ends Sunday at midnight." Don't use vague urgency.
- **Margins:** make sure 25% off everything works on your lowest-margin items and shipping.
- **Timing:** Saturday morning is a reasonable guess. Check your past sends if you have them.
- **Send order:** newsletter subscribers first, then the re-permission email to the giveaway group.
