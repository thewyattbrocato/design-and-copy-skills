Not quite. I'd change three things.

**1. Don't send to all 6,400 at once.**
- **Giveaway entrants (3,900, about 61% of the list):** They entered a contest in 2022. They didn't necessarily ask for marketing, and they've been silent for years. A cold blast to that group tends to bring spam complaints, bounces and dead addresses. Those hurt delivery for everyone, including your 2,100 real subscribers.
- **What to do instead:** Send to the newsletter list first. Then send to the giveaway group only if the sign-up form said they'd get emails from you, and do it in small batches. Watch complaints and bounces between batches, and stop if they spike. If the form didn't say that, send them one plain "do you still want to hear from us?" email with a working unsubscribe link, and mail only the ones who say yes. Consent rules vary by place, so check yours.
- **Wholesale (400):** 25% off everything may undercut their pricing, and retail customers' discounts can annoy them. Give them a separate message, or leave them out.

**2. Test two subject lines, not six.**
- Even if you did mail everyone, six variants leave about 1,000 people each. With typical click and purchase rates, the differences between variants would be mostly noise.
- Don't judge on opens. Mail privacy features inflate them. Judge on clicks and orders.
- Pick two lines that differ in one way, such as "25% off all sauces" against a flavor- or product-led line. Split the newsletter segment 50/50. Send the winner to the next batch.
- Keep the preview text from repeating the subject. Use it for the deadline or the code.

**3. Make the offer honest and simple.**
- Give it a real end date, such as "ends Sunday midnight", and don't extend it. Skip "last chance" unless it's true.
- Use one button, labeled with what happens ("Shop 25% off"), and one job for the email.
- Put a working unsubscribe link in plain words at the bottom.

**What's fine:** A Saturday-morning send is reasonable, and a discount email works for a hot sauce shop. If you send the winning line to the rest on Sunday, make sure the deadline still gives them enough time.
