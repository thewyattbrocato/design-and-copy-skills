**From:** Dunmore Housing Co-op Board
**Subject:** Notice of change to 2027 monthly charges

Dear Members,

This notice is to inform you of a change to the monthly charge payable by each member of the Dunmore Housing Co-op.

**The change**

Effective January 1, 2027, the monthly charge will increase from $412 to $438. This is an increase of $26 per month.

**Reasons for the change**

The increase is the result of two factors:

1. The premium for the building's insurance has increased.
2. The Board is adding to the roof replacement reserve.

**Members' meeting**

The Board will hold a members' meeting to explain the change and to answer questions:

- Date: November 19
- Time: 7:00 pm
- Location: Community room

**Questions and further information**

Members who wish to ask a question in writing may contact the treasurer at treasurer@dunmorecoop.example. The full budget is available from the treasurer on request.

Sincerely,

Dunmore Housing Co-op Board
