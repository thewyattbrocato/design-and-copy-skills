**From:** Dunmore Housing Co-op Board
**To:** All Members, Dunmore Housing Co-op
**Subject:** Notice of change to 2027 monthly charges

Dear Members,

The Board of Dunmore Housing Co-op writes to give formal notice of a change to the monthly charge payable by members.

**Change to the monthly charge**

Effective January 1, 2027, the monthly charge will increase from $412 to $438.

**Reasons for the change**

The increase is the result of two factors:

1. The premium for the building insurance has increased.
2. The Board is making an additional contribution to the roof replacement reserve.

**Members' meeting**

A members' meeting will be held on November 19 at 7:00 pm in the community room. At this meeting, the Board will explain the change and answer members' questions.

**Questions and further information**

Members who wish to raise a question in writing may contact the treasurer at treasurer@dunmorecoop.example. The full budget is available from the treasurer on request.

Yours sincerely,

Dunmore Housing Co-op Board
