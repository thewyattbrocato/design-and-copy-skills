**From:** Dunmore Housing Co-op Board
**Subject:** Notice of change to 2027 monthly charges

Dear Members,

This notice informs you of a change to the monthly charge payable by each member of the Dunmore Housing Co-op.

**Change to the monthly charge**

Effective January 1, 2027, the monthly charge will increase from $412 to $438.

**Reasons for the change**

The board has approved this change for two reasons:

1. The premium for the building insurance has increased.
2. The board is adding to the roof replacement reserve.

**Members' meeting**

A members' meeting will be held on November 19 at 7:00 pm in the community room. At this meeting, the board will explain the change and take questions from members.

**Further information**

Members may write to the treasurer at treasurer@dunmorecoop.example with any questions about this change. The full budget is available from the treasurer on request.

Sincerely,

Dunmore Housing Co-op Board
