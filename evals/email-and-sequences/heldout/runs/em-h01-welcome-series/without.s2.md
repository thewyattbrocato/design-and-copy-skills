## Email 2: Send 2 days after sign-up

**Subject:** In defense of the humble test tile
**Preview:** A small pep talk before your first glaze shows up.

Hi [First name],

Have you cracked open the testing guide yet? If not, here's our pitch.

A glaze can look great in a photo and totally different on your pot, in your kiln, over your clay body. Test tiles let you find that out without sacrificing a bowl you spent three hours on.

That's why every box includes a test tile, and why we made the guide. Tiles are cheap, fast, and honest.

One easy thing to do before your box arrives: make a few tiles from the clay you use most, and bisque them. Then you can test the day your jars land instead of waiting on the kiln.

Guide, in case you need it again: [GUIDE LINK]

Nerdily yours,
Marit & Dov
