Quick nerd check before your first box.

A cone number is shorthand for how much heat the firing has done, so it matters more than the settings on the controller.

Every Kilnside glaze is cone 6, for mid-range electric kilns. If you fire cone 10 or raku, this club probably isn't for you yet, and that's genuinely okay.

If you're firing cone 6 electric, you're set. Each card lists the firing range, so you can check it against your kiln before you load anything.

Not sure what your kiln does? Reply with the make and model. Marit or Dov will tell you straight whether it's a match.

And if it isn't, you can cancel anytime from your account page. No hard feelings.
