Hi,

The quickest way to see if Parlay Hours fits your restaurant is to run one swap yourself.

1. Post a shift you can't work (or a made-up one).
2. Have a coworker claim it.
3. Approve it with one tap as the manager.

That's the whole loop. Staff post a shift they can't cover, coworkers claim it, and you approve it. Nobody has to chase anyone down.
