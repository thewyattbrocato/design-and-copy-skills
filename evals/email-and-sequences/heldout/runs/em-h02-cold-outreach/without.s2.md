## Tips to keep it from feeling like a blast

- **Personalize the first line.** With only 40 clinics, adding one real detail takes about 30 seconds each. Examples are the clinic's town, a service they offer, or a mention that they're a small independent practice. It's the biggest difference between a one-to-one email and a mass blast.
- **Send in small batches** of 5–10 a day from your own address, and don't use a mail-merge tool that shows "undisclosed recipients." Never CC or BCC the whole list.
- **Skip the "Dr." guess if you're unsure** of the owner's name or title. "Hi [First name]," or "Hello [Clinic Name] team," is better than getting it wrong.
- **Stop the sequence as soon as someone replies**, including a "no thanks." Honor any opt-out right away.
- **Legal basics:** commercial email to businesses still falls under CAN-SPAM. That means a real physical mailing address in the footer (included above), an honest subject line, and a way to opt out. The simplest way is to add a line like "If you'd rather I not email again, just reply and let me know." You can add that to Email 1 or to each email.

If you tell me a bit about what sets you apart, such as turnaround time, software you use (QuickBooks, etc.), or flat monthly pricing, I can work one line of that in. I'd keep it to one line so the email stays short.
