Here's a three-email sequence. Each one is short and plain, and none of them uses "just circling back" or fake urgency.

---

**Email 1: Cold intro**

**Subject:** Bookkeeping for [Clinic Name]

Hi Dr. [Last Name],

I'm a freelance bookkeeper in Tucson. For about six years I've done the books for small animal clinics around Arizona and New Mexico. I found [Clinic Name] on the state licensing board list.

I handle three things for clinic owners: monthly bookkeeping, payroll reconciliation, and sales tax filing. Most of the clinics I work with started out doing those themselves, or leaning on an office manager who already had a full plate.

If your books are already in good hands, ignore this. If they're a recurring headache, I'm happy to have a 15-minute call to see whether I could help. There's no pitch deck and no obligation.

Thanks for your time,
[Your name]
[Phone] | [Website, if any]
[Mailing address]

---

**Email 2: Follow-up, about 5 business days later**

**Subject:** Re: Bookkeeping for [Clinic Name]

Hi Dr. [Last Name],

I wanted to follow up on my note from last week, in case it got buried. Clinics often have their own quirks, like inventory, in-house pharmacy sales, and payroll for techs who work irregular shifts. I'm used to untangling those.

If it would help, I can look at one month of your books and tell you what I see, at no cost. If now isn't a good time, no worries.

[Your name]

---

**Email 3: Final follow-up, about 7–10 business days after #2**

**Subject:** Re: Bookkeeping for [Clinic Name]

Hi Dr. [Last Name],

This is my last note. I don't want to clutter your inbox.

If bookkeeping, payroll reconciliation, or sales tax filing ever becomes more than you want to handle, you can reach me at this address or at [phone]. I'd be glad to help.

Best of luck with the practice,
[Your name]

---

**Notes before you send:**

- **Personalize the first line.** If you can add one real detail per clinic, like their city, a specialty, or how long they've been open, the email won't read as a blast. With 40 clinics, that's worth doing.
- **Send in small batches** of 5–10 a day from your own address. That looks more natural, and it keeps your account from getting flagged for spam.
- **Check the free-review offer in #2.** I added it as an option, so cut it if you don't want to commit to it. If you keep it, be sure you'll do it.
- **Legal footer:** CAN-SPAM applies to B2B cold email too. You need a real mailing address (a PO box is fine) and an easy way to opt out. A line like "If you'd rather not hear from me, just reply 'no thanks' and I won't email again" works. Add it to email 1 and honor any opt-outs.
- **Sensitive data:** don't mention any client names or numbers unless you have permission.

I can also write a shorter version, or one with a more casual tone, if you'd like.
