**Cadence and stop rules**

- Send 5 to 8 a day rather than all 40 at once, so each one reads as individual.
- Stop a clinic's sequence on any reply (including "no" or "not now"), a bounce, an unsubscribe request, or after follow-up 2. Don't send a third follow-up.
- If you get an out-of-office reply, note the return date and send the next step then instead.

**Before you send**

- Fill in or delete the optional line in Email 1. Only include a detail you've actually verified.
- Follow-up 1 offers standalone sales tax and payroll work, so confirm you're happy to take those on alone. Also check local rules on commercial email; the postal address and opt-out line are there for that reason.
