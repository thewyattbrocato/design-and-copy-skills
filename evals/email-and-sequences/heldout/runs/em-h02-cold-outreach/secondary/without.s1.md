**Notes**

- **Personalize the first line.** A specific detail (a clinic review, a local news item, a service they list) makes a big difference. Even one sentence per clinic keeps it from reading like a blast.
- **Keep the opt-out line.** It sets a respectful tone and helps with CAN-SPAM compliance, which applies to commercial email even B2B.
- **Send from your own address, one at a time.** Avoid mail-merge tools with open-tracking pixels, since those can make a personal note feel automated.
- **Pace it.** Sending 40 emails over a few days, rather than all at once, looks more natural and is easier to manage replies.
- **Confirm the no-cost review offer is one you're willing to honor** before sending. Remove that line if you'd rather not offer it.
