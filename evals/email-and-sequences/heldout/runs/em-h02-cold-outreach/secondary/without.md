**Notes for sending**

- **Personalize the first line.** Add one specific detail per clinic, such as a service they list, a recent review, or a local event. That's what separates this from a blast, and it's worth the few minutes for 40 emails.
- **Keep the thread intact.** Send follow-ups as replies to the original message rather than new emails.
- **CAN-SPAM basics.** Include your real business name and a physical mailing address in every email, use an honest subject line, and stop emailing anyone who asks you to. Since you're sending one at a time from your own address, this is straightforward.
- **Pace it.** Sending 5 to 10 a day is more natural than all 40 at once, and it reduces the chance of spam filtering.
- **Check the list.** Licensing board addresses are sometimes shared or outdated, so a quick look at each clinic's website to confirm the owner's name and email is worth doing.
