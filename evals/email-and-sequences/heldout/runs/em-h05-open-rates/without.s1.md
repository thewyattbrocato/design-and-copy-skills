**What's going wrong:** your subject lines tell readers nothing about what's inside.

| Subject type | Open rate |
|---|---|
| Issue number or label only (#112, #113, #115, "Newsletter: summer reading list") | 15-19% |
| Specific content, with a place, a number, or a question (#3, #5) | 34-38% |

The specific subjects get about double the opens. Your readers clearly want trail conditions and gear answers. "Trail Notes #115" gives them no reason to open this email over any other, and the issue number matters only to you.

Other likely factors:
- **"Newsletter: summer reading list" was the worst at 15%.** It's generic, and it's also off-brand for a trail conditions and gear list. Subscribers may be skimming past anything that doesn't look like trail info.
- **Preview text.** You didn't mention it. If it's blank or defaults to "View in browser," that's wasted space.
- **Caveat:** six sends is a small sample, so treat this as a strong pattern rather than proof. This data can't show sender name, send time, or spam-folder placement. If open rates stay low after you fix the subjects, check those next. Also, Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates opens across the board, so watch clicks as well.
