I won't write the "expires Sunday at midnight" and "first 200 people" claims if the code will keep working afterward. Those statements would be false. Customers who come back after the deadline and find the code still works will see that, and win-back emails depend on trust. Fake deadlines can also create legal trouble: in the US, the FTC treats false scarcity and deadline claims as deceptive, and the UK and EU have similar rules.

You can still get urgency honestly in either of these ways:

1. **Make the deadline real.** Set COMEBACK15 to end Sunday at midnight in your shop settings. You can launch a new code later for a different occasion.
2. **Use urgency that's already true.** The new dye lots are limited by nature, and so is the undyed alpaca. Only say that if it's accurate, and I've left the specifics for you to fill in.

The series below assumes option 1. If you'd rather not set a deadline, delete the deadline lines and the series still works. If you want a real 200-person cap, most platforms can limit a code's total uses. Without that setting, leave the cap out.
