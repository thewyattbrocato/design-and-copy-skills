I've written the series, but I left out the "expires Sunday at midnight" and "first 200 people" lines. Neither is true, and if you keep the code live, customers who notice will trust the next email less. Hobby knitters talk to each other, and a code that still works on Monday is easy to spot. I've used two honest options instead:

- **Email 2 has no deadline.** The new yarn is the reason to write.
- **Email 3 can carry a real deadline.** If you want urgency, pick an actual end date, such as the end of the month, and let the code stop working then. A real deadline gets more action than a fake one.

**Email 1 — send day 0**
**Sender:** Fernwood Yarn Co.
**Subject:** New merino dye lots are in
**Preview:** Plus undyed alpaca, if you like to dye your own.

Hi [first name, or "there"],

It's been a while since your last Fernwood order, so here's what's new.

We just got in new dye lots of our merino. We also have a new line of undyed alpaca, for knitters who like a natural palette or want to dye their own.

[See what's new]

Happy knitting,
Fernwood Yarn Co.

[Unsubscribe]

**Email 3 — send day 12**
**Sender:** Fernwood Yarn Co.
**Subject:** Still want Fernwood emails?
**Preview:** Your 15% code works through [real end date].

Hi [first name, or "there"],

This is our last note for now. Your 15% off code, **COMEBACK15**, works through [real end date, or delete this sentence if there isn't one].

[Shop with COMEBACK15]

If you'd rather not hear from us, no hard feelings. [Unsubscribe] takes one click. If you'd like to stay, you don't need to do anything.

Fernwood Yarn Co.

[Unsubscribe]

---

**Stop rule:** Anyone who buys or unsubscribes drops out of the series at once. After email 3, stop mailing anyone who hasn't opened or clicked. Don't keep emailing a customer who has said no by ignoring you.

**Notes:**
- If you set a real end date, change the code in your shop so it actually stops working then. Otherwise the deadline is false again.
- Check the sender name and unsubscribe handling against the email rules that apply where you and your customers are. I haven't covered those.
