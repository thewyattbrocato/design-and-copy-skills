I won't write the "expires Sunday" and "first 200 people" claims, because they'd be false and your customers would be making decisions based on them. It also tends to backfire with a small shop's audience. Hobby knitters talk to each other, and a code that's still live the next week teaches people to ignore your deadlines.

You can still get real urgency, and it's easy:

- **Make the deadline true.** Set COMEBACK15 to actually end Sunday at midnight in your shop platform. You can bring it back later as a different code if you want.
- **Use the real scarcity.** New dye lots are limited by nature, and undyed alpaca may be too. If that's true, say so.
