Some ways to make it stronger without making claims you can't back up:

- **Offer a free sample audit.** "Send 2–3 months of invoices and we'll show you what we found" is a concrete offer, and it gives you real results to cite later.
- **Name your experience.** Add which carriers or modes you handled, such as LTL, parcel, or FTL, and how many years. Specifics build credibility.
- **Use real numbers once you have them.** After your first couple of audits, you can quote actual recovery figures and, with permission, name the client.
