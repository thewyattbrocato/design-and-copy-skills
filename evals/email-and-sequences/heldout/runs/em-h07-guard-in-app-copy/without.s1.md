**Banner (≈2 lines)**

> **Carrier updates are running behind.** Tracking info may be out of date by up to 30 minutes. We'll update it as soon as the carrier catches up.

Shorter alternative:

> **Tracking may be out of date.** This carrier's updates are delayed by up to 30 minutes.

**Toast (1 line)**

> Couldn't refresh. Check your connection and try again.

Shorter alternative if it wraps on small phones:

> Refresh failed. Please try again.

Notes:
- The banner says what's happening and what it means for the user. It doesn't blame anything or make promises about timing beyond the 30-minute figure.
- The toast covers both causes (no network or no server response) with "check your connection," and tells the user the fix is to retry.
- The first banner's last sentence assumes the app refreshes on its own once the carrier catches up. If it doesn't, drop that sentence or change it to "Pull to refresh later."
