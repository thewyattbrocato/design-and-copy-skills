**1) Banner (carrier feed delayed, about two lines)**

> Tracking updates from this carrier are delayed by up to 30 minutes. Some info on this screen may be out of date.

**2) Toast (refresh failed, one line)**

> Couldn't update tracking. Try again.

The banner names the cause and the 30-minute window, and says what the user should expect. The toast says what happened and what to do, and it stays short enough for one line. It doesn't say "no network" or "server error" because the user can take the same action either way.
