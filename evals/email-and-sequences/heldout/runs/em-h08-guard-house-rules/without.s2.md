Subject: Dispatch 31 — notes from the bench

Hello all,

The walnut stool is finished. It's sitting by the door right now, waiting to go to my sister's house, which is where it was always headed.

I'll be honest about how the glue-up went, because it didn't go well. I rushed the clamps. I had the legs and stretchers dry-fitted and everything felt fine, so I got impatient once the glue was spread, and I didn't take the time to set the clamps evenly or check that each joint was seated before the open time ran out. When I came back and loosened things up, one joint on a leg had opened up. It wasn't a huge gap, but it was enough that I could see daylight and feel it with a fingernail. I wasn't going to hand that to my sister.

The fix was a spline. I cut a thin kerf across the joint, glued in a sliver of walnut with the grain running the right way, and trimmed it flush once it cured. It's now a bit of structure I chose to put there, and it holds better than the original joint would have. Matching the grain took a few tries, but from a few feet away you have to know where to look.

The lesson is an old one: the glue-up is the one step you can't slow down afterward, so slow down before it. Clamps out, cauls ready, rehearse the sequence.

Next issue I start on a workbench vise. I've been planning it for a while and I'm looking forward to it.
