**Subject:** Dispatch 31 — notes from the bench

This week the walnut stool is finished, and it almost went another way.

I rushed the glue-up. I didn't give the clamps the time they needed, and when they came off, a joint had opened up on one of the legs. That was the mistake that could have cost me the whole piece, so I stopped and dealt with it properly instead of hoping it would hold.

The fix was a spline. I cut a slot across the joint, fit a thin strip of wood into it, glued it in, and trimmed it flush. It's a repair I'd rather not have needed, but it's sound, and the stool is stronger for it. If you look closely you can find the line. I'd rather you know that than not.

The lesson is simple, and I keep relearning it: the clamps set the pace, not me.

The stool is going to my sister's house. It's the first piece in a while that has left the shop for someone else's home, so the mistake and the fix both come with it. She's getting the repaired version, and I'm fine with that.
