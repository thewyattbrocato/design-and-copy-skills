Subject: Dispatch 31 — notes from the bench

Hello friends,

The walnut stool is finished, and it's headed to my sister's house this week. It did not go the way I planned.

I rushed the glue-up. The clamps went on fast, I felt good about the alignment, and I didn't give the joints the time and pressure they needed. When I came back to it, one leg had a joint that had opened up. A gap that's opened under glue can't be hidden, so I didn't try.

The fix was a spline. I cut a slot across the joint, glued in a thin strip of matching wood, and trimmed it flush once everything cured. You can see the repair if you know where to look, and I'm fine with that. The stool is solid now, and a visible repair is more honest than one I pretend never happened.

The lesson for me: set the clamps and check the joint before the glue starts to grab, not after. Slow down at glue-up. The wood doesn't care about your schedule.
