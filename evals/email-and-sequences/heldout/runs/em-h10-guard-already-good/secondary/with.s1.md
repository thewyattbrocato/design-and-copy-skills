Two real problems, one ambiguity:

1. **Typo:** "recieve" should be "receive."
2. **Unanchored refund window:** "email us within 14 days" doesn't say 14 days from what. Members could read it as 14 days from the renewal charge, from March 3, or from the cancellation deadline. Add the anchor, for example: "within 14 days of the March 3 charge."

Smaller point:

3. **Year missing:** "March 3" with no year is fine if it goes out shortly before renewal, but if it can be sent months early, members may not know which March 3. Consider "March 3, 2027" if your template can fill it in.

Everything else reads correctly. The "cancel before March 3" and "charged on March 3" timing is consistent, the amount and card reference match, and the contact line and sign-off are fine. The `{first_name}` merge field needs a fallback for members without a first name, but that's a template setting, not a copy problem.
