1. **"Before 6 today"**: Check that "today" will still be true when Priya reads it. If the email might sit unread until tomorrow, use the actual day and date (e.g., "before 6 today, Wednesday") so it can't be misread.

2. **Pickup hours**: "Tomorrow from 10 to 6" is fine, but add "pm"/"am" or say "10am to 6pm" if you want zero ambiguity.

3. **Total**: "$142, same as the quote" is good. If the quote was a range or included tax differently, it's worth a quick check that $142 is the final amount with tax.

4. **Work description**: "Trued the back wheel" and "adjusted the gears" are accurate shop terms, and most customers will understand them. If you think Priya might not, "straightened the back wheel" is plainer.

5. **Optional additions**: A phone number for questions, and your shop's hours beyond the next day, in case she can't make either window. Also, "bring it back and we'll sort it out" is friendly but vague. If you have a set window, like 30 days, it helps to say so.
