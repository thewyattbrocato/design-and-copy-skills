This is ready to send. It's clear, it leads with the point, and the details a customer needs are all there: what was done, the price, pickup times, address and payment. The subject is short and says what's inside. I wouldn't rewrite anything.

Two things to check before you send:
- **"Today" and "tomorrow":** these only work if she reads the email on the day you send it. If it might sit unread overnight, use the actual day names, like "Thursday before 6, or Friday from 10 to 6".
- **Hours:** confirm that 6 today and 10 to 6 tomorrow are right. They're the only details a customer could act on wrongly.
