This is ready to send. The subject says what's inside, the work and price are clear, and the pickup details (hours, address, how to find the door, payment) are all there. There's one ask, and the last line about bringing it back is a good touch. I wouldn't change any wording.

Two things to check before you send:
- **"Today" and "tomorrow":** these only work if the email goes out before 6 today. If it might sit in a queue or be read tomorrow, swap in the actual day, for example "Thursday."
- **Tomorrow's hours:** confirm the shop really is open 10 to 6 tomorrow.
