# Layout for the Harbourview Physio before/after

Don't put the two full-page screenshots side by side. At 720px each would be about 350px wide, and the text would be unreadable. Crop instead, and stack the two versions at the same width.

## Arrangement

1. **One line of problem first.** Quote the brief or the issue, for example "Booking took six pages, with promos beside every form." Put the comparison directly under it.

2. **Before: a strip, then one enlarged step.**
   - **Strip:** the six steps as equal-size crops of each page's first screen, in a row. At 720px that's about 110px each, numbered 1–6. The strip shows the length of the flow, which a single tall image can't.
   - **Enlarged step:** one step, ideally the one with the promo sidebar, at the full 720px. This is where you annotate.

3. **After: one crop at the same 720px width.** Use the same crop height as the enlarged step. It shows the date strip, the time list and the start of the details form. If the form runs long, crop where it ends rather than showing the footer.

4. **Label each side.** Use plain "Before" and "After" tags in the same position on both figures, in the same style.

5. **On mobile,** the strip wraps to 3×2 or scrolls sideways. The two main figures stay stacked.

6. **Side by side is an option only if you break out clearly wider,** around 1000px or more, with two equal columns. A slightly wider figure looks like a mistake. Stacked within 720px is the simpler choice.

7. **Reserve each figure's box size in advance** so the page doesn't jump as images load. Leave more space below each caption than above the figure.

## What to annotate

Use few pins with a few words each, about three or four on the old version and three on the new one.

- **Before, on the strip:** the step count ("6 pages"). It's a fact, not a result.
- **Before, on the enlarged step:** pin the faults where they occur.
  - "Promo sidebar competes with the form" on the sidebar.
  - "Only one choice on this page" on the sparse content.
  - A pin on the step where date and time are chosen, if they're split across pages.
- **After:** label the three regions: "Date strip", "Times for the chosen day" and "Details form, same page". Where it's true, add a small "was steps [x–y]" to each. Matching colors or numbers on a few strip steps and the new regions makes the mapping visible without arrows.
- **Captions:** one present-tense sentence each, saying what the viewer can't see from the image. Example: "Choosing a date updates the time list in place; no page load." Don't repeat the paragraph above.

## What to leave out

- The full-height screenshots, and all six steps at full size.
- Browser chrome, device mockups and drop shadows. The two figures should share one frame style.
- Annotations on every element, and arrows pointing at everything.
- Any claim of improvement: faster, easier, fewer drop-offs, more bookings. You have no data, and the layout shouldn't imply any. Say what changed ("six pages became one") and let the reader judge.
- If you later get a result, add it as a separate line, next to this comparison. Until then you can leave `[result: how it was measured, and the number]` in your draft as a reminder.
