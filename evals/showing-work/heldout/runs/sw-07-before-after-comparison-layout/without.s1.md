# Layout for the Harbourview Physio comparison

Full-page screenshots side by side at 360px each would be unreadable, so don't do that. Use two stacked views instead: one that shows the structure, and one that shows the detail.

## 1. Structure view (shows what changed)

- **Before:** a single horizontal strip of the six step screens as thumbnails, about 110px wide each, in order. Crop each to its first viewport-height (roughly the top 700-800px) instead of the full tall capture. Number them 1-6 with a thin connector between them.
- **After:** directly beneath, the single page at the same thumbnail scale, or a little larger (up to about 360px wide, left-aligned). Keep the scale consistent with the strip above so the reader sees "six screens, then one" without reading anything.
- **Caption:** state it plainly, for example "Six pages, each with a promo sidebar → one page." This is a description of the design, not a result.

## 2. Detail view (shows how it changed)

Below that, one side-by-side pair at about 350px each, with a small gutter. Both are cropped to the same viewport height:

- **Left:** a representative wizard step, with the sidebar visible.
- **Right:** the new page, top of the fold.

Use the same crop height and the same browser width on both so the comparison is fair.

## What to annotate

Keep it to 4-6 numbered callouts total, with the legend in text beneath the images instead of long labels on top of them.

- **On the strip:** which old steps became which new section. For example, steps 1-3 map to the date strip and time list, and steps 4-6 map to the details form. Fill this in from your real step contents. Also note any step that was dropped or merged.
- **On the old step:** the promo sidebar, marked "removed."
- **On the new page:** the date strip, the time list, and the short details form.
- **Fields:** if the form asks for fewer fields than the old wizard did, say so with the count. That's a factual design change.

Use one accent colour for callouts, and the same one on both sides.

## What to leave out

- **Full-length tall screenshots**, stitched or scrolling. If you want to offer them, put them in a linked appendix or expandable image.
- **Readable promo content.** Dim or blur the sidebar and label it. Its text isn't the point.
- **Browser chrome, identical headers and footers**, and anything else that's the same on both sides.
- **Real patient or booking details.** Use dummy data in the form and time list.
- **Improvement claims.** Skip "faster," "easier," "higher conversion," and any invented numbers. Write captions as "what changed" (six steps to one, sidebar removed, fewer fields), and say what you intended. For example, "The goal was to reduce steps and distractions" is fine, but "reduced drop-off" is not. If you later get data, add it as a separate labelled section.
- **Arrows and callouts on every element.** More than about six and the reader stops looking at the screens.

If your case study covers mobile, treat it as a separate pair and don't squeeze it into this one.
