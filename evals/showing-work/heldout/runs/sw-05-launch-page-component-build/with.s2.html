Why it replaces the dropdown

The Dropdown Time Select showed one time at a time, behind a click. The Time Slot Picker puts every available time on the page, grouped by day, so patients can compare options without opening anything.

We haven't measured adoption or scheduling outcomes for v2.1 yet, so this page makes no performance claims. [result: how it will be measured, and the number]

Arrow keys move through the whole grid

The grid is one tab stop. Arrow keys move focus between slots, and continue across day boundaries, so keyboard users never tab through dozens of buttons.

Selection is announced, not just highlighted

The grid is a radio group, and each day is a labelled group inside it. Choosing a slot updates a polite live region with the full day and time, so the result doesn't rely on color.

An empty day points to the next opening

When nothing is available, the picker says so and offers a single action: jump to the next date that has openings. Use the “No times available” scenario above to see it.

Two densities for two amounts of room

Comfortable is the default, with larger touch targets. Compact fits more times in tighter layouts such as side panels. Toggle the density above to compare them.
