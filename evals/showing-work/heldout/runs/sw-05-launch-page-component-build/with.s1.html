Time Slot Picker

Stable · v2.1

A grid of selectable appointment times grouped by day, for the patient scheduling app. It replaces the Dropdown Time Select.

All the times are visible at once

The Dropdown Time Select hid every option behind a closed control. The Time Slot Picker lays the day's times out as a grid, grouped under each day, so a patient can scan for a time instead of opening a list to look.

Arrow keys move through the grid, Tab moves past it

The grid is one tab stop. Once focus is inside, arrow keys move between times, including across day groups, so a keyboard user never has to Tab through every slot.

Selection is announced, not only highlighted

A filled button is invisible to a screen reader user. Each selection also writes a full sentence, with weekday, date and time, to a polite live region, and each time button reports its pressed state.

No times? Say so and offer the next date that has some

When nothing is open in the range, the picker does not render an empty grid. It states that nothing is available and gives one button that jumps to the next date with openings.

Two densities for two amounts of room

Comfortable gives each time a 44px-tall target and is the default. Compact drops to 32px with smaller type and tighter spacing for constrained panels. Behaviour, keys and announcements are identical.

Status and open items

Stable at v2.1, replacing the Dropdown Time Select. We have not measured adoption, completion rates or user feedback yet, so this page does not report any. Times in the demos are sample data generated in the page, not real availability.
