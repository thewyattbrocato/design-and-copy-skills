## Results

### What I tested
I ran informal sessions with five people. Each was asked to plan a full week of meals in the prototype, with no walkthrough beforehand.

- **3 of 5** completed the task on their own.
- **2 of 5** needed a hint to get through it.

This was a small, informal sample, so I treat it as a directional signal and not as proof. Even so, a majority finishing the core task unaided, in a prototype that was never polished or iterated after testing, suggested the main flow was on the right track.
