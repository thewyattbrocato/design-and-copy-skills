Your plan is mostly fine. The task wording is good because it doesn't use the words "pause" or "deliveries", so it doesn't hand people the label. Here's what I'd tighten.

**Fix before this afternoon**
- **Use a test account on a clean state.** Give them an active subscription with an upcoming box. Start every person on the home screen, and reset the pause switch between sessions. Otherwise the second and third people see the first person's changes.
- **Decide what counts as success now.** I'd use three levels: found the switch, turned it on correctly (right dates), and confirmed it worked. Also note whether they took a wrong turn like cancel subscription or skip a single box, because that's useful in itself.
- **Set a give-up point.** Pick about 3 to 4 minutes. If they're lost by then, stop and record "didn't find it". Don't hint, even when the silence is uncomfortable.
- **Ask them to think aloud** ("say what you're looking for as you go"). Your notes on where they hesitate will be much richer, and you can stay quiet while they talk.
- **Add one question at the end:** "Tell me about the last time you went away and something was delivered or scheduled." Don't ask if they like the feature.

**Limits to be aware of**
- **You're the designer, so you're the weakest facilitator.** People tend to perform for you or look to you for cues. Staying silent helps. Keep your face neutral too, and if you can, have someone else read the task out.
- **Support staff aren't typical customers.** They know the app and meal-box vocabulary far better than a new customer does. If they find it easily, that's weak evidence. If they struggle, that's strong evidence. Ask each one whether they've used a pause or skip feature before, and note it.
- **With three people, count "2 of 3", not percentages.** If two or more stall in the same place, fix that spot. If all three find it easily, you've only learned it's not obviously broken. Don't treat it as proof.

**Housekeeping**
- Tell each person you're testing the app, not them, and ask before taking notes or recording.
- Write your notes on what they did (tapped Account, then Subscription, hesitated about 20 seconds). Keep your interpretation of why in a separate column.

You'll need to recruit the three people and run the sessions yourself.
