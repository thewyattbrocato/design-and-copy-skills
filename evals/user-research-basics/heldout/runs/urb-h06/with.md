**Summary of 6 interviews with home cooks about Pantrylog (Oct 6-9)**
Participants were recruited from our email list, so all of them already use the app.

**What we saw**
- 4 of 6 kept a separate phone note for what to buy, even though the app has a shopping list. 2 of those 4 didn't know the list could be shared.
- 2 of 6 scanned barcodes when adding items. The other 4 typed names by hand.
- 1 of 6 mentioned expiry reminders unprompted. We didn't ask about them, so we can't say how many others care.

**What this suggests**
The shared shopping list may be hard to discover. This is a lead, not a conclusion. Six existing users is a small, friendly sample.

**Proposed next step**
Watch 3 new users try to set up a shared list without help, and note where they stall.

**Not covered**
People who tried Pantrylog and left.

---

What I changed:
- **Numerals:** I used "2 of those 4" so the numbers match the rest of the list.
- **Hedging:** I changed "probably hard to discover" to "may be hard to discover". Only 2 of 6 said they didn't know about sharing, so "may" fits the evidence better.
- **Wording:** I tightened a few phrases, such as "see where they stall" to "note where they stall".
- **Layout:** I made "Not covered" a heading so it doesn't get lost at the bottom.
