## Screener

**Intro (shown at top)**
> We're a small team building software to help rural clinic front desks manage walk-in queues and missed appointments. We're looking for front desk staff to share their experience in a 30-minute video call. You'll receive [$X incentive]. This is research only, not a sales call. Answers to these questions take about 3 minutes.

**Questions**

1. **Which best describes your current role?**
   - Front desk / reception / patient registration at a clinic *(continue)*
   - Office or practice manager who also covers the front desk *(continue)*
   - Clinical staff (nurse, provider, MA) *(screen out; optional waitlist)*
   - Other / not currently working in a clinic *(screen out)*

2. **How long have you worked at a clinic front desk?**
   - Under 6 months *(screen out)*
   - 6–12 months
   - 1–3 years
   - 3+ years
   *(Aim for a mix. Cap 3+ years at about 3 people.)*

3. **How would you describe the area where your clinic is located?**
   - Rural, with a small town or open country, and the nearest city of 50,000+ is 30+ minutes away *(continue)*
   - Small town, but near a metro area *(screen out, or use as a backup)*
   - Suburban or urban *(screen out)*

   Also ask for the **state and zip code**, to verify rurality and spread the sample geographically.

4. **What type of clinic?** (primary care, rural health clinic/FQHC, urgent care, dental, behavioral health, specialty, other.)
   *(Prioritize primary care and RHC/FQHC. Allow 1–2 others for contrast.)*

5. **How many providers see patients at your clinic?**
   - 1–2 / 3–5 / 6–10 / 11+
   *(Prefer 1–10. Screen out 11+.)*

6. **How does your clinic handle walk-ins today?**
   - We don't accept walk-ins *(screen out)*
   - Walk-ins are fit in around scheduled appointments *(continue)*
   - We have dedicated walk-in hours *(continue)*
   - Walk-in only *(continue)*

7. **In a typical week, roughly how many scheduled patients don't show up?**
   - Rarely or never *(screen out)*
   - A few (1–5)
   - Several (6–15)
   - Many (16+)

8. **Which tools do you use to manage the schedule and the waiting room?** *(select all)*
   - EHR/practice management system (please name it: ___)
   - Paper log or sign-in sheet
   - Whiteboard or spreadsheet
   - Text or reminder service (please name it: ___)
   - Other: ___

9. **In 2–3 sentences, describe the last time the waiting room or schedule got out of hand at your front desk. What happened?**
   *(Open-ended. This is your quality filter: look for specific, concrete answers. Reject one-word or generic replies.)*

10. **Are you comfortable doing a 30-minute video call on Zoom or Google Meet, with a stable internet connection?** *(Yes required.)*
    - Also ask for **availability windows** (ideally a few weekday slots, since front desk staff may have limited time) and **time zone**.

11. **Do you have permission to talk about your workflows?** *(Yes/No/Not sure)*
    Say: "We won't ask for any patient information. Please don't share names or details about patients." Some employers require approval, so flag any "Not sure" for follow-up.

12. **Contact info:** name, email, phone (optional), clinic type/state (if not already gathered).

**Disqualify** anyone who works for a competing vendor (for example, a scheduling or EHR company) or who works in market research or UX.

**Target mix for 6 (recruit about 9–10 to allow for no-shows):**
- At least 4 different states or regions
- At least 4 primary care or RHC/FQHC, and no more than 2 others
- At least 2 with a paper or whiteboard process, and at least 2 with an EHR
- A mix of 1–2 provider clinics and 3–10 provider clinics
