# Screener: front desk interviews (30 min, remote)

**Target:** 6 participants. Targets, not guarantees: at least 2 from clinics with 1–2 front desk staff, at least 2 from clinics with 3 or more, and a mix of clinic types.

## Invitation text (the version people see)

> We're talking with people who work at medical offices about how they handle their day. It's a 30-minute video or phone call, and you'll get **[incentive]**. First, a few quick questions to see if it's a fit (about 3 minutes).

## Questions

**1. Which of these have you done as part of your job in the past month? (Select all that apply.)**
- Checked patients in at a front desk or reception area
- Answered phones for a business
- Handled billing or insurance claims
- Scheduled or rescheduled appointments
- Ordered supplies or managed inventory
- Managed staff schedules
- None of these
*Continue only if "Checked patients in" and "Scheduled or rescheduled appointments" are both selected.*

**2. Where do you do most of this work?**
- Medical or dental clinic
- Hospital
- Pharmacy
- Veterinary office
- Other office
- I don't do this work
*Continue only if "Medical or dental clinic." Hospital and other answers are excluded.*

**3. How would you describe the area around your clinic?**
- Large city
- Suburb
- Small city or town
- Rural area, with the nearest large town more than 30 minutes away
- Not sure
*Target: "Small city or town" or "Rural." Ask the participant to name the town or county, and the recruiter checks it against a rural definition such as the USDA RUCA codes. Don't rely on self-description alone.*

**4. In a typical week, which of these happen at your front desk? (Select all that apply.)**
- Patients arriving with appointments
- Patients arriving without appointments (walk-ins)
- Patients who miss or cancel appointments
- Patients calling to reschedule
- Referrals from other offices
- Patients paying at the desk
*Continue only if both "walk-ins" and "miss or cancel" are selected.*

**5. In the past month, how many days a week did you work the front desk?**
- Fewer than 2 / 2–3 / 4–5
*Continue if 2 or more.*

**6. Do you, or does anyone in your household, work in any of these?** (Select all that apply.)
- Software or app design
- User research or market research
- Healthcare software or scheduling products
- Marketing or advertising
- None of these
*Exclude anyone who selects one of the first four.*

**7. Have you taken part in a paid research study or interview in the past 3 months?**
- Yes / No
*Exclude "Yes."*

**8. (Open text, 2–3 sentences.) Think about the last time your waiting room was busy. What was going on, and what did you do?**
*The recruiter reads this for specifics: a real day, real decisions, their own words. Decline vague, generic or copy-pasted answers. Anyone who guessed their way through the earlier questions usually can't answer this one.*

**9. Logistics**
- Can you do a 30-minute video or phone call on a weekday between **[hours]**? Which days work?
- Do you have a private spot to talk, away from patients?
- Name, email or phone, clinic name and town.

## Recruiter notes

- Don't name the product or the sponsor to candidates at any stage.
- Anyone screened out who completed the form still gets a thank-you. Anyone who does a session and turns out not to fit still gets the incentive, and you set their data aside.
- **Sample bias to state in your findings:** this sample leaves out staff who can't take a call during work hours, clinics with no front desk, and clinics too busy to respond to outreach. Those are likely the ones who most need this tool.
- In the session intro, ask the participant not to share patient names or details. Ask for consent before recording.
