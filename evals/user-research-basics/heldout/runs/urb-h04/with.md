# Screener: front desk staff at rural clinics

**Study description shown to candidates (the product isn't named):**
"We're talking with people who work at medical clinic front desks about how their days run. It's a 30-minute video call, and you'll receive [incentive: to decide]. This short form takes about 3 minutes."

**Q1. Which of these best describes your main job at the clinic?** (single choice)
- Front desk / reception / patient check-in *(continue)*
- Office or practice manager *(continue only if Q3 shows they do desk tasks themselves)*
- Nurse or medical assistant *(end)*
- Provider (physician, NP, PA) *(end)*
- Billing or coding only *(end)*
- Other: ___ *(end)*

**Q2. Where is your clinic located?** (single choice)
- Rural area or small town *(continue)*
- Small city *(continue only if the clinic serves a rural area, which you check on the call)*
- Suburb or large city *(end)*

**Q3. Which of these have you personally done at work in the last two weeks?** (select all)
- Checked in patients who walked in without an appointment
- Scheduled or rescheduled appointments
- Called or texted patients about appointments
- Verified insurance
- Answered the main phone line
- Collected payments or copays
- Ordered office supplies
- Scanned or filed records
- None of these *(end)*

*Qualify if they select **both** "checked in walk-ins" and "scheduled or rescheduled appointments".*

**Q4. When did a patient last miss an appointment without calling?** (single choice)
- This week / within the last month *(continue)*
- 1–3 months ago, or can't recall *(end)*
- Never happens at my clinic *(end)*

**Q5. How does your desk currently keep track of who is waiting and who is scheduled?** (select all; used for quotas, not to disqualify)
- Paper log or sign-in sheet
- Whiteboard or sticky notes
- Spreadsheet
- Scheduling module in our electronic health record
- Separate scheduling or waitlist software
- Other: ___

**Q6. Open response:** "Tell me about the last time a patient didn't show up, or the last time the waiting room got backed up. What happened, and what did you do?" (2–4 sentences)
*Reject blank, one-word, generic or copy-pasted answers. Keep people who describe a specific event with their own actions.*

**Q7. Do any of these apply to you?** (select all)
- I or a close family member works for a healthcare software company, or in UX, design or market research *(end)*
- I took part in a paid research study in the last 3 months *(end)*
- None of these *(continue)*

**Q8. Logistics:**
- Do you have a computer or tablet with a camera and a quiet place to talk for 30 minutes? (Y/N, must be Y)
- Which time windows work between [dates to set]? ___
- Would you need your manager's OK to take part during work hours? (Y/N; flag for follow-up)
- Name, email and best phone number: ___

**Quotas (targets, not guarantees):** 6 total. Aim for a spread across current tools (Q5, at least one paper or whiteboard desk and one EHR-based desk) and clinic size. Aim for no more than 2 office managers.

**Who this leaves out:** staff at urban and suburban clinics, and clinics with no front desk. Because the sample is volunteers who respond to outreach, it likely leans toward people comfortable on video calls.

**Notes for the session invitation:**
- Ask for consent to record before recording starts.
- Tell participants not to share patient names or identifying details.
- Say they can stop or skip any question.
- Give the same incentive to anyone who turns out not to fit, and set their data aside.
