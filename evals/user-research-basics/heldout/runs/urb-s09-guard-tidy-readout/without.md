**Rebooking after a cancelled physio appointment: what we heard**
8 interviews with front-desk staff at 8 independent clinics, October

**What matters most**
1. **Rebooking is manual.** 7 of 8 staff rebook cancelled patients by phone, from memory or a sticky note. Our app's waitlist is used at 1 clinic.
2. **Staff don't trust the app's open slots.** 6 of 8 said therapists block time on paper, which the app can't see. "If I offer a slot and it's gone, I look like an idiot."
3. **Same-day cancellations cost the most, and no one can fill them quickly.** 5 of 8.

**What we're less sure about**
- Two clinics said patients would welcome a text offering a slot. We didn't ask the other six.
- All 8 clinics have 3 or fewer therapists. Larger clinics may work differently.

**What I suggest**
- Fix slot accuracy (finding 2) before building auto-fill for late cancellations. Otherwise staff won't use it.
- Have 2–3 clinics send same-day text offers by hand for two weeks, and count how many slots get filled.
