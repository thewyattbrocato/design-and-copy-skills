**Rebooking after a cancelled physio appointment: what we heard**
8 interviews with front-desk staff at 8 independent clinics, October

**What matters most**
1. **Rebooking is manual.** Staff rebook cancelled patients by phone, from memory or a sticky note (7 of 8). Our app's waitlist is used at only 1 clinic.
2. **Staff don't trust the app's open slots** (6 of 8). Therapists block time on paper, and the app doesn't know. "If I offer a slot and it's gone, I look like an idiot."
3. **Same-day cancellations are the costly ones**, and nobody has a quick way to fill them (5 of 8).

**What we're less sure about**
- Two clinics said patients would happily get a text offering a slot. We didn't ask the others.
- All 8 clinics have 3 or fewer therapists. Larger clinics may work differently.

**What I suggest**
- Fix slot accuracy (finding 2) before building auto-fill for late cancellations, or staff won't use it.
- Ask 2–3 clinics to try a same-day text offer by hand for two weeks and count how many slots get filled.
