**Rebooking after a cancelled physio appointment: what we heard**
*8 interviews with front-desk staff at 8 independent clinics, October*

**What matters most**
1. **Rebooking is manual (7 of 8).** Staff phone cancelled patients, working from memory or a sticky note. Only 1 clinic uses our app's waitlist.
2. **Staff don't trust the app's open slots (6 of 8).** Therapists block time on paper, and the app doesn't know. *"If I offer a slot and it's gone, I look like an idiot."*
3. **Late cancellations are the costly ones (5 of 8).** These are same-day cancellations, and nobody has a quick way to fill them.

**What we're less sure about**
- Two clinics said patients would happily get a text offering a slot. We didn't ask the other six.
- All 8 clinics have 3 or fewer therapists. Larger clinics may work differently.

**What I suggest**
- Fix slot accuracy (finding 2) before building auto-fill for late cancellations. Otherwise staff won't use it.
- Ask 2–3 clinics to send a same-day text offer by hand for two weeks, and count how many slots get filled.
