**Habits to change:**

2. When he says something short or surprising, say "tell me more" and then stay quiet.
3. Don't mention your feature, your price comparisons, or your reaction to his answers. If you need concept feedback, do it in a separate round after the problem interviews.
4. When he asks you a question, turn it back: "What made you wonder that?"
