Tidewell: App Support Tickets, Last Week

Summary of 9 tickets that mentioned the app

Rescheduling is the biggest problem. 4 of 9 tickets were people who couldn't find a way to change their pickup day. 2 more were surprised by an extra-bag fee they weren't told about before paying.

What we're seeing

1. No visible way to reschedule (4)

Customers looked for a reschedule option and didn't find one. One said there is no button on the order after payment. Another gave up and cancelled and rebooked.

"There's no reschedule button on the order after payment?"

2. Extra-bag fee comes as a surprise (2)

A $6 extra-bag fee showed up at the end of checkout. Customers said they weren't told beforehand, and one saw the total jump.

"Nobody told me about that before I paid."

3. Late driver with no update (1)

A driver ran 40 minutes late and the customer got no text.

4. Missing item and praise (1 each)

One blue sock was missing from a delivery. One customer just wanted to say thanks.

Suggested next steps

  1. Add a clear reschedule option on the order screen, including after payment. This has the most tickets behind it.
  2. Show the extra-bag fee before payment. Put it in the booking flow and the price summary so the total doesn't change at the end.
  3. Check late-driver messaging. Find out whether a delay text exists and why it didn't send.
  4. Handle the missing sock through the normal lost-item process.
Keep in mind: this is only 9 tickets, so treat the counts as a signal, not a measure. The late driver and missing sock tickets are about service, not the app itself. Whether the app is at fault there is unconfirmed.
