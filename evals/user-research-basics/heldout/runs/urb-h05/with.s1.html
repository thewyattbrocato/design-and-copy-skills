App Support Tickets: Last Week

Tidewell · 9 tickets mentioning the app · Prepared for the team

Key takeaway

Rescheduling is the biggest app problem. 4 of 9 tickets were from customers who couldn't find a way to change their pickup day. One had to cancel and rebook. A further 2 tickets were about a surprise $6 extra-bag fee at checkout.

What customers said

1. Rescheduling a pickup (4 tickets)

Customers looked for a way to move their pickup and couldn't find one. One said there is no reschedule button after payment. Another gave up and cancelled and rebooked.

"Paid for a Tuesday pickup but I'm away. There's no reschedule button on the order after payment?"

2. Extra-bag fee surprise (2 tickets)

A $6 extra-bag fee appeared at the end of checkout, and customers say they weren't told about it beforehand. The total jumped.

"There was a bag fee at the end that I didn't expect, the total jumped."

3. One-offs (3 tickets)

One late driver with no text (40 minutes). One missing sock. One customer who loves the service.

Suggested next steps

  1. Add a clear "Reschedule pickup" option on the order screen, including after payment. Check whether it exists today but is hard to find, or is missing.
  2. Show the extra-bag fee before payment, with the bag allowance and the $6 price stated up front.
  3. Reply to the late-driver and missing-sock tickets directly. Check whether late-arrival texts are meant to send automatically.
Based on a small sample (9 tickets, one week), so treat counts as a signal rather than a measurement. Themes were grouped by hand from the ticket text.
