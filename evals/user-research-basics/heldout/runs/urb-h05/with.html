App Support Tickets: Last Week

Tidewell support · 9 tickets that mention the app

Bottom line: 6 of 9 tickets came from two app gaps. Customers can't find a way to reschedule a pickup (4 tickets), and an extra-bag fee appears without warning before payment (2 tickets). Both look fixable in the app.

Suggested next steps

  1. Add a clear "Reschedule pickup" action to the order screen, including after payment. This is the biggest source of tickets.
  2. Show the extra-bag fee before checkout (a note on the order screen, or in the total preview) so the final price isn't a surprise.
  3. Send an automatic text when a driver is running late. This is one ticket, but it's cheap to check whether delay alerts exist.
  4. Follow up on the missing sock through the usual lost-item process.

This is a small sample (9 tickets), so treat the counts as a signal, not a rate. Each ticket is counted once, under its main issue. Three of the nine (late driver, missing sock, praise) don't mention the app itself.
