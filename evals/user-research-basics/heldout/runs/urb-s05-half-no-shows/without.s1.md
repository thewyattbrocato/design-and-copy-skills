A 47% no-show rate (7 of 15) is a small sample, but it's high enough that I'd trust the direction. I can't see your data, so these are likely causes, not confirmed ones.

## What's probably going wrong

1. **The slots are 2-3 weeks out.** Someone who clicked a newsletter link on impulse has forgotten by then, or their plans have changed. This is probably the biggest factor.
2. **The slots are weekday mornings.** Most climbers fit sessions around work, so they climb evenings and weekends. Anyone who books a 9am Tuesday slot is hopeful, not sure. It also biases your sample toward people with flexible weekday schedules, which matters for research quality.
3. **There's one confirmation email and nothing after it.** There are no reminders, no calendar invite, and probably no easy way to reschedule. Cancelling takes effort, and just not showing up takes none.
4. **The incentive comes after the session, and it's small for a weekday-morning commitment.** The $20 is fine as a reward, but it does little to get people to show up.
5. **The booking is low-commitment.** A newsletter link plus a form is cheap to submit, with no personal contact and no sense that a real person is waiting.

## What to change, roughly in order of impact

1. **Shorten the lead time.** Offer slots within 2-5 days of booking. If the newsletter is monthly, run a rolling sign-up and release slots weekly.
2. **Offer evening and weekend slots.** Do this alongside the weekday ones, then check which ones people actually attend.
3. **Send a calendar invite (.ics) with the confirmation.** Include the format, the length, and the link or location.
4. **Send reminders.** Send one at 24 hours and one at 1 hour, and put one-click reschedule and cancel links in each. SMS usually beats email if you can collect a phone number.
5. **Ask for a reply or confirmation 48 hours ahead.** Release the slot if they don't respond, so you can backfill it.
6. **Overbook by about 20-30%** until the rate drops, and keep a short waitlist to backfill.
7. **Make the incentive more concrete.** Say "$20 gift card sent within 24 hours of the session" in the confirmation and reminders. If you can afford it, test a higher amount for the harder-to-fill slots.
8. **Add a personal touch.** A short email from a named person ("Looking forward to talking about your climbing") tends to improve attendance.

## How to check it

Track no-show rate by lead time, slot time, and reminder status for the next 15-20 bookings. If you change everything at once you won't know which fix worked, but at this volume that's probably acceptable. The first things I'd change are the lead time, the evening slots, and the reminders.
