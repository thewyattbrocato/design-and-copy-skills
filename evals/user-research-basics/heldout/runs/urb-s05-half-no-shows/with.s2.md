Seven of 15 no-shows is a lot, but your process is probably the cause rather than your members. I can't tell from this data which cause matters most, so the causes below are hypotheses. You can check them cheaply.

## What's likely going wrong

1. **The booking is too far ahead.** People book two to three weeks out. Plans change in that time, and the confirmation email is the only thing they get. Nothing reminds them.
2. **There's no reminder.** One automatic email, then silence, is the most common reason people miss appointments. It's also the easiest to fix.
3. **The slots don't suit climbers.** Weekday 9 or 10am is work time for most people. Gym members tend to climb evenings and weekends. Many may have booked on impulse from the newsletter and then found they couldn't get away. This also biases who you can reach. The people who show up skew toward those with flexible days.
4. **Booking costs nothing and the person is anonymous.** A newsletter click to a form is a low-commitment action. Nobody knows who the interviewer is, and no human has contacted them.
5. **The incentive comes after the session.** A $20 card is modest for an hour of a weekday morning. Because it arrives later, it does little to bring people in. The slot also asks a lot of them compared with the reward.
6. **Cancelling is probably hard.** I'm assuming the confirmation email has no easy reschedule or cancel link. If it doesn't, people who can't make it just don't come.

## What to change

**Do these first (cheap, this week):**
- Send reminders 3 days, 1 day and 2 hours before. Use SMS or in-app push for the last one if you have a phone number or the app can send it.
- Put a one-click reschedule/cancel link in the confirmation and every reminder. A cancellation lets you refill the slot, and a no-show wastes it.
- Make the confirmation personal. It should come from a named person, say what the session is, and say how long it is and whether it's remote. A short "reply to confirm" step helps. Treat a booking with no reply as unconfirmed, and offer the slot to someone else.

**Then change the offer:**
- Offer evening and weekend slots, and book within about 3 to 7 days. Keep a few weekday slots.
- Raise the incentive, or pay part of it up front. Any change here is a test, so track whether no-shows move.
- Overbook slightly. Some no-shows will remain. Book one or two extra people per batch of sessions rather than aiming for zero.

**Check your own data before changing everything:**
- Your 15 bookings should show whether the 7 no-shows are the ones who booked furthest out, took the 9am slot, or never opened the confirmation. Seven of 15 is too few to read as a statistic, but it can point to the lead worth fixing first.
- Email the no-shows and ask what happened. Keep it short and non-judgmental, and offer a new slot. This tells you more than guessing, and it may recover a few sessions.

## Limits

I can't see your booking data or emails, so I assumed there's no reminder and no cancel link beyond what you described. A person on your team has to make the changes in your booking tool and send the follow-ups. After one round of changes, compare the new no-show count with 7 of 15. Count it as "n of N", not a percentage, until you have a lot more sessions.
