Paddlepoint · Kayak Renter Interview Guide

40 min · one person at a time
Participant: ______________ Date: ________
Goal: Learn how people actually find, book, and pay for kayak rentals at small outfitters, and where it goes wrong. Not to pitch or validate Paddlepoint. Ask about the past, not the hypothetical.

1 · Open & consent 0–4

“Thanks for your time. I’m researching how people rent kayaks. There are no right answers, and I didn’t build anything you need to be nice about. Mind if I take notes / record for my own reference? You can skip any question or stop anytime.”

☐ Consent to notes/recording   ☐ Told them it’s ~40 min

2 · Background 4–9

  1. How often do you paddle, and how did you get into it?
  2. Do you usually own gear or rent? Why rent?
  3. Who do you usually go with (solo, family, friends, group)?

3 · The last rental (core story) 9–24

“Walk me through the last time you rented a kayak, starting from when you first thought about going.”

  1. What made you decide to go? How did you pick this outfitter?
  2. What did you look at or compare? What other options did you consider?
  3. How did you book: online, phone, text, walk-up? What happened step by step?
  4. Was anything unclear (availability, price, gear, tides/weather, group size, waivers)?
  5. What happened on the day: check-in, payment, waiting, pickup, return?
  6. What was the best and the most frustrating moment from start to finish?

Probes: “Then what?” · “What did you do next?” · “Why was that?” · “Can you show me the message/page/email?” · “How did that feel?”

4 · Booking & payment details 24–32

  1. Have you ever wanted to book but couldn’t? What happened? Where did you go instead?
  2. Have you changed, cancelled, or rescheduled? How did that go (weather, fees, refunds)?
  3. How do you handle deposits, payment, and group payments?
  4. How do you check whether kayaks or times are available?

Probes: “How often does that happen?” · “What did it cost you (time, money, trip)?”

5 · Workarounds & wishes 32–37

  1. What have you done to make renting easier (apps, texting the shop, always going to the same place)?
  2. What would make you choose one outfitter over another next time?
  3. If you could change one thing about renting, what would it be?

6 · Wrap up 37–40

  1. “Is there anything I should have asked but didn’t?”
  2. “Who else rents kayaks that I could talk to?”

Thank them. Confirm any follow-up or thank-you (gift card, etc.). Stop recording.

Rules for a first-timer

  • Listen ~80%. Talk 20%.
  • Ask for stories (“last time…”) over opinions (“would you…”).
  • Stay silent 5 seconds after an answer. They often add more.
  • One question at a time. Keep it open-ended, not yes/no.
  • Follow the energy. It’s fine to skip questions.
  • Don’t explain or pitch Paddlepoint until the very end, and only if asked.
  • Write down exact words, not your interpretations.

Avoid

  • “Would you use an app that…?” (people say yes to be nice)
  • “Don’t you hate when…?” (leading)
  • Fixing or defending: just note it.
  • Filling awkward silence with your own answer.

If they get stuck

  • “Tell me more about that.”
  • “What do you mean by ___?”
  • “Can you give me an example?”
  • “What did you do instead?”

Listen for

  • Workarounds (texts, spreadsheets, calls)
  • Money or time lost; strong emotion
  • Things they did more than once
  • Surprises that don’t match your assumptions

After the call (5 min)

  • Write the top 3 surprises.
  • Pull 2–3 direct quotes.
  • Note what you’d ask differently next time.
