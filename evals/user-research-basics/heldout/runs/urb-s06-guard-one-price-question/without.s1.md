Here are a few non-leading options, depending on what you want to learn:

**Most direct replacement (spending behavior):**
"How do you currently keep track of warranties for the things in your home, and what, if anything, do you spend on that?"

**If you want to probe willingness to pay without anchoring:**
"Tell me about the last time you needed warranty information for an appliance. What did you do, and what did it cost you in time or money?"

Follow up later, if needed: "What would a solution to that have been worth to you?"

**Why the original leads:**
- "An app that tracks…" presumes the solution and that they'd want it.
- "How much would you pay" presumes they'd pay something, and hypothetical pricing answers tend to be unreliable.
- "All your household appliances" assumes the problem is broad and shared.

If you only want one line to drop in, I'd use the first. It's open, asks about real current behavior, and still surfaces whether money or effort is already going toward the problem.
