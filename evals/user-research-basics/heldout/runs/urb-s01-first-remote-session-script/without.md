# Moderator script: pothole report form usability session (30 min)

Text in **bold** is what you say. Text in *[italics]* is a note to you. Read it almost word for word. It's fine to sound like you're reading.

## Before the day (needed because nobody has agreed to recording yet)

1. **Email each resident 1-2 days ahead** with a short consent note: the session will be recorded (screen and audio), who will see the recording, how long you'll keep it, and that they can decline recording and still take part and get the voucher. Ask them to reply "I agree" or tell you if they'd rather not be recorded. Fill in the retention and access details from your city's policy. I don't know them.
2. **Tell your PM** to say hello at the start (script below). A silent observer you haven't mentioned is the thing most likely to make a participant feel misled.
3. **Don't start recording until they've agreed out loud.** Even with the email, ask again on the call.
4. **Prepare a "reset" link**, the prototype link ready to paste again, in case it breaks.
5. **Have your fallback phrases** (last section) on a sticky note.

## 0:00-0:04 Welcome and consent

**Hi [Name], thanks so much for joining. I'm [your name], and I work on [the city's website team]. Can you hear and see me okay?**

*[Wait for yes.]*

**Today we're looking at an early version of a new form for reporting potholes on the city website. The session takes about 30 minutes. I'll ask you to try a few things, and I'll ask you to think out loud as you go.**

**The most important thing to know is that we're testing the form, not you. You can't do anything wrong. If something is confusing, that's useful for us, because it means the form needs fixing. Please be as honest as you like. You won't hurt anyone's feelings.**

**Two things before we start. First, my colleague [PM's name] is also on the call. They're here to watch and take notes. Their camera and mic are off, and they won't interrupt. [PM's name], would you say hello?**

*[PM says a quick hello, then goes quiet.]*

**Second, I'd like to record this session, both your screen and our voices, so I can review it afterward instead of relying on my notes. The recording will be seen by [who: e.g., the project team], and we'll keep it for [how long]. It won't be shared outside the team or published. Are you okay with me recording?**

*[If yes:]* **Great. I'm starting the recording now.** *[Start recording.]* **And just to confirm for the recording, you're okay with being recorded?** *[Wait for a verbal yes.]*

*[If no or hesitant:]* **That's completely fine. I'll just take notes instead, and nothing changes about the session or your voucher.** *[Do not record.]*

**You're free to stop at any point or skip anything you're not comfortable with, for any reason. Your $25 grocery voucher is yours either way, and I'll email it to you today after we finish.**

**Do you have any questions before we begin?**

## 0:04-0:08 Warm-up

**Let's start with a few quick questions.**

- **Have you ever reported a problem to the city, like a pothole, a streetlight, or missed trash pickup? How did you do it?**
- **When you've used the city's website before, what was that for?**
- **How do you usually feel about doing that kind of thing online?**

*[Listen. Follow up with "Tell me more about that" or "What happened next?" Don't correct or defend the site.]*

## 0:08-0:10 Setting up the prototype

**Now I'm going to send you a link in the chat. It opens an early prototype, which is a mock-up that looks real but isn't fully working yet. Nothing you enter will be sent to the city.**

*[Paste the link.]*

**Please click it, and share your screen when it loads so I can see what you see.**

*[Help with screen share if needed.]*

**Because it's a prototype, it may occasionally glitch. If something breaks, that's not because of anything you did.**

**While you work, please say out loud what you're looking at, what you're thinking, and anything that surprises or confuses you. There are no wrong comments.**

## 0:10-0:18 Task 1: Report a pothole

**Imagine you noticed a pothole on your street, and you'd like the city to fix it. Using this prototype, please report it. Use a real street near you, or any address you like. Go ahead and start whenever you're ready.**

*[Bug note: the prototype breaks if they tap the map before typing an address. Two options:*
- *Slightly steer: add "**Start by typing the address**" to the prompt. This is the safest choice, but you'll lose any insight about whether people reach for the map first.*
- *Or leave it open and use the recovery below if they tap the map. Watch and note whether they reach for the map, because that's a finding for the team.]*

**If they tap the map early and it breaks:**

**That's a glitch in the prototype, not something you did. I'm going to send the link again so we can reset it. This time, could you start by typing the address?**

*[Paste the link. Note the incident in your log. Move on without making a big deal of it.]*

**While they work, only say things like:**

- **"What are you thinking right now?"**
- **"What are you looking for?"**
- **"Is that what you expected?"**

*[Stay quiet otherwise. Silence is okay. Count to ten in your head before saying anything.]*

**When they say they're done:**

**Thanks. How did that feel? Was anything unclear or unexpected?**

## 0:18-0:23 Task 2: Add a photo

**Now imagine you took a photo of the pothole on your phone. Show me how you'd add a photo to your report.**

*[They may not have a real photo handy. They can pretend, or you can say: "**You can just show me where you'd do it, and pretend**.""]*

**Afterward:**

**How did that go? Was there anything you weren't sure about?**

## 0:23-0:27 Task 3: Check on the report later

**Now imagine it's a week later and you'd like to know whether the city has fixed the pothole you reported. Show me how you'd find out.**

*[Prototype may not support this fully. If they're stuck for over a minute, ask: "**Where would you expect to find that?**" and note where they click.]*

**Afterward:**

**How did that feel? What would you expect to see?**

## 0:27-0:29 Wrap-up questions

- **Overall, what stood out as easy or hard?**
- **If you could change one thing about this form, what would it be?**
- **Is there anything you expected to see that wasn't there?**

## 0:29-0:30 Closing

**That's everything I needed. Thank you, this was really helpful.**

*[Check with PM, in the chat or by asking aloud:]* **[PM's name], do you have any questions you'd like me to ask?**

*[PM can unmute briefly and ask one question or say no.]*

**Do you have any questions for me?**

**I'll stop the recording now.** *[Stop recording.]* **You'll get your $25 grocery voucher by email today. If it hasn't arrived by tomorrow, reply to my email and I'll sort it out. Thanks again, and have a great day.**

## When they ask you something mid-task

This is your biggest worry, so here is a small system. You never need to know the answer. Your job is to turn the question back to them, kindly. Pick one of these:

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| **"Is this right?"** | **"There's no right or wrong here. What would you expect to happen?"** |
| **"What does this button do?"** | **"What do you think it does?"** or **"What would you expect it to do?"** |
| **"Should I click here?"** | **"What would you do if I weren't here?"** |
| **"Did I do it correctly?"** | **"You're doing great. Tell me what you're thinking."** |
| **"I'm stuck."** | **"What are you looking for?"** or **"What would you try next?"** |
| **"Can you just tell me?"** | **"I'd love to, but I want to see how it works for you without help. Take your best guess."** |
| **"Sorry, I'm bad at this."** | **"You're not at all. If it's confusing, that means the form needs work. That's what we're here to find out."** |

If they're truly stuck for more than 1-2 minutes, or clearly getting frustrated:

**Let's move on to the next part. You've shown me something really useful.**

Never tell them where to click, and don't apologize for the design.

If you freeze entirely, say: **"Say more about that."** It works almost anywhere.

## Notes

- **If someone reports a real, active pothole problem,** don't promise action. Say: "**Thanks for telling me. After this I'll make sure that gets passed to the right team.**" Only say that if you will.
- **If a participant is late or has tech trouble,** trim the warm-up first. Never cut consent.
- **Voucher:** I've worded it as "yours either way" so it isn't tied to recording or finishing the tasks. Confirm that matches how it's actually been set up. If it's not true, don't say it.
