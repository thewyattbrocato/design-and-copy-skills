I'd break the 14 toggles into four labeled groups, ordered by how often people touch them. Put the two master switches at the top of their channels rather than in the list.

**1. Quiet hours** (own section, first or near the top)
- Start/end time pickers, with a note: "Pauses push notifications."
- Quiet hours is a schedule, not a notification type, so it shouldn't look like one more toggle.

**2. My garden**
- Watering reminders
- Frost alerts
- Harvest windows
- Plant-of-the-week
- New plant suggestions

These are the features the app is built around, so they should default on. Frost alerts are time-sensitive, so consider making them non-disableable or at least defaulting them on with a clear description.

**3. Community**
- Friend requests
- Comments on my beds
- Shared bed invites

These are people-driven and can be noisy, so group them together so users can silence the whole set at once.

**4. Email digests and marketing**
- Weekly summary email
- Product news
- Sale announcements

Put marketing (product news and sales) under its own label, default it off, and keep the weekly summary on. Separating these makes it clear that the marketing emails are optional and that the summary is useful.

**Master switches:** Put "Push notifications" and "Email notifications" at the top of their respective sections, or as a header switch for the whole list. When a master is off, grey out the toggles beneath it and keep them readable. Don't remove them, or users will lose track of their own choices.

**Two things to consider:**
- **Grid instead of stacked toggles.** If users need to pick channels per category, a table works well: rows are the event types (watering, frost, friend requests, and so on) and columns are Push and Email. That's about 11 rows of checkboxes and is easier to scan than 14 stacked toggles. I'd only go this route if people need per-channel control. Otherwise the four groups above are simpler.
- **Summaries on collapsed sections.** Show a one-line status on each group header, such as "5 on" or "Quiet 10pm–7am," and expand only when tapped. This helps most on mobile.

The main fix is grouping. Right now the toggles have equal visual weight, so nothing signals what matters. Grouping, a clear default for each group, and grey-out behavior under the masters should resolve most of the overwhelm.
