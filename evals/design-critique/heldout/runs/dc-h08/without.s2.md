The 14 toggles mix two different things: channels (the push and email masters), and topics (the other 11). Quiet hours is a modifier on push. Splitting them out and grouping the topics should fix most of the overwhelm.

**1. Delivery (top of screen)**
- Push notifications (master)
  - Quiet hours, nested under push as an indented row that opens a time picker. It only applies to push, so it shouldn't sit beside the topic toggles.
- Email notifications (master)

**2. Garden care** (the core value, so it goes first)
- Watering reminders
- Frost alerts
- Harvest windows

**3. Community**
- Friend requests
- Comments on my beds
- Shared bed invites

**4. Inspiration**
- New plant suggestions
- Plant-of-the-week

**5. Emails & promotions**
- Weekly summary email
- Product news email
- Sale announcements

**Behaviors that help**
- **Dim dependent toggles when a master is off.** Grey them out with a short note like "Turn on push to receive these". Weekly summary and the two marketing emails depend on the email master. Don't hide them, so people can still see what exists.
- **Keep marketing separate and default it to off.** Product news and sale announcements are the ones people resent. Putting them in their own section also makes the care alerts feel more important.
- **Give each section a short header and a one-line summary** such as "Garden care · 3 on". On a small screen, you could collapse Community, Inspiration and Emails by default and leave Garden care open. That leaves about 5 visible rows on first view instead of 14.
- **Make frost alerts hard to miss.** They're time-sensitive and tied to plant loss. Consider a "Recommended" tag, or an "are you sure?" prompt when someone turns them off.

**If you want to go further later:** replace the single toggle per topic with a small Push / Email pair of checkboxes on each row. That's a matrix, so it gives users more control. It also adds visual weight, so I'd only do it if people ask for per-channel control.
