Reviewing the five screens together turned up a few issues that per-screen review would miss.

**Must fix before launch**

1. **The progress counter is inconsistent.** Screen 1 says "Step 1 of 4". Screens 2–4 say "of 5". Pick one number. Screen 5 has no bar, so the flow is probably 4 steps plus a finish screen, or 5 if Welcome counts. Whichever you choose, update every screen to match. As written, the first screen promises a shorter flow than the user gets.

2. **"Plot" on Screen 4 should be "plant".** The footer says "you'll need at least one plot before reminders can start." Everywhere else the word is "plant". Users will wonder what a plot is, or whether they missed a step.

3. **Screen 4's footer contradicts its "Skip" link.** The footer says you need a plant for reminders, and the screen offers a skip. If skipping is allowed, the footer should say what happens: "You can skip for now, but reminders won't start until you add a plant." Screen 5 then says "Your sanctuary is ready" to someone who may have no plants and no reminders. That's a dead end. Either show different completion copy when they skipped, such as "Add a plant whenever you're ready", or reconsider whether Skip belongs there.

**Should fix**

4. **Terminology drifts.** "Garden" (Screen 1), "spaces" (Screen 2), "sanctuary" (Screen 5), and "plot" (Screen 4) all name the same thing. Choose one, probably "garden", and use it throughout. "Sanctuary" is also tonally different from the rest of the copy. Screen 2 says "rooms" in the title and "spaces" in the body, so unify that too.

5. **Screen 1 promises more than the flow delivers.** "So we can take care of the rest" suggests Fernway will water the plants. It only sends reminders. Something like "so we can remind you when they need care" is more accurate.

6. **Screen 3 has two small issues.** "One nudge per day" is a commitment, so make sure the product actually batches reminders that way. "Choose a time that suits you" is filler, and the default of 8:00 AM already shows it can be changed. Also, Screen 2's chips have no stated minimum. If the user picks none, can they proceed?

7. **Button labels switch from "Continue" to "Next".** It's minor, but use one label for the same action.

8. **Screen 4 says Fernway "will work out how thirsty it is".** That covers watering only, but the app also handles feeding and repotting. It's also unclear whether a photo identifies the species. Consider "Fernway will work out what it needs."

**Minor**

- The "Done" button on Screen 5 could be more specific, for example "Go to my garden".
- The emoji on Screen 5 is the only one in the flow. That's fine if it's deliberate.

The first three are the ones I'd treat as launch blockers. The rest is polish, though unifying the vocabulary is cheap and makes the flow feel much more coherent.
