Each screen reads well alone, but the flow has a few cross-screen problems to fix before launch. The most serious is that the plot requirement is never set up.

**Blocking or likely to confuse users**

1. **"Plot" appears for the first time on Screen 4.** Screen 2 says "rooms" and "spaces," and Screen 4 says "plot." Users won't know whether a plot is a room, a space, or something they still need to create. Pick one term and use it everywhere.

2. **The plot requirement isn't enforced on Screen 2.** Screen 2 lets users press "Next" with no rooms selected, yet Screen 4 says reminders can't start without a plot. Disable "Next" until at least one room is chosen, or create a default plot from the room selection. That makes the Screen 4 footer unnecessary.

3. **Screen 4's "Skip" has no stated consequence.** If someone skips, they get no reminders, and the footer is the only hint. Either make skipping lead somewhere clear, such as a "Add a plant later to start reminders" state, or remove "Skip" and require a plant.

4. **Screen 5 says "You're all set!" even if the user skipped the plant.** That is false in that path. Show different copy when no plant has been added, for example "Almost there. Add a plant to start your reminders."

**Inconsistencies**

5. **Progress counts don't match.** Screen 1 says "Step 1 of 4," but Screens 2–4 say "of 5." Screen 5 has no progress bar, so the total is either 4 or 5 and should be the same on every screen. Use "of 4" if Screen 5 is the finish screen.

6. **Button labels vary.** Screen 1 says "Continue," Screens 2 and 3 say "Next," and Screen 5 says "Done." "Continue" is the simplest choice for all of them, or keep "Next" for the middle steps and "Finish" for the last.

7. **The metaphor shifts.** Screen 1 says "garden," Screen 5 says "sanctuary," and Screen 5 also says "growing." Choose one image. "Your plants are set up" might be the clearest.

**Smaller copy notes**

8. Screen 4's "Fernway will work out how thirsty it is" is vague and slightly overclaims. Something like "Fernway will figure out its watering schedule" is more concrete.

9. Screen 3's "Choose a time that suits you" repeats the purpose of the screen. Consider "We'll send one reminder a day with everything that needs attention."

Once the room/plot term and the zero-plant path are settled, the flow should hold together. I'd treat items 1–4 as launch blockers and 5–9 as polish.
