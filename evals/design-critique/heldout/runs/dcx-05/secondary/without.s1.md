Each screen reads well alone, but the flow has a few problems that only show up when you read the five together.

**Must fix before launch**

1. **The progress bar is wrong.** Screen 1 says "Step 1 of 4," but Screens 2–4 say "of 5." Screen 5 has no bar. Pick one total. Since the welcome screen is counted, I'd show "of 4" on screens 1–4 and keep the bar off screen 5, or use "of 5" and count the welcome screen as step 1 of 5.

2. **"Plot" appears from nowhere.** Screen 4's footer says "you'll need at least one plot before reminders can start," but no earlier screen mentions plots. Screen 2 asks about "rooms" and says "spaces." Users will not know whether a room is a plot, or whether they need to create one. Use one term throughout. Also make clear how a room selected on Screen 2 relates to the plot the footer requires. If selecting rooms already creates plots, the footer may be unnecessary.

3. **The footer contradicts the Skip link.** Screen 4 offers "Skip," and the footer says reminders can't start without a plot. A user who skips will reach "You're all set!" with no reminders and no explanation. Either block Skip until a plot exists, or tell them on Screen 5 that reminders are off until they add a plant.

**Worth checking**

4. **Screen 2 with nothing selected.** The body copy says "Pick the rooms," but it doesn't say whether Next is disabled or what happens if they pick none. Given point 2, this may be the same gap.

5. **Screen 5 doesn't confirm anything.** "Your sanctuary is ready" doesn't tell the user when they'll get their reminder or whether they added a plant. A line like "Reminders start at 8:00 AM" would make the ending useful instead of decorative.

6. **Vocabulary drifts.** Screen 1 says "garden," Screen 5 says "sanctuary." Pick one. "Garden" fits the watering and repotting theme better.

7. **Screen 4 makes a vague promise.** "Fernway will work out how thirsty it is" doesn't say whether this needs a photo, a species match, or a manual pick. If search-by-name is a fallback, say that, since users who can't take a photo may think they can't add a plant.

Points 1–3 are factual or flow errors, and I'd fix them before shipping. The rest are copy polish. Each screen passed review on its own, so I'd suggest one pass that reads the flow end to end, with someone who hasn't seen the design work going through it.
