Each screen reads fine alone, but together they have a few real problems. Here they are, most serious first.

**Fix before launch**

1. **The step count changes.** Screen 1 says "Step 1 of 4" and screens 2–4 say "of 5". Screens 1–4 plus the final screen make five, so "of 5" is probably right. If so, screen 5 should either show "Step 5 of 5" or the total should be 4. Pick one.
2. **The footer on screen 4 uses a word nothing else does.** "You'll need at least one plot" is the only place "plot" appears. Everywhere else it's "plant", so use "plant".
3. **Skip contradicts the footer, and screen 5 ignores it.** The footer says reminders can't start without a plant, and the screen offers "Skip" anyway. A user who skips still gets "You're all set! Your sanctuary is ready." That's untrue for them, because nothing will remind them of anything. Either:
   - show a different final screen for skippers, such as "You're in. Add a plant whenever you're ready and we'll start reminding you", or
   - change the footer so it says what happens on skip.

   Also, "Heads up" in a footer is easy to miss. Consider putting this note near the Skip link.

**Worth tightening**

4. **The vocabulary drifts.** Screen 1 says "garden", screen 5 says "sanctuary", and screen 4 says "plot". Choose one metaphor, or drop it and say "plants". Screen 2 also moves from "rooms" to "spaces", and Balcony isn't a room. "Pick the spaces that have plants" covers all of them.
5. **Screen 1 overpromises.** "So we can take care of the rest" suggests Fernway looks after the plants. It reminds people to. Something like "so we can remind you when each one needs care" is more accurate.
6. **Screen 4 mentions only watering.** "Work out how thirsty it is" leaves out feeding and repotting. "Work out what it needs" covers all three.
7. **The primary button label changes.** It's "Continue" on screen 1 and "Next" on screens 2–3. Use one label for the primary button on screens 1–3. Screen 4 has no primary button, so check that a photo or search result moves the user on cleanly.

**Smaller notes**

- Screen 3: "one nudge per day" is a good expectation to set. If people can change the time later, add "You can change this anytime in Settings".
- Screen 2: say whether choosing no rooms is allowed. If Next stays disabled until they pick one, tell them.
- Screen 4: "Take photo" will trigger a camera permission prompt. A short line on why you need it will help.
- Screen 5: the emoji is fine, but "Time to get growing" is generic. A specific next step, such as "We'll send your first reminder tomorrow at 8:00 AM", would be more useful, and it ties back to screen 3.
