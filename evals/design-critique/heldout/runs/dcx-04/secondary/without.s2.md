The structure is clean and the pricing is easy to scan. The main problem is that the copy is generic enough to fit any SaaS product, and a few lines make claims without giving the reader anything to check. Here are the issues, most important first.

## Biggest issues

1. **"Items" is never defined.** This is the core pricing unit, and a studio owner can't tell whether it means members, wheels, kilns, bookings, or something else. Basic's "Up to 100 items" and Pro's "Unlimited items" can't be compared until that's clear. Name the unit in the copy, e.g. "Up to 100 members" or "Up to 100 bookable stations and kilns."

2. **The FAQ answers don't answer.** "Do you offer support?" → "Yes, our friendly team is here to help" says nothing a reader can use. "How does it work?" → "Simply sign up…" describes signup, not what the product does.

3. **"Is my data secure?" → "Absolutely. We take security very seriously."** This is the weakest answer on the page. Every vendor says it, so it reads as a non-answer, and a studio handling member names, contact info, and possibly payments will want specifics. State what's true: encryption, where payments are handled, backups, data export and deletion. If you can't make specific claims yet, that's worth knowing before launch.

4. **The Enterprise audience doesn't fit.** "For large organizations" is an odd fit for a pottery studio. If you expect multi-location studios or community centers, say that ("For multi-location studios and community centers"), since that's what the tier is for.

## Pricing section

- **"Perfect for getting started"** and **"Everything in Basic, plus more"** are filler. "Plus more" is the only thing Pro's description says. Name the things: "Everything in Basic, plus online member payments, waitlists, and…"
- **"Advanced analytics"** is vague. What can the owner see: utilization by kiln, revenue per member, no-show rates? A concrete example sells better.
- **"Priority support" vs. "Email support"** needs a response-time difference, e.g. "24-hour response" vs. "same business day."
- **The CTA "Select"** is weak. "Start free trial" matches the trial line and tells the reader what happens next.
- **The trial line** should say what happens at the end: "After 14 days, choose a plan. Your data stays for [X] days." Owners will wonder whether they lose their schedule.

## FAQ additions I'd recommend

These are questions a pottery studio owner will likely ask before signing up, and they're missing:
- What counts as an item / what happens if I go over 100?
- Can I switch plans later? Is there a setup fee?
- Can I import my existing member list?
- Do members need an account to book, and can they pay through Kilnbook?
- If I cancel, can I export my data? (Important for a booking system.)

## Rewrite samples

- **Q:** Do you offer support?
  **A:** Basic includes email support. Pro adds priority support with a [same-business-day / 4-hour] response time. Enterprise accounts get a dedicated account manager.
- **Q:** Is my data secure?
  **A:** [Your specifics: e.g., "All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Payments are handled by [processor]; we never store card numbers. You can export or delete your studio's data at any time."] Only include claims you can back up.
- **Q:** How does it work?
  **A:** You set up your studio's wheels, kilns, and time slots, and invite your members. They book and manage their sessions online, and you see the schedule in one place.
