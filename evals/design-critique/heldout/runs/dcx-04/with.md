Your structure is right: three tiers, a badge on the middle one, a trial line, and a short FAQ. The problem is the words. Right now they could belong to any SaaS product, and that's where I'd put your effort.

## Biggest issues

**1. Nothing here is about pottery.**
Swap "Kilnbook" for any other product name and the section still works. That means it isn't persuading anyone. A studio owner should see their own problems: double-booked wheels, kiln firing schedules, chasing members for dues.

**2. "Items" doesn't mean anything.**
"Up to 100 items" is your only real limit, and a studio owner can't tell what counts as an item. Price on something they already count, like members, locations, or kilns. "Up to 50 members" lets them place themselves in a tier right away.

**3. Pro is defined by vague words.**
- "Everything in Basic, plus more" is filler. The list below it already says what's extra.
- "Advanced analytics" doesn't say what they'd see. "See which wheels and kilns are booked most" would work.
- "Priority support" and "Email support" don't say how fast. "Replies within 4 hours" is concrete.

**4. The FAQ answers don't answer anything.**
- "Absolutely. We take security very seriously" is what every site says, so it reassures no one.
- "Our friendly team is here to help" tells me you're friendly instead of showing it.
- "How does it work?" repeats "sign up and start using it."
- "Can I cancel anytime?" skips what they actually worry about: what happens to their data, and whether they get a refund.

**5. Likely missing information.**
- Do you charge payment processing or transaction fees when members pay through Kilnbook? Studios will ask this first.
- Is there annual pricing or a discount?
- What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

## Smaller points

- **Subheading:** "Choose the plan that's right for you" says nothing. Use that line to say who it's for, e.g. "From a six-wheel community studio to a multi-location school."
- **Heading:** "Simple, transparent pricing" is a claim every pricing page makes. The page earns it by being specific, and a "Custom" tier cuts against it.
- **Buttons:** "Select" is passive. Use "Start free trial" on Basic and Pro, which also reinforces your "no credit card" line. Move that line up next to the buttons, since it's your strongest objection-killer and currently sits at the bottom.
- **Enterprise:** "Large organizations" isn't who buys this. Name them: multi-location studios, university art programs, co-ops.
- **Tone:** "Yes!" in the cancel answer is fine once, but the section reads as generic-friendly. Let the studio-specific details carry the personality.

## Stronger FAQ questions

- Can I import my existing member list and bookings?
- Do members need to download an app?
- Can I charge different rates for different kiln loads or firing types?
- What happens to my data if I cancel? Can I export it?
- Are there transaction fees on member payments?
- What happens when my trial ends?
- Can I switch plans later?
