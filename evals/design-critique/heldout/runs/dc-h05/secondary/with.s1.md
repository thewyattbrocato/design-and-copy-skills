While you gather those, these are the usual booking-flow killers, so you can check them yourself:

1. **Too many steps before showing price or availability.** If someone has to create an account or enter a phone number before seeing a time slot, many will leave.
2. **Availability that's wrong or hidden.** Fully booked days shown as open, time zones that don't match the visitor's, or no "next available" option.
3. **Mandatory account creation** for a first-time booking.
4. **Unclear pricing until the last step**, including add-ons, taxes, fees, and the cancellation policy.
5. **Mobile date pickers** that are hard to tap or scroll, and forms that don't trigger the right keyboard.
6. **Dead-end errors**, like "Something went wrong" with no way to recover, or a lost cart on refresh or back-button.
7. **Payment friction**: no Apple Pay or Google Pay, a card form that rejects valid input, or a deposit requirement that isn't explained up front.
8. **Weak confirmation**: no email or text, or a confirmation page with no date, address, or what to bring.
