Each page checks out alone, but the flow as a whole has a few problems, mostly about what the customer knows before they pay.

**1. The cancellation policy appears only after the customer has chosen and is about to pay.** It sits in small print under "Complete purchase" on page 3. Nothing on page 1 or 2 mentions it, so the customer agrees to a non-refundable charge before they see the rule. This is probably what the manager senses. Policy terms that affect money should be shown before the price, with an explicit acknowledgment near the button.

**2. The policy can't be acted on.** The page says cancellations must be at least 24 hours before the slot, but no page mentions how to cancel. The confirmation page doesn't state the deadline either (Sat 14 Nov, 6:00 PM means Fri 13 Nov, 6:00 PM). A customer who wants to cancel has no visible path, and the deadline is never reported to them after purchase.

**3. The sport isn't carried through.** Page 1 has a Tennis/Pickleball filter, but the result shows only "Court 3" with no sport. The summary on page 3 doesn't name the sport either. A customer who selected Pickleball can't confirm from the results or the receipt that they're booking a pickleball court.

**4. Guests are collected but never explained.** Page 2 asks for 0–3 guests with no price or rule attached. If guests are free, say so. If they're charged, the $24 + tax total is wrong. Either way the customer gets no signal and the number doesn't reach the summary.

**5. There's no full review step before payment.** Page 3 shows the court and time, but not the sport, guest count, cancellation deadline, or the email the confirmation will go to. The customer is never shown everything they're agreeing to in one place.

**6. The confirmation is thin.** Page 4 says the reservation "is on its way to your inbox" but shows no court, date, time, amount, or cancellation deadline. If the email is delayed or goes to spam, the customer has nothing to check against.

**7. Smaller inconsistencies:**
- Times are 24-hour on page 1 (18:00–19:00) and 12-hour on page 3 (6:00 PM to 7:00 PM).
- The date on page 3 has no year or time zone.
- Nothing says what happens if the slot is taken during checkout or the card fails.

The math is fine: 8% tax on $24 is $1.92, and the total of $25.92 is correct. Nov 14, 2026 is a Saturday.

**Suggested fixes, in priority order:**
1. Show the cancellation policy on the page 1 result or in the page 2 header, and require a checkbox or explicit acknowledgment before "Complete purchase."
2. Add a booking summary to page 3 with sport, court, date, year, time, guest count, total, and the cancellation deadline as a specific date and time.
3. Show the sport on the result card.
4. Clarify guest pricing, or remove the field if it's not used.
5. On the confirmation page, repeat the booking details and deadline, and add a "Manage booking" or cancel link.
