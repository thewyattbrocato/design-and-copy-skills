I think the problem is that the flow tells you what matters only after you've committed, and it changes voice partway through. Each page is fine alone. The problems show up when you read them in order.

**1. The terms arrive after the decision.**
- Page 1 shows $24. The real total ($25.92, with $1.92 tax) first appears on page 3, after the user has entered personal details.
- The 24-hour no-refund cancellation rule appears only as small print under the pay button. Someone choosing a slot on page 1 never sees it.
- Fix: show the tax-inclusive price on the results card, and put the cancellation rule on page 1 or 2 in normal-size text. You could also show the cancel-by time ("Cancel free until Fri 13 Nov, 6:00 PM") instead of "24 hours before".

**2. The wording shifts from club to online shop.**
- The flow uses "Reserve", "booking", "reservation", "purchase" and "order" for the same thing.
- It starts as "Reserve a court" and ends as "Order confirmed" and "Thanks for your purchase!". That reads like a retail receipt, not a neighborhood club. This is probably the "something's off" feeling.
- Fix: pick one noun ("booking" or "reservation") and use it everywhere. For example, use "Confirm and pay" on the button and "You're booked!" on the confirmation.

**3. Times and dates change format.**
- Page 1 uses `Sat 14 Nov` and `18:00-19:00`. Page 3 uses `Sat 14 November` and `6:00 PM to 7:00 PM`.
- A user who sees 24-hour time on one page and 12-hour on the next may briefly wonder whether they changed something.
- Fix: choose one format and use it on all four pages.

**4. The details thin out as you go.**
- Page 1 has the sport, date, time and court. The page 3 summary drops the sport, so "Court 3" could be either one.
- Page 2 has no summary at all.
- Page 2 collects "Number of guests" but never says whether guests affect the price or what they're for. Page 3 shows no trace of that field.
- Fix: keep the same booking summary, with sport and guest count, at the top of pages 2 and 3.

**5. The confirmation page doesn't confirm much.**
- It gives a reservation number and says an email is "on its way", but it doesn't show the email address.
- It omits the court, date, time, amount paid and cancel-by deadline.
- The only action is "Back to home". There's no way to cancel or change the booking, and no add-to-calendar option.
- Fix: repeat the full booking summary, show the email address used, and add "Manage booking" and "Add to calendar" buttons.

**6. A policy edge case.** A user booking a slot less than 24 hours away can never cancel for a refund. They'd be agreeing to a no-refund booking without being told. A short note such as "This slot starts within 24 hours and can't be refunded" would fix that.

If you can only do a few things, do 1, 2 and 5. They are the most likely cause of the manager's unease and also the most likely cause of disputes and support emails.
