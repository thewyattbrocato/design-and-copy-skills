Each page is fine on its own. The problems show up between pages, and the biggest one is the cancellation policy.

**Likely what's bugging her**

1. **The cancellation policy shows up too late.** It first appears as small print under "Complete purchase". For a booking, "late cancellations are not refunded" is a key term. It belongs on page 1 next to the price, or at least above the payment fields in normal-sized text. It also isn't on the confirmation page, which is where someone would look when they need to cancel.

2. **The price changes between pages.** Page 1 says $24, and page 3 says $25.92 once tax is added. The $1.92 is small, but a price that goes up at the last step feels like a hidden fee. Show the tax-inclusive price on page 1, or say "+ tax".

3. **The wording changes from "reserve" to "buy".** Page 1 says "Reserve a court" and the confirmation says "Your reservation". Pages 3 and 4 say "Complete purchase", "Order confirmed" and "Thanks for your purchase". It reads like a webshop template on top of a booking tool. Using "Book" or "Reserve" throughout would fix it, for example "Confirm booking" and "Booking confirmed".

4. **The guest count goes nowhere.** Page 2 asks for "Number of guests (0-3)", but it doesn't change the price. It also doesn't appear on the payment summary or the confirmation. The heading "Who's playing?" implies you'll be asked about the other players, but you only enter one name. Either use the number (guest fees, capacity) and show it, or remove the field.

**Smaller inconsistencies**

5. **Times and dates are formatted differently.** Page 1 has "Sat 14 Nov" and "18:00-19:00". Page 3 has "Sat 14 November" and "6:00 PM to 7:00 PM". Pick one format for both.

6. **The sport disappears.** It's a filter on page 1, but the results, the payment summary and the confirmation never say Tennis or Pickleball.

7. **The confirmation page says too little.**
   - It gives the reservation number but not the court, time, amount paid or cancellation policy.
   - "Is on its way to your inbox" gives no fallback if the email doesn't arrive.
   - It has no add-to-calendar or cancel/manage link.
   - The only button is "Back to home".

8. **There's no review step.** Payment is the first time all the details appear together, and there's no obvious way to go back and fix them from there.

If you only fix two things, fix #1 and #2. Those are the ones most likely to make a customer feel misled, so they're probably what she's reacting to without being able to name it.
