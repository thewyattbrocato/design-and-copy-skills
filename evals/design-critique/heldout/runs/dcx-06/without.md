The pages work individually, but across the flow the club makes its commitments too late and describes the transaction inconsistently. I only have the text, so I couldn't check layout, emphasis or behavior.

**What probably bugs her**

1. **The price changes.** Page 1 says $24, and the real total ($25.92) first appears on page 3. The tax is correct (8% of $24), but the person has already picked a court and entered their details by then. Show "$25.92 incl. tax" on the results.

2. **The cancellation policy shows up last.** It's the most consequential rule, and it appears only as small print under the pay button. It should be on page 1 or in the page 3 summary box, in normal-size text. It's also a no-refund rule, so a user who misses it has no recourse. The confirmation page and email should repeat it.

3. **The confirmation reads like a retail receipt.** "Order confirmed", "Thanks for your purchase" and "on its way to your inbox" tell the user almost nothing about the booking. It doesn't restate:
   - the court, date and time
   - the amount charged
   - which email address it was sent to
   - how to cancel or change the booking
   - the cancellation deadline

   The only action is "Back to home". Someone who closes the tab can't prove what they booked.

4. **The same thing has five names:** "Reserve a court", "booking", "reservation", "order" and "purchase". The page 1 heading says reserve, the button says "Complete purchase", and the confirmation says "Order confirmed". Pick one or two terms, such as "Book" and "Booking confirmed".

5. **Formats change from page to page:**
   - 18:00 on page 1 becomes 6:00 PM on page 3.
   - "Sat 14 Nov" becomes "Sat 14 November".
   - "$24" becomes "$24.00".

   None of these is wrong, but together they make it feel unfinished. Use one time format, one date format and one price format.

**Smaller gaps**

- **Sport:** it's a filter on page 1, but it's never shown again. The results card, the payment summary and the confirmation should all say "Pickleball" or "Tennis". "Court 3" alone is ambiguous if courts are shared or converted.
- **Guests:** page 2 collects a guest count, but it never appears in the payment summary and doesn't change the price. If guests are free, say so. If there's a cap or a fee, it's currently invisible.
- **Slot hold:** nothing says the slot is held while the user pays, and nothing handles someone else taking it. A short "held for 10:00" timer and a failure state would cover both.
- **Page 2:** "Who's playing?" asks only for the booker's name, not the guests'. That's probably fine, but the heading promises more than the form asks for.

**Suggested priority:** all-in price on page 1, the cancellation policy in the payment summary, a full-detail confirmation with a cancel link, and one consistent vocabulary. The first three involve money or commitment, so they most likely explain the manager's unease.
