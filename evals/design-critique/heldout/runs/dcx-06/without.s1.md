The problem is that the pages are fine on their own but don't agree with each other. Each page was checked against itself, and the flow as a whole wasn't. These are the main gaps, in rough order of how much they probably bother the manager.

**1. The cancellation policy shows up too late.**
It's small print under the "Complete purchase" button, so people see it only after they've decided to pay. A no-refund rule within 24 hours should appear on page 1 or 2, and be clear on page 3. It's also vague: "24 hours before your slot" makes the customer work out the deadline. Say "Free cancellation until Fri 13 Nov, 6:00 PM" instead.

**2. The price changes between pages.**
Page 1 says $24, and page 3 says $25.92 once tax is added. It's a small jump, but it's the classic "surprise at checkout" pattern. Show the tax-inclusive price, or "$24 + tax", on page 1.

**3. Information is collected and then dropped.**
- **Sport:** it's the first choice on page 1 and never appears again. The payment summary and confirmation just say "Court 3".
- **Guests:** page 2 asks for the number of guests, but they appear nowhere afterward. It's unclear whether they affect price or capacity, or why the club wants the count.
- **Name:** page 2 collects it, but the confirmation doesn't use it ("Thanks for your purchase!").

**4. The wording changes at each step.**
The pages go from "Reserve a court" to "Your booking" to "Complete purchase" to "Order confirmed" to "Thanks for your purchase!" and "reservation". It reads like a shop checkout bolted onto a booking. This is probably the vague feeling the manager has. Use "reservation" or "booking" throughout, with a button like "Pay and confirm booking" and a heading like "Booking confirmed".

**5. The date and time formats don't match.**
Page 1 uses "Sat 14 Nov" and "18:00-19:00". Page 3 uses "Sat 14 November" and "6:00 PM to 7:00 PM". Pick one format for both.

**6. The confirmation page is thin.**
It gives a reservation number and nothing else. It should repeat these details:
- sport, court, date and time
- total paid
- the email address it was sent to
- the cancellation deadline and how to cancel
- an "Add to calendar" option
- a way to view or manage the booking, not just "Back to home"

**Smaller points to check:**
- Is there any hold on the court while someone enters payment details? What happens if someone else books it first?
- Can people go back and edit from page 3?
- Is "Who's playing?" a good heading for a form that only asks for one name and a count?

If I had to fix three things first, I'd pick the cancellation policy placement (1), the price jump (2), and the confirmation page (6). Those are the ones most likely to cause disputes and support emails.
