Each page passes on its own. The problems are in what carries over from one page to the next, and in the voice of the whole flow.

**1. Details you enter or choose don't carry forward**
- **Sport:** the user picks Tennis or Pickleball on page 1. It never appears again, so the payment summary and confirmation say only "Court 3".
- **Guests:** page 2 asks for 0-3 guests. That number is missing from the payment summary and the confirmation. The user can't tell whether guests affect the price or whether the number was saved.
- **Name and email:** the confirmation says "your inbox" without naming the address. The user can't spot a typo.

**2. The price changes at the end**
- Page 1 shows $24. Tax ($1.92) first appears on page 3, so the total is $25.92. It's a small jump, but it's the kind people notice. Show "$24 + tax" or the all-in price on page 1.

**3. The cancellation policy is shown too late**
- It's small print under the "Complete purchase" button, after the user has committed. It appears nowhere on page 1.
- The confirmation doesn't repeat it and gives no way to cancel or change the booking.
- A booking made less than 24 hours ahead is non-refundable the moment it's paid. The flow never says so.

**4. The confirmation is thin**
- It has no court, date, time, sport, amount paid or cancel link. The user sees only a reservation number.
- The only next step is "Back to home".

**5. The wording is inconsistent and reads like a shop**
- The flow calls the same thing a reservation, booking, order and purchase ("Reserve a court", "Complete purchase", "Order confirmed", "Your reservation").
- For a neighborhood club, "Thanks for your purchase!" and "Order confirmed" feel like a retail checkout. "You're booked" would suit it better, and the button could read "Pay and book".

**6. Date and time formats don't match**
- Page 1 uses "Sat 14 Nov" and "18:00". Page 3 uses "Sat 14 November" and "6:00 PM to 7:00 PM".
- Pick one format, ideally 12-hour with the abbreviated date, and use it everywhere.

**7. Page 2 has no booking context**
- The user can't see which court or time they're booking while typing their details. "Who's playing?" also suggests the form collects player names, but it only collects the booker's name and a guest count.

**Fix first:** carry a persistent booking summary (sport, court, date, time, guests, price with tax) through every page. Move the cancellation policy above the pay button. Then make the confirmation a proper receipt with a cancel link. My guess is this is what's bugging the manager: the flow never feels like one booking being built up. It feels like four unrelated forms.
