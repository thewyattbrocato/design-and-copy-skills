Use a teal-tinted off-white like **#f6f9f9** for page backgrounds and a deep teal-gray like **#1b2a2b** for body text. That keeps the neutrals in the same hue family as your brand, and I estimate body text at about 13:1 contrast (I didn't run a checker). Your teal on that background comes to roughly 4.7:1, so it also passes for links and buttons.
