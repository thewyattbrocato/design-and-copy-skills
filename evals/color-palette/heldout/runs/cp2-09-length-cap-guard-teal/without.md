Use a slightly cool, near-white like #f6f9f9 for backgrounds and a deep blue-slate like #1c2b2d (teal-tinted charcoal) for body text, which keeps contrast high (~14:1) and harmonizes with the teal. Avoid pure white and pure black, and reserve #0f7c80 for links, buttons, and accents (it passes AA on the light background at about 5musics:1).
