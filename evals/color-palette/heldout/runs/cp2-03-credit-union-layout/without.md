**Pick: deep forest green.** It fits the name (Fernhollow), and it reads as steady and local. Navy is the default for banks, so a green credit union stands out from them. Plum is distinctive but feels more lifestyle than money, and it gets tricky when you need status colors. Choose a green dark enough to pass contrast with white text. Something near `#1F4D3A` works. Add one warm accent, such as a muted gold or amber, for small highlights.

**Where color should show up**

- **Hero:** This is the strongest use of color. Use a green field, or a photo with a green overlay, with white headline text. Make the **Join today** button the one high-contrast element. Fill it with the warm accent and dark text, so it's the only thing on the page that looks like that. A green button on a green hero would blend in.
- **Rates strip:** Keep the background a very pale green tint or plain white. Show the rate numbers in dark neutral or deep green, and keep them large. The color should help people scan, not decorate. Use a thin green rule or label to separate loans from savings.
- **Calculator tiles:** Use white or off-white tiles with a neutral border. Color only the icon and the tile's call to action, in green. All three tiles should look the same. Don't give each its own hue.
- **Testimonials:** Mostly neutral. Use dark gray text on white or warm off-white. A small green quote mark or a thin green accent line is enough. Photos and the members' words should carry this section.
- **Footer:** Make it a very dark green, close to black, rather than pure charcoal. That ties it back to the brand. Use off-white text, and use the accent only for links on hover or focus. Branch hours need strong contrast, because people look for them in a hurry.

**Where color shouldn't show up**

- **Body text and long paragraphs.** Keep these neutral, and don't tint them green.
- **Every heading.** Use green for a few anchor points, not for all of them.
- **Rate numbers as good/bad signals.** Don't use red and green to imply meaning. Green is your brand color, so it would be confusing.
- **Backgrounds on every section.** Alternate white and a faint tint. Full-color bands in every section make the page look heavy and weaken the hero.
- **The accent color beyond the CTA and small highlights.** If gold shows up everywhere, the Join button stops standing out.

**Rule of thumb:** About 70% of the page should be white or neutral, about 20% green, and about 10% accent. If you can only remember one thing, use the green in the hero and footer, keep the middle neutral, and save the accent for the button.
