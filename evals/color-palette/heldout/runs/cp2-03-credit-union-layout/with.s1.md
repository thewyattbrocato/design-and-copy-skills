**Pick: deep forest green, `#1F4D3A`.**

The name is Fernhollow, so green is the one color that belongs to the brand rather than to the category. Navy is what every bank and credit union already uses, so it makes you look like the bank you're an alternative to. Plum reads as warm and distinctive, but it says less about money and trust. Green says growth and stability, and it suits a member-owned local institution.

**Palette**

| Role | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lead green | `#1F4D3A` | White text on it: about 9.6:1 |
| Page ground | `#F7F5EF` | Warm off-white. Green on it: about 8.8:1 |
| Rate strip tint | `#E3ECE6` | Pale green-gray |
| Ink (text) | `#17211C` | Green-tinted near-black |
| Footer ground | `#14211B` | Text `#E8EFEA` on it: about 14:1 |
| Action accent | `#E8B04A` | Warm gold, used only for Join today. Dark text `#14211B` on it: about 8.5:1. The gold against the green hero is about 4.9:1. |

I couldn't run a contrast checker here, so all ratios are hand-calculated estimates. Re-run them before you ship.

**Where color shows up**

- **Hero:** This is the one place green fills a large area. Use a solid `#1F4D3A` ground with white headline text. The Join today button is gold with dark text, the only gold on the page, so it's the clear next step. Don't use a gradient or a photo wash.
- **Rate strip:** Use the pale `#E3ECE6` band with the rate numbers in ink. Make the numbers large and the labels small. Keep the numbers out of green so they read as data. A thin green rule or label is enough.
- **Calculator tiles:** Use white or cream cards with a hairline border. The only green is the icon and the link or arrow. Don't fill the tiles with color. Three filled tiles would compete with the hero.
- **Testimonials:** Keep this row neutral: ink on cream. At most, add a small green quote mark or name rule. Don't use color to decorate it, because it should feel like real people rather than marketing.
- **Footer:** Use the green-black ground with off-white text. Branch hours are plain text. Green links are fine only if they're lightened to about `#9CC7AE` (roughly 8:1 on the footer). Don't put gold here.

**Where color should not show up**

- **Success states:** Don't use the brand green for "approved" or "rate went down." Use a separate, more yellow or bluer success green with a check icon and a word, so brand and status stay distinct.
- **Errors:** Use a red with an icon and text, never the brand color.
- **Body text and long copy:** Keep it ink, not green.
- **Every heading:** Don't make them green. Green on the hero, small accents, and icons is enough. If it's everywhere, the hero stops standing out.
- **Gold:** Don't use it for anything but the primary action.

**Notes**
- I assumed no existing brand color. If Fernhollow already has a logo color, I'd match the green to it.
- The trade-off is that a green hero plus a dark green footer bookends the page heavily. That's intentional, and the cream middle keeps the rates and calculators easy to read.
