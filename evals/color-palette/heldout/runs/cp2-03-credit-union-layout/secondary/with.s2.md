**Main color: deep forest green, `#1F4D3A`.**

Credit unions are member-owned and local, and forest green signals stability and community without copying the navy every large bank uses. Plum reads as novelty or luxury, which doesn't fit a savings-and-loans brand.

**Palette**

| Role | Value | Use | Contrast (estimated by hand) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead | `#1F4D3A` | Join button fill, headings, rate numbers | White on lead ≈ 9.6:1 |
| Page ground | `#F7F8F5` | Hero, testimonials, page background | Body text `#4A5750` on it ≈ 7.1:1 |
| Tint band | `#E9F0EB` | Rate strip | Lead on tint ≈ 8.3:1 |
| Footer ground | `#17372A` | Footer | `#E8EFEA` text ≈ 11:1; secondary `#B9CCC0` ≈ 7.7:1 |
| Card | `#FFFFFF` with `#D9E0DA` border | Calculator tiles | Lead on white ≈ 9.6:1 |
| Accent (small) | `#B7791F` | Focus rings and one thin rule only | ≈ 3.6:1 on white, passes the 3:1 non-text floor; fails as text |

I computed these by hand, so treat them as estimates and re-measure in your tooling before shipping.

**Where color shows**

- **Hero:** Light ground with a lead-green headline and one filled Join today button. This is the only solid color block above the fold.
- **Rate strip:** Tint band, lead-green rate numbers, neutral labels. Show direction with an arrow and the word "up" or "down", not color.
- **Calculator tiles:** White cards with a neutral border. Each tile gets a lead-green title and a text link with an underline. The links are not filled buttons, so the hero button stays the main action.
- **Testimonials:** Mostly neutral. Keep the quote text dark gray and any avatars grayscale. Use visible placeholders until you have real member quotes; don't invent them.
- **Footer:** Dark green ground, light text, hours in `#E8EFEA`, secondary text in the hue-matched tint `#B9CCC0`. Links are underlined.
- **Focus rings everywhere:** The accent `#B7791F`.

**Where color should not show**

- Not on the hero background. A full green hero next to a green footer makes the page heavy.
- Not as a second filled button color in the tiles or footer. Only the hero button is filled.
- Not in red or green for rates or status. Use arrows, words, or sign.
- Not in a different hue per tile or section. That creates the five-accent problem.
- Not as gold or translucent white text on green. Gold fails as text, and translucent white looks disabled.
- No gradients.

**Notes**

- Your rates and branch hours are the most important content on the page, so keep their numbers in the lead green and at full text size.
