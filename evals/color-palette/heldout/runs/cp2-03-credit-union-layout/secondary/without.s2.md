**Pick: deep forest green.** It fits a member-owned institution: local, stable, and a little less corporate than navy, which nearly every bank uses. Plum is distinctive, but it reads as lifestyle or beauty brand and is harder to keep trustworthy at small sizes. Start around `#1F4D3A` and check that white text on it reaches at least 7:1 contrast.

**Where color should show**

- **Hero:** Use the green as the background, or as a green overlay on a photo. This is the one place the brand color should dominate.
- **Join today button:** Use a brighter or warmer accent than the hero background, so the button stands out against green. If the hero is solid green, a white or cream button with green text can work better than a second green.
- **Calculator tiles:** Use green for the heading, a thin top border, or the icon. Use it for focus rings and the result figure. Keep the tile surfaces white or off-white.
- **Rate strip:** Keep the numbers in near-black text. Use green only for the small labels or a thin rule. Don't color rates green or red to show movement, because brand green would then also mean "up" or "good."
- **Links and form focus states:** Use green here as well, so the color signals interaction consistently.

**Where color should not show**

- **Testimonials:** Keep the cards neutral (white or a pale warm gray). Don't put green behind quotes, since it competes with the text. A small green quote mark or member name is enough.
- **Calculator inputs and body copy:** Keep these neutral. Color on form fields makes them harder to scan.
- **Rate numbers:** Keep them neutral for the reasons above.
- **Footer:** The footer is already dark, so don't use the same green. Use a near-black green or charcoal and keep the branch hours in light neutral text. Use a single thin green rule or the logo for the brand link. A full green footer makes the page feel heavy and makes the bottom of the page look like another hero.
- **Section backgrounds:** Use white and one light neutral. Avoid alternating green and white bands.

**Rule of thumb:** Green marks where to act and what the brand is. Everything the member reads, including rates, quotes, and numbers, stays neutral.
