Every penny goes to the roof fund

All proceeds from the sale go toward repairing the church roof. Whether you take home a loaf or a whole pie, thank you for helping us keep the parish warm and dry.

Want to bake with us?

Volunteers are warmly welcome to bring a dish. Please drop it off at the church hall by Friday evening so we can set up the tables in time.

Cakes, breads and pies are all wonderful. Whatever your family recipe is, we'd love to have it.
