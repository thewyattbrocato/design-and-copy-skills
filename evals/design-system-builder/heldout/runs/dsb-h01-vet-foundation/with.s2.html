Design foundation · v0.1

Calm, clear, and kind to anxious owners.

Everything on this page is built from the same small set of tokens. Build new screens from these roles and components; if something is missing, propose it (see Changing the system) instead of inventing a local value.

Principles

Warm, not playful

Soft neutrals, one teal accent, rounded corners. No mascots, gradients, or exclamation marks in UI copy. People come here when a pet is unwell or a bill is due.

Say it plainly

Status is always words plus a shape, never color alone. Errors say what happened and what to do next.

Easy on a phone

Targets are at least 44px. Body text is 16px. Forms are a single column on small screens. Everything works with keyboard and screen reader.

Color roles

Components use roles, never raw ramp values. Light and dark themes remap the same role names to different values; use the toggle above (it also follows your system setting). Text pairs below were checked by hand for at least 4.5:1 in both themes.

Space, shape, elevation, motion

Short scales. If a design needs a value between two steps, pick the nearer step; do not add one.

Spacing (8 steps)

space-1 4
space-2 8
space-3 12
space-4 16
space-5 24
space-6 32
space-7 48
space-8 64

Inside a component: 8–16. Between related items: 8–16. Between groups: 24–32. Between page sections: 48–64.

Radius (3 + pill)

sm 4
md 8
lg 14
pill

sm: small inner marks. md: buttons, inputs, alerts. lg: cards and panels. pill: badges.

Elevation (2 + flat)

flat
raised
overlay

Raised for cards that can be acted on; overlay only for menus and dialogs.

Motion (3 durations, 3 easings)

Hover or focus a track to preview. Motion confirms a change; it never decorates. All of it is reduced to near-instant when the user prefers reduced motion.

duration-fast 120ms
duration-base 200ms
duration-slow 320ms

Easings: ease-standard for movement within the screen, ease-enter for things appearing, ease-exit for things leaving. Fast: hover and press. Base: expanding and messages. Slow: page-level transitions.

Badge & status message

Confirmed Needs confirmation Cancelled Reminder sent

Badges name state in words; the dot is secondary. Status messages have four tones and always a plain-language title.

Do use var(--text-secondary) for hints and metadata, and var(--space-4) for padding inside a card.

Don’t write color:#4a5753 or padding:15px. Raw values break dark mode and drift out of the scale.

Changing the system

Small on purpose: this page and the token block at the top of the stylesheet are the single source of truth. Owners and review dates are to be filled in by the team.

ChangeHow
AddA pattern needed by a second screen joins the system. It must read semantic roles, document every state, and list edge content (long names, empty values, small screens).
ModifyChange the tier-2 mapping or the component here once; every screen follows. Check text pairs in light and dark.
DeprecateMark in this page and the changelog, name the replacement, and give a migration window before removal.
RemoveOnly after nothing uses it. Owner: [assign]
Local exceptionIf a screen truly needs a one-off value, keep it local and comment it /* exception: reason */. Exceptions are reviewed, not copied.

Checklist for any new screen

  • No hex codes, pixel sizes, or radii outside the token block.
  • Works in light and dark, at 320px wide, and with keyboard only.
  • Status is communicated in words, not color alone.
  • Every interactive element has hover, focus, disabled, and (where relevant) loading and error states.
  • Components named for what they are (appointment-card), not where they live.
