How we build

Four rules keep screens consistent no matter who builds them.

  1. Tokens only. Never write a raw color, size or radius in a component. Use var(--…). Need something new? Add a token first.
  2. Never color alone. Status always has a text label and an icon or marker. Owners may be stressed, color-blind or on a bright phone screen.
  3. Plain, kind language. Say what happened and what to do next. No blame, no jargon, no exclamation marks.
  1. Easy to hit, easy to read. 44px minimum targets, 17px body text, 4.5:1 text contrast, and a visible focus ring on everything interactive.
  2. Primary action once. One filled button per view. Everything else is secondary or quiet.
