Principles

Calm, clear, and caring. Friendly in tone, precise in information. Pet owners are often anxious, so we state what happens next, use plain language, and never rely on color alone to convey meaning.

Clear first

One primary action per screen. Dates, times, and names are shown in full.

Trustworthy

Neutral, restrained color. Confirmations state exactly what was saved and when.

Accessible by default

44px touch targets, visible focus, AA contrast, and labels on every input.

Typography

System font stack only, so pages load instantly and read native on every device. Times and dates use tabular numerals so columns line up.

--font-size-2xl · semibold · page title Your pet's care, in one place
--font-size-xl · semibold · section Upcoming appointments
--font-size-lg · semibold · card title Biscuit's annual wellness exam
--font-size-md · regular · body Please arrive ten minutes early so we can check Biscuit in.
--font-size-sm · regular · supporting Bring any current medication and your vaccination record.
--font-size-xs · semibold · labels Next visit · Thu 15 Oct · 9:30 AM

Spacing, shape, elevation

Buttons

One primary button per view. Use secondary for alternatives and ghost for low-emphasis actions. Destructive actions use danger and always confirm first.

Status messages and badges

Every message pairs a color with an icon and a text label. Errors use role="alert"; confirmations and info use role="status".
