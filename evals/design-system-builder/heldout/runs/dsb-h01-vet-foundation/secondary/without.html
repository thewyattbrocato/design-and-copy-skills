Design foundation

Every screen in the app is built from these tokens and components. If a page needs something that isn't here, add it to this file first, then use it. Don't write one-off colours, sizes, or spacing.

Voice and tone

  • Calm and specific. "Your appointment is confirmed for Tuesday, 13 October at 9:30 AM." Not "Yay, you're all set!"
  • Warm, not cute. We care about pets and their people. We don't use exclamation marks, emoji, or playful copy in clinical flows.
  • Plain language. Say "vaccine booster" not "immunoprophylaxis." Use medical terms only when the owner needs them, and explain them.
  • Tell people what happens next. Every error and confirmation should say what the person can do now.

Typography

Font: system UI stack (--font-sans). No web fonts, so pages load fast and feel native on every device.

Radius and elevation

Radius: sm 6 · md 10 (inputs, buttons) · lg 16 (cards). Shadows: --shadow-1 at rest, --shadow-2 on overlays only.

Minimum touch target: 44px (--target-min).

Buttons

One primary action per view. Use verbs that say what happens: "Book appointment", not "Submit".

Status messages

Use role="status" for confirmations and updates, and role="alert" for errors that need attention.

Appointment confirmed
Biscuit is booked for Tuesday, 13 October at 9:30 AM. We've sent a confirmation to your email.
Vaccine booster due soon
Biscuit's rabies booster is due on 20 October. Book before then to keep vaccinations current.
Clinic closed on Monday
We're closed for staff training on Monday, 12 October. Urgent care line: (555) 010-0199.
We couldn't book that time
The 9:30 AM slot was just taken. Choose another time below, and we'll hold your other details.

Before you add a new screen

  • Use tokens for every colour, font size, space, and radius.
  • Every input has a visible label. Hints and errors are linked with aria-describedby.
  • Every interactive element can be reached by keyboard and shows the focus ring.
  • Touch targets are at least 44px.
  • Status is shown with text and icon, never colour alone.
  • Copy follows the voice rules above.
