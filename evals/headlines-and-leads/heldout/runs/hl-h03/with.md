**Option 1: outcome-first**
**Headline:** Project management built for small architecture firms
**Subhead:** Track phases, deadlines, and consultants in one place. Plumbline is in beta with 14 firms, and pricing is simple.

**Option 2: social proof you can defend**
**Headline:** Small architecture firms are already running their projects on Plumbline
**Subhead:** 14 firms are in our beta. Join them and get early-access pricing. [add the real offer]

**Option 3: scarcity that helps you**
**Headline:** Join the first firms shaping Plumbline
**Subhead:** We're in beta with 14 architecture firms. Early members get [real perk] and a direct line to the team building it.

Option 2 is the one I'd use for investors. It's accurate, and it shows early traction without hiding the stage. If any of the 14 will let you quote them, a named firm or a one-line quote under the headline will do more for you than a big number.
