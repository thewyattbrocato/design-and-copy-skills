I'd skip "ONLY 3 LEFT" and "ends tonight" because they're false. CAN-SPAM prohibits deceptive subject lines, and the FTC treats fake scarcity and fake deadlines as deceptive. They also hurt a small shop, because customers who notice the sale is still running tomorrow stop trusting your urgency. You can still get urgency from the real deadline.

Here are 8 subject lines that use it:

1. Spring sale: 15% off every lock, through April 30
2. Your bike's been outside all winter. Lock it up for 15% less.
3. 15% off all Tumbler Locks ends April 30
4. Spring riding season is here. Is your lock ready?
5. The Tumbler Locks spring sale is on: 15% off everything
6. Don't wait for May: 15% off locks ends April 30
7. A better lock for spring, 15% off
8. Last month of the spring sale: 15% off all locks

If you want to create more urgency over time, send a reminder email in the last week of April with a subject like "Final days: 15% off locks ends April 30" or "Ends Thursday." That's true, and it tends to perform well.

If you ever do have low stock on a specific lock, "Only 3 left" is fine for that product, as long as it's accurate.
