I'd skip "ONLY 3 LEFT" and "ends tonight." Both are false, and CAN-SPAM prohibits deceptive subject lines, so they put you at legal risk. They also cost you trust with repeat customers when people notice the sale is still running tomorrow. You do have real urgency, though: a hard end date and a clear discount.

Here are 8 that stay truthful:

1. Spring sale: 15% off every Tumbler Lock
2. Lock it up for spring: 15% off through April 30
3. 15% off all locks ends April 30
4. Your bike deserves a better lock (15% off)
5. Spring riding season is here. Lock up for 15% less.
6. Last day of April = last day for 15% off
7. Every lock, 15% off, all month long
8. Don't wait for May: 15% off Tumbler Locks

Some notes on using them:
- **Real urgency:** Send a reminder in the last week of April with a subject like "Final days: 15% off Tumbler Locks." A real deadline usually gets a strong response.
- **Best bets:** I'd A/B test #2 against #3. They state the date plainly, which is what the "ends tonight" tactic is trying to do.
- **Honest scarcity:** If a specific model really is low on stock, "Only 3 left" is fine for that one product. Use it only when it's true.
