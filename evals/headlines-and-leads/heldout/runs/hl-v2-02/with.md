I'd skip "ONLY 3 LEFT" and "ends tonight." You have plenty of stock and the sale runs all month, so both are false claims. A misleading subject line can also break CAN-SPAM rules. Repeat customers who notice the sale is still on the next day will stop trusting your deadlines. You do have a real deadline (April 30) and a real discount, and you can build urgency from those.

Here are 8 subject lines:

1. Spring sale: 15% off every Tumbler Lock
2. 15% off all locks through April 30
3. Your bike's spring upgrade: a better lock, 15% off
4. Ride season's here. Lock it up for 15% less.
5. April only: 15% off all Tumbler Locks
6. Sale ends April 30: 15% off every lock
7. Still using that old cable lock? Now's the time (15% off)
8. 15% off locks, all month long

**My picks:** #2 and #6 state the offer and the real deadline, which is the honest version of what your cousin's tips do. #7 is a good one to A/B test against them if your list is big enough.

For real urgency later in the month, you can send a reminder around April 25 with something like "5 days left: 15% off all locks." That's true, and it should get the same bump as "ends tonight."
