I left out "ONLY 3 LEFT" and "ends tonight." Both are false for your sale, and a deceptive subject line can break CAN-SPAM. It's also a fast way to lose the trust of a small shop's customers, who will notice when the sale is still running tomorrow. Real urgency can work nearly as well, and yours is true: the sale has a hard end date.

1. Spring sale: 15% off every Tumbler Lock
2. 15% off all locks, through April 30
3. Your bike deserves a better lock (15% off)
4. Spring's here. Lock it up for 15% less.
5. April only: 15% off all Tumbler Locks
6. Don't wait until May to pay full price
7. 15% off locks ends April 30. Ride safe.
8. Ride more this spring, worry less (15% off)

Number 6 is the closest to your cousin's style, since it creates pressure without lying. If you want a countdown feel, you can send a second email in the last week of April with a subject like "Last week: 15% off all locks" or "Final day: 15% off ends tonight." Those become true on April 30.
