I left out "ONLY 3 LEFT" and "ends tonight". Neither is true for your sale, and a false claim in a subject line can count as a deceptive subject line under CAN-SPAM. It also teaches your customers that your deadlines don't mean anything. You can still get urgency from true facts, like the 15% and the April 30 end date.

1. Spring sale: 15% off every Tumbler Lock
2. 15% off all locks through April 30
3. Your bike deserves a better lock (now 15% off)
4. Spring riding season is here. Lock it down for 15% less.
5. The Tumbler Locks spring sale is on
6. Sale ends April 30: 15% off every lock
7. Stop worrying about your bike. 15% off locks this month.
8. Spring sale, 15% off. Get your lock before the month is out.

Two ideas for real urgency:
- **Send a reminder in the last few days of April.** "Ends tonight" or "Last day: 15% off locks" is honest then, and it usually works well.
- **Use real stock numbers if a model is running low.** "Only 3 left" is fine if it's true. It's effective because it's true.
