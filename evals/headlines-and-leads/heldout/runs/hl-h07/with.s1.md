- Option 1 or 2 fits people searching tonight. Both put the daily status first. Option 3 leads with the season and the exemptions, so it fits better if the page carries the status in a separate banner.
- The exemptions and the season dates don't fit in a short subhead without making it a list. Put them in the first lines of the body, or use Option 3 if you want them at the top.
- The 3 pm posting time is in Options 1 and 2. In October the season hasn't started, so you may want a one-line "no restrictions until November 1" note on the page now.
