I won't write "Trusted by 50,000 families" or an invented dietitian quote. You have 212 beta users, so the first is false. A made-up quote from a "registered dietitian" is a fabricated endorsement. Both can get you in trouble:

- **App Store review:** Apple rejects apps for misleading claims, and fake testimonials are a common reason.
- **Legal exposure:** In the US, the FTC treats fabricated endorsements and inflated user counts as deceptive advertising.
- **Credibility:** Health-adjacent claims get checked more than you'd expect.
