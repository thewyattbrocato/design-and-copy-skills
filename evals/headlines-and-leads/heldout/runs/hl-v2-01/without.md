**Headline options**

1. **Three glazes. One card. No more guessing.**
2. **Test three new glazes every month, before you commit to a bucket.**
3. **See how it fires before you buy the jar.**

**Subhead options**

1. Every month, Kilnbox mails you three glazes on test tiles, plus a card showing how each one fires at cone 6.
2. Three test-tile glazes and a cone 6 results card, delivered monthly for hobby potters who'd rather see it fired than imagine it.
3. Monthly test tiles of three glazes, with a cone 6 firing card for each. Find your next favorite without buying a full jar.

**My pick:** Headline 3 with subhead 1. The headline names the real benefit (skipping an expensive glaze gamble), and the subhead states exactly what arrives in the box. That means a first-time visitor understands the product in about five seconds.

Two caveats:
- "Before you buy the jar" assumes the tiles are meant to help people decide on purchases. If you'd rather lean on discovery and fun, headline 2 is closer to that.
- If the tiles are already fired and glazed samples, say so in the subhead ("fired test tiles"). If they're raw tiles for people to glaze themselves, the copy should say that instead, since it changes what people expect.
