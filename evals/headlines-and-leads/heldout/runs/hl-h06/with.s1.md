I'd keep option 4 or use option 1. The original already names the reader's situation in their own words, and a headline that works shouldn't be rewritten just because it sounds plain. What it lacks is the mechanism, which a subhead can supply. Option 1 is the stronger rewrite because it opens with the reader's own statement and leaves the "how" to the next line.

If you want to settle it, A/B test option 1 against 4 and judge on bank-connection rate, not clicks. Connecting a bank is the step where people hesitate, so it's the number that matters.
