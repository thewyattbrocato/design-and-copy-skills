4. Freelancing for designers for years. Free webinar Thursday.
5. Ready to raise your rates? Join free this Thursday.

**Quote slot:** "[Real attendee quote]" – [first name, role]

I left out "Only 12 seats left, closes tonight" and the "tripled my rates" quote. The seat cap doesn't exist, and the quote isn't from a real person. Both are fabricated claims, and Meta's ad policies prohibit false urgency and fake testimonials. Ads that get reported can be rejected, and repeat violations can get the ad account restricted. People who sign up because of a false deadline also tend to skip the webinar.

- **Honest urgency:** the event is already time-limited. Use "Thursday 7pm ET" and "Free live session", and add "Registration closes Thursday at 6pm" only if you'll really close it then.
- **Testimonials:** if you've run this before, ask a past attendee for a line and permission to use it. If this is the first run, use a line about your own track record that you can back up.
