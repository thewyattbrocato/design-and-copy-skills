Here are some options.

**Straightforward**
- Pickup changes at Greenfold Market: new weekday hours, no more Saturdays
- Greenfold pickup update: weekdays 3-7 pm, Saturday pickup ending
- What's changing with your Greenfold pickup

**Lead with the benefit (wider window)**
- More time to pick up: Greenfold weekday pickup is now 3-7 pm
- A wider pickup window at Greenfold, plus a Saturday change

**Date-focused**
- New Greenfold pickup hours start October 6
- Last Saturday pickup is October 4: new weekday hours follow

**Reassuring**
- Pickup is changing, but ordering and prices aren't
- Greenfold pickup update: same ordering, same prices, new hours

My pick is **"Greenfold pickup update: weekdays 3-7 pm, Saturday pickup ending."** It covers both changes, so members who only skim the subject line still get the news, and it's clear without being alarming. If you expect pushback on losing Saturdays, the "same ordering, same prices" option sets a calmer tone.

Your session date shows October 7, so the October 6 start and October 4 final Saturday may already be past. If so, the email is going out after the change. In that case I'd use present-tense subjects like "Greenfold pickup is now 3-7 pm on weekdays" instead of the future-leaning ones. If the send date is earlier, the options above work as written.
