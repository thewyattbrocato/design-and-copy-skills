Here are subject line options for the Greenfold Market pickup email.

**Clear and direct**
- New pickup hours at Greenfold Market, starting Oct 6
- Greenfold pickup is changing: new weekday hours, no more Saturdays
- Pickup update: weekdays 3-7 pm, Saturday pickup ending

**Lead with the benefit**
- More time to pick up: weekday pickup now runs 3-7 pm
- Pick up on your schedule: wider weekday hours at Greenfold

**Lead with the Saturday change**
- Last Saturday pickup is Oct 4
- Heads up: Saturday pickup ends after Oct 4

**Reassuring**
- Pickup changes at Greenfold (ordering and prices stay the same)
- New pickup hours, same easy ordering

**Shortest**
- Our pickup hours are changing
- Greenfold pickup: what's new Oct 6

**My pick:** "Pickup update: weekdays 3-7 pm, Saturday pickup ending." It states both changes up front, so members who rely on Saturdays see it right away. If you want a softer tone, use "More time to pick up: weekday pickup now runs 3-7 pm," and put the Saturday news at the top of the email body.

Two other things:
- **Preview text:** "Orders are still due by 9 pm the night before, and nothing else is changing" would work well, since it answers the first question members will have.
- **Dates:** October 4 falls on a Saturday in 2025, but my system date shows October 7, 2026, when both dates would already be past. If this is going out before the change, check that the dates and wording are right, because "starting Oct 6" and "after Oct 4" would read oddly otherwise.
