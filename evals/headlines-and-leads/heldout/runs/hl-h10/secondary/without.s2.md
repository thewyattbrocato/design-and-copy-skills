Here are some subject line options, varied by tone:

**Direct & Clear**
- "Important: Pickup Hours & Saturday Service Changes Starting October 6"
- "Changes Coming to Greenfold Pickup – New Hours, No Saturday Service"

**Friendly & Informational**
- "We're Updating Pickup Hours – Here's What's Changing"
- "Greenfold Update: Extended Weekday Pickup & Schedule Changes"

**Action-Oriented**
- "Plan Ahead: New Pickup Hours & Important Changes for October 6"
- "Heads Up: Pickup Schedule Changes Starting This Week"

**Concise**
- "Pickup Schedule Update – Extended Hours, Saturday Service Ending"
- "Your Pickup Options Are Changing October 6"

**Benefit-Focused**
- "Good News: Extended Pickup Hours Now Available Weekdays"
- "More Flexibility: New Pickup Window for Greenfold Members"

---

I'd lean toward **"Important: Pickup Hours & Saturday Service Changes Starting October 6"** or **"We're Updating Pickup Hours – Here's What's Changing"** depending on whether you want straightforward/urgent or warmer/community-focused. The first works well for people who scan quickly; the second invites them to read more.
