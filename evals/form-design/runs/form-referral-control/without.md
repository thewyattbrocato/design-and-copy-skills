**Radio group.** With only four options, showing them all at once lets users answer in a single click without opening anything. Dropdowns hide the choices and add a click for no benefit at this size.

Two tweaks:
- Add an "Other" option with an optional text field. Otherwise people whose source isn't listed will pick something at random, which skews your data.
- Randomize or sensibly order the options, and keep the question optional so it doesn't add friction to sign-up.

Free text gives richer answers, but it's slow to fill in and messy to analyze. Use it only as the "Other" follow-up.
