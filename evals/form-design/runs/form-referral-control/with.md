**Use a radio group.**

With four options, visible radios let people compare everything at a glance and answer in one click. A dropdown hides the choices behind an extra click and gains nothing at this size.

- **Add "Other" as the fourth option, with an optional text field.** The text field should appear only when "Other" is selected. This captures answers you didn't anticipate without making everyone type.
- **Mark the question optional in the label.** It doesn't block sign-up, and forcing an answer produces junk data.
- **Randomize the order** if you can. Keep "Other" last.
- **Skip pure free text.** The answers are hard to aggregate and cost more effort to give.
