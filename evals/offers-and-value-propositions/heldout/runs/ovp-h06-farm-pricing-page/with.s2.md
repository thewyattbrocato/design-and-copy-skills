# Ledgerleaf pricing

## Bookkeeping that keeps up with the farm

Ledgerleaf is bookkeeping software for small farms. Record sales by market stall or CSA, expenses by field or crop, and mileage as you go. At year end, hand your accountant a summary they can open, as CSV or PDF, instead of a shoebox of receipts.

Try it free for 30 days. No credit card needed.

## Pick a plan

All prices are per month, billed monthly. Pay yearly and the annual price is below.

### Seedling: $15/month
**For a single farm just getting its books in order.**
- 1 farm
- Up to 200 transactions a month
- Sales and expense tracking

Seedling doesn't include per-crop profit reports or the mileage log. Those start on Harvest.

### Harvest: $34/month
**For farms with busy market seasons, or any farm that wants to know which crops pay.**
- Unlimited transactions
- Profit reports per crop
- Mileage log
- 2 users

We suggest Harvest for most farms. It removes the 200-transaction cap, and it's the first plan that shows profit crop by crop.

### Co-op: $79/month
**For farms with a CSA, a larger team, or an accountant who wants direct access.**
- Up to 10 users
- CSA member billing
- A login for your accountant
