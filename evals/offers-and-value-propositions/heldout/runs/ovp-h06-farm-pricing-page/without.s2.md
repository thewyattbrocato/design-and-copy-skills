Ledgerleaf is bookkeeping software built for small farms. Track sales by market stall or CSA. Track expenses by field or crop. Log your mileage. At year-end, Ledgerleaf makes a summary your accountant can open, with CSV and PDF export, so tax season starts with tidy records instead of a shoebox of receipts.

Pick the plan that fits how your farm runs today. You can try it free for 30 days first.

## Frequently asked questions

### How does billing work?
You can pay monthly or annually. Monthly plans are billed each month at the price listed above. Annual billing gives you twelve months for the price of ten. Please note that we don't offer refunds on monthly payments.

### Can I cancel?
Yes, any time. There's no lock-in. You can also export all of your data at any time, whether you're cancelling or just want a copy for your own files.

### What's not included?
Ledgerleaf doesn't do payroll, and it doesn't file taxes. It gives your accountant a clean year-end summary, but the filing itself is up to you and them. We also don't offer phone support. Support is by email, and we answer within one business day.

### Which plan should I start with?
If you're not sure, start the free trial. You'll have all the Harvest features for 30 days, so you can see what you actually use.
