## Pay annually, get two months free.

Bill by the year instead of monthly and pay for 10 months. You'll receive 12 months of service. That's a savings of $24–$94 per year, depending on your plan. You can switch to annual billing anytime.

## Try Harvest free for 30 days.

Start with all Harvest features—unlimited transactions, profit reports, mileage log, and two users—at no cost. No credit card required. If you stay, your first charge will be the price of your chosen plan. You can downgrade, upgrade, or cancel before the trial ends.

## Questions about pricing

**Do you charge differently if I bill monthly vs. annually?**  
Yes. Monthly billing is the standard rate ($15, $34, or $79 per month). Annual billing costs 20% less—you pay for 10 months and receive 12. You can switch between monthly and annual anytime, and the change takes effect on your next billing date.

**Can I cancel anytime?**  
Yes. Cancel whenever you want. Your access continues through the end of your current billing period, and you can export all your data at any time. There's no fee to cancel.

**What if I want to cancel my monthly plan—do I get a refund?**  
No. Monthly payments are not refundable. If you cancel partway through a month, you'll have access through the end of that month.

**What's not included?**  
Ledgerleaf handles bookkeeping: sales, expenses, mileage, and year-end summaries for your accountant. It doesn't handle payroll or tax filing. We offer support by email, answered within one business day. We don't provide phone support.

**How do I get help?**  
Email us. We respond within one business day. Common questions are answered in our help center.

**Can I export my data?**  
Yes, anytime. Export your transactions, reports, and summary data as CSV or PDF to use in other software or share with your accountant.
