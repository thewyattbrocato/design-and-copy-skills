## FAQ

**Billing: What if I switch plans mid-month?**
We'll pro-rate the difference. If you upgrade partway through your billing cycle, you'll only pay for the days you use the new plan. Downgrades take effect on your next billing date.

**Billing: Can I change my billing frequency?**
Yes. Switch between monthly and annual billing anytime from your account settings. We'll adjust your next charge accordingly.

**Cancelling: What happens to my data if I cancel?**
Your data is yours. You can export everything—transactions, reports, mileage logs—as CSV or PDF at any time, whether you're a current customer or not. Cancel your subscription whenever you want; we don't require notice or charge early-termination fees.

**What's not included?**
Ledgerleaf handles bookkeeping and record-keeping, not payroll or tax filing. We also don't offer phone support; all support is by email, answered within one business day. If you need a CPA to prepare your taxes, the accountant login on Co-op lets them access your data directly.

**No refunds on monthly payments?**
Correct. Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable, but you can cancel anytime. If you switch to annual billing, you're protected—that's a year of access for one upfront charge.
