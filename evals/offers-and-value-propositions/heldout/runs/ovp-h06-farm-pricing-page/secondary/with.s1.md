### FAQ

**How does annual billing work?**  
Pay for 10 months, get 12. You save two months on the annual plan. Switch anytime; your next charge is prorated.

**What happens when I cancel?**  
Cancel anytime, no notice needed. You can export all your data whenever you want—CSV and PDF, ready for your accountant.

**What's not included?**  
Payroll (use separate payroll software), tax filing, and phone support. Support is by email, answered within one business day.

**Refunds?**  
No refunds on monthly payments. If you switch from monthly to annual, the difference is credited toward your next billing.
