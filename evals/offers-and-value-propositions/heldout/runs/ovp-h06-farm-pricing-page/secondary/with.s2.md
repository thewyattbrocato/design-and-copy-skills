# Bookkeeping for Your Farm

**Get year-end summaries your accountant can actually use—track sales by market or CSA, expenses by field or crop, and mileage, all in one place.**

Most farms keep bookkeeping in spreadsheets or notebooks. That works until tax time, when you're hunting through months of data to answer questions only your accountant can ask. Ledgerleaf is bookkeeping software built for the way you actually farm: record sales and expenses as they happen, organize them by market stall, CSA, field, or crop, and watch your profit margins in real time. At year-end, export a clean summary—CSV and PDF—that your accountant can open and work from. No transcription, no back-and-forth, no missing receipts.
