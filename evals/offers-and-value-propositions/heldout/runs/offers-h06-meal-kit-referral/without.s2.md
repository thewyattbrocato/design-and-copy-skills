## The reward

| | Reward | When it's earned |
|---|---|---|
| **Friend** | $20 off their first box | At signup, through your link |
| **Referrer** | $25 Fernway credit | After the friend's 3rd box is delivered |

**Why these numbers**
- **Total cost is $45, under your $55 ad cost.** The cost is also lower for friends who leave early. If they quit after one box, you've spent only the $20 discount.
- **The referrer's reward waits for box 3.** Many people cancel after one or two boxes, and ads cost you $55 even for those people. Paying only after box 3 means you never pay $25 for someone who left early.
- **The friend's discount is paid up front.** It costs you your whole margin on box 1, but it's the main reason a friend tries the box.
- **At box 3 the friend has returned about $40 net.** That's $60 of margin minus the $20 discount, and $15 remains after the $25 credit. Each later box adds $20.
- **The reward is credit, not cash.** It's applied to the referrer's next box, which also encourages them to keep ordering.

Before launch, check two things. I assumed a weekly box, so "3rd box" means about 3 weeks. If you ship less often, consider rewarding after box 2. You also need to confirm that your billing system can track credits and cap them. I added a cap of 10 referral credits per customer. That cap is my suggestion, not something you gave me.
