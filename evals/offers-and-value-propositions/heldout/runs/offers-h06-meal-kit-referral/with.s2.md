## The offer

| | Reward | When it's earned |
|---|---|---|
| **Friend (new customer)** | $15 off their first box | Automatically at checkout when they sign up with your link |
| **Referrer** | $25 Fernway credit | After the friend's **third** box is delivered |

**Why these numbers**
- **Friend:** $15 off leaves about $5 margin on box one, so the first box isn't a loss. A bigger discount would cancel out the $20 you keep on it.
- **Referrer:** Paying after box three means the friend has already earned you about $45 ($5 + $20 + $20) before you pay $25. Many customers cancel after one or two boxes, and you never pay for those.
- **Cost:** Counting the discount, a successful referral costs $40 at most, against $55 for an ad-acquired customer. Referrals that don't reach box three cost you only the $15.
- **Form of the reward:** Paying in credit instead of cash keeps the referrer ordering.
