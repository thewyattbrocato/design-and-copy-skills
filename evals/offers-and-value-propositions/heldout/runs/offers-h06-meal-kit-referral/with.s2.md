**The offer**
- **Friend:** $20 off their first box.
- **Referrer:** $25 box credit, earned when the friend's third box is delivered.
- **Limits:** new customers only, up to 10 credits per referrer, and credit expires 12 months after it's added.

**Why these numbers**
- **The friend never costs you money.** The first box earns about $20 and the discount takes $20, so you break even on box 1. Every later box is profit.
- **You only pay for friends who stay.** Many people cancel after one or two boxes, so the reward waits for box 3. By then the friend has earned you about $40, and the $25 reward leaves about $15 net.
- **It costs less than ads.** The most you spend on a retained referral is $45 ($20 + $25), against $55 for an ad-acquired customer. You also pay nothing on referrals that churn early.
- **Credit, not cash.** Credit keeps referrers ordering, and you only bear the cost of the box when they use it.
