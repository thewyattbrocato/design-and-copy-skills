## The design

| | Reward | When it's earned |
|---|---|---|
| **Friend** | $20 off their first box | At checkout, through the referrer's link |
| **Referrer** | $25 box credit | After the friend's **3rd** box is delivered |

**Why these numbers**
- **Cost stays under your $55 ad cost.** The most you can pay is $45 ($20 + $25), and only for a friend who stays through three boxes. A friend who cancels after one box costs you $20 and no referrer reward.
- **Paying after box 3 filters out churners.** Many people cancel after box 1 or 2, so paying at signup would mean paying for customers who leave. By box 3 you've earned about $60 in margin ($20 × 3, less the $20 discount on box 1 and a $25 credit, you're at roughly +$15). Every box after that is margin.
- **The friend's discount is sized to your margin.** $20 off a $68 box takes your margin on box 1 to about zero, but it lowers the barrier to trying it. A bigger discount would put you underwater on a box many people never reorder.
- **Credit instead of cash.** It only pays out if the referrer keeps ordering, and it's easier to run in the app.
- **Cap at 10 rewarded friends.** This limits your exposure to $250 per person and discourages gaming.

The numbers are estimates. The "5 months" is for customers who stay, so the real average is lower. After launch, check what share of referred friends reach box 3. If it's low, move the referrer reward to box 4 or lower the friend's discount.

## Email to existing customers

**Subject:** Give $20, get $25 with Fernway
**Preview:** Share your link. Your friend saves on their first box.

Hi [First name],

Know someone who'd love a week of dinners sorted? Share your personal link and they'll get $20 off their first Fernway box.

Once they've received their third box, you'll get $25 credit toward your next one.

Find your link in the app under **Menu → Refer a Friend**.

**The details**
- Friend must be new to Fernway and sign up through your link.
- $20 off applies to their first box only and can't be combined with other first-box offers.
- Your $25 credit is added after their third box is delivered, applies automatically to your next box, and expires after 12 months.
- Credit has no cash value. Limit of 10 rewarded friends per customer.

Thanks for eating with us,
The Fernway team

*(About 145 words including the terms.)*
