Some honest ways to get the proof you want:

- **Savings claim:** Use a sourced, general statement, such as "Standby power can account for roughly 5–10% of household electricity use" (check the exact figure and cite the DOE or LBNL). Or build a clearly labeled example: "Example: a 15-year-old freezer using 1,000 kWh/yr at $0.16/kWh costs about $160 a year to run." Show the math.
- **Real quotes:** Give 10–20 people a free or discounted unit before launch and ask for feedback. Real quotes with permission are usable, and "Beta tester" labels are fine.
- **Early-stage trust:** Add a refund policy, a ship-date guarantee, and a short founder note. A preorder page with those often converts better than generic testimonials.
