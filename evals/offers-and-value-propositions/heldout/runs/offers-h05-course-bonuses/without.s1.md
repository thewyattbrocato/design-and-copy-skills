## Notes on what I did and why

- **No invented scarcity.** The honest urgency is the Nov 3 start, plus an early-bird bonus with a real cutoff. If you want a stronger push, a deadline-based bonus converts better than a "price goes up" claim unless the price truly goes up.
- **No outcome promises.** I avoided "your baby will sleep through the night." Parents of infants are sensitive to that claim, and it can create legal or trust problems. If you have testimonials, put them right above the price instead.
- **Safe sleep.** If the program touches sleep environment or age guidance, add a line like "Always follow your pediatrician's and AAP safe-sleep guidance." It's good practice for this audience.
