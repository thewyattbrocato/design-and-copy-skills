Notes for you and your cofounder:
- **Keep the last line.** "Free is fine and always will be" is the strongest line in the blurb. It builds trust, and it makes the upgrade feel like a choice.
- **Keep "no email needed."** It's a specific promise that most competitors can't make, so I'd resist swapping it for something vaguer.
- **I added a few claims you should check.** "Any mistake is undoable" and "look exactly like your design" are my phrasing. Soften them if they overstate what version history or export actually does.
- **The original wasn't broken, just quiet.** If your cofounder wants more energy, benefit-led phrasing like "so any mistake is undoable" gets you most of the way. Exclamation points and hype words would cost you the plainspoken tone that makes this card stand out.
