# Halyard Gutter Care guarantee

**The Halyard Promise**

**If you're not happy with the work, we'll come back and fix it free. If we can't make it right, you don't pay for that job.**

Here's what that means:

- **Clean when we leave.** Every gutter and downspout runs clear, and we show you with a photo or a hose test before we go.
- **Free re-visit for 30 days.** If a gutter we cleaned clogs or overflows because of our work, we come back at no charge.
- **Repairs are guaranteed for 1 year.** If a repair we made fails, we redo it free.
- **We cover our mistakes.** If we damage something, we fix it or pay to have it fixed.
- **No surprise charges.** You get a price before we start. If we find something extra, we tell you first and wait for your OK.
- **Pay after the work.** You pay when the job is done and you've looked it over. No deposit.

**We're a small local crew, so you'll deal with the same two people from your first call to the final walk-through. Our name is on every job.**

## Before you publish

This copy only calms people down if you can honor every line. Check these first:

1. **"Pay after, no deposit"** is the strongest trust-builder for a company nobody has heard of. Keep it if cash flow allows.
2. **"We pay for damage"** should be backed by general liability insurance. If you have it, add "fully insured" near the guarantee. If you don't, get it before you promise this, or change the line to "we'll fix it."
3. **The 30-day and 1-year windows** are my suggestions. Shorten them if you don't want to be on the hook that long. A promise you keep beats a bigger one you resent.
4. **Add fine print in one sentence:** "Doesn't cover new debris from storms or trees after the visit, or pre-existing damage we pointed out beforehand." That protects you without making the promise sound weaker.
5. **Don't add "licensed"** unless Eastbrook requires a license for this work and you hold one.

Two cheap additions that help as much as the wording:
- Put a real photo of the two of you next to the guarantee.
- Add one or two neighbor reviews, even from friends or family jobs, with street or neighborhood names.

If you tell me what insurance you have and how long you're willing to stand behind repairs, I'll tighten the wording and write a shorter version for the homepage banner.
