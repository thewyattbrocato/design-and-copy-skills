## The Halyard Guarantee

**Book us with nothing to lose.**

1. **Right or we come back free.** If you're not happy with the work, tell us within 7 days. We'll return and fix it at no charge.
2. **Still not happy? You don't pay.** If we can't make it right, we refund the cost of that job in full.
3. **Price you're quoted is the price you pay.** We confirm the price before we start. No surprise add-ons. If we find extra work, we show you and ask first.
4. **Photos of every job.** You get before-and-after photos of your gutters, so you can see the work without climbing a ladder.
5. **Pay after the work is done.** No deposit for cleanings. You pay once you've looked at the result.
6. **Damage covered.** If we damage your property, we'll fix it or pay for the repair.

*We're a local two-person crew. Our name is on every job, and we'd rather lose the money than your trust.*

## Why this version

- **Pay-after and photos** do the most to calm nerves about an unknown company. They show you're not asking anyone to take a leap of faith.
- **The 7-day window** keeps the promise from being open-ended. Cleaning is easy to judge right away, so it's fair for both sides.
- **The last line** turns being small into a strength.

## Check before you publish

- **Damage coverage (#6):** Only include it if you carry general liability insurance. If you do, say "fully insured" on the site. That's one of the biggest trust signals for a homeowner. If you don't, drop #6 and consider getting a policy.
- **Refund (#2):** Make sure you can afford to refund your most expensive job. If a small repair is pricey, you could limit the refund to cleanings and use "we redo it free" for repairs.
- **Repairs:** Workmanship on repairs, like reseals or hanger replacements, is usually guaranteed for longer, such as 90 days or a year. Consider adding that as a separate line.
- **Weather and pre-existing damage:** Add a short fine-print line, such as "Guarantee doesn't cover new debris after a storm or damage that existed before our visit." It keeps you protected without sounding defensive.
