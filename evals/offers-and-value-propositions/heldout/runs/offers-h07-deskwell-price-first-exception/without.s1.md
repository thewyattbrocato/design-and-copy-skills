A few notes on the choices:

- The headline is just the number and "flat," so there's no benefit language in it. "Flat" is a pricing term, not a benefit claim.
- The subhead gives the scope the price covers (weekly, 5,000 sq ft, Kestrel Valley), so an office manager comparing quotes can tell right away whether it's comparable. The terms they'd otherwise have to ask about come next: supplies and equipment, and month-to-month.
- The button names the free walkthrough. The written quote comes after the walkthrough, so I left "quote" out of the button. If you'd rather lead with the quote, "Get your written quote" works too, though it's less precise about the first step.

If you want a shorter subhead, this version keeps only the scope and drops the terms: "Weekly office cleaning, up to 5,000 sq ft, Kestrel Valley. Supplies included." Then the month-to-month and free-quote details can sit in a line under the button.
