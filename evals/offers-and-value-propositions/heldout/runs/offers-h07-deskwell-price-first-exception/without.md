**Headline:** $320 a month, flat.

**Subhead:** Weekly office cleaning for offices up to 5,000 sq ft in the Kestrel Valley. Supplies and equipment included. Month-to-month, with two weeks' notice to cancel.

**Button:** Book your free walkthrough

A few notes on the choices:

- The headline is only the number and "flat," so there are no benefits in it. "Flat" is part of the price itself and tells them there are no add-ons.
- The subhead gives the scope that makes the number comparable: size cap, weekly visits, what's included, and the contract terms. An office manager lining up three quotes needs those to check the $320 against the others.
- The button names the next step and the free part. The written quote comes after the walkthrough, so I kept the button to what they actually book. If you'd rather it mention the quote, "Get your free written quote" works too.

If you want the size limit in the headline, "$320 a month, flat, up to 5,000 sq ft" is the alternative. It's longer but removes any doubt about what the price covers.
