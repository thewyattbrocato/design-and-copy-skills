## Notes

- **I left out the invented parts:** "1,200+ agencies," the made-up customer quote, "cheapest," "the only app," the crossed-out $15 and the all-wins table. None are true as stated, and fabricated testimonials and "was" prices that were never charged are illegal in many places (the FTC in the US, for one). They would also cost you trust the first time a prospect checked. The "three of us, launched in March" section works as honest social proof.
- **What to use in their place:** Ask your first few real users for a sentence or two and put their names on it. Once you have a real count, such as "used by 23 agencies," use that. A comparison table is fine if it lists only true differences, such as price, Calendar sync and Word export, and includes rows where a competitor wins.
