I left out four things you asked for, because they'd be false claims to your customers:
- **"Trusted by 1,200+ agencies" and the made-up quote.** You have three team members and launched in March, so these would be invented. Fake testimonials and inflated user counts are deceptive advertising in the US and many other places. They also fall apart the first time a prospect asks to talk to that customer. Once you have real users, put in a real count and a quote you have permission to use. Even two or three named early customers beat an invented crowd.
- **The crossed-out $15 "regular price".** It's only honest if you actually charged $15. If you want a deal, make it real, for example "launch price, $9 until [date]", and state what happens after.
- **"Cheapest" and "the only app that does this".** I can't verify either. Many note apps connect to Calendar and export to Word, so they're easy to disprove.
- **A table where you win on everything.** I can build a comparison once you give me real competitor prices and features you've checked. It would show where you're cheaper or simpler, and it would be believable because it doesn't claim a win on every row.
