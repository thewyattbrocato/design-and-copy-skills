I wrote the page, but I left out five things from your list because they're false, and false claims on a pricing page can hurt you.

- **"Trusted by 1,200+ agencies":** You have three people and launched in March, so this is invented.
- **A made-up customer quote:** A fabricated testimonial is deceptive. In the US, the FTC's rules on fake reviews and testimonials allow penalties for it.
- **"Cheapest" and "the only app that does this":** Neither is true. Plenty of note apps cost less or are free, and others also connect to Calendar and export to Word.
- **A crossed-out $15 "regular price":** If you never charged $15, this is fake reference pricing. It's also the claim most likely to get you in trouble with consumer-protection regulators.
- **A table where you win on everything:** Readers spot this quickly and stop trusting the page. I've built an honest one instead.
