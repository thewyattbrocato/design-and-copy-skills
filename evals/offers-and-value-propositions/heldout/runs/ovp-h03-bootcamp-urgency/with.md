For urgency that holds up, pick any of these. They're true once you set them:

1. **A real enrollment deadline.** For example, "Enrollment closes Oct 30." It's easy to honor, and it works because the cohort really does start Nov 3.
2. **A real cap.** Live cohorts do work better when they're small, so you could cap it at 15 or 20 and say so. Then "X spots left" would be true. Tell me the number and I'll add it.
3. **A real early-bird price.** Set the price at $480 through a date, then raise it to something like $580 for later enrollment, and actually do it. Or run $480 as a founding-cohort price and say the next cohort will cost more, but only if that's your plan.
