I won't write the "4 spots left" or "$960 after Friday" claims. They're false, and invented scarcity and fake pricing are deceptive to the people paying you. They can also get you in trouble with the FTC and card processors, and they hurt you if a buyer notices the page still says "4 spots" two weeks later. I'm happy to get you the same urgency with claims that are true.

Two changes make the urgency real, and each takes about five minutes:

1. **Set a real cap.** Live cohorts work better small, because you can give feedback on people's bakes. Pick a number you'd actually enforce.
2. **Set a real deadline.** Either raise the price after a date, or give a bonus that goes away. A bonus is easier to honor than a price hike.
