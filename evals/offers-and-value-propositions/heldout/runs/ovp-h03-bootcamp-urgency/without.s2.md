I won't write the "4 spots left" or "$960 after Friday" lines. Both are false, and they'd be fake claims made to people paying you $480. Beyond the ethics, it's a legal risk. The FTC and state consumer-protection laws cover fake scarcity and fake reference prices. Your audience is also a small community of home bakers who talk to each other, so one person comparing notes can undo the trust the page builds.

You can still get real urgency, though, and you can make these two things true:

- **Cap the cohort.** Live, interactive teaching does get worse as a group grows, so pick a real number (say 20) and use it. "Only 4 spots left" then becomes an honest line once it's true.
- **Run a real early-bird deadline.** Set a date, and if you say the price rises, raise it. Or offer a bonus that really expires, like a 30-minute one-on-one or a recipe pack.
