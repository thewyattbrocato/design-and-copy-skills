I won't write the "Only 2 seats left!" line, the nightly-resetting countdown, or the "sold out within 48 hours" claim. They'd be false, and customers who notice (a timer that resets, seats that never run out) stop trusting a small studio quickly. In many places they're also illegal under consumer protection rules on deceptive pricing and scarcity claims.

## Honest urgency that works

- **A real countdown:** Run a timer to your actual registration deadline (say Oct 17) and don't reset it. It's the same mechanic with a true deadline.
- **A live seat counter:** Update the number as people sign up. Real scarcity gets more convincing as it drops.
- **A waitlist line:** "Full? Join the waitlist for the January session." That also builds your next class's list.
- **Social proof:** Add a student quote or a photo of finished pots. It does more for slow sign-ups than pressure does.
- **A small deadline incentive:** For example, "Register by Friday and get a free glaze upgrade," or bring-a-friend pricing, if you can afford it.
