I won't write the "Only 2 seats left!" line, the nightly-resetting countdown, or the "sold out in 48 hours" claim. None of them is true, and customers would be paying based on them. They also tend to backfire for a small studio. Your students are local, and they talk to each other and see the same page day after day. A timer that resets is easy to spot, and it costs you the trust a studio depends on. In some places it's also a deceptive-advertising problem.

## Ways to make the urgency real

- **A real deadline.** Close registration on a specific date and stick to it. A countdown timer to that date is fine, because it doesn't reset.
- **An early-bird price.** For example, $220 until [Date], then $240. This is usually the strongest honest lever.
- **A real seat count.** Even "6 of 10 seats open" works, and the number can go down as people sign up.
- **A bring-a-friend offer.** For example, $20 off each if two people register together. It fills seats faster than pressure does.
- **A waitlist for the next session**, shown once you actually sell out.
