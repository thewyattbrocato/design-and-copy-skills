## Honest ways to get more urgency

- **Real seat count:** If 6 of 10 are taken, "4 seats left" is true and works well. Update it by hand, or have the form pull the number.
- **Real deadline:** A countdown to the actual close of registration (say, Oct 18 at 5pm) is fine. It just can't reset.
- **Early-bird or referral perk:** For example, "Sign up by Friday and bring a friend for $20 off." That gives people a real reason to act now.
- **Send two follow-ups:** One at about 3 days out and one the day before registration closes. Most last-minute sign-ups come from the reminders.
- **Add a testimonial or a photo:** Show last session's finished pieces. Slow sign-ups are often a trust problem rather than a timing problem.
