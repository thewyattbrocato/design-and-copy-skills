## Honest urgency you can use
- **Real seat count.** If 6 are taken, say "4 of 10 seats left." It's true, and specific numbers persuade.
- **A real countdown.** A timer to the registration deadline or the Oct 20 start is fine, as long as it doesn't reset.
- **A real incentive.** For example, "Register by Oct 16 and get a free extra open-studio hour." Only offer it if you'll honor it.
- **Waitlist language.** If the class fills, say "Join the waitlist for the January session."
