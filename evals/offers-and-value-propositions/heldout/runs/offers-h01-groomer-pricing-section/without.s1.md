# Pricing

## Simple pricing for groomers who work out of a van

Tallybird is bookkeeping built for mobile dog groomers. You can invoice from your phone, log your miles, track tips, and hand your tax preparer a clean quarterly summary. There are two plans, and both include every feature.

## Pick your plan

**Solo: $14/month**
For one groomer running their own route.

**Team: $29/month**
For up to 5 groomers working under one business.

**Both plans include:**
- Invoicing from your phone
- Mileage log
- Tips tracking
- A quarterly summary you can hand to your tax preparer
- Import from a spreadsheet or CSV

The only difference between the plans is how many groomers can use the account.

### Pay yearly, get two months free

Choose annual billing and you pay for 10 months instead of 12:

- **Solo: $140/year** (instead of $168)
- **Team: $290/year** (instead of $348)

## Start with 21 days, every feature unlocked

You don't need a demo or a sales call to find out whether Tallybird fits the way you work. Start a free trial and use it on real appointments.

- **No card needed to start.** Sign up and you're in.
- **Everything is unlocked.** You get every feature for all 21 days, whichever plan you end up choosing.
- **Bring your records with you.** Import your existing spreadsheet or CSV so you're not starting from a blank page.

Our six beta users took between 15 and 30 minutes to get set up. Plan on an afternoon break, not a weekend project.

## What happens when the trial ends

When your 21 days are up, you pick a plan or your account goes read-only. Nothing is deleted for 90 days. If you need more time to decide, your invoices, mileage, and tips are still there to look at.

You can also export all of your data as CSV at any time, during the trial or after you've paid. Your records belong to you.
