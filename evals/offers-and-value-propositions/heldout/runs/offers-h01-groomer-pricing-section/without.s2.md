# Pricing

## Bookkeeping that fits between appointments

Tallybird costs less than one full groom a month. It's built by a former mobile groomer, so it covers the things your day involves: invoicing from your phone, logging miles between stops, and keeping track of tips. At tax time, it gives you a summary your preparer can use.

Pick a plan below, or skip that step and start with the free trial.

## Two plans, same features

Both plans include everything:

- Invoice from your phone
- Mileage log
- Tips tracking
- Quarterly summary you can hand to your tax preparer
- Import from a spreadsheet or CSV

The only difference is how many groomers use the account.

### Solo
**$14/month**, or **$140/year**
For one groomer.

### Team
**$29/month**, or **$290/year**
For up to 5 groomers.

**Annual billing means you pay for 10 months and get 12.** That's $140 a year for Solo and $290 for Team.

## What happens when you sign up

**Day 1: Start your trial.** You get all 21 days with every feature unlocked. We don't ask for a card.

**Your first session: Get your numbers in.** If your records are in a spreadsheet, import the spreadsheet or CSV and you're set. Our six beta users took between 15 and 30 minutes to get set up. Plan on an hour at most, so you can take your time.

**Days 2–21: Use it on real work.** Send an invoice from your phone after a groom. Log a day's mileage. Record a tip. Real use is the best way to find out whether Tallybird fits how you work.

**Day 21: You choose.** Pick Solo or Team, monthly or annual, and carry on. If you don't pick one, your account goes read-only. You can still see everything, but you can't add to it. **Nothing is deleted for 90 days**, so you can come back when you're ready and find your records waiting.

## Your data stays yours

You can export all your data as CSV at any time, during the trial or on a paid plan. If Tallybird isn't right for you, you can take your records with you.

**Why I picked the trial over a demo call:**
- Your audience works alone or in tiny teams, and they're often in a van or at a client's house. A scheduled call is a big ask. A trial they can start from their phone fits their day.
- Your facts support it well: no card, everything unlocked, a 15–30 minute setup, and a 90-day safety net. A demo call would add friction to a product that's easy to try.
- The copy is about 600 words. I didn't add claims you didn't give me, such as "cancel anytime," testimonials, or "most users." The beta line says exactly what the six users did. If you have more beta quotes, add one near the CTA.

If you want to offer a call later, add it as a small "Questions? Email us" line under the button. That keeps the trial as the main action.
