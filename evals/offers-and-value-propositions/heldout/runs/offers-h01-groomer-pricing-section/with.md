## Pricing

**Bookkeeping for mobile groomers, built by a former mobile groomer.**

Tallybird handles invoices, mileage, tips, and the quarterly summary your tax preparer asks for. You can do all of it from your phone, between appointments. Two plans, one set of features, and the only difference is how many groomers use it.

### Solo
**$14/month**, or **$140/year**
For one groomer running their own van.

### Team
**$29/month**, or **$290/year**
For a shop with up to 5 groomers. Same features as Solo, with room for everyone on one account.

### On both plans
- **Invoice from your phone.** Bill a client from the driveway.
- **Mileage log.** Keep a record of your drives.
- **Tips tracking.** See what you earned in tips, separate from what you billed.
- **Quarterly summary.** A summary you can hand straight to your tax preparer.
- **Import from a spreadsheet or CSV.** Bring your existing records with you instead of retyping them.

### Pay yearly, get two months free
Annual billing is 10 months for the price of 12. Solo is $140 a year instead of $168. Team is $290 a year instead of $348. The only reason for the lower price is that you're paying for the year up front.

### Start with the 21-day free trial
Every feature is unlocked for the full 21 days, and you don't need a card to start. Nothing is charged, because we don't have a payment method to charge.

We'd rather you try Tallybird with your own clients than take our word for it. Our six beta users took between 15 and 30 minutes to set up. Import your spreadsheet, send an invoice, and see whether it fits how you work.

### What happens when you sign up
1. **Create your account.** Email only, no card.
2. **Import your records.** Upload a spreadsheet or CSV, or start fresh.
3. **Send your first invoice.** Try it on a real client or a test one.
4. **Use it for 21 days.** Log mileage and track tips along the way.
5. **Pick a plan when the trial ends.** Choose Solo or Team, monthly or yearly.

If you don't pick a plan, your account goes read-only. You can still see everything, but you can't add to it. Nothing is deleted for 90 days, so you can come back and pick up where you left off.

### Your data stays yours
You can export all of your data as CSV at any time, during the trial or on a paid plan.
