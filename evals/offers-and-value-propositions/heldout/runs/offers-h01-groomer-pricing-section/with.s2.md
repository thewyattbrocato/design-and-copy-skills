## Pricing

### Keep your grooming books without the Sunday-night paperwork

Tallybird is bookkeeping built for mobile dog groomers. You can send invoices from your phone, log mileage, track tips, and hand your tax preparer a quarterly summary. You don't have to piece it together from a spreadsheet and a glovebox full of receipts.

It was founded by a former mobile groomer. Our six beta users took between 15 and 30 minutes to set up.

### Two plans, same features

Both plans include:

- **Invoice from your phone**
- **Mileage log**
- **Tips tracking**
- **A quarterly summary** you can hand to your tax preparer
- **Import from a spreadsheet or CSV**, so you can bring your existing records with you

The only difference is how many groomers use the account.

**Solo: $14/month**
For one groomer running their own van. Billed yearly, it's $140/year.

**Team: $29/month**
For a shop with up to 5 groomers on one account. Billed yearly, it's $290/year.
If you have more than one groomer, this is your plan. Solo covers one groomer only.

**Paying yearly:** Annual billing is 10 months for the price of 12. Solo is $140 instead of $168 (12 × $14). Team is $290 instead of $348 (12 × $29). That's two months free for paying once a year.

### Start with 21 days free

Every new account gets 21 days with every feature unlocked. You don't need a card to start, so there's nothing to cancel and no first charge to watch for.

Here's what happens from sign-up on:

1. **Create your account.** You only need to sign up. No payment details.
2. **Bring in your records.** Import a spreadsheet or CSV, or start fresh. Our beta users finished setup in 15 to 30 minutes.
3. **Use it on real jobs.** Send an invoice from your phone, log a drive, and record a tip. Over 21 days you'll see how it handles your actual week.
4. **Choose at the end of the trial.** Pick Solo or Team, monthly or yearly. If you don't pick, your account goes read-only. You can still see everything, but you can't add to it. Nothing is deleted for 90 days, so you can come back and pick a plan without losing your records.

### Your data is yours

You can export all your data as CSV at any time, during the trial or on a paid plan. If Tallybird isn't right for you, you can leave with your records.
