Tallybird is bookkeeping for mobile dog groomers. You can send invoices from your phone, log mileage, track tips, and hand your tax preparer a quarterly summary. You don't need a spreadsheet for any of it. A former mobile groomer founded Tallybird, so it's built around how a groomer's day goes.

Start with the 21-day free trial. Every feature is unlocked and you don't need a card.

### Two plans, same features

Both plans include:

- Invoicing from your phone
- Mileage log
- Tips tracking
- A quarterly summary you can hand to your tax preparer
- Import from a spreadsheet or CSV

The only difference is how many groomers use the account.

**Solo: $14 a month**
For one groomer running their own route. Pay yearly and it's $140 a year.

**Team: $29 a month**
For a shop with up to 5 groomers. Pay yearly and it's $290 a year. Solo covers one groomer, so you'd need Team once a second person joins.

**Paying yearly:** Yearly billing is 10 months for the price of 12. Solo is $140 instead of $168, and Team is $290 instead of $348. Choosing to pay once a year is the only reason for the lower price.

### What happens when you sign up

1. **Create your account.** It takes an email address. You don't need a card.
2. **Set up.** Our six beta users took between 15 and 30 minutes. If your client list is already in a spreadsheet or CSV, you can import it instead of retyping it.
3. **Use it for 21 days with nothing held back.** Send invoices, log your miles, and record tips on real jobs. You can see how it fits your week before you decide.
4. **Pick a plan when the trial ends.** If you don't, your account goes read-only. You can still see your records, but you can't add to them. Nothing is deleted for 90 days, so you can pick a plan later and carry on where you stopped.

You can export all of your data as CSV at any time, during the trial or on a paid plan. Your records stay yours, and you can leave with them whenever you want.

### Questions you might have

**Do I need a card to try it?** No. Nothing is charged at the start, and the trial doesn't turn into a paid plan by itself. When it ends, you choose a plan or the account goes read-only.

**What if I'm not ready to pay when the trial ends?** The account goes read-only and your data is kept for 90 days. You can export it as CSV whenever you like.
