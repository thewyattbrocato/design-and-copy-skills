- **Led with a bold first line** so the main offer is what people see first.
- **Cut filler:** "and" before "every feature," "whenever you like" became "anytime," and "isn't right for you" became the shorter "not right for you."
- **Changed "Your notes are always yours" to "Your notes stay yours."** It's a little plainer and sounds less like a slogan.

I kept every term as you had it: 14 days, no card, three-day reminder, both prices, the 45-day refund, and plain-text export. If you want it even shorter, you could drop the email-reminder sentence. I'd keep it, though, since it builds trust for people wary of trials that auto-bill.
