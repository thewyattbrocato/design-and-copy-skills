Here's a tighter version:

> **Try Fieldnote free for 14 days.** No card needed, and every feature is unlocked. We'll email you three days before the trial ends.

> If you subscribe ($12/month or $120/year) and it isn't right for you, tell us within 45 days and we'll refund that payment in full.

What I changed:

- **Split into three beats.** Trial, refund, ownership. Each is easier to scan than one block.
- **Bolded the lead sentence** so the main offer reads first.
- **Trimmed small words.** "$12 a month" became "$12/month", "whenever you like" became "anytime", and "Your notes are always yours: export them" became two short sentences.
- **Left the substance alone.** The 14 days, the three-day reminder, the 45-day full refund, and the pricing are all unchanged. The copy was already plain and honest, so I didn't add energy it didn't need.

One thing to check: the refund wording says "that payment," which is accurate if it only covers the first charge, but some people may read it as a promise about any payment. If that's the intent, it's fine. If you'd rather make it clearer, "we'll refund that first payment in full" would help.
