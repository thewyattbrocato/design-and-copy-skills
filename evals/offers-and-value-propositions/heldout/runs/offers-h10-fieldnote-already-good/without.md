Here's a tighter version:

> **Try Fieldnote free for 14 days.** No card needed, every feature unlocked. We'll email you three days before the trial ends.
>
> Subscribe for $12/month or $120/year. If it's not right for you, tell us within 45 days and we'll refund that payment in full.
>
> Your notes stay yours. Export them as plain text files anytime.

What I changed:
- **Split it into three beats:** trial, pricing and refund, then export. The box is easier to scan, and each part has one job.
- **Cut filler:** "and" became a comma, "whenever you like" became "anytime", and "If you subscribe… and it isn't right for you" became a direct "Subscribe for… If it's not right for you".
- **Made the last line active:** "Your notes stay yours" is shorter than "are always yours", and "always" was doing no work.
- **Kept every term:** trial length, no card, the reminder timing, both prices, the 45-day window and the full refund.
