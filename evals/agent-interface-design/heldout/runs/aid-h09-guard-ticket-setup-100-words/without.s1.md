**First question: "What should the agent never handle on its own?"**

Before anything else, have the lead define the red lines: billing disputes, legal or security issues, angry or VIP customers, outages, anything involving account deletion or refunds. Those always go to a human.

This matters most because the agent sends replies without review. Everything it's allowed to auto-answer follows from what's off-limits. Then ask which ticket types count as "simple," who owns each category, and where escalations go.
