**First question: "Which types of tickets am I allowed to reply to on my own, and which should always go to a human?"**

Autonomy is the highest-stakes setting, since a wrong auto-reply goes straight to a customer. Asking it first also sets the boundaries for everything else. Suggest they start narrow, with something like password resets and order-status questions, and widen later.

Next, in order:
1. How should I route and assign tickets (by skill, load, or tier)?
2. What should always escalate (billing disputes, legal, angry customers, VIPs)?
3. Should I draft replies for review at first, or send them?
