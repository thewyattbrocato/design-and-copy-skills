**First question: "Which kinds of tickets may I reply to without a human looking, and how many a day?"**

Don't start with a blank form. Propose a draft from their past tickets, like "password resets, order-status questions, and shipping-address changes." Let the lead edit it and set a daily cap.

Auto-replies are the one thing that can't be unsent, so settle that first. Everything else, like routing rules and assignment preferences, can wait until after the first run.

Before it goes live, restate the rule in plain words with three example tickets it would answer. Until the lead confirms, it should only draft replies for approval.
