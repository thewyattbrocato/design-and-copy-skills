Before setting up the support agent, ask the team lead:

1. Which queues and ticket sources should it read?
2. Who owns each team, and what routing rules apply?
3. What counts as "simple"? Give examples to auto-reply to, and topics it must always escalate (billing, refunds, legal, security).
4. Which knowledge base or macros can it use for replies, and what tone should it follow?
5. Should it draft replies for review first, or send them directly?
6. Who gets alerted when it's unsure or makes a mistake?
