Before the agent touches any tickets, ask the team lead:

1. Which ticket categories exist, and which are simple enough for auto-reply?
2. Who are the agents or teams, and what does each one handle?
3. How should tickets be assigned: round-robin, by skill, or by product area?
4. Which replies need human review before sending, such as refunds, legal, outages, or VIP accounts?
5. What tone and sign-off should replies use, and is there a knowledge base to cite?
6. What should the agent do when it's unsure?
