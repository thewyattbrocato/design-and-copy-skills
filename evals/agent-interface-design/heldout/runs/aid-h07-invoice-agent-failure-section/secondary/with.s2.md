## When it goes wrong

Nudge emails late clients on the freelancer's behalf, from the freelancer's own address, so a wrong email reaches the client as if the freelancer sent it. A sent email cannot be recalled. Freelancers can stop a reminder only before it sends, and support cannot change anything on their behalf. This section covers the failure cases, what the freelancer can do in each, what support can do, and what we do not yet offer.

### Normal schedule

Reminders go out 3, 10 and 21 days after the due date, unless the freelancer has paused that client. Every reminder is logged with the text sent, the time sent, and the reason it was sent.

### Failure cases

**1. A reminder goes out for an invoice that is already paid (known beta issue)**
- **When:** The client pays by bank transfer and the freelancer has not marked the invoice paid in Nudge.
- **Seen so far:** 3 times across 40 beta accounts.
- **Effect:** The client receives a reminder for an invoice they have already settled. The email cannot be recalled.
- **Freelancer can:** Mark the invoice paid. This cancels all remaining reminders for it but does not undo a reminder already sent.
- **Support can:** Read the log to confirm what was sent and when. Support cannot cancel or resend anything.

**2. A reminder the freelancer does not want to send**
- **Freelancer can:** Stop any scheduled reminder up until 15 minutes before it sends. Pause the client to stop reminders for them.
- **After 15 minutes before send:** The reminder cannot be stopped.
- **Support can:** Read the log. Support cannot pause, cancel or recall.

**3. The invoice is marked paid after reminders have started**
- **Freelancer can:** Mark it paid at any time. This cancels all remaining reminders for that invoice.
- **Effect:** Reminders already sent stay sent.

**4. Wrong text, wrong reason, or unexpected timing**
- **Check:** The log records the exact text, the time, and the reason for each reminder.
- **Support can:** Read the log and use it to explain what happened. Support cannot edit, resend or cancel.

### Who can do what

| Action | Freelancer | Support |
|---|---|---|
| Stop a scheduled reminder (until 15 minutes before send) | Yes | No |
| Pause a client | Yes | No |
| Mark an invoice paid (cancels remaining reminders) | Yes | No |
| Recall a sent email | No (cannot be recalled) | No (cannot be recalled) |
| Read the reminder log | Not specified | Yes |
| Edit, resend or cancel on the freelancer's behalf | Not specified | No |

### Gaps

- **No apology or credit policy yet.** Support has no approved apology or credit to offer for a wrong reminder.
- **Open question:** Who besides support can read the reminder log? This section does not specify it.
