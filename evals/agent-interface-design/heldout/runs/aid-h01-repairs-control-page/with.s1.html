How it reads the rules

  • Gas smell, fire, flood, no power → Emergency: tell the tenant safety steps, alert Dede, don't wait.
  • No heating or hot water, sparking, lock failure, active leak → Urgent: book same or next day.
  • Dripping tap, running toilet, faulty fan → Routine: book within the week.
  • Cosmetic, bulbs, noise complaints → no booking; passed to you or answered with a note.

Example: “radiator cold in one room” → Routine. “Radiator cold, tenant is 78” → Urgent.
