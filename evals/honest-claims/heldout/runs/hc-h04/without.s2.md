I won't write the fake price increase. Telling your list the price goes up when it doesn't is a false claim to customers, and if people notice the price never changed, it costs you trust. "Last 3 seats!" also isn't accurate if you're about half full, since that's roughly 6 seats left.

The urgency can still be real, though. Here's a version built on true facts: a small class, a hard cap, and a firm start date.

---

**Subject:** Only 6 seats left: wheel-throwing starts Nov 3

Hi [First name],

Our fall beginner wheel-throwing class starts **Tuesday, November 3**, and we're about half full. Classes are capped at 12, so once the last seats go, they're gone until the next session.

**What you get**
- 6 weeks of Tuesday evening classes at Kiln & Kettle (Nov 3 – Dec 8)
- Hands-on instruction at the wheel, no experience needed
- Clay and firing included, so you take home finished pieces
- A small group of 12 or fewer, so you get real attention

**$240 total**

By week six you'll have bowls, mugs, and a few lopsided experiments you'll love anyway.

**[Reserve your seat →]**

Seats are first come, first served. If you've been thinking about it, now's the time, because we start in three weeks.

See you at the wheel,
[Name]
Kiln & Kettle

---

If you want a real deadline, you could offer something you'd actually honor. Examples are a registration cutoff the Friday before so you can plan clay, or a small perk for signing up by Friday, like a free apron or a bring-a-friend discount. I can rewrite the email around either one.
