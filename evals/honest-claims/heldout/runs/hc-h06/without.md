**Subject: Three weeks on a medium-firm mattress**

Quick note before we start: Pinewake sent me the queen for free, and I earn 8% if you buy through my link. Now, the mattress.

I'm not a person who gets excited about mattresses. I get excited about a good blanket and a decent pillow, and I assume the rest is mostly marketing. But I've now slept on the Pinewake for three weeks, and I have some thoughts.

**The basics**

It's a medium-firm queen, priced at $1,150. For context, I'm a side sleeper at about 170 lb, so I'm the kind of person who notices when a mattress is too hard on the shoulders and hips. That's the lens for everything below.

**What I liked**

The edges are the surprise. Most mattresses collapse a little when you sit on the side to put on socks, or when you sleep close to the edge. This one doesn't. I could sit, sprawl, and migrate toward the edge in the night without feeling like I was sliding off. It sounds minor until you've had a mattress that fails at it.

It also didn't sleep hot. I tested it through the summer weeks, which is when a lot of mattresses show their true colors, and I never woke up feeling like I'd been slow-roasted. I didn't need to kick the covers off or flip the pillow to the cool side. That's about all I ask of a mattress in July.

As a side sleeper, I found the medium-firm feel comfortable for my shoulders and hips. I'd call it supportive rather than plush. You sit on top of it more than you sink into it.

**What I didn't love**

It weighs 52 lb. That doesn't matter once it's on the bed, but getting it there is a project. If you rearrange furniture often, or you live up a flight of stairs, plan on a second person and some patience.

There was also a smell for the first two days. It faded, and by day three I'd stopped noticing, but I'd air out the room if you can.

And my partner found it a bit firm. I'll say it plainly: this was my verdict more than theirs. I liked it, and they were more lukewarm. If you share a bed and one of you prefers a softer feel, that's worth talking about before you buy. I'm a side sleeper who likes some support, but your mileage will vary.

If you want something medium-firm, with great edges, that stays cool, I think it's a solid option. If you want a cloud, or you're sharing with someone who does, I'd look elsewhere.
