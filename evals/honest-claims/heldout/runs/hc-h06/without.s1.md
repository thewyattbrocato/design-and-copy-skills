**Three weeks on the Pinewake mattress**

Quick note before we get into it: Pinewake sent me a queen to try for free, and if you buy through my link I earn 8%. That's the whole deal, and everything below is my honest take.

I'm a side sleeper, about 170 lb, and I've spent three weeks on the medium-firm. Here's how it went.

**What I liked**

The edges. I didn't expect to care about this, but I do. On a lot of mattresses, sitting on the edge to put on socks feels like slowly sliding into the sea. On this one, the edges hold you up. I could sit there, sleep near the edge, and not feel like I was about to roll off. If you share a bed, that's more usable space than it sounds like.

It also didn't sleep hot. I tested it through some properly warm summer weeks, which is the stretch when most mattresses show their flaws. I never woke up damp or kicked off the covers at 3 a.m. For me that's most of what I want from a mattress, and it did it quietly.

For my body, medium-firm worked well as a side sleeper. My shoulders and hips sank in enough to stay comfortable, and I didn't wake up with the usual morning grumbles.

**What I didn't love**

It's heavy. The queen weighs 52 lb, which is awkward to handle alone. Rotating it or moving it up a flight of stairs is a two-person job, or a one-person job followed by some muttering. If you move often, factor that in.

There was a smell for the first two days. It faded, and by day three I'd stopped noticing it. Still, I'd open a window and let it air out before you make the bed.

My partner found it a bit firm. I didn't, but I'm the one who picked "medium-firm" and then slept on it happily, so take that with a grain of salt. If one of you likes a softer feel, this is the thing to think about. They didn't hate it, they just wouldn't have chosen it.

**The money part**

The queen is $1,150. That's real money, not a casual purchase, though it's not out of line for a mattress in this category. Their site lists a 100-night trial, which I'd count on to take some of the risk out. I haven't used the return process, so check the current terms before you buy.

**So, who's it for?**

I'd point this at side sleepers who run warm, want a supportive edge, and lean toward a firmer feel. If you or your partner like a softer, sink-in bed, I'd try one in person first or lean on that trial period. If you're hauling it up three flights alone, bring a friend and snacks.

For me, it's a keeper. It does the unglamorous things well, like staying cool and holding its shape, and I'll take that over a flashy feature any day.
