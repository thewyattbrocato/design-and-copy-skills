**Subject: Three weeks on the Pinewake mattress**

Quick note before we start: Pinewake sent me the queen for free, and I earn 8% if you buy through my link. Now, on to the mattress.

I'll admit I'm hard to impress when it comes to mattresses. They're big, they're expensive, and most of them are described in language that makes me want to lie down on the floor instead. So I tried to be fair about this one. I slept on the Pinewake for three weeks, and here's where I landed.

**The basics**

It's a medium-firm mattress. For context, I'm a side sleeper at about 170 lb, which is the kind of sleeper who finds out quickly whether a mattress is too hard on the shoulders and hips. The queen costs $1,150.

**What I liked**

The edges are the surprise. Most mattresses go a bit mushy near the perimeter, and you can feel yourself sliding toward the floor when you sit on the side to put on socks. The Pinewake doesn't do that. The edges are properly supportive, so you can sit there, sleep near them, and use the whole bed instead of the middle two-thirds.

It also didn't sleep hot. I tested it through summer weeks, which is when a mattress that traps heat gets found out. I didn't wake up sweaty, and I never did the 3 a.m. flip-the-pillow-to-the-cool-side routine. That alone earns it a lot of goodwill from me.

For side sleeping, the medium-firm feel worked. I had no shoulder complaints and woke up feeling like a person who'd been asleep, which is the whole job.

**What I didn't love**

It's heavy. At 52 lb, it's a real pain to move. If you're carrying it up stairs or rotating it, bring a friend and lower your expectations.

There was also a smell for the first two days. It faded, and by day three I'd forgotten about it, but if you're sensitive to that sort of thing, plan to air the room out before your first night.

And my partner found it a bit firm. I didn't, but I'm the one with the shoulder-and-hip situation that likes a firmer surface. If you share a bed with someone who likes things softer, think about that before you buy. It wasn't a dealbreaker for us, but it did come up.

**Who it's for**

If you like a medium-firm feel, sleep warm, and want a bed that's usable edge to edge, the Pinewake is a strong pick. If you or your partner lean soft, it might feel too firm.

Their site says there's a 100-night trial, which is a good safety net for a purchase like this. Check the return terms before you order, since mattress trial details can be fiddly.

**Bottom line**

It's a solid, no-drama mattress that's good at the things I care about (edges, temperature) and annoying in ways that mostly happen once (weight, smell). At $1,150 for a queen, I'd call it reasonable, and I'm keeping it.

That's it for this week. Go sleep on something nice.
