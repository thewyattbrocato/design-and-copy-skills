**Three weeks on the Pinewake mattress**

Quick housekeeping first: Pinewake sent me the queen for free, and if you buy through my link I earn 8%. That's the whole arrangement. Everything below is what I actually thought, including the parts they probably wish I'd skip.

I slept on it for three weeks. For context, I'm a side sleeper, around 170 lb, and the Pinewake is a medium-firm. That combination matters, because "medium-firm" can mean very different things depending on who's lying on it.

**What I liked**

The edges are the surprise winner. I didn't expect to care about this, but I do. Most mattresses collapse a little when you sit on the side to put on socks, or when you sleep near the edge. This one stays put. You can use the whole surface, which, in a queen shared with another adult, is a real luxury.

It also didn't sleep hot. I tested it through some properly warm summer weeks and never had that 2 a.m. "why am I a baked potato" moment. I'm not going to promise it's a cooling miracle, but I never once kicked off the covers out of desperation, and that's the bar I care about.

**What I didn't love**

It's heavy. The queen weighs 52 lb, which is fine until you want to rotate it, change a sheet set aggressively, or move it up a flight of stairs. Plan on a second person, or on being quietly annoyed.

There was also a smell for the first two days. Nothing alarming, just that new-mattress scent. It faded, and I'd air the room out and not think about it. But if you're sensitive to that kind of thing, know it's coming.

And the firmness isn't for everyone. My partner found it a bit firm. I thought it was a good match for my side-sleeping shoulders and hips, but they'd have preferred something softer. If you share a bed, this is worth testing together rather than assuming one person's verdict covers both.

**The practical stuff**

The queen is $1,150. That's real money, though not outrageous for the category. Their site lists a 100-night trial, which is the thing that makes me more comfortable recommending it. Three weeks told me a lot, but a body takes longer than that to settle into a new mattress. I'd check the return terms yourself before buying, since I'm only going by what's on their site.

**Who it's for**

If you're a side sleeper in roughly my weight range who wants a supportive, not-too-soft feel, and you sleep warm, the Pinewake is worth a look. If you like to sink in, or your partner does, I'd keep shopping or lean hard on that trial period.

For me, it's a keeper. I'm still sleeping on it, which, after three weeks and a free mattress, is probably the most honest endorsement I can give.
