**Three weeks on the Pinewake mattress**

A quick note before we start: Pinewake sent me the queen for free, and I earn a small commission (8%) if you buy through my link. That's the whole disclosure. Everything below is what I actually thought.

I've now slept on the Pinewake for three weeks, which is long enough to get past the honeymoon phase and short enough that I can't tell you how it'll feel in year six. Consider this a field report, not a verdict.

**The basics**

It's a medium-firm mattress, and the queen costs $1,150. For context, I'm a side sleeper at about 170 lb, so I'm the kind of person who needs a mattress to give a little at the shoulder and hip without turning into a hammock. Your body and your sleeping position matter a lot here, so read everything with that in mind.

**What I liked**

The edges are the surprise winner. I didn't know I cared about edge support until I had it. You can sit on the side of the bed to put on socks without feeling like you're slowly sliding off, and sleeping near the edge doesn't give you that precarious, about-to-roll-out feeling. If you share a bed and one of you is a perimeter sleeper, this matters more than it sounds.

It also didn't sleep hot. I tested it through some properly warm summer weeks, and I never woke up feeling like I'd been slow-roasted. I'm not going to claim it's a cooling miracle, since I only have my own sweaty data, but I never once thought about the temperature of the bed, and that's about the best review a mattress can get.

**What I didn't love**

It weighs 52 lb, and it's awkward to move. If you're the type who rearranges the bedroom every few months, or you live up a flight of stairs, plan on recruiting a friend and promising them pizza.

There was also a smell for the first two days. It wasn't terrible, just that new-mattress odor. I aired the room out, and by day three I'd stopped noticing it. Worth knowing about if you're sensitive to smells, and I'd open a window.

The last one is the most important: my partner found it a bit firm. I'm happy with medium-firm as a side sleeper, but it wasn't a slam dunk for both of us. If you and the person you share a bed with disagree about firmness, take that seriously. This is the thing most likely to decide whether the Pinewake works for you.

**The trial period**

According to Pinewake's site, there's a 100-night trial. I haven't tested the return process, so I can't tell you how smooth it is. Check the current terms before you buy, but a trial that long takes some of the risk out of a $1,150 purchase, especially with a partner who might vote differently than you.

**Who it's for**

If you're a side sleeper who likes a supportive, medium-firm feel, runs warm, and wants a bed you can use right to the edge, the Pinewake is worth a look. If you like a plush, sink-in feel, or you're sharing with someone who does, you'll probably want something softer.

I'm keeping it, partner's mild grumbling and all. It's a solid, well-behaved mattress that does what it says, and after a few weeks I mostly forgot about it, which is the point.
