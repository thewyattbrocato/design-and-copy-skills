## Website results section

**Headline:** Across 212 homes, the median heating bill dropped 14%

**Subhead:** Real results from our 2023–2025 air-sealing and attic insulation jobs, measured on actual bills.

**How we measured:** Between 2023 and 2025 we air-sealed and added attic insulation in 212 homes. For each one, we compared weather-adjusted heating bills for the 12 months before and the 12 months after the job.

**What we found**
- **Median reduction: 14%.**
- **The middle half of homes landed between 9% and 21%.**
- **Best result: 38%.** That was a 1920s farmhouse with no attic insulation at all. Homes starting from that point have the most to gain, and most homes aren't there.
- **No measurable change in 9 of the 212 homes (about 4%).** We'd rather you hear that from us up front.

**What it costs:** A typical job runs $2,900.

**Who does the work:** Our own crew does every job. We don't subcontract.

**Call to action:** Want to know what to expect in your house? Ask us for a quote.
