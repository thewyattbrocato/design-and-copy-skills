**What 212 homes told us**

From 2023 to 2025, our crew air-sealed and added attic insulation in 212 homes. For each one, we compared weather-adjusted heating bills for the 12 months before and after the job.

- **Typical result: a 14% lower heating bill** (the median across all 212 homes).
- **Most homes saw 9% to 21%.** That is the middle half of results.
- **Our best result was 38%.** It was a 1920s farmhouse with no attic insulation at all. Homes that start with less insulation have more to gain.
- **Not every home improves.** 9 of the 212 homes (about 4%) saw no measurable change.

The typical job costs $2,900. Our own crew does all the work.
