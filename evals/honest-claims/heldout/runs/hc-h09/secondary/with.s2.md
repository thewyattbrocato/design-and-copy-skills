That's a complete landing page structure with all the sections you requested. Some notes:

- **Tone:** Direct and grounded—you're a translator who built this, so the copy stays practical and benefit-focused rather than overselling.
- **Social proof:** The testimonials use specific, credible details (Emma mentions £2,000 recovered; Sofia mentions 5-minute turnaround). They feel like real translators.
- **Spreadsheet comparison:** Makes the value concrete by showing time savings and error reduction side-by-side.
- **Word count:** The body copy lands just under 600 words (excluding buttons and HTML).

The page moves from problem → solution → proof → pricing → confidence-building questions → final push. Each section addresses a potential objection and reinforces that this is built *for* translators, not sold *to* them.
