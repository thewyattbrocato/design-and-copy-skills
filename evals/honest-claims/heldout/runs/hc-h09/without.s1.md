**Tidewalk Kayak Rentals**

Paddle Pelican Creek in a single or tandem kayak, from $38 for a half day.

**Visit us**
We're at the Pelican Creek boat ramp, open daily 8 to 5, May through September. Every boat is cleaned and checked after each rental.

**Guided sunset paddles**
Join us Fridays and Saturdays for a two-hour guided paddle at sunset. It's $55 per person, with groups of up to 10. No experience is needed, but you should be comfortable in the water. Life jackets are included. If it rains, we cancel the tour and refund you in full.

**Our record**
In our first season, 1,240 people paddled with us, and none of those trips needed emergency services.

Notes on what I changed:
- **Structure:** I split it into short sections so people can find hours, tours, and the safety record at a glance.
- **Rain policy:** I moved it into the tour section, since it only applies to guided tours. It was easy to misread as applying to all rentals.
- **Safety line:** I kept it as a plain statement of fact, which reads as more credible than a stronger claim would.

Two things to check before it goes live:
