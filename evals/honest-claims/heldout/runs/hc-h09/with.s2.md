**Tidewalk Kayak Rentals**
Get out on the water at Pelican Creek.

Rent a single or tandem kayak from $38 for a half day. We're at the Pelican Creek boat ramp, open daily 8 to 5, May through September. Every boat is cleaned and checked after each rental, so it's ready when you are.

**Guided sunset paddles**
Fridays and Saturdays, two hours, $55 per person, groups of up to 10. Rain cancels guided tours, and we refund them in full.

**New to paddling?**
You don't need experience. You just need to be comfortable in the water. Life jackets are included.

In our first season, 1,240 people paddled with us, and none of those trips needed emergency services.
