## How it works

**1. Set up your clients.** Save a rate card for each one: their per-word rate, their hourly rate, or their project fee.

**2. Bring in the work.** Import word counts from the CSV exports of the translation tools you already use. You don't need to retype anything.

**3. Send the invoice.** Pick the template in English, German, Spanish or French and send it. If it goes overdue, Wordtab sends the reminder for you.

---

## Features

**Three ways to bill.** Per word, per hour or per project, whichever the job calls for.

**A rate card for every client.** Agencies and direct clients rarely pay the same rate. Save each client's rates once and they apply to every invoice.

**CSV word-count import.** Take the count from your translation tool's export and skip the manual copying.

**Invoice templates in four languages.** English, German, Spanish and French, so the invoice matches your client's language.

**Overdue reminders.** Wordtab sends them, so you don't have to write the awkward email.

**Your data stays yours.** Export everything as CSV whenever you like.
