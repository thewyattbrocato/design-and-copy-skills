**Tallybee: Budgeting for Freelancers**

**Know what's safe to spend, even when your income isn't steady.**

Freelance income comes in lumps. Tallybee takes the guesswork out of it. It sets aside your tax money from every client payment and tells you what's left to spend this week.

**TAX MONEY, SET ASIDE AUTOMATICALLY**
Log a client payment or let it sync from your bank. Tallybee puts your tax share aside right away, so April doesn't surprise you.

**A "SAFE TO SPEND THIS WEEK" NUMBER**
Open the app and see one number: what you can spend this week without dipping into taxes or upcoming bills. It updates as money comes in and goes out.

**BUILT FOR LUMPY INCOME**
Big month, slow month, or a client who pays 60 days late. Tallybee smooths out the swings, so your budget doesn't fall apart when income does.

**CONNECTS TO YOUR BANK, READ-ONLY**
Link your bank account to import payments and spending. Tallybee can only view your transactions. It can't move money.

**HOW IT WORKS**
1. Connect your bank or log a payment manually.
2. Tallybee sets aside your tax share.
3. Check your safe-to-spend number for the week.
