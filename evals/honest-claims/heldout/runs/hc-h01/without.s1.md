## Born at the Saturday Market

Copper Hollow started at a folding table at the Saturday farmers market. People tried a sauce, came back the next week, and brought friends. We still show up at that table, and now you can order from anywhere.

Every batch is made by hand in a commercial kitchen outside Duluth. We make small runs, so each bottle gets real attention.

## The Lineup

**Smoked Peach Habanero**
Sweet peach and smoke, with habanero heat that builds as you eat. Try it on grilled chicken, pork, or a breakfast sandwich.

**Green Ghost**
Jalapeño and tomatillo make a bright, tangy green sauce. It's good on tacos, eggs, and just about anything that wants a squeeze of lime.

**Black Garlic Mild**
Deep, savory, and easy on the heat. It's the one to hand someone who says they don't like hot sauce.

**Copper Reaper**
Our hottest. It's for people who ask for more heat and mean it. Start with a drop.

## Why Three Weeks

We ferment every batch for three weeks before it goes in a bottle. Fermenting takes patience, and it builds depth you can't get by blending peppers and vinegar and calling it done. You can taste the difference in the first spoonful.

---

## Peppers We Can Shake Hands With

Our peppers come from two farms we know personally. One of them is in our own county. We know who grows them, how they're grown, and when they're ready. When you know the farmer, you can trust the pepper.
