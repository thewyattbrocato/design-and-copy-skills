## The Sauces

**Smoked Peach Habanero**
Sweet peach, smoke, and a habanero kick that builds slowly. Good on pork, chicken, and anything off the grill.

**Green Ghost**
Jalapeño and tomatillo. Bright, green, and tangy. Try it on eggs, tacos, or a bowl of beans.

**Black Garlic Mild**
Deep, savory black garlic with the heat turned down. This is the one for people who say they don't like hot sauce.

**Copper Reaper**
The hot one. It's for people who ask for the hottest thing on the menu and mean it.

All four are $9 a bottle.

## Why Three Weeks

Every batch ferments for three weeks before it goes into a bottle. We could move faster, but fermentation is where the flavor gets its depth, so we give it the time.

We make each batch by hand, in small runs, in a commercial kitchen outside Duluth. We don't scale it up or cut the timeline. If a batch isn't ready, it waits.
