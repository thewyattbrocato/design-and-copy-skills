## Why it takes three weeks
Every batch ferments for three weeks before we bottle it. That's the whole secret, and it's slow on purpose. Fermenting gives the sauce a rounder, more complex flavor than a quick cook can. We'd rather wait than rush a bottle out the door.

## Peppers from people we know
Our peppers come from two farms. We know both of them personally, and one is in our own county. When you know the farmer, you know what's going into the pot.

## Small batches, start to finish
We make everything in small batches in a rented commercial kitchen outside Duluth. We make fewer bottles at a time, so we can pay attention to each batch.
