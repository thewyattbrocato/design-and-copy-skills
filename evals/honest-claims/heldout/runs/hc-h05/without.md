**Before you publish**

- **Testimonials:** Ask your first patients for a sentence or two while their progress is fresh. Get written permission to use their words and first name. If the section is thin at launch, it's fine to ship it with only "What we measure" and "What you can expect" and add quotes later. Delete the placeholder blocks rather than leaving them visible.
- **Claims:** I avoided phrases like "best" or "proven results" and any success rates. Add figures later only if you actually track them. Check your regulator's advertising rules on testimonials, since some jurisdictions restrict them for health professionals.
- **Details:** Add your physiotherapists' names and credentials to the About blurb if you like. That's often the biggest trust builder, and I didn't want to guess at it.

I can adjust the tone (warmer, more clinical) or draft a short message for asking patients for feedback.
