## About Northgate Physio

Northgate Physio is a two-physiotherapist clinic on the east side, focused on knee and shoulder rehab. We work out of a converted bakery. Every new patient starts with a full assessment, and every session after that is 45 minutes of one-on-one time. We want you to understand your injury and trust the plan for getting better.
