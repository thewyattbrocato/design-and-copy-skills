Bold works by contrast. It stands out only because the text around it is regular weight. When everything important is bold, nothing contrasts, so the eye has no clear place to land.

The page also turns into a wall of heavy text. Readers can't tell what matters most, so they skim or skip it.

Instead, rank your content. Choose one or two levels of emphasis, and use size, color, spacing, or position for the rest. Reserve bold for the few things a reader must not miss.

If you're unsure, squint at the page. Whatever you can still make out is your real hierarchy. If it's unclear, you have too much emphasis.
