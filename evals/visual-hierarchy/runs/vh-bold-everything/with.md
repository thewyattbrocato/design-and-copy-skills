Bold works by contrast. It stands out only because the text around it isn't bold. If every important label is bold, nothing is lighter to stand out against, so the eye has no starting point. The page reads as one flat level, and people have to scan everything to find what matters.

Emphasis is a limited budget. Spend it on about a tenth of what's visible. Choose one technique, such as semibold, and use it the same way every time.

Rank your labels first. Give only the top level the weight, and let the rest stay regular. The few bold items will then read as important.
