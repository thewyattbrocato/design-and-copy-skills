**Pick: a small icon plus a bold sentence in the billing section.**

It puts the warning where customers look when thinking about payment, and it can carry the specifics: "Your card ending 4242 will be charged $29 tomorrow." The icon draws the eye, and the bold sentence can't be misread.

The other options each have a flaw. A site-wide red banner is disruptive, reads as an error, and gets tuned out. A modal blocks users and is dismissed without being read. A bold line by the plan name lacks room for amount and card details, and it's not near the payment context.
