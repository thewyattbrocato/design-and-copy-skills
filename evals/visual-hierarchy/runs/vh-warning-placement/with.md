**Pick: the small icon plus a bold sentence in the billing section.**

The charge is a fact about billing, so it belongs where the customer looks when they think about money. A bold sentence like "Your card ending 4242 will be charged $29 tomorrow" sits next to the card details and plan cost. It's specific and it's in context.

The other options fail in different ways:
- **Red banner on every page:** red signals an error, and a routine charge isn't one. A banner on every page also gets ignored within days.
- **Bold line beside the plan name:** it has no card or amount context, and it competes with the plan name.
- **Modal on page load:** it interrupts the user, trains them to dismiss it, and is gone once closed.
