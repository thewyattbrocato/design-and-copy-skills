Use system stacks, since an offline PWA shouldn't depend on a web font being cached: `font-family: system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif;` for the interface, and `font-family: Charter, "Iowan Old Style", Palatino, Georgia, serif;` for recipe titles and instructions if you want a warmer reading feel. If you'd rather ship a custom font, self-host it and precache the files in your service worker, keeping the same stack as the fallback.
