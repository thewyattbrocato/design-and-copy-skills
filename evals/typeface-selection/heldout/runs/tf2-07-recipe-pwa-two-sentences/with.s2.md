Use the system font stack, since an offline PWA can't count on a web font loading: `font-family: system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif;`. If you want a warmer feel for the recipe steps, give long reading text a serif stack like `Charter, "Iowan Old Style", Georgia, serif` and keep the system sans for buttons and labels. I assumed a Latin-script audience on screens.
