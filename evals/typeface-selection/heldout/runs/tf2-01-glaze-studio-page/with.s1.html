Two people, one small studio

Kilnfolk is a two-person studio. We throw, trim, glaze and fire everything ourselves, and we pack every order ourselves too.

We make a limited number of pieces each month because that’s what one kiln and two sets of hands can do well. You may see a fingerprint, a drip or a slight wobble. That’s the pottery being made by people.
