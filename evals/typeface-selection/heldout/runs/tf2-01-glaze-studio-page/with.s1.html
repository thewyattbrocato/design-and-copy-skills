Two people, one studio, a lot of clay.

Kilnfolk is run by the two of us out of a shared garage studio. One of us throws on the wheel, the other mixes and tests the glazes, and we both load the kiln.

We make only what fits in a single month’s firings. That means small runs, slight differences between pieces, and the occasional drop that sells out in an afternoon. Every mug and bowl is dishwasher and microwave safe, and meant to be used daily.

— The Kilnfolk studio
