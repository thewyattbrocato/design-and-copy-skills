Two people, one small studio.

Kilnfolk started in a shared garage with a secondhand wheel and a kiln that ran hotter than advertised. We still throw every piece ourselves, mix our own glazes, and fire in batches small enough to know each one.

We release one drop a month rather than keeping a standing inventory. It lets us slow down, test new glazes, and make things we'd want in our own cupboards. Dishwasher and microwave safe, and made to chip a little charmingly over the years.

— The two of us, covered in slip
