Two people, one small studio.

Kilnfolk started in a shared garage with a secondhand kiln and a lot of cracked mugs. Two years on, there are two of us: one who throws and one who glazes. We argue happily about both.

We make fewer things on purpose. A small monthly run lets us pay attention to each piece, and it means you’re buying something we’d keep for our own cupboards.

— The Kilnfolk pair
