Two people, one small studio

Kilnfolk is a two-person studio. One of us throws, the other glazes, and we both argue about the kiln schedule.

We make mugs and bowls in small batches so we can pay attention to each one. You'll see fingerprints, small variations in the glaze, and the occasional uneven rim. We think that makes them better.

Each piece is food-safe, dishwasher-safe, and made to be used every day.
