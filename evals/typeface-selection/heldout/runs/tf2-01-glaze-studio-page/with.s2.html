Two people,
one small studio.

Kilnfolk is a two-person pottery studio. We throw, trim, glaze, and fire everything ourselves, from the first lump of clay to the last wipe of the foot ring.

We work in small monthly drops because that's what two people and one kiln can do well. It also lets us try new glazes without making hundreds of anything. Some pieces will have a thumbprint, a drip, or a spot where the glaze pooled. We think that's part of what makes them yours.

Everything is food-safe, dishwasher-safe, and microwave-safe. We hope it gets chipped, stained, and loved.

— The Kilnfolk potters
