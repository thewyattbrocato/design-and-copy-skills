Two people, one small studio.

Kilnfolk started with a secondhand kiln and a shared habit of keeping the mugs that came out a little lopsided. We now make a few dozen pieces a month, and that’s on purpose.

We throw, trim, glaze and fire everything ourselves. Working in small batches means we can test glazes properly, fix what isn’t right, and make things we’d want to use every day: dishwasher-safe, microwave-safe, and sturdy enough to be dropped once or twice.

— The Kilnfolk potters
