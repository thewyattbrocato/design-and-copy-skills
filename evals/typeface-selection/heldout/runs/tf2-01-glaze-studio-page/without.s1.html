Two people, one kiln, no hurry.

Kilnfolk is a pottery studio run by two of us out of a converted garage. We started making mugs for friends and never quite stopped.

Everything is wheel-thrown in stoneware and glazed by hand, so you'll find thumbprints, drips, and small variations. We think that's the good part. Pieces are dishwasher and microwave safe, and built to take daily use.

We make a limited number each month rather than a lot all at once. It keeps the work careful and the studio manageable.

— The Kilnfolk studio
