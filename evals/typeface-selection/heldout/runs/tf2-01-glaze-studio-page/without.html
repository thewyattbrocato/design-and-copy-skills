Two people, one kiln.

Kilnfolk is a two-person studio. We make a small number of mugs and bowls each month because that's what fits in the kiln and what we can make well.

Every piece is wheel-thrown, glazed by hand, and fired to cone 6. You'll see fingerprints, drips, and small differences from one piece to the next. We think that's the point: these are meant to be used every day, not kept on a shelf.
