**Your positioning works. You don't need a rewrite—just tighten three things.**

This statement has all the elements of a solid position: specific audience ("night-shift nurses who struggle to sleep during the day"), clear alternatives ("generic blackout curtains or sleep apps"), proof of difference (it's one kit, bundled for shift work, includes a checklist), and a benefit your audience can repeat. Your disclamer about clinical studies is actually a credibility move. It tested well with the people who buy, and it's truthful. A rewrite would likely lose that.

**Fixes needed:**

1. **"a set of blackout-and-white-noise kits"** → **"a blackout-and-white-noise kit"** (singular, not "set of"). You're selling one thing with three components, not multiple kits.

2. **"turn any bedroom into a place you can sleep at noon"** → **"lets you sleep soundly at noon"** or **"lets you rest at any hour."** The original drifts ("a place you can sleep" is what bedrooms already are); you're solving *when* they can sleep, not whether the room is a bedroom.

3. Optionally break up the last sentence if it feels cramped. **"We have not run clinical studies—our claim is practical, not medical."** (em dash instead of semicolon reads faster).

That's it. The positioning is strong enough that your lead might be seeing something else: unclear messaging in other places (your site copy, your ads, how sales pitches it), which is a different problem. But the core statement itself? Keep it.
