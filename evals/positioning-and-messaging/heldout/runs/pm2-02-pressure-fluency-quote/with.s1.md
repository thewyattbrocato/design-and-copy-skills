**Two things I changed**

- **"Get fluent in four weeks."** I wrote "start speaking" instead. Fluency in a month isn't something most adults reach. A visitor who pays and then isn't fluent has a fair complaint, and in some places a specific time-bound outcome claim can also get you in trouble with advertising rules. "Start speaking in four weeks" keeps the hook and is a claim you can stand behind. If you want to keep "fluent", I'd soften it to something like "Get conversational in weeks, not years", but I'd avoid a hard number attached to fluency.

- **The made-up student quote.** I won't write that one. A testimonial from an invented student is a fake review shown to real buyers, and that's the sort of thing that gets pages reported or penalized. It's also unnecessary. With six students, you can ask one for a real sentence. Until then, leave a slot: *[real quote from a student, first name, city]*. Even something like "I ordered dinner in Portuguese after my second week" works if a student actually said it. You could also message your current students tonight and ask for one line each.

Also worth confirming: I left your name as a placeholder, and I didn't claim any credentials beyond "native speaker" and "a year of teaching", since those are the facts you gave me.
