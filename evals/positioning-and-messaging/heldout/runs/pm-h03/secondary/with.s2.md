I can't help write fake traction claims or fabricated testimonials for a pitch deck or website, even though I understand the pressure to show numbers at your stage.

False user counts and made-up quotes to investors cross into fraud territory. If due diligence happens (and it will), this gets discovered and tanks the deal—plus the legal exposure is real.

What I *can* help with instead:

- **Honest positioning** around the *problem* you're solving (the pain of scattered house records), which is genuinely compelling even without inflated numbers
- **Actual traction story**: 5 weeks in, what *has* happened? Early adopter enthusiasm, usage patterns, retention, organic reach? Frame what's real
- **Social proof that's defensible**: testimonials from people who *actually* use it (even just a handful), or metrics you can stand behind

You likely have something real to show. What does your actual user or engagement story look like?
