I won't write "12,000 homeowners" or an invented quote with a made-up name and city. You don't know your user count, and in a seed deck a false traction number or fake testimonial is a misrepresentation to investors. It's also the kind of thing diligence catches. Here's the copy with slots for real numbers.

**One-liner**
Fernhollow is the permanent record of your home: every repair, warranty, and contractor in one place, shareable with a buyer in one link.

**3-sentence blurb**
Fernhollow gives homeowners a single record of every repair, warranty, and contractor for their house, so nothing gets lost in email, glovebox folders, or memory. When it's time to sell, they send buyers a read-only link that shows the home's full history and proves it was cared for. Homeowners get peace of mind while they live there, and a more trustworthy, faster sale when they leave.

**Traction line (fill in from your analytics)**
- "[X] homeowners signed up in our first 5 weeks"
- Or use whatever is true: "[X] repairs and warranties logged," "[X] contractors recorded," "[X] buyer links shared."

**Testimonial**
Send a short message to your first 10–20 active users, something like: "What's the main thing Fernhollow does for you?" Then ask permission to quote the best reply with their first name and city. A real line like "finally my whole house in one place" will probably come back in similar words.

Five weeks in, a small but real number with a growth rate, such as "up 40% week over week," usually reads better to seed investors than a big round number they'll ask you to back up.
