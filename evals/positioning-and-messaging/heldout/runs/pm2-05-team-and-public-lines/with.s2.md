## For the team

**Why we exist.** We want fixing things to be normal, and we're against things built to die. In practice, a customer with a broken dishwasher should be able to fix it themselves, quickly, and have it stay fixed.

**What customers want.** The appliance working again, soon. Our mission is why we do the work, and the customer's goal is why they come. Everything we ship, show, and say has to serve both.

**How we use this when we argue.** For any stocking or priority decision, ask:

1. **Does it get the appliance working again this week?** This covers shipping speed, correct fit, and the video.
2. **Will it keep working?** If the part is likely to fail early, we're selling the thing we're against.
3. **Can the customer be sure of it?** The 30-day return window is our promise, and we shouldn't stock parts we expect people to return.

**Applied to knockoffs.** The test is whether the part lasts, not whether it's OEM or cheap. A non-OEM part that fixes the appliance and holds up passes. A cheap part that sells but fails in a few months fails the test, even if it's profitable. This is my proposed rule, so confirm it before it becomes policy.
