**Option 1** (130 characters)
"Mobile app that turns job site photos and voice notes into finished quotes and invoices—before you leave the driveway."

**Option 2** (123 characters)
"Plumbline turns job photos and notes into invoices and quotes using your pricing—capture payment before you leave the site."

**Option 3** (140 characters)
"Turns job photos and voice notes into customer-ready quotes and invoices for plumbing and HVAC contractors to collect payment faster."

---

**Option 1** leads with the speed and convenience angle—the "before you leave" hook that sets you apart from doing it later at the office. **Option 2** emphasizes that contractors use their own pricing (integration), not a generic system, and shifts the outcome to payment collection. **Option 3** is outcome-focused—it's about faster payment, which is the real reason they'd care, with "customer-ready" showing that quality isn't sacrificed.

For an accelerator, Option 1 or 2 will read faster because they're more distinctive. Option 3 is broader but may sound more familiar.
