Here are three options. I counted the characters by hand, spaces included.

1. **130 characters:** Turns plumbing and HVAC techs' phone photos and voice notes into a customer-ready quote or invoice before they leave the driveway.

2. **135 characters:** Plumbline turns a plumber's or HVAC tech's job-site photos and voice notes into a finished quote or invoice, using your own price list.

3. **128 characters:** Plumbline: for independent plumbing and HVAC contractors. Snap photos, record notes, send the quote before leaving the driveway.

- **Option 1** leaves out the product name, since the form probably already shows it. It's the tightest description of what the product does.
- **Option 2** names the product and mentions the price-list feature. That feature is a real differentiator because quotes use the contractor's own prices.
- **Option 3** names the target customer (independent contractors) and reads most like a tagline.

I left out team size, the March launch, and the missing accounting integration, since none of them help in 140 characters. I also made no traction claims.

Paste your pick into the form and check that it isn't cut off. Curly apostrophes or em dashes can count differently in some forms.
