## Marrow & Co Voice & Tone Guide

**Who We Sound Like**

We sound like a former restaurant manager who now understands financial reports—because that's who we are. We've worked the line, managed staff during dinner rush, and sweated the small stuff. Now we translate spreadsheets the same way we'd explain a menu to a nervous server: clearly, directly, without pretense.

**Core Principles**

*Knowledgeable without gatekeeping.* We know our stuff, but we don't prove it by using jargon. Big words don't make us smarter; they just make clients nervous. We explain *why* something matters before we explain *what* it is.

*Confident but humble.* We've seen what works and what doesn't. We're not here to tell you you're doing it wrong—we're here because you need someone to handle the numbers so you can run the restaurant. That's a partnership, not a lecture.

*Real over polished.* We use plain language. We say "money left over" instead of "operating margin." We write like we talk. A little bit casual is fine; sloppy is not.

*Restaurant-specific.* We don't talk to restaurants like we talk to retail shops. We reference seasonal swings, labor cost ratios, food cost changes, cash flow timing. We sound like we've actually worked here.

**What We Avoid**

- Corporate jargon ("best-in-class," "synergy," "take your business to the next level")
- Assuming you don't know your own business
- Sounding cheerful when discussing problems
- Fancy words when simple ones work better
- Acting like finances are rocket science

**How It Shows Up**

Our tone shifts slightly based on context:
- When explaining concepts: patient, step-by-step, no rushing
- When discussing problems: direct and solution-focused, not alarming
- When celebrating wins: genuinely pleased, not over-the-top
- When giving advice: "Here's what we've seen work" not "You should"

## The Rewrite

**Original:** "At Marrow & Co, we provide best-in-class financial solutions tailored to the unique needs of your hospitality business. Our dedicated team of experts is committed to delivering exceptional results."

**New:** "We handle your bookkeeping and monthly reports so you can actually see what's happening in your restaurant. Every team member here managed a restaurant—we know how cash flow works when you're short-staffed on a Saturday night, and we explain your numbers in a way that makes sense."

---

**Original:** "Contact us today to learn how we can help take your restaurant to the next level!"

**New:** "Curious how much you're actually spending on labor, or why your food costs spiked last month? Let's talk. We'll walk through your numbers and show you what they actually mean."

---

The rewrite trades the generic confidence for something more specific: actual proof that you get restaurants, and an invitation that assumes they have real questions (not a vague aspiration). It sounds like people talking, not a template.
