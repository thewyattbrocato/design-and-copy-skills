# Voice & Tone Guide for Marrow & Co

**Who We Are**
We're ex-restaurant people. We've been in your shoes—managing chaos, hitting deadlines, dealing with impossible vendors. We get that you didn't go into restaurants to become an accountant. We did, but only after figuring out that this is what we actually love doing.

**Our Voice: Straight Talk from Someone Who Gets It**

We speak like we actually work in restaurants, because we do. Our clients are busy. Their margins are thin. They don't have time for corporate speak or financial jargon that makes them feel behind. So we don't use it.

We explain things plainly because clarity is a sign of respect—not simplification. If we can't explain something in a way that lands the first time, we haven't understood it well enough ourselves. We'll use specific numbers, concrete examples, and "here's what this means for you" language.

We're confident without being cocky. We know what we're doing. We've seen P&Ls from a hundred restaurants. But we never make it sound like there's an obvious thing you should've already known. Restaurant financials are genuinely complicated. Anyone who makes you feel stupid for asking a question doesn't deserve your business.

We're helpful without being pushy. We're not here to sell you things. We're here to help you see what's actually happening in your business so you can make better decisions. Sometimes that means telling you something you don't want to hear—but we'll explain why it matters.

**Tone: Practical, Warm, Direct**

- **Practical**: We focus on what actually helps. Not trends, not theory—what moves the needle.
- **Warm**: We remember that behind every number is a person who built something. We celebrate wins with you.
- **Direct**: We cut through the noise. Short sentences. No hedging. No "at this time" or "we're here to explore possibilities."

**What We Sound Like**
- "Here's what your food costs actually are, and why that matters"
- "Your labor number is creeping up. Let's talk about where"
- "This looks good. You should feel good about it"
- "We missed something. Our bad—here's how we fix it"

**What We Don't Sound Like**
- "Leveraging synergies"
- "Best-in-class solutions"
- "Optimize your fiscal trajectory"
- Anything that makes someone feel small
