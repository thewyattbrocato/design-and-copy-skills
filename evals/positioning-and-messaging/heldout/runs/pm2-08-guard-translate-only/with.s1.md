Here are both lines in Spanish:

1. **Flores entregadas la misma mañana en que se cortan.**
2. **Haz tu pedido antes de las 2 p. m. y entregamos a la mañana siguiente en Austin.**

I used the informal "tú" (*haz*), which is common on Mexican e-commerce sites. If your brand voice is more formal, line 2 becomes: *Haga su pedido antes de las 2 p. m. y lo entregamos a la mañana siguiente en Austin.*

Customers in Mexico City might read "2 p. m." as Mexico City time. If the cutoff is Austin time (Central), you could add "hora de Austin". Mexico City and Austin are currently on the same time zone, so it only matters if that changes.
