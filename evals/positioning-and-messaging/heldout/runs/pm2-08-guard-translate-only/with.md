Notes:
- If your site addresses customers as "usted" rather than "tú," line 2 becomes: *Haga su pedido antes de las 2 p. m. y recíbalo a la mañana siguiente en Austin.*
- Since the customers are in Mexico City but the delivery is in Austin, you may want to add "(hora de Austin)" after "2 p. m." if the cutoff is in Austin's time zone. Austin is typically 1 hour ahead of Mexico City in summer and the same as Mexico City in winter, so the cutoff could otherwise be ambiguous.
