## Tidewell Launch Brief

**What We Are**

Tidewell is operations software purpose-built for small commercial laundries. We track every bag and load from drop-off to delivery using barcode tags, automate customer notifications via text, and let shops price by weight or piece with per-customer rate cards. It runs on a tablet at the counter and phones for drivers, works offline, and syncs when connection returns. Two people who ran a laundry for six years built it because they lived the problem.

**Who It's For**

Wash-and-fold shops, hotel and spa linen contractors, and similar operations with 3 to 25 employees. These are businesses where the owner or a key manager currently spends hours a week on what Tidewell automates: tracking loads, managing customer pickups, pricing jobs, and keeping drivers and customers in sync. They're losing money to mistakes and inefficiency but can't afford a six-figure enterprise system or a dedicated operations person.

**What We're Up Against**

The incumbent is chaos: paper tickets stuffed in bags, spreadsheets that fall out of sync, or retail point-of-sale systems that don't understand laundry workflows at all. No shop owner wants to run operations this way; they do it because there's never been an alternative that fit their size and budget. There are laundry-specific systems out there, but most are built for industrial scale or corporate chains, cost $500+ per month, and require weeks of training and integration work. Smaller shops look at them and decide to stick with what they know.

**Why We're Different**

We're built *by* operators *for* operators at this scale. That shows in three places: the product works offline because laundries lose signal in the back. Barcode tags stay on bags through the whole cycle so nothing gets lost or delayed, and customers know the minute their order is ready. Pricing is flexible because every shop does it differently—by weight, by piece, or per-job—and rate cards let you set per-customer pricing without rebuilding it each time. We're also cheap. We're not trying to be enterprise software. We're trying to save a shop owner three hours a week and $200 a month they're wasting on mistakes, and we're pricing it so that's immediately worth it.

**What We Say About Ourselves**

Here's the version for the outside:

Tidewell brings operations software to small commercial laundries. Built by people who ran one, it solves the core problem: tracking thousands of bags, bottles, linens, and loads without losing them, losing time, or losing your mind. A barcode tag stays on each order from drop-off to delivery. Text notifications keep customers updated automatically. Flexible pricing by weight, piece, or job means you can set per-customer rates without rewriting them. It works on a tablet at the counter and phones for drivers, stays on if the connection drops, and syncs later. For shops with 3 to 25 people, it's the first software that feels built for them instead of forced onto them.

**What's True Right Now**

We have 9 shops in pilot and they're using it. We don't have published case studies yet, customer quotes, or measured numbers on hours saved—that comes next. We have a roadmap but we're not there yet: no payroll, no accounting software integration (CSV export only). We're starting focused on the core—tracking, notifications, pricing—and building from there based on what pilots need most.

**For the Team**

This is honest positioning. We're not overpromising. We're not chasing every feature. We're solving one real problem for a real group of people who have no good options today, and we're doing it at a price they can afford. That's enough. Stay close to that when you talk to prospects, press, or each other. The credibility isn't in case studies; it's in the fact that we've lived the problem and we're solving it the way we would want it solved.
