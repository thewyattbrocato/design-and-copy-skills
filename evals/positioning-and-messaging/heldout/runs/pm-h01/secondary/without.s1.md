**TIDEWELL MESSAGE BRIEF**

**What We Are**

Tidewell is operations management software built specifically for small commercial laundries. It replaces the patchwork of paper tickets, spreadsheets, and hastily-configured retail systems that currently run most wash-and-fold shops, hotel linen contractors, and spa services. Tidewell tracks customer bags and linen loads from drop-off to delivery using barcode tags, automates customer notifications, manages pricing by weight or piece with per-customer rate cards, and runs on a tablet at the counter and phones in the field—with offline capability and automatic sync when the internet returns. It's built for the reality of a 3 to 25-person operation: simple enough to learn in an hour, fast enough to not slow down your day, and robust enough to handle the steady chaos of a real laundry.

**Who It's For**

Small commercial laundries: wash-and-fold shops, hotel and resort linen contractors, spa and salon service providers—the shops that are profitable but drowning in manual operations. These are businesses that already have customers and revenue, but spend hours each day managing tickets by hand, reconciling with spreadsheets, or trying to bend a retail point-of-sale system into doing laundry work. They're run by people who care about doing the work right and scaling without hiring chaos.

**What We're Up Against**

The status quo is fragmented and broken-in-place. Most small laundries operate with paper tickets stapled to bags, a spreadsheet tracking who owes what, and text messages sent manually or not at all. Some have bought retail POS systems that don't understand laundry's weight-based or piece-based pricing. Others have built patchwork solutions in their accounting software or used one-off apps that do one thing well and nothing else. The result: lost tracking, billing errors, missed pickups, customers who don't know when their order is ready, and owners who can't see their business clearly.

There's no dominant software player in this space—no "this is what laundries use." That's an opportunity, but it's also a clue: someone would have built this already if it were easy. Small laundries have been considered too small a market. We disagree.

**Why We're Different**

Tidewell was built by two people who actually ran a laundry for six years. We're not guessing at what the problems are or how to solve them. We know the difference between a customer who needs their order Friday versus the customer who only cares about cost. We know why your driver needs an app that works offline. We understand that a tablet at the counter needs to be fast and obvious—no training manuals. We built this for the person who is both the owner and the person washing the linens at 7 a.m.

That foundation matters. We've built software that fits the shape of the business, not the other way around.

**Key Messages**

- **Tidewell eliminates manual operations that don't scale.** Paper tickets and spreadsheets work until they don't. Barcode tracking and automated customer notifications mean your team spends time on the work, not managing the work.

- **It's built for small laundries by people who ran one.** We understand the business. We know what matters and what doesn't.

- **It works offline because your internet will go down.** Tidewell runs on tablets and phones in the field, syncs when you're back online, and never loses a booking or a bag.

- **Pricing flexibility.** Weight-based, piece-based, per-customer rate cards—Tidewell adapts to how you price, not the other way around.

- **Simple to set up and use.** No consultants, no implementation project, no learning curve that takes three weeks.

**What We're Not Saying Yet**

We don't have case studies, hard time-saved numbers, or customer testimonials—we have nine shops in our pilot program and we're still learning from them. We're not positioning on features we don't have: there's no payroll integration yet, no accounting software connectors yet (CSV export only). We will build those, but we're launching on what we have and what works. Tidewell is proven in the field by real shops. We're not pretending to be bigger or more complete than we are.
