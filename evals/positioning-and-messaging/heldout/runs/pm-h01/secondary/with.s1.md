# TIDEWELL MESSAGE BRIEF

**What We Are**

Tidewell is software for small commercial laundries—wash-and-fold shops, hotel linen contractors, spa services—that replaces paper tickets and spreadsheets with digital tracking. When a customer drops off a load, we barcode it, track it through wash and fold, text the customer when it's ready for pickup, and keep the laundry's records clean. It runs on a tablet at the counter and a phone in the delivery van, works when the internet is down, and prices jobs the way laundries actually price them: by weight, by piece, or with custom rate cards per customer.

We started Tidewell because we ran a laundry for six years. We know what's broken about the existing choices.

**Who It's For**

Our core customer is a small commercial laundry with 3 to 25 employees. Most of them run a wash-and-fold service, handle linen for hotels or spas, or do both. They have dozens of customers, hundreds of loads a month, and a mix of repeat and one-time orders. Right now they track everything on paper tickets, in a spreadsheet, or in a retail point-of-sale system that wasn't made for laundry. They want to work faster and make fewer mistakes, but they can't afford a $10,000 system or the time to learn something overcomplicated.

**What They're Up Against**

Paper tickets work, but they fall out of stacks, get illegible, and create no paper trail. If a customer disputes a load count or a price, the laundry has no record to back it up. Paper slows down counter staff, drivers, and the customer who has to call to ask if their order is ready.

Spreadsheets give you data, but they're fragile. One typo kills the numbers. You can't see at a glance which loads are ready for pickup or know when a customer is coming. A spreadsheet doesn't text customers.

Retail POS systems are built for stores, not laundries. They're overkill for pricing (you don't need inventory tracking or gift cards), they don't handle laundry pricing logic (per-piece, per-pound, customer rate cards), and they assume you're online all the time—which you aren't when a power line goes down or your ISP drops.

**Why We're Different**

Tidewell is built by people who actually ran a laundry, which means we get the workflow. We built in the things that matter:

- **Barcode tracking + SMS:** Every load gets a barcode tag when it arrives. Customers get a text when their load is ready—no more calling to ask. The laundry has a complete record of who dropped off what, when, and at what price.
- **Works offline:** Your tablet and drivers' phones keep working even if the internet goes down. Changes sync back when you're online again. No lost orders, no guessing.
- **Laundry pricing:** You can price by weight, by piece, or set custom rates per customer, all in one system. No spreadsheet math, no confusion.
- **Built for the workflow:** Counter staff use the tablet, drivers use their phone, customers get a text. Each person sees exactly what they need.

**What We Say About Ourselves**

When you're talking about Tidewell—to a prospect, an investor, a pilot shop—lean on these three things:

1. **We're built for laundry, not retail.** We understand wash-and-fold shops because we ran one. Every choice we make reflects the way you actually work.

2. **We keep it simple.** No payroll, no accounting integrations, no features that slow you down. We do one thing: make tracking clear and fast so you don't lose money and your customers know when to pick up.

3. **We're reliable offline.** You don't stop working when the internet goes down, and neither do we. Your team keeps moving, orders stay safe, and everything syncs when you're back online.

We're launching with real customers in pilot. We have nine shops using Tidewell today, and we're building on what they need.
