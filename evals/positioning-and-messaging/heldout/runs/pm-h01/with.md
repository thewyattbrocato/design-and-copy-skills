# Tidewell message brief

**One-liner**
Tidewell is laundry management software for small commercial laundries. It tracks every bag and linen load from drop-off to delivery and texts the customer when it's ready to pick up.

**Controlling idea**
Tidewell knows where every load is, from drop-off to delivery, so you don't have to chase it. Use this to test any headline, feature request or sales line. If it doesn't help a shop know where a load is or tell a customer, it isn't our message.

**Who it's for**
Wash-and-fold shops and hotel and spa linen contractors with 3 to 25 employees. Loads from many customers move through these shops at once, and someone has to keep track of them. [assumed: the strongest fit is shops that lose time to "where's my order?" questions. Check this with the pilot shops.]

Not for shops that need payroll in the same tool, or that need accounting software connected today.

**What we're up against**
We have no rival to name. The real competition is what shops use now:
- **Paper tickets.** Familiar, cheap, and they work anywhere. They can't tell a customer anything, and finding a load means someone going to look.
- **A spreadsheet.** Flexible and already on hand. It doesn't tag bags or send texts, and it only knows what someone typed in.
- **A retail point-of-sale system.** Good at taking payment at a counter. It's built for selling items, not for following a load through a shop.

We don't call these bad. We say they weren't made for laundry.

**What's different**
Each point is a feature anyone can check in a demo.
1. **A barcode tag on every bag and load.** Scan it to see where the load is, from drop-off to delivery.
2. **A text at pickup-ready.** Customers know when to come without calling.
3. **Pricing by weight or by piece, with a rate card for each customer.** A hotel contract and a walk-in can run in the same system.
4. **A tablet at the counter, a phone for drivers, and it works offline.** It syncs later, so a dropped connection doesn't stop the counter.
5. **Built by two people who ran a laundry for six years.** This is true and we can say it. [unproven: that it shows in the product. A pilot shop saying so in their own words would back it up.]

**Proof we have**
Nine shops use Tidewell in a pilot. That is all we have. There are no case studies, no time-saved figures and no customer quotes yet, so we don't claim speed, savings or satisfaction.

**Objections, answered plainly**
- *"Does it do payroll?"* No.
- *"Does it connect to our accounting software?"* Not yet. Today it exports CSV files. We don't promise a date.
- *"Is it proven?"* It's in use at nine pilot shops. We'll share results when we have them.
- *"What if the internet drops?"* The tablet and the driver phone keep working and sync later.

**Words**
Use: bags, loads, drop-off, pickup-ready, rate card, shop, tag. Avoid: solution, platform, streamline, seamless, end-to-end. Use one name per thing: "tag" for the barcode tag, "text" for the customer notice.

Don't say: faster, saves time, customers love it, integrates with accounting, trusted by.

**The few things we say about ourselves**
1. Every bag and load tracked, drop-off to delivery.
2. Customers get a text when it's ready.
3. Priced by weight or piece, with a rate card for each customer.
4. Works offline, syncs later.
5. Built by people who ran a laundry for six years.

**Variants**
The claim stays the same for every audience. Only the emphasis changes. Wash-and-fold shops hear points 1 and 2 first. Hotel and spa contractors hear points 3 and 1 first.
