**Elevator pitch (say this out loud):**
"We help independent bookstores run events—readings, book clubs, signings—so they can focus on building community instead of managing the logistics."

**Website tagline:**
"Event management built for independent bookstores"

---

These work because they name who (independent bookstores) and what they use instead (manual logistics), then show what that frees them to do. But they'll land harder if I know one thing: **What's the single biggest pain point bookstores mention when they talk about running events?** Is it getting people to show up? Managing signups? Promoting the event? Handling the logistics? Something else?

That answer will let me sharpen what makes your thing different from a spreadsheet or Eventbrite. Right now I'm betting the pain is coordination/admin overhead, but if it's something else—like they can't get visibility or they lose money on events—that changes how you'd position it and which sentence you'd lead with.
