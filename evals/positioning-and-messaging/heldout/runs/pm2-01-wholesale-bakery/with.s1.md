Marrow Lane Bread is two bakers, Dessa and Calloway, working out of a unit behind a tire shop. We make three breads and nothing else: a country sourdough, a seeded rye, and a milk bun built for sandwiches. The sourdough gets a long cold ferment, about 36 hours, so the flavor is already there before it reaches the oven. We bake through the night and deliver to cafés and restaurants before 6am, six days a week, so your bread is on the shelf before you unlock the door. Our minimum is 20 loaves per delivery. Because we're small, the person who mixed your dough is the person who drops it off.
