> Country sourdough, seeded rye, and milk buns, baked by two bakers and delivered to your café before 6am, six days a week.

**About paragraph:**

> Marrow Lane Bread is Dessa and Calloway, two bakers making three breads for the cafés and restaurants around [town]: a country sourdough, a seeded rye, and a milk bun made for sandwiches. The sourdough gets a 36-hour cold ferment. We do it slowly on purpose, because time is what gives the loaf its flavor. Our van arrives before 6am, six days a week, so your bread is in the kitchen before the day starts, not mid-morning. We keep the list short so we can make each loaf properly, and we set a minimum of 20 loaves per delivery so every run is worth making. If your bread now comes late and could have come from anywhere, we'd like to change that.

- **Why it sounds established:** it doesn't claim anything you can't yet show. I left out "since 20XX," "trusted by," and "best." A made-up track record would hurt you the first time a chef asks about it. What reads as established is the specifics: the 36 hours, the 6am schedule, the 20-loaf minimum, and a short list of breads. Once you have real café names or a start date, add them as a line under the paragraph.
- **Fill in the town:** replace [town] with the real one. I left the tire shop out, since it matters less to buyers than the delivery time. It could make a good, honest line on a "where we bake" section if you want some personality.
