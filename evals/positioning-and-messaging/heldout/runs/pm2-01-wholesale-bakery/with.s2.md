**About paragraph**

Marrow Lane Bread is a wholesale bakery run by two bakers, Dessa and Calloway. We make three breads and do them properly: a country sourdough with a 36-hour cold ferment, a seeded rye, and a milk bun built for sandwiches. We deliver to cafés and restaurants before 6am, six days a week, so the bread is on your counter before you open and not mid-morning after the rush has started. When you call, one of the two people who baked your order answers. Our minimum is 20 loaves per delivery, which keeps us focused on kitchens that bake and serve bread every day. If that's you, we'd like to be your bread.
