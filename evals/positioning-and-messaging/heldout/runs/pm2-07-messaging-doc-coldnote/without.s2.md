# Coldnote Messaging Doc

## 1. Who it's for
Small food producers who keep product in coolers and freezers: creameries, butchers, small caterers, farmers-market vendors. Today most keep a paper log and check a dial thermometer by hand. Our buyer is the owner or the person who opens up and closes down.

## 2. How we describe it
**One line:** Coldnote is a wireless temperature sensor and app that texts you when your cooler or freezer drifts out of range, and keeps the log for you.

**Pricing:** $29 per sensor, $12 a month per location. Battery lasts about 14 months.

## 3. Versions by buyer
**Creamery / dairy:** Your product is the most sensitive thing in the building. Set the range you want, and Coldnote texts you when a reading leaves it, even at 3 a.m. "The alert came at 3 a.m. and the walk-in was at 46°F. We moved the dairy before it turned." — Marisol Quen, owner, Quen Family Creamery.

**Butcher:** Stop walking to the walk-in to check a dial. Coldnote watches the cooler and freezer all day and night, alerts you by text, and keeps the readings. Walk-ins with metal walls will need our $79 hub.

**Small caterer:** Your coolers move between kitchens and vans. A $29 sensor in each one means you aren't relying on someone remembering to check. Confirm Wi-Fi is available wherever it sits (see section 4).

**Farmers-market vendor:** Cheap to start: $29 for a sensor and $12 a month. Note that sensors need Wi-Fi, so this fits best for storage at home or at a unit, not a stall without a connection.

**Buyers in Marlow or Dunmore County:** The log exports as a PDF laid out like the temperature log forms health inspectors in your county use. Other counties get a plain PDF.

## 4. The awkward bits
- **Wi-Fi.** Sensors need it. Metal-walled walk-ins often block the signal, so those locations need the $79 hub inside the cooler. Say this early, before the sale.
- **Cold limit.** Sensors are rated to -25°C. Below that, we don't guarantee them. Don't promise performance in colder units.
- **Compliance.** Coldnote is not a certified food-safety system. We make no compliance claim. Say it plainly. The PDF is a convenient record, not an approval.
- **Inspector-style PDF.** Only for Marlow and Dunmore. Don't imply it for other counties.
- **We're small.** Two people. Lean into it: you'll talk to the people who built it.

## 5. What we can back up today vs. what we need
**Can back up today**
- Price, battery life (about 14 months), -25°C rating, Wi-Fi and hub requirements.
- Text alerts when a reading leaves the owner's range, and a saved log.
- The Marlow and Dunmore PDF format.
- 9 kitchens have used it in a pilot since spring.
- One approved quote from Marisol Quen.

**Still need**
- Published case studies.
- Survey or outcome numbers. Don't say "saves X% of spoiled product" or "most customers say…".
- Any compliance or certification claim.
- Wider testimonials. One quote is one customer, so don't say "customers say."

Coldnote is built by a two-person team in Madison and has been used by nine kitchens in a pilot since spring.
