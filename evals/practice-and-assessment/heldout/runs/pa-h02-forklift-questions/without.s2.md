# Forklift Safety Quiz – Kestrel Ridge Cold Storage

**10 questions** (a mix of multiple choice and true/false), one per topic from the session, with two on the pre-shift check and load handling since those matter most.

---

**1. How high off the floor should the forks be when you travel, and how should the mast be tilted if you're carrying a load?**
- A) 30–40 cm, tilted forward
- B) 10–15 cm, tilted slightly back
- C) 10–15 cm, tilted slightly forward
- D) Resting on the floor, mast upright

**Answer: B.**

---

**2. What is the speed limit in the aisles, and what is it inside the freezer chambers?**
- A) 10 km/h in aisles, 8 km/h in freezers
- B) 8 km/h in aisles, 5 km/h in freezers
- C) 5 km/h in aisles, 3 km/h in freezers
- D) 8 km/h everywhere

**Answer: B.**

---

**3. You're approaching a blind corner. What do you do?**

**Answer:** Sound the horn and slow to a crawl.

---

**4. True or False: It's fine to give a coworker a short lift on the forklift if they're only going a few meters.**

**Answer: False.** Never carry passengers, ever.

---

**5. Where do you find the maximum load a forklift can lift, and what do you do if your load is heavier?**

**Answer:** The capacity plate on the mast gives the maximum load. If the load is heavier, do not lift it.

---

**6. Who has right of way, you or a pedestrian? What should you do before moving near a pedestrian?**

**Answer:** Pedestrians always have right of way. Make eye contact before moving near them.

---

**7. The load you're carrying blocks your view forward. What should you do?**
- A) Lean out and look around it
- B) Drive forward slowly with the horn sounding
- C) Drive in reverse
- D) Have a coworker walk in front to guide you

**Answer: C.** If the load blocks your view, drive in reverse.

---

**8. You're going down a ramp with a load. Which way do the forks point?**
- A) Downhill
- B) Uphill
- C) Either, depending on the load
- D) Sideways

**Answer: B.** The forks point uphill.

---

**9. Name at least five things you check in the pre-shift inspection.**

**Answer:** Forks, tires, horn, brakes, lights, and hydraulic leaks (any five of these six).

---

**10. During your pre-shift check, the brakes feel weak. What do you do?**

**Answer:** Tag the forklift out and report it. Do not operate it. Anything that fails the check gets tagged out and reported.
