# Forklift Safety Quiz – Kestrel Ridge Cold Storage

## Multiple Choice

**1. When traveling, how high should the forks be off the floor?**
A) Touching the floor
B) 10–15 cm
C) 30–40 cm
D) As high as the load allows

**Answer: B**

**2. How should the mast be positioned when carrying a load?**
A) Tilted slightly back
B) Tilted forward
C) Fully upright
D) It doesn't matter

**Answer: A**

**3. What is the speed limit inside the freezer chambers?**
A) 3 km/h
B) 5 km/h
C) 8 km/h
D) 10 km/h

**Answer: B**

**4. What is the speed limit in aisles?**
A) 5 km/h
B) 8 km/h
C) 12 km/h
D) 15 km/h

**Answer: B**

**5. Where do you find the maximum load your forklift can lift?**
A) Ask your supervisor
B) On the pallet label
C) On the capacity plate on the mast
D) In the break room manual

**Answer: C**

**6. At a blind corner, what should you do?**
A) Speed up to clear it quickly
B) Sound the horn and slow to a crawl
C) Flash your lights and continue
D) Stop and wait for someone to wave you through

**Answer: B**

**7. A pre-shift check finds the brakes are not working properly. What do you do?**
A) Drive carefully and report it at end of shift
B) Use the forklift only for light loads
C) Tag it out and report it
D) Fix it yourself

**Answer: C**

**8. You are driving down a ramp with a load. Which way do the forks point?**
A) Downhill
B) Uphill
C) Sideways
D) Either, as long as you go slowly

**Answer: B**

## True / False

**9. A coworker can ride on your forklift if they hold on tightly.**
**Answer: False.** Never carry passengers, ever.

**10. If a load is slightly heavier than the capacity plate allows, you can lift it carefully.**
**Answer: False.** If a load is heavier than the capacity, do not lift it.

**11. Forklifts have right of way over pedestrians in work aisles.**
**Answer: False.** Pedestrians always have right of way.

**12. If the load blocks your view forward, you should drive in reverse.**
**Answer: True.**

## Short Answer

**13. List the six items checked in the pre-shift inspection.**
**Answer:** Forks, tires, horn, brakes, lights, hydraulic leaks.

**14. What should you do before moving near a pedestrian?**
**Answer:** Make eye contact with them.

**15. Name two situations where you must slow down or change how you drive, and say what to do in each.**
**Answer (any two):**
- Blind corner: sound the horn and slow to a crawl.
- Load blocks view: drive in reverse.
- Ramp with a load: forks point uphill.
- Freezer chamber: keep to 5 km/h.
- Pedestrian nearby: make eye contact and yield.
