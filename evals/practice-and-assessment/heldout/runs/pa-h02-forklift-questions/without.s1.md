# Forklift Safety Quiz: Kestrel Ridge Cold Storage

**1. How high off the floor should the forks be when you travel, and how should the mast be tilted if you're carrying a load?**
**Answer:** 10 to 15 cm off the floor, with the mast tilted slightly back.

**2. What is the speed limit in the aisles, and what is it inside the freezer chambers?**
**Answer:** 8 km/h in aisles and 5 km/h inside the freezer chambers.

**3. You're approaching a blind corner. What two things do you do?**
**Answer:** Sound the horn and slow to a crawl.

**4. A coworker asks for a quick ride to the other end of the warehouse. Can you give them one on the forklift?**
**Answer:** No. Never carry passengers, ever.

**5. Where do you find the maximum load a forklift can lift?**
**Answer:** On the capacity plate on the mast.

**6. A pallet is heavier than the capacity plate allows. What do you do?**
**Answer:** Do not lift it.

**7. True or false: if you're in a hurry, a forklift has right of way over a pedestrian.**
**Answer:** False. Pedestrians always have right of way. Make eye contact with a pedestrian before moving near them.

**8. The load you're carrying blocks your view forward. How should you drive?**
**Answer:** In reverse.

**9. You're driving down a ramp with a load. Which way should the forks point?**
**Answer:** Uphill.

**10. Name at least five items on the pre-shift check.**
**Answer:** Forks, tires, horn, brakes, lights, and hydraulic leaks. Any five of the six is correct.

**11. During your pre-shift check, the brakes don't feel right. What do you do?**
**Answer:** Tag the forklift out and report it. Don't operate it.

---

**Optional scenario question (good for discussion):**

**12. You're carrying a tall load into a freezer chamber, and you can't see over it. A coworker is walking toward the door. List everything you should do.**
**Answer:** Drive in reverse because the load blocks your view. Keep your speed at or below 5 km/h. Keep the forks 10 to 15 cm off the floor with the mast tilted slightly back. Make eye contact with the pedestrian and let them go first, since they have right of way. Sound the horn and slow to a crawl if the approach is a blind corner.
