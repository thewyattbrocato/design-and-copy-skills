## Forklift Safety Quiz

**1.** When traveling with a load, how high should the forks be?
A) Fully lowered on the floor
B) 10 to 15 cm off the floor
C) At the height of the racking
D) About 30 cm off the floor

**2.** When you travel with a load, how should the mast be positioned?
A) Tilted forward
B) Vertical
C) Tilted slightly back
D) Fully raised

**3.** What is the maximum speed in aisles?
A) 5 km/h
B) 8 km/h
C) 10 km/h
D) 15 km/h

**4.** What is the maximum speed inside freezer chambers?
A) 5 km/h
B) 8 km/h
C) 10 km/h
D) Walking pace only

**5.** True or false: A coworker can ride on the forks or the back of the truck for a short trip if they hold on.

**6.** You approach a blind corner in the aisle. What do you do?
A) Speed up so you clear it quickly
B) Sound the horn and slow to a crawl
C) Turn off your lights
D) Reverse around the corner

**7.** What does the capacity plate on the mast tell you?
A) The truck's empty weight
B) The maximum load the truck can lift
C) The minimum load the truck must carry
D) The recommended tire pressure

**8.** A load is heavier than the capacity plate allows. What do you do?
A) Lift it slowly with the mast tilted back
B) Lift it if the forks are at the lowest height
C) Do not lift it
D) Ask a coworker to help balance it

**9.** True or false: Pedestrians always have right of way, even if you are on a forklift with a clear path.

**10.** Before you move near a pedestrian, what should you do first?
A) Sound the horn
B) Make eye contact
C) Slow to 5 km/h
D) Raise the forks

**11.** Your load blocks your view of the path ahead. What do you do?
A) Drive forward slowly and watch the sides
B) Drive in reverse
C) Ask a pedestrian to walk ahead of you
D) Lower the load to the floor and drive forward

**12.** You are going down a ramp with a load. Which way should the forks point?
A) Downhill
B) Uphill
C) Level with the floor
D) Either direction is fine

**13.** During your pre-shift check, you find a hydraulic leak. What do you do?
A) Note it and finish the shift carefully
B) Tag the truck out of service and report it
C) Wipe it up and keep working
D) Report it at the end of the shift

**14.** Your pre-shift check finds the brakes are soft. What do you do?
A) Operate the truck at low speed to test the brakes
B) Operate it only in the freezer
C) Tag it out and report it
D) Operate it only with an empty load

---

## Answer Key

1. **B.** 10 to 15 cm off the floor when traveling.
2. **C.** Tilted slightly back when loaded.
3. **B.** 8 km/h in aisles.
4. **A.** 5 km/h inside freezer chambers.
5. **False.** Never carry passengers, ever.
6. **B.** Sound the horn and slow to a crawl at blind corners.
7. **B.** The maximum load the truck can lift.
8. **C.** Do not lift a load that is heavier than the capacity plate allows.
9. **True.** Pedestrians always have right of way.
10. **B.** Make eye contact before moving near a pedestrian.
11. **B.** If the load blocks your view, drive in reverse.
12. **B.** Uphill. The load stays on the uphill side of the truck.
13. **B.** Anything failing the pre-shift check means tag it out and report it.
14. **C.** Tag it out and report it. Brakes are on the pre-shift list, so a failure means the truck does not run.
