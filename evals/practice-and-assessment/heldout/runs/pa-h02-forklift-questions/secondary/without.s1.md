# Forklift Safety Quiz

**1. How high should the forks be when traveling?**
- A) Fully lowered, flat on the floor
- B) 10 to 15 cm off the floor
- C) At the height of the load's top
- D) Any height, as long as the load is secure

**2. What is the speed limit inside a freezer chamber?**
- A) 8 km/h
- B) 5 km/h
- C) 10 km/h
- D) Walking pace, no faster than a crawl

**3. You are approaching a blind corner. What do you do?**
- A) Speed up so you clear the corner quickly
- B) Sound the horn and slow to a crawl
- C) Flash the lights and keep your speed
- D) Stop completely and wait for someone to call out

**4. Can a forklift carry a passenger, such as a coworker riding on the forks or the back of the truck?**
- A) Yes, if the trip is short
- B) Yes, if the passenger holds on
- C) Yes, if a supervisor approves it
- D) No, never

**5. Where do you find the maximum load a forklift can lift?**
- A) In the operator's manual only
- B) On the capacity plate on the mast
- C) On the load's pallet label
- D) It's posted at the warehouse entrance

**6. A load is heavier than the capacity plate allows. What do you do?**
- A) Lift it slowly so the truck can handle it
- B) Lift it if the load is well balanced
- C) Do not lift it
- D) Lift it, but only partway

**7. A pedestrian is walking near your route. Who has the right of way?**
- A) The forklift, because it's the larger vehicle
- B) Whoever gets there first
- C) The pedestrian, always
- D) Whoever is closest to the aisle end

**8. Before moving near a pedestrian, what should you do?**
- A) Sound the horn and keep moving
- B) Make eye contact
- C) Slow down and drive around them quickly
- D) Wave them out of the way

**9. Your load blocks your view ahead. What do you do?**
- A) Drive forward slowly and look around the load
- B) Drive in reverse
- C) Ask a coworker to walk ahead and guide you
- D) Lower the load to see over it

**10. You're driving down a ramp with a load. Which way do the forks point?**
- A) Downhill
- B) Uphill
- C) Level with the ramp
- D) It doesn't matter as long as the load is secure

**11. True or false: During the pre-shift check, if the horn doesn't work, you can still run the forklift as long as the brakes and lights are fine.**

**12. Which of the following should you check during the pre-shift inspection?**
- A) Forks, tires, horn, brakes, lights, and hydraulic leaks
- B) Only the forks and brakes
- C) Only items that look visibly damaged
- D) The seat belt and the fuel level

**13. During the pre-shift check, you find a hydraulic leak. What do you do?**
- A) Note it and keep working, since it's probably minor
- B) Top off the fluid and start the shift
- C) Tag the forklift out of service and report it
- D) Move it to a different aisle and see if the leak gets worse

---

## Answer Key

1. **B.** Forks ride 10 to 15 cm off the floor while traveling.
2. **B.** Freezer chambers have a 5 km/h limit. The aisle limit is 8 km/h.
3. **B.** Sound the horn and slow to a crawl at blind corners.
4. **D.** Never carry passengers, under any circumstances.
5. **B.** The capacity plate on the mast shows the maximum load.
6. **C.** If the load is heavier than the capacity plate allows, do not lift it.
7. **C.** Pedestrians always have the right of way.
8. **B.** Make eye contact with the pedestrian before moving near them.
9. **B.** If a load blocks your view, drive in reverse.
10. **B.** Going down a ramp with a load, the forks point uphill.
11. **False.** Any failing item during the pre-shift check means the forklift gets tagged out and reported. A horn that doesn't work is a failing item.
12. **A.** The pre-shift check covers forks, tires, horn, brakes, lights, and hydraulic leaks.
13. **C.** Any failing item means tag it out and report it. Don't operate the forklift.
