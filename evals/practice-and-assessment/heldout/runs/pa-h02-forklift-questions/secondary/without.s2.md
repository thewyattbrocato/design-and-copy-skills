# Forklift Safety Quiz

**1.** What height should the forks be when traveling?
- A) Flat on the floor
- B) 10 to 15 cm off the floor
- C) At the height of the load's center
- D) 30 cm or more off the floor

**Answer: B.** Forks at 10 to 15 cm clear the floor while staying low for stability.

---

**2.** When you're carrying a load, how should the mast be tilted?
- A) Forward
- B) Slightly back
- C) Fully upright
- D) It doesn't matter

**Answer: B.** Tilting the mast slightly back keeps the load secure against the carriage.

---

**3.** What is the speed limit in the aisles?
- A) 5 km/h
- B) 8 km/h
- C) 12 km/h
- D) 15 km/h

**Answer: B.** The aisle limit is 8 km/h.

---

**4.** What is the speed limit inside the freezer chambers?
- A) 5 km/h
- B) 8 km/h
- C) 3 km/h
- D) The same as the aisles

**Answer: A.** Freezer chambers are limited to 5 km/h.

---

**5.** You're approaching a blind corner. What do you do?
- A) Speed up so you clear it faster
- B) Sound the horn and slow to a crawl
- C) Flash your lights and keep your speed
- D) Stop completely and wait for someone to call out

**Answer: B.** Sound the horn and slow to a crawl at every blind corner.

---

**6.** Can you give someone a ride on the forklift if they're a coworker and it's a short trip?
- A) Yes, if the trip is short
- B) Yes, if you're going slowly
- C) Yes, with supervisor approval
- D) No, never

**Answer: D.** Passengers are never allowed, for any distance or reason.

---

**7.** The capacity plate on the mast says 1,500 kg. A pallet weighs 1,700 kg. What do you do?
- A) Lift it slowly
- B) Lift it with the mast fully tilted back
- C) Do not lift it
- D) Lift it if you're an experienced operator

**Answer: C.** Never lift a load heavier than the capacity plate allows.

---

**8.** You see a pedestrian walking near your route. Who has right of way?
- A) You, because you're on a forklift
- B) The pedestrian, always
- C) Whoever gets there first
- D) Whoever is in the aisle with the larger vehicle

**Answer: B.** Pedestrians always have right of way.

---

**9.** Before moving near a pedestrian, what should you do?
- A) Sound the horn once
- B) Make eye contact
- C) Slow down to 5 km/h
- D) Ask them to step aside

**Answer: B.** Make eye contact to confirm they see you before you move near them.

---

**10.** Your load blocks your view ahead. What should you do?
- A) Lean out and look around the load
- B) Drive forward slowly and watch the sides
- C) Drive in reverse
- D) Ask a coworker to walk ahead of you

**Answer: C.** If the load blocks your view, travel in reverse.

---

**11.** You're going down a ramp with a load. Which way should the forks point?
- A) Downhill
- B) Uphill
- C) Sideways across the ramp
- D) It doesn't matter as long as the load is secure

**Answer: B.** On a downhill ramp with a load, the forks point uphill.

---

**12.** Which of these is part of the pre-shift check?
- A) Fuel level and oil change date
- B) Forks, tires, horn, brakes, lights, and hydraulic leaks
- C) Seat belt and rearview mirror only
- D) Battery charge and paint condition

**Answer: B.** The pre-shift check covers forks, tires, horn, brakes, lights, and hydraulic leaks.

---

**13.** During your pre-shift check, you find a hydraulic leak. What do you do?
- A) Top off the fluid and start your shift
- B) Note it and check again at the end of the shift
- C) Tag the truck out of service and report it
- D) Drive it slowly to the maintenance bay on your own

**Answer: C.** Any failing item means you tag the forklift out and report it.

---

**14.** Scenario: You're starting your shift. The horn doesn't work, but everything else checks out. You're needed on a pallet run in the freezer. What should you do?
- A) Use the truck, since the horn is only needed sometimes
- B) Use the truck, but use your voice to warn people at blind corners
- C) Tag the truck out, report the horn, and get a working truck
- D) Use the truck only in the aisles, not the freezer

**Answer: C.** A failing horn is a failing item on the pre-shift check. Tag the truck out and report it. The horn is needed at blind corners, so it can't be skipped.

---

**Scoring suggestion:** 13 or 14 correct is a strong pass. Review any missed item with the new hires before they operate a truck alone.
