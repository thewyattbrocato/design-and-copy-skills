Tidewater Coffee · Milk Steaming Quiz

For new baristas, after the half-day session

Quick reference (look at it any time)

Temperature

  • Target: 55 to 65 C
  • Over 70 C scalds the milk and kills the sweetness

Air and texture

  • Add air early, while the milk is still cold
  • Then lower the tip and let the milk roll

Wand

  • Wipe the wand right after steaming
  • Purge before and after

Oat milk

  • Heats faster than dairy
  • Foam falls apart sooner
