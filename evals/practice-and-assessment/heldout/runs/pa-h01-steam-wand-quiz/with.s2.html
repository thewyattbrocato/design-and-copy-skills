Eight short situations. The reference card is open beside you, so use it. No timer, and nothing here is shared or saved.

Half-day reference card
  • Temperature: aim for 55 to 65 C. Over 70 C scalds the milk and kills the sweetness.
  • Air: add air early, while the milk is still cold. Then lower the tip and let the milk roll.
  • Wand: purge before and after steaming. Wipe it right after steaming.
  • Oat milk: heats faster than dairy, and its foam falls apart sooner.
