Cheat sheet
  • Target milk temperature: 55 to 65 C. Over 70 C scalds the milk and kills the sweetness.
  • Add air early, while the milk is still cold. Then lower the tip and let it roll.
  • Wipe the wand right after steaming. Purge it before and after.
  • Oat milk heats faster than dairy, and its foam falls apart sooner.
