Milk Steaming Quiz

Tidewater Coffee · new barista practice

Reference card

Keep this open. On the bar you can look things up too.

Temperature
Target milk temperature is 55 to 65 °C. Over 70 °C scalds the milk and kills the sweetness.
Air
Add air early, while the milk is still cold. Then lower the tip and let it roll.
Wand
Wipe the wand right after steaming. Purge it before and after.
Oat milk
Oat heats faster than dairy, and its foam falls apart sooner.
