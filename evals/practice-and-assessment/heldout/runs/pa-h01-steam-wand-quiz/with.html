Steaming reference

  • Temperature: target 55–65 °C. Over 70 °C scalds the milk and kills the sweetness.
  • Air: add air early, while the milk is still cold. Then lower the tip and let it roll.
  • Wand: purge before and after steaming. Wipe it right after steaming.
  • Oat milk: heats faster than dairy, and its foam falls apart sooner.
