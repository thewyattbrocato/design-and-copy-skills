Tidewater Coffee: Milk Steaming Quiz

8 situations from the counter. Pick what you would do next. You get the result of each choice straight away.

Open the steaming cheat sheet (you can look things up)
  • Target milk temperature: 55 to 65 C. Over 70 C scalds the milk and kills the sweetness.
  • Add air early, while the milk is still cold. Then lower the tip and let the milk roll.
  • Purge the wand before and after steaming. Wipe it right after steaming.
  • Oat milk heats faster than dairy, and its foam falls apart sooner.
