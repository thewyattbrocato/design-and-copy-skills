Milk Steaming Quiz

Tidewater Coffee · After your half-day. The reference card is there to use. Take your time, and you'll see feedback after every answer.

Reference card

  • Target temperature: 55 to 65 °C.
  • Over 70 °C scalds the milk and kills the sweetness.
  • Air: add it early, while the milk is still cold. Then lower the tip and let the milk roll.
  • Wand: purge before and after steaming. Wipe it right after steaming.
  • Oat milk: heats faster than dairy, and its foam falls apart sooner.
