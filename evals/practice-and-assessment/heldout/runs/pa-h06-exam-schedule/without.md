# 9-Week Pharmacy Tech Exam Schedule

**Time budget:** 30 min Mon–Fri, 90 min Sat, Sun off. That's about 4 hours a week and 36 hours total.

**Weekday routine:** Mon–Thu are for learning or reviewing the topic. Fri is 30 minutes of practice questions on that week's topic.

| Week | Focus | Mon–Thu (30 min each) | Fri (30 min) | Sat (90 min) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Sterile compounding** (new) | Mon: aseptic technique, hand hygiene, garbing<br>Tue: cleanrooms, laminar flow hoods, BSCs<br>Wed: IV dilutions, flow rates, admixtures<br>Thu: hazardous drugs, USP 797/800 | Practice Qs | 45 min recap and flashcards, then 45 min calculation drills |
| **2** | **Inventory and ordering** (new) | Mon: ordering, par levels, wholesalers, NDCs<br>Tue: receiving, storage, temperature, expiration dates<br>Wed: controlled substance ordering (DEA 222/CSOS)<br>Thu: recalls, returns, formulary, billing basics | Practice Qs | 45 min recap, then 45 min on sterile compounding Qs |
| **3** | **Safety and error prevention** (new) | Mon: types of errors, error reporting, quality assurance<br>Tue: look-alike/sound-alike drugs, Tall Man lettering<br>Wed: high-alert meds, ISMP lists<br>Thu: prescription verification, barcode scanning, MedWatch | Practice Qs | 45 min recap, then 45 min on inventory and ordering Qs |
| **4** | **Calculations** (review) | Mon: conversions, ratios, proportions<br>Tue: days' supply, quantities<br>Wed: percent, alligation, dilutions<br>Thu: IV rates, markup/pricing | Timed calculation set | 90 min mixed calculation problems, then redo the ones you missed |
| **5** | **Drug classes, part 1** (review) | Brand/generic names and classes for cardiovascular, diabetes, and respiratory drugs, plus one class's side effects and interactions each day | Self-quiz | 60 min flashcards, then 30 min practice Qs |
| **6** | **Drug classes, part 2** (review) | The same approach for CNS/psychiatric, pain, anti-infectives, GI, and hormones | Self-quiz | 30 min flashcards on weeks 5–6, then 60 min timed mixed drug Qs |
| **7** | **Law and ethics** (review), then a sweep of the new units | Mon: federal law (CSA, FDCA, HIPAA)<br>Tue: controlled substance schedules, refills, recordkeeping<br>Wed: tech scope of practice, ethics<br>Thu: quick pass over sterile, inventory, and safety notes | Law practice Qs | 90 min timed mixed set across all six units |
| **8** | **Weak spots** | Mon–Thu: pick the 4 lowest-scoring topics from your week 7 set, one per day | Mixed Qs | 90 min timed practice test, then review every miss |
| **9** | **Taper and exam prep** | Mon: calculations<br>Tue: drug classes<br>Wed: law<br>Thu: sterile, inventory, and safety summary sheets | Light skim only | 30–45 min of flashcards, then rest |

## Notes

- **Count backward from your exam date.** Week 9 assumes the exam falls at the end of that week. If it's earlier in the week, drop the later days. Don't add new material in the last two days.
- **Track your misses.** Keep a running list of questions you get wrong. Weeks 7 and 8 use it to pick your weak spots.
- **Don't skip Fridays.** Practice questions teach you more than rereading does.
- **Check your exam's blueprint.** The PTCB and ExCPT exams weight these areas differently, and the weights may change which topics deserve extra time. You may want to move time toward the heavily weighted areas, especially drug classes.
