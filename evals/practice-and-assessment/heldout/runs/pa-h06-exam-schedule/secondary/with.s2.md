# 9-Week Study Plan (4 hours/week)

Weeks run Monday to Sunday, starting **Mon Oct 12**. Your exam lands in mid-December. If the date differs, shift the phases to fit.

**Every weekday (30 min):** 10 min calculations drill, then 20 min on the day's focus.
**Saturday (90 min):** Practice questions and review, as listed below.
**Sunday:** Off.

| Week | Mon–Fri focus (after the 10-min calc drill) | Saturday (90 min) |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Sterile compounding (new) | Sterile: 60 min questions, 30 min review misses |
| **2** | Mon–Tue: Sterile wrap-up. Wed–Fri: Inventory & ordering (new) | Sterile + Inventory: 60 min questions, 30 min review |
| **3** | Inventory & ordering (new) | Inventory: 60 min questions, 30 min review |
| **4** | Safety & error prevention (new) | Safety: 60 min questions, 30 min review |
| **5** | Safety & error prevention (finish) | Safety + Sterile: 60 min questions, 30 min review |
| **6** | Mon: Drug classes · Tue: Law & ethics · Wed: Sterile · Thu: Inventory · Fri: Safety | Mixed set of ~45 questions across all six units, 60 min, then 30 min review |
| **7** | Same rotation, but in reverse order · Focus on last week's misses | Mixed set of ~45 questions, 60 min, then 30 min review |
| **8** | Focus on the 2 weakest units from the mock | **Full timed mock exam (90 min)**. Note your score for each unit. |
| **9** | Mon–Thu: 20 min on your weakest unit. Stop new material by Thu. Fri: off. | 60 min light review of flashcards and your error log. No new questions. |

## Rules for the fridge

- **Calc drill every weekday.** It's your sharpest skill-decay risk, so keep it short and daily.
- **Keep an error log.** Write down every wrong answer and the reason for it. Review the log on Saturdays.
- **Missed a day?** Don't double up. Let it go and continue.
- **Exam week:** No new content. Calc drill and flashcards only.

**Time check:** 4 hrs/week × 9 weeks = 36 hours total. About 15 of those go to the three new units.
