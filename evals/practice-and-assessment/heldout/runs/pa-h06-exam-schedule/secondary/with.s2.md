# 9-Week Study Schedule

**Weekly time:** 4 hours (Mon–Fri 30 min each, Sat 90 min, Sun off). Start Monday, and if your exam date is earlier than week 9, cut from the end of week 8.

**Weekday slots:**
- **Mon/Wed:** new unit (weeks 1–6)
- **Tue:** calculations, every week
- **Thu:** drug classes or law & ethics, alternating
- **Fri:** 30 min flashcards across all units

**Saturday (90 min):** 60 min on the week's new unit, then 30 min of practice questions on it.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Sterile compounding | Calc | Sterile compounding | Drug classes | Flashcards | Sterile 60 + Qs 30 |
| **2** | Sterile compounding | Calc | Sterile compounding | Law & ethics | Flashcards | Sterile 60 + Qs 30 |

| **4** | Inventory & ordering | Calc | Inventory & ordering | Law & ethics | Flashcards | Inventory 60 + Qs 30 |

| **7** | Weak-spot review | Calc | Mixed practice Qs (30) | Drug classes or law | Flashcards | **Full timed practice test (90)** |
| **8** | Review missed Qs from Sat | Calc | Mixed practice Qs (30) | Weakest unit | Flashcards | **Full timed practice test (90)**, then review misses |
| **9** | Calc (light) | Flashcards | Weak spots only | Light review | Rest | Light review (30), then rest |

**Fridge rules:**
1. **Never study new material in week 9.** Only review.
2. **Missed a day?** Don't double up. Just move on, and Friday's flashcards will catch the old material.
3. **Calc every Tuesday, no exceptions.** Math skills fade fastest without repetition.
4. **Check your score on the week 7 and 8 tests.** Anything under about 70% gets an extra Thursday slot in week 9.

**Trade-off to know about:** sterile compounding and safety are the heaviest new units, and each gets only about 4 hours of new study here. If your practice scores on those units are low after week 2 or 6, I can shift the Thursday slots toward them.
