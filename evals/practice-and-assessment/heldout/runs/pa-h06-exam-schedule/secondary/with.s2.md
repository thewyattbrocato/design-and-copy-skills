## Rules for the fridge

1. **Never skip Saturday.** It's your biggest block. If you fall behind, drop Friday's flashcards first.
2. **Keep a miss log.** For every wrong answer, write the topic and why you missed it. Friday and Monday reviews use this log.
3. **Calculations is the most-tested skill.** It appears at least twice a week, so don't let it slip.
4. **Use Mondays after Saturday tests** to review what you got wrong before moving on.
5. **Sundays are off** as you asked. If a session gets missed, don't make it up on Sunday.
