# 9-Week Study Schedule

**Daily rule:** Weekdays are 30 minutes: **20 min new material, 10 min review**. Saturday is 90 minutes. Sunday is off.

**Review rotation (10 min, weekdays):** Mon and Thu = Calculations · Tue and Fri = Drug Classes · Wed = Law & Ethics. Use flashcards or practice questions, and write any question you miss on an index card.

**Assumed exam date:** about Thu Dec 17. Adjust if yours differs.

| Wk | Dates | Mon–Fri (20 min new + 10 min review) | Saturday (90 min) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Oct 19 | **Sterile compounding I:** Mon–Tue cleanroom classes, USP 797 basics; Wed–Fri aseptic technique, garbing, hand hygiene, BSC | Read sterile notes from Mon–Fri, then 40-question sterile quiz |
| 2 | Oct 26 | **Sterile compounding II:** IV admixture, beyond-use dating, hazardous drugs (HD/NIOSH), TPN basics, IV flow rates | Review missed sterile questions, then 40-question sterile quiz |
| 3 | Nov 2 | **Inventory & ordering I:** purchasing, receiving, NDCs, FEFO and stock rotation, par levels | Inventory notes, then 40-question inventory quiz |
| 4 | Nov 9 | **Inventory & ordering II:** controlled substance records (CII–CV), returns and recalls, drug shortages | Review missed inventory questions, then 40-question inventory quiz |
| 5 | Nov 16 | **Safety & error prevention I:** error types, look-alike/sound-alike drugs, tall-man lettering, high-alert meds | Safety notes, then 40-question safety quiz |
| 6 | Nov 23 | **Safety & error prevention II:** ISMP and error reporting, root cause analysis, dispensing verification steps | Review missed safety questions, then 40-question safety quiz. *Thanksgiving (Nov 26):* skip that day if traveling. Don't double up. |
| 7 | Nov 30 | **Calculations deep review:** all 20 min on calc problems (dosage, ratios, IV rates, conversions). Review block stays at 10 min for drug classes and law | Timed 40-question calc set (45 min), then review every miss |
| 8 | Dec 7 | **Drug classes + law review:** Mon–Wed law and ethics (DEA schedules, Rx rules, HIPAA, counseling); Thu–Fri drug classes | **Full timed practice exam** (90 min). Note your weakest unit. |
| 9 | Dec 14 | **Weak-spot review only:** Mon–Wed 30 min on your lowest unit from the Saturday test. No new material. | Exam is Thu Dec 17. Sat is after your exam, so no session. |

**Why this shape:**
- The three untouched units get the new-material slots in Weeks 1–6, because they need the most time.
- The three units you've already done get 10 minutes of review every weekday, so they don't fade.
- Weeks 7–8 switch to review and timed practice, since you'll retain more from testing yourself than from rereading.

**Rules to tape to the fridge:**
1. Don't skip the 10-minute review. It's what keeps the first three units alive.
2. If you miss a weekday, don't make it up. Move on.
3. If a unit runs over, cut its Saturday quiz short. Don't skip the Saturday practice.
