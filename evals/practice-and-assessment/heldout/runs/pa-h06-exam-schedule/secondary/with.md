# 9-Week Study Schedule

**Weekly total:** 4 hours (weekdays 30 min each, Saturday 90 min, Sunday off). Over 9 weeks that's about 36 hours.

## Every Weekday (Mon–Fri, 30 min)

- **10 min: Calculations drill.** Do 5 problems. Calculations is the unit most people lose points on, so it gets a little every day.
- **15 min: This week's focus unit** (see table below).
- **5 min: Log misses.** Write down any question you got wrong and why.

## Saturday (90 min)

Weeks 1–5: new material or a focused review session, as listed below.
Weeks 6–8: timed practice questions, then review every wrong answer.

## Week by Week

| Week | Weekday focus (15 min) | Saturday (90 min) |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Sterile compounding: aseptic technique, cleanroom classes, hand hygiene | Sterile compounding: full first pass with notes |
| **2** | Sterile compounding: BSCs, IV admixtures, beyond-use dating | Sterile calculations (BUD, IV rate and flow) plus 20 practice questions |
| **3** | Inventory & ordering: purchasing, stock levels, expiration dates, controlled-substance counts | Inventory & ordering: full first pass plus 20 practice questions |
| **4** | Safety & error prevention: error types, look-alike/sound-alike drugs, high-alert meds | Safety & error prevention: full first pass with notes |
| **5** | Safety & error prevention: root-cause analysis and error-reporting processes | 50-question mixed quiz on all 6 units. Score each unit separately and review every miss. |
| **6** | Weakest unit from Week 5 (15 min daily) | Mixed quiz on all 6 units. Re-score by unit and update your weak list. |
| **7** | Weakest 2 units from Week 6 | Timed practice set on weak units, then reteach anything still below 75% |
| **8** | Light flashcards on all units | **Full timed practice exam.** Spend the rest of the session reviewing every miss. |
| **9** | Flashcards and calculation drills only. No new material. | Light review of your miss log. Rest the day before the exam. |

## Notes for the Fridge

- **Law & ethics** gets only one pass so far, so it's covered in the Week 5 quiz and the Week 8 exam review. Add a 10-minute refresher during Week 7 if the Week 6 score is low.
- **Don't skip Sundays.** They're your rest days, and the schedule relies on them.
- **If you miss a week,** don't double up on the next Saturday. Drop the lowest-priority item and keep going.
- **If a unit is still weak in Week 7,** move Saturday time from new material to targeted practice. Scores matter more than covering every page.
