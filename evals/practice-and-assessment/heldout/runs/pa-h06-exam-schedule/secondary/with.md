# Study Schedule: 9 Weeks

**Every week:** Mon–Fri 30 min each, Sat 90 min, Sun off. That's 4 hours a week.

**Daily 30 min (Mon–Fri):**
- **10 min: Calculations drill.** Do 5–10 problems. Never skip this.
- **20 min: This week's focus unit.** Read, make notes, or do flashcards.

**Saturday 90 min:**
- **60 min:** Deep study of this week's focus unit.
- **30 min:** Practice questions on this week's unit, and redo every miss.

| Week | Dates | Weekday focus (20 min) | Saturday (90 min) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Oct 12–18 | Sterile compounding: cleanroom, USP 797, aseptic technique, BSC, garbing | Sterile compounding basics + 30 min questions |
| 2 | Oct 19–25 | Sterile compounding: IV admixtures, beyond-use dates, flow rates, TPN | Sterile compounding part 2 + 30 min questions |
| 3 | Oct 26–Nov 1 | Inventory & ordering: purchasing, stock rotation, storage, expiration | Inventory & ordering + 30 min questions |
| 4 | Nov 2–8 | Safety & error prevention: error types, look-alike/sound-alike, high-alert meds | Safety part 1 + 30 min questions |
| 5 | Nov 9–15 | Safety & error prevention: adverse reactions, interactions, allergies, reporting | Safety part 2 + 30 min questions |
| 6 | Nov 16–22 | Mixed review: sterile compounding and inventory (your weakest sections first) | Mixed questions across sterile, inventory, and safety |
| 7 | Nov 23–29 | Drug classes review (flashcards for top-selling and high-alert drugs) | Calculations deep dive on your weakest problem types + 30 min questions |
| 8 | Nov 30–Dec 6 | Law & ethics review (weekday mornings: one topic a day) | **Full timed practice test** (or the full question bank in one sitting), then review every miss |
| 9 | Dec 7–13 | Light review of your personal weak-spot list only; no new content | Short review of weak spots. Rest before the exam. |

**Rules to keep it working:**
- **If you miss a weekday, don't make it up.** Doubling up tires you out. Just move on.
- **Keep a "miss list"** of every question you got wrong and why. Review it in week 9.
- **Week 7 onward is about retrieval, not reading.** Close the book and answer questions from memory.
- **Week 8 Saturday is your dress rehearsal.** Use the time limit and no notes. Your score tells you what to fix in week 9.
- **If you're behind by week 6,** cut drug classes review in week 7 and keep the calculations drill.
