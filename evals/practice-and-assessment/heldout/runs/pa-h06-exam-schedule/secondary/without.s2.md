# 9-Week Fridge Schedule

**Weekly total: 4 hours** (5 × 30 min weekdays + 90 min Saturday). Sundays off.

**Weekday (30 min):** 10 min flashcards from earlier sessions, then 20 min on this week's focus.
**Saturday (90 min):** 15 min recall, 60 min timed practice questions, 15 min reviewing every wrong answer.

| Week | Mon–Fri (30 min each) | Saturday (90 min) |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Sterile compounding:** read and make notes | **Sterile compounding:** 30 practice questions, review misses |
| **2** | **Sterile compounding:** practice questions and flashcards (aseptic technique, cleanroom and BSC basics, beyond-use dating) | **Sterile compounding:** mixed quiz plus IV rate and dilution calcs |
| **3** | **Inventory and ordering:** read and make notes (ordering, expiry and FIFO, storage, controlled-substance counts) | **Inventory:** practice set, review misses |
| **4** | **Safety and error prevention:** read and make notes (high-alert meds, look-alike/sound-alike, error types) | **Safety:** practice set, review misses |
| **5** | **Safety:** practice questions. Mon/Wed/Fri: 5 calc questions added to the session | **Safety + inventory:** mixed set on weak spots |
| **6** | **Calculations:** 10 questions daily. Thu: drug-class flashcards | **Calculations:** full timed set |
| **7** | **Law and ethics:** review and questions. Tue/Thu: drug-class review | **Mixed:** timed set across all six units |
| **8** | **Weak areas only:** whatever the week 7 Saturday showed | **Full timed mixed practice test** (stop at 90 min) |
| **9** | **Light:** Mon–Wed flashcards on weak spots only. Thu–Fri: rest, no new material | **Light:** one short set, then rest |

**Rules for the fridge:**
- Keep a running list of every wrong answer. Redo that list each Saturday.
- Don't skip the Saturday practice test, even if you feel behind. Timed practice is what the exam tests.
- Calculations get a few minutes every week. They're the easiest topic to let slip.
- No new material in week 9. Sleep matters more than one more chapter.
- If you miss a session, don't double up. Just pick up the next one.

**Choices I made:** I front-loaded the three untouched units (weeks 1–5) because they carry the most risk of running out of time. Calculations and drug classes come back after that. Week 9 is intentionally light. If you tell me your exam date, I can line up the final days precisely.
