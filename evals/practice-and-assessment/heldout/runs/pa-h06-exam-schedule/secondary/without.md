# 9-Week Pharmacy Tech Study Schedule

**Weekly total: 4 hours** (5 × 30 min weekdays + 90 min Saturday). Sunday is off.

**Every weekday (30 min):** 10 min calculations drill, then a 20 min topic slot.
**Saturday (90 min):** quiz on the week's unit, then review every miss.

**Calc drill, every weekday (10 min):** 8 to 10 timed problems covering dosage, IV flow rates, dilutions, reconstitution, ratio and proportion, and unit conversions. Calculations is the easiest unit to lose, so don't skip it.

| Week | Mon, Tue, Wed, Fri (20 min) | Thu (20 min) | Sat (90 min) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** (starts Thu Oct 8) | Thu/Fri: Sterile compounding, intro and aseptic technique | Drug classes review | Sterile: finish reading, then 25 questions |
| **2** | Sterile compounding: garbing, BUD, cleanroom rules | Law and ethics review | 40-question sterile quiz, review misses |
| **3** | Inventory and ordering: purchasing, stock levels, expiration | Drug classes review | 40-question inventory quiz, review misses |
| **4** | Inventory and ordering: controlled substances, returns, recalls | Law and ethics review | 40-question inventory quiz, review misses |
| **5** | Safety and error prevention: error types, root cause, look-alike and sound-alike drugs | Drug classes review | 40-question safety quiz, review misses |
| **6** | Safety and error prevention: high-alert drugs, reporting | Law and ethics review | 40-question safety quiz, review misses |
| **7** | Mixed: work through your miss log from Weeks 1–6, weakest unit first | (Thu: calc drill and a light review of drug classes) | Timed mixed practice test (about 60 questions), review every miss |
| **8** | Mixed: fix the weakest unit from the Week 7 test | (Thu: calc drill and a light review of law) | Full-length timed practice test, review every miss |
| **9** (exam week) | Mon–Wed: review only the miss log, no new material | Calc drill and one-page cheat sheet | 30-question warm-up, then stop studying |

**Rules for the fridge:**
- **No new material after Week 8.** Week 9 is for review only.
- **Missed a day?** Skip it and keep going. Don't double up.
- **Keep a miss log.** Write every wrong answer and the reason. Weeks 7–9 are built from it.
- **Week 1 starts today (Thu Oct 8).** It has three study days (Thu, Fri, Sat), so the schedule runs to about Dec 6. If your exam is earlier or later than about Dec 10, tell me and I'll shift it.
