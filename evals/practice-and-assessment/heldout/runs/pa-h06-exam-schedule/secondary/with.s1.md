# Fridge Schedule: 9 Weeks, 4 Hours a Week

**Every week (same routine)**
- **Mon–Thu, 30 min:** Current unit. Read, take notes, do 10 practice questions.
- **Fri, 30 min:** 15 min calculation drill (10 problems, every week, since math fades fastest). 15 min flashcards on the rotating review unit below.
- **Sat, 90 min:** Timed mixed practice set (about 50 questions, 60 min), then 30 min reviewing every miss.
- **Sun:** Off.

**Week by week**

| Week | Mon–Thu: current unit | Fri: review rotation | Sat focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sterile compounding | Drug classes | Mixed set |
| 2 | Sterile compounding | Law & ethics | Mixed set |
| 3 | Inventory & ordering | Drug classes | Mixed set |
| 4 | Inventory & ordering | Law & ethics | Mixed set |
| 5 | Safety & error prevention | Drug classes | Mixed set |
| 6 | Safety & error prevention | Law & ethics | Mixed set |
| 7 | Mixed review: weakest 3 units from your Sat misses | All units | Full timed practice exam |
| 8 | Mixed review: weakest 2 units | All units | Full timed practice exam + review |
| 9 | Light review only, no new material | Skim flashcards | Practice exam early in the week, then rest |

**Rules for the fridge**
- Use a timer. When 30 minutes is up, stop, even mid-question.
- Keep a "misses" list on one page. Recheck it every Friday.
- If you fall behind, skip the Friday flashcards before you skip a Saturday.
- Week 9 is exam week. Your exam date is about 9 weeks from today, so if you sit it near the end of the year, check that it is not clashing with the holidays.

**Totals:** 36 hours. Each new unit gets about 8 hours, and the last 3 weeks are for review and practice exams.

**Assumptions**
- You have a question bank or practice exam book. If not, get one this week, since Saturdays depend on it.
- Calculations are the most failure-prone section for most candidates, so if your Saturday misses cluster there, move a Mon–Thu block to calculations for that week.
