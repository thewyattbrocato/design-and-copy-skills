# 9-Week Study Schedule (4 hours/week)

**Every week:**
- **Mon–Fri (30 min):** 20 min study the day's topic, then 10 min closed-book recall. Close the book and write what you remember, then check.
- **Sat (90 min):** 30 min recall of the week's topics, then 60 min practice questions. Review every wrong answer.
- **Sun:** Off.

| Week | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat (90 min) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Sterile: aseptic technique | Sterile: cleanroom, hood, PPE | Sterile: IV compounding steps | Sterile: beyond-use dating | Sterile: hazardous drugs | Sterile recall + mixed questions |
| **2** | Sterile: quality checks | Sterile: dose verification | Sterile: sterile calc tie-ins | Sterile: common errors | Sterile recall | Sterile questions + review |
| **3** | Inventory: ordering & purchasing | Inventory: receiving | Inventory: storage & stock rotation | Inventory: expiration dating | Inventory: controlled substance counts | Inventory questions + mixed |
| **4** | Safety: error types & causes | Safety: high-alert medications | Safety: look-alike/sound-alike & tall man | Safety: error reporting | Safety recall | Safety questions + review |
| **5** | Safety: patient identification | Safety: barcoding & double checks | Safety: near misses & root cause | Safety: medication safety tools | Safety + inventory recall | Safety/inventory questions + mixed |
| **6** | Calculations: formulas & ratios | Calculations: practice set | Drug classes: by category | Drug classes: practice set | Flashcards (calc + drugs) | Mixed questions, all 6 units |
| **7** | Law: federal & state rules | Ethics: scenarios | Sterile recall | Inventory recall | Safety recall | Mixed questions, all 6 units |
| **8** | Review last Sat's misses | Weakest unit drill | Weakest unit drill | Review last Sat's misses | Formula sheet recall | **Timed practice test** (or 60 min timed + 30 min review) |
| **9** | Weakest unit drill | Weakest unit drill | Second-weakest unit drill | Formula sheet, 15 min | Rest | Light practice, no new material |

**Notes:**
- Weeks 1–5 cover the three units you haven't started. Weeks 6–7 rotate back through the three you've already done, since recall fades without review.
- Week 8 is where you find your weak spots. Week 9 is for fixing them, not learning new material.
- I assumed an exam around **December 10**. If your date is different, let me know and I'll rebalance the weeks.
- Use practice questions from your prep course or a question bank for the Saturday sessions. If you tell me which certification you're sitting (PTCB, ExCPT, or another), I can check the topic order against its content outline.
