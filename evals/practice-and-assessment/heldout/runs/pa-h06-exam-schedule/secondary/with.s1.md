# 9-Week Study Plan

**Day 1 is today (Thu, Oct 15).** I've assumed the exam is at the end of Week 9. Weekdays are 30 minutes, Saturdays are 90 minutes, and Sundays are off.

## Every weekday (30 min)
- **10 min calculations drill** (same every day, so the math stays fresh):
  - **Mon:** ratio and proportion, dosage calculations
  - **Tue:** unit conversions (metric, apothecary, household)
  - **Wed:** dilutions and alligation
  - **Thu:** IV flow rates and infusion time
  - **Fri:** days' supply and compounding math
- **20 min focus unit** (see the table below)

## Every Saturday (90 min)
- **0:00–0:30:** Timed mixed practice questions from all units
- **0:30–1:30:** Review every miss. Write one line per miss in a **wrong-answer log**: the topic and the rule you got wrong.

## Weekly plan

| Week | Mon–Fri focus (20 min) | Saturday |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Sterile compounding: read and take notes | Mixed quiz and review |
| **2** | Sterile compounding: practice questions and gaps | Mixed quiz and review |
| **3** | Inventory and ordering: read and take notes | Mixed quiz and review |
| **4** | Inventory and ordering: practice questions and gaps | Mixed quiz and review |
| **5** | Safety and error prevention: read and take notes | Mixed quiz and review |
| **6** | Safety and error prevention: practice questions and gaps | Mixed quiz and review |
| **7** | **Mon/Wed/Fri:** law and ethics review. **Tue/Thu:** drug classes review | Mixed quiz and review |
| **8** | **Mon–Wed:** drug classes full review. **Thu–Fri:** the unit with the most entries in your wrong-answer log | **Full timed practice test (90 min)**, then score it and log every miss |
| **9** | Weakest units from the wrong-answer log. No new material | Light: 30 min skimming the wrong-answer log, then rest |

## Why it's built this way
- The three untouched units get two weeks each because you have zero exposure to them.
- Calculations gets a little every day because math skills fade fastest when they're not used.
- Law, ethics, and drug classes come back in Week 7 so they don't fade before the exam.
- If you miss a day, skip it. Don't double up the next day.
