Here's a 9-week plan. It comes to about 36 hours: 30 min × 5 weekdays plus 90 min on Saturday, with Sundays off. I assumed your exam falls at the end of week 9.

**How it's built**
- **Weeks 1–3:** one new unit per week (sterile compounding, then inventory and ordering, then safety and error prevention). Calculations and drug cards stay warm on Thursday and Saturday.
- **Weeks 4–6:** a second pass on one old unit per week (calculations, then drug classes, then law and ethics). Friday keeps the new units fresh.
- **Weeks 7–8:** mixed practice and timed tests, focused on whatever you keep getting wrong.
- **Week 9:** light review and rest.

## Weeks 1–3: Learn the new units
Week 1 = Sterile compounding, Week 2 = Inventory and ordering, Week 3 = Safety and error prevention.

| Day | Time | Task |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | 30 | New unit: read and take notes |
| Tue | 30 | New unit: finish reading, make flashcards |
| Wed | 30 | New unit: practice questions |
| Thu | 30 | 15 min calculations + 15 min drug cards |
| Fri | 30 | New unit: quiz yourself, redo missed questions |
| **Sat** | 90 | 30 new unit weak spots, 30 calculations, 30 drug classes or law review |
| Sun | – | Off |

## Weeks 4–6: Second pass on the old units
Week 4 = Calculations, Week 5 = Drug classes, Week 6 = Law and ethics.

|---|---|---|

| Wed | 30 | Focus unit: practice questions, redo misses |

| Fri | 30 | Quick review of one new unit (rotate: sterile → inventory → safety) |

| Day | Time | Task |

| Tue | 30 | Drug cards: only the ones you still miss |
| Wed | 30 | Skim notes on safety, sterile compounding and inventory |
| Thu | 30 | Skim law and ethics, then stop |
| Fri | 30 | Light skim or rest. Get your ID and test-center details ready. |
| **Sat** | 30–60 | Optional light review, then rest |
| Sun | – | Off |

## Tips
- **Keep a miss log.** Write down every question you get wrong, with the reason. In weeks 7–9, study from that log first.
- **Do calculations most days.** It's a skill, and it fades if you skip it.
- **Drug cards:** a few minutes a day beats a long session once a week.
- **If you fall behind,** drop the Friday review rather than skipping a new unit in weeks 1–3.
- **If your exam is earlier or later in week 9,** keep the last two days before it as rest or very light review.
