# 9-Week PTCE Study Schedule

**Time:** Mon–Fri 30 min, Sat 90 min, Sun off. That's 4 hours a week, 36 hours in total.

**Plan:** The three new units come first, one per week, while they're fresh and you have the most time. Then you re-review the three you've already done. The last weeks are mixed practice and timed sets.

## Weeks 1–3: New material

| Week | Mon–Thu (30 min each) | Fri (30 min) | Sat (90 min) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Sterile compounding**: read and take notes | 15 min quiz on sterile compounding + 15 min calc drills | 45 min sterile practice questions + 45 min **calculations** review |
| **2** | **Inventory & ordering**: read and take notes | 15 min quiz on inventory + 15 min drug class flashcards | 45 min inventory practice questions + 45 min **drug classes** review |
| **3** | **Safety & error prevention**: read and take notes | 15 min quiz on safety + 15 min calc drills | 45 min safety practice questions + 45 min **law & ethics** review |

## Weeks 4–6: Second pass on the units you've done

| Week | Mon–Thu (30 min each) | Fri (30 min) | Sat (90 min) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **4** | **Calculations**: work problems, don't just reread | Mixed quiz on the 3 new units | 45 min calc practice test + 45 min **sterile compounding** review |
| **5** | **Drug classes**: flashcards, brand/generic, class, use | Mixed quiz on the 3 new units | 45 min drug class practice test + 45 min **inventory** review |
| **6** | **Law & ethics**: rules, schedules, DEA forms | Mixed quiz on the 3 new units | 45 min law practice test + 45 min **safety** review |

## Weeks 7–8: Mixed practice

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| Mon | Calculations (30 min) |
| Tue | Drug classes (30 min) |
| Wed | Law & ethics (30 min) |
| Thu | Sterile compounding (15 min) + inventory (15 min) |
| Fri | Safety (15 min) + your weakest topic (15 min) |
| Sat | **Timed practice exam**: 60–70 min under test conditions, then 20–30 min reviewing every miss |

## Week 9: Final week

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| Mon–Thu | 30 min each on your weakest areas, using your Saturday exam misses |
| Fri | Light flashcards and calc formulas only |
| Sat | 45 min light review, then stop |
| Sun | Off |

## Tips
- **Calculations** is a skill, so do problems rather than rereading. It's also why calc drills show up on Fridays in weeks 1–3.
- **Drug classes** is memorization. Short daily flashcard sessions beat long ones.
- **Practice exams:** Keep a running list of missed questions by unit. It tells you what to spend week 9 on.
- **Slip days:** If you miss a weekday, don't double up. Skip it and keep the next day on plan. Saturdays are the ones to protect.

I assumed your exam falls right after week 9 ends. If it's earlier in that week, drop the later days of week 9 and keep the Saturday practice exam in week 8. I can also save this as a printable file or change the order of units if you'd like.
