# 9-Week Pharmacy Tech Exam Plan

**Time:** Mon–Fri 30 min, Sat 90 min, Sun off. That's about 4 hours a week.
**Rule for every session:** close the book and answer questions or write from memory. Don't just re-read. Put every miss in a one-page **mistake log** and redo it the next day.

## Weekday pattern

| Weeks | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1–4** (new unit) | 20 min new unit + 10 min calcs problems | 20 new + 10 drug class flashcards | 20 new + 10 law questions | 20 new + 10 calcs problems | 30 min closed-book recall of this week's new unit |
| **5–8** (review) | Calcs problems | Drug classes | Law + safety | Sterile + inventory | Mixed set of 20 questions from all units |

## Saturday (90 min)

| Week | Focus | Saturday plan |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Sterile compounding, part 1 | 30 recall quiz on the week + 30 mixed old-unit questions + 30 finish lessons |
| **2** | Sterile compounding, part 2 (including its calculations) | Same split as week 1 |
| **3** | Inventory & ordering | Same split as week 1 |
| **4** | Safety & error prevention | Same split as week 1 |
| **5** | Second pass: calculations + drug classes | 60 mixed questions on those two + 30 mistake-log redo |
| **6** | Second pass: law, sterile, inventory, safety | 60 mixed questions on those four + 30 mistake-log redo |
| **7** | Full mixed practice | **Practice test #1**, timed, as many questions as fit in 90 min. Score it by unit. |
| **8** | Fix the weakest 2 units | 60 targeted practice on the weakest units + 30 mistake-log redo. Retake the missed items from test #1. |
| **9** | Taper, no new material | 45 min light mixed review, then stop. |

## Week 9 (exam week)

- **Mon–Thu:** 30 min each. Go through your mistake log and flashcards, plus 10 calcs problems.
- **Fri:** 15 min skim only. Confirm the test center, ID and travel time.
- **Sat–Sun:** rest. Sleep matters more than one more session.

## What "ready" means

Each unit is ready when you score **[your target, e.g. the passing score plus a cushion]** on a mixed question set on a *different day* from when you studied it, using questions you haven't seen. Check this in weeks 7 and 8. One good score on the same day you studied doesn't count. Any unit below target gets the weekday slots in week 8.

## Why it's built this way

- **Untouched units go first** (weeks 1–4), one at a time, while the units you've covered get a 10-minute slot each weekday. They won't fade before the second pass.
- **Friday recall** and **Saturday quizzes** bring each new unit back after a night's sleep and again a few days later.
- **Weeks 5–6** are a second pass spaced weeks after your first. **Weeks 7–8** mix every unit, because the exam will.
