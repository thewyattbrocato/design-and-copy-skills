# 9-Week Pharmacy Tech Exam Plan

**Time per week:** Mon–Fri 30 min, Sat 90 min, Sun off (about 4 hours a week).

## Daily routine
- **Mon–Thu (30 min):** 20 min on the week's focus unit, then 10 min of closed-book recall on a unit from the "10-min recall" column.
- **Fri (30 min):** Closed-book self-quiz on the week's unit. Write or say answers before checking notes. Mark every miss.
- **Sat (90 min):**
  - 15 min: write everything you remember about last week's unit from a blank page.
  - 60 min: mixed practice questions from all units you've covered so far.
  - 15 min: log your misses and pick next week's weak spots.

## Rules
- **Do the Sunday off.** Sleep between sessions is part of how this works.
- **Use closed-book recall, not re-reading.** Re-reading feels productive but holds up worse on the exam.
- **Calculations get touched most weeks.** It's a skill, so keep doing problems.
- **Fix misses within 48 hours.** Redo any missed question two days later, in a different form if you can.
- **The day before the exam, do about 15 minutes of review at most.** Exam date: [fill in].

## Check you're on track
- **End of Wk 3:** You can answer Friday quizzes on the three new units without notes, and you can name the mistakes you keep making.
- **Wk 7 vs Wk 8 practice tests:** Your score on [your target score] should rise, and your misses should be fewer and more scattered. If one unit keeps showing up, give it more of Wk 8–9.
