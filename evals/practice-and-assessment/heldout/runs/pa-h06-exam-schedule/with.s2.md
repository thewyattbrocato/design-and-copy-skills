# 9-Week Pharmacy Tech Exam Plan

**Weekly budget:** Mon–Fri 30 min, Sat 90 min, Sun off.

## Daily routine
| Day | Minutes | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon–Thu** | 30 | **10 min:** write what you remember from yesterday on a blank page, then check your notes. **20 min:** the week's topic. |
| **Fri** | 30 | **15 min:** calculation problems with no notes. **15 min:** blank-page recall of the week's topic. |
| **Sat** | 90 | **30 min:** fix what you missed this week. **30 min:** old-unit review (rotates, see below). **30 min:** mixed practice questions. Check answers after you finish all of them. |
| **Sun** | – | Off |

Recall means writing or saying it before you look. Rereading doesn't count.

## The 9 weeks
| Week | Mon–Thu topic (new material in **bold**) | Saturday old-unit review |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sterile compounding, part 1** | Calculations |
| 2 | **Sterile compounding, part 2** | Drug classes |
| 3 | **Inventory & ordering** | Law & ethics |

| 5 | Mon calcs · Tue drug classes · Wed law · Thu sterile compounding | Drug classes + law |
| 6 | Mon calcs · Tue inventory · Wed safety · Thu drug classes | Sterile compounding + calcs |
| 7 | Weakest two units, based on your Saturday scores | **Full practice test** (90 min) |
| 8 | Weakest two units again, then mixed questions from all six | **Second full practice test** |
| 9 | Light review only: Mon–Thu 30 min of mixed questions, Fri rest | Short skim, then rest |

## Rules
- Do the new units first, in weeks 1–4. You have the least time with them, and they build on calculations.
- Calculations get practice every Friday and most Saturdays. Doing the math is the skill, so reading about it won't help.
- Treat a unit as solid only when you score well on it on a later day, in a mixed set. Pick your own bar, for example [your target %].
- Keep a "missed it" list. Each miss goes on it with the reason, and the list drives the Saturday fix-up block.
- Week 9 is deliberately light. Sleep matters more than cramming.
