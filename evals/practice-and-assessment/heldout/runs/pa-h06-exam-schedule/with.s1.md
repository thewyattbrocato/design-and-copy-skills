Here's a plan for the fridge. It assumes your exam is around **Thu Dec 10** (bracket: [exam date]). That gives you a head start this Thursday through Saturday, 8 full weeks, and a short final stretch. Your total is about 4 hours a week.

## How each session works
- **Weekdays (30 min):** 20 minutes on the focus unit, then 10 minutes of recall on an older unit. For the recall, close your notes and answer practice questions from memory, then check.
- **Saturdays (90 min):** 45 minutes on the focus unit, then 45 minutes of mixed practice questions from all units you've met.
- **Error log:** Write every missed question on one page, with why you missed it. Reread it each Saturday.
- **Sundays:** Off. If you miss a day, skip it and don't double up.

## Moving on
Move on from a unit when you hit [your target score] on mixed questions on a later day, not the day you studied it. If a Saturday set falls short, give that unit one extra weekday recall slot the next week. Don't drop a scheduled unit.

- **Calculations** is a skill, so it gets a little practice in nearly every week. Work the problems yourself before looking at worked solutions.
- **Exam date:** If your date is earlier or later than Dec 10, change Week 8 and the final stretch. Keep the weeks of new material as they are.
