## Every session, same shape
- **Mon–Thu, 30 min:**
  - 5 min: on a blank page, write what you remember from yesterday's session. Don't look at your notes.
  - 20 min: new material or practice problems.
  - 5 min: write down 3 things you got wrong or couldn't recall.
- **Fri, 30 min:** Close the book and recall the week's material. Then look up only what you missed.
- **Sat, 90 min:**
  - 45 min: practice questions on this week's unit. Answer first, then look up the misses.
  - 30 min: practice questions on an older unit, so it doesn't fade.
  - 15 min: update your miss list and set Monday's focus.
- **Sunday:** off.
- **If you miss a weekday:** skip it. Don't double up the next day. Saturday absorbs the gap.
