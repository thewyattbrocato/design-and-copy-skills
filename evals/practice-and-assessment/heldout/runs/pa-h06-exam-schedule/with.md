# 9-week pharmacy tech exam schedule

**Weekdays (30 min):** Mon–Thu are 20 min on the week's topic plus 10 min of recall from earlier weeks. Fri is 30 min of recall only.
**Saturday (90 min):** 60 min on the topic, then a 30 min closed-book mixed quiz.
**Sunday:** off.

**Recall means closed book.** Write or say the answer first, then check. Re-reading doesn't count.

| Wk | Focus | Mon–Thu (20 + 10 recall) | Fri (30) | Sat (60 + 30 quiz) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sterile compounding A** (new): aseptic technique, garbing, cleanroom basics | Learn one sub-topic a day; recall the previous day's | Recall the week, no notes | Finish the week's material, then quiz on sterile compounding |
| 2 | **Sterile compounding B** (new): hazardous drugs, labeling, beyond-use dating, IV calcs | New material; recall wk 1 | Recall sterile compounding A and B | Finish B, then quiz on all sterile compounding |
| 3 | **Inventory & ordering** (new) | New material; recall sterile compounding | Recall inventory | Finish inventory, then quiz on inventory and sterile compounding |
| 4 | **Safety & error prevention** (new) | New material; recall inventory and sterile compounding | Recall safety | Finish safety, then quiz on all three new units |
| 5 | **Calculations**, 2nd pass | Work problems cold first, then review misses; recall the three new units | Calculation problems only | Timed-free problem set, then mixed quiz |
| 6 | **Drug classes**, 2nd pass | Flashcards from memory, then check; recall calculations | Recall drug classes | Drug classes quiz, then mixed quiz |
| 7 | **Law & ethics**, 2nd pass | Scenario practice ("what would you do?"); recall drug classes | Recall law | Law quiz, then mixed quiz on all six units |
| 8 | **Mixed practice** | 30 min mixed questions from all six units; spend the last 10 min on the weakest unit | Redo Mon–Thu misses | **Full practice test** (90 min) |
| 9 | **Weak spots, then taper** | Mon–Wed: 30 min on the 2 weakest units from the wk 8 test. Thu–Fri: light recall only | Rest or 15 min light recall | **Second full practice test** (this week's Saturday). Skim only afterward, no new material |

## Rules for the fridge
- **Missed a day?** Skip it. Don't double up. Only protect Saturdays.
- **Score each quiz by unit.** The week 8 and week 9 tests tell you where to spend the last days.
- **A unit counts as solid** when you pass it on a later day, in a mixed set, with questions you haven't seen. A same-day score doesn't count. Pick your own target: [your target %].

Two notes:
- I assumed the exam falls at the end of week 9. If it's earlier, tell me the date and I'll compress weeks 5–9.
- I put the new units first because you've never seen them. Calculations, drug classes and law get a second pass in weeks 5–7, and the weekday recall slots keep them from going cold in between.
