# Pharmacy Tech Exam: 9-Week Fridge Schedule

Week 1 starts Mon Oct 19 and Week 9 ends Sat Dec 19. I've assumed your exam falls right after that, so move the weeks if it doesn't. Sundays are off. You have 4 hours a week, 36 hours in total.

## How each session works
- **Start every session by answering from memory.** Spend the first 5 minutes on yesterday's material with the notes closed.
- **Test yourself, don't re-read.** Do practice questions and write out answers.
- **Keep an error log.** Write every miss on one page with the reason, such as "mixed up units" or "forgot the rule". Fridays and Saturdays use it.

## Weeks 1–4: Learn the three new units, keep the old three alive

| Week | Mon–Thu (30 min) | Fri (30 min) | Sat (90 min) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** (Oct 19) | **Sterile compounding** part 1 | Calculations: work problems cold, then redo misses | 45 sterile questions + 30 drug classes recall + 15 error log |
| **2** (Oct 26) | **Sterile compounding** part 2 | Calculations, including sterile calculations | 45 sterile questions + 30 law and ethics recall + 15 error log |
| **3** (Nov 2) | **Inventory and ordering** | Calculations, including inventory math | 45 inventory questions + 30 drug classes recall + 15 error log |
| **4** (Nov 9) | **Safety and error prevention** | Calculations | 45 safety questions + 30 law and ethics recall + 15 error log |

- **Mon–Thu split:** 5 minutes recalling yesterday, 20 minutes on new material, 5 minutes writing or answering questions on it.
- **Saturday's 30-minute recall block:** Write down everything you remember about that unit, then check your notes.

## When a unit counts as solid
A unit is solid when you score at your target on a mixed set on a later day, not the same day you studied it. Set that target yourself: [your exam's passing score + a safety margin]. If a unit falls short, make it Friday's focus until it clears.
