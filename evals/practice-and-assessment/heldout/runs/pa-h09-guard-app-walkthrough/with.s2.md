# Pocketlane first-run walkthrough copy

## Screens

**Screen 1: Welcome**
- **Headline:** Welcome to Pocketlane
- **Body:** Let's get your money organized. This quick tour takes about a minute.
- **Buttons:** Next · Skip tour
- **Footnote:** Skipping? You can find this tour again anytime in Settings.

**Screen 2: Link your bank**
- **Headline:** Bring in your transactions
- **Body:** Link your bank account and Pocketlane can see your transactions. Access is read-only, so we can't move money. Setup takes just a minute or two.
- **Buttons:** Next · Skip tour

**Screen 3: Set your budgets**
- **Headline:** Give each category a limit
- **Body:** Pick a category, like groceries or fun, and set a monthly budget for it. Your budgets reset on the 1st of every month, so each month starts fresh.
- **Buttons:** Next · Skip tour

**Screen 4: Add expenses by hand**
- **Headline:** Paid cash? No problem.
- **Body:** You can add any expense yourself. All you need is an amount and a category. A note is optional.
- **Buttons:** Get started
- **Footnote:** Want to see this tour again? It's under Settings.

## Tooltips

**Linking a bank account**
> Link your bank in a minute or two. It's read-only, so Pocketlane can see your transactions but can't move money.

**Setting a monthly budget for a category**
> Set a monthly limit for this category. It resets on the 1st of every month.

**Adding an expense by hand**
> Enter an amount and pick a category. Adding a note is up to you.
