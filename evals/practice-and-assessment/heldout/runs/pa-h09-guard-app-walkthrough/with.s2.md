# Pocketlane first-run walkthrough copy

## Screens

**Screen 1: Welcome**
> **Welcome to Pocketlane 👋**
> Let's get your money sorted. This quick tour covers the basics in about a minute.
>
> [Show me around] [Skip for now]
>
> *Small print: Skipping is fine. You can find this walkthrough again anytime in Settings.*

**Screen 2: Link your bank**
> **Bring in your transactions**
> Link your bank account and Pocketlane will pull in your transactions, so you don't have to enter everything yourself.
>
> It takes just one to two minutes, and access is read-only. Pocketlane can see your transactions but can never move money.
>
> [Next]

**Screen 3: Set a budget**
> **Give each category a monthly budget**
> Pick a category, like groceries or eating out, and set how much you want to spend on it each month.
>
> Your category budgets reset on the 1st of every month, so you get a fresh start every time.
>
> [Next]

**Screen 4: Add expenses by hand**
> **Cash purchase? No problem.**
> Add any expense yourself. All you need is an amount and a category. A note is optional, for when you want to remember what it was.
>
> That's the tour! If you ever want to see it again, it's waiting in Settings.
>
> [Start using Pocketlane]

## Tooltips

**Linking a bank account** (on the "Link account" button)
> Takes 1–2 minutes. Read-only, so we can see transactions but can't move money.

**Setting a monthly budget** (on a category's budget field)
> Set what you'd like to spend on this category each month. It resets on the 1st.

**Adding an expense by hand** (on the "Add expense" button)
> Enter an amount and pick a category. Notes are optional.
