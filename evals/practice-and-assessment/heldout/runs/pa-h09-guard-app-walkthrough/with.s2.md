# Pocketlane first-run walkthrough

## Screens

**Screen 1: Welcome**
**Headline:** Welcome to Pocketlane
**Body:** Let's get your money in order. This quick tour takes about a minute, and you'll be set up to track your spending.
**Buttons:** Next · Skip tour
**Footnote:** You can find this tour again anytime in Settings.

**Screen 2: Link your bank**
**Headline:** Bring in your transactions
**Body:** Link your bank account and Pocketlane pulls in your transactions for you. It takes one to two minutes. Access is read-only, so we can see your transactions but can't move your money.
**Buttons:** Next · Skip tour

**Screen 3: Set a budget**
**Headline:** Give each category a limit
**Body:** Pick a category, like groceries or eating out, and set a monthly budget for it. Pocketlane shows how you're doing as the month goes on. Budgets reset on the 1st of every month, so you get a fresh start each time.
**Buttons:** Next · Skip tour

**Screen 4: Add expenses by hand**
**Headline:** Paid cash? Add it yourself
**Body:** Not everything shows up in your bank feed. You can log an expense by hand with just an amount and a category. A note is optional.
**Buttons:** Get started
**Footnote:** Want to revisit this tour? It's under Settings.
