# Pocketlane First-Run Walkthrough

## Screen 1: Welcome
**Headline:** Welcome to Pocketlane 👋
**Body:** Let's get your money sorted, one small step at a time. This quick tour takes less than a minute.
**Buttons:** Next · Skip tour

## Screen 2: Connect your bank
**Headline:** Bring in your transactions
**Body:** Link your bank account and Pocketlane will pull in your spending automatically. No more typing in every coffee.
**Buttons:** Next · Skip tour

## Screen 3: Set your budgets
**Headline:** Give every category a limit
**Body:** Pick a category, like groceries or eating out, and set a monthly budget. Your budgets reset on the 1st of every month, so you get a fresh start each time.
**Buttons:** Next · Skip tour

## Screen 4: You're ready
**Headline:** You're all set!
**Body:** Spent cash or want to log something yourself? You can add expenses by hand any time. Want to see this tour again? You'll find it under Settings.
**Buttons:** Get started

*Note: "Skip tour" appears on screens 1–3. If you'd like skipping to be reassuring, you could add a small line beneath it: "You can find this tour later in Settings."*

---

## Tooltips

**Linking a bank account**
> Linking takes about one to two minutes. Pocketlane gets read-only access, so it can see your transactions but can never move money.

**Setting a monthly budget for a category**
> Choose a category and set an amount for the month. It resets on the 1st, so you start fresh every month.

**Adding an expense by hand**
> Enter an amount and pick a category. Want to remember what it was for? Add a note, though that part's optional.

---

A few choices worth flagging:
- I put the read-only reassurance in the bank tooltip rather than on screen 2, since that's where people hesitate. If you'd rather address it earlier, it fits well on screen 2 too.
- The emoji on screen 1 is optional. Cut it if it doesn't match your brand.
- I kept the Settings mention on screen 4 and in the skip note, since both are moments when people wonder whether they'll lose the tour.
