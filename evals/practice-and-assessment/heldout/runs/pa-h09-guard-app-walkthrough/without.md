# Pocketlane first-run walkthrough

## Screens

**Screen 1: Welcome**
**Welcome to Pocketlane**
Let's get your money feeling a little more manageable. This quick tour takes about a minute.
Buttons: **Show me around** · Skip

**Screen 2: Connect your bank**
**Bring in your transactions**
Link your bank and Pocketlane will pull in your spending automatically. Access is read-only, so we can see your transactions but can never move your money.
Buttons: **Next** · Skip

**Screen 3: Set your budgets**
**Give every category a limit**
Pick a category, set a monthly amount, and watch your progress as you spend. Budgets reset on the 1st of every month, so you always get a fresh start.
Buttons: **Next** · Skip

**Screen 4: You're all set**
**Ready when you are**
Cash purchase? Missed transaction? You can add expenses by hand anytime. If you want to see this tour again, you'll find it in Settings.
Buttons: **Start using Pocketlane**

**Skip confirmation (optional toast):** No problem! You can find the walkthrough later under Settings.

## Tooltips

**Linking a bank account**
Link your bank in 1–2 minutes. It's read-only, so Pocketlane can see transactions but can't move money.

**Setting a monthly budget for a category**
Set a monthly limit for this category. It resets on the 1st of every month.

**Adding an expense by hand**
Enter an amount and pick a category. Want to remember what it was for? Add a note (optional).
