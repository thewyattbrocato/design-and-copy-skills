# Pocketlane first-run walkthrough

## Screen 1: Welcome
**Headline:** Hi, welcome to Pocketlane!
**Body:** Let's get your money in one place. It only takes a few minutes, and you can skip any step and come back to it later.
**Button:** Let's go

## Screen 2: Connect your bank
**Headline:** Bring in your transactions
**Body:** Link your bank and we'll pull in your spending for you. It takes about one to two minutes. We have read-only access, so we can see your transactions, but we can't move your money.
**Buttons:** Link my bank / Maybe later

## Screen 3: Set a budget
**Headline:** Give your categories a limit
**Body:** Pick a category like Groceries or Dining, then set a monthly amount. Budgets reset on the 1st of every month, so each month starts fresh.
**Buttons:** Set a budget / Skip for now

## Screen 4: You're all set
**Headline:** You're all set!
**Body:** Your money is ready to track. If you skipped anything, you can find this walkthrough again anytime under Settings.
**Button:** Take me to my dashboard

## Tooltips

**Linking a bank account**
Takes about 1–2 minutes. We have read-only access: we can see transactions, but we can't move money.

**Setting a monthly budget**
Set a limit for this category. It resets on the 1st of every month.

**Adding an expense by hand**
You'll need an amount and a category. A note is optional.

---

I kept the tooltips to one or two sentences each and used the Settings path from your notes for the skip option. The button labels are placeholders, so swap in your own wording. Before launch, have someone confirm the "read-only" and "can't move money" language matches what your bank-linking provider actually does, since that's the claim users are most likely to check.
