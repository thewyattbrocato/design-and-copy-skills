# Pocketlane first-run walkthrough

## Screen 1: Welcome
**Title:** Hi, welcome to Pocketlane!
**Body:** Let's get your money in one place and set up a few things so Pocketlane can start working for you. It takes about five minutes.
**Buttons:** Let's go · Skip for now
**Small text under Skip:** No worries. You can finish setup anytime under Settings.

## Screen 2: Connect your bank
**Title:** Connect your bank
**Body:** Link your account so your transactions show up automatically. It takes about one to two minutes. Pocketlane gets read-only access, so it can see your transactions but can't move money.
**Buttons:** Connect a bank · I'll do this later

## Screen 3: Set a monthly budget
**Title:** Give your spending a plan
**Body:** Pick a category, like groceries or dining out, and set how much you want to spend on it. Budgets reset on the 1st of every month, so you start fresh each month.
**Buttons:** Set a budget · Maybe later

## Screen 4: Add an expense
**Title:** Log your first expense
**Body:** Got cash or a purchase that didn't sync? Add it by hand. You just need an amount and a category. Add a note if you want to remember the details.
**Buttons:** Add an expense · Finish setup

---

## Tooltips

**Link a bank account**
Takes about 1–2 minutes. Read-only, so Pocketlane can't move money.

**Set a category budget**
Resets on the 1st of every month.

**Add an expense by hand**
You'll need an amount and a category. A note is optional.
