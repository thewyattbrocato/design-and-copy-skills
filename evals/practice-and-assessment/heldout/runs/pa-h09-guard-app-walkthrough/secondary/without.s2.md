# Pocketlane first-run walkthrough

## Screens

**Screen 1: Welcome**
- **Headline:** Hi, welcome to Pocketlane!
- **Body:** Let's get your money in one place. This takes about five minutes, and you can skip any step.
- **Button:** Let's go

**Screen 2: Connect your bank**
- **Headline:** See your spending automatically
- **Body:** Link your bank and your transactions will show up here on their own. It takes one to two minutes. Pocketlane has read-only access, so it can see your transactions but can't move any money.
- **Buttons:** Link my bank / Maybe later

**Screen 3: Set a budget**
- **Headline:** Give your categories a monthly goal
- **Body:** Pick a category like groceries or eating out, and set how much you want to spend on it. Budgets reset on the 1st of every month, so you start fresh each month.
- **Buttons:** Set a budget / Maybe later

**Screen 4: You're all set**
- **Headline:** You're all set!
- **Body:** Your dashboard is ready. Add an expense any time with the + button, or check your progress on your budgets whenever you like.
- **Button:** Take me to my dashboard

*Skip link on every screen:* "Skip for now. You can find this walkthrough anytime under Settings."

## Tooltips

**Linking a bank account**
> Takes about 1–2 minutes. Pocketlane can only read your transactions. It can't move money.

**Setting a monthly budget**
> Budgets reset on the 1st of each month, so this goal starts fresh every time.

**Adding an expense by hand**
> You just need an amount and a category. Add a note if you want, but it's optional.
