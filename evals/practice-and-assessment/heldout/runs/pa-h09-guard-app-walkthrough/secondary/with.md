## First-run walkthrough

**Screen 1: Welcome**

> **Hi, welcome to Pocketlane!**
> We'll help you see where your money goes, one step at a time. Setup takes just a few minutes.
>
> [Let's go] [Skip for now]

**Screen 2: Connect your bank**

> **See your transactions automatically**
> Link your bank and your recent spending shows up here on its own. It takes about 1 to 2 minutes. Pocketlane has read-only access, so it can see your transactions but can't move your money.
>
> [Link my bank] [Maybe later]

**Screen 3: Set a monthly budget**

> **Give each category a limit**
> Pick a category, like groceries or dining out, and set how much you'd like to spend. Budgets reset on the 1st of every month, so each month starts fresh.
>
> [Set a budget] [Next]

**Screen 4: Add expenses by hand**

> **Track the rest too**
> Paid in cash, or something your bank doesn't show? Add it yourself. You only need an amount and a category. A note is optional.
>
> [Add an expense] [Finish setup]

*Footer on any screen:* You can skip this anytime and find it later under Settings.
