# Pocketlane First-Run Walkthrough

## Screens

**Screen 1: Welcome**
**Headline:** Welcome to Pocketlane 👋
**Body:** Let's get your money organized. This quick tour takes less than a minute, and you can skip it anytime.
**Buttons:** Next · Skip tour

**Screen 2: Link your bank**
**Headline:** Bring your transactions in
**Body:** Link your bank account and Pocketlane will pull in your transactions automatically. Access is read-only, so we can see your activity but can never move your money.
**Buttons:** Next · Skip tour

**Screen 3: Set a budget**
**Headline:** Give every category a limit
**Body:** Pick a category, like groceries or dining out, and set a monthly budget. Your category budgets reset on the 1st of every month, so you get a fresh start each time.
**Buttons:** Next · Skip tour

**Screen 4: Add expenses**
**Headline:** Cash spending? We've got you.
**Body:** Add an expense by hand whenever you need to. All it takes is an amount and a category. Want to remember what it was for? Add a note. You can find this tour again anytime in Settings.
**Buttons:** Get started

## Tooltips

**Linking a bank account**
Linking takes about one to two minutes. It's read-only, so Pocketlane can see your transactions but can't move money.

**Setting a monthly budget for a category**
Set how much you want to spend in this category each month. It resets on the 1st.

**Adding an expense by hand**
Enter an amount and pick a category. A note is optional.

## Notes
- Screen 4 mentions that the tour lives in Settings. If you'd like that reassurance earlier, add "Skipped? Find this tour later in Settings" as small text under the Skip button on screens 1–3.
- Screen 4's headline, "Cash spending?", is a small assumption about why people add expenses by hand. If you'd rather not imply that, "Add expenses your way" works as a neutral alternative.
