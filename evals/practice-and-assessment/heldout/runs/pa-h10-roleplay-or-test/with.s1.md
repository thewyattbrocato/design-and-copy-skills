**Do B, but restructure it so it doesn't eat the team lead's time. Don't do A as the main assessment.**

**Why not A.** Objection handling is a live, spoken skill. A 40-question multiple-choice test measures whether reps can recognize a good response when they see the options. It doesn't show whether they can produce one with a skeptical homeowner pushing back. A same-day score also can't certify the skill. A rep can pass A and still freeze on a real call.

**Why B, with a fix.** Role-plays are observed performance, which is the evidence you need. The problem is the team lead's load. 15 reps × 3 role-plays × 20 minutes is 15 hours of live time, plus feedback.

Ways to cut that load without losing the live practice:

1. **Pair the reps.** In each 2-hour block, reps role-play each other, with the team lead rotating between pairs. The lead's live time per rep drops sharply, and the partner plays the customer.
2. **Give the observer a short rubric.** The partner scores each round on 3–4 observable behaviors, such as acknowledging the concern before responding, asking one clarifying question, and not leading with price. Use [your actual objection list and rubric criteria].
3. **Reserve the team lead for one role-play per rep.** Make it the last one, as the check that counts. Rounds 1 and 2 are peer-run practice with rubric feedback.
4. **Prep from a job aid.** Use the prep time to build a one-page objection cheat sheet from [your top objections], not to read about the objections.

**What to tell your VP.** A scales, but it measures the wrong thing, so it scales a weak signal. If the VP wants something scalable, use a short scenario-based quiz, not 40 recall questions. Pair it with the role-plays, as a 10–15 item prep or follow-up where each item puts a named rep in a specific call and asks what they'd say next. Auto-scoring works for that. It shouldn't replace the role-plays.

**How to know it worked.** Look at the metric you already track, such as [current objection-to-next-step conversion rate], and compare reps' first live calls against it. Do a short re-check role-play a week or two later with different objections. One same-day performance isn't mastery.
