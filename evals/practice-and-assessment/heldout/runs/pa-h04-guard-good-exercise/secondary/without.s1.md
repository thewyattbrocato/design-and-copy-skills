**Marguerite (Part 3): the key says 38.5 hours and $847.00, but the correct answer is 37.5 hours and $825.00.**

- Whole hours: 8.0 + 7.0 = 15
- Halves: 7.5 + 8.5 + 6.5 = 22.5
- Total: 15 + 22.5 = **37.5 hours**
- Pay: 37.5 × $22.00 = **$825.00**

The key's $847.00 is 38.5 × $22, so the total was wrong and the pay was calculated from it. This matters because Part 3 is the one meant to be done unaided, and a clerk who checks their work against this key would be marked wrong for a correct answer, or would learn the wrong total.

**Checked and correct:**

**Optional tweaks before printing:**
