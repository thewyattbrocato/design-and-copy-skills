I found one error. The answer key for Marguerite is wrong.

**Marguerite's answer should be 37.5 hours and $825.00, not 38.5 and $847.00.**

- Whole hours: 8.0 + 7.0 = 15
- Halves: 7.5 + 8.5 + 6.5 = 22.5
- Total: 15 + 22.5 = **37.5 hours**
- Pay: 37.5 × $22.00 = **$825.00**

The key's $847.00 is 38.5 × $22, so it carries the wrong total through. Because Part 3 is the one the clerks do alone, a wrong key there would teach them the wrong answer, so fix it before printing.

The other two check out:
- **Priya:** 38 hours, $760.00 ✓
- **Tomas:** 7.5 + 8 + 8 + 7 + 8 = 38.5 hours; 38.5 × $26 = $1,001.00 ✓

Two smaller suggestions:
- **The "add again" check is weak.** It only catches totals over 40, and none of these weeks is near 40, so a clerk who adds wrong and gets 30 or 45 won't be caught by it. A better check is "your total should fall between 37 and 39 hours" for all three, or a note to compare the whole-hours and halves subtotals with a second pass.
- **Mixed number formats.** Priya's whole hours are written as "8" and Marguerite's as "8.0". It's fine, but writing every hour with one decimal (8.0, 7.5, 6.5) would look more consistent on the printed sheet.
