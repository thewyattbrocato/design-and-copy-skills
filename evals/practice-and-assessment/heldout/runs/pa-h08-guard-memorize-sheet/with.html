How to study (recall beats re-reading)
  1. Cover the Meaning column with a card. Say each meaning aloud from the signal. Slide the card down to check.
  2. Missed one? Say it aloud three times, then re-try it straight away.
  3. Do part 2 below with the table covered. Tick a box only if you got all ten without peeking.
  4. Come back the next day and again after a few days, with the sheet covered. Do the "Later" box on a different day from the first two.
