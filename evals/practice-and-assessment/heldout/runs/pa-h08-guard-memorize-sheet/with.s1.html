How to study: fold a blank card over the meanings, say each one out loud from the code, then cover the codes and go the other way. Do not just re-read.

2. Easy to mix up (say the difference out loud)

M-1 Available vs M-8 Out of service: one can take a call, one cannot.
M-2 En route vs M-3 On scene: still driving vs already there.
M-4 Backup vs M-6 Medical: more officers vs medical help.

3. Drill (cover section 1 first; write the code, e.g. M-7)

  1. Unit pulls up at the address and reports arriving.
  2. Officer says more units are needed.
  3. Unit has left for the call and is driving there.
  4. Officer reports a fallen tree across the road.
  5. Officer says the person is hurt and needs medical help.
  6. Unit has cleared its last call and can take another.
  7. Officer reports they have pulled over a vehicle.
  8. Unit cannot take calls right now.
  9. Officer reports the suspect is under arrest and held.
  10. Unit is told to head back to base.
