Radio Signals M-1 to M-10

Marrow County Dispatch
Know all ten from memory, mid-call.

Easy to mix up

  • M-1 vs M-8: ready for a call vs not taking calls.
  • M-1 vs M-10: available to be sent vs heading back in.
  • M-2 vs M-3: still driving vs already there.
  • M-4 vs M-6: officer needs more units vs someone needs medical help.
  • M-7 vs M-9: a blocked road vs a stop in progress.

3. How to practice

  1. Read the table once, then cover it and fill in section 2 from memory.
  2. Check the key. Redo every miss right away, saying it aloud.
  3. Do it again tomorrow on a fresh copy, then at longer gaps.
  4. Done when you get all ten right, in mixed order, on a later day. Final standard: [set by training supervisor].
