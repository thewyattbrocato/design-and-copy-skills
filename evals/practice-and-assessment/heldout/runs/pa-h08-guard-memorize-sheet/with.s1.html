Radio Signals M-1 to M-10

Marrow County Dispatch
Know them cold. No lookup mid-call.

The ten signals

M-1Unit available
M-6Medical needed
M-2Unit en route
M-7Road blocked
M-3On scene
M-8Out of service
M-4Need backup
M-9Traffic stop
M-5Suspect in custody
M-10Return to station

Easy to mix up: say the difference out loud

  • M-1 / M-2 / M-3: free to take a call, moving toward one, already there.
  • M-1 / M-8: available for calls vs. not taking calls.
  • M-8 / M-10: out of service is a status; return to station is an instruction to go back.
  • M-4 / M-6: backup means more officers; medical means medical help.

How to study

Do not re-read. Cover the list above and fill in the quizzes from memory. Check, then circle misses and redo only those. Repeat on a different day with the same sheet; the order below is scrambled so you can't lean on position. Log each try in the box at the bottom.
