Marrow County Dispatch: Radio Signals M-1 to M-10

Know these cold. No lookups on live calls.

Signal Meaning Memory cue Drill
M-1 Unit available Free and ready for a call
M-2 Unit en route Rolling to the call
M-3 On scene Arrived and working the call
M-4 Need backup Send more units now
M-5 Suspect in custody Suspect secured
M-6 Medical needed Send EMS
M-7 Road blocked Roadway closed or obstructed
M-8 Out of service Unit unavailable for calls
M-9 Traffic stop Pulled over a vehicle
M-10 Return to station Heading back to base
  • Repeat the signal back to the unit by number and meaning before you move on.
  • Quiz yourself: cover the Meaning column and say each signal aloud.
  • Check the Drill box once you can recall the signal in under two seconds.
