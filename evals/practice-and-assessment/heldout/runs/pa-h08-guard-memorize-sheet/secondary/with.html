M-Signal Quick Reference: Know These Cold

Signal Meaning
M-1 Unit available
M-2 Unit en route
M-3 On scene
M-4 Need backup
M-5 Suspect in custody
M-6 Medical needed
M-7 Road blocked
M-8 Out of service
M-9 Traffic stop
M-10 Return to station
Drill tip Cover the Meaning column and say each signal aloud until you can call all ten in order without hesitation. Then reverse it: hear the meaning, give the signal. Practice until both directions are automatic.
