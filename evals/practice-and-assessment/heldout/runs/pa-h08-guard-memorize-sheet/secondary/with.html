Marrow County Dispatch: Radio Signals

Know all ten cold. No notes on the console.
M-1 through M-10
Code Meaning Memory cue Self-check
Unit status
M-1 Unit available Free and ready for assignment
M-2 Unit en route Rolling to the call
M-3 On scene Arrived, working the call
M-8 Out of service Unavailable for any call
M-10 Return to station Clearing the call, heading back
Requests and situation
M-4 Need backup Requesting additional units
M-5 Suspect in custody Subject detained and secured
M-6 Medical needed Send EMS to the scene
M-7 Road blocked Route closed, reroute units
M-9 Traffic stop Vehicle stopped by an officer

Quick reference rules

Status flow Available (M-1) to en route (M-2) to on scene (M-3) to return to station (M-10) to available (M-1).
Pull-away signals M-8 takes a unit out of rotation. Confirm the unit is back in service before assigning it.
Escalation M-4 requests more units. M-6 requests EMS. Use both when the scene needs more than one unit or a medic.
Scene hazards M-7 closes a route. M-5 means the subject is secured. Relay both to every unit that may be affected.
