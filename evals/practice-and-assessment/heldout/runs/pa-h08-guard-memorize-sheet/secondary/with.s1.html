M-Signal Codes

Marrow County Dispatch · Know these cold

Ten signals · Memorize · No lookup on live calls

M-1
Unit available
M-2
Unit en route
M-3
On scene
M-4
Need backup
M-5
Suspect in custody
M-6
Medical needed
M-7
Road blocked
M-8
Out of service
M-9
Traffic stop
M-10
Return to station
Quick sequence: M-1 (available) → M-2 (en route) → M-3 (on scene) → M-5 (custody) or M-10 (return to station). Use M-4 for backup and M-6 for medical at any point in the call. M-8 takes a unit out of rotation.
Review aloud until each code and meaning comes without hesitation. Marrow County Dispatch
