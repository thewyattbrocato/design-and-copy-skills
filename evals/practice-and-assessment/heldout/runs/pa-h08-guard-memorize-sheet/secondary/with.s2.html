Marrow County Dispatch

M-Signal Radio Codes

Know all ten cold. No lookups on live calls.

Signal Meaning Drilled
M-1 Unit available
M-2 Unit en route
M-3 On scene
M-4 Need backup
M-5 Suspect in custody
M-6 Medical needed
M-7 Road blocked
M-8 Out of service
M-9 Traffic stop
M-10 Return to station

Drill Tips

  • Cover the Meaning column and say each signal out loud before checking.
  • Quiz yourself in random order, not 1 to 10, so the codes stick by meaning.
  • Mark a box each time you recall a signal without hesitation. Re-drill any you miss.
