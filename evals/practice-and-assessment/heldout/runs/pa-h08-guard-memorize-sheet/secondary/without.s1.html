Self-Check Drill

  • Cover the Meaning column and say each signal's meaning aloud, then check your answer.
  • Cover the Signal column and state the signal for each meaning, without looking.
  • Check the box once you can answer a signal correctly three times in a row, from memory.
  • Review any unchecked signals at the start of every shift until all ten are checked.
