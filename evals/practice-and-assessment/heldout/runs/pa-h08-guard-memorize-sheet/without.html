Marrow County Dispatch

Radio Signals Study Sheet

Know all ten cold: from memory, mid-call, no lookups.

  1. M-1Unit available
  2. M-2Unit en route
  3. M-3On scene
  4. M-4Need backup
  5. M-5Suspect in custody
  6. M-6Medical needed
  7. M-7Road blocked
  8. M-8Out of service
  9. M-9Traffic stop
  10. M-10Return to station
How to drill
  • Fold or cover the right-hand column. Say the meaning for each code, then check.
  • Reverse it: cover the codes, and give the signal for each meaning.
  • Have a partner call out codes in random order. Repeat until there are no misses.
