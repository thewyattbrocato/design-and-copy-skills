How to study (don't just re-read)

  1. Cover the right-hand column with a card. Say each meaning aloud, then slide the card to check.
  2. Fold or cover this table, then fill in the blanks below from memory. Check against the key.
  3. Redo every miss right away, then repeat the test in a different order on later days.
