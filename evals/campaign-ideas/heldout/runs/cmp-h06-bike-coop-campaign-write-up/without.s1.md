# Spoke Yard Fall Campaign Concept: "Bring the Flat"

## The idea

A flat tire is the most common first visit to Spoke Yard, according to our volunteer sign-in sheet. The campaign builds on that. Most people who ride have had a flat, and many have a bike leaning in a hallway because of one. We invite them to bring it in.

**Headline:** *Bring the flat.*
**Supporting line:** *Learn to fix it yourself. $9 a month, tools and help included.*

The campaign makes the smallest, most familiar bike problem the reason to walk in. It also tells people what membership gets them: a stand, tools, a free walkthrough for beginners, and volunteers who will show them how.

**The goal:** we have 64 members and want 100 by December 31, which is 36 more. We'll show that number publicly throughout the campaign.

## Why it fits us

- **It's true to how people already use us.** We aren't inventing a pitch. We're repeating what our own sign-in sheet shows.
- **It lowers the barrier for beginners.** Beginners get a 30-minute walkthrough on their first visit, so "I don't know how" isn't a reason to stay away.
- **It shows the value of $9 a month.** Membership covers the stand and tools during open hours. It also includes the shelf of donated used parts, which are free with membership.
- **It suits a volunteer-run co-op.** All our instructors are volunteers and we have no staff. The tone should be neighborly and hands-on, not slick. We're people who'll show you how.
- **It has a clear, public goal.** A target of 100 gives people something to join, not just a service to buy.

## How it shows up

### Poster

A simple, high-contrast layout with one large image: a bike wheel with a flat tire on a repair stand. Text:

> **Bring the flat.**
> Learn to fix it yourself.
> $9 a month. Stand, tools, and volunteer help included.
> Free used parts shelf with membership.
> First visit? We'll walk you through it. (30 minutes, beginners welcome.)
> **Wednesdays & Saturdays, 10–4**
> Back of the old laundromat, Pell Street

The footer reads "Run by volunteers. 64 members and counting. Help us reach 100 by December 31." The poster goes up wherever people park or lock bikes.

### Email

**Subject:** Got a flat? Bring it in.

**Preview text:** Learn to fix it yourself, with volunteers beside you.

**Body (short):**

> The most common reason people first walk into Spoke Yard is a flat tire. If that's you, you're in good company.
>
> Membership is $9 a month. It covers the repair stand and tools during open hours (Wednesdays and Saturdays, 10 to 4, in the back of the old laundromat on Pell Street). If it's your first visit, a volunteer will give you a 30-minute walkthrough. Members can also take what they need from our shelf of donated used parts at no charge.
>
> We're volunteer-run, with no staff. We have 64 members and want to reach 100 by December 31. Come fix your flat and help us get there.
>
> **[Join for $9/month]**

This goes first to our existing list, with a line asking members to forward it to a friend with a bike.

### Instagram post

A short carousel:

1. **Slide 1:** The flat tire image with "Bring the flat."
2. **Slide 2:** "Most common first visit at Spoke Yard? A flat tire. (It's on our sign-in sheet.)"
3. **Slide 3:** "$9/month: stand + tools during open hours. Free used parts shelf. 30-minute walkthrough for beginners."
4. **Slide 4:** "64 members. Goal: 100 by Dec 31. Wed & Sat, 10–4, Pell Street."

**Caption:** *Flat tire? Bring it in. All our instructors are volunteers, and we'll show you how. Link in bio to join.*

### Shop window

This is the most distinctive piece. Hang a real bike wheel with a flat tire in the window, with the headline "Bring the flat." Beside it, put a progress thermometer or a row of 100 hand-drawn spokes or tags. Color in 64 now, and update it each open day. Passersby can watch it grow.

Under the display, put the hours and a small sign: "Open now, come in" on Wednesdays and Saturdays. The window will be the only part of the campaign that changes, so we'd need a volunteer to own the weekly update.
