# Fall Campaign Concept: "Bring the Flat"

## The idea

A flat tire is the most common first visit, according to our volunteer sign-in sheet. So we build the campaign around that moment. Instead of pitching "join a repair co-op," we speak to someone with a bike that won't roll and tell them where to take it.

**Headline:** *Bring the flat.*
**Supporting line:** *$9 a month. Stand, tools, and someone to show you how.*

The goal is 100 members by December 31. We have 64, so we need 36 more. We'd show that number publicly all season as a progress count (64 → 100), so people can see they'd be helping us hit it.

## Why it fits us

- **It's true to what we see.** The sign-in sheet shows what actually brings people through the door, so we aren't guessing at what people want.
- **It lowers the barrier.** Many people with a flat have never used a bike stand. Beginners get a 30-minute walkthrough on their first visit, so we can say plainly that no experience is needed.
- **It shows the value of $9.** The membership covers the stand and tools during open hours, plus free used parts from our donated shelf. A flat is the kind of problem that gets people to try us.
- **It suits a volunteer-run shop.** We have no staff, and every instructor is a volunteer. We can say that proudly: this is a neighborhood co-op, and members are part of it. We shouldn't imply a professional service.
- **It's cheap to produce.** Everything below can be made with a printer, a marker, and a phone.

## How it shows up

### Poster

- **Top:** BRING THE FLAT. (large, hand-lettered feel)
- **Middle:** "Join Spoke Yard for $9 a month. Use the stand and tools, grab free used parts from our shelf, and get a 30-minute walkthrough your first visit."
- **Bottom:** Open Wednesday and Saturday, 10 to 4. Back of the old laundromat on Pell Street.
- **Small line:** "Run entirely by volunteers."
- **Corner:** a hand-drawn tally, "64 members. Goal: 100 by Dec 31."

Post it at coffee shops, laundromats, bike racks, and community boards. Leave a blank line at the bottom for the sign-up method, which I'll need from you.

### Email

**Subject options:** "Got a flat? Start here." / "Bring the flat (and $9)" / "We're at 64. Help us get to 100."

**Body:**
> Most people's first visit to Spoke Yard starts with a flat tire. That's what our sign-in sheet says.
>
> If you've been meaning to learn to fix your own bike, this is the season to do it. For $9 a month, you get use of the stand and tools during open hours, free pick of the donated used parts on our shelf, and a 30-minute walkthrough on your first visit if you're new to this.
>
> We're run entirely by volunteers, and we're at 64 members. Our goal is 100 by December 31. Come Wednesday or Saturday, 10 to 4, in the back of the old laundromat on Pell Street.
>
> *[Sign-up link or instructions]*

This works for current members too. Ask them to forward it to one friend with a bike.

### Instagram

**Format:** a single image or a three-slide carousel.

- **Slide 1:** A photo of a flat tire on the stand, with the text "Bring the flat."
- **Slide 2:** "What $9 a month gets you" as a short list: stand and tools, free used parts, a 30-minute walkthrough for beginners.
- **Slide 3:** "64 members. Help us reach 100 by Dec 31." with hours and address.

**Caption:** "A flat tire is the #1 reason people first walk into Spoke Yard. Bring yours Wednesday or Saturday, 10 to 4, and we'll get you started. Volunteer-run, $9 a month."

Later in the season, post the updated member count every couple of weeks as a simple graphic.

### Shop window

- A large "BRING THE FLAT." sign, readable from the sidewalk.
- A **hand-drawn thermometer** showing 64 of 100, which we color in as members join. This is the most visible part of the campaign, and it gives passersby a reason to look again.
- Hours posted clearly beside it.
- If we have a spare tire or tube, hang it in the window as a prop.
