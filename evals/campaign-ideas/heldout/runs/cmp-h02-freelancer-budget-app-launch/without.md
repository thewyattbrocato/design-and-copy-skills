# Campaign idea: "Budget what you've banked"

**The hook:** Every budgeting app assumes a paycheck. Freelancers don't have one. Tally Nest only works with money that has actually landed. The line is something like *"Your income is lumpy. Your budget shouldn't be fiction."*

That's your differentiator, and it's a pain freelancers already complain about. The campaign should make that contrast obvious in about five seconds.

## The centerpiece: "The Lumpy Year"

Make one shareable visual: a bar chart of a freelancer's real monthly income, with huge swings, next to the flat "$4,000/month" line a normal budgeting app expects. The gap between the two is the ad.

- **Founders post their own version first.** Use real numbers from your own freelance or beta experience.
- **Then invite others to share theirs** with a hashtag like #LumpyIncome. Optionally, the app generates a clean, shareable image of your income shape, with amounts hidden by default.
- This gives people a reason to post that isn't "please download my app."

## Where to put it

Freelancers gather in specific places, so go to them rather than buying broad reach:
- r/freelance, r/graphic_design, r/webdev, and similar subreddits (read the self-promo rules first and lead with the story, not the link)
- Freelancer-focused Slacks and Discords
- Bluesky and X, from your personal accounts as the builders
- Newsletters aimed at freelancers or designers (a few small ones, pitched directly, beat one big one)
- Product Hunt on Nov 3, as a secondary channel

## Three-week timeline

- **Oct 14–20:** Pull numbers from the beta (see below). Get 3–5 beta users to agree to be quoted. Build the income-shape visual.
- **Oct 21–Nov 2:** Post the "lumpy income" content and build in public. Collect emails or TestFlight and App Store pre-order interest. Pitch newsletters.
- **Nov 3:** Launch post from both founders, Product Hunt, and DMs to the beta users. The free month is your call to action: "Try it free for a month."
- **Nov 4–17:** Follow up with real user stories and answer questions in public.

## What I need from you

You said you don't have beta numbers yet. For this campaign I'd want:
1. **Beta quotes and stories.** These matter more than stats at your size.
2. **One or two simple numbers**, like how many beta users, or how many are still using it after a few weeks. Only use numbers you can back up.
3. **Your own freelancer story.** Are either of you freelancers? That would be the most credible opening.

## Optional add-on

You could offer a "founding member" lock-in of $6/month for life to launch-month signups. It rewards early adopters and gives the free-month offer some urgency. That's a pricing decision, so it's yours to make.
