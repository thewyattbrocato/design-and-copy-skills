**The idea: "Banked, not budgeted."** Tally Nest launches by showing freelancers the one number a monthly budget never gives them: what they can spend from money that has actually landed.

The buyer truth [assumed: check it in beta feedback]: freelancers already do this in their heads. They look at the account, ignore the invoices that haven't paid, and guess. A monthly budget assumes a paycheck they don't have. The tension is that guessing is the habit and Tally Nest is the tool for it.

**Three forms (each works alone)**

1. **A short demo post or video, "The lumpy month."**
   - Show a real-looking freelancer month: $0 in week one, $4,200 in week three.
   - Split the screen. A normal budget app says "you're $1,800 over" in week one. Tally Nest says "You've banked $0, so you've planned $0," and the number then updates when the money lands.
   - The line: *"Your income isn't monthly. Your budget shouldn't be."*
   - Use a clearly labeled sample month, not presented as a customer's.

2. **A free "Lumpy Income Month" template.**
   - It's a one-page PDF or Notion/Sheets file where a freelancer logs what they've banked and sees what's safe to spend, no app needed.
   - It works without the brand and is easy to share in freelancer communities. The last line says "Tally Nest does this automatically," with the App Store link.

3. **A launch-day message from the two of you.**
   - Post it on your own accounts and send it to the beta list, in your own words: why you built it, who it's for, and that the first month is free, then $6.
   - Add one line about what you changed because of the beta. [add: a real change you made]

**Ask the owner**
- Which real beta feedback or quote can we use, with permission? [add: real beta user line, with consent]
- Do you have a few beta users willing to share their experience on the record, disclosed as beta users? Without them, skip any testimonial and let the demo carry it.
- Do you have time before Nov 3 to build the template? It's the only piece with real work in it.

**Plain fallback**
Skip the template and run only forms 1 and 3: a clear demo video of the banked-money number, plus your founder post, both pointing at the free month.

**One note:** Because you have no numbers yet, don't claim user counts, savings or "freelancers love it." The demo and your own story are strong enough without them. If you pull even two or three honest beta stats before Nov 3, add them to form 3.
