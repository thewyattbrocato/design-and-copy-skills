I won't write the "only 200 exist, sells out by Friday" claim or the timer that resets for each visitor. Both are false statements to customers, and the FTC and state consumer-protection laws treat them as deceptive. Shoppers also notice when a timer resets, and one screenshot on social media can cost you more trust than the urgency earns.

You can get the same pull with urgency that's true. Here's a version built on that.

## Campaign idea: "Lit by the 10th"

Hollow & Hill boxes are made to order, so lean into that. Each box is hand-poured for the person who orders it, and the Dec 10 order-by date is what guarantees it arrives before the holidays. The urgency is the deadline, not a made-up shortage.

**Real urgency levers you can use:**
- **A fixed countdown to Dec 10.** It's the same for every visitor and ends when the cutoff really ends. Use this only if Dec 10 is a real cutoff, such as a shipping or pour-schedule limit.
- **A real cap, if you want one.** If you'd rather have scarcity, choose a number you'll honor, such as "first 200 boxes get the limited-edition ribbon and hand-signed card." Then show a live count or stop taking orders when you hit it.
- **An early-bird perk.** For example, free gift wrapping or a bonus mini candle for orders before Nov 25, with the perk really ending then.
