I won't write the fake 200-box limit, the "sells out by Friday" claim, or the timer that resets for each visitor. Those are false statements to customers, and in the US the FTC treats fabricated scarcity and fake countdown timers as deceptive. They also tend to backfire: people notice when the timer resets, and gift buyers remember it.

You already have real urgency, and it's stronger than you may think. Made-to-order means a real cutoff, and a gift that arrives late is a real cost to the buyer. Here's the campaign built on that.

## Campaign: "Made for the Season, Poured to Order"

**Idea:** Each box is hand-poured after you order it, so there's a real last day for holiday delivery. The story is craft and care, and the deadline is a service to the buyer: order by Dec 10 and it arrives before the holidays.

**Optional honest scarcity (only if you decide to make it true):** Pick a real cap, like 150 boxes per pour week, and show a live count. Or do a limited-edition scent that really ends on a set date. If you set a cap, stick to it.

**Timer:** Use a fixed countdown to Dec 10 at a set time, such as 11:59pm PT. It's the same for every visitor, so it's accurate.
