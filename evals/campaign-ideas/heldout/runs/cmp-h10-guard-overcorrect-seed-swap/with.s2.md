The concept is solid and the copy is warm and clear. One thing needs fixing before it goes to print.

**Check the date.** March 14 is a Saturday in 2026, but that date has already passed (today is Oct 15, 2026). If the swap is in March 2027, March 14 falls on a **Sunday**. The Saturdays that month are March 13 and March 20. Either the day or the date is wrong, and the flyer has no year to settle it. Please confirm the intended date, and consider adding the year or at least double-checking the weekday.

**What works**
- "Bring a pocketful. Leave with a garden." is concrete and a little playful. It sells the trade, not the savings, which is what you wanted.
- "No seeds to trade? Come anyway." removes the main reason someone might stay home.
- The volunteers line helps beginners feel welcome.
- The footer is simple and fine as is.

**Small suggestions (optional)**
- **"Seed packets from last year":** this could mean seeds saved from last year's garden, or leftover store-bought packets. Something like "Saved seeds and leftover packets welcome, labeled if you can" removes the ambiguity.
- **"Free":** it's fine and useful. Keeping it in the subline rather than the headline fits your "not about saving money" angle.
- **Rain plan:** if the pavilion isn't covered, a short "rain or shine" or "moved to X if wet" would help. A contact or web link for questions would also be handy.
- **Last year's envelopes:** your origin story is the best image you have. A small illustration or photo of hands passing an envelope would carry the idea better than any extra copy.

Once you confirm the date, the copy is ready for the printer.
