**Check the date before printing.** I couldn't confirm that March 14 is a Saturday.
- March 14, 2026 was a Saturday, but that date has passed. Today is Oct 14, 2026.
- If this is for next spring, March 14, 2027 is a **Sunday**. The nearest Saturday is March 13.
- Either the weekday or the date is wrong, so confirm which one you mean. Anyone who reads "Saturday" will show up on the wrong day if it's off.

**Smaller notes, all optional:**
- **"Free" in the subline.** It's useful practical information, and it doesn't undercut the trading idea. Just keep it in the subline so the headline stays about trading.
- **Overlapping seed lines.** "Bring seeds you saved" and "Seed packets from last year welcome" say nearly the same thing, and "last year" could be read as leftover store-bought packets. Consider merging them. For example, the small print could say "Last year's seeds and store-bought packets welcome, labeled if you can."
- **"By the pavilion."** This is fine for regulars. For newcomers, a street address or "off [street name]" would help, if there's room.
- **Rain plan.** A March event might need one, such as "Rain or shine" or "Moves inside if wet." Skip it if it doesn't apply.
- **Small print.** "Volunteers at the long table" is a good detail. It tells nervous first-timers that help is available.
