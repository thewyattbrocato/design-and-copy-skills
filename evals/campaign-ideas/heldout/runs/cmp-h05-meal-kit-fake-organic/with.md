I won't write these as described. Posts that pose as independent customers, a weight-loss result nobody has had, and "4.9 stars from 2,000+ reviews" before you've shipped a box are all fabricated. That's deceptive advertising. The FTC's rules on fake reviews and undisclosed endorsements carry per-violation civil penalties, and a made-up health claim adds more exposure. It also tends to backfire at launch, because one person noticing that "customers" are all employees can undo the whole campaign.

You can still get the organic feel with honest posts. Here's a campaign built on that.

## Campaign idea: "First Box" (the founding table)

Your real advantage is that you haven't shipped yet. Position the first 100 or so boxes as a founding group. Real people get early boxes, and their real reactions become the content.

**How it runs**
1. **Pre-launch (now to launch):** Team and friends post as themselves with clear disclosure ("I work at Fennel & Fig" or "got an early box, #ad / gifted"). Posts are about the process: recipe testing, sourcing, packing the first box.
2. **Launch week:** Ship founding boxes to 50-100 real people, including micro-creators, in exchange for honest posts. They disclose, and you don't script their opinions.
3. **After launch:** Repost real reactions and quote real reviews. Once you have actual numbers, show them ("4.8 from our first 120 reviews").

## 8 posts (disclosed, true, and usable now)

1. **Team, recipe testing:** "I work at @fennelandfig and I've eaten the same braised fennel dish six times this month. Version 7 is finally right. Launching in two weeks. (Team member, obviously biased.)"
2. **Founder, origin:** "We're two weeks from shipping our first box. Here's why I started Fennel & Fig, and what I'm still nervous about."
3. **Team, sourcing:** "Visited the farm that'll supply our first-week produce. Photos of what's in season. #FennelAndFig (I work there)"
4. **Friend, early look (gifted):** "My friend's company is launching a meal kit and I got to preview the menu. Here's what I'd order first. Gifted preview, opinions mine."
5. **Team, packing day:** "Practice packing run. We've dropped three ice packs and learned a lot. First box ships soon."
6. **Founder, ask:** "We're picking 100 founding members for our first boxes. You'll tell us what's wrong as well as what's right. Link in bio."
7. **Team, behind the scenes:** "Taste-test notes from this week: what made the cut and what got cut. (Yes, the beet thing is gone.)"
8. **Friend, countdown (gifted):** "Tried a pre-launch recipe card from Fennel & Fig and cooked it on a Tuesday. 35 minutes, one pan. Honest take in comments. Gifted."

Don't make weight-loss claims unless you can substantiate them. Nutrition claims need real support.
