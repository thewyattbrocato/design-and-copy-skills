# Three directions, and the one I'd pick

## A. "Not Creative" (the objection as the headline)
Most people tell you "I'm not creative" before they sign up, so say it first.
- **Headline ideas:** "'I'm not creative.' Great. Tuesdays at 6:30." / "You don't need to be creative. You need to be on Dunmore Street by 6:30."
- **Format:** A series of ads, each one a version of the sentence, answered by the class details. Use the real emails only if you ask those people first. Otherwise write paraphrases.
- **Strength:** It's true to what you hear, and it sounds like a person, not a template.
- **Risk:** It can reinforce the "not creative" label if the answer is only reassurance. The answer should be concrete: six weeks, a wheel, and pieces you bring home.

## B. "Eight Chairs" (smallness and scarcity)
Eight seats and one class this fall is the whole offer.
- **Headline ideas:** "Eight seats. Six Tuesdays. One fall class." / "Not a mass-market skill class."
- **Format:** A countdown of seats left, posted as they fill.
- **Strength:** The scarcity is real, so it doesn't feel manufactured.
- **Risk:** It's about the format, not the person, so it's the least emotional of the three.

## C. "Made Tuesday, Held Next Tuesday" (the kiln)
The second kiln cuts turnaround from about three weeks to about one. Most pottery classes make you wait for the payoff, and you don't.
- **Headline ideas:** "Throw it Tuesday. Hold it in about a week." / "Pottery without the three-week wait."
- **Strength:** It's a concrete, new reason to choose this class over others.
- **Risk:** It speaks to people already curious about pottery, not to the hesitant ones.

## What I'd pick
**A as the hook, with two supports.**
- **Proof:** "31 of 46 students signed up again" (about two in three). It answers the doubt without arguing with it. Don't imply the "not creative" people were the ones who returned, because you don't know that.
- **Reasons to act now:** B's eight seats and C's one-week kiln turnaround, as the lines under the headline.

A sample ad:

> **"I'm not creative."**
> Most people who email us say some version of that. Then 31 of last spring's 46 students signed up again.
> Six Tuesdays, 6:30 to 9, starting Oct 21. Eight seats. $240, clay and firing for up to 4 pieces included. Pieces come back in about a week.
> Above the hardware store on Dunmore Street. Parking's behind the building.

## Two things to check
1. **Timing:** Registration opens "next week" and the class starts Oct 21, which is also next week. If registration opens that day, the campaign may need to run on a very short timeline, or the start date may need another look.
2. **Quoting emails:** Get permission before using anyone's actual words.
