**Partly.** Promoting annual with its real savings is fine. Pre-selecting it and shrinking the monthly price is the part that steers. A pre-selected paid plan can charge someone $89.99 up front when they only meant to look, and grey text makes the monthly option harder to see.

**Fair version:**

- **Show both plans at equal size and weight.** Monthly: $12.99/month. Annual: $89.99/year (about $7.50/month).
- **Label annual as the best value, with the math:** "Save $65.89 a year vs. monthly." That's a real saving, about 42%, so the label is honest and it's the main pull toward annual.
- **Don't pre-select a plan.** Let the person choose, or select annual only after they tap it. Keep the "Start" button disabled until a plan is chosen, or default to whichever plan they picked last time.
- **Before the card field, state the charge:** "You'll be charged $89.99 today, then renews yearly on [renewal date] unless you cancel." Put that at the same size as "free trial" if there is one.
- **Send a reminder before renewal** (e.g., 7 days out, with the amount and how to cancel).

**Notes:**

- Track refunds and cancellations within 30 days next to conversion. If annual converts better but refunds rise, the pre-select is buying revenue that churns.
- Check the renewal and cancellation rules for your markets with counsel; they vary by place and change over time.
