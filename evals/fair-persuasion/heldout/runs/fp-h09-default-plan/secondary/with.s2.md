**Partly.** Making annual the default can be fair, since a committed user would sensibly pick it. The grey monthly price is what makes it unfair. It hides the option most people will want to compare, and it's the part a growth test would be tuning, not the shopper's choice.

Fair version of the pricing block:

- **Monthly:** $12.99 per month
- **Annual:** $89.99 per year ($7.50 a month). Save $65.89 a year compared with monthly (about 42%).
- Annual is labelled "Recommended for a full year of practice." Keep that only if it's true for your users.
- Both options sit at the same size and weight. The toggle switches with one tap.
- Annual can start selected, but the plan you chose is stated before the card field: "You'll be charged $89.99 today and renew each year on [renewal date] unless you cancel." Send a reminder before the first renewal.

Notes:
- Rules on auto-renewal and default plan selection vary by place and change over time, so have counsel check the final flow.
- Track refund requests within 14 to 30 days of annual sign-up. If they cluster, the default is working against the user.
