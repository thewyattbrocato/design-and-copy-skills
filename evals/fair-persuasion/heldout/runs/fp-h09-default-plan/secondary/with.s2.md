**Partly. Pre-selecting annual isn't the problem. Hiding the monthly price is.** Annual is a sensible plan to recommend: $89.99 is about $7.50/month against $12.99, a real saving of $65.89 a year (about 42%). But a default that charges $89.99 up front while the alternative sits in small grey text steers people by making the other option hard to see. That's the part I'd change.

**What I'd do:** Keep annual as the recommended plan, but don't pre-select it. Make both plans the same size, and make the annual saving the reason to pick it. You'll get most of the lift without a default charge the user didn't choose.

**Fair version (placeholders in brackets):**

> **Monthly** — $12.99 / month
> Billed monthly. Cancel anytime.
>
> **Annual** — $89.99 / year *Recommended: save $65.89 a year (about 42%)*
> Billed $89.99 once a year, renewing each year until you cancel. That's $7.50 a month.
>
> [If trial: Free for [X] days, then $89.99 on [date] unless you cancel before then.]
> Cancel anytime in [location in app or account settings].

**If growth still wants annual pre-selected:** I'd accept it only if the monthly option is as legible as annual, the charge amount and renewal date sit right above the payment button at the same size, and there's a reminder before the first renewal. Without those, I'd push back.
