**Verdict: partly.** Pre-selecting annual is defensible. The steering is the grey monthly price, which makes the other option look like an afterthought. Keep the default if you want it, but show both prices at equal weight and state the charge plainly before the card.

**What I'd do:** make annual the labelled recommendation, show the real saving, and pre-select it only with a clear charge summary.

**Pricing block:**

> **Monthly** — $12.99 per month. Billed monthly. Cancel any time.
>
> **Annual** — $89.99 per year ($7.50/month). Saves $65.89 vs. monthly. *Recommended*
> Billed once a year. Cancel any time.

Both options use the same type size and weight. Annual has the "Recommended" label and the saving stated in dollars. Monthly isn't greyed out.

**Checkout summary, above the card field, same size as "free":**

> Today you'll be charged $89.99. It renews every year on [renewal date] unless you cancel. Cancel any time in Settings > Subscription.

If you pre-select annual, don't pre-select anything beyond the plan itself (no add-ons, no marketing opt-in).

**Notes:**
- Fill in `[renewal date]` and confirm "cancel any time" and any annual refund terms are true before you ship. If annual has a refund window, state it next to the price.
- Auto-renewal disclosure rules vary by place and change over time, so have legal check the summary wording, especially for US states, the UK and the EU.
