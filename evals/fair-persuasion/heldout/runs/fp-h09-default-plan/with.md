**Yes, do it, with a few changes.** Pre-selecting annual is a normal default. Burying the monthly price is where it could turn into a dark pattern.

**Why it's fine:** Annual is a plan many of your users would sensibly choose. A person who read the whole page calmly would still be okay with it. The toggle stays visible and costs one click to change.

**What I'd change:**

1. **Don't shrink and grey out the monthly price.** The small grey text is the part that steers by being easy to miss. Show both options at the same size and weight. Annual can carry a "Recommended" or "Best value" label, and monthly stays readable.
2. **Show the real math.** The annual plan is $89.99/yr, about $7.50/mo. Monthly for a year is $155.88. That saves about $65.89 (42%). Put the dollar amount next to the percentage.
3. **State the charge plainly.** Use something like "Billed $89.99 today, renews yearly on [date]. Cancel anytime in Settings." This sits next to the button, before the card field. Annual is the plan where surprise renewals cause the most anger, refunds, and chargebacks.
4. **Send a renewal reminder.** Email them before the annual renewal, for example 7 to 14 days ahead ([reminder timing] is your call). It's the right thing to do, and it prevents the angry "you charged me $90" tickets.
5. **Make cancelling easy.** It should work in the same place and take about as many steps as signing up did. Annual plans tempt teams to make leaving harder, which is the main way this tactic goes wrong.

**Layout I'd ship:**

- Two equal cards or a toggle, with Annual selected.
- Annual: **$89.99/year** · $7.50/month · Save $65.89 (42%) · "Recommended"
- Monthly: **$12.99/month** · Cancel anytime
- Under the button: the charge date, the amount, and the renewal terms.

**Notes:**

- Track refund and cancel-within-30-days rates for annual buyers. If they climb after the change, the default is pulling in people who didn't want it.
- Rules on auto-renewal disclosure vary by place and change over time, so have the renewal and cancellation wording checked. Apple and Google also have their own requirements if you bill through the app stores.
