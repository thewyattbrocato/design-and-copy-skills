## Cancel page copy

**Headline**
Before you go, can we ask one thing?

**Subhead**
Cancelling takes one more click. First, what's the main reason? It helps us fix what's not working.

**Reason options** (single select, each reveals a short response)

| Reason | Response shown | Button |
|---|---|---|
| **It's too expensive** | The annual plan is $79, which works out to about $6.58 a month instead of $9. That's $29 less per year. | Switch to annual |
| **I'm not using it enough** | Totally fair. The plan gets better the more receipts it sees. If scanning is the hassle, you can forward email receipts or upload a photo in a few seconds. [See how] | Keep my plan |
| **The meal plans aren't right for me** | Tell us what's off (diet, household size, foods you skip) and we'll adjust your next plan. Most fixes take under a minute. [Update my preferences] | Update preferences |
| **I'm cooking less right now** | Life gets busy. [Pause option goes here, see below.] | |
| **Something else** | (Optional text box: "What could we have done better?") | |

**Primary cancel button** (always visible, same visual weight as the keep button)
Continue to cancel

**Secondary button**
Keep my subscription

**Reassurance line under the buttons**
You'll keep access until [renewal date]. No further charges after that.

**Confirmation screen after cancelling**
You're all set. Your subscription ends on [date], and you won't be charged again. Your receipts and meal history will be saved for 90 days if you want to come back. Thanks for trying Pennywise Pantry.

## Pause block (use only if eng confirms it's built)

> **Need a break instead?** Pause your subscription for 1, 2, or 3 months. You won't be billed while paused, and your receipts and preferences stay saved. We'll email you 3 days before your plan resumes.
> **[Pause my plan]**

If you add it, show it to everyone, not only the "cooking less" group. Month 2–3 churn is often a temporary lapse in use, so pause is likely your strongest save.

## Things to check before launch

- **Pause:** ask eng whether it exists, and whether paused users keep their data and get the resume reminder.
- **Annual math:** I used your numbers ($9 × 12 = $108 vs. $79). If a user is mid-month, check how proration works before saying "switch now."
- **The 90-day data retention line:** it's a placeholder. Use your real retention policy or cut the sentence.
- **Discounts:** I left out a "50% off for 2 months" offer because I don't know your margins. It's a common save tactic if you want to test one.
- **Dark patterns:** the cancel button stays equal in weight, with no guilt copy, no hidden steps, and no "are you sure?" loops. This is partly ethical. It's also a legal risk, because the FTC's click-to-cancel rules and several state laws penalize cancel flows that are harder than sign-up.
- **Measure it:** log the reason selections. After a few weeks, they'll show whether the churn is mostly price, usage, or plan quality, and that should shape the next round of copy.
