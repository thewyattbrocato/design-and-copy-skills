## Notes

- **Check the pause with eng before shipping.** If it isn't built, delete that block. Don't show a pause that can't be used. Also confirm the billing behavior, resume date and whether access continues during the pause, then fill in the brackets.
- **I left out a discount on purpose.** A discount offered only to people who try to leave isn't a real price change. If you want one, make it a public price cut anyone can get. To check that this copy isn't just delaying cancellations, track how many paused users resume, and how many cancel within 30 days of resuming.

Rules on cancellation flows and auto-renewal disclosures vary by place and change over time, so have your legal contact check this before launch.
