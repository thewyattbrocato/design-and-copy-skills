Here's cancel-page copy in two versions. Version A uses the pause offer; Version B is for launch if eng says pause isn't built. Both give the user a clear path to cancel, which keeps the page honest and avoids chargeback and "hard to cancel" complaints.

## Version A: pause is built

**Headline:** Before you go, want to try something different?

**Body:** Cancelling is always an option. Many people find a break helps more than leaving. You can pause for up to 3 months. Your receipts, saved recipes, and plan history stay put, and you won't be charged while you're paused.

**Options (in order):**
1. **Pause for 1, 2, or 3 months** *(recommended)*
   Resume whenever you like, or we'll remind you before it ends.
2. **Switch to yearly: $79/year**
   That's $29 less than 12 months at $9/month.
3. **Keep my plan and tell us what's not working**
   Pick a reason below and we'll use it to improve the app.
4. **Continue to cancel**

**Reason prompt (optional, single select):**
- Too expensive right now
- I don't have time to plan meals
- The meal plans don't fit my family or diet
- I don't shop enough to need it
- Something else: ____

**Confirm screen after "Continue to cancel":**
**Your subscription will end on [date].** You'll keep full access until then. You can restart anytime from your account settings, and your receipt history will still be there if you come back.
*[Confirm cancellation]* *[Go back]*

## Version B: pause is not built

Use this if eng says no at launch. Don't mention pause in the UI, since it would promise something that doesn't exist.

**Headline:** Before you go, is there a plan that works better for you?

**Body:** We'd love to keep you. Here are a few options.

**Options:**
1. **Switch to yearly: $79/year**
   You save $29 compared with monthly, and you won't have to think about it for a year.
2. **Tell us what's not working**
   Pick a reason below and we'll use it to improve the app.
3. **Continue to cancel**

**Reason prompt:** same as Version A.

**Confirm screen:** same as Version A.

## Notes

- **Targeting month 2–3:** Common reasons for dropping off then are that the first receipt-to-plan flow felt like work or the plans didn't match what the household eats. A "not what I expected" reason option will show you which one it is. Consider a short in-app tip or a plan-tuning prompt sent in week 3, which may do more than the cancel page.
- **Yearly discount:** $79 is about 27% off $108. Showing "save $29" is accurate and is a stronger hook than a percentage.
- **Don't add friction:** Don't hide the cancel link, add a second confirm loop after they've opted to leave, or make the pause the only exit. Some states, including California, require cancellation to be as easy as signup, and those rules can change, so check with counsel before launch.
- **Pause details to settle with eng:** whether a paused account keeps its receipts and meal history, whether it can be resumed early, whether a paused user is still counted as churned in your metrics, and whether a payment-method update reminder is sent before resume.
