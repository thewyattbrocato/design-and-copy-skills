Here's a version you can ship. I built it so it works whether or not pause exists, because I wouldn't put a pause offer on the page until eng confirms it's built. Promising a pause the app can't deliver would cause more churn and support tickets than it saves.

## Cancel page copy

**Headline**
Before you go, can we help?

**Subhead**
Your plan stays active until [renewal date], so you won't lose anything today. Tell us what's not working and we'll see if we can fix it.

**Prompt**
What's the main reason you're leaving?

Each option below reveals its own response when selected:

| Reason | Response shown | Button |
|---|---|---|
| **Too expensive** | "Totally fair. The annual plan is $79/year, which works out to about $6.58/month instead of $9. That's $29 back in your pocket." | Switch to annual |
| **I'm not using it enough** | [If pause is built] "Life gets busy. Pause for 1, 2, or 3 months and pick back up when you're ready. Your receipts and preferences will be waiting." <br>[If not built] "Most people get the most out of Pennywise when they scan receipts right after shopping. Want a reminder after your next grocery run?" | Pause my plan / Send me a reminder |
| **Uploading receipts is a hassle** | "That's the part we most want to get right. Tell us what's getting in the way and we'll read every response." (opens a short text box) | Send feedback |
| **The meal plans didn't fit my household** | "Plans get better with more receipts, but we know that isn't much help if the first few weeks missed. Tell us about your household (diet, size, foods you skip) and we'll tune your next plan." | Update my preferences |
| **I found another tool** | "Thanks for telling us. What does it do better? We'd like to learn from it." (opens a text box) | Send feedback |
| **Something else** | Open text box | Continue |

**Primary actions**

- Secondary button (outlined): **Keep my subscription**
- Primary text link, equal visual weight: **Continue canceling**

Keep "Continue canceling" easy to see. If it's buried, people feel tricked, and that hurts reviews, chargebacks, and the chance they come back.

**Confirmation screen, after they confirm**
You're all set. Your subscription is canceled and you won't be charged again.

You can keep using Pennywise Pantry until [date]. If you change your mind, you can restart anytime and your receipt history will still be here.

[Restart my plan]

## Notes

- **Check with eng:** whether pause exists, whether it truly allows up to 3 months, and what happens to billing and data during a pause. If it isn't built, use the fallback lines in the table. If it is, consider showing the pause offer to everyone as a first step rather than only under "not using it enough."
- **Month 2–3 churn:** that timing suggests people haven't built a habit by then. This page can save some of them, but a nudge in weeks 3–6 (a "you haven't scanned a receipt in a while" email) will likely do more than anything on the cancel page.
- **The annual offer:** I used the existing annual price, not a new discount. If you want a stronger incentive, like a month free or a lower first-year price, that's a business decision I'd want you to make deliberately.
- **Test it:** measure save rate by reason, and also 30- and 60-day retention of the people you save. A save that cancels again a month later isn't a win.

I can also write the follow-up emails: the win-back email after cancellation, and the week 3–6 usage nudge.
