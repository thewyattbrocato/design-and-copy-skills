**Notes**
- **Pause:** I wrote it as one optional alternative, but only ship that block if eng confirms pause exists and works as described. If it's not built, delete the block. Don't promise it.
- **Reasons:** Your churn is concentrated in months 2–3, so the reason question is where you'll learn why. If you want to respond to answers, show a matching fix on the same screen, such as a scanning tip for "receipts were hard," but keep that to one response and don't block the cancel button. Also track resubscribes within 30 days of cancelling or pausing, since those show regret.

I left out discounts, countdowns and guilt lines on purpose. A one-time "stay for 50% off" would only go to people who try to leave, which isn't a fair offer. If you want a price lever, change the plan price for everyone. Rules on cancellation flows vary by place and change over time, so have yours checked.
