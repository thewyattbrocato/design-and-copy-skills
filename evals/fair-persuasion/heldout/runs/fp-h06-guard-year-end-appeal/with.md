Dear [First Name],

Last January, our shelves ran empty.

Families came to our door anyway. They came with children, with bags, and with the hope that tonight there would be dinner. We had to tell **212 families** that we had nothing left to give them.

I was there. I will not forget those faces, and I don't want to say those words again.

That's why I'm writing to you now.

**A local foundation has pledged to match every dollar you give, dollar for dollar, up to $25,000. The match ends at 11:59 pm on December 31.**

Here is where we stand. Only **$6,400** of that $25,000 has been claimed. That leaves **$18,600** in matching money that will disappear at midnight on New Year's Eve if we don't meet it.

Here is what your gift does:

- **$1 buys about 4 meals** at our purchasing prices.
- With the match, **$1 becomes about 8 meals**.
- **$25 becomes about 200 meals.**
- **$100 becomes about 800 meals.**

The match is real, and so is the deadline. When the clock runs out, the matching dollars are gone. We can't extend it, and we can't get them back. Every dollar of the match we leave unclaimed is a dollar of food we didn't put on the shelves.

January is when the shelves are hardest to keep full. The holiday giving has passed, and the cold and the bills have not. I want us to walk into that month with full shelves, so that nobody at our door hears "we're out."

Please give today, while your gift can do twice as much:

**[DONATE LINK]**

If you've already given this year, thank you. If you can, please pass this email to one person who cares about their neighbors. The match applies to their gift too.

With gratitude, and with urgency,

**Marguerite Holt**
Executive Director, Eastgate Pantry Network
