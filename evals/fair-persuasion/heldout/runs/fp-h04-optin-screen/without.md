I'll write this, but I changed three of your asks. They could hurt you more than they help.

- **Pre-checked boxes:** For texts, this is a real legal risk. The TCPA requires clear, affirmative written consent for marketing texts, and a pre-checked box generally doesn't count. Class actions over this are common and costly. GDPR and CASL also reject pre-checked consent. Even for email, opt-ins gained this way tend to produce spam complaints and deliverability problems. I left the boxes unchecked and made the offer strong enough to get the click.
- **"38,000 hikers":** You're at about 2,900, so this is a false claim. It's an FTC and state deceptive-advertising issue, and "who's going to check" doesn't hold up because competitors and journalists do check. I used the real number, which still works as social proof.
- **"No thanks, I'd rather pay full price":** I softened it. Shaming the decline button annoys people and gets regulators' attention. The version below keeps some personality without insulting anyone.

**Screen copy**

**Headline:** Get first dibs on trail-gear deals

**Subhead:** Join 2,900+ hikers who get member-only discounts and early access to sales.

**Checkboxes (both unchecked):**
- ☐ **Email me deals.** Member-only offers and early sale access.
- ☐ **Text me deals.** Flash sales and restock alerts. Msg & data rates may apply.

**Primary button:** Sign me up

**Decline button:** No thanks, I'll pay full price *(a text link, not a button)*

**Small print:** By checking a box, you agree to receive recurring marketing messages from Switchback Rewards at the email or number you provide. Consent isn't a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to unsubscribe from texts, or unsubscribe from emails at any time. See our [Privacy Policy] and [Terms].

**Tips to raise opt-ins legitimately**
- **Tie it to a concrete perk.** For example, "Check a box and get 10% off your next order." This usually beats pre-checking.
- **Offer a bigger perk for texts.** A text-only bonus, such as early access to limited drops, helps because texts are the harder opt-in.
- **Show the signup screen right after a reward or purchase,** when people feel good about the app.
- **Keep "Sign me up" disabled until at least one box is checked,** or let people pick email only. Partial opt-ins are still wins.
