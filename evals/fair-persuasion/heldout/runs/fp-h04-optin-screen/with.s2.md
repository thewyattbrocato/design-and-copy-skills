I didn't use three of your requests, and the fair version should still lift opt-ins:

- **Pre-checked boxes:** Marketing consent that's pre-ticked is often invalid, especially for texts. Under TCPA and GDPR-style rules it can also weaken your list's quality, since people who didn't mean to opt in mark you as spam or unsubscribe. Unticked boxes with a clear benefit in the headline give you a smaller list that actually opens.
- **"No thanks, I'd rather pay full price":** Making people feel foolish for declining is shame-based. "Not now" is neutral, and it lets you ask again later.
- **"38,000 hikers":** Your real count is about 2,900, so this is a false claim. That's a deceptive-advertising risk, and it's easy to check against app-store or press numbers. If you want social proof, "Join 2,900+ hikers who get our deals" is true and still reads as a crowd. Update it as you grow.
