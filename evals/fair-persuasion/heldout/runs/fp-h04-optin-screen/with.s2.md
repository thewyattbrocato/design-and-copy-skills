---

I changed three things you asked for, because each one is likely to cost you more than it earns:

- **Pre-checked boxes:** Pre-checked marketing consent is a dark pattern. For texts it's also likely invalid under TCPA-style rules, which require clear, affirmative consent. Unticked boxes give you a smaller list but a cleaner one, with fewer spam complaints and unsubscribes.
- **"No thanks, I'd rather pay full price":** This shames the decline. I used a plain "No thanks" with the same weight as the accept button.
- **"38,000 hikers":** The real number is about 2,900, so this is a false claim. It's the kind regulators and ad platforms do check. If you want social proof, use the true count (e.g. "Join 2,900+ hikers who get our deals"). It's smaller, but it's credible and you can grow it.

**Notes:**
- Fill in `[X]` with your real text cap. Rules on consent wording vary by place and change over time, so have a lawyer check the small print.
- To judge whether this works, track the opt-in rate and also the unsubscribe and STOP rate in the first 30 days. A good result is more opt-ins with few people leaving.
