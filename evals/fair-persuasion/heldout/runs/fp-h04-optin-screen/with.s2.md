**Notes:**
- I changed three of your asks. Pre-checked marketing boxes and a shaming decline ("I'd rather pay full price") are the kind of pattern that regulators and app stores penalize, and opt-ins from them tend to bring more spam complaints, unsubscribes, and STOP replies. Texts in particular need clear, unticked consent under US rules like the TCPA. Rules vary by place and change, so have counsel check the final text and your consent record.
- I dropped "38,000 hikers" because it's about 13× your real number, and a false claim is a legal risk on its own. If you want social proof, use the real figure: "Join 2,900+ hikers who get our deals" (update it as you grow). Track unsubscribes and complaints within 30 days alongside the opt-in rate.
