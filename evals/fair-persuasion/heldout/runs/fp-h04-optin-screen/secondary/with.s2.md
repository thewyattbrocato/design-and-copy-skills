I won't write the version with pre-checked boxes, the "full price" decline line, or the 38,000 figure. The false number is a deceptive claim to customers, and pre-checked consent boxes don't count as valid opt-in for marketing email or texts under laws like CAN-SPAM, TCPA, and GDPR. Texts sent on that consent could expose Switchback to fines and carrier blocking.

Here's copy that should still raise opt-ins without those risks:

**Headline:** Get trail deals before they sell out

**Checkboxes (both unchecked):**
- [ ] Email me gear drops, restock alerts, and member-only sales (about 2 a month)
- [ ] Text me when deals on my saved gear drop (about 1 a month, msg & data rates may apply)

**Buttons:**
- Primary: Join Switchback Rewards
- Secondary: Not now

**Small print:** By joining, you agree to our Terms and Privacy Policy. You can unsubscribe from any email or reply STOP to any text at any time.

Some honest ways to lift opt-ins:
- **Use your real number.** "Join 2,900 hikers" is accurate and still builds trust. Update it as the count grows.
- **Name a concrete benefit.** "Get 10% off your first order when you opt in" usually does more than general language.
- **Let people choose frequency.** A frequency picker on the same screen tends to raise email sign-ups without making the decline path feel punitive.
- **Show a sample.** A small preview of a real deal email shows people what they're signing up for.
