I won't write the version with pre-checked boxes, the "38,000" number, or the decline button that guilt-trips people. Those would mislead your customers and expose the company to legal risk:

- **The count is false.** At 2,900 members, "38,000 hikers" is a fabricated claim. That's deceptive advertising under FTC Act Section 5 and state consumer-protection laws, and it's easy to check.
- **Pre-checked boxes don't create consent for texts.** The TCPA requires prior express written consent for marketing texts, and a box the user didn't tick doesn't meet that. Pre-checked email opt-ins also violate GDPR and CASL, and they draw spam complaints that hurt deliverability.

Here's copy that should still lift opt-ins, based on real numbers and a clear choice:

**Headline:** Want deals on the gear you actually use?

**Subhead:** Join 2,900+ hikers getting member-only sales and restock alerts.

**Checkbox 1 (unchecked):** Email me member deals and new gear drops. About 2 emails a week.

**Checkbox 2 (unchecked):** Text me restock and sale alerts. Up to 4 texts a month. Msg & data rates may apply.

**Primary button:** Join Switchback Rewards

**Secondary button:** Not now

**Small print:** Texting and email are optional and not required to join. Reply STOP to end texts or HELP for help. Unsubscribe anytime from any email. See our Privacy Policy.

Some things that tend to raise opt-in rates without the risky parts:

- **Say what they get, and how often.** The frequency line reduces the fear of being spammed, which is often why people decline.
- **Put the value in the headline.** Specific benefits ("restock alerts on your size") beat generic "stay in the loop" language.
- **Show the real number and let it grow.** A true count like 2,900 is still social proof, and you can update it as you pass milestones.
- **Make the decline neutral.** "Not now" keeps people in the flow. Guilt-based text tends to produce resentment and unsubscribes later.
- **Test the flow.** A/B test the headline and the checkbox wording against real opt-in rates.
