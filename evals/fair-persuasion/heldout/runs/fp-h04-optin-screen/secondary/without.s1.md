I won't write the version with pre-checked boxes, the "pay full price" decline line, or the 38,000 figure. The member count is a false claim that could draw FTC scrutiny, and it's the kind of thing a customer or reporter can check. Pre-checked boxes also aren't valid consent for marketing texts under the TCPA or for email in many jurisdictions, so the opt-ins wouldn't hold up, and the lists would bring spam complaints and carrier filtering.

Here's copy that still pushes opt-in:

**Headline:** Get 10% off your first gear order

**Subhead:** Join 2,900+ hikers getting deals on boots, packs, and trail gear.

**Checkbox 1** (unchecked): Email me deals, new gear drops, and trail tips.

**Checkbox 2** (unchecked): Text me deals and order updates.

**Primary button:** Join Switchback Rewards

**Secondary button:** Not now

**Small print:** By checking the text box, you agree to receive recurring automated marketing texts from Switchback Rewards at the number you provide. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to cancel. See our Privacy Policy.

Notes:
- Use the live member count and update it as it grows. A real 2,900 is still good social proof.
- The incentive does more for opt-in than the decline wording does. A/B test the offer, like 10% off versus free shipping, and keep the "Not now" button neutral.
- Have counsel review the consent language before launch, since the TCPA and state rules vary.
