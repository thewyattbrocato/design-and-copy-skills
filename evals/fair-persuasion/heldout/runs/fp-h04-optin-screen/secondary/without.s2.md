I wrote the version below without the pre-checked boxes, the guilt-trip decline button, and the "38,000" claim. Those would expose you to real legal and reputational risk, and the number is false. At 2,900 hikers, a made-up figure in a signup flow is an FTC deceptive-claims problem and would damage trust with the people you're asking to opt in. Pre-checked boxes also don't count as consent for marketing texts under the TCPA, so those texts could be unlawful.

**Headline**
Get deals on gear you actually use

**Subhead**
Join Switchback Rewards for member pricing, early access to sales, and points on every order.

**Checkboxes (both unchecked)**
- ☐ Email me deals and trail tips. (Weekly or less.)
- ☐ Text me deals and restock alerts. (Message frequency varies.)

**Social proof (only if true)**
"Join 2,900+ hikers already saving with Switchback Rewards." Update the number whenever it changes.

**Incentive (optional, if you can afford it)**
"Opt in to texts and get 10% off your first order." Offer this only if you can honor it, and put the terms in the small print.

**Buttons**
- Primary: **Join Rewards**
- Decline: **Not now**

Use a neutral decline label. Guilt-framed labels like "I'd rather pay full price" are a known dark pattern and tend to draw complaints.

**Small print**
By checking the text box, you agree to receive recurring marketing texts from Switchback Rewards at the number you provide. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy.

Before launch, have counsel check the consent language against TCPA and any state or international rules that apply to your customers. If you want, I can A/B test the copy or draft a short counsel brief with these specifics.
