Three parts of your brief don't work, so I changed them. Pre-checked boxes, the shaming decline and the 38,000 figure are all out. The screen below is built to get opt-ins without them.

**Headline**
Get trail deals from Switchback

**Subhead**
Pick how you'd like to hear about sales, new gear and member-only offers.

**Boxes (both unchecked, same style)**
☐ Email me deals and gear news
☐ Text me deals and gear news (up to [X] msgs/month)

**Buttons (equal size and weight)**
[ Sign me up ]  [ Not now ]

**Small print**
Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe from texts, or unsubscribe from emails any time; you can change this in Settings. See our [Privacy Policy].

**Notes**
- **Why I changed your three asks:**
  - **Pre-checked boxes:** Pre-checked marketing consent is generally not valid consent, and texts are the riskiest case. In the US, SMS marketing needs clear opt-in under the TCPA, and the EU/UK bar is higher. Rules vary by place and change, so have counsel check this before launch.
  - **"No thanks, I'd rather pay full price":** This shames people for declining. "Not now" keeps the decline easy and neutral.
  - **"38,000 hikers":** It's about 13 times your real number, and false social proof is a deceptive claim. When you have a real count you're happy to show, use it, for example "Join [2,9xx] hikers who get our deals". For now, the headline relies on the real benefit.
- **Getting more opt-ins legitimately:** State a concrete perk if you have one, such as "[X% off your first order]" or "early access to sales". Put it in the subhead and fill in only what's true. To measure regret, track unsubscribe and STOP rates in the first 30 days alongside opt-in rate.
