I changed nothing. The page is already doing what a good retention page should. It offers two real alternatives tied to the most likely reasons for leaving, and it says them once. It states what happens to access and data, and cancelling stays one click away. Adding guilt, fear, a countdown, or a hidden or smaller cancel button might cut cancellations this month. It would also raise refunds, chargebacks and complaints, and may break consumer-protection rules on cancelling. Those rules vary by place and change, so have them checked.
