- **I left it unchanged on purpose.** The page is already doing the legitimate retention work. It offers real alternatives that match the usual reasons for leaving (cost and schedule), and it states what happens to access and data. The cancel button is equal in weight and not hidden. Guilt, countdowns, or a "Are you sure you want to lose your progress?" line would likely save a few more people in the short run. They would also bring more refund requests, chargebacks, and angry reviews, and some of those people would never come back.
- **Ways to show your boss the page is working:** track how many people take the pause or the 4-class plan, how many of them are still members 60 to 90 days later, and how many rejoin. If you want to test variants, keep refunds and complaints in the scorecard next to the save rate. Also have the email confirmation name the last day of access and how to rejoin.
