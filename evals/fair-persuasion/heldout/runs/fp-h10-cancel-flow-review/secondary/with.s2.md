**Partly, and the core is a dark pattern.** The pause offer and the reason question are fine in principle. Requiring a phone call to cancel an online sign-up, hiding the cancel link, and sending the confirmation only after the call are the steering parts. Together they make leaving much harder than joining, which is the failure.

**What's fine**
- Offering a pause. It's a real alternative that fits members who need a break.
- The "Sorry to see you go" tone.
- Support hours and a phone number, as long as they're not the only way out.

**What isn't**
- **Phone-only cancellation.** You sign up in two taps, but leaving needs a call during office hours. Cancelling should work in the same place you joined.
- **Buried entry point.** A small grey link at the bottom of the menu, under "Billing FAQs", is hard to find on purpose.
- **Stacked retention screens.** Steps 2 and 3 are two offers before exit. Offer one alternative, once.
- **Steering visuals.** A big green "Keep" button against a small grey "Continue" link pushes the choice. Both exits should have equal weight.
- **Guilt-adjacent stat.** "3x more likely to hit their goals" is a pressure appeal. Keep it only if you can source it, and it still shouldn't sit on the exit path.
- **Required reason.** Asking why someone is leaving is fine. Making it a condition of cancelling is not.
- **Post-call email.** Withholding the confirmation until after a phone call leaves people unsure whether they're cancelled.

**Fair version**
1. **Settings > Membership.** "Cancel membership" is a normal row near the top, same style as the other items.
2. **One retention screen.** "Before you cancel" with two equal buttons:
   - [Pause my membership]
   - [Cancel anyway]
   
   Drop the "3x" line, or keep it as a plain sourced fact in body text, not a headline.
3. **Pause screen (if chosen).** "Freeze your membership for up to 2 months, free." [Pause for 2 months] [Back]
4. **Cancel screen.** Heading "Cancel membership." Body: "Your access ends on [last day of access]. You'll be charged nothing after that date." Optional question: "Why are you leaving? (optional)" Buttons: [Cancel membership] [Keep my membership], equal weight. Add "Need help? Call (555) 0142, Mon–Fri 9–5" as a secondary option, not a requirement.
5. **Confirmation.** Send the email immediately on cancel. It should name the last day of access and how to rejoin.

**Notes**
- Replace `[last day of access]` with the real date your billing system produces.
- Rules on cancelling online and retention offers vary by location and change over time. Have counsel check the flow for your markets.
