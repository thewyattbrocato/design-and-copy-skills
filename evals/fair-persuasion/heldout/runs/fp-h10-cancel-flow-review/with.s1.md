**Yes, it's a dark pattern.** Steps 1, 4 and 5 are the serious problems. Steps 2 and 3 contain legitimate ideas that are done in a way that steers.

## What's fine
- **A save offer.** Offering a pause is a real alternative for people who are leaving over schedule or cost. Free, up to 2 months is a fair offer, and [Pause] / [No thanks] have equal weight.
- **Asking why they're leaving**, as long as it's optional.
- **Some confirmation before cancelling.** That's fine if it's one clear step.

## What isn't
1. **Buried entry (step 1).** "Cancel membership" is a small grey link at the bottom, under "Billing FAQs". Sign-up takes two taps, so cancelling should be about as easy to find.
2. **Lopsided buttons (step 2).** A big green [Keep] sits beside a tiny grey [Continue]. The decline is deliberately hard to see.
3. **Unsupported stat (step 2).** "3x more likely to hit their goals" is a persuasion line aimed at someone who has already decided. If it's not a real, sourced number, it's a separate honesty problem. Either way, it's a second pitch before the pause offer.
4. **Stacked retention screens (steps 2 and 3).** There are two save attempts. The fair limit is one alternative, shown once.
5. **Required dropdown (step 4).** Making the reason a condition of leaving is a barrier.
6. **Phone-only finish, business hours only (steps 4 and 5).** This is the worst part. Someone who joined online in two taps has to call during Mon–Fri 9–5 to leave. There's no confirmation until they do, so they can't tell whether they've cancelled or will keep being billed. Many places regulate exactly this ("click to cancel" style rules), so have it checked legally. Rules vary by place and change over time.

## What to change
1. **Move "Cancel membership"** to a normal, readable item in Settings > Membership, visible without scrolling past the FAQs.
2. **Make cancelling one flow, fully in-app**, with no call.
3. **Show one retention screen**, not two. Merge the offer into the pause screen:
   - Heading: "Want to pause instead?"
   - Body: "Freeze your membership for up to 2 months, free."
   - Buttons: [Pause] and [Continue cancelling], same size and weight.
   - Drop the 3x stat, or keep it only if it's true and sourced, and don't pair it with a dominant button.
4. **Make the reason optional**: "Why are you leaving? (optional)" with a skip, and don't block [Cancel membership] on it.
5. **Finish with a clear confirm screen**: a plain [Cancel membership] button, what happens to their account and billing, and `[last day of access]`.
6. **Send the confirmation email immediately**, naming the last access date and how to rejoin.
7. **Keep phone support as an option** for people who prefer it, never as the only route.

The result is: Settings > Membership > Cancel, then one pause offer with an equal-weight decline, then an optional reason with the confirm button, then an immediate email. That's about the same effort as joining and still gives you a real chance to retain people.
