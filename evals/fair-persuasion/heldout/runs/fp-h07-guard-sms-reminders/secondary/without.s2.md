2. `{first_name}, your Birchwood Dental visit is {date} at {time}. Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule. Changes need 24 hrs notice or a $45 fee applies.` (150)

3. `Birchwood Dental: see you {date} at {time}, {first_name}! Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule. Under 24 hrs notice = $45 fee. Call (555) 0100.` (150)

4. `Hi {first_name}, Birchwood Dental here. Appt {date} at {time}. Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule. Need to change? 24 hrs notice avoids a $45 fee.` (149)

Real values replace the placeholders, so the final length depends on them. Each text has about 8–10 characters of room. Short formats like "Thu Oct 9" and "10:30 AM" fit. Full day names or a long name could push a text over, so check the tool's character counter with a test send before going live.
