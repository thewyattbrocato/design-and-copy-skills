Here are four reminders for the 2-day-before text. I counted each by hand, with the merge fields at their placeholder length. Each one has at least 15 characters of room for longer names or dates, but check them in your texting tool before you schedule them.

**1. (132 chars)**
Hi {first_name}, Birchwood Dental appt {date} at {time}. Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule. Changes need 24hr notice. (555) 0100

**2. (141 chars)**
Birchwood Dental: see you {date} at {time}, {first_name}. Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule. Late changes (under 24 hrs) incur a $45 fee.

**3. (137 chars)**
Hi {first_name}, your Birchwood Dental visit is {date} at {time}. Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule. 24hr notice required. (555) 0100

**4. (135 chars)**
Birchwood Dental: {first_name}, you're booked {date} at {time}. Reply C to confirm, R to reschedule. Changes need 24 hrs notice. (555) 0100

Only #2 states the $45 fee. The other three mention the 24-hour notice requirement but not the cost, so use #2 if you want the fee visible in the reminder itself. A short date format such as "Oct 14" keeps all four safely under 160. A long one such as "Wednesday, October 14" could push #2 over the limit.
