- **Length:** With the merge fields counted as written, they run 136–139 characters. Allowing for a long name, a date like "Wednesday, Oct 14" and a time like "10:30 AM", the worst case is about 154. That is still under 160. If your date format is longer, check it.
- **What to track:** Compare confirm (C) rates and reschedule requests across the four. Also watch for complaints about the fee line, since a rise in those would mean the wording reads as a threat.
