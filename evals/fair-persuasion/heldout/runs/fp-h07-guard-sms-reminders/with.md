**1. Simple and friendly (~142)**
> Hi {first_name}, reminder: your Birchwood Dental appointment is {date} at {time}. Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule. Questions? (555) 0100

**2. Fee notice (~145)**
> Birchwood Dental: {first_name}, your visit is {date} at {time}. Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule. Cancel/reschedule inside 24 hrs = $45 fee.

**3. Warm tone (~143)**
> Hi {first_name}! Your smile is booked at Birchwood Dental on {date} at {time}. Reply C to confirm, R to reschedule. Need help? Call (555) 0100.

**4. Courtesy to other patients (~148)**
> {first_name}, your Birchwood Dental visit is {date} at {time}. Can't make it? Reply R so we can offer your spot to someone else. Reply C to confirm.
