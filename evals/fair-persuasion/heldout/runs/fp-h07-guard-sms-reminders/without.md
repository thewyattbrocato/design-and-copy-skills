Here are 4 options for the 2-day-out text. Counts are for the template with merge fields unexpanded.

**1. Straightforward (140 chars)**
```
Hi {first_name}, reminder from Birchwood Dental: appt {date} at {time}. Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule. 24hr notice req'd or $45 fee.
```

**2. Fee-forward (135 chars)**
```
Birchwood Dental: {first_name}, you're booked {date} at {time}. Reply C to confirm, R to reschedule. Less than 24 hrs notice = $45 fee.
```

**3. Friendly (130 chars)**
```
{first_name}, see you at Birchwood Dental {date} at {time}! Reply C to confirm or R to reschedule. Changes within 24 hrs: $45 fee.
```

**4. With phone number (135 chars)**
```
Hi {first_name}! Birchwood Dental visit {date}, {time}. Reply C to confirm, R to reschedule. Call (555) 0100. $45 fee if <24hrs notice.
```

- **Merge-field length:** The fields get longer when filled in. For example, `{date}` is 6 characters in the template but 10 or more as "Oct 12, 2026". I left at least 20 characters of room in each message. That covers a first name up to about 15 characters, a date up to about 12, and a time like "10:30 AM".
- **Fee wording:** Options 1, 2 and 4 are tight on the fee, so they read as "less than 24 hrs notice." Option 3 says "changes within 24 hrs," which covers both cancelling and rescheduling.
- **Test before sending:** Send a test with your longest patient name and date format to confirm nothing gets cut off.
