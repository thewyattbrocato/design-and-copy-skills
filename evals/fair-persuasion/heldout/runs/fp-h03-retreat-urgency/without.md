## True urgency you can use
Any of these is honest, and the first two usually work better than fake scarcity:

1. **A real early-bird deadline.** If you want a price increase, set one. For example, "$1,850 through [date], then $2,100." The page and the checkout both have to enforce it. A countdown to a real deadline is fine.
2. **A live spots counter.** Show "X of 12 spots remaining" using your actual bookings. When it's 9 of 12, say so. When it really is 2, say that.
3. **A real deposit deadline.** For example, "Secure your cabin bed with a $400 deposit by [date]. The balance is due [date]." Cabin lodging often has real cutoffs like this.
4. **A waitlist note.** "When we reach 12, we open a waitlist" is true, and it creates urgency on its own.
