I won't write the fake countdown, the "only 2 spots left" line, or the Saturday price jump to $2,300. They're false claims that push people into paying, and that's deceptive, even if it's common. It's also a practical risk. Writers talk to each other, and a "2 spots left" banner that's still up three weeks later will cost you more trust than it gains.

Below is page copy that creates urgency from things that are true. It works if you make one of the levers real: an actual early-bird deadline, or the real count of spots left. Both are free to set up.

---

## Wren Hollow Writing Retreat
### Six nights in the Adirondacks. Twelve writers. One April week with nothing to do but write.

**April [dates] · Wren Hollow Cabins, Adirondacks, NY**
**$1,850 per person · all meals included · shared cabins**

---

### The week
Six nights in the mountains, a long table, and a small group of writers who are there to get words on the page. There are no panels and no networking mixers. You get quiet mornings, shared dinners, and time to finish the thing you keep meaning to finish.

### What's included
- Six nights in a shared cabin at Wren Hollow
- All meals, from breakfast through dinner
- [Workshops / one-on-one sessions / evening readings, whichever are real]
- Protected writing time every day

### Who it's for
Writers at any stage who have a project and want a week to move it forward. Fiction, nonfiction, memoir, poetry, or something that doesn't fit a label.

### Why twelve
We cap it at twelve so the cabins stay comfortable and everyone gets real attention. When it's full, it's full.

---

### Reserve your place
**[Reserve my spot, $1,850]**

*Spots are limited to 12 and are confirmed in the order deposits arrive.*

---

### Make the urgency real
Pick one or two of these:

1. **A real early-bird deadline.** For example: "Register by [date] for $1,850. After that, the price is $2,000." Only use it if you'll actually charge the higher price. You can set the date to land this week.
2. **A true spots counter.** Show "X of 12 spots remaining" and update it as people book. It's honest, and it gets more convincing as it drops.
3. **A real registration close.** "Registration closes [date] so we can finalize cabin assignments and meal planning." This is true for most retreats anyway.
4. **A bonus for early registrants.** A free 20-minute manuscript consult, a signed book, or a welcome care package for the first 6 to register.
5. **A short waitlist note.** "Full? Join the waitlist. Cancellations do happen." This works well once you sell out.
