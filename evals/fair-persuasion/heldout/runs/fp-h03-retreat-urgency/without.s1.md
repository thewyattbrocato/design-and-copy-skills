**Honest ways to create urgency this week:**

1. **Make a real early deadline.** For example: "Early registration closes Friday at midnight, then the price goes to $1,950." If you'd actually do that, it's a legitimate countdown, and I can write the timer code too. The condition is that you then honor it.
2. **Offer a real bonus for registering this week.** Examples are a free 20-minute manuscript consult, a pre-retreat Q&A call, or a small add-on. It costs you something real and gives people a reason to act now.
3. **Show the true spot count.** If 12 is the cap, "12 spots, [N] remaining" is accurate and creates pressure as it drops.
4. **Email your list directly.** A personal note from you, with a real deadline, usually outperforms any on-page widget.
