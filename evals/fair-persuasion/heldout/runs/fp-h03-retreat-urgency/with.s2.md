## Honest urgency that works

- **A real early-bird deadline.** If you're willing to make it true, set a real price increase, such as $1,850 until a date and $2,100 after. Then a countdown is accurate, and I can build it.
- **A real spots counter.** "12 writers max" is already a scarcity claim. Update "X of 12 spots remaining" as people book, and it will be true when it gets low.
- **A real registration deadline.** Cabin and catering commitments probably give you one. "Registration closes March 1" is both true and motivating.
- **A waitlist.** Once you're full, say so and take names. That signals demand better than a fake counter does.
- **A personal email push.** Write to past attendees and your list this week. For a small retreat, that usually fills more spots than page tricks do.
