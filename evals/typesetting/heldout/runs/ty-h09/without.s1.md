Here's the cleaned-up version:

> Thanks for stopping by Harlow & Finch—we’ve been making oak shelves since 1998, all by hand. Our “Pocket” shelf is 6′ × 2″, ships in 3–5 days, and costs $48–$60. It’s the one our customers call ‘the keeper’. Don’t take our word for it—ask a neighbor.

What I changed:
- **Dashes:** `--` became an em dash (—) with no surrounding spaces.
- **Ranges:** `3-5` and `$48-$60` now use en dashes (–).
- **Quotes and apostrophes:** straight marks became curly ones (“Pocket”, ‘the keeper’, we’ve, It’s, Don’t).
- **Dimensions:** `6' x 2"` became `6′ × 2″`, with true prime marks for feet and inches and a multiplication sign instead of the letter x.

The wording is unchanged. If you prefer spaces around the em dashes (` — `), that's an easy swap.
