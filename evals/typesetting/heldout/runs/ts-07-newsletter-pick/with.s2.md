**Option B.**

- **Size:** 17px is comfortable on both laptops and phones. A is 14px, which is too small for 600–900 word stories.
- **Line length:** 36rem is about 576px, or roughly 60–70 characters in Georgia. That's the range the eye follows easily. A has no cap, so lines run across the whole laptop screen. C's 30rem is narrow, and at 22px it comes to only about 35 characters per line.
- **Line height:** 1.55 suits serif body text. C's 1.95 is so loose that the lines stop reading as one block. A's 1.3 is cramped.

One optional tweak: switch `17px` to `1.0625rem` so the text follows people's browser font-size settings.
