**What I changed and why**
- **Headline:** "World's #1" and "revolutionize" are claims everyone makes, so visitors tune them out. The new headline says what the product feels like to use.
- **Removed "AI," "thousands of happy teams," and "never double-book again."** I don't know if these are true. Unverifiable claims hurt trust, and "never" is easy to disprove. If you do have a real customer count or a double-booking guarantee, add them back with the actual number or wording.
- **Removed "Act NOW before this deal disappears."** There's no deal, and fake urgency undercuts a no-credit-card trial, which is already a low-pressure offer.
- **Added who each plan is for.** A price alone doesn't help someone choose between Solo and Team.
- **Added a button.** The live copy has no clear next step.

**Things to check**
