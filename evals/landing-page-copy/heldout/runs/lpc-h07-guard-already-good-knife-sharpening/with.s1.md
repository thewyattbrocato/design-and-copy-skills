Here is what I changed and why:

- **Timing:** "Back at your door in about a week" doesn't match the steps. Box delivery, transit to you, 5 business days of sharpening and return shipping add up to well over a week. A customer who counts will feel misled. I cut the claim from the intro. If you want a total, say "about two weeks door to door", but only if that's true.
- **Pricing:** The serrated price was hidden under "What we can't do", but you do sharpen serrated knives. I moved all prices into one "What it costs" section. The six-knife total is just your numbers added up.
- **Box cost:** The copy never says whether the box is free. That's the first thing people will wonder before clicking the button, so I left a placeholder. Fill it in.
- **Grammar:** "We sharpen each one... and it's back" mixed singular and plural, so I fixed it.
- **Repeated button:** The page now has the button at the end as well, so people don't have to scroll back up.

Before it goes to the designer, I'd also add a line on what happens if a knife is damaged or lost in transit, and whether you take anything besides kitchen knives, such as scissors or pocket knives. Those are the other questions a cautious buyer will ask. I didn't invent answers, so those lines are yours to write.
