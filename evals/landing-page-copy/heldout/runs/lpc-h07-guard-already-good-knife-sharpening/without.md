Your draft is already good. It's specific, it sounds like a person, and the "tell you honestly" line works. These are the problems I'd fix before it goes to the designer.

**Things to fix**

1. **The timing claim is ambiguous.** The headline block says "back at your door in about a week." The steps say 5 business days from when you receive the knives. Add the 2-3 days for the box to arrive and the transit each way, and the customer's real wait is closer to two weeks. "About a week" will read as a broken promise. Say "about a week after they reach us" and let the steps carry the detail.
2. **The serrated price is under the wrong heading.** Serrated knives are something you do, at a different price. Under "What we can't do" it's easy to miss, and it looks like a surprise fee. Give pricing its own short block.
3. **It doesn't say whether the box is free.** "Get a shipping box" is your main button. People will wonder if it costs money. If it's free, say so. If it isn't, the price needs to be in the pricing block.
4. **"Any carrier location" doesn't match a prepaid label.** The label is for one carrier. Name it, for example "any UPS location."
5. **The quote placeholder and the email address.** Don't let the designer lay out around a bracketed note. Either get two real quotes today or have them design the page without that section. Also confirm `hello@sharpmail.example` is a placeholder and swap in the real address.

**Smaller edits**
- "$12 flat for return shipping" is slightly awkward. "Plus $12 flat return shipping" is cleaner.
- Use "per knife" instead of "a knife."
- "What we can't do" can shrink to the one thing you can't do.
