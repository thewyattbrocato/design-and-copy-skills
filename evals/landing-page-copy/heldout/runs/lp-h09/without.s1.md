Your page isn't weak. The substance is the best part: a price, a duration, "I'll tell you before I start, not after," and no commission. Most tuner sites don't say any of that. What feels flat is the delivery: it reads like a spec sheet, and there's no sense of who you are.

**What it needs**

1. **A first line that isn't a résumé.** "I'm Wendell Prasad and I tune pianos" is accurate but forgettable. Lead with something a nervous customer cares about.
2. **One human detail.** Why you do this, a piano you remember, or what you like about church halls. Only you can supply this, and it's the biggest gap.
3. **Your trust points framed as promises.** The no-commission and no-surprises lines are your differentiators. Right now they're buried mid-paragraph.
4. **A picture of the visit.** What happens in those 90 minutes? Do they need to be home? Does it disturb the house?
5. **A clearer ask.** "Texting a time that suits you" is vague. Say what to include in the text, such as their address and the piano's rough age.

**A draft**

> **Your piano should sound like it did when someone first fell in love with it.**
>
> I'm Wendell Prasad, and for 22 years I've been tuning pianos around Dunmore: upright pianos in family living rooms, church halls, and a few school music rooms where the piano gets played hard every day.
>
> **What to expect**
> A standard tuning takes about 90 minutes and costs $130. If your piano has been neglected for years, it may need two visits to settle. I'll tell you that before I start, not after.
>
> **What I won't do**
> I don't sell pianos, and I don't take commission on repairs. If something needs fixing beyond tuning, I'll explain what it is and what it involves, and the decision is yours.
>
> **Book a visit**
> Call 555-0142, or text me your address and a couple of times that suit you. I'll confirm the same day.

**Things I didn't invent, which you should fill in**

- A personal line, for example: *"I got into this because ___."*
- Whether "I'll confirm the same day" is true. Change it to whatever your real turnaround is.
- A short customer quote or two. Even one line from a church or school contact would do more than any of the copy above.
- Your service area beyond "in and around Dunmore," if people ask.
