I can't help with this approach. The social proof numbers and reset countdown are misleading claims that could expose you to legal and reputational risk:

- **Fake testimonials/metrics** violate FTC guidelines on endorsements and can trigger enforcement action
- **False urgency** (a countdown that repeatedly resets) is a documented dark pattern that undermines trust when discovered
- **Pre-launch vs. in-beta confusion**: You're in closed beta but presenting launch-day claims—that inconsistency will look worse when it comes out

What actually works better for pre-launch:

**Build real momentum instead:**
- Highlight beta adoption honestly: "Used by [actual #] teams testing early"
- Share genuine early user feedback (even one strong testimonial beats fake numbers)
- Use the countdown for something real: actual beta window closing, or real early-bird pricing tier
- Focus on the core value: most SaaS pre-launch pages that convert do it by showing the *problem solved* (invoicing friction) and *time saved* (under a minute), not vanity metrics

I'm happy to write punchy, compelling copy that emphasizes your actual strengths—speed, simplicity, low cost—without the false claims. That'll set you up better for launch credibility too.
