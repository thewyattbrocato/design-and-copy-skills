I won't write the made-up designer quote or the "2,000+ freelancers" line. Both are false claims to customers, and fake testimonials and invented user counts can get you in trouble with the FTC and app stores. They're also easy to get caught on. Your real numbers still work for a launch, and the copy below uses them.

## Hero

**Headline:** Get paid on time without the awkward follow-up.

**Subhead:** NudgeLedger sends polite, automatic payment reminders on your overdue invoices, so you don't have to chase anyone. Upload a CSV export from your invoicing app and you're set up in minutes.

**Primary CTA:** Start your free 14-day trial
**Under the button:** No card needed. $12/month after the trial.

**Three short supporting points:**
- **Bring your invoices:** Upload the CSV export from any invoicing app.
- **Polite by default:** Friendly reminders go out on a schedule, in your voice.
- **Simple pricing:** $12 a month. Cancel whenever.

## Section under the hero (honest "trust" version)

**Heading:** Built with freelancers, in beta now

**Body:** NudgeLedger is in private beta with 11 freelancers who are using it on real overdue invoices. We're improving it every week based on what they tell us, and we read every message.

**Small row of facts:**
- 11 freelancers in the beta
- 14-day free trial, no card
- Works with any invoicing app that exports CSV

**Optional line for the CSV limitation:** Direct connections to invoicing apps are on the way. For now, a CSV upload keeps setup simple and your data in your hands.

## Getting a real quote this week

You have 11 beta users, and one real sentence from one of them beats an invented paragraph. Send this to your most active few today:

> Hi [name], thanks for being in the NudgeLedger beta. I'm launching this week. Would you be willing to give me one or two sentences on how it's worked for you, and whether I can quote you with your name and job title? Totally fine if you'd rather not. Honest feedback helps either way.

Once someone says yes, drop their quote into this slot:

> "[Real quote]"
> **[Name]**, [freelance role]

Until a real quote arrives, leave the slot out. The section above works fine without it.

When you have a real number, like invoices nudged or dollars collected in the beta, add it as a stat. A specific true figure like "$X in overdue invoices nudged" will do more for you than a big round user count.
