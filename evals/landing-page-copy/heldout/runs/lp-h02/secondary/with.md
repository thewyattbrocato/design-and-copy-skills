**THE PROBLEM**

**Heading:** You probably have invoices scattered across emails and no clear idea of what you owe in taxes.

**Body:** Most freelance creatives don't have a bookkeeping system. You track your rate, you send invoices, you do the work—but when tax season arrives or you want to know if you're actually making money, the money itself stays a mystery. Invoices are in three places. You don't know exactly who owes you what or whether they've paid. You're not sure how much to set aside for quarterly estimated taxes. And the thought of handing your accountant a shoebox of receipts makes you anxious.

The problem isn't that bookkeeping is hard. It's that nobody teaches it to you in a way that makes sense for how you actually work.

**WHAT YOU'LL LEARN**

**Heading:** Five systems freelance designers and illustrators actually need.

**Body:** 
- **Separate your personal and business money.** This is the foundation that makes everything else work. Most new freelancers skip this and regret it.
- **Track invoices and who owes you.** So you know your real cash flow without logging into ten different platforms.
- **Set aside money for quarterly estimated taxes.** US freelancers only. No more surprises when April arrives.
- **Organize receipts and expenses.** The way accountants actually need to see them, so your tax prep is fast and cheap.
- **Hand a clean year to your accountant.** They get a clear file. You get peace of mind. Everyone's happy.

This course is built for the way you actually work—not a generic accounting class written for someone else.
