# Quiet Books
## Bookkeeping for Freelance Illustrators and Designers

You didn't start your freelance career to become an accountant. But at some point, the invoices pile up. You're not sure how much you actually made last year. Tax season looms, and you realize you have no idea what to set aside for quarterly payments. The spreadsheets are a mess, or maybe they don't exist yet.

**Quiet Books** teaches you the bookkeeping skills you actually need—the ones that keep your finances clear, your taxes manageable, and your accountant happy.

### What You'll Learn

Over 5 weeks, you'll work through 18 video lessons (about 12 minutes each) on the core practices that matter:

- **Separating personal and business money.** This single shift clarifies everything. You'll understand which purchases belong in your business accounts and why it matters.
- **Tracking invoices and who owes you.** A simple system so clients can't accidentally slip through the cracks, and you always know where your money is.
- **Setting aside money for quarterly estimated taxes.** The US tax system for freelancers means you pay throughout the year, not just at tax time. This lesson shows you exactly how much to reserve and where to keep it.
- **Handling receipts.** You'll learn which receipts matter, how to organize them, and what information to capture so nothing's lost when you hand everything to your accountant.
- **Preparing a clean year-end for your accountant.** This means less back-and-forth, fewer revisions, and lower accounting fees.
