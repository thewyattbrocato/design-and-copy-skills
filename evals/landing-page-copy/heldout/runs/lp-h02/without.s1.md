# Quiet Books
### Bookkeeping for freelance illustrators and designers

You went freelance to make work, not to manage a spreadsheet. But the money side doesn't go away. Invoices go out and you lose track of which ones are paid. Receipts pile up. Personal and business spending end up in the same account. Then tax time arrives.

Quiet Books is a self-paced course that gives your finances a simple, calm system, so they stop competing with your creative work for attention.

## What you'll learn

The course covers the five parts of freelance bookkeeping that matter most:

**Separating personal and business money.** Set up a clean line between the two, so you always know what's yours and what belongs to the business.

**Tracking invoices and who owes you.** Know what you've billed, what's been paid, and what's overdue, without digging through your inbox.

**Setting aside money for quarterly estimated taxes.** Build a habit of putting money aside, so quarterly payments are planned for and don't catch you off guard. (US only.)

**Handling receipts.** A straightforward way to capture and file them, so they're not a pile you dread.

**Handing a clean year to an accountant.** Finish the year with records your accountant can work from, instead of a scramble of loose ends.

## Who it's not for

Being clear about this up front saves us both time:

- **It's US-only.** The course covers US freelancers, and the quarterly estimated tax material applies to the US only. If you freelance elsewhere, this isn't the right fit.
- **It's not tax advice.** Quiet Books teaches bookkeeping: how to organize, track, and prepare your finances. For advice about your specific tax situation, talk with a qualified tax professional. The course will help you arrive at that conversation with your records in order.
