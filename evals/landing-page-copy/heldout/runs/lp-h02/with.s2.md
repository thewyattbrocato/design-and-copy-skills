## Your work is making things. The money side shouldn't be a pile.

Business money mixes with personal money. Invoices go unchased because nobody wrote down who owes what. Receipts sit in a camera roll. Quarterly taxes show up as a surprise.

Quiet Books replaces that with a few habits and ready-made spreadsheets you can keep up between projects.

## Five things you'll be able to do

**Separate personal and business money.** Set up a clean line between the two, so you always know which is which.

**Track invoices and who owes you.** See what's been sent, what's been paid and what's late.

**Set aside money for quarterly estimated taxes.** Build the habit of putting money away before the deadline arrives. This is US only.

**Handle receipts.** Decide once how receipts get captured and filed, then stop thinking about them.

**Hand a clean year to an accountant.** Finish the year with records your accountant can work from, instead of a box of loose ends.
