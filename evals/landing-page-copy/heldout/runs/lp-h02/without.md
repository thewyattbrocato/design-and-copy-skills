## Built for freelancers who make things

If you're an illustrator or designer in the US and your money lives in a mix of personal accounts, half-remembered invoices, and a folder of receipts you keep meaning to deal with, this course is for you.

You don't need a finance background. You need a simple system you can keep up with, and that's what Quiet Books gives you.

## What you'll learn

Over five weeks, you'll work through the parts of bookkeeping that matter most for a solo creative business:

- **Separating personal and business money.** Set up a clear line between the two, so you always know what's yours and what belongs to the business.
- **Tracking invoices and who owes you.** See what you've billed, what's been paid, and what's overdue, without digging through your inbox.
- **Setting aside money for quarterly estimated taxes (US only).** Build a habit of putting money aside, so quarterly payments don't catch you off guard.
- **Handling receipts.** Create a simple routine for capturing and filing them, so they stop piling up.
- **Handing a clean year to an accountant.** Finish the year with records your accountant can work from without a scramble.
