## Five parts of bookkeeping, taught in plain order

**Separate personal and business money.** Set up your accounts so every dollar has a clear home and your records stop tangling.

**Track invoices and who owes you.** Keep one running picture of what you've billed, what's been paid, and what's still outstanding.

**Set aside money for quarterly estimated taxes.** Build a routine for putting money away before each payment comes due. US only.

**Handle receipts.** Decide what to keep and how to keep it, so receipts stop piling up.

**Hand a clean year to an accountant.** Finish the year with records your accountant can work from, not a pile to sort through.
