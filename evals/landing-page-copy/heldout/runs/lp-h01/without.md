## The bread

**Country White**
Our everyday loaf. Crisp crust, open crumb, and the mild tang that comes from a long ferment.

**Rye-Caraway**
Deeper and earthier, with caraway all the way through. Good with butter, better with a sharp cheese.

**Seeded Spelt**
Nutty and a little sweet, with a seeded crust.

## Nothing else in it

Flour, water, salt. That's the whole ingredient list. We give the dough about 36 hours to ferment, and that time does the work that other bakeries leave to additives. You get flavor, a good crust, and bread that keeps well.
