**Notes on the content**
- **Slide 2:** this is where the builders hear their case stated fairly before you contest it. Slide 6 does the same for what they'd lose.
- **Slide 5:** this is one case. Don't present it as a trend.
  - You said the customer complained about results. If that means relevance rather than speed, 180 ms may not fix it, and the builders will say so. Find out what the complaints were about, and whether the vendor's trial results were better. Otherwise keep this slide to one line, or cut it first if time is short.
- **Renting versus owning:** I'd use it as one spoken sentence on slide 4, or drop it. It isn't proof. The builders can turn it against you: owners build equity, and the people who built this are the owners. Slides 3 and 4 are the evidence, so keep the analogy next to them and never make it a slide.
- **Gaps:** I haven't filled in anything you didn't give me. The bracketed items are placeholders.
  - The 2.5 engineers versus $96k comparison is only convincing with a cost per engineer.
  - Migration cost isn't in your notes, and someone will ask about it.

**If time shrinks:** cut slide 5 to a spoken line, then merge slide 1 into your first sentence.

2. What does migration cost, and who does it?
