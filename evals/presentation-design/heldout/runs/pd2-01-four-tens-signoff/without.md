[One line: e.g., 'We're losing X hours a month to overtime and absence on nights, and turnover on that shift is Y%.'] Then handovers, including what we'd do to protect them and how we'd know if it's not working. Then Ana will cover overtime and rest-period compliance. Then I want to hear from all of you.
>
> To be clear about what I'm asking for: this is a time-boxed pilot with a defined rollback trigger, not a permanent change. If handover quality drops below [threshold], we revert.
>
> So the question I'll come back to at the end is: *are we comfortable running this as a pilot, and if not, what would need to change?*
>
> Let me start with why."

Why this opening works:
- It states the ask in the first 15 seconds, so Maria knows what she's deciding.
- It names the objection yourself, which takes the sting out of Tom and Jess raising it.
- It frames the request as a reversible pilot, which is much easier to approve than a permanent change.
- It ends on the question you'll close with, so the whole meeting points at the decision.
