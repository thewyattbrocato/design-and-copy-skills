## Where the pauses go

In a recording there's no room to read, so a pause is a 1–2 second beat you cut in on purpose. Put one at each of these points:
- After the headline number, before you move on.
- Right before the story starts.
- Right after you name the margin cause, before you say what you're doing about it.

Never leave the screen blank or black during a pause. Hold a quiet slide that belongs to the moment.

## Slide plan

| Time | Slide (claim title) | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:35 | "Q3 [headline result], with margin down [X] points" | One big number, nothing else. Say the takeaway and preview the three parts by voice. |
| 0:35–1:45 | "[Revenue/key metric] grew [X]% on [driver]" | One chart or 2–3 numbers. |
| 1:45–2:45 | "Gross margin fell [X] points because of [cause]" | See below. |
| 2:45–3:00 | Transition: "Here's what that looks like from a customer's side" | Quiet slide: customer name or logo only. |
| 3:00–4:10 | Customer story: one real line from them | See below. |
| 4:10–5:30 | "Q4: [2–3 priorities], and how they address margin" | Three short labels. |
| 5:30–6:00 | Takeaway plus any ask, with owner and date | Stays up to the end. |

I put margin before the story because bad news should come early, and the story then lands as relief in the middle. If the story illustrates the margin cause, swap them.

## The margin minute (explanation)

- **Slide:** a single bridge (waterfall) chart from last quarter's margin to this quarter's, with one bar per cause. Keep it to two or three causes at most.
- **Title:** states the cause as a claim, not "Gross margin".
- **Build:** reveal one bar at a time, in the order you say them. The final bar and the total stay on screen through your beat.
- **Pause:** one beat after the biggest cause, then say the fix.
- **Not on screen:** cost-line tables or derivations. Put those in a linked doc if finance wants them.

## The customer story

- **Before it:** a short spoken handoff over the quiet logo slide, then the beat.
- **During:** a photo or logo and one pull quote under about 20 words. It must be a real quote with the customer's permission.
- **Structure:** problem, what we did, result in the customer's terms, in roughly that order. Say one concrete number if you have a real one.
- **Afterward:** a beat, then one sentence tying it to Q4.

## Because people watch alone on laptops

- Titles should make sense if someone pauses or scrubs, because they will.
- Use big text, about 32 px minimum on a 1920-wide frame, since many will watch in a small window.
- Add chapter markers on the intranet for "Results", "Margin" and "Customer", so people can jump.
- If you have to cut for time, cut the Q4 slide's detail first, then the results chart.
