Don't present this as "the emails caused the churn." Present it as a leading hypothesis with a cheap test and a decision for Leo. You can't prove causation yet, and a VP will spot that fast. Showing you know what's unproven makes you more credible.

**Slide 1: The headline and the ask**
- "Monthly churn rose from 3.1% to 4.4% (+1.3 pts) in the 3 weeks since the new onboarding emails launched on the 2nd."
- "The emails are the leading suspect, but not yet confirmed."
- The ask: "Approve a 2-week holdback test, with 50% of new signups getting the old emails, so we can confirm or rule it out."

**Slide 2: What we know and don't know**
- A chart of weekly churn with a vertical line at the 2nd. Show the weeks before it, so Leo can see whether the rise was already underway.
- For: the timing, and no other known launches in the window.
- Against or unclear: the March annual cohort improved (1.6% to 1.2%).
- Say plainly that "nothing else shipped" is what you know, not something you've verified. Say who you checked with.

**Slide 3: The March cohort, and what would settle it**
- Don't hide this one. Lead with it before Leo does.
- First, the question to answer: did that cohort receive the new emails? If the emails only go to new signups, March annual users were never exposed, so their numbers say nothing either way. If they did receive them, it's a real counterpoint.
- Second, it's an annual cohort, so the base is small and the movement of 0.4 pts may be noise. Put the actual counts on the slide.
- The cut that matters most is churn by signup cohort and tenure. If the spike is in users who signed up after the 2nd, the emails are very likely the cause. If it's in tenured users who never got them, the emails are probably not the cause.

**Slide 4: Plan and decision**
- Do this week: split churn by tenure and signup date, and check other causes (pricing or billing changes, payment failures, marketing campaigns, seasonality, plan mix).
- Run a holdback A/B on new signups for 2 weeks.
- The decision for Leo is whether to approve the holdback now or pause the emails outright. Pausing is the faster fix, but it costs you the clean test. Recommend the holdback unless the tenure cut shows the damage is large.
