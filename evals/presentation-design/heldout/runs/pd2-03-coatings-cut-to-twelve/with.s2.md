## What stays out of the live talk (the other ~35 slides)

- **Chemistry and mechanisms:** how the formulations work, composition, why they fail. Procurement can't use these. Move them to backup.
- **Test method and setup:** one spoken sentence covers it ("18 months, real seawater"). Keep the detailed protocol in backup.
- **Chronology:** the order you ran things in over three years. Don't tell it. Start from the result.
- **Per-coating data tables for all six:** show the chart for C2 and C5 only.
- **Intermediate results and dead ends:** leave them out entirely.
- **Team, funding, lab history, "thank you" and "questions" slides:** cut.
- **Agenda slide:** cut. Preview by voice in the first 30 seconds: "what we tested, what worked, what didn't, what to do next."
