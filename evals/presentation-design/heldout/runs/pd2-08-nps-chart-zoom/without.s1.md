Most of the room is just listening while a chart sits on screen, so your words have to do the pointing. Build the walkthrough around that.

**Before Thursday: make the chart do half the work**
- Color APAC in one strong color and the other three in muted gray. You can then say "the highlighted line" and everyone knows which one.
- Label the lines directly at their right ends instead of using a legend, so nobody has to match colors.
- Put the headline in the chart title, e.g. "APAC NPS fell 8 points in two quarters; other regions held flat."
- Annotate the 41 and the 33 on the APAC line. Without them, people have to eyeball the axis.

**The walkthrough (about 2–3 minutes)**

1. **Say the takeaway first (15 sec).** "One thing to take from this chart: APAC NPS has dropped two quarters in a row, from 41 to 33, while every other region stayed roughly flat."

2. **Orient them (20 sec).** Describe the axes out loud, because someone will be on their phone and half-listening. "This is NPS over the last eight quarters. Each line is a region. Time runs left to right, and higher is better."

3. **Establish the baseline (20 sec).** Point out the stable regions first. "NA, EMEA, and LATAM, the gray lines, have barely moved. They sit within a few points of where they started." This makes the APAC drop read as a real signal and not general noise.

4. **Show the drop (30 sec).** Use position words, not "here" or "this." "Now the highlighted APAC line. It was at 41 and holding steady until [quarter]. Then it fell, and it fell again the next quarter, to 33. That's 8 points over two quarters."

5. **Say what it means for them (30 sec).** Tailor this to the audience.
   - Sales: "If you work APAC accounts, expect more questions on renewals."
   - Support: "This is where we need to understand what customers are telling us."

6. **State what you know and don't know (20 sec).** If you have a cause, say it. If you don't, say that too: "We don't yet know why. Here's what we're doing to find out." People trust that more than a guess.

7. **Close with the ask and a repeat.** "To recap, APAC is down 8 points over two quarters, the other regions are stable, and [next step / what you need from them]."

**Tips for a cameras-off Zoom**
- Pause for 2–3 seconds after the key number. You won't see faces, so silence is your only way to let it land.
- Say each key number twice, once when you reveal it and once in the recap.
- Don't say "as you can see." Describe what's there.
- Ask for input in a way that works without faces: "I'll pause for questions, or drop them in chat." Or call on a person who knows APAC, such as an APAC support lead, to add context.
- Keep a backup line for anyone who joins late or has the chart hidden: "In case you missed it, APAC is down from 41 to 33."
