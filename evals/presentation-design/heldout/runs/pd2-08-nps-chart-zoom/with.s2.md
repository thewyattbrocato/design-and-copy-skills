The plan below is for the chart-on-screen part of the talk, about 5 minutes on one slide. The takeaway is: **APAC NPS fell from 41 to 33 over two quarters while NA, EMEA and LATAM held flat, and we need to find out why.**

With cameras off, your voice and the chart are all they have. Don't blank the screen at any point. Tell them where to look before each point.

**Slide title (a claim, not "NPS by region"):**
"APAC NPS fell 8 points in two quarters while every other region held flat"

**Chart setup**
- Draw NA, EMEA and LATAM in light gray, with small direct labels at the line ends instead of a legend.
- Draw APAC in one strong color, and label its two endpoints, "41" and "33".
- Pick a color that doesn't already mean "alarm" in your team's dashboards.
- Keep the axis range fixed and leave off the gridlines.
- Put a small source line underneath.

**Walkthrough**
1. **Set up the axes (about 20 seconds).** Say it out loud, because people can't ask: "Each line is one region's NPS. Time runs left to right, eight quarters. Higher is better."
2. **Show the context first.** If your tool supports builds, show only the three gray lines. Say: "NA, EMEA and LATAM have moved within a few points for two years." Replace "a few points" with the real spread from your data.
3. **Bring in APAC.** Say: "Now APAC. It was at 41 in [quarter]. It's 33 now. That's two straight quarters of decline." Pause for a few seconds. On a call, a silent beat gets people to look up.
4. **Say what it means for them.** This audience is sales and support, so tie the drop to their work: "This is the one region where customers are telling us something has changed."
5. **Close with what's known and what's next.** Say what you know about the cause, what you don't, and what you want from the room. Examples are "support leads in APAC, send me X by Y" or "sales, flag any APAC accounts you've heard complaints from." Name an owner and a date.

**Opening line:** Start with the finding. For example: "One number to remember from this quarter's NPS: APAC is down 8 points in two quarters, and no other region moved." Then show the chart.

**Things to prepare**
- **Cause:** Someone will ask "why?" Have a hypothesis or an honest "we don't know yet, here's how we'll find out." Don't speculate on the call.
- **Size of the drop:** Someone may ask whether 8 points is statistically meaningful. Know the APAC sample size per quarter. If it's small, say so.
- **Backup slide:** Keep a hidden slide after the end with APAC split by segment or by driver, if you have it.

**Zoom logistics**
- Share the screen with the chart already at the "gray lines only" step.
- Use the pointer or a highlight to show where to look, since nobody can see you gesture.
- Put the exact numbers in the chat or a follow-up message rather than on the slide.
