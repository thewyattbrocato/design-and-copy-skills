Use short labels in the progress bar and keep your full titles as the headline on each slide. Seven labels across one strip need to be about 1-2 words each, so they stay on one line at a readable size.

| # | Progress bar label | Slide headline |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Agenda | Agenda |
| 2 | Q3 Delivery | Q3 delivery performance |
| 3 | Late Shipments | Late shipments: root causes |
| 4 | Quality | Quality incidents |
| 5 | Costs | Cost changes |
| 6 | Fixes | Proposed fixes |
| 7 | Next Steps | Next steps |

- **Parallel style:** the labels are all short nouns. "Q3 Delivery" keeps the quarter without needing the word "performance".
- **Highlighting:** the current section is highlighted in the bar, so the slide headline doesn't need to repeat it exactly. Once you have the numbers, you can turn the headline into a takeaway (for example, "Late shipments: two causes drive most delays"). The bar label stays the same.
- **Possible tightening:** if "Late Shipments" is too wide for the template, use "Delays". It's still clear to a procurement audience.
- **Pacing:** 15 minutes across 7 slides is about 2 minutes each. Spend the extra time on Late Shipments, Quality and Fixes, and keep Agenda to about 30 seconds. A bar that shows all seven sections will make the audience notice if you spend too long on any one.
