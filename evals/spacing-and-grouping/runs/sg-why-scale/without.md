A fixed spacing scale (say 4, 8, 16, 24, 32) turns spacing from a thousand tiny decisions into a small vocabulary.

- **Consistency:** Components built by different people line up and feel like one product. Values chosen by eye drift, like 13px here and 15px there. They look slightly off even when no one can say why.
- **Speed:** You pick from a short list instead of tweaking and second-guessing.
- **Maintainability:** Spacing lives in tokens, so you can change density or theme in one place.
- **Rhythm:** Values built on a shared base unit, like 4px, create visual harmony and line up with grids and type.
- **Communication:** Designers and developers can say "space-4" and mean the same thing.
