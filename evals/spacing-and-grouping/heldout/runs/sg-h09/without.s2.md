**Recommendation: headings plus extra space, with no cards or rules.**

12 fields in 3 short sections is a small form. Cards and lines add visual weight that it doesn't need.

- **Spacing and headings:** A clear heading with about 32–40px above it and 16–24px between fields groups the sections well. Proximity does the work, and the form stays light and easy to scan.
- **Horizontal lines:** These are a reasonable addition if the headings and spacing feel too weak. They work best as thin, low-contrast dividers between sections. Don't put one under every heading as well, since that gets noisy.
- **Cards:** Use these only if the sections are independent. That means they're saved separately, collapsible, or reorderable, or the form sits on a busy or tinted background. Otherwise you get boxes inside a page that's probably already a container, with extra padding and nested borders. On mobile they also eat horizontal space.

**Details that matter more than the divider choice:**
- Make headings visibly stronger than field labels, using size or weight.
- Keep gaps between sections clearly larger than gaps between fields. If they're about equal, nothing will look grouped, whichever divider you pick.
- The Preferences toggles differ in kind from the text inputs. A short helper line under the heading, or a slightly tighter toggle list, is usually enough to set them apart.

**When I'd change this:**
- If the form grows to long, multi-step sections, move to cards or separate steps.
- If users edit sections independently, use cards with per-section save buttons.
