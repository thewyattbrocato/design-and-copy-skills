Why per active project

  • Matches the work. A project is the unit customers track. Seats that only view or sit idle stop inflating the bill.
  • Charges heavy project load. Fennmark runs 20 active projects on 2 seats and pays $40 today. Per-user pricing undercharges that usage.
  • One number to explain. $15 per active project per month, with no seat counting or tiers.
