Why it fits

Price follows the work customers actively run in the tool. Teams that add people without adding projects stop paying for seats they do not use, and large project portfolios pay for the value they get.

The tradeoff

Accounts with many projects and few users see large increases. Fennmark Studio would go from $40 to $300 per month.

Decision requested

Approve $15 per active project per month, with a transition path (phased rollout or capped increase) for accounts whose bills rise sharply.

Figures are monthly list prices for three sample customers, before discounts. Based on current per-user seat counts and active project counts. "Active project" definition to be confirmed before rollout.
