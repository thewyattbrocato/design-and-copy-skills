Why it fits

Price follows the work a customer runs in the tool, not how many people log in. Teams with many viewers and few active projects no longer pay for seats they do not use.

What we need to decide

Fennmark-type accounts (few users, many active projects) pay 7.5× more under this model. Decide how to handle them, such as a transition period or a cap, before the change takes effect.
