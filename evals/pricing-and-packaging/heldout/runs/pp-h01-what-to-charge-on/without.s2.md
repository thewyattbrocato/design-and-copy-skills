I'd charge per teacher in three tiers, with the tier set by active student count.

| Tier | Active students | Price | Teachers (est.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | up to 12 | $7/mo | ~360 |
| Standard | 13-30 | $12/mo | ~405 |
| Studio | 31+ | $24/mo | ~135 |

If nobody changes tier or leaves, that's about $10,600/mo against $8,100 today, roughly 30% more.

**Why tiers beat the other two options**
- **Per lesson** is the worst fit. It charges teachers more for doing more of their job, and the bill swings with holidays and recital season. Teachers also tend to resent metered pricing, and a surprise invoice is a reason to leave.
- **Per student** is fairer in theory, but it makes teachers hesitate to add a student, and it adds billing noise (prorating, mid-month changes). Your costs don't scale with students either, so you'd be pricing on something that isn't driving your costs.
- **Tiers** keep the bill predictable and make the fairness argument you care about: a teacher with 6 students pays less than one with 55. They also match the shape of your base, since 12 and 30 are natural breakpoints in your numbers.

**The top tier is where the support cost goes.** The big studios email you the most, so a higher Studio price covers that. They also get the most value from the app and are the least likely to switch over a $24 bill.

**Details that matter**
- **Count "active" students** (for example, one with a lesson in the last 60 days). Otherwise old students who've left push teachers into a higher tier.
- **Don't auto-upgrade on a single spike.** Move a teacher up only after they've been over the line for 2 months, and tell them before you do.
- **Grandfather current teachers** at $9 for a few months, with 60-90 days' notice. The 6-student teachers get a cut, which makes the announcement easy. The Studio teachers are the ones who will notice, so explain it to them directly.
- **Try it on new signups first** if you can. The numbers above are my guess at what your market will bear, not something I've tested. The main risk is the Standard tier, since it's your biggest group and goes from $9 to $12.

If you'd rather not raise anyone's price at all, use $7 / $9 / $18 instead. That comes to about $8,800/mo, still above today's revenue, with less churn risk.
