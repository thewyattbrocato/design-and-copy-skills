**Recommendation: charge per active student, with a $9 base that covers up to 18 students, then $0.50 for each additional active student.**

Every teacher with 18 or fewer students pays exactly what they pay now, so nobody gets a cut. Teachers with more students pay more, in proportion to the students they manage.

| Teacher size (assumed avg. students) | Teachers | Now | Proposed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under 12 (6) | ~360 | $9 | $9 |
| 12–30 (20) | ~405 | $9 | $10 |
| Over 30 (50) | ~135 | $9 | $25 |
| **Total per month** | ~900 | **$8,100** | **~$10,700 (+32%)** |

If the over-30 group averages 40 students, the total is about $10,000 (+23%). If it averages 80, the total is about $12,700.

**Why this unit:**
- **Active students** is the thing teachers think in, and the thing your scheduling work scales with. A teacher can predict their bill from their roster.
- **Per lesson booked** is a poor choice. Lesson counts swing with holidays and recitals, so bills become unpredictable. It also charges for the calendar usage that your servers barely notice, since your costs are roughly flat per teacher.
- **Fixed size bands** (under 12, 12–30, over 30) are simple, but they create price cliffs. A teacher with 13 students would face a large jump, so they would be tempted to stay at 12. The base-plus-increment version avoids that.

**Before you launch:**
- Define "active student" in one line, for example "has a lesson booked in the next 60 days". Otherwise former students inflate the bill.
- Check your support email per teacher by size band. The large studios' extra support load is the main argument for charging them more, so confirm it is real.
- Ask 10–15 teachers from the over-30 group what they would pay. Their answers, not my averages, should set the increment.

**Assumed:** the average student counts above, that "active" means roughly what I described, and that no solo teacher will leave over a price that does not change their bill.

**Would change if:** support email does not rise with student count (then a flat price plus a paid studio plan with extra support or multiple staff logins fits better), or if the over-30 teachers say they would leave above about $20 (then use a lower increment or a cap).
