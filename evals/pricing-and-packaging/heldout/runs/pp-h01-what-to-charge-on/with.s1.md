**I'd charge per teacher in three size bands, counted by active students. I wouldn't charge per student or per lesson.**

| Tier | For | Price | Teachers (of 900) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | Up to 12 students | $7/mo | ~360 |
| Studio | 13 to 30 students | $12/mo | ~405 |
| Large | 31+ students | $20/mo (range $18 to $24) | ~135 |

**Why bands instead of per student or per lesson**
- **Per lesson** taxes the behavior you want, which is booking through the app. It also swings with holidays, summers and weekly versus biweekly lessons, so a teacher can't predict the bill.
- **Per student** tracks value best. But the bill moves every time a student joins or drops, and "who counts as a student" becomes a support argument.
- **Bands** keep per-student fairness (6 students and 55 students stop paying the same) and keep a bill the teacher can predict. Count "active" students as those with a lesson in the last 60 days.

**Arithmetic**

Today you collect 900 × $9 = $8,100/mo. Using your percentages, with no one changing behavior:
- $7 for Starter: 360 × $7 = $2,520
- $12 for Studio: 405 × $12 = $4,860
- $20 for Large: 135 × $20 = $2,700
- Total: **$10,080/mo, about 24% more**

The Large price is the one that matters most. With Starter and Studio fixed, here is what it does to the total:

| Large price | Total/mo | If 25% of Large leave |
|---|---|---|
| $15 | $9,405 | $8,642 |
| $20 | $10,080 | $9,330 |
| $24 | $10,620 | $9,764 |

Even if a quarter of your biggest studios walked, the total stays above today's $8,100. That's why I'd lean toward the upper half of the range. These studios have the most students, so they get the most value from the app, and they're also the ones emailing you the most.

**Details that make it work**
- **Starter is a price cut ($9 to $7).** The cause is that the flat fee feels unfair to your smallest teachers. It costs about $720/mo, which the other tiers more than cover. It's the entry tier and doesn't expire.
- **Each tier must include something the one below lacks, beyond the student cap.** Large should have priority support, since that's the cost they drive. Studio needs one thing Starter lacks. Candidates are invoicing, make-up-lesson handling or group reminders, depending on what you've built.
- **Existing teachers:** announce the change with a date. Everyone who would pay more keeps $9 for a stated period, say 3 months, then moves to their band. Large goes from $9 to $20, so the longer hold is worth it for them, maybe 6 months.
- **Watch** downgrades and cancellations among Large and Studio teachers for the first 90 days. If more than about a third of Large leave, drop that price toward $16 to $18.

**Assumed:** the 40/45/15 split is accurate and teachers don't change tiers. I have no data on churn, willingness to pay, or support cost per tier. The Studio feature is a placeholder.

**Would change if:**
- Many teachers sit at 10 to 14 students. Then the band edges will cause friction, and per-student pricing may fit better.
- Talking to 8 to 10 large studios shows they'd pay $30 or more, or balk at $20.
- Support cost for big studios turns out to be much higher than it feels. Then the Large price or its support terms need to go up.
