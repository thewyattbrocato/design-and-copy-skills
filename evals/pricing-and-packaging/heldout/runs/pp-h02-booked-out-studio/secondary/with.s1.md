**Why the risk is small.** Revenue stays flat or rises as long as the volume you lose is smaller than the share below. You still have a queue, so some lost volume is just a shorter waitlist.

| New price | Revenue breaks even if volume falls by at most |
|---|---|
| $2,000 | 10% |
| $2,400 | 25% |
| $2,800 | 36% |
| $3,200 | 44% |

Formula: 1 − 1,800 ÷ new price. For profit, use 1 − (1,800 − c) ÷ (new price − c), where c is your cost per video (freelancers, voice, music, stock). If c is $600, the $2,400 break-even on profit is a 33% volume loss, and $2,800 is 45%.
