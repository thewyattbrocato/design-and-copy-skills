## Recommendation: three plans, one per segment

| | **Plus** (casual shooters) | **Family** (shared libraries) | **Pro** (working photographers) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly price | **$2.99** | **$9.99** | **$29** includes 1 TB; **+$15 per extra TB**, up to 5 TB ($89 at 5 TB) |
| Annual | $29 (10 months) | $99 (10 months) | Monthly only (see below) |
| Storage | 50 GB | 500 GB shared | 1 to 5 TB |
| Members | 1 | Up to 6, one library | 1 |
| AI search cap | 200 per month | 600 per month | 3,000 per month |

**What goes in each (nested, so each tier includes the one below):**

- **Plus:** auto backup, face grouping, shared albums (view links), 30-day trash, original-quality downloads, AI search, RAW backup (counts against storage).
- **Family:** everything in Plus, plus the shared library for up to 6 people and shared albums with members.
- **Pro:** everything in Family, plus RAW tools (assumed: whatever your RAW support includes beyond backup), client galleries with watermarks, the Lightroom plugin, and the higher search cap.

**Arithmetic at full use** (cost = storage + search + payment fees, with fees assumed at 2.9% + $0.30):

- **Plus at 50 GB and 200 searches:** $0.60 + $0.60 + $0.39 = $1.59 cost, about $1.40 margin. At a typical 20 GB, margin is well above that.
- **Family at 500 GB and 600 searches:** $6.00 + $1.80 + $0.59 = $8.39 cost, about $1.60 margin at the cap. Most families sit at 50 to 300 GB, so typical margin is higher.
- **Pro at 1 TB and 3,000 searches:** $12 + $9 + $1.14 = $22.14 cost, about $6.86 margin (24%).
- **Pro at 5 TB and 3,000 searches:** $60 + $9 + $2.88 = $71.88 cost, about $17 margin (19%).

**Why Pro's extra-TB price is $15:** storage alone costs $12 per TB, so anything near that loses money on the heaviest users. Pro annual at $290 would leave only about $2 a month at 1 TB, so I'd keep Pro monthly until you see real usage.

**Free option:** I'd offer a 30-day free trial of Plus, not a permanent free tier. A free tier with 5 GB costs about $0.06 per user per month in storage alone, which is cheap, but casual users with 20 GB would not stay on it, so it mostly adds support load without converting. If you want one, cap it at 5 GB with no AI search.

**Test range (not validated):** Plus $2.99 to $3.99; Family $9.99 to $12.99; Pro base $29 to $39, with the extra-TB price never below $12.

Assumed:
- No willingness-to-pay data exists yet. These prices are starting points, not findings. Check them with beta users in each segment: ask what they'd pay as a range, then test with paid pre-orders at each price.
- The $0.012 per GB figure includes redundancy. If it doesn't, Pro margins shrink.
- Payment fees are 2.9% + $0.30, and support cost is roughly zero. Support is not in these numbers and will matter most on Family and Pro.
- Photographers use about 1 TB at the base and stay under 3,000 searches a month.

Would change if:
- Beta data shows Pro users sit at 3 TB or more, which pushes the extra-TB price or base price up.
- Searches exceed the caps, which means raising the caps or moving to overage packs.
- Support costs run above about $1 per user per month, which squeezes Plus first.
- Plus users mostly stay under 10 GB, which would justify a lower entry price.
