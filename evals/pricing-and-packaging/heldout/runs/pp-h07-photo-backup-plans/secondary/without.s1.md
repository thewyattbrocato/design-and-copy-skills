**Free:** Auto backup, 30-day trash, face grouping, 20 AI searches/mo. No RAW, shared albums, or client galleries.

**Plus:** Everything in Free, plus 100 GB, unlimited face grouping, 100 AI searches/mo, RAW backup, original-quality downloads, and 3 shared albums.

**Family:** Everything in Plus, plus a shared library with per-person views, unlimited shared albums, family face grouping, 500 AI searches/mo, and up to 6 accounts.

**Pro 2 TB / Pro 5 TB:** Everything in Family, plus client galleries with watermarks, the Lightroom plugin, faster AI search with a 2,000 searches/mo fair-use cap, and priority support.

## Cost analysis

- **Storage dominates.** At $0.012/GB, one TB costs $12/mo. Search is minor: 1,000 searches/mo costs $3.
- **Photographers are the margin problem.** At 5 TB you spend $60/mo, which is $720/yr against a $799 price. Many consumer clouds charge far less per TB. Options:
  - Put originals older than a set age in a cheaper cold tier, which may cut costs by well over half. I don't have your provider's pricing, so confirm before relying on this.
  - Raise the Pro price, or charge for overage above 2 TB.
  - Offer annual plans at no more than about 15% off.
- **Trash counts toward storage.** The 30-day trash holds space, so include it in the quota.

## Costs I couldn't model

- **Download bandwidth** for original-quality downloads and Lightroom sync. Egress can be significant for RAW-heavy users.
- **Compute** for face grouping and indexing. These are one-time costs per photo, not per search.
- **Payment fees** of roughly 3% on web checkout.
- **App store fees.** If you sell through iOS or Android in-app purchase, the store takes 15–30%. That would push the Pro tiers into losses, so price with that in mind or sell on the web.

## Decisions for you

1. Whether Pro 5 TB should be priced above $80 or capped at 2 TB until storage costs drop.
2. Whether you can move older originals to cheaper storage. That changes every Pro price.
3. Whether to sell through app stores. If so, I'd raise all paid prices by about 20–30%.
