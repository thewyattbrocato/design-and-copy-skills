**Recommendation: five plans, three of them built around your segments.** Storage is the main cost, at $12 per TB per month, so each plan's storage cap sets its price. The table prices at full allowance, the worst case for each plan, before payment fees.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | Trying it | $0 | 5 GB | 0 | $0.06 | n/a |
| Plus | Casual phone shooters | $2.99/mo or $29/yr | 50 GB | 200 | $0.60 + $0.60 = $1.20 | $1.79 (60%) |

**What goes in each:**
- **Free:** auto backup, 30-day trash, basic search without AI, 5 GB. The free cost is capped at $0.06 per user per month, and the storage cap keeps it there.
- **Plus:** everything in Free, plus 50 GB, AI search, face grouping, and original-quality downloads.
- **Family:** everything in Plus, plus the shared 300 GB library for up to 6 people, shared albums, and RAW backup.
- **Pro:** everything in Family, plus 1 TB, client galleries with watermarks, the Lightroom plugin, and priority search. Pro is the only tier where RAW and galleries are the reason to pay.
- **Studio:** the same features as Pro with 5 TB. Keep it as a storage tier, not a different product.

Those tiers map to your segments: Free and Plus for casual shooters, Family for families, Pro and Studio for photographers. Keep the 30-day trash on every tier. Don't offer a blanket discount; annual plans are the only discount, at about 17–19% off.

**Assumed:**
- Search volumes: about 100 per month for casual users, 500 for families, 1,000 for Pro, and 2,000 for Studio. The caps are the same numbers.
- Payment fees (about 3% + $0.30) and client-gallery bandwidth are not in the margins. Galleries may be the largest cost you haven't measured.
- The $0.012/GB storage rate is all-in, including redundancy.
- Casual users stay under the 50 GB Plus cap.

**Would change if:**
- Gallery downloads are expensive. If so, add a bandwidth cap or charge for gallery traffic. Studio's margin is the first to go.
- Beta families regularly exceed 150 GB. If so, raise Family's price or sell storage add-ons.
- Real search counts are well above these caps. Then set the AI search cap lower and sell more searches.
