**Recommendation: three paid plans, one per buyer group, with a 30-day trial instead of a free tier.**

| Plan | Buyer | Price | Includes | Cost at cap* | Margin at cap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Plus** | Casual phone shooters | $2.99/mo or $29/yr | 50 GB, auto backup, face grouping, 30-day trash, 100 AI searches/mo, original-quality downloads | $1.29/mo | $1.70 (57%) |
| **Family** | Shared libraries, 3–6 people | $9.99/mo or $99/yr | 300 GB pooled, up to 6 members, shared library and albums, face grouping across members, 500 AI searches/mo pooled, 30-day trash, original-quality downloads | $5.70/mo | $4.29 (43%) |
| **Pro** | Working photographers | $99/mo or $999/yr | 5 TB, RAW, client galleries with watermarks, Lightroom plugin, shared albums, face grouping, 1,000 AI searches/mo, 30-day trash, original-quality downloads | $66.27/mo | $32.73 (33%) |

*Cost at cap = storage + AI search + payment fees (3% + $0.30). Annual margins work out similarly: Plus about 59%, Family about 28%, Pro about 21%.

**Why these splits**
- Each tier is built for one group. Shared albums are the step from Plus to Family. Client galleries, the Lightroom plugin and the 5 TB ceiling are what Pro buyers pay for.
- Pro is not priced from storage cost alone. At full 5 TB, storage is $60/mo, so a $24.99 price would lose money. Pro's price rests on the photographer's need, and they are the group most likely to pay for it. Storage is the largest cost in every tier, so check it again if the price moves.
- No free tier. A free user at a 15 GB cap still costs about $0.18/mo in storage, with no limit on how many stay. A 30-day trial of Plus caps the cost per trial user at about $0.90.
- Discounts are limited to annual billing. No blanket discounts.

**Assumed:** search volumes per plan (the caps above); payment fees; $0.012/GB as the all-in storage cost with no extra charges for redundancy, egress or face-grouping compute; buyers sit below their caps. The prices and the value each group sees are unvalidated. Run range questions or a paid pilot with 10–20 people per group before launch.

**Would change if:**
- Face grouping or ingest compute adds meaningful per-GB cost. The Family and Pro margins would shrink.
- Pro users run around 10,000 AI searches/mo. That is about $30 of search cost and would cut Pro's margin to around 6% at cap.
- Pilot buyers show Family pays under about $7/mo or Pro under about $60/mo.

Plan blurbs and tier names should go through offers-and-value-propositions.
