### What goes in each

**Plus** (casual shooters)
- Auto backup (phone photos and videos)
- RAW backup (counts against storage, no extra gate)
- Face grouping
- Shared albums
- 30-day trash
- Original-quality downloads

**Family** (everything in Plus, plus:)
- Shared family library for up to 6 people, with one pooled quota
- Unlimited shared albums
- Per-person face grouping inside the shared library
- Higher search allowance

**Pro 2 TB / Pro 5 TB** (everything in Family, plus:)
- Client galleries with watermarks
- Lightroom plugin
- Largest search allowance and priority search
- Full RAW handling for search and previews

### Why I drew the lines here

- **RAW backup stays on every plan.** It's just storage, and the quota already limits it. Gating it would hurt conversion from Plus to Pro.
- **Client galleries and the Lightroom plugin are Pro-only.** They're the features working photographers pay for, and they don't add much cost.
- **Shared library is Family-only.** Shared albums go to Plus so casual users can share without needing a family plan.

## Things to decide before launch

1. **Pro pricing is the hardest part.** At $0.012/GB, a 5 TB user costs you $60/month just to store files. Pricing at about $20 per TB keeps you near 40% gross margin, which is why the Pro tiers sit higher than the market. Adobe's 1 TB photography plan is roughly $20/month, so a 2 TB plan at $39.99 will get compared to that. Consider a 1 TB Pro tier at $19.99 for lighter photographers, or check whether you can get a cheaper cold tier for originals older than a set age. Fast search and galleries still need hot storage, so only move originals you rarely touch.

2. **Family at full quota is your thinnest margin.** If that's a concern, drop Family to 300 GB (matches your beta range) or raise the price to $12.99.

3. **Trash counts toward quota.** The 30-day trash can add 5–10% storage overhead, so the cost numbers above are slightly low.

4. **Over-quota behavior.** Don't delete or lock originals when someone goes over. Pause backup, notify them, and offer the next tier. Deleting originals is the fastest way to lose beta trust.

5. **Free tier (optional).** A 5 GB free tier costs $0.06 per user per month and could help acquisition. It's not in the plan above because you didn't ask for one.

These numbers are my arithmetic from the costs you gave me. I haven't checked them against your actual usage data, so validate the storage-per-user distribution in your beta before you commit to prices.
