## What goes in each plan

**Free** is the on-ramp for casual shooters.
- Auto backup, 30-day trash, original-quality downloads.
- Limit it to 5 GB, because it's cheap to carry and converts well.

**Plus** is for the casual phone shooter, who is mostly under 20 GB.
- Everything in Free, plus face grouping, 50 AI searches a month, and basic shared albums.
- 100 GB is generous for this group. It makes the plan feel like it "just works" and keeps your cost low at about $1.20 worst case.

**Family** is for 3–6 people sharing one library.
- Everything in Plus, plus shared albums with member roles and one pooled 500 GB.
- It also includes 300 pooled AI searches and RAW support, since some family members will shoot RAW.
- 500 GB gives headroom over the 300 GB top end of your range. Worst case is about $6.90, a 42% margin.

**Pro 2 TB / Pro 5 TB** are for working photographers.
- Everything in Family, plus client galleries with watermarks, the Lightroom plugin, and 1,000 AI searches a month for fast search.
- Photographers with 1 TB still land on the 2 TB plan. Their unused headroom is where your margin comes from.
- Worst-case margin is 31% for the 2 TB plan and 29% for the 5 TB plan, including searches. If you want more cushion, price the 5 TB plan at $99.

## Decisions behind this

- **Gate on what's valuable, not on what's costly.** Client galleries, the Lightroom plugin, and heavy search are what photographers pay for, so they go in Pro. Storage is your main cost, so quota is the main lever between tiers.
- **Never gate original-quality downloads or trash.** For a backup product, holding people's own files hostage destroys trust. Every tier, including Free, gets them.
- **Cap AI search rather than offering unlimited.** At $0.003 a search, a power user running 5,000 searches costs $15. Add a search pack (for example, 1,000 extra searches for $3) instead of raising the plan price.
- **Sell extra storage in add-ons.** Offer 1 TB for about $17 a month on Pro. That covers your $12 cost with a margin, and heavy shooters don't push you into a plan you can't price.
- **Offer annual billing.** Two months free improves cash flow and retention. It still leaves positive margins.

## Things to check before launch

1. **App-store fees.** If people subscribe through iOS or Android, take 15–30% off the margins above. Plus is the most exposed, and you may want $3.99 there. Web checkout avoids the fee.
2. **Real usage data from the beta.** Check how full each segment's quota actually gets. If Family members average 150 GB, you could add features or cut price and still keep margin.
3. **Egress and processing costs.** You gave me storage and search only. Bandwidth for client galleries and downloads, RAW previews, and face grouping all have costs. Pro client galleries in particular can generate a lot of traffic.
4. **Free-tier abuse.** Require email verification and rate-limit uploads. Free costs almost nothing per user, but bulk account creation can still add up.
5. **Price testing.** Beta photographers will tell you whether $39 for 2 TB feels right. Compare it with what they pay now before locking it in.
