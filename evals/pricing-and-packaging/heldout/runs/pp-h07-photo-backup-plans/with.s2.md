## Recommendation

Charge for storage, because that is what costs you money and what each group feels. Gate the plans on the feature that group pays for. Every price below is a starting point to test, not a measured willingness to pay.

| Plan | For | Price / mo | Included | What the plan below it lacks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | Trying it | $0 | 5 GB, auto backup, 30-day trash, original-quality downloads | n/a |
| **Personal** | Casual phone shooters | **$2.99** (test $2–4) | 100 GB, face grouping, AI search capped at 50/mo | More than 5 GB, face grouping, AI search |
| **Family** | 3–6 people sharing one library | **$9.99** (test $7.99–12.99) | 500 GB shared, up to 6 members, shared albums, AI search capped at 300/mo | Multiple members, shared albums |
| **Pro** | Working photographers | **$29** (test $29–49) | 1 TB, client galleries with watermarks, Lightroom plugin, RAW support, AI search capped at 2,000/mo | Client galleries, Lightroom plugin, RAW support |

Extra storage is sold per block on Family and Pro:
- Family: $2.50 per 100 GB.
- Pro: $20 per TB, so 3 TB costs $69 and 5 TB costs $109.

An annual option at 10 months' price (about 17% off) is worth offering. I'd only commit to that discount once you know how long monthly subscribers actually stay.

## Arithmetic

Cost per user is storage at $0.012/GB plus AI search at $0.003 per search, both at the plan's cap. I left out payment fees, which I assumed at about 3% plus $0.30.

| Plan | Typical cost | Worst case (full plan, all searches used) | Margin at worst case, before fees |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0.06 (5 GB) | $0.06 | n/a |
| Personal | $0.24 (20 GB) + $0.05 | $1.20 + $0.15 = $1.35 | $1.64 |
| Family | $2.10 (175 GB) + $0.30 | $6.00 + $0.90 = $6.90 | $3.09 |
| Pro, 1 TB | $12 + about $1.50 | $12 + $6 = $18 | $11 |
| Pro, 3 TB | $36 + about $1.50 | $36 + $6 = $42 | $27 |

- **Free tier:** A free user costs 6 cents a month at the 5 GB cap, and the cap stops it from covering what Personal buyers want. It has no face grouping or AI search, so the cost stays at 6 cents.
- **Personal:** It is profitable even if a casual user fills the whole 100 GB. Most will use under 20 GB, so the real margin is much higher.
- **Family:** I sized it at 500 GB, not 1 TB. A full 1 TB costs you $12, which is more than the $9.99 price. Your families use 50–300 GB, so 500 GB leaves headroom.
- **Pro:** The price comes from what a client gallery and the Lightroom plugin are worth to someone who earns money from their photos. The cost only sets the floor. Storage over the first TB is priced at $20 against your $12 cost, so heavy RAW users pay for what they use.

## Questions for the team

- **AI search caps:** At $0.003 a search, an uncapped heavy user on Personal could cost more than they pay. The caps are the cheapest protection. Show them as "fair use" limits and review them once you see real usage.
- **RAW support:** I assumed this means RAW previews and processing. Storing RAW files is just storage, so I wouldn't block it on lower plans.

Assumed: the prices, the typical usage figures (20 GB Personal, 175 GB Family), the search caps and the payment fee rate. I had no evidence of willingness to pay, and I know of no competitor prices.

Would change if:
- Photographer pilots won't pay $29, or happily pay $49 or more. Run 5–10 paid pilots in the Pro range before launch.
- Beta usage shows Personal users averaging well over 20 GB, or searching far more than the cap.
- Families mostly sit near 300 GB, which would justify a 1 TB Family plan at about $14.99.
