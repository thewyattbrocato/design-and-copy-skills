## Recommendation

Four plans, each built for one beta group. The free tier is a funnel for the casual group.

| Plan | For | Price (monthly) | Storage | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | Casual shooters trying it out | $0 | 5 GB | Auto backup, 30-day trash, original-quality downloads |
| **Personal** | Casual phone shooters | **$2.99** | 100 GB | Everything in Free, plus face grouping, shared albums, AI search (100/mo) |
| **Family** | Shared libraries, 3–6 people | **$9.99** | 500 GB shared, up to 6 members | Everything in Personal, plus a shared library with per-member accounts and AI search (300/mo pooled) |
| **Pro** | Working photographers | **$45** | 2 TB, then $20 per extra TB (up to 10 TB) | Everything in Family, plus RAW support, client galleries with watermarks, the Lightroom plugin, and fast AI search (1,000/mo fair use) |

Annual billing is 10× the monthly price (two months free) on the paid plans.

## The arithmetic

I've used your $0.012/GB and $0.003/search. Payment fees are assumed at about 3% + $0.30. I've counted 1 TB as 1,000 GB.

| Plan | Worst-case cost (full storage and search) | Price | Margin at worst case | Margin at typical use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0.06 (5 GB, no search) | $0 | −$0.06 | n/a |
| Personal | $1.20 storage + $0.30 search + $0.39 fees = **$1.89** | $2.99 | $1.10 (37%) | About $2.20 at 20 GB |
| Family | $6.00 + $1.80 + $0.60 = **$8.40** | $9.99 | $1.59 (16%) | About $6.50 at 175 GB |
| Pro, 2 TB | $24 + $3 + $1.60 = **$28.60** | $45 | $16.40 (36%) | Higher, since many users sit near 1 TB |
| Pro, 5 TB | $60 + $3 + $3.40 = **$66.40** | $105 | $38.60 (37%) | n/a |

- **Free tier:** It costs at most $0.06 per user per month. The 5 GB cap is below what any of your three groups needs, so it can't replace a paid plan. Search is excluded to keep the cost fixed. Keep it only if conversion to Personal justifies it. At that cost, 1,000 free users is $60 a month.
- **Storage is the charging unit.** Photographers' costs run from $12 to $60 a month on storage alone, and that is the only cost that moves much. A flat price would lose money on 5 TB users or overcharge 1 TB users. The $20/TB add-on costs you about $12.50 and earns about 37%.
- **Pro range:** The price band is $39–59 for the base plan. Client galleries, RAW and the Lightroom plugin tie the plan to how photographers earn money, so they're priced against that value, not against storage. One extra booking a month probably covers the whole plan. That's why Pro isn't priced at cost plus a small markup.
- **Family is the thinnest margin** at full use, but families sit at 50–300 GB, so the typical margin is healthy. If the average family exceeds 300 GB, move Family to $12.99 or cut the included search.
- **Personal range:** $2.99–3.99. I picked the low end because casual users are the most price-sensitive and cost almost nothing to serve.
- **Why each tier differs from the one below:**
  - Personal adds face grouping, search and sharing.
  - Family adds multiple members on a shared library.
  - Pro adds RAW, client galleries, the plugin and 4× the storage.
- **Original-quality downloads are in every tier.** Locking people's own photos behind a paywall would hurt trust more than it earns.
- **Annual billing:** The 17% discount is a placeholder. Check it against how long monthly subscribers actually stay before launch. Annual should save you fees and churn, not just give money away.

**Assumed:** Payment fees of 3% + $0.30. Typical use of 20 GB for casual users, 175 GB for families and about 1.5 TB for photographers. I don't know how much AI search each group really uses. I also don't know what rival services charge, since you didn't give that and I haven't guessed it.

**Would change if:**
- Photographers in the beta say $45 is too high, or say they'd pay $70 or more. Ask 5–10 of them for a price range, with a "why" after each answer.
- Beta data shows real search volume above the caps.
- The average Family library is above 300 GB.
- Free users convert to Personal at a rate that doesn't cover their cost.
