## Unit economics

This assumes payment fees of about 3%. It does not include app-store cuts or other overhead.

| Plan | Cost at full quota | Cost at typical use | Net revenue | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plus | $1.20 + $0.30 search = $1.50 | 20 GB: about $0.30 | about $2.60 | about 42% at full, about 88% typical |
| Family | $6.00 + $0.90 = $6.90 | 150 GB: about $2.50 | about $9.40 | about 27% at full, about 73% typical |
| Pro | $24 + $6 = $30 | 1.2 TB: about $16 | about $37.60 | about 20% at full, about 57% typical |
| Studio | $60 + $15 = $75 | 3.5 TB: about $50 | about $86 | about 13% at full, about 40% typical |

Search cost is $0.003 per query, so the caps are what protect your margin. A fully used 5,000-search Studio quota costs you $15. Treat the caps as fair-use limits that throttle or prompt an upgrade, not hard walls.

## Why this shape

- **Free is 5 GB, not 15.** Your casual users mostly sit under 20 GB. A small free tier costs about $0.06 per user per month and pushes them to Plus when they outgrow it.
- **Plus at 100 GB is a no-brainer upgrade** at $2.99, and it's nearly all margin because most users will use 20 GB or less.
- **Family stops at 500 GB, not 1 TB.** Your beta families use 50–300 GB, so 500 GB gives headroom. A 1 TB family plan at $9.99 would lose money for anyone who fills it.
- **Studio is the weakest margin.** I'd keep it, since 5 TB users exist in your beta and the add-on price covers them. But don't discount it. If it shows low uptake, replace it with Pro plus add-ons.
- **Gate by feature, not by trust.** Original-quality downloads and trash stay on every plan. Gating them on a backup product feels hostile and hurts word of mouth. RAW, client galleries, and the Lightroom plugin are where photographers see value, so they go on the Pro tiers.
- **RAW on Family** is a judgment call. It costs you nothing extra beyond storage, which the quota already limits, and it helps hobbyist shooters in families. You could move it to Pro-only if you want a sharper upgrade line.

## Caveats to check before launch

1. **App-store fees:** Apple and Google take 15–30% on in-app subscriptions. At 30%, Pro and Studio margins at full quota go near zero or negative. Consider web checkout, or a higher price on mobile.
2. **My usage assumptions are guesses.** Replace the "typical use" numbers with real percentiles from your beta. In particular, check the share of Pro users above 3 TB, and how many searches people actually run.
3. **Egress and processing costs are not included.** Thumbnails, face grouping, gallery bandwidth, and downloads cost money beyond $0.012 per GB. Client galleries could be a real cost for photographers.
4. **Test Pro at $39 against $29 and $49** with beta photographers. Their willingness to pay is probably the least certain number here.
