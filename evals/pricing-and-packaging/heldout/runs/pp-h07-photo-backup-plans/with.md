## Recommendation: four plans, one per group, and storage is the charging unit

| Plan | Price (monthly) | For | Includes | What the plan below it lacks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | Casual shooters trying it | 5 GB, auto backup, 30-day trash, no AI search | n/a |
| **Personal** | $2.99 | Casual phone shooters | 100 GB, plus face grouping, original-quality downloads, 20 AI searches/mo | Free lacks the room for a phone library, face grouping and AI search |
| **Family** | $7.99 | 3–6 people, one library | 400 GB shared, up to 6 members, shared albums, 100 AI searches/mo | Personal lacks shared albums and multiple members |
| **Pro** | $29, including 1 TB | Working photographers | RAW support, client galleries with watermarks, Lightroom plugin, 1,000 AI searches/mo | Family lacks RAW, client galleries and the Lightroom plugin |

Extra storage:
- **Family:** $2.50 per extra 100 GB.
- **Pro:** $17 per extra TB, up to 5 TB.

Annual billing: about 15% off, paid up front. Treat that as a placeholder until you see how long monthly subscribers actually stay.

## The arithmetic (cost = $0.012/GB/mo plus $0.003/search, before payment fees)

| Plan | Cost if the user fills it | Revenue | Margin at full use | Typical user |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 5 GB = $0.06 | $0 | –$0.06 | n/a |
| Personal | 100 GB + 20 searches = $1.26 | $2.99 | $1.73 (58%) | A 10 GB user costs about $0.12, so about 96% margin |
| Family | 400 GB + 100 searches = $5.10 | $7.99 | $2.89 (36%) | A 150 GB family costs about $2.10, so about 74% margin |
| Pro, 1 TB | $12 + $3 of searches = $15 | $29 | $14 (48%) | n/a |
| Pro, 3 TB | $36 + $3 = $39 | $63 | $24 (38%) | n/a |
| Pro, 5 TB | $60 + $3 = $63 | $97 | $34 (35%) | n/a |

- **Why storage is the unit:** it's the cost that grows with each customer, buyers can predict it, and it tracks value for photographers. A flat "unlimited" Pro price would lose money on a 5 TB user, whose storage alone costs $60 a month.
- **Pro price range:** $24–$35 for the 1 TB base. Below about $24 the margin gets thin once fees and support are counted. Above $35 you're pricing near what photographers pay for a gallery tool alone, which you should check first.
- **Search caps:** the caps hold the worst-case AI search cost to $3 on Pro and $0.30 on Family.
- **Free tier:** at $0.06 per user a month, it's cheap enough to carry. The 5 GB limit is below what a phone library needs, so it forces an upgrade instead of replacing Personal. I'd delete free accounts after about 90 days of inactivity.
- **Billing channel:** bill on the web if you can. Mobile app-store fees of 15–30% would cut the Family margin to about 20–25% at full use.

Assumed:
- Payment fees of about 3%, plus support costs, are small next to storage cost.
- No other costs per user.
- Family storage averages well under the cap, as your beta range of 50–300 GB suggests.
- Photographers weigh client galleries and the Lightroom plugin about as much as the storage itself.
- I haven't checked these prices against what your users pay today for iCloud, Google One, Dropbox or a gallery tool. Those are the real alternatives they'll compare you to.

Would change if:
- Beta photographers say $29 is too high. Then test $24 for a 1 TB base, with add-ons at $17.
- Family usage clusters near 400 GB. Then raise the price or lower the cap.
- The beta shows Personal users rarely pass 20 GB. Then cut Personal to 50 GB at $1.99.
- Free users convert at under a couple of percent. Then replace the free tier with a 30-day trial.
