I can't tell you the prices are right without your per-page crawl cost, but I'd fix several things before Monday.

**1. Agency is probably underwater.**
- If cost scales with pages checked, frequency matters as much as page count. 100,000 pages checked hourly is about 72M page-checks a month, if each check recrawls every page.
- Team's 5,000 pages daily is about 150K page-checks a month.
- Agency is therefore roughly 480x Team's crawl load for 13x the price.
- Either "hourly" applies only to a subset of pages, or you need to cap it. Options are hourly for key pages only, or daily or 6-hourly for the full set. Otherwise one heavy Agency customer could cost you more than $249.

**2. Starter doesn't work for your main audience.**
- Most signups are freelancers with 2 to 5 client sites, and Starter allows 1 site.
- It's also nearly the same as Free: same sites and pages, only weekly instead of monthly checks, plus a user seat.
- Those freelancers skip Starter and go straight to Team. Team's price is fine for them, but you've built a tier nobody in your core segment can use.
- Consider making Starter 3 sites and about 500 pages, or dropping it.

**3. Team is very cheap for what it includes.**
- It has 50x Starter's pages and daily checks (7x the frequency) for 2.1x the price.
- That's your most likely plan, and it's priced like a loss leader. I'd test $29 to $39, since freelancers can pass the cost to clients.

**4. The 55% annual discount is too deep, and the "limited time" label is a problem.**
- Annual Team works out to about $8.55/mo, which is cheaper than monthly Starter. Annual Starter is about $4/mo.
- Typical annual discounts are 15 to 20% ("2 months free"). 55% gives away a lot of margin on plans that may already be thin.
- A "limited time" offer that has run for two years is a false urgency claim. It can draw FTC or state-level scrutiny in the US, and it can breach consumer-protection rules in the UK and EU. Either make it a permanent, plainly stated discount or run a real promotion with an end date.

**Smaller point:** the gap from $19 to $249 is steep. An in-between tier, say 15 sites and 25K pages daily for about $79, would catch growing freelancers and small agencies.
