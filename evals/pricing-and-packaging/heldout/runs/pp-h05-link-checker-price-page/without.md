Short answer: no, I wouldn't ship these as they are. Some problems are legal or trust risks, and others are about margin.

**1. Fix before Monday: the "limited time" claim.**
A "limited time" offer that has run for two years is fake urgency. It can count as a deceptive pricing practice (FTC rules, UK CMA, and EU consumer law all cover this), and savvy buyers will notice. Either make the discount permanent and say so, or set a real end date and honor it.

**2. 55% off annual is too deep.**
It works out to about 5.4 months' price for 12 months of service. The usual range is 15–20% (about 2 months free). Your costs grow with usage, so a big upfront discount also locks in low revenue from your heaviest users. Annual plans are meant to cut churn, and that doesn't take 55%.

**3. Hourly checks on 100k pages could lose money.**
If each check re-crawls everything, the monthly crawl volumes are:

| Plan | Crawls per month |
|---|---|
| Starter | about 400 |
| Team | about 150,000 |
| Agency | about 72,000,000 |

Agency is 480 times Team's volume for 13 times the price. Even at a tiny cost per page, one customer using the full allowance could cost more than $249. Plug in your real cost per 1,000 page-crawls. If you don't do full re-crawls (sampling, incremental checks), say so, or cap the frequency or page count. Otherwise Agency's margin depends on customers not using what they bought.

**4. Starter is weak.**
It's identical to Free except weekly instead of monthly checks and a user seat. Nobody who needs 2–5 sites can use it, because it allows 1 site. In practice it's a decoy that pushes your main audience to Team. That can work on purpose, but then:

**5. Team at $19 is probably underpriced.**
Your typical customer is a freelancer with 2–5 client sites. For them, Team gives daily checks, 5,000 pages, and 5 sites for $19. They'd happily pay $29–49, since they bill clients for this. Starter is only $10 less, and Team is 50 times the pages for about twice the price, so the ladder isn't consistent.

**6. Gap between Team and Agency.**
There's nothing between 5 sites at $19 and 50 sites at $249. A freelancer growing to 8–15 clients has no good option. Consider a mid tier at around $79–99 with 15–20 sites.

**A sketch to test, not a final answer:**
- Free: 1 site, 100 pages, monthly
- Starter: about $12, 1 site, 500 pages, weekly
- Team: about $39, 5 sites, 5,000 pages, daily
- Business: about $99, 20 sites, 25,000 pages, daily
- Agency: priced off your real hourly crawl cost, or limit hourly checks to a smaller page subset
- Annual: 2 months free, stated plainly
