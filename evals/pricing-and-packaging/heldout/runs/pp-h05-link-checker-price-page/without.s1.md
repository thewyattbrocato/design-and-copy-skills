Short answer: no, I wouldn't ship these as they are. The biggest issues are below, most important first.

**1. Agency's hourly checks may lose money.**
Your cost scales with pages checked, so frequency matters as much as page count.
- Team at 5,000 pages daily is about 150k page-crawls a month for $19.
- Agency at 100,000 pages hourly is up to 72M page-crawls a month for $249.
- That's about 480x the crawl volume for 13x the price. Per crawl, Agency is roughly 36x cheaper than Team.

Few customers would use the full allowance, but the ones who do would be your most expensive. Either cap hourly checks to a subset of pages, make hourly an add-on, or add a fair-use limit. Run the numbers on your real cost per crawl before Monday.

**2. Your core customer fits only one plan.**
Freelancers with 2 to 5 sites can't use Starter (1 site), so they all land on Team. That's fine, but Team at $19 is probably underpriced for what it gives them: 5 sites, daily checks and 10 users they won't use. Since this is your main segment, I'd test $29 to $39. Freelancers bill clients for this kind of monitoring, so they're not very price-sensitive.

**3. Free and Starter look too similar.**
Both have 1 site and 100 pages. Starter only adds weekly checks and a user seat. That's thin for $9. Consider adding something clearly valuable, like email alerts, exports or history, or 3 sites.

**4. There's a gap between Team and Agency.**
Going from $19 to $249 is a 13x jump. A freelancer who grows to 6 sites faces a big price jump. A $79 to $99 tier with about 15 to 20 sites would catch them.

**5. The annual discount is risky.**
- 55% off is far above the usual 15 to 20% (about 2 months free). Annual Team works out to about $8.55 a month, which is less than monthly Starter. That undercuts your own ladder.
- "Limited time" on a discount that has run for two years is false urgency. Regulators in the US, UK and EU have acted on this kind of claim. Drop the wording and make it the standard annual price, or set a real end date.
- If you keep a deep discount, check that the annual price still covers your crawl costs for the full year, since you can't adjust it mid-term.

**Suggested shape (to test, not final):**
- Free: 1 site, 100 pages, monthly
- Starter $9: 1 site, 500 pages, weekly
- Team $29 to $39: 5 sites, 5,000 pages, daily
- Business $89 or so: 20 sites, 25,000 pages, daily
- Agency $249: 50 sites, 100,000 pages, with hourly limited to priority pages
- Annual: about 20% off (2 months free), with no "limited time" wording
