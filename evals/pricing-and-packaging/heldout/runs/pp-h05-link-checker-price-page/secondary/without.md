**Short answer:** The monthly prices are reasonable for the 1-to-5-site freelancer segment, but three things need fixing before Monday. The Agency tier is likely underpriced for what it includes, "limited time" is a legal risk after two years, and Starter barely differs from Free.

## 1. Price doesn't track your cost

Your cost scales with page-checks (pages × check frequency), not pages alone. At full cap, each tier works out to:

| Tier | Price | Page-checks/mo at cap | Price per 1,000 page-checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 100 | n/a |
| Starter | $9 | ~433 (100 × weekly) | $20.78 |
| Team | $19 | 150,000 (5,000 × daily) | $0.13 |
| Agency | $249 | 72,000,000 (100,000 × hourly) | $0.0035 |

Per check, Starter costs about 160x what Team does, and Team costs about 37x what Agency does. Agency is the problem. Hourly checks on 100,000 pages is 72M checks a month for $249. It only breaks even if your cost is under about $0.0035 per 1,000 checks. Team breaks even at about $0.13 per 1,000. Real usage will sit below caps, but Agency is the tier most likely to be bought by someone who runs it hard.

**Suggested fix:** Keep the Agency price, but don't offer hourly on the full 100,000 pages. Either make Agency daily-only, or allow hourly on a smaller page subset (for example, 10,000 pages hourly, the rest daily).

## 2. Drop "limited time" or make it true

A "limited time" offer that has run for two years is fake urgency. That can be deceptive under FTC Act §5 and state consumer protection laws. The UK's DMCC Act 2024 bans false "limited time" claims outright. The "55% off" also needs a real reference price. If nobody actually pays the undiscounted annual rate, the strike-through is misleading.

Options:
- A permanent annual discount with no urgency language. Two months free is about 17% and is easy to defend.
- A real promotion with an end date.

At 55% off, annual pricing would be Starter $48.60/yr, Team $102.60/yr, and Agency $1,344.60/yr ($112/mo). Annual Agency buyers are the costliest per dollar, so the discount is steepest where you can least afford it. Check the wording with whoever handles your terms. I'm not a lawyer.

## 3. Starter is barely above Free

Starter and Free have the same site and page limits. The only differences are weekly checks and $9. Then Team jumps to 5x sites and 50x pages for about 2x the price. Most of your 2-to-5-site freelancers will skip Starter and go straight to Team, so Starter mostly serves single-site hobbyists.

**Options:** Raise Starter's pages (for example, to 500) so it's a real step up, or keep it as a cheap entry point and say so in the copy.
