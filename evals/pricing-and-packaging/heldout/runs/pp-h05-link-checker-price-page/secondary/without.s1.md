**Short answer: not yet.** The Agency tier is the main problem, and the annual promo needs a change before launch.

**1. Agency is badly underpriced for a crawl-cost business.** Your cost scales with pages checked, so compare the maximum volume each tier allows:

| Tier | Price | Max page-checks/month | Price per 1,000 page-checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $9 | ~430 (100 pages, weekly) | ~$21 |
| Team | $19 | 150,000 (5,000 pages, daily) | ~$0.13 |
| Agency | $249 | 72,000,000 (100,000 pages, hourly) | ~$0.003 |

Agency allows about 480x Team's crawl volume for about 13x the price. A customer who uses it fully could cost you far more than they pay. Before launch, either:
- lower Agency's page or frequency limits (for example, hourly only for a smaller page count), or
- raise the price, or
- cap per-page check frequency so the worst case stays within your margin.

I don't have your per-page crawl cost, so I can't say exactly where the break-even is. If you share it, I can work out the margin at each tier's limits.

**2. The Team limits are ambiguous.** "5 sites, 5,000 pages" could mean 5,000 total or 1,000 per site. Your target customer (2 to 5 client sites) lands on Team, so this is the tier that matters most. Make the wording explicit.

**3. Starter is hard to justify.**
- It has the same 100 pages as Free and only adds weekly checks. Anyone who wants the extra frequency is likely to want more than one site.
- Freelancers with 2 to 5 sites can't use it, so they'll go straight to Team. That makes Starter mostly a trap for people who would have paid $19 anyway.
- Consider raising Starter's page limit (for example, 500 to 1,000 pages) so it's clearly better than Free, or fold it into Free and Team.

**4. There's a gap between Team and Agency.** Going from $19 to $249 is a big jump with 5 to 50 sites and 10 users to unlimited. A mid tier around $49 to $79 for 10 to 20 sites would catch growing freelancers and small agencies, and it would protect the upper end.

**5. The "limited time" annual discount is a risk.** At 55% off, annual Team is $102.60 a year (about $8.55 a month). A promo that has run for two years isn't a limited-time offer, and "for a limited time" is the kind of false urgency that consumer-protection rules increasingly target. Either make the annual discount permanent and state it plainly, or end it on a real date. A plain-language discount (for example, "2 months free") would also be easier to defend.

**What I'd change before Monday:**
- Fix Agency's limits or price (the biggest risk).
- Clarify whether Team's 5,000 pages are total or per site.
- Rework or remove Starter, or give it a clear reason to exist.
- Drop the "limited time" wording from the annual discount.
