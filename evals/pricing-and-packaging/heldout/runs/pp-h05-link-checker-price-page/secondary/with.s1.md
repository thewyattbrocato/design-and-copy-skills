**Verdict: the Free, Starter and Team prices are reasonable. Agency is the one most likely to lose money, and the plan gap between Team and Agency is too wide. Fix the annual "limited time" claim before Monday.**

**Why Agency is the risk.** Your cost scales with page-checks (pages × checks per month), not pages alone:

| Plan | Max page-checks/mo | Price | $ per 1,000 checks (at max) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 100 × 1 = 100 | $0 | $0 |
| Starter | 100 × ~4.3 = ~430 | $9 | ~$21 |
| Team | 5,000 × 30 = 150,000 | $19 | ~$0.13 |
| Agency | 100,000 × 720 = 72,000,000 | $249 | ~$0.0035 |

Agency at full use is 480 times the crawling of Team for 13 times the price. It only stays profitable if your cost per 1,000 checks is below about $0.0035 at full use. Check that number against your actual crawl cost before launch. If it's higher, cap hourly checks to a smaller page set, or charge per check above a threshold.

**The Team-to-Agency gap.** Going from 5,000 pages to 100,000 and from daily to hourly, with no tier between, leaves a large group with no plan that fits. Add one middle tier, for example about 20 sites, 25,000 pages, daily checks. Set its price from your per-check cost, not a rule of thumb. At assumed cost, that tier should land somewhere in the $50 to $100 range. This is the number I'd most want you to verify.

**Segments.** Freelancers with 2 to 5 sites don't fit Free (1 site) or Starter (1 site), so they will land on Team. Team is the plan that matters most for your stated signups, and $19 for 5 sites is a sensible anchor. Starter is essentially "one site, weekly checks, one user", which is a real but small group. Keep it, and say that is its buyer in the plan description.

**Annual billing.** A discount labeled "for a limited time" that has run for two years is a misleading claim. Either make it a standing annual price or remove the label. 55% off is also deep for a plan whose costs grow with use: Team annual would be $102.60 a year. A standard default would be about two months free (around 17%), but that is assumed. Set the real figure from how long monthly customers stay and how many choose annual.

**Summary of changes**
1. Verify Agency's cost per 1,000 checks; cap hourly or meter it if the number is above the breakeven.
2. Add a middle tier between Team and Agency.
3. Remove the "limited time" label or end the 55% discount, and set a standard annual discount.

**Assumed:** your crawl cost per 1,000 checks is unknown to me, so every Agency and middle-tier conclusion depends on it. I also assumed Starter's buyer is a solo user with one site who wants weekly checks, and that monthly customers' retention is typical for this kind of tool.

**Would change if:** the real crawl cost per check is well above the Agency breakeven, which would make Agency a loss-maker at full use; or if most Agency buyers only use a fraction of their 100,000 pages, which would make the current price workable.
