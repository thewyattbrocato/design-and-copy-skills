**Short answer: not yet.** Team at $19 fits your freelancers. The other three need changes before Monday, most of all the Agency tier and the annual offer.

| Tier | Price | Checks/month at full allowance* | Price per 1,000 checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 100 | $0 |
| Starter | $9 | 430 (100 pages, weekly) | about $21 |
| Team | $19 | 150,000 (5,000 pages, daily) | about $0.13 |
| Agency | $249 | 72,000,000 (100,000 pages, hourly) | about $0.004 |

*Pages × checks per month. Since your cost scales with pages checked, the price per check varies about 6,000-fold across tiers.

**1. Agency is the biggest risk.** It costs 13× Team but allows 480× the checks at full use. If an Agency account uses its full allowance, your crawl cost could exceed its price. Also, nothing sits between $19 and $249, and your signups are mostly freelancers. Options:
- Add a middle tier for small agencies, priced on pages checked.
- Or make Agency "talk to us," priced on pages and hourly checks.
- Either way, cap hourly checks by page count, or make hourly an add-on.

**2. Starter vs Free is a thin fence.** Both have 1 site and 100 pages; Starter only checks weekly instead of monthly. Your real fence is the 1-site limit, which pushes 2–5 site freelancers to Team. To give Starter a clear reason to exist, add a feature it has and Free lacks, such as alerts or exports, or raise its page limit. Then check that cost against the page-check math above.

**3. The annual offer is priced too deep, and "limited time" is untrue.** 55% off means annual buyers pay 5.4 months for 12:
- Starter: $48.60/yr ($4.05/mo)
- Team: $102.60/yr ($8.55/mo)
- Agency: $1,344.60/yr ($112.05/mo)

Annual only pays if buyers would otherwise stay under about 5.4 months, and people who expect to stay will choose it. A standard annual discount, such as 2 months free (about 17% off, $190/yr for Team), is a safer default. Remove "limited time" or end the offer. A promotion that has run for two years is a false-urgency claim, so have someone check the copy against consumer-protection rules.

**Assumed:** one check is one page fetch; cost is roughly linear in checks; "hourly" applies to all pages, not a subset; a month is 30 days; you have no data on how long monthly buyers stay.

**Would change if:** real crawl cost per check is far above or below what's assumed; Agency buyers exist and would pay more for hourly checks; or annual buyers stay about 12 months, which would support a deeper annual discount.
