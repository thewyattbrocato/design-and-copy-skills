Don't launch these as they are. The biggest problem is that the tiers don't scale with your cost, and the annual offer has a credibility problem.

## 1. Frequency multiplies page volume, so Agency is a cost trap

Your cost grows with pages checked, but the tiers let frequency multiply pages. Worst-case checks per month, assuming full use of each tier:

| Tier | Price | Pages | Frequency | Max page-checks/mo | Checks per $ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 100 | monthly | 100 | n/a |
| Starter | $9 | 100 | weekly | ~430 | ~48 |
| Team | $19 | 5,000 | daily | 150,000 | ~7,900 |
| Agency | $249 | 100,000 | hourly | 72,000,000 | ~289,000 |

- Agency's ceiling is about 480× Team's for about 13× the price. Even a modest Agency customer checking 1,000 pages hourly generates 720,000 checks a month for $249.
- Team's ceiling is about 350× Starter's for about 2× the price. A freelancer with 5 sites at 1,000 pages each, checked daily, is 150,000 checks for $19.

Get your actual cost per page-check and calculate the margin at each tier's ceiling. My guess is Team and Agency lose money at full use. The fix is to put a monthly page-check budget on each tier (for example, "X checks/month" with overage pricing), or to cap frequency on the higher pages-per-site tiers. Don't rely on a page count plus a frequency promise alone.

## 2. Your core customer doesn't fit Starter

Most signups are freelancers with 2 to 5 sites. Starter allows 1 site, so anyone with 2+ sites skips it and goes straight to Team at $19. Starter then mainly exists to be a step below Free, which gives you little reason to pay. Free to Starter is only weekly checks and one more user for $9.

Options: raise Starter to 2–3 sites, or drop its user limit and use the price gap for something that matters, like more pages or faster checks. Either way, make sure someone with 3 sites has a clear reason to pick Starter over Team.

## 3. The annual offer

- **"For a limited time" for two years is a problem.** Regulators and consumer-protection agencies look closely at fake urgency. Also, people who notice it will trust the rest of the page less. Either make the discount permanent and drop the phrase, or set a real end date.
- **55% is steep.** At 55% off, Agency's annual price is about $112/mo, which may be below your cost at its ceiling. Something around 15–20% (roughly two months free) is typical. Confirm that "55% off" means off the monthly price, and make that explicit on the page.
