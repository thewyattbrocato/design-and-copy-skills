**These prices aren't ready to ship.** The annual discount and the Team-to-Agency jump are the biggest problems.

**1. Annual billing: 55% off "for a limited time" (fix before Monday)**
- After two years it isn't limited, so the label is false. Either make it the real annual price or end it on a date you actually keep.
- 55% off means 12 months for the price of 5.4. Team becomes about $8.55/mo and Starter about $4.05/mo.
- That only pays off if monthly customers would have stayed under about 5.4 months. Check your real retention first.
- Annual also doesn't lower your crawl cost, because you still crawl all 12 months.
- Suggested replacement: about 15–20% off, or "2 months free" (about 17%), shown as a permanent annual price. Check it against your actual monthly churn.

**2. Your main buyers fit only Team.** Freelancers with 2–5 client sites can't use Starter, which allows 1 site. That makes Team the real product for them, and it's priced at $19.
- If a freelancer bills clients even $25/mo each for link monitoring, 3.5 sites is about $87/mo of value. $19 captures roughly a fifth of that. (Both figures are assumed.)
- I'd test $29–39 for Team, with a few sign-ups at each price. Check this before moving anything else.

**3. Cost per tier doesn't track price.** You said cost grows with pages checked, so the real driver is pages × checks per month:

| Plan | Page-checks/mo (max) | Price | Price per 1,000 checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | ~430 | $0 | n/a |
| Starter | ~430 | $9 | ~$21 |
| Team | ~150,000 | $19 | ~$0.13 |
| Agency | ~72,000,000 | $249 | ~$0.003 |

- Agency allows about 480× Team's crawl volume for 13× the price. If those customers use their full limits, Agency loses money.
- Either cap hourly checks to a subset of pages, or reprice Agency by volume. Put your cost per 1,000 checks into this table first.
- The same logic applies to Team's daily 5,000-page checks at $19.

**4. Free and Starter are nearly the same product.** Both get 1 site and 100 pages. Starter adds only weekly checks and a user seat (Free's seat count isn't listed).
- Give Starter something real, such as 500 pages. Otherwise people will stay on Free.
- Free costs you crawls too. Write down the cost per free user and keep the limit (100 pages monthly is reasonable).

**5. There's a gap between Team and Agency.** A freelancer with 6–10 sites jumps from $19 to $249. A middle tier (around 15–20 sites) would catch them and make Agency a real step for agencies.

Starter itself is fine for single-site owners. It's just not aimed at your main signups.

**Assumed:** the $25/client billing figure, that customers use their full limits, and that cost scales linearly with page-checks. I don't know your cost per crawl, churn, or how many customers are on each plan.

**Would change if:** your real cost per 1,000 checks makes Team or Agency profitable at current prices (then the cost-side fixes shrink). Also if the Team price test shows freelancers won't pay more than $19.
