- It's also very deep. Annual at 45% of list means 12 months cost 5.4 months of monthly price, so it only breaks even if the average monthly customer stays about 5.4 months or longer. Check your real monthly retention. If it's longer, you're giving away money.

- Freelancers with 2–5 client sites don't fit Free or Starter, which allow 1 site. They land on Team.

| Plan | Page-checks per month | Price per check |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 100 × 4 = 400 | about $0.0225 |
| Team | 5,000 × 30 = 150,000 | about $0.00013 |
| Agency | 100,000 × 720 = 72M | about $0.0000035 |
