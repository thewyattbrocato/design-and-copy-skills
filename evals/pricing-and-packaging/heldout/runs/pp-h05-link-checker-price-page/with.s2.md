**No, I wouldn't launch these as they are.** Four things need fixing, in this order of importance.

**1. Your main customers fall between the plans.**
- Freelancers with 2 to 5 client sites can't use Free or Starter, which are both 1 site. Their only option is Team at $19.
- Starter is therefore a plan for people your signups mostly aren't. The $9 to $19 step buys 5x the sites and 50x the pages, so almost everyone you want will skip to Team.
- Fix: build the freelancer plan on purpose. Make it 3 to 5 sites, weekly or daily checks, and 1 user. Its price should reflect what a freelancer earns from catching a client's broken links, which is probably well above $19. Test $29 to $49 (assumed range, not measured).
- Starter's only other difference from Free is checking weekly instead of monthly, which is thin. If you keep a 1-site paid plan, it needs something real, such as email alerts or reports.

**2. Agency is cheap per crawl, and the jump to it is large.**
- Assuming each check re-crawls every page, the monthly crawl counts are about 150k for Team (5,000 pages daily) and about 72M for Agency (100,000 pages hourly).
- Agency costs 13x Team's price but allows about 480x the crawling, so its price per crawl is roughly 36x lower.
- If your cost grows with pages checked, Agency is where you'd lose money or where one heavy customer could. Either cap hourly checking to a smaller page count, or price on pages crawled per month.
- There is also nothing between $19 and $249. A studio with 8 to 15 sites has nowhere to go. A mid tier at roughly $79 to $99 (assumed) would suit them and make $249 look like a deliberate step up.

**3. 55% off annual is too deep, and "limited time" is untrue.**
- At 55% off you collect about 5.4 months of revenue for 12 months of crawling. Annual Team works out to about $8.55/mo, and annual Agency to about $112/mo.
- A discount that has run for two years isn't limited. It's your real annual price, so calling it a sale is a misleading claim.
- Fix: make it a permanent annual price and say so, with no countdown. Set the size from how long your monthly customers actually stay. If they usually stay more than about 10 months, a discount of 15% to 20% (about 2 months free) costs you little and still rewards prepaying.
- Current annual subscribers keep their rate for a stated period, such as through their next renewal. Say that plainly when you change it.

**4. You haven't tested the price level.**
Cost is mostly crawling, but I don't have your cost per 1,000 page checks. With it I can build a profit table for each tier. Without it I can't say where the floor is, or whether the free plan and Agency are profitable.

**Assumed:** each scheduled check re-crawls every page, freelancers value this well above $19, and no tier has a custom enterprise deal behind it.

**Would change if:**
- Your cost per crawl is tiny, in which case the Agency concern shrinks.
- Freelancers say in calls that $19 is already their ceiling.
- Monthly customers churn within 3 to 4 months, in which case a deeper annual discount is justified.
