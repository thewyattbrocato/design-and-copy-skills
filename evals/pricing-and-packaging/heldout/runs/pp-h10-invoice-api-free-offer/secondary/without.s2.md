Why this mix:

- **A one-time credit is the cheapest way to let people test on real invoices.** A forever free tier is the only option where cost grows with your user base, which is the wrong shape when runway is tight.

- **Price the starter so pages clear your cost.** At 2¢ per page, $10 buys 500 pages at breakeven. Price per page above your cost so the starter has margin.
- **Track free-to-paid conversion for two or three months.** If it's very low, shrink the credit to 25 to 50 pages or cut it. If it's healthy, you have a good reason to keep it.
