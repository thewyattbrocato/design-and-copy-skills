No, $49/month is not too much. It comes to about $0.06 per appointment (40 patients × ~22 days ≈ 880 visits a month), and if one missed visit is worth ~$150 (assumed), recovering a single no-show a month covers the fee about three times over. It's only a concern if the plan caps messages below ~900 a month (reminders plus confirmations could double that), charges per dentist, or if the clinic already gets free reminders from its practice-management software, so check those three things first.
