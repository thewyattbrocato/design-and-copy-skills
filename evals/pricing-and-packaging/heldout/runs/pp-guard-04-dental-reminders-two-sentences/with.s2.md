No, $49/month is very likely not too much. At 40 patients a day, a reminder service only has to prevent about one no-show a month to pay for itself, and a dental visit is worth far more than $49 (assumed, roughly $100–$300 or more per chair-hour). Text costs alone would be about 880 messages a month, or about $7–$10 at typical rates (assumed), so the margin is fine.

It would become expensive if the clinic already gets reminders free through its practice-management software, or if the plan caps messages well below about 900 a month. Check both before buying.
