Marrow & Rye started in 2019, when Dani Okafor and her brother Tunde turned a garage on Pell Street into a bakery. The heart of it is older than the shop: a rye starter Dani has kept going since 2016, which leavens everything we bake. Nothing here rises any other way. Our flour comes from a mill two counties over, and we bake six days a week, closed Mondays. Tunde runs the counter, and he also handles our wholesale deliveries to four cafes nearby, so you may run into our bread there, too. Whether it's your first loaf or your fiftieth, come by any day but Monday and say hello.
