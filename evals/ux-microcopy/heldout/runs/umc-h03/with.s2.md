Marrow & Rye started in 2019, when Dani Okafor and her brother Tunde opened the doors of a converted garage on Pell Street. Everything we bake is naturally leavened with a rye starter Dani has kept going since 2016, and our flour comes from a mill two counties over. We bake six days a week and close on Mondays. Tunde runs the counter, so he's likely the first face you'll see. He also handles our wholesale deliveries to four cafes nearby. Come by, say hello, and see what's just come out of the oven.
