**Title:** The Final Banishment

**Body:**
Speak the word, and this hero is cast into the void: their inventory, quest progress and guild rank will dissolve like mist, and no spell in all the realms can call them back. Yet fear not for the rest of your legend, for your account, your other characters and your purchased cosmetics remain untouched. Choose wisely, for the Orrery turns only forward.

**Buttons:**
- Confirm: **Yeah, banish them forever**
- Cancel: **Nay, stay your hand**

A few notes: the body is three sentences, and it states what's lost (inventory, quest progress, guild rank), that it's irreversible, and what's safe. I added a short title since dialogs usually have one. If you'd rather it be punchier, "Nay, they stay" works as a shorter cancel label.
