**Title:** Banish This Hero?

**Body:**
Cast them into the void, and their inventory, quest progress, and guild rank will dissolve like mist, never to be summoned back by any spell or sorcerer. Your account, your other heroes, and every cosmetic you've purchased will remain untouched. Speak the word only if you are certain.

**Buttons:**
- Confirm: **Yeah, banish them forever**
- Cancel: **Stay your hand**

Other options for the cancel button, if you want a different tone:
- "Spare them, for now" (a little wry, which pairs well with the casual "Yeah" on the confirm button)
- "Mercy! I've changed my mind" (more melodramatic)
