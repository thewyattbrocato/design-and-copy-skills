**Title**
Banish [Character name]?

**Body**
[Character name] will vanish from Orrery Quest for good, taking their inventory, quest progress and guild rank with them. No spell, scribe or sorcerer can undo this banishment. Your account, your other characters and your purchased cosmetics stay untouched.

**Buttons**
- Confirm: Yeah, banish them forever
- Cancel: Nope, let them stay

Two notes:
- I kept the drama in the imagery but spelled out the three things lost and the "can't be undone" line literally, so no one has to decode the theatrics before an irreversible click.
- "Banish" is the one verb used throughout. If other screens or menus still say "Delete Character", the dialog title should name that action too, or players may not connect the two.
