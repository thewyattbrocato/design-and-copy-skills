**Title**
Banish [Character name]?

**Body**
Banish them, and their inventory, quest progress and guild rank vanish into the void. This cannot be undone, not by any spell, scroll or sorcerer. Your account, your other characters and your purchased cosmetics remain untouched.

**Buttons**
- Confirm: Yeah, banish them forever
- Decline: No, let them stay

Note: I kept the drama in the phrasing but stated each consequence literally, so nobody has to guess what "the void" costs them. The three things lost and the three things kept are all named. Swap in the character's name for `[Character name]`.
