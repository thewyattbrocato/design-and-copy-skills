**Dialog title**
Banish [Character name]?

**Body**
Banishing [Character name] will erase their inventory, quest progress and guild rank, and no magic in Orrery can bring them back. Your account, your other characters and your purchased cosmetics remain untouched. Speak the word only if this tale truly ends here.

**Buttons**
- Confirm: Yeah, banish them forever
- Decline: No, let them stay

Notes:
- The dialog now says "banish" where your game's menu says "Delete Character". Make the menu entry say "Banish character" too, so players don't wonder whether those are two different actions.
- I kept the stakes in plain words (what's lost, what's safe, no undo) and put the theatrics around them. Players should never have to decode the flourish to know what they're agreeing to.
