**Title:** The Final Unmaking

**Body:**
Speak the word, and this hero's inventory, quest progress and guild rank will be unwritten from the Orrery's great ledger, with no spell, scholar or sorrowful prayer able to restore them. Your account, your other characters and every cosmetic you've purchased will remain untouched, safe beyond the reach of this dark rite. Choose with care, for the stars themselves will not remember what you cast away.

**Buttons:**
- Confirm: **Yeah, banish them forever**
- Cancel: **No, stay your hand**

A couple of notes:
- The three sentences cover each fact: what's lost, that it's permanent, and what's safe.
- If you'd like a different cancel tone, alternatives are "Spare them, for now" (gentler) or "Let them live to adventure on" (warmer).
