**(a) Free-ride period about to end**
1. `Your 30 free minutes are almost up. End ride to avoid fees.` (59)
2. `Included 30 minutes almost up. Then billed per minute.` (54)
3. `You're at 30 min. Riding on is billed per minute.` (49)

**(b) Reservation hold, 5 minutes left**
1. `Your bike is held for 5 more minutes. Unlock it by then.` (56)
2. `5 minutes left to unlock your reserved bike.` (44)
3. `Your reserved bike is waiting. 5 minutes left to unlock it.` (59)

- I left the price out of all six. At 49 to 59 characters, there's little room to add it. If you want it in (a), option 3 is the easiest to change, for example "…Riding on is [price]/min."
- Option (a)1 says "free" and (a)2 says "included". The day pass covers those minutes, so "included" is the more accurate word, and you can swap it in if you want to match your app's wording.
