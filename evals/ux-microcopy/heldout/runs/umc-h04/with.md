If either turns out to be wrong, people lose trust in everything else you've said, and they may have been relying on it when they skipped checking their notes. Once you've confirmed it, you can say "we've found the cause and fixed it" and "here is what we changed." If the audit does show nothing was lost, say "we've confirmed no data was lost" in a follow-up. That is a claim you can stand behind. You could put this to your cofounder as the stronger version of what they want.
