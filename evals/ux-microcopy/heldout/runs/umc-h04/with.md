Title: We may have lost some of your recent edits

Body: Edits made between about 11:20 pm and midnight UTC on [date] may not have saved because of a sync problem on our side. We're sorry. We're still checking which accounts were affected and we'll email you if yours was. [If you made edits in that window, check your notes now.]

Buttons: **Review my notes** / **Dismiss**

**Email, first paragraph**

Subject: Some of your Quillmark edits may not have saved

Edits you made in Quillmark between about 11:20 pm and midnight UTC on [date] may not have saved. A sync problem on our side caused this, and we're sorry. Our check shows your account was affected. [Describe what we found, for example which notes or edits.] [Describe what we're doing to recover them, or say we don't know yet.] We don't yet know the cause, and we'll write again by [date or time] with what we've learned.

**Notes**
- I left out "no user data was lost" and "this will never happen again". The first contradicts the notice, because you know edits may be gone. The second is a promise you can't make while the cause is unknown. If either turns out false, it will cost you more trust than the outage did. Once you've confirmed the cause and the fix, you can say what you changed, which is a promise you can keep.
- The email says "our check shows your account was affected", so send it only to accounts you've confirmed. For anyone still unconfirmed, the in-app notice already uses "may have". Fill the brackets only with facts you've verified, and drop the line about recovery if you don't have one yet.
