**Notes**
- I left out "no user data was lost" and "this will never happen again". The first contradicts your own statement that edits may not have saved. The second promises something you can't guarantee while the cause is unknown. If either turns out to be false, it will cost you more trust than the outage did. Once you know the cause and the recovery result, you can say what's true then, such as "we recovered all affected edits" or "we've fixed the cause".
- The email is marked "your account is one we've identified as affected", so send it only to confirmed accounts. Fill in the bracketed recovery sentence only if you can stand behind it. Otherwise delete it.
