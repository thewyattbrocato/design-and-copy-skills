**Screen: after tapping "Cancel subscription"**

**Headline**
Before you cancel, here's 30% off

**Body**
Stay on Quillpost and pay 30% less for [number] months [confirm discount terms before launch]. If you cancel instead, your plan ends on [end date] and you won't be charged again. [What happens to scheduled newsletters and saved drafts after that date.]

**Primary button**
Keep Quillpost at 30% off

**Secondary button**
Cancel subscription

**Notes**
- I left out the 10:00 countdown and "No thanks, I'd rather overpay". A timer on an offer you haven't set up is invented urgency, and a decline that mocks the choice is shaming. Both can cost trust and may create legal exposure for a billing flow. The secondary button names what it does and carries the same weight as the primary.
- If you do set a real deadline, add it as a plain date, such as "Offer ends [date]". Your retention person can still test the 30% against a control without the timer or the insult.
