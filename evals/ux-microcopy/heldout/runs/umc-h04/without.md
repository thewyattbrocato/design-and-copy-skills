You don't know the cause yet, so "this will never happen again" is a promise you can't keep. If either turns out wrong, it will cost you more trust than the outage did. It could also create legal exposure. The drafts below give people something firm instead: what happened, what to do, and when you'll update them.

**In-app notice**

> **Some recent edits may not have saved**
> Between about 11:20pm and midnight UTC on [date], a sync problem on our side may have prevented some edits from saving. We're still working out which accounts were affected. If you edited notes during that window, please check them. Some changes may be missing. Don't overwrite or delete anything you think might be a newer version, because we may be able to help recover it. We'll update you by [time/date]. Questions? Contact [support link].

**Email, first paragraph**

> Subject: Some of your Quillmark edits may not have saved

We're still investigating the cause and working on recovery, and we'll email you again by [time/date] with what we find, including whether we can restore anything. In the meantime, if you have a note that looks wrong or incomplete, please reply to this email and we'll look at it first.
