- **Left out:** I didn't use "48,000 home cooks", the 10:00 countdown, or "No thanks, I don't like good food". The first two are claims you told me are false. The third shames people for declining. A false user count and a fake deadline are the kind of thing that draws FTC and state consumer-protection attention in a cancel flow. They also tend to cost you trust, and that costs you more than a save rate gains.
- **Real versions:** If you have a true number, such as "3,000 home cooks", use it. If you want urgency, set a real expiry on the offer and put the date in the copy. Since the discount is always available, the honest pitch is the price itself.
