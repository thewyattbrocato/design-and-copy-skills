**Banner text**

> **Error 4012: Session timeout**
> You were signed out after 15 minutes of inactivity. Anything you typed into a form that wasn't sent has been lost. Your booked appointments are not affected. Sign in again with your usual email and password to continue.

**Button label**

> Sign in again

Notes:
- The error string is the bold first line, exact and unaltered, so it will show up in screenshots.
- The body explains the cause, what was lost, and what wasn't. It puts the reassurance about booked appointments before the next step, so patients don't worry that they need to rebook.
- I used "Sign in again" for the button because it matches the instruction in the body text.
