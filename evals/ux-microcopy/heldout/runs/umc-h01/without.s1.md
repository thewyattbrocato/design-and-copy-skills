Here's copy that doesn't state a cause or ETA, since nobody has given you either.

## Banner

**Option A (short):**
> Card payments are temporarily unavailable. You can still book by bank transfer, and your existing bookings are not affected. We're working on a fix.

**Option B (with a link):**
> We're having trouble processing card payments right now. Bank transfer works normally, and confirmed bookings are safe. [Details]

## Email

**Subject:** Your Fernleaf Sitters booking: card payment issue

> Hi [First name],
>
> You may have seen an error when you tried to book with your card this morning. We're having a temporary problem with card payments, and our team is working on it.
>
> **What this means for you:**
> - Your existing confirmed bookings are not affected.
> - You can still complete a new booking now by choosing **bank transfer** at checkout.
> - If you'd rather pay by card, you can try again later today. We'll email you as soon as card payments are working again.
>
> [Billing line, see below]
>
> Sorry for the hassle. If you have questions or need help with a booking, reply to this email and we'll help.
>
> The Fernleaf Sitters Team

## Before you send

1. **Billing line.** Ask engineering or payments whether failed attempts could have created pending authorizations or double charges. If not, use: *"Your card was not charged."* If they can't confirm, use: *"If you see a pending charge from this attempt, it should drop off automatically. Reply to us if it doesn't."* I left this as a placeholder because I don't want to promise something that may not be true.
2. **Follow-up promise.** The email says you'll write when card payments are back. Only keep that line if someone will actually send it.
3. **Timing.** Once engineering gives you an ETA, add "We expect this to be resolved by [time]." Until then, leave it out. A missed estimate worries people more than no estimate.
4. **Recipient list.** Confirm the email goes only to people who hit the error, and that "this morning" still fits when it goes out.

I can also write a short "card payments are back" banner and email for when it's fixed.
