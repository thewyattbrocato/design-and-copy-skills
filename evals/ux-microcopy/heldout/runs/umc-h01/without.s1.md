**Title:** Cancel your booking?

**Body:** This will release your seat in the 2-hour wheel-throwing class. You can rebook any open class from this screen.

**Buttons:**
- Primary (destructive): **Cancel booking**
- Secondary: **Keep booking**

I left money out on purpose, because I don't want the dialog to promise or imply anything about refunds before you've checked the policy. People will probably expect a line about it. Once you know the policy, add one sentence after the first, such as "You'll get a full refund to your original payment method" or "This booking is non-refundable." If the answer depends on timing, you may also want the dialog to show a different line depending on how close the class is.
