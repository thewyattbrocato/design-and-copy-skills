**Title:**  
Cancel booking?

**Body:**  
You'll free up your spot in this class. You can rebook this class or any other open slot whenever you'd like.

**Buttons:**  
Cancel booking | Keep booking

The body acknowledges the consequence (seat released) and reminds them rebooking is frictionless—both reduce cancellation anxiety. Once you know the refund policy, you can add a note at the end ("Refunds processed in X days" or whatever applies).
