**Title:** Cancel this booking?

**Body:**
Your seat in this wheel-throwing class (2 hours) will be released. You can rebook any open class from this screen.

**Buttons:**
- Primary (destructive): **Cancel booking**
- Secondary: **Keep booking**

The body says nothing about money because the refund policy isn't settled. Once you know it, add one sentence after the first, for example "You'll be refunded [X] within [Y days]." or "This booking is non-refundable." Don't ship the dialog without that line if cancelling affects what they paid. People will assume a refund unless you tell them otherwise.

I used "Keep booking" instead of "Cancel" or "Go back" so it can't be confused with the destructive "Cancel booking" button.
