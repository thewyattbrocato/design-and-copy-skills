**Things to check before sending**

- **"On our end":** I wrote that the problem isn't with the customer's card, but engineering hasn't confirmed the cause. If it could be the payment processor, the sentence is still true. Just confirm it's not a bank-side issue before you send.
- **Charges:** If any customers might have been charged without a booking going through, say so in the email ("you have not been charged" or "any pending charge will drop off within X days"). That's the question most likely to cause worry, and I didn't want to promise something I can't verify.
- **No ETA:** I left out a time on purpose. If engineering gives you one, add "We expect this to be fixed by [time]" to both. Don't add a guess.
- **Banner removal:** Swap in a short "Card payments are back" note once it's resolved, or take the banner down.
