Here's copy for each screen. I kept it to what Shiftbook does today: posting shifts, signing up, and reminders.

**1. New coordinator, no shifts yet**

> **Post your first shift**
> Add a shift and parents can sign up for it. It only takes a minute.
> [Post a shift]

**2. Parent, no open shifts**

> **No open shifts right now**
> Every shift is covered, or none have been posted yet. Check back soon.

If you'd rather steer parents toward reminders, you could add: "Turn on reminders and you won't miss your shift." Only use that line if the prompt is reachable from this screen.

**3. Reminder permission prompt**

This is your pre-prompt, shown before the browser's own dialog. It's a good place to explain what the notifications are.

> **Want a reminder before your shift?**
> We'll send a notification to this browser so you don't forget. We'll only use it for your shifts.
> [Turn on reminders] [Not now]

I wrote "notification to this browser" instead of "email" because push notifications show up on the device, not in an inbox. Calling them email-like could confuse people when nothing arrives in their mail.

Optional line for after someone taps "Not now": "No problem. You can turn reminders on later."

**4. Shift just taken by someone else**

> **Oh no, that shift was just taken**
> Someone else signed up a moment ago. Take a look at the other open shifts.
> [See open shifts]

If no other shifts are open, the parent should land on the "No open shifts" screen above.
