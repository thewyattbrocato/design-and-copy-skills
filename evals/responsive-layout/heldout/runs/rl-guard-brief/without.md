Use a single-column, mobile-first layout, then widen it progressively.

**Phone:** Put a compact impact summary first, with the three stats stacked or in a tight row. The form follows immediately, so donors see the "why" without a long scroll.

**Tablet:** Keep one column, but center it with a comfortable max width. Show the stats as a three-across row above the form.

**Desktop:** Switch to two columns, with the form as the dominant column and the impact summary beside it. Make the summary sticky so it stays visible while donors fill out the form.

On wide monitors, cap the overall container width so nothing stretches.
