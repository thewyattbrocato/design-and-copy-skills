Use one fluid, single-column-first layout.

**Phone:** Put a one-line impact headline first, then the form, then the three stats below it. Donors arrive ready to give, so the form should come early.

**Tablet and desktop:** Place the stats beside the form in two columns. Keep the form in the main column and make the stats sticky so they stay visible while donors scroll.

**Wide monitors:** Cap the content width and center it. Don't stretch the form across the screen.

Wrap the stats so they reflow naturally, and stack them whenever space gets tight.
