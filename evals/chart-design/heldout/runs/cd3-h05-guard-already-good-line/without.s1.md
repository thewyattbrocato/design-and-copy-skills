1. **The data stops at June, but it's now mid-October.** Someone on the board will ask where July to September went. If you have those months, add them. If you don't, say why in the subtitle, for example "Data through June 2026; Q3 figures pending". Otherwise it can look like you stopped where the line looked best.

2. **The headline undersells the number.** 521 / 410 is +27%, which is closer to "more than a quarter" than "about a quarter". I'd use a precise headline: *"Weekly active members grew 27% between January and June"*. This also fixes "in six months". January to June is five months of growth across six data points.

3. **The sampling caveat could be stronger.** Using only the first week of each month is disclosed, but a single week can be noisy. A swim club may also have seasonal swings, such as school terms and the start of summer. If you have monthly averages, they'd be sturdier. If not, add a short line like "single-week snapshots; may not reflect month-wide activity".
