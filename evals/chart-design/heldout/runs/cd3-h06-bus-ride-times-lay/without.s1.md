- Use horizontal bars with the labels written on or next to each bar (for example, "56 of every 100 trips: 20–29 min").
- Leave out gridlines, a y-axis, and a legend.
- Use one color for all bars. Make the 30+ bars a darker or accent shade only if you want to draw attention to the slow trips.

**Wording tips**
- Say "out of every 100 trips" instead of percentages or "probability." It's easier to picture.
- Don't use "average," because the slow trips pull it up and it won't match what parents experience. "Typical" or "most" is better.
- Avoid "on time" unless you've defined it against the schedule.
