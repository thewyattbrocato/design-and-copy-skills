## Wording tips

- Say "out of every 100 trips" rather than percentages. Parents handle it more easily.
- Lead with what the data supports. If the typical ride is on schedule but 1 in 6 trips runs long, say both. Parents are reacting to the bad days, and a headline like "Route 14 is mostly on time" will read as dismissive.
- Skip "average." The few very long trips would pull it up, and "typical" is clearer.
- Add one line on what the district is doing about the long trips, if anything. That's what parents will look for next.
