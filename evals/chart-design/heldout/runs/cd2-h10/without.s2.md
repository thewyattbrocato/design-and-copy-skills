A pie is fine for this. Three parts of a whole, with one clearly dominant, is the case where pies work, and an all-hands audience will read it instantly.

**Do this:**
- **Direct labels, no legend.** Put "Hardware 62%", "Software 25%" and "Other 13%" on or beside the slices.
- **Emphasize hardware.** Make it one strong color and the other two slices muted grays or tints. The point of the slide is that hardware dominates.
- **Put the takeaway in the title.** For example, "Hardware made up 62% of our 1,240 repair tickets this year."
- **Start at 12 o'clock and order largest to smallest, clockwise.** Consider a donut with "1,240 tickets" in the center.

**Consider a bar instead if** you want people to compare software (25%) with other (13%). Angles are harder to compare than bar lengths. A single horizontal bar with three labeled segments, or three horizontal bars, makes that easier. If the story is only "hardware is most of it," the pie is fine.
