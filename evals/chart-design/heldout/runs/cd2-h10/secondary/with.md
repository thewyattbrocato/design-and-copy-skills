Use a horizontal bar chart, not a pie. Pie charts work with three slices, but at all-hands distance the audience has to compare angles to judge 25% against 13%, and bars make that comparison easy.

- **Order:** Hardware (62%), Software (25%), Other (13%), largest at top.
- **Labels:** Put the percent and count directly on each bar, such as "62% · 769 tickets." The counts are 769, 310, and 161, which add to 1,240.
- **Color:** Make Hardware the accent color and gray the other two, so the slide shows one point.
- **Title:** State the takeaway, such as "Hardware drove most of our 1,240 repair tickets this year." Avoid "Ticket breakdown by category."
- **Other:** Consider naming what "everything else" covers, if you can do that in a few words.

A pie is still acceptable if the only point is "most of it is hardware." If you want a single-bar version instead, a 100% stacked bar works too, but the horizontal bars are the clearest choice here.
