**Use a horizontal bar chart, not a pie.** Three slices can work as a pie, but the bar is easier to read on a projected slide.

- **Bars compare better.** The gap between 25% and 13% is hard to judge by slice angle and easy to judge by bar length.
- **Labels fit.** Horizontal bars leave room for direct labels like "Hardware: 62% (769)", so the audience doesn't need a legend.
- **It's a ranking.** Sorting largest to smallest and highlighting hardware makes the point without extra text.

Suggested setup:
- **Order:** Hardware (769, 62%), Software (310, 25%), Other (161, 13%). Those counts add up to 1,240.
- **Color:** Hardware in your accent color, the other two in gray.
- **Title:** "Hardware caused 62% of our 1,240 repair tickets this year."
- **Axis:** Start at zero, and drop gridlines if you use direct labels.

If the point is that the three parts make up one whole, a single 100% stacked bar is the other good option. The pie is fine if you're committed to it, but I'd go with the bar.
