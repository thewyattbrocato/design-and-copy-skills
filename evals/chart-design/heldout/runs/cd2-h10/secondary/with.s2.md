**Use a horizontal bar chart, not a pie.** With three slices, a pie works, but bars are easier to read from the back of a room because people compare lengths, not angles.

- Sort the bars largest to smallest: Hardware 62%, Software 25%, Everything else 13%.
- Label each bar directly with its percentage and count (about 769, 310, and 161 tickets). Skip the legend.
- Highlight Hardware in your accent color and grey out the other two. The slide should make one point, and that point is that hardware dominates.
- Put a title that states the takeaway, such as "Hardware drove 62% of our 1,240 repair tickets this year."

If you want something even simpler, a single large "62%" with "hardware" beneath it, plus a small bar or stacked strip for the other two, also works well on a slide.

Consider renaming "everything else" to something specific if you can. If you can't, add a footnote saying what it includes.
