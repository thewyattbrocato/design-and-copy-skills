Your coworker is only partly right. A line chart doesn't need a zero baseline. Bar charts do, because bar length is the value, but a line's meaning is in its position, so a non-zero axis is standard for line charts. A zero-based axis here would squash the 21.4 to 24.9 range into the top sliver of the plot and hide the daily pattern you're trying to show.

Your 20 to 26 axis is reasonable. It contains all the data with a little room on each side, and the 3.5°C swing is a real change for a lake. The one thing to check is that the axis is labeled clearly, with tick marks at whole degrees and the unit (°C) on the axis title. That way nobody mistakes the scale for something else.

You don't need to redo the chart. If your coworker still objects, you could add a note such as "Y-axis starts at 20°C" in the caption, which makes the choice explicit.
