**The core problem:** the page has one layout repeated five times, so nothing signals what matters. Visitors scanning scroll-wise get no rhythm, no focal point, and no sense of where the product actually is.

**Specific issues**

1. **Uniform treatment flattens hierarchy.** Pricing, Testimonials and Features have very different jobs, but they look identical. Pricing needs comparison, and testimonials need credibility, so three equal cards fit neither.
2. **No product visible.** For a scheduling tool, there's no calendar UI, booking flow, or screenshot. Icon-in-circle cards are abstract and say nothing about Tidewell specifically.
3. **Generic copy slots.** Two lines per card forces every section to the same length, which encourages filler.
4. **"How it works" as cards** hides sequence. Steps should read as 1→2→3 with connecting visuals.
5. **Integrations as prose cards** is weak. Three cards can't convey breadth. Logos do that better.
6. **Testimonials without faces, names, companies or metrics** read as invented. Icon circles here are especially wrong.
7. **Same background and spacing** makes sections blur together and removes any pacing.

**What I'd change**

- **Features:** one or two large alternating blocks, each pairing a real screenshot with a short benefit-led description. Keep a small grid for secondary features.
- **How it works:** numbered horizontal steps with a connecting line and a small UI snippet per step.
- **Integrations:** a logo wall (Google Calendar, Outlook, Zoom, Slack) with one line of text.
- **Testimonials:** one large pull quote with photo, name, company, and a concrete result, or a staggered set of quotes.
- **Pricing:** tiered cards with the recommended plan highlighted, a feature list, and a clear CTA.
- **Rhythm:** alternate background tones, give the pricing and final CTA sections more space, and tighten Integrations.
- **Add a closing CTA** after Pricing.

Keep the shared heading style and grid for consistency, but let each section's content determine its layout.
