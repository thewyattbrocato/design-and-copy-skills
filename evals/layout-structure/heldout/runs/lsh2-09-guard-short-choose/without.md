Go with the smaller image plus her name and a one-line description. For a freelancer, the first screen has to tell a visitor who she is and what she does, so they can decide whether to hire her, and a name tucked in a corner leaves that unanswered. The full-bleed option looks dramatic, but it suits an artist whose reputation is already established, and it makes the page heavy on mobile.

If you want some of that drama, make the image large but still keep the name and the line in view.
