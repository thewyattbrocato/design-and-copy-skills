Go with the full-bleed illustration, but don't leave her name as an afterthought: keep it small yet high-contrast, and add a short line about what she does (even tucked beneath the name). For an illustrator the work is the pitch, so letting one strong piece fill the screen sells her style instantly, which a small image next to text can't do. The exception is if she needs to attract commissions from people who don't know her, in which case the descriptor line matters more and should be clearly visible, not hidden.
