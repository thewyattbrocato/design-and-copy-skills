Getting there

By car
Search for “Quinta do Moinho” in your maps app. Please aim to arrive by 3:30 pm so you’re seated before the ceremony begins. Sharing a car with other guests is very welcome.
Taxi or ride-share
Quintas are often outside town, so it’s a good idea to book your ride home in advance for the end of the evening.
Staying nearby
If you’d rather not drive after dinner, we recommend booking accommodation close to the venue. Ask us if you’d like suggestions.

Dress code

Dressy and celebratory: think cocktail or formal attire. The day moves from the chapel to the gardens, so comfortable shoes are a good idea, and bring a light layer for the evening.
