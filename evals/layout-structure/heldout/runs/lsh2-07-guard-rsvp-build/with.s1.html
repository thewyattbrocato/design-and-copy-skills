Getting there

The ceremony takes place in the chapel at Quinta do Moinho, and the celebration continues at the same venue. Search for “Quinta do Moinho” in your maps app of choice.

We recommend arriving by 3:30 pm so everyone is seated before the ceremony begins. If you are driving, carpooling is encouraged, as parking may be limited. Please get in touch with us if you need help with transport or directions.

Dress code

Smart and comfortable. The ceremony and drinks are outdoors, so consider light layers for the evening, and shoes that will cope with grass or gravel.
