Getting there

The ceremony takes place in the chapel at Quinta do Moinho, and everything after happens at the same estate, so there’s no need to travel between venues.

Search for “Quinta do Moinho” in your maps app. If you’re driving, please plan to arrive a little before 4:00 pm so you can park and find your seat. Taxis and ride-hailing are a good option if you’d like to enjoy the evening.

What to wear

Smart and comfortable. Think summer wedding: suits, dresses, and light fabrics. The afternoon may be warm and the evening cooler, so a light layer is a good idea. Flat or block-heel shoes will be easier on any grass or gravel paths.
