Getting there

The ceremony takes place in the chapel at Quinta do Moinho, with everything afterwards at the same venue, so there's no need to move between places during the day.

We recommend arriving a little before 4:00 pm so everyone is seated when the ceremony begins. Searching for "Quinta do Moinho" in your maps app is the easiest way to find it. Since the party goes on late, we suggest sharing a taxi or arranging a driver for the way home.

Dress code

Smart and festive: cocktail or semi-formal. Since there will be dancing, comfortable shoes are a good idea. Bring a light layer for the evening.
