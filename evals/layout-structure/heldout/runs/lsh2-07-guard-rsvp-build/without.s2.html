Getting there

The ceremony takes place in the chapel at Quinta do Moinho, and the celebration continues on the estate afterwards.

If you are driving, plan to arrive by 3:30 pm so you can park and find a seat before the ceremony begins. We suggest sharing cars where you can.

Since there will be wine with dinner, you may prefer a taxi or ride service for the journey home. Book your return ahead of time, as cars can be scarce late in the evening.

Dress code

Smart and comfortable. The ceremony and drinks are outdoors, so choose shoes you can walk in on grass and gravel, and bring a light layer for the evening.
