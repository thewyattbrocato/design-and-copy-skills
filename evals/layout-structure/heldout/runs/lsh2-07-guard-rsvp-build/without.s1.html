Getting there

The ceremony takes place in the chapel at Quinta do Moinho, and the celebration continues on the grounds afterwards.

We recommend arriving by 3:30 pm so everyone is seated before the ceremony begins. Search for "Quinta do Moinho" in your maps app, and consider sharing a ride or taxi, as the evening runs late.

Dress code

Smart and festive. Think suits and cocktail dresses. Part of the day is outdoors, so comfortable shoes are a good idea, and you may want a light layer for the evening.
