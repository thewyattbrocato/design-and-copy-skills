Getting there

The ceremony takes place at the chapel at Quinta do Moinho, and the celebration continues on the same grounds. Search for “Quinta do Moinho” in your maps app of choice for directions.

We recommend arriving a little before 4 pm so you can find your seat. If you’re sharing a car, a designated driver for the journey home is a good idea.

Dress code

Smart and festive. It’s a June day with some time outdoors, so light fabrics and comfortable shoes will serve you well, and you may want a layer for the evening.

Please let us know if you can join us.
