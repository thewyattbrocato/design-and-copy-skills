Getting there

The ceremony takes place in the chapel at Quinta do Moinho. Search for “Quinta do Moinho” in your maps app to get directions.

Since the evening goes on well past dinner, we suggest sharing a car or booking a taxi for the way back. Please try to arrive a little before 4:00 pm so everyone is seated for the ceremony.

Dress code

Smart and festive. It’s a June day with a chapel, so think light fabrics, and bring a layer for the evening. Comfortable shoes are a good idea if you plan to dance.

Please let us know if you’ll be joining us.
