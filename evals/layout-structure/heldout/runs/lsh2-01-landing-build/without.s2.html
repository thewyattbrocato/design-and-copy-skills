A small studio in Tacoma

Kilnroom is a working ceramics studio. Everything in your box is made here, by hand, in batches small enough that we know each piece.

We started shipping boxes so more people could have handmade pottery in their daily routine, not just on a shelf.

Questions

How often do boxes ship?

Every other month, so six boxes a year if you never skip.

Can I choose mugs or bowls?

Yes. Pick one when you sign up, and you can change it before the next shipment.

How do I skip or cancel?

You can skip any box or cancel your subscription at any time. There are no fees.

Are the pieces dishwasher safe?

Yes, and microwave safe too. Hand washing will keep glazes looking their best for longer.

Will I get to pick the glaze?

Each box is a surprise from our latest firings. Every piece is one of a kind.
