Questions

How do I skip a box?

Let us know before your next box is made and we will move you to the following round. You are not charged for skipped boxes.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There is no contract or minimum. Cancel whenever you like and you will not be billed again.

Can I choose mugs or bowls?

You pick mugs or bowls when you sign up, and you can switch for any future box.
