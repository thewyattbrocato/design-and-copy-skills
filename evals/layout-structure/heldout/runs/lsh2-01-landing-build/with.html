The studio

Made in a small room in Tacoma.

Kilnroom is a working ceramics studio. Everything in your box is thrown, trimmed, glazed, and fired here, then packed up and sent to you.

It's meant to be an easy way to have handmade pottery in your home, without the commitment of a big set.
