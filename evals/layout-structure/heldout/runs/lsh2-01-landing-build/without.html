Because the work is handmade, you may see small variations in color and shape. That's part of why they're nice to use.

Questions

Good to know

How often do boxes ship?

Every other month, so six boxes a year if you never skip.

Can I skip a box or cancel?

Yes, either one, anytime. Skipping just moves you to the following shipment. There's no minimum commitment.

Can I choose between mugs and bowls?

Yes. Pick one when you sign up, and let us know if you'd like to switch for a later box.

Will my pieces match?

They'll look good together, but each is handmade, so expect natural variation in shape and glaze.

What if something arrives damaged?

Get in touch with a photo and we'll sort it out.

Are the pieces dishwasher and microwave safe?

Details on care come with each box. If you have a question about a specific piece, just ask.
