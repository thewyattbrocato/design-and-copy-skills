**Same in all four:** one type family, one color relationship, and one reading order: **title, then date and time, then venue, then "Free entry".** "Four readers + open mic after" is the last line. Put the mark in the same corner of every version. Only size and position change. You haven't given me reader names, so I've treated them as optional below.

**A3 portrait (297×420mm, add 3mm bleed)**
- This is the only version with room for everything, and it gets read from a few metres away.
- Title is the biggest element, in the top third, set large across the width over 2–3 lines.
- The date and time sit directly under it at about half the title's size. They should be the second thing a passer-by reads.
- The middle can be open space or the art. Don't fill it.
- The bottom band is the practical block, left-aligned on one edge: venue and street address, "Free entry", "Four readers, open mic after".
- Reader names go in the bottom band at small size, one line each or two columns. They're the only thing you can shrink without losing the message.
- Keep roughly 20mm margins, and make the bottom margin deeper than the top.

**Square post (1080×1080)**
- Title is biggest and fills the upper half.
- Date and time sit under it, about 60% of the title size.
- Venue and "Free entry" go on one line at the bottom.
- Drop the street address, the reader names and the open mic detail. Put them in the caption.
- Keep about 80px margins. The grid previews crop to the centre, so nothing important goes near the edges.

**Story (1080×1920)**
- Keep the top and bottom 15% (about 290px) clear for the interface. All text goes in the middle band, roughly y 290 to 1630.
- Stack it, with the title as the largest element, then the date and time right under it.
- Leave a gap, then venue, then "Free entry".
- Use one short line for "Four readers + open mic". Skip the names.
- Don't scale down the poster. Tall space is for stacking and for letting the art breathe above the title.
- The sticker or link goes in the lower safe area, not at the very bottom.

**Event page banner (1600×400, 4:1)**
- Go side-by-side. Title on the left, taking about 60% of the width and filling the height.
