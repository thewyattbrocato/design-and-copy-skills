**Shared hierarchy (all formats):**
1. Title: *Low Tide Readings*
2. Date and time: Friday 14 Nov, 7:30pm
3. Venue: Harbor Street Boathouse
4. "Free entry"
5. "Four readers + open mic"

Keep that order of importance everywhere. Only the arrangement changes.

---

**A3 portrait poster (297 × 420 mm)**
- **Top third:** Title, largest element, set across 2–3 lines if the type is big.
- **Middle:** Date and time, second largest, with the day and date on one line and the time on the next.
- **Below that:** Venue, a clear step smaller than the date.
- **Lower area:** "Four readers, then a short open mic" in body size. This is the only place there's room to say it in full. If you want reader names on the poster, they go here, in a small list.
- **Bottom strip:** "Free entry" as a bold label or band. Add the address, and a website or QR code if you have one.
- **Margins:** Leave generous margins and about 20% breathing space. People read this from 1–2 m away, so the date and venue need to be legible from there.

**Square Instagram post (1080 × 1080)**
- **Top-left or centre:** Title, about 40% of the height.
- **Centre:** Date and time on two lines.
- **Below:** Venue.
- **Bottom corner:** "Free entry" as a small badge or tag.
- **Dropped or shortened:** "Four readers + open mic" becomes one short line, or moves to the caption. Reader names go in the caption.
- **Safe zone:** Keep everything about 80 px in from the edges, because the grid crop can trim the sides.

**9:16 story (1080 × 1920)**
- **Safe zone:** Keep the top ~250 px and bottom ~340 px clear. The app's UI covers them, and nothing important should sit there.
- **Upper-middle (about y 300–800):** Title, stacked on 2–3 lines, as large as it will go.
- **Middle (about y 850–1200):** Date and time, big and stacked.
- **Lower-middle (about y 1250–1450):** Venue, then "Free entry".
- **Dropped or moved:** The "four readers + open mic" line becomes a small line under the venue, or goes in a sticker or text overlay. Skip the address, and add a link sticker if you need one.
- **Layout:** One column, centred or left-aligned, with lots of vertical space.

**Wide banner (1600 × 400)**
- **Left half:** Title, in 1–2 lines at the largest size the 400 px height allows.
- **Right half:** Date and time on one line, venue below it, and "Free entry" as a small tag on the right edge.
- **Dropped:** The readers line and any names. The event page body text carries those, and a banner this short can't fit them.
- **Safe zone:** Keep text about 60 px from the edges, and further in on the right if the page overlays a button or date widget. Check the mobile crop, since the sides often get trimmed. The title and date should still read in the centre ~1000 px.
- **Legibility:** Use no more than three type sizes, and don't let the artwork sit behind the text without enough contrast.
