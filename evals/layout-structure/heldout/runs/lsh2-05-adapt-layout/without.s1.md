The title and the date/time should always be readable at a glance. Everything else can shrink or drop depending on the format.

**A3 portrait (297 × 420 mm, 3 mm bleed, ~15 mm margins)**
- **Top third:** title, largest element, set on 2–3 lines if that helps the type size.
- **Middle:** date and time, about half the title size. Venue directly under it, a bit smaller.
- **Lower middle:** the four reader names in a clean list, with a line like "followed by open mic".
- **Bottom strip:** "Free entry" as a tag or band, plus the street address, any contact or website, and sponsor logos.
- This is the only format with room for everything, so use it for the full reader list.
- It should read from 2–3 m away, so keep the title and date/time large and the details at body size.

**Square Instagram post (1080 × 1080)**
- **Center-weighted:** the grid preview crops the edges, so keep all key text in the middle ~80%.
- **Top or center:** title, largest.
- **Directly below:** date/time on one line, then venue.
- **Bottom:** one small line, "Free entry · Four readers + open mic".
- **Dropped:** reader names and address. Put them in the caption.

**9:16 story (1080 × 1920)**
- **Safe zone:** keep the top ~250 px and bottom ~340 px empty of text. The app's profile bar and reply field cover them.
- **Upper-middle:** title stacked on 2–3 lines, very large.
- **Center:** date and time as a big block, then venue.
- **Lower-middle, above the unsafe zone:** a "Free entry" badge, and a "Four readers + open mic" line.
- **Space for a sticker:** leave a clear area for a link or location sticker. That replaces the address.
- **Dropped:** reader names. If you want them, put the names in a second story frame.

**Event page banner (~1600 × 400)**
- **Layout:** one horizontal band. Title on the left, about 50–60% of the width, on 1–2 lines. Date/time and venue stacked on the right in a smaller size.
- **Free entry:** a small tag at the far right, or tucked under the venue.
- **Dropped:** readers, open mic and address. The event page's own text can carry those.
- **Mobile crop:** pages often crop banners to a narrower ratio on phones, so keep all text inside the central ~70% and don't put anything critical at the edges.
- **Height:** at 400 px high there's room for about two text sizes, so don't add a third.
