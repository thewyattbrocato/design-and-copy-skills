# Ledgerlark landing page plan

Your readers are tradespeople who will probably open this on a phone between jobs. They're skeptical of software and short on time. They read the page through, so the order should follow their questions: what is this, is it for me, how does it work, is it real, what does it cost, what do I do next.

## What the top of the page has to do

The first screen needs to answer three things without any scrolling:

- **What it is:** bookkeeping software for freelance electricians and plumbers.
- **What they do with it:** snap receipts, track mileage, send invoices, and know their tax number.
- **Why it suits them:** it runs from the phone, in the van, not at a desk on Sunday night.

Put in the hero:
- A plain headline that names the trades. For example, "Bookkeeping for electricians and plumbers, done from your phone." Don't use a slogan.
- One supporting sentence that names the features in the order they get used.
- One button: "Start your 30-day free trial". Right beside it, small: "$12/month after. Cancel anytime." Showing the price at the top removes the "what's the catch" question early.
- The demo video, with the headline and button on one side and the video on the other. On a phone it stacks as headline, line, button, then video. The button must not end up below the video.

Don't put quotes, a feature list or a nav menu with many links in the hero. A logo and one "Start free trial" link are enough.

## How it varies in rhythm

- **Density alternates:** open, open, woven, open, woven, loud, full, open, full-quiet, open. Nowhere do two full sections sit together. The comparison is the only truly dense block, and it has a calm price band on each side.
- **One loud section:** the tax estimate. It's the thing a freelancer fears most, and it's the only place with a big colour field and oversized type. Keep sections 3 and 5 on the plain background so it stands out.
- **Five features, five different treatments:** don't make five icon cards. Receipts and mileage are phone features and get screenshots. Invoices and the bank feed are about money in and share a section. The tax estimate gets the full-bleed band. Each feature gets its space by how much it matters, not equal boxes.
- **Quotes are spread out, not stacked:** three quotes in a row is a box row. Instead, place each one beside the claim it supports (quote 1 after the receipt and mileage features, quote 2 at the comparison, quote 3 before the questions). Each is large, one at a time.
- **One shared left edge:** every section's content lines up on the same edge and the same outer margin. Backgrounds can run edge to edge, but the text doesn't wander. The variety comes from image side, background and density.
- **Only two grid breaks, each with a job:** the full-bleed tax band (it's the loud moment), and the quotes hanging slightly into the margin (they mark the pauses). If you're tempted to add a third kind of break, fold it into the system or drop it.

## Things to check before design

- **Phone order:** read the stacked order top to bottom. The hero button should be visible near the top, and each feature's text should come before its screenshot.
- **Screenshots:** show them whole, in phone proportions, not cropped into card shapes.
- **Text length:** body text stays at about 60 to 70 characters a line. Only the comparison table runs wider.
- **Worst case:** a quote that runs long, a comparison row label that wraps, and screenshots that need to still read on a small screen.
- **Repeating the call to action:** hero, after the tax band or comparison, and the close. Don't add one to every section.

## Questions that change the plan

1. **Which countries does the tax estimate cover?** If it's one country, say so in the tax section. Trades readers will ask in the first line.
2. **Is a card needed to start the trial?** This is the most common objection and belongs in the hero's small print and the questions.
3. **Do you have real screenshots of the receipt, mileage and tax screens?** Real ones will beat any illustration, and the tax figure in the loud band should be a real product number.
4. **How long is the demo video?** Under 60 seconds can autoplay muted in the hero. Longer, use a poster frame with a play button.
