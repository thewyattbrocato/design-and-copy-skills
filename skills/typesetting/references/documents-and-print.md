# Documents and print

Load this when the output is a paper or PDF document (letter, report, proposal, memo, handbook, contract, résumé, one-pager) or a document that came out of a word processor and needs fixing. The ranges below are print-only: text read on a screen keeps the ranges in SKILL.md and reading-text.md. They are common practice, not rules; judge a printed or full-size sample.

## Body first

Set the body text first and let everything else follow from it.

- **Size and leading.** Default 10 to 12pt in a text face with leading of about 1.25 to 1.4 times the size, set as an exact value (11pt on 14 to 15pt). Use the upper end for long lines or a large x-height. Go to 10pt only when length forces it, never below for continuous text. The word-processor "single" preset is often tighter than this; "double" is a submission habit, so keep it only when the recipient's spec asks for it.
- **Margins set the measure.** Default: choose side margins that give 55 to 75 characters on a counted line. At 11 to 12pt on Letter or A4 that is usually 1.5 to 2in; the stock 1in margin runs 85 to 95 characters and is the usual reason a long paragraph tires the eye. Other print guidance allows about 90 characters and leading down to 1.2; that is the outer edge, for short or scanned documents. Bottom margin a touch deeper than the top. When a template or page limit is fixed, use two columns or a larger size rather than keep a long line.
- **Paragraph signal.** One, set in the paragraph style: an indent of about 1 to 2 times the size, or a gap of half to one line. Never tab characters, space runs or empty paragraphs. No indent after a heading.

## Headings and numbers

- Default: at most three heading levels. A document that seems to need more has a structure problem; merge or split sections before styling anything.
- Separate levels by one change each, in this order: space, weight, size. Not capitals plus underline plus bold; underlining is a typewriter habit and reads as a link.
- Number headings only when people will cite them (policy, contract, specification). Then use one tiered scheme (1, 1.1, 1.1.1) rather than an outline that alternates Roman numerals, capitals, digits and letters, which nobody can cite in one breath. Change when an existing numbering is already referenced elsewhere: keep it.
- Keep with next: a heading never ends a page, a figure label stays with its figure, a table's header row repeats on the next page.

## Foreground and background

- The foreground is the body and the facts a skimmer scans for (names, dates, totals, the ask). Background is sender address, date, reference lines, footer, page numbers. Background is the same face, a size or two smaller, one weight, grouped and left aligned; not centered, large or bold.
- A letter starts near the top; shrink a letterhead that takes a third of the page.
- Résumé or one-pager: the strongest material and the facts the reader is deciding on go on page one. If it fits only below 10pt, cut or take a second page instead of shrinking.
- A signature block stays on the same page as at least the last two lines of the body, with its lines joined by soft line breaks (not separate paragraphs).

## PDF and delivery

- Default: send a PDF when the page must look as designed. An editable file reflows when the reader lacks the font; if it must be editable, use fonts the recipient has or accept the fallback.
- Export with fonts embedded, then check the exported file, not the editor: page breaks, headings at page feet, figures, live links. Judge it at actual size, not zoomed.

## Inherited documents

Turn on formatting marks before judging. Usual findings: runs of spaces or tabs for alignment, empty paragraphs for spacing, manual line breaks inside paragraphs, double spaces after periods, headings made by hand (bold at body size) instead of styles, underlines for emphasis, justified text with hyphenation off. Fix by defining the body and heading styles once and applying them. Leave the content, order and wording the user did not ask about exactly as it is.

## Checks

- A counted line is 55 to 75 characters; body is 10 to 12pt with exact leading near 1.25 to 1.4.
- One paragraph signal, set in styles; no empty paragraphs or space runs.
- Three heading levels or fewer, no underline, no caps-bold stack; numbers only where clauses are cited.
- No heading at a page foot; no signature alone on a page.
- PDF has fonts embedded.
