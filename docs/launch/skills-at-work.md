# Skills at work: transcript

Text version of [skills-at-work.mp4](skills-at-work.mp4), a silent film of about 88 seconds with captions burned in. There is no audio. A [still-image version](skills-at-work-still.png) is beside it for anyone who prefers no motion.

Everything shown is saved material from this repository's test runs: screenshots, answers and request text from `evals/<skill>/heldout/`, and counts read from the results files by `scripts/results_table.py`. Each of the six examples was picked to show the move, so they are favorable examples and not typical ones. Nothing in the film is generated by an image or video model. The title and the skill-list picture in move 2 are drawn, and move 2's caption says so.

## The record

Across 38 skills, counting only the held-out tasks where the skill loaded (312 in all): 218 wins, 46 losses and 48 ties against the same model without it. 12 of the 38 passes are marginal.

In the latest round of updates, 12 proposed updates were tried and shelved because they missed the bar. They are not in the library.

Two merged updates show weaker evidence: the usability references added to user-research-basics, and the fresh second held-out set for that skill, which shows no measurable gain on the new set.

## Rebuild

```sh
python3 scripts/make_film.py
```

Needs Chrome (set `CHROME_BIN` if it is not at the default path) and ffmpeg. The script reads the counts, request text, screenshots and answers from this repository, so a rebuild after new test results shows the new numbers. `python3 scripts/make_film.py --check` fails if this transcript is out of date.
