The chart is mostly sound. I checked the geometry against the data: the axis scale is consistent, and the 410 and 521 points sit where they should. The baseline starts at zero, so the rise isn't exaggerated. I'd change three things.

1. **The data stops at June, and today is mid-October.** The board will likely ask about July through September. If you have those weeks, add them. If not, say in the subtitle why the chart ends in June, or be ready with an answer.

2. **The headline undersells the number.** 410 to 521 is +27%, which is a bit more than "about a quarter." I'd use the exact figure: "Weekly active members rose 27% in six months, from 410 to 521". That also puts the endpoints in the headline.

3. **"In six months" is slightly loose.** January to June is five months of growth across six monthly readings. "January to June" or "over the first half of 2026" avoids a pedantic objection.
