# Free items, trials and reasons to act

Load for a lead magnet, token price, trial, referral or other offer asking effort, a reason to act now, or a rival comparison. Brackets come from the user or stay visible.

## Pick the step
Match the ask to the stage: browsing gets a free item, comparing a trial or demo, ready gets price and terms. Take the lowest friction that still keeps out the wrong people; name them.

## A free item
- Name what it is and who it is for. Replace generic labels ("white paper", "ebook") with the thing itself: checklist, template, selection guide.
- Say what is inside and the obstacle it removes. Give a money value only if it was sold at that price.
- A little friction (work email, one qualifying question) lowers volume and raises quality. Judge it by downstream results, not downloads.

## A token price
A small charge filters out freebie seekers and cuts volume; the net effect needs a test. Show both sides, suggest a free-versus-token test, and name the metric (qualified conversations, not downloads).

## Trials
"Free" means nothing is charged unless the buyer acts. "Refundable" means charged now, returned later. Never call it free. State card or no card, the first charge's date and amount, and a one-step cancel.

## Payoff matches effort
For referrals, reviews, data sharing and setup-heavy trials, the reward must fit the work asked. State reward, limits, expiry and when it arrives, using only the user's terms.

## Reasons to act now
Only a real one: a start date, a batch size, a seasonal price, a bonus that really ends. Decline a countdown that resets per visitor, a repeating "last day", an invented stock limit; offer the true date or none, with why it exists.

## Proof and comparison
- Pair a big promise with a believable second one.
- An absolute ("fastest", "best", "never") needs scope, date and source, or it shrinks: "[median] in [month], from our own data".
- Compare with a rival only on facts the user gave, like with like, described fairly. Leave an unknown rival unnamed.
- Value: benefit before price, then what the price replaces (a person, a tool, hours), from the user's facts. On a pricing page lead with practical benefits; emotional ones need the buyer's own words, else [add a vouched-for quote].

## Pairs
- Before: "Download our free white paper!"
- After: "[Checklist name]: [what it covers] for [role]. About [minutes] to use. Needs [work email]."
- Before: "Refer friends and earn rewards!"
- After: "Share your link. When a friend [action], you get [reward] and they get [reward]. Up to [limit], ends [date]."
