# Terms and tiers: before and after

Short pairs with placeholders. Replace each bracket with the user's real fact, or leave it visible.

## Trial

- Before: "Start your free trial. Cancel anytime."
- After: "Try [plan] free for [length]. [Card needed / No card needed]. If you keep it, the first charge is [amount] on [date]; cancel before then in [where] and pay nothing."

## Renewal and intro price

- Before: "Only [intro price] a month."
- After: "[Intro price] a month for [length], then [renewal price] a month. [Yearly total] a year if billed annually."

## Guarantee

- Before: "Satisfaction guaranteed."
- After: "If [result] has not happened by [date] after you [buyer step], email [address] and we will [remedy]."
- No stated policy: leave "[refund policy]" and say it is missing. Do not write a guarantee for the business.

## Discount

- Before: "Save 20% today!"
- After: "Pay yearly and get [months] free: [yearly price] a year instead of [monthly price] a month." The reason is the billing choice, and both figures come from the user's prices.

## Bonus

- Before: "Includes a $[invented] bonus bundle."
- After: "Includes [bonus]: [what it does for the buyer]." No money value unless it was sold at that price.

## Tier blurbs

- Before: "Pro: more projects, more storage, more seats."
- After: "[Name]: for [situation or buyer]. Adds [the thing that situation needs]. Lacks [what lower tier leaves out]."
- Recommend one, with a reason ("[reason], so most [buyer type] start here"). Say "most popular" only from data the user gave.
