# Messages by moment

Short before-and-after pairs. Names and details are invented; adapt the pattern, not the words.

## Failure

- Before: "Oops! Something went wrong. Error 503."
- After: "We couldn't load your reports. Nothing was changed. Try again in a minute, or contact support if it keeps happening. (Reference: 503)"

A failure caused by the user's own action still reads as the system's limit: "That file is over 10 MB. Choose a smaller file or compress it first."

## Destructive confirmation

- Before: "Are you sure? [OK] [Cancel]"
- After: "Delete 'Q3 plan'? It will be removed for all 6 members and can't be restored. [Delete plan] [Keep plan]"

When the action can be undone, skip the dialog and offer an undo in the confirmation instead.

## Empty state

- Before: "Nothing here yet."
- After: "No saved searches. Save a search from the results page to get alerts when new matches appear. [Browse results]"
- Good news: "No overdue invoices."

## Permission prompt

- Before: "Allow notifications?"
- After: "Get a message when a delivery is on its way. You can change this any time in Settings. [Turn on] [Not now]"

## Cancellation

- Before: "We're sorry to see you go! Are you sure you want to lose all these benefits? [Stay] [No, cancel anyway]"
- After: Heading "Cancel your subscription". Body "You'll keep access until 14 March. Your files stay for 30 days, and you won't be charged again. [Cancel subscription] [Keep subscription]" An optional single line, shown once: "Need a break? You can pause instead."

## Price-change or outage notice

State the fact first, then date and amount, the action, and where to get help.

- "Your plan will cost $12 a month instead of $10 from 1 June. You don't need to do anything. To change or cancel before then, go to Billing."
- "Uploads were unavailable for part of this morning. No files were lost. We're sorry for the disruption."

## Notification

- Before: "Don't miss out! Check out what's new in your account today!"
- After: "Maya commented on your design. Reply"

## Success

- Before: "Woohoo! Your settings have been saved successfully!"
- After: "Settings saved." For a result with a next step: "Invoice sent to Dana. You'll see it as paid when she pays."
