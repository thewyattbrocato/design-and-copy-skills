# Live messages and reviews

Load when copy reports what is happening now (viewer counts, recent-purchase pop-ups, stock and seat badges, "popular" or "booked today" labels), or when reviews, ratings and review requests are written, summarized, placed or collected. Every example is invented and generic. These rules shape the requested copy itself: a label or scope phrase belongs inside the line, not in a note beside it.

## Live messages

A live message is a claim about the present. It is true only for one source, one scope and one window, so the line says all three or the number does not appear.

| Message | What the user must have supplied | Wording that stays true |
|---|---|---|
| Recent purchase pop-up | Order records with time and, if shown, place | "Bought 9 times in the last 24 hours" |
| Viewers | A live count of sessions on this item or page | "6 people are looking at this now" |
| Stock or seats | Inventory for the exact variant, size or date shown | "3 pitches left for 12-14 June" |
| Popular or best seller | A ranking, its set and its period | "Most booked workshop this month" |
| Booked recently | Booking records with timestamps | "Last booked 3 hours ago" |

- **Scope in the line.** Default to naming what is counted, where and when. A count over all dates, sizes or branches does not sit on a badge for one date, size or branch; if only the wide count exists, say so ("across all sizes") or leave it off.
- **No record, no number.** Without data, deliver the slot as a placeholder that names the field needed (`[bookings in last 24h]`) and a true benefit line beside it. Do not write the opposite as filler ("plenty left", "no rush"), and do not rotate invented names, towns or times through a pop-up.
- **People in a pop-up.** Default to aggregates. Show a first name or town only if the user says the buyers agreed to that; otherwise "someone nearby".
- **Freshness.** If the number comes from a daily export, write "today", not "right now"; "just now" needs a feed that updates as it happens. A message that would read zero or embarrassingly low is hidden by a threshold the user sets in advance and applies to everyone, never picked case by case to flatter.
- **Stock badges** follow the real count and clear when it changes. Default to showing one only at or below a threshold the user gave; change when the user wants it always on, then show the number whatever it is.
- **The reader's own action** is not social proof. "2 others have this in their basket" needs a count that excludes the reader and their other tabs.

## Reviews and ratings

Reviews are evidence only while the reader can tell where each came from and nothing that bears on trust is out of sight.

- **Summary.** The average and the count cover every review collected in the stated period, not a chosen subset. A perfect average on few reviews shows the count beside it. Syndicated or imported reviews from another product, variant or site carry their source.
- **Incentivized reviews.** A review from someone who got the product free, a discount, a gift, an entry or payment is labelled on the review itself, at the point of reading, in plain words next to the stars ("Received the product free"). A policy page or footer does not count, and the label travels with the review into any excerpt, carousel or ad. Default to keeping them in the strip with the label; change when the user would rather leave them out of the average, then say what the average covers. Staff, relatives, resellers and agencies get the same label for their relationship.
- **Asking for reviews.** Send the same request, at the same time, to every buyer. Do not screen first with "how was it?" and route only the pleased to the public page while the rest go to a private form. A private route may be added for everyone as well, with the public one still open. Any reward is for writing a review, whatever it says, and is disclosed as above.
- **Moderation.** Default to publishing negative reviews on the same terms as positive ones. Remove or hold for reasons stated up front that apply to every star level (abuse, personal data, spam, wrong product), and keep the reviewer's text unedited. A public reply is the right answer to a complaint; delaying, burying or rewriting it is not.
- **Ordering and filters.** Sort by recency or a named measure ("most helpful"), not by stars unless the control says so. Every star level stays one step away.
- **Excerpts.** A pulled line comes from a review whose overall verdict it matches and is shown with that reviewer's rating; a delighted clause cut from a lukewarm review is a splice, whatever the length. Quotes follow the quote rule in the main skill.
- **Writing reviews.** Never compose reviews, ratings or "customer feedback" in a customer's voice, even as a placeholder demo that will ship. Use the request message and `[approved quote, name, role]` slots from claim-fixes.

## When the request pushes the other way

| Request | Deliver instead |
|---|---|
| "Show only the 4 and 5 star ones" | The strip with the real average and count, best and most helpful first, all levels reachable; one line of reason |
| "Drop the free-product label, it looks bad" | The label kept, worded neutrally and briefly, with how many reviews that was and the average with and without them if known |
| "Send happy customers to the review site" | One request to all buyers; a private option offered to everyone |
| "Pop-ups with made-up customers until we have orders" | A benefit line now; the pop-up wired to real orders once they exist |
| "Show 'only 2 left' on every product" | The badge tied to each product's stock, threshold agreed in advance |

Give one line of reason, then the work; never refuse the whole task. Rules on endorsements, reviews and pricing claims differ by place and change; for anything beyond wording, say once that it should be checked.

## Quick checks

- Each live line names what is counted, where and over what window, and each number comes from a record or field the user gave or is a visible placeholder.
- No incentivized review appears without its label inside the same card, excerpts included.
- The rating summary shows its count and covers every collected review, or states what it leaves out.
- Review-request copy goes to every buyer; no step sends only satisfied ones to the public page.
- Low or inconvenient numbers are hidden only by a rule applied to all items alike.
