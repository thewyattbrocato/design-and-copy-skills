# Substantiation ladder

Load when a claim's strength is the question: "make it stronger", one customer's result, a comparison, or thin proof. Write the highest rung the evidence reaches, never one above it. Examples are invented.

## Rungs, by claim type

| Evidence the user gave | Strongest wording it allows | Disclose beside it |
|---|---|---|
| One customer's result | "One customer cut X by 40%" | That it is one case, with its basis |
| A small set or sample | "In our test of 30 accounts, median X fell 22%" | Sample size, date, what was measured |
| Own count of customers | The exact count: "140 teams", never "thousands" | Date or "since 2022" |
| Rating | The exact average and number of ratings | Where they came from |
| A comparison fact | One dimension, one named alternative, dated | Where the rival wins, if the user said |
| A "free" or trial offer | Free for what, until when | The price and date billing starts, one-step cancel |
| Nothing | What it does, or `[figure]` | Nothing; do not hint at proof |

A small true number stated exactly beats a large vague one: "140 customers" outruns "thousands".

## Framing versus misleading

Choosing which true fact leads is ordinary advocacy. Test: would the reader conclude something the writer knows is false? If so, add the missing fact beside the claim ("shipped from our partner's warehouse"), or compare like with like. A best case may appear only when labelled as one, with the typical case next to it.

## Requests under pressure

| Request | Deliver instead |
|---|---|
| "Write testimonials; we have none yet" | The customer request message and `[approved quote, name, role]` slots |
| "Say 'Save 40%'; one customer did" | "One customer saved 40%" plus a placeholder for the typical range |
| "Add 'Only 3 left'; stock is plentiful" | The real reason to act now, or the plain offer with no limit |
| "Make it look like news" | A page labelled as sponsored, same quality, sender named in the first lines |
| "Beat Competitor X on every row" | Rows where the user's evidence wins, plus one where the rival does |
| "Say 'no obligation'" where one exists | What happens after the request, step by step |

Give one line of reason, then the work; never refuse the whole task.

## Regulated areas

For health, money and safety, lead with what the product does and its limits; add an outcome claim only with the user's substantiation, plus one line to have it checked.
