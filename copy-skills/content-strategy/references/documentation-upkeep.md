# Documentation upkeep

Load for a help centre, docs site, manual or knowledge base that must stay right as the product changes. Scale to the team: a two-person shop needs the first, second and last items only. Never invent a figure, a version, a title or a reply time; use the user's or leave a placeholder.

## Before a page ships

- **Run risky steps.** If following a page could change or delete data, move money, or change security or access, a named person runs the steps on the current version before it goes out. Silence is not approval for these pages. An unrun page waits. Reading it through is not a test. Pages that only explain keep the lighter review rule.
- **Docs are part of done.** A product change is finished when its page change ships with it. Log a doc error like a product defect: the symptom, the page, the fix. The owner triages them.

## When a fact changes

- **Ripple list.** Before a changed value, limit, name or step ships, list every page, example, screenshot and translated copy that states it, and tell the owners of related pages. Reusing a snippet shortens the list but does not replace it.
- **Patch or rebuild.** A new audience or workflow needs the page's structure reworked, not another note. Rebuild when notes pile up, headings no longer sort the content, one caveat repeats, or steps for two kinds of reader are interleaved.
- **One page per fact.** Do not keep a full copy per plan, platform or version when a few lines differ. Keep one page and mark the lines that differ; copies drift and compete in search.

## Versions

Write a short version policy: which versions keep their own pages; how an old page is marked and linked to its current equivalent; what a reader sees when the page they want does not exist in their version (the release that added it, or a redirect); when a version's pages retire and where they point. Reference entries name the release that added or deprecated each option.

## Readers

- **Show freshness and take replies.** Where staleness matters, a page shows when it was last reviewed, not last edited, and offers a feedback route that reaches the owner. The owner sets a reply target in days (`[days]` until the user chooses one) and records what each message changed.
- **Read exits and ratings by page type.** A short visit to a reference or task page may mean the reader found the answer. A quiet page with several low ratings can matter more than a busy page with one.
- **Gap list.** Keep a list of subjects that pages mention but nothing explains. Check repeated support questions against it: first ask whether the answer exists, then why nobody finds it.

## Scope

- **Other companies' products.** Lightest coverage that works, in order: none; a table of the settings your product needs, in the other product's terms, without screenshots; a pointer with a search phrase, rechecked each release; full steps only when no usable vendor page exists and readers need the walk-through.
- **Translation multiplies every word.** Before content goes out for translation, cut topics with a tiny audience and lines that restate the screen. Give translators a term list, with approved and retired terms and one owner, before work starts. Do not reword stable sentences for style; each change is paid again in every language.
